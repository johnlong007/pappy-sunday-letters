Cast of characters

The Family

A guide to the people who appear in eighteen years of letters

Kenneth Wayne Buchanan — "Pappy"

Born January 15, 1936, in Unicoi County, Tennessee. Mechanical engineer, AT trail maintainer, car restorer, and for eighteen years, the faithful chronicler of family life. He wrote his last Sunday letter on May 3, 2026, ten days before he passed. He was 90.

Patricia Anne Showers — "GSH"

Born March 12, 1936, in Lancaster, Pennsylvania. She and Pappy were classmates and married in 1955. Mother of six, fiercely independent. He called her GSH — Gracious Serving Helper — a joke drawn from a Baptist sermon that amused him precisely because it so perfectly didn't fit. She passed on October 31, 2023. He wrote the following Sunday anyway.

The family tree

Pappy was a dedicated genealogist. His research traces the Buchanan and Garland lines back to the mid-1700s — James Buchanan, born June 1734 in North Carolina, is among the earliest documented ancestors. The family is rooted in the Appalachian mountains of western NC and eastern TN.
