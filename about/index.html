How this archive was made, and why

The letters

For eighteen years — from sometime in 2008 until May 3, 2026 — Kenneth Wayne Buchanan sent a weekly email to his family. He titled each one with a subject line that described the week from his perspective. He never missed a week for illness, travel, grief, or age.

This archive

This site was compiled by John Long (Anita's son, Pappy's grandson) in May 2026, the week after Pappy passed. The archive contains 1,521 emails, 10,167 photographs, and 466,478 words. Every letter is preserved exactly as he wrote it.

Adding to this archive

If you have memories, corrections, or additional photos, please reach out to John at john.7.long@gmail.com. A printed book edition is planned.

A note on the subject lines

Pappy titled every email himself. They are, in themselves, a kind of poetry: Calm After, Biking the Yucatan, He is Risen, Mom, Snowbound at 88 — still feeling unloved and unwanted, Abishag.

Kenneth Wayne Buchanan · January 15, 1936 – May 2026

Compiled by John Long, May 2026
