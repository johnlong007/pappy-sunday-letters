How this archive was made, and why

The letters

From sometime in 2008 until May 3, 2026, Kenneth Wayne Buchanan sent a weekly email to his family. He titled each one himself. The subjects describe the week from his perspective.

This archive

This site was compiled by John Long (Anita's son) in May 2026. The archive contains 1,115 curated family letters, 10,167 photographs, and 466,478 words. Every letter is reproduced exactly as written.

Corrections and additions

If you have corrections, additional context, or photos that belong here, contact John at john.7.long@gmail.com. A printed edition is planned.

The subject lines

Pappy titled every email himself: Calm After, Biking the Yucatan, He is Risen, Mom, Snowbound at 88 — still feeling unloved and unwanted, Abishag.

Kenneth Wayne Buchanan · January 15, 1936 – May 2026
