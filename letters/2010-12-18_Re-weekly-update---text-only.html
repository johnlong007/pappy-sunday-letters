This week was considerably more photogenic than last week. 
 
Dwight has not had a particularly good week.  Not particularly bad, just not good.  Dialysis was rough all three days, and he doesn’t do as well in the cold.  But, there is good news – he went in for his yearly screening for any signs of cancer recurring, and there weren’t any!  That is a test that it is really good to pass!  He’s been having trouble with his skin, especially his scalp, and he finally caved in and went to the dermatologist.  Sort of funny, in a way – the dermatologist was delighted to have Dwight show up as it turns out he is a Classic Case of one of the occasional side effects of dialysis, so they took pictures for training and were quite sympathetic.  Dwight wound up with two more prescriptions, for some relief, although unfortunately some of it is going to be just one more thing to cope with.  There are two pictures for the Dwight Category – one, when even in spite of the weather, he decided he just HAD to work on the yard project some, and two, when he gave up & came back inside & did more weather-appropriate activities.  Junior seems to feel that dignity is over-rated, and well worth sacrificing for a good belly rub.
 
My week has been okay; work was okay, especially since I only had three days of it.  I got a technical report finished!  FINISHED – a very good thing, to finish something – anything!  I have taken some walks, in between spells of bad weather – two pictures here, one of some impressive icicles on the Rhino Shed (isn’t that bent one neat?  I think maybe the wind did that?) and one of the neighbor’s barns, which if you look close you can see some more icicles on the eaves of the barns, too. 
 
The big news in the Critter Category is that the Posh Vacation Spa for Little Dogs has been open this week.  For critter pictures, I have one of each of the dogs – Roscoe, being sedate & dignified; Sam, in the middle of a rousing game of squeeker toss & chase (the squeeker is outgoing as he had just flung it, NOT incoming although it looks like it is about to bonk him that is not the way it was going); and Junior, right after I discovered that Junior can not be trusted in the closet as he has some kind of obsession with Dwight’s Taz slippers.  I have several more of Sam & Junior playing squeeker toss & chase, as we have substituted several rounds of that for the daily bulletin board walk because the yard is knee deep in mud, ice, and snow.
 
The last category, the TN = Pretty one, is a little different this week.  We had a winter storm go through Wednesday night, and conditions were such that we got ice instead of snow.  Thursday morning, we had a glittery coat of ice on everything, with more freezing rain coming up until the early afternoon.  I put the camera into a plastic bag, bundled up, and went for a couple of walks & took lots of pictures.  Here’s the five I liked the best – grass, pine needles, holly leaves & berries, one of the birdfeeders, and the pine trees on the east side of the hill behind the house – all covered with ice.  Those drips are ICE not water!
 
That’s it for this week.
