First come the black raspberries. Wayne and GSH picked quite a few after we
put up the bird inhibitors.

Then the red raspberries. No effort to shield them as seem to have plenty
for all God's creatures great and small.
But, the next few days will end their season.

However, so far it is looking good for the Blackberries.

Early in the week I mowed the hill up to upper RR patch. I do not believe
any have been picked there. Someone should check the patch.

While I was mowing, Max decided to investigate a ground hog hotel. Must be
quite a colony as I mowed over four doors, front and rear. Maybe like the
man who had 9 cats. Installed 9 cat doors. "When I say skat I mean skat".

For the first time in quite a while, I went with the AT crew. We worked at
Carver's Gap. The AT gets very heavy traffic there and many people choose
to stray off the trail.

A bypass had been worn in around a steep rocky section of trail

We positioned and installed rock steps up the steep part of the trail and
closed off the bypass.

One can sort of see the entire site in this scene.

This picture was made on the big rock to be seen in picture above.

Linda, and Andrew came for a visit. Those young'uns sure can put food away.

For an early July 4th celebration, we had a family event.

Laura planted some of Jack's (no pun intended) bean seeds. So, we installed
some of Lisa's purloined bean poles.
