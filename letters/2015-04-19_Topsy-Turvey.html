April 19, 2015 · Sunday

Topsy Turvey

8 photographs·483 words

I had my regular 1,00 mile checkup with Dr. Shafer. I took a list with
about 6-7 items to ask about. Answer seems to be "you are getting old, suck
it up". He did say I have some heart issues and has me scheduled for some
sort of test tomorrow. Stay tuned.

Herb's infection kept getting worse leading to his admission into the
hospital. He remains cooped up and hooked to various IVs metering various
fluids. Last report a few minutes ago is that it is looking better. (Leg
wound was red as a pickled beet when he went in). Stay tuned.

Some time back I read about a new worlds hottest pepper. Unable to resist,
I ordered 40 seeds. I planted 10 in little pods and gave the rest to Herb.
He planted 6 and took the rest to the vocational school to get the pros
involved.

Attached a picture of my results so far. When I was over to see Herb at
home early last week I looked in on his six. All were alive and well maybe
2 inches high. No report from the vocational school efforts. Stay tuned as
the season rolls on.

The potato patch looks more encouraging. Last year, Linda ordered some
expensive blue potatoes. She sent a few to Laura who harvested her crop and
gave me the leftovers to plan this Spring. I planted in accordance with
official signs and weather has been good (that is wet) since then. So far
so good. Also, all the trees are in bloom and looking good. About time for
a good crop.

No pictures of the LBT but I did get the oil pump installed and checked
out. Ready to roll out and fire it up I think. Wayne ordered shocks for it.
As soon as they are installed it will be ready to take to Benny to get and
exhaust system. Nothing can go wrong !

AT work was delayed until Friday when we went back to Doll Flats. A lot of
through hikers from all over the world. From Doll Flats, trail North drops
down for 2-3 miles to cross the highway. The first, steepest part of the
drop is in pretty rough shape so Carl plans to be working there for a
while. Also he plans to bring the volunteers in from Trail days to work
there. Friday the ones I was with cut a tree which did not fall where
planned. I spent the day rebuilding a section of trail.

After quitting time, Pat and I decided to walk out to the highway. Last
Sunday, I spent the day hunting for a rock for Alf to climb. We should have
walked out last week. We found a very impressive rock not very far down the
trail

Gray station old tractor event is this coming week. I will be working
registration from Wed. evening on.
