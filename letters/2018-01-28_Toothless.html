January 28, 2018 · Sunday

Toothless

9 photographs·248 words

The trip to Memphis to see Dr. Allison went better than I could have hoped.
Sunday evening a fine meal with Allison and Max at Central BBQ. Monday
morning a full dental exam, tooth by tooth.
Monday PM tooth extracted then on the road home. Back in two weeks for the
next chapter, stay tuned.

At home we are trying to get serious about the '63. I had ordered carpet
some time back and took advantage of some transients to get frony section
fitted.

​Since then, work has continued on putting panels in around the front
carpet and starting to fit rear piece.

​My able helpers are ready to punt on getting the seat covers GSH cut out
many years ago onto the new foam cushions, So, my plan is to go find an
expert to enlist.

​No AT work this week and not much outside work. I did go salvage a part
from the swamp car '63 for Dave to use on his Plymouth. Really glad I am
still able to do such things.

​We did get the truck unloaded as I figured will need it to transport the
seats.

​

Today, 1/2 our children stopped by at same time. Not sure what are whom
they were discussing.

​ ​We , as all know, have a lineup of all 26 grandchildren, Now, as the
family divides and multiplies, we have extended the gallery. Pretty sure
this is not all but perhaps it is the important ones?
