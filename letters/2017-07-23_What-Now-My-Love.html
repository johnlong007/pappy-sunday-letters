July 23, 2017 · Sunday

What Now My Love

10 photographs·370 words

This was my week for new and creative mistakes. At my age not easy to find
new ones but I try harder.

GSH is painting/staining the deck.

She ran out of stain. In order to make sure I got the correct replacement I
took the empty can to go to Lowe's. Turns out it was not quite empty and
the lid was not secure. I put can on its side in the top well. It sure does
stink and is proving difficult to remove.

I cracked a tooth at Rachel's wedding reception. Allison allowed it would
require a crown. It did. Did get that installed this week. Should have held
out and let Allison do it. I hear she has a list to work off. Sounds like a
family project.

I decided to start the IH by letting it roll down driveway. It did not
start. Many trudges up and down the drive later I decided it was not
getting fuel. Wayne bailed me out again today. He can still hear and
determined the electric fuel pump was not running. After more trudging
trips the problem was corrected. Now ready to find a new home.

​When we came home from church today , momma deer and Bambi were picking
crab apples.

​On another day, We delivered a group of intrepid hikers unto these
hills.It all looked pretty normal but something seemed to be missing. Not
sure what (or who).

​
AT work was an easy day for me. The group went to Osbourn farm section (our
section) to lay out work assignments for the upcoming ASU visit. Andrew
joined the group last years. While Carl and others were laying out
sections, he sent a group of us on out to the shelter to install screen
below the sleeping platform.

​
​GSH has been dragging brush from all around to thrown on brush pond. She
is about caught up so I cut down some of the "tree of Heaven" trees around
the soccer field. They will return.

As usual, inflicted a bit of damage.
