December 9, 2018 · Sunday

Dec.7

9 photographs·213 words

I remember Pearl Harbor. Do they teach anything about it in school now? Did
everyone see this picture of Adam?

I served 8 years in the Army reserves and two in ROTC. This picture is the
only one we have of me in Uniform.

Our social season is upon us ! Monday night we went with Dave and Carol to
watch ETSU play a basketball game. A fun outing.

Then on Saturday our SS class had dinner at the Food City feed lot. The
most over priced meal I have ever had but event was still fun.

I suppose the big event for week was getting new shingle roof on car
storage bldg. A crew of what appeared to be skilled workers did the job in
about 6 hours.

Who thinks they got all the nails?

Plan is to come back and put new tin roof on the barn and lean to.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, GSH blew leaves and I worked on the '63 rear
fender.

Can anyone identify this item? Ripe and ready to eat. (sorry about sideways)

It snowed last night, and today. We did not venture away from the house.
