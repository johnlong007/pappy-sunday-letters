December 1, 2013 · Sunday

Thanks

6 photographs·427 words

I had the opportunity to talk with, listen to and watch quite a few members
of the family this week. A couple were missing, Benj. and Christopher.

Perhaps the best part was getting to reminisce about the good ole days. Of
course, when I was young Thanksgiving was hog killing time. Apparently
Rachel has had some experience in working with pigs in the academic setting
so I determined she was very interested in hearing all about the process
used for open air butchering of hogs, cutting up, salting down, trimming
the fat, making both sausage and lard. Probably I should have asked someone
to video the entire presentation.

Since the official Thanksgiving events were elsewhere, I did not take
pictures. But I did get a couple here.

Rachel and Meredith came to town earlier than some others. GSH is making a
coat for Rachel's Christmas wish so she came by for a fitting. Meredith
came along for B & G.

Since we got a touch of snow and it was somewhat chilly, I mostly stayed
indoors and fiddled with the MM. Dwight procured paint and has been working
to color match it. It is getting close. I painted part for the grill with
his custom blend and the lower structure with the remnants of a spray can
of genuine MM 'Prairie Gold".

GSH has also been making some sort of outfit for Linda. Must be a leisure
suit.

While Linda and family were here, I conscripted Dale to check out the
electrical on the MM. You can note in the picture that one headlight is on
! Pretty much seems in order now except the starter motor did not run. It
may also be noted that a headlight lens is missing.

Aaron flew through Blountville and dropped in long enough to deliver
Vanessa ans sprat to Anita's delayed dinner. Fortunately, Beth and Jake
were already there so Charlie's favorite nephew had a chance to be
introduced to his cousin.

On the local scene, it was a pretty routine week with the cat. One day he
was keeping watch under the bird feeder when a flock of turkeys came
wandering down past the ramp and started over the hill. The cat immediately
went into stalking mode but upon getting with close range stopped to think
it over. Somewhat to my surprise, the turkeys just looked the cat over and
went on about their business.
