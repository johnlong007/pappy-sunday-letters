September 16, 2012 · Sunday

All's Well

10 photographs·537 words

What have I learned this week?

I do not know much about the care and feeding of a PC. It appears to be
alive and well now. I intend to get a remote, high capacity, hard drive and
keep everything backed up.

Remember when I tried to put a ten foot two inch plastic pipe in a ten foot
car? It broke the windshield. Finally got around to getting it replaced.
$325. I do not intend try that move again.

I have worked myself into the position of designated rock splitter on our
AT crew. We went back to Cloudland (top of Roan Mtn.) this week. Carl said
they had encountered a big rock in the relocated trail and I should go deal
with it (along with four willing workers). I will attempt to include a
before picture and picture of the steps in the trail when we finished.
There is still a lot of work to do in that area.

Most of the discretionary time this week was spent mowing. I am still very
pleased with the new mower. It is better and faster. Plus, it will climb a
tree (almost). The ground was dry so I worked some on the steep hill above
the soccer field. It works good as long as I go straight up and back down.
Now I am going to have to clean off a bunch of limbs before I can finish no
willing workers have appeared here.

Speaking of willing workers, for this weeks "what do GSH's do?" pictures
for Katy I have two. I hope she is catching on fast.

I stopped at the apple orchard on Roan Mtn. The late frost and a hail storm
wiped out the Winesap crop. They will have "apple butter" apples in Oct. I
have forgotten the name. I purchased a couple of bags full of apples which
can be seen in finished form under what a GSH does above.

While over that way, I stopped by the shop at which I thought I was going
to get a turnkey job on the '63. Turns out I was wrong. He only does body
work. Everything else must be done elsewhere. Rats.

The windshield man did say to bring into their shop in JC and they would
install the new windshield. Now, all I need is someone to take to get
exhaust system installed, windshield, top, interior, chrome refinished,
underneath bead blasted and finally painted and trim replaced and it will
be done! Wow!

Pepper crop is booming. I have dried quite a number, I am going to keep
each kind separate so can run taste tests. Now, I have been string to let
them air dry. Next I am going to start putting bags full on porches,
ringing bell and running away.

We have a fine persimmon crop this year. Anyone want to have a go at making
a persimmon anything?

Bonnie Bowman died. She was the wife of Junior Bowman, a cousin with whom I
grew up and went through school together including UT. He took agriculture
and spent career in the milk business.

Several unnamed family groups are quite derelict in keeping interested
parties abreast of their exploits, wise or otherwise.
