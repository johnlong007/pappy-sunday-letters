August 6, 2023 · Sunday

Round 2

12 photographs·355 words

After a 4 week respite, I was back on chemo this week,five days infusion, 7
days pills. I am to go in tomorrow for lab work to see what hath been
wrought.

I have a port which I suppose is here to stay, But, for infusion they hang
plumbing on which stays Monday AM until after infusion on Friday. Cramps
my style a bit.

We moved the hummingbird feeder over to Beth's limb. Now. We have a
steady stream of hummingbirds.

The cat seems to think one would make a tasty snack.

Tis sweet to be remembered.. Some time back Adam gave us two Grandparent
West Point cups.Turns out, they were not dishwasher safe. He must have
noticed because two new cups arrived.

SF broke her little finger . I think she said sitting on the edge of bed
reading to her 5year old daughter, slipped off the edge, stuck hand out and
broke finger. Not as exciting as breaking both wrists skiing. I gave her
several more interesting stories to tell people.

GM's peaches were a hit with her for breakfast this week PBBD put a net
under the peaches which has been a success, I noticed one in the net as I
was mowing. I think maybe one more on the tree yet.

Plenty of deer. A young buck and a mother with fawn, They like the
crabapples. Very few Turkeys. Not sure what has happened.

Wayne reported the errant master cylinder has been found and should be
repaired and returned shortly., No more 5 year plans for me but I have been
trying to sort out what I would really like to get done.
I think getting the 55 on the road in a safe, reliable condition is near
the top of the list. To get ready, PBBD washed off all the grime which had
accumulated.. Wayne was here today and I asked if I should go get a tag for
it. He suggested waiting a bit longer.
