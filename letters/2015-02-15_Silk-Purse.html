February 15, 2015 · Sunday

Silk Purse !

8 photographs·271 words

At last, at least one of the ole folks at home believe the silk purse
project is completed.

In other equally exciting news, The '53 Plymouth has a completed brake job.
I doubt it could have been accomplished without the active help of the
little dog.

When I was not engaged in aiding and abetting on the SP project I worked on
getting wheels ready for the LBT. Also repaired leaks in a bunch of other
old Chrysler product wheels with tires. I am not a bit sure what is to be
done with these but I am ready !

Wednesday, Herb and I went to Vanderbilt for pre-admission processing. We
learned nothing but confirmed the medical right hand has no clue what the
left one is doing, even inside the same building and the paperless office
is yet to come. He is scheduled to check in at Vanderbilt hospital at 6 AM
on the 25th .

On the subject of health BC/BS has been really pushing home Dr. visits the
last few years. They sent a Dr. by to check us over Saturday morning. No
surprises at all and we both ,once more, passed the Dementia quickie test.
(Maybe that is a surprise to some but it was easier than last year.). My
blood pressure was way up but back to normal after he left and I did my
running around.

We had a touch of snow last night and it was quite cold this morning. About
20 degrees when I ran around at noon today. Wonder if it was that cold for
the runners in Dechard or Cookeville yesterday?
