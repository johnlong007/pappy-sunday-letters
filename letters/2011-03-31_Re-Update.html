As some may know this Spring Break I journeyed to Disney World with
the band. We drove down Saturday night and got there Sunday morning.
If it was me driving I would not have chosen to drive at night but
since it's a charter bus I'm happy to just sleep. As we speak we are
driving back to good ole Blunvull Wednesday night to return on
Thursday morning which is my birthday!!

Ok, so Sunday we went to Animal Kingdom for the first half of the day
and continued on to Disney's Hollywood Studios for the second half.
Animal Kingdom is a really great place. They had a safari thing and I
got to see an 11 month old baby elephant which the tour guide lady
informed us will sometimes start running too fast and trip over its
own trunk. Adorable! There was one pretty good ride called Mount
Everest that was good. At Hollywood studios two exciting things
happened. I saw Meredith on her physics class trip and my Aunt Helen
and Uncle Ken and their two daughter who live there came and saw us!!
It was really good to see them and have their experienced Disney
knowledge. The car stunt show at Hollywood was really cool. It had
cars, motorcycles, power sliding and fire so I was happy.

The next day we spent at Magic Kingdom which is the biggest park and
what comes to mind if you just think "Disney World" (Cinderella's
Castle, Mickey, Tinklebell and all that good stuff.) Unfortunately all
day Tuesday it poured rain. However, since Disney is such a huge place
they don't close anything for a little rain. It was a tad miserable
but it didn't impede MY fun. About my favorite thing was the glass
shop. They had a castle there that was diamond and jewel studded that
was very expensive. Guess how much. Nope, wrong.  $37,500!! And the
lady said they've sold 6 or 7 in the last few months! I guess it's the
movie stars and such that buy them. Around the middle of the day we
went back to Hollywood Studios for our music workshop. It was very
neat! We sight read Aladdin music and they put it to the movie clip.
It was fun and the oboes only made it moderately painful. The best
part after that was the light show. It was incredible. I think if all
the Middle Eastern people could see some of Disney's light shows they
might be less violent.

Alrighty, Tuesday we went to Island's of Adventure which is not
actually part of Disney. This did not actually take away from its
awesomeness. It had some great rides including but not limited to: the
Hulk, Dueling Dragons, and the Harry Potter ride. The craziest thing
about this park was Harry Potter World. Imagine please, a whole piece
of a park dedicated to Harry Potter. It had all the stores from the
books and you could even buy Butterbeer (which is tasty). We rode
rides, stood in lines, did not get rained on and a good time was had
by all.

Today, Wednesday, we went to EPCOT. This was my favorite. It's a huge
park divided up into countries. It was so cool!! In China I really
really wanted to get a traditional silk Chinese shirt but it was
$96...ah well. In Italy I really wanted to get some Ferrari regalia
but it was similarly priced. In Germany I would've made my big sister
proud! I spoke German to the girl behind the counter! I said, "kommst
du auf Deustchland"? She said, "ja, Berlin" I said, "oh, meine
schwester leben innen Vienna." After that I admitted that "I sprechen
kein viel Deutsch" and i only took two years of it. (by the way, sorry
for all the grammar I butchered, Katy) Well needless to say the girl
was impressed and obviously smitten by me and my charms. Well, I was
impressed with me anyways.

I also bought some cool carved Elephants at the African part for my
collection. The fireworks show at the end of the night at EPCOT was
incredible. It really really made me happy. All the countries
represented around there was really awesome. After talking to the
Chinese and Japanese people I understand why Charlie hangs with Asians
so much. They are so nice and their culture is so history filled and
fascinating.

Well, that's all I can think of right now. Writing this on an iPod has
been fun though and it took up a little of my 11 hour bus ride. Well,
10 minutes til I am 18!! I probably won't send this til I get home
though so by that time I will be perfectly legal. :)

John
