November 16, 2025 · Sunday

Fall Break

9 photographs·181 words

The Signs indicate Winter is coming.

We are looking for an outdoor project to work on during warm
weather breaks. This week, I went with Sanford to the junk yard in NC where
I bought the little crawler and the '49 Dodge. Owner died, and the son in
-law cleared the lot, it is an empty field. We visited another site and
looked at an 8N.
Wayne is considering a couple of other options. I asked Farmer Lisa about
another possibility. Stay tuned.

Fortunately, help arrived from far away places to split wood for next
Winter.

Bring up a child in the way they should go.

We are considering starting our own museum of antiques and artifacts.

Work finally restarted on the '37.

Diane received her new, electric chain saw and hurried out to put it to use.

We do eat well.

And warm.

A & C have many projects underway. This week, they got the drive wheels
back on the IH crawler.
