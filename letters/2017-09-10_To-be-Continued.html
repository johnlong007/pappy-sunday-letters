September 10, 2017 · Sunday

To be Continued

9 photographs·455 words

First the good news, The big tilt trailer is functional, legal and ready to
find a new home.

The less good news is the other trailer is none of the above. I think the
brakes are working on all wheels but cannot get the lights working
properly. Too many connections and splices I believe. If I can find rolls
of wire I intend to rip all the wiring out and do it over.

​AT work was moved to Friday this week and I could not get act together so
did not go. Surprised to learn that they went right on without me.
For do-gooder work this week, we went to retrieve the '65 Plymouth I sold
to Dave Edwards years ago. I towed it to Rochester then. Somehow he got it
moved to NC and now I towed it back here.

On the way home, another tire had cord separate . The original tires on
truck were 215/85-16. Since then I have gone to 235/85. But the spare had
never been used and I had kept one of the original tires. So, today I
removed the tire with cord separated and mounted the old 215, Then mounted
the "new" spare and the just mounted (but not balanced) tires on the front
wheels of the truce. Making do and getting by. Should give me a while to
decide what to do next.

A tree fell on the hill in front of the house, I am pretty sure it made a
noise but I did not hear it. Anyway, while the BIG party was going on
Saturday, I dragged the tree through the woods and out in the open. Made
quite a mess in the woods. Too bad John and other Summer Campers are not
available. Maybe during apple butter making ?

Not sure when I will get the log sawed and split.

​​
​The IH tractor is ready to go. I know the Long family leans IH. Perhaps
someone is interested? Beth already has her tractor. Perhaps Charlie has
enough room for this one. It has a nice set of spare wheels/tires.

​
​​Winter is coming. I have been keeping an eye out for a hard top for the
Miata, p & I'd one this week. Rather pleased with it. Now, if I can get the
after market heated seats to fit comfortably we will be almost ready.

LLLL and Rachel came for a visit. No pictures here but maybe someone at the
party took some. Anyway, it was fun having them. Rachel worked her magic on
my hearing aide and brought it back to life. Also, Linda sheared my golden
locks. A fine visit for my part !
