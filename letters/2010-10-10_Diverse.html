October 10, 2010 · Sunday

Diverse

11 photographs·262 words

What should I report on first ? I know, Maria ! Charlie we have a confirmed
sighting, at least I am pretty sure it was her but a bit of a blur going
by.

Speaking of confirmed sighting, how about this one?

My older sister had some repair work done at the drive by window at
Vanderbilt Monday. Since she is the only sister I have, I felt compelled to go
check on her. I suppose it will take a while to confirm success but at least
the procedure went well. Since she had expressed an interest in the new dog,
we took him along.

Tuesday, I went with Dwight to Seymour. He and Pat have decided to change
the rear end ratio in the drag car. Pat wants to go from 4.9 to 4.7. The
test will come this coming Friday night. Check back. Oh, the hulk for the next
drag car has been obtained. A stock body but a home built tubular
Chrome/Moly frame.

The AT crew worked on the Curley Maple Gap shelter both Wed. and Friday. It
is ready for Gwendolyn et al. We will have one more day of touch up work.


The gaps in my week were filled in by running the weed eater on the hill.
About three more hours will finish that part I believe.

Anita is extending the fence on the hose pasture to include the part all
the way in the rear (where I encountered my first Yellow Jackets for this
season).

The new dog AKA "Junior" is coming along very well.
