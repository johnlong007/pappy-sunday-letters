July 20, 2014 · Sunday

Mini-Camp

13 photographs·328 words

Anyone know what this means?

Probably brings back memories for some. I had a fine time this week with
three willing workers. I suppose it will take checking with the
participants to see if the fine time was shared by all. The relative (pun
intended) group worked well (pun intended). I would like to try it with WW's
spread among cousins.

They did a variety of things almost all of which were things I wanted to
get done and for which I needed helpers (other than GSH).

​I wonder what they are thinking?

​

All the wood got split. A lot with sledge and wedges before putting the
splitter on the tractor.

​As you can see, Charlie showed up toward the end of the week. I thought he
had come to see what was happening to his engine stored in the shed but
turns out he had other objectives in mind.

Bait and switch perhaps? Anyway, the Miata trailer hitch is now on loan
from GSH to Charlie.

Andrew had a birthday (as most folks do). But, not everyone gets a birthday
brownie.

And I am sure even fewer people get a custom made cake delivered and served.

We had biscuits and gravy a couple of times. Once for Allison's friend from
Colorado (they do not eat B & G there ) too busy hugging trees I suppose.
She did a fine impersonation of a person really enjoying the food.

​The willing workers also seemed to enjoy eating. Not at all standard
Summer Camp fare. Hand made B-L-T-C sandwiches several times.

But only because Linda left a quantity of tomatoes. I am seriously
considering making a run to Dechard to get another bucket full. Perhaps the
WW'S would like to come back for an encore?

The workers also enjoyed resting.

​

​

​

Oh, one last thing:

​
​For the first time in my life, my (our) home has central A/C.

What is happening elsewhere?
