September 12, 2021 · Sunday

Muddling Through

8 photographs·257 words

For some who live in far away places with strange sounding names:

PBBD was a more or less willing worker several times this week as we
attempted to do what Trump could not, drain the swamp. In one of her
efforts she incurred a wound drawing blood and qualifying for a band aid.
Immediately insisted I take a picture for her contribution to the blood and
gore section.

Not to be outdone by a mere child !

I sure do like having Catherine back handy. She is an instrument expert. I
had one quarter of instrumentation over 60 years ago. I did not like it.
One year Santa brought me a Heathkit ignition analyzer. It has not been
used since Alan left home. Catherine read and understood the manual and the
two hooked it up and checked out the Chrysler, 392 Hemi in the Studebaker.

I pretty well got the mowing under control and spent a lot of time working
on draining the swamp.
It is clear PBBD required close supervision.
Picture credit goes to our ace photographer (GSH).

My plan is to make this deep upper part bigger and deeper using the removed
material to make the retaining dam higher. At least a foot, maybe two.
Sept, Oct and Nov are our driest months. Good earth moving weather.

In case you are wondering, Wayne did get the PU running well. It is still
parked out front waiting for tags etc.
