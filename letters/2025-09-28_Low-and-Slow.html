September 28, 2025 · Sunday

Low and Slow

13 photographs·308 words

The post drill is finished ! Well, almost. Another coat of paint.

I bought the corn meal mill which is the ag building from Henry Dykes who
lived on Long Island but grew up on a farm in Blairs Gap. I met him there
one Saturday morning to get the mill and while there I purchased the little
laundry stove and the post drill.

Once we started getting it ready to put on display, I noticed the workpiece
table was missing. Fabricating one has been a challenge.

Alan procured some angle iron which we were able to use as supports for the
table.

The table had to be clamped to the post to allow height adjustment.

Look closely.

Can you picture Cliff and Janet in 20 years or so?

Or Beth and Charlie?

How about Christopher and Allison?

Who knows.

We did indeed get the A_C running. I had taken the carb off and cleaned it
but did not disassemble. Tractor runs well but I think the carb float may
be sticking. Hope to turn that over to Wayne.

Walnut season has arrived. Last year was a bummer. For those who have been
obtaining and consuming Diane walnuts, there is a lot of work before the
cupcakes.

We need some small, agile willing workers for collecting the walnuts.

We are using our all purpose ATV to transport to the cleaning site.

Hulling is slow, messy work. Tough on ole folks with back pains.

After hull removal , serious cleaning is required. For the last couple of
years the pool room has been the drying room. Warm, dry and no squirrels.
Plan for this year is formulating.

Reward ! Positive, immediate and sincere.
