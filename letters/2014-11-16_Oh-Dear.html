November 16, 2014 · Sunday

Oh Dear

9 photographs·524 words

Many, perhaps most, of the Summer Campers have had an opportunity to help
stack wood. Some know it is a combination of skill and knowledge. Evidence
is that a lot pf physics is involved as Kenneth specialized in stacking.
Last week, we brought the last load of firewood up and I stacked it between
two trees right out front. Conveniently located to use in the fireplace.

The stack turned over, down over the hill. I commissioned GSH to hand the
pieces up the hill to me while I re stacked the wood.

For years I have given her a pain in the neck. This time, it is a pain in
the back. Having done this to myself several times, I assured her it would
go away in about three weeks. I had better be correct, Laura gave her a
book of exercises, the cat helps.

I fiddled with the LBT door latches and windows quite a bit this week. All
the latches are operable now and all but one window latch. I am having to
do some part fabrication for it.

The tractor is all peeled down, pressure washed, and waiting for new (in
hand) water pump installation. Stay tuned.

AT work was Thursday, It met all the basic requirements, cold, wet, and
miserable. We worked on the "blue" access trail along the Laurel Fork river
to the Falls.' The river has been running down the gorge for the last few
million years. Of course doing zig zags, cutting through the rock and
washing away all small everything. The trail is quite narrow and very slick
in many places. Four of us worked all day and rebuilt about 80 feet. It is
another 1 1/2 mile to the falls. Math majors can figure out when we will
get finished.
I took a picture of my section at lunch break and another at quitting time.
If you look in the background you can see how the trail is cut into the
rock face around the curve.

Katy commented on the Fall colors. So I took another evening picture for
her. GSH has suggested that we use the stack of barn lumber we have and
rebuild the smokehouse. That would have been an excellent Summer Camp
project. Maybe we will get some volunteers this Summer.

GSH cooked a picnic ham for us. It was very good. The dogs thought so also.

Bob's mother dies. We went to Welch to the funeral service Saturday. It was
a very well done service. It was also good to see Christopher, Allison,
Meredith and Benjamin.

A scenic drive from I-77 South on 52 to Welch. I should have stopped to
take pictures of some of the buildings but did not. Perhaps I will hitch a
ride and go back with Bob foe some happier occasion.

Herb was doing very well with his HPO2 treatment but caught a bug in mid
week. Hope/think he is about over it now.

Little sister has made an offer on a townhouse. Should know in a day or
two. I have promised to come help her move. May need some willing workers
to help.Stay tuned.
