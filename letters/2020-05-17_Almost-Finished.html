May 17, 2020 · Sunday

Almost Finished

12 photographs·389 words

It has been 11 weeks since I posted this picture.

Tuesday I pronounced the job finished on Albert's side of the fence.

Actually, there is no fence now but plan for tomorrow is to piece fence
back together and close the gap. We dragged all the wood to our side and
threw the brush into gully as seen in background per Albert's instructions.
All that pile in the center is the rootball which had to be taken apart a
chunk at a time to gain access to the trunk.

The big pieces are over 40" in diameter. Not yet decided on their fate.

The dog started cutting a shine one evening and upon checking I saw a
masked intruder had climbed the woodpecker feeder pole and was eating
supper. He skinned down by the time I found camera.

A pine tree up above the apple trees died. I hated to cut as then I have to
get rid of the remains but the dead pine needles bothered me. So, I cut it
and dragged down to the brush pond.

Took the tractor with mower up to the Oak stump to collect all the tools
which had migrated there. My willing workers rode along.

Saturday, I made the annual run to Doll GFlats to harvest ramps. Timed it
just right this year. Too early and not full grown, too late and cannot
find beneath the May Apples. As best I could tell, no one else had been
there, I did not make a dent in the crop.

Last year the deer cleaned out our strawberry crop. I have screen over the
patch this year and at least one berry has been harvested,

Wehad company. David lives in Atlanta, hope he did not import a virus.

I have been dithering some time about buffing or not buffing the "63. With
the help of PBBD decided to do some test sections. Still pondering but I
think we decided it should be done. Carefully.

Some may remember Andy Langford A friend from church time in UK. Joan and
Andy have visited a few times. He posted a facebook picture of his bird
food robber. Literally caught in the act.
