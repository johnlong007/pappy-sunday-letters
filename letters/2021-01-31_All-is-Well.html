January 31, 2021 · Sunday

All is Well

8 photographs·300 words

This morning we went to Perkins then on to church, normal Sunday morning.
Not many people at either place. How long can they each stay in business?

But ! tonight we went to dinner (not supper mind you we are moving upscale)
with old friends. Any way you slice it they are old friends. Friends from
church and friends from Friday Hikers. It was great ! Sort of like not
missing water until the well runs dry.

PBBD has been leading me by the hand as we jump through hoops in
preparation for the upcoming valve job. Several this week. Monday the
inspection of my heart. Dr. found 4 of the 5 bypasses were still
functioning and all three of the stints. The only obstruction is the Aortic
valve, hence the pressing need for a valve job. Took Dr. 30 minutes. It
took us 13 hours.

At home I mostly fiddled with the '37 windshield frame. The warped frame
led to my breaking the glass. Now trying to get frame proper before
installing new glass. The helpers fabricated and installed a
practice windshield but visibility is going to be a problem.

We have had a lot of rain. The retaining basin seems to be working and if
weather stays cold may be able to sell tickets as a skating rink.

When we go out and about we normally have escorts.

Our cat got a present ! Who would send a cat a present? All the way from
Portland, home of the mostly peaceful riots.

The home health care people are off to a good start. The neck exercises I
was given are helping and they are working with GSH on her balance and
stability.
