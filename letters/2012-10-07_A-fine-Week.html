October 7, 2012 · Sunday

A fine Week

10 photographs·511 words

I took Anita and went to Fancy Gap to get apples for apple butter. The plan
(per Anita) is to peel Friday evening and cook Saturday. I suppose that
will all take place right here in River City.

Linda called a few days back whimpering about trees being down on her
fences and neighbors who did not really care for horses making deposits on
their front lawn. (anyone remember the goats and the grapes?). So, I loaded
up my trusty chain saw and rode off into the sunrise to rescue the matron
in distress. It was fun, we removed two downed trees, Cut a whole lot of
brush for the kids to burn on some cold winter day, put an electric fence
up to allow moving the horses away from the newly downed wild cheery trees
and ran the disc over her garden. All in one day.

Dale has his Dad's little car. I believe it is looking for a good home (for
a nominal fee).

On Wed., we went back to Roan mountain to continue trail relocation. Only
five turned out and one of those was a handicapped person. Bob, Tim and Ted
worked on putting rock steps in for the switchback, Kim supervised them and
I dug trail. I roughed out about 75 feet which included moving some quite
large rocks. It still boggles me at the rich, black dirt on top of that
mountain. I think one more day and we will open that section. The leaves
were in full color and it was a lovely day to be out

The big time eater for the week was the Studebaker windshield wiper push
rod. I brought the Studebaker and the Ferrari out last week for repairs.
Wipers on the Studebaker and brake failure warning light on the Ferrari.
Then I discovered that the broken turn signal arm is still broken.

I figured about 1/2 day should do both jobs. I spent the better part of
three days on the wiper project and job is not finished.

First I had to remove the heater from the Studebaker to get to the broken
arm. Finally got it out, To make a short story, I cut and welded the arm
three times. I think I have it ready to weld once more but fear I am out of
inert gas (Argon) so I may have to wait. Working up under the dash with
only one hand and it with damaged fingers has proven very difficult. But, I
do not give up easily.

We went to an old tractor owners pot luck dinner. GSH fixed pickled eggs
and Anita fixed a tastefully decorated tray of hot to real hot peppers.
Both were big hits.

master pickling peppers. I may have to harvest them this week. It must be
before the first frost which will be in the next couple of weeks I would
