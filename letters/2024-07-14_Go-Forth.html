July 14, 2024 · Sunday

Go Forth

9 photographs·220 words

And multiply. Seems like "going out West" will do it.

Meanwhile, back at the homefront, we had a celebration revolving around
Andrew and co-starring Raclel and children. Not to be outdone, Meredith was
here with her supporting cast.

In spite of the heat, we made progress on the renovation project. If we
can only figure out how to generate square hole for the spikes all will go
along.

Expensive mistake for the week.

The '63 with its brand new top is stored in the car shed. Jacked up and on
stands. It is on the list to start and run through the gears once each
month. My role is to stand and enjoy the sound. Try that with your hybrid.

A dead tree fell on the hill above the soccer field. I dragged it down
beside the sawmill in order to determine what next.

I find it sad and perplexing that some people do not like tomatoes. Farmer
Lisa has influence with an employee of Scott's farm. Hence the plump, ripe,
red tomato.

Every week I get the same instructions, don't get sick, don't bleed. As a
result of my newly found careful behaviors, blood and gore pictures are
scarce and pitiful.
