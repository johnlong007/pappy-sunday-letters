April 16, 2023 · Sunday

Listing

15 photographs·488 words

I always have a list of things to do.

Some will remember being greeted with a list.

Now, I am limited in my selection of willing workers but each day when PBBD
9aka, Diane) shows up she is greeted with a list for today. Below is the
list for Saturday .

If you look at the list as of Saturday evening, you will note:
a few items have been checked off as completed
Even more items have been added.

Almost no car or tractor work this week. A lot of lawn work. Most of the
red rocks from Erwin have been pressure washed. Are you aware these are
sandstone from a seashore shaped millions of years ago before the
Appalachian Mountains were formed? Many show the ripple marks found in the
sand at the water's edge .

GSH did all the lawn work some years back. It seems like a good time to get
it all back in its original condition. So, here is WW toiling away closely
supervised by GSH and our new caretaker, Steph

Another willing worker arrived and knowing the house rules dealt with our
laundry.

Freshly pressure washed rocks. GSH did everybit of this work except I
positioned a few of the biggest rocks (with the Bobcat).

In other exciting news, The sawmill crew arrived with a very large, long
chainsaw and a rig to hold it while cutting very large logs. The log I
believe is about 48 inches across, It is the one I worked for a long time
dragging bak from where it fell in Albert's field. I am really glad to see
it being processed.

One of our Friday Hikers always dressed to the 9s. I would tell her that if
you can't be good you must strive to look good.

Niece Lisa has had my lawn mower for some time. Hers has been here in
storage. She sent a note saying the mower had died a sudden death so I said
bring it back and we will swap (again). Lawn mowers are shut off by
grounding the spark plug. The ground on this one was set too close and My
nimble fingers could not correct, Fortunately WW3 came by and dealt with
it. Lisa sent her minions over to enact the swap today. I shall await a
report concerning her ability to get it running while I check in to the
deceased mower for an autopsy. Stay tuned.

Another picture to stir homesick in those who live confined spaces.

This is the first row of ramp sets I planted several years back. If my
distant cousin from Flag Pond (and fellow trail worker) does not come
though with a supply of ramps I will harvest enough for one mess.
