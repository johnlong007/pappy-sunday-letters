December 15, 2024 · Sunday

fast Lane

12 photographs·513 words

We are moving along restoring old farm implements to like new. This week,
the second five shovel cultivator is almost wrapped up.

The almost part is the handles. Ordered from ebay with dec. 6 scheduled
delivery. Still waiting. Poised.

Most of our dry wood is softwood. It burns hot and fast. We brought up
another load this week, some of which needed split
Not too long back I could not have imagined splitting wood while seated.

We started accumulating VHS tapes many years ago, The plan was to watch
them when we got old. A few years back in preparation for getting old, I
bought a VHS / TV from a church in Bluff City. The first time I tried to
use it I got the tape jammed and stopped the works. When Wayne was here, he
got it working for me. Now, I am all set to get old !

I think about 1970 I bought a grist mill from Henry Dykes who had grown up
in Blairs Gap. While there to get the mill I also bought a couple more
items.
A laundry heater. It has served us well, Alan used it as a slow smoke
generator to smoke
Later we used it to preheat utility water coming into the house before
going to our electric water heater. pork in the smokehouse. Since heating
water was our biggest electricity usage for years it saved us a lot of
money.
Now, Diane has decided we need to find a worthy usage. Stay tuned.

Two things here. Aunt Mae's husband, Ralph Hice was an MP in WW2. When he
came home after he was discharged, he gave me his m1 carbine bayonet. A
ten year old kid could give it a lot of abuse in all these years.
The other item is more recent. I told one of my AT work friends I was
looking for a 1911, 45. He gave me this one. We cleaned it good, now need
something to put into it.

A long story.
About 15 years ago, I decided I needed a wood burning cook stove like the
one my mother used until I think about 1947. I paid too much and went to
far but I got one. Patricia said "You must be kidding" . So it has been
parked in the ag building since then.
In the meantime, Herb found and procured one locally.In shuffling old lawn
mowers around, I moved it out of the barn where Lisa covered it with a blue
sheet. Instead of putting it back where I found it, I forgot all about it
until she gently reminded me last week.
I told her if she would get her minions to bring it over, I would try to
do something better than letting it rust away.

She did. Stay tuned.

Once more, The Blountville Christmas parade.

In case you haven't heard. Local boy does good in big time.
