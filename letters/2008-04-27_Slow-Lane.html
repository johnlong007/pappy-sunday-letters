April 27, 2008 · Sunday

Slow Lane

7 photographs·196 words

Tuesday was work on the AT day. I had my usual lunch ,but, I had salad to
go with lunch.
We met a couple of through hikers. They had heard about ramps and wanted to
know what they were and how to harvest and eat. Needless to say, I showed
them.
We also came across a school group from Ohio. I told the teacher I was from
Erwin. She asked me to tell the students about Erwin. Knowing full well they
only wanted to hear me talk, I put on my best accent and told Erwin stories.
It was fun. She has been bringing a class for one week hike in the area for
the last 10 years. We promised to put her in contact with Bob Peoples who will
put them to work next year.
Blade repair.
? bird in Allison's Blue Bird house. They have a family to feed.
I am moving my tomato patch to the soccer field. Got the ground ready just
in time to plant a row of potatoes before the rain arrived.
