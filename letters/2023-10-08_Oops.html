Oops

10 photographs·178 words

For a while I have been having trouble getting my feet tangled up. I
stopped the truck in lower drive to help PBBD load some walnuts. Clearly
failed to properly engage the parking brake.
Rats, a historic first.

Continuing to preparing for Winter, we undertook to clean the wood stove.

Not who "we" includes.
"

Of course more to preparation than cleaning the stove.

Every day calls for 8 laps at the track. GSH does not like getting cold.

One day SF went to the track with us. On the way home we stopped to visit
the Taj McStall. Turning into a 4 star abode.

Getting the stove out was one thing, pushing it back was another matter. We
called in the young and strong.

Anyy one heard the British song"My father was a dustman". You should. My
father installed coal furnaces. When one had to be cleaned, guess who got
the job. Clearly, skill has been passed along.
