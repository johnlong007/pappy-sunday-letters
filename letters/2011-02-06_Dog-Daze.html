February 6, 2011 · Sunday

Dog Daze

11 photographs·363 words

I understand why the previous owner flung Yappy out in the Kroger parking
lot.She has a very piercing Yap which she seems to use incessantly. But, I
am getting a bit attached to her.
Pat found a deer antler. If we can find another, we can outfit Yappy for a
role in the Grinch.

We took her to the vet to get her "fixed". Paid a bundle of money to find
out she had already been to the repair shop. Not content with that
investment, it was decided we need to fence off an area so we can get away for more
than a few hours.

The people report is a lot like previous weeks. For AT work, we continued
the by-pass at Watauga lake.

Just for fun, we took our little animals and walked the relocation Sunday
PM.

Other than dogs, what did we do? Well, I worked trying to adjust the 1/4
windows in the car. I did not get the job done. GSH has started on the
interior upholstery project. The doors and side walls require a backing panel
behind the upholstery material.

No do-gooder projects this week but Dave did come over one day to help on
the car project.

In much more exciting news, we made a multi purpose trip to work on the
race car Saturday.We delivered the race crew track support vehicle AKA golf
cart. Since the last trip, Pat had scrubbed the new race crew transport
vehicle AKA motor home. It looks like new!

The old fashioned ignition system was replaced by a new and improved
digital ignition system. The new system can control spark intensity and duration
all the way through the burn cycle on each cylinder.
It is all part of the chase for the elusive 4.7 second run.

I offered the opinion they could get the requisite .2 seconds by reducing
the size of the air inlet to the actual carb inlet size. Pat was mildly
offended and Dwight skeptical. I have asked the engineering technical support
staff to run the calculations but got no encouragement it would actually be
done. Something about which side of the bread has butter on it.
