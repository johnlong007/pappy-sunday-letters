June 25, 2023 · Sunday

Broke Down and Stove Up

9 photographs·229 words

How I spent the arrival of Summer.

Fortunately we have a lot of willing workers who kept us fed and watered.

LLLL and Rebecca spent the week here. Linda shuttled me while Rebecca
checked to see if she would like to be a traveler nurse. She also picked up
a new skill .

My view for a lot of the week.

I did get some mowing done. There are RR briars all round the edge of the
woods. When we came from track time, a deer was standing in the driveway
picking berries.

In addition to track time a fair amount of bird watching.

GSH continues to shrink But she is still her and we can still
communicate (in our own secret codes).

A & C are over most evenings to aid and abet. Saturday, Alan brought his
long, long chainsaw and the restraining rig. I believe he plans to do some
serious sawing quite soon.He may need helpers or even watchers.

Today, because of all my restrictions, I did not go to BFST or church. God
will understand. We did go for a Sunday afternoon ride. Out to scheck on
Meredith's orchard. It seems one of her seedlings has come a cropper.

The dog and the cat are as usual.
