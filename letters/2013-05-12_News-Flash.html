May 12, 2013 · Sunday

News Flash

12 photographs·302 words

Confirming sightings of two GGC.
Today is Mother's Day. I trust that the mothers were treated to breakfast
in bed by the male Buchanans. Not sure how Jake and Aaron did but hope they
got off to a good start.

Note the relaxed pose(s).

GSH report !

She held a baby for the first time in a while. Ground up a 100 year supply
of peppers. Kept in separate containers to suit any taste. Helped install a
new pulley on the VW alternator and worked on Rebecca's uni suit (picture
withheld by instruction!) and fed the hungry.

I went of a ramp digging trip with Kim and Meredith and fed the hungry
hiker.

If anyone wants to eat ramps for breakfast or supper, come on over, we have
plenty to share.

I also spent another day working on the AT. We finished beating on The Rock
and worked on improving the trail leading to the rock. I did not get a
final picture of the rock after side log was installed but did after
hammering had stopped. A lot of through hikers this week. They are
assembling for Trail Days.

A couple of graduations this week. I could not find an online picture of
Clemson graduation but did get TTU with graduate and supporting cast.

I spent a lot of time trying to fit the door windows into the '37. I almost
have it .
Also spent a bunch of time on the VW. Why on earth did they put an over
running clutch on the Alternator?

How bout elsewhere?
