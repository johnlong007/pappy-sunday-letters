This is a close up picture of the invasive grass species which is trying to
take the place. Nepalese Brown top or something akin. Not even a goat will
eat it. But, it has inspired me to mow the entire place.


I should finish this week.

My cohort Dave is not doing very well. I tried to get him out to work on
the AT this week but he could/would not go.

I worked Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Tuesday we cleared the access
road to deliver the building materials to the jump off point for the
shelter, Wed we loaded the materials and on Thursday I delivered to the site.

Tearing down the old shelter was scheduled for Thursday and Friday and
rebuilding for Saturday and Sunday. I helped with tearing down on Thursday.

Delivery of the materials was to be done by the Forest Service folks. It
did not go well on Thursday. We had cleared almost two miles of access road.
The driver expected to make a roundtrip in less than an hour. He only
delivered one load Thursday. I will be interested in learning how it went from
then on.

Friday,GSH and I went to visit a cousin in Northern VA. The same one I
visited about a year ago. It was a fun visit, a nice drive up and home a
relaxed stay.
Saturday, we went to a local event.

They had two large kettles cooking, strictly by the numbers. 235 degree F,
six hours, 35 pounds of sugar then one more hour.

Back to reality on the home front.
