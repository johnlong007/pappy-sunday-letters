September 14, 2012 · Friday

Main Report

13 photographs·477 words

As some may remember, GSH and I went on a bike trip along the river Main in
Germany. Joe delivered us to the airport and Charlotte then He and Anita
met us upon our return late last Friday night.

All was well with the dogs, the house and the PC on Saturday. I downloaded
all my trip pictures in preparation for the grand report. Alas, the
computer would not open Sunday. A modern version of blue laws as I got a
blue screen. I took the machine in to repair shop Monday and got it back
late today. It has a new little cooling fan (apparently the source of the
mysterious noise all along) and a new hard drive. The repair person
speculated heat and dust along with failing fan led to crashing and
burning. It appears he was able to save all my files and pictures as
everything seems to be working now as before.

Back to last Sunday night report:

Joe delivered us to Charlotte, we flew to Atlanta and on to Frankfurt
arriving about 6 AM as I remember. The group assembled at 10AM for the bus
ride to bike ride starting point. Except for Beth and Jake. Fortunately, we
milled around for 15 minutes or so until Beth appeared and announced that
Jake was retrieving the luggage.

A girl named Sue is the manager for IBT. She goes on all new tour routes
and most of the Miller group tours are new routes. So, she was on this
tour. That was fortunate as I have no doubt the German who was group leader
(trainee) would have had the bus on the road at 10:01. Anyway, I like Sue
and am glad she was along as we had an accident along the way.

The short report. We rode a bus to tour start, rode our bikes along the
river Main back to Frankfurt and flew home.

The first 1/2 of the ride was through beer country. The last 1/2 through
wine country. We saw a lot of churches (big, old and empty) , a lot of
forts (to collect toll on river traffic), a lot of castles and I ate a lot
of brotwurst and brot.

About the third day, one of the riders got a stick caught in his spokes,
locked the front wheel sending him headfirst into the asphalt. He was
carted off to the hospital and stayed there all week. Then, he was released
but told to stay in town until the 14th for a follow up CT scan. His
daughter was on the tour and promised to let me know his status.

I will include a tastefull selection of pictures. If you want more, let me
know. If you want a private showing, let me know.

It was a good trip but as usual I was glad to get home.
