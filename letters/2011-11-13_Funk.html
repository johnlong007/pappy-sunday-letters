Funk

4 photographs·190 words

At least one interesting and exciting event this week. We went with Joe and
Anita to visit Beth and Jake. Actually, some of us went to visit Jake's
yellow submarine.
Jake was on duty so he got to lead us on a through and detailed tour of his
workplace and, home away from home. We pretty much saw it except the
reactor room.
He said picture taking was ok with good judgement so I took one outside
(where it was cold, dark and windy) and one inside of his office and
bedroom (shared with two other officers !

On further reflection I had better not include the inside shot as it might
be off limits. If you really want to see the picture, ask Jake.

For AT work, we were back at the Osbourne Farm site . We cut blowdowns,
repaired bog bridges and staked out future relocations.

PBBD said she did not get a picture of her son while he was in for his
brief visit. I did. Notice the TLC being given to this family heirloom.

Is anyone really interested?
