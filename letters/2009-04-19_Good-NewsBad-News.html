April 19, 2009 · Sunday

Good News/Bad News

8 photographs·357 words

Which do you want to hear first?

Do you remember a while back when Sis and I went to Fayetteville NC to
visit a cousin Joseph Leonard Davis? The contact with him put me in contact
with some members of my Grandfather, Joseph Leonard Buchanan's first family.
Joseph Leonard is the son of Maude Buchanan Davis, a daughter in the first
family. Anyway last week a sister of JLD died (Bessie Davis Vest), she was
94 years old and had gone full speed to the end.
Also, Bobby Williams and Jim Anderson died. Both friends from CHCC days.
Bobby was a year older than me, Jim two months younger. I believe they have
me bracketed so a couple more rounds should do the job.Of course, Bessie was
94 and some of the Buchanan branch live a long time. Stay tuned.

For good news, Hal was able to get out and make both the ISCC funerals.

For other good news. After getting help from a sterling pit crew:

The Cletrac lives! Can you identify the fortunate drive?

In other exciting news, chipping continues on Anita's rock. It is liken
unto "how to eat an elephant".

I went to chainsaw recertification school Saturday and Sunday. Saturday was
class and was dull. I learned a couple of new things but not worth driving
200 miles and spending 8 hours in a class room.
Today was field work. Displaying skill and knowledge. I was first to be
tested and had alreadt determined to learn as much as I could. I tried some
different cuts and also tried to make the trees go places they did not really
want to go.
I was aiming for the rock beside the tree trunk with this tree. The upper
stump is one in which I did some plunge cutting. Today was fun. I suppose I
passed, will hear by-and-by.
Dwight is helping with insulating the pool. He bought some for expensive
spray adhesive to cement EPS to the plaster. We have panels submerged now for
trial. Stay tuned.
