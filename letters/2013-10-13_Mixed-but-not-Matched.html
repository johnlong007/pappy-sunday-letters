October 13, 2013 · Sunday

Mixed but not Matched

8 photographs·506 words

Since Wayne and Linda got me started about 1980. I have been
running/jogging/shuffling about every day. In 1997 I started having light
headed spells from time to time while running leading to my by-pass
operation. After the operation, the Dr. told me I might need a stint
installed "in a few years".

Here lately, I have had minor light headed incidents while running. During
my scheduled visit to the heart Dr. I asked about it. He offered to
schedule a stress test. I told him I would it it got more severe or more
often.

He then dispatched a prescription for nitro glycerin pills and said I
really should carry them while running.

So, It looks like I may have a stint procedure lurking in my future. Closer
than it used to be.

The vw still has a noise in the drive train. I have about decided to keep
driving it until something happens. However, since I had promised to go to
Norfolk to retrieve Granny Anita, I had to drive the Miata and GSH did not
get to go. But, I got to see Paul, Beth, Charlie and Jack in some order.

That child is a cheerful soul, a keeper. He is also solid as a brick
outhouse.

I got a report from Charlie on his work and on the great adventure of going
on an aircraft carrier sea trial.

Does everyone know that John and Maria are in various musical endeavors at
TTU and some are live streamed?

I understand other institutions also live stream some programs but have no
details.

Saturday, I went with Sanford to a pretty small old car event in Glen
AyreNC. Close to
Buladean. After we left we drove up Beans Creek and I pointed out the
various places which I had visited when we made our trips over to visit my
Grandmother.

On the way back, we stopped by the more recent ancestral home place to
check on apple butter making and to scavenge a couple of car parts to help
keep Laura's Daytona in original dress.

After I I got home, I found Gwendolyn had put in a request for a welcome
home (from school) dinner.

Perhaps some others will drop by before too long.

Today, I did get out to check around the home place. Getting pretty close
to having it ready for Winter. Ready or not, here it comes.

I seeded the upper garden with Rye. Tom and his harem are appreciative.

Speaking of Tom, it had been going well with GSH making friends with the
cat until today. We were sitting in the swing with the dogs under the swing
when the cat came sauntering up. Dogs saw it and when into full chase mode
around the house and cat up a tree. The cat has not been seen since then.
Rats.
