March 9, 2025 · Sunday

Beyond Measure

15 photographs·431 words

I have really missed my AT trail working and the people with whom I worked.
Jim Foster, a distant cousin, and fellow trail worker came by. He got me
caught up on some of the workers. He also told me how to find the trail to
access and overlook this side of the Nolichucky river. I had the starting
point wrong and with the wrong start you just can't get there. I may decide
to try it.

Today, we went to the track. A fine day, fine company. I made 5 laps, most
in a good while.

All who know me know I preach and practice that the length of a visit is
not related to the length of the trip. Not many have proven willing to buy
in . Charlie has and did. He drove down from Pa. to spend Saturday morning
with me then drove back home. I am deeply appreciative.

I am also appreciative of Meredith for coming to visit.

I think she and Charlie talked about work and children. Two of my little
Summer Campers. Time flies by.

Did I mention Wayne got a bike ready for me to try? I have had to give up
on doing a bike ride across the US, but still hope to turn a few laps on
the Blountville or Central track.

I am afraid to try to bike in the gravel drive but Charlie checked it out
for me. I think he said it is not shifting into first gear.

We got a lot of work out of Charlie, no wonder he made such a short visit.
He washed the pool walls. I have not quite given up on seeing the pool back
in operation. It is a thermodynamic issue for me.

The crawler repair crew were also blessed with helpers.

Finishing the scooter required fabricating a replacement bumper.

Now, when we get a seat belt and a roll cage it will be ready for usage.

We have several hand tools with handles missing . I had a small axe handle
left from equipeing Paul with an ax. I cut it down with a rasp and
installed a hatchet head. Almost ready for a test run as soon as Bob
fits it with a wedge.

Apple cider press is ready !!

We brought up the last of our cured hardwood for the fireplace. Next, the
furniture.

Dinner and a show.
