July 19, 2015 · Sunday

Ponder This

7 photographs·434 words

According to my calculations, I am now 79&1/2. Not too long ago I would
have thought that was old, real old. On the other hand. I am the youngest
of four and the other three are still ticking. Not necessarily on all
cylinders but still ticking.

One of our children who has two GGC was over here one evening along with
some other well experienced parents. The question came up as to at what age
child number two learns how to pull the chain of child one.

Anyone want to voice and opinion? Perhaps we can get some interested party
to collect data?

Some time and money spent on trying to get the truck to start properly this
week. Installed a new lift pump. But no success. Stay tuned. I also worked
on the Alfa electrical a bit and very quickly wished I had not even
started. Is there and electrical engineer in the house?

Made a couple of trips to Erwin. Herb is doing well but going bonkers w/o
his drivers license. Pulled for six months because of the seizure. I took
the little dog once. She behaved very well but tried to intimidate Sugar
Bear.

For AT work we started at Low Gap and walked in 3 1/2 miles (each way) to
do water bar clearing. All highways cross the trail at one gap or another.
So, on the AT it is always up hill going away from the highway crossing.
The name "Low Gap" is a clue that one is in for a long uphill drag. It was
hot an muggy.

I have followed Katy's exploits with interest. But, have not given her any
tips on being a GSH for a while. So, more pictures. I especially like the
tree lopping . One of the GOB's at breakfast some time back pronounced that
anyone over 65 had no business being up on the roof.

Kingsport Fun Fest this week. Some people ran in the road race, some people
did not.

I am having a difficult time keeping up with all the grandchildren
scattered hither and yon this Summer.Germany to California, Switzerland to
Utah , Alabama to Madison.It would be interesting to see all locations
posted on a map At least Aaron is in the states for w spell, at least that
is what I hear.Allison left KPT this morning to start her next chapter, Not
even close to engineering, oh well maybe I can get bargain dental care.

I hope my Lil' Sister is aware that she is nearby and will drop in to check
on her from time to time.
