The (Local) graduates !

Monday, Tuesday and Wed., we went for a backpacking trip on the AT in VA.
We covered about 30 miles including McAfee Knob, the most photographed spot
on the AT I am told.

After we got home, we undertook to cover the cherry tree. Many a slip twixt
cup and lip. I had evolved a perfect plan involving a 28 by 28 foot
netting and some long PVC poles. The poles come in 10 foot lengths which will
just fit into the car diagonally. However, if one leaves the coupling on the
end and closes the rear hatch the pole has enough column stiffness to break
the windshield.
I suppose I could blame it on the hail storm rather than stupidity.

Pressing onward, I was able to enlist some sort of willing workers to
actually install the netting. Wanting to preserve the event for posterity, I
took my little camera to the work site. However, the Kroger dog found the
camera and shook it out of the case before during or after thoroughly chewing
the bottom from the case. It took a long time, a lot of ill thoughts and a
bit of luck to find the camera.

Saturday, It fell to my lot to deliver the lonesome travelers to the
airport at Charlotte. No pictures there as I just drove slowly by and pushed
them out.

Upon getting home, I found that GSH was busily engaged in working on her
table project. Signs are it is going to be both sturdy and pretty.

Herb said strawberries are selling for $12/gallon this year.

On the way back from Charlotte, I stopped in Erwin to visit my relatives.

A bonus.
