February 24, 2008 · Sunday

Hum Drum

7 photographs·231 words

Dave was out of town so no do-gooder work Monday and AT work was moved to
Wed. due to bad weather.

Saturday evening we had B & G for Wayne's departure for the grand tour. But,
I did not take any pictures. Just put this in so Katy and Aaron would know
what they missed.

We repaired a couple of bogs in the trail Also cut some big blowdowns.

But, I did make some B & W pictures with new camera while working at
Blountville. Did not intend to but did anyway.

Bad news! The Southside fire dept decided they wanted to reclaim the fire
engine and refurbish it themselves. Herb told them to bring the money back and
they could have it. Oh well, easy come, easy go. Stay tuned to find out if
they do indeed come and get it. It had sat 4 years in a field w/o being touched.

Good news ! Herb kept his numbers up by getting a new old Jeep. actually, he
has owned it a look time but it was being restored.

Herb came over and got the crawler going. He operated it while my helper and
I used the Bobcat to level the soccer field. I want to get it level within a
couple of weeks to get it seeded.
