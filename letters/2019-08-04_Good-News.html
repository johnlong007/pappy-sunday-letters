August 4, 2019 · Sunday

Good News

11 photographs·388 words

Went a long way toward canceling out the bad news.

Adam passed his fitness exam. Chelsea ,looking good. Hazel ? Humm, I have a
sister Hazel as does Joe, wonder how they picked hazel? Anyway, without
being asked, I approve. Janet has a new job, Gwendolyn likes her new job.
Greer is going to be OK, Lots of good news, lots for which to be thankful.

For bad news one cousin died and one high school buddy died. Each lived
long, full, meaningful lives.

I went in Monday to get checked out on the UTI. No news back so I am
willing to assume that is good news.

Finally got the new tire mounted on the Bobcat. I made a very hard job
mounting tire on wheel. I have to relearn how to do it every dang time.
Wayne pulled bottom pans off so now I will pressure wash from underneath,
start engine, operate hydraulics and look for the leak. Stray tuned.

One evening when we came in there were 7 deer under the apple trees. One
had antlers. The next evening they were all back out in the circle and this
time had the fawn along.

One day, we took Granny Anita to visit some of her grandchildren. We went
to visit their herd. When we arrived the scene brought back many memories
for me.

Oh, the goats ! I do believe Jake is going to be able to keep them penned.
At least as long as they are happy in his lot.

One day we went with Sanford to have breakfast with his friends in
Elizabethton . It was quite good and we went again on Friday morning. This
time his B-I-L drove his Studebaker.

Peaches are an iffy crop in this area. But, this year we did pretty well.

I purchased some tractor implements from our neighbor, Albert. One item was
a scoop. In getting ready to get serious about the holding basin I decided
to check out the scoop. The trip linkage was inoperable so I did the F & I
bit. It works well.

But, the big news ! Sunday PM Pat came and painted the '63 !!!!

I am delighted.
