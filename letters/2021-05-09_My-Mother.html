May 9, 2021 · Sunday

My Mother

13 photographs·409 words

I miss both my parents. Not sure which I miss the most. Not sure which had
the most Impact on my life. My earliest memory of my Mother is sitting on
the arm of her chair (Jimmy on other arm) while she read bible stories.(
Hurlbut's story of the bible. My sister has the one Mom read from but I
have a copy if anyone is interested.) Herb and Hazel in the floor. Very
good memories of the years while they were all in school before I starter.
I was an only child for three years.
After I retired, I got to spend a lot of time with her. Took her a lot of
places, Glad I did.

I made two trips to Erwin this week. No pictures on either trip. Monday I
took Flo some ramps and got to see both Mike and Gary. Saturday, I took
ramps to Dan and stopped by to see Jimmy.

On the subject of ramps, Even better than in potatoes. Lightly cook ramps
then scramble eggs with the ramps.

This picture is my ramp patch. They are starting to die down, as they die
down, the weeds come up making the ramps very tough to find. I think this
coming week will be about the end of the season.

We have been going to Wed. lunch with the GOB's.A lot of looking up
information.

Saturday we went to PBBD's and helped trim juniper hedges. Fortunately she
had smuggled in some willing workers. Summer Camp material !

Organizing shop continued at a snails' pace

Some know the first drawer in big tool cabinet has one hand operated hand
tools. All these came out of that one drawer. We are having trouble coming
up with a plan for storage and access. Stay tuned.

One day, I got pretty much all caught up on mowing. Looks good. But, the
mower quit just before I was ready.

The new tie rod end broke and I burned the starter mower by leaving it hot
wired while removing the broken tie rod end. Both new parts on order.

Today was Mother's Day. GSH heard from almost all her children. Laura
brought a cake by and stayed to sample my pickled eggs/beets.
I ,in turn, did more than sample the cake.

Memories ?
