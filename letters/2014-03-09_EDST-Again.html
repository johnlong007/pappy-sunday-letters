March 9, 2014 · Sunday

EDST Again !

10 photographs·321 words

The plan last fall was to drag the LBT in for a project during EST (Winter
!). EST has come and gone but the project is a long way from finished. Stop
where I am and put it back in the shed until next Winter or press bravely
onward?

This week I worked on repairing the rusty body posts.

It went very well but I have now done all the easy parts .

We did AT work on Wed. The main group went to Chestoa but Carl sent me with
Pat Loven to remove a blowdown on the trail near Dennis Cove. The Forest
Service people are leaning on us more and more to follow all the rules by
which they are supposed to operate. Namely protective equipment while
operating a chainsaw. To keep them from harassing Carl I promised to wear
it.

Even more exciting, Dwight's son Pat and wife Rhonda have purchased an in
ground swimming pool kit. I took Dwight to Seymour Saturday to aid and abet
in installation.

Helpers in action:

I left and went off to visit Katy's favorite Knoxville hangout.

$2 admission and no picture taking allowed but still well worth the price

Perhaps the big event for the week was Benj home for Spring break with
presentation of the knit project and the obligatory B & G event.

As the party went along, more guests appeared.

And still they came.

In other news:

Herb finished his radiation treatment series and seems to be healing.
Sis had a hand operation and is now playing the role of a one armed bandit
I suppose.
GSH got a card from "Jimmy and Betty".
