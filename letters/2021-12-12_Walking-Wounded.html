December 12, 2021 · Sunday

Walking Wounded

12 photographs·399 words

As though it is not enough what age,mileage and mother nature does to me I
have to do dumb things to aid the process.
Also made a routine visit to Dr. Sago. She zapped several around head and
shoulders. None serious.

GSH is doing well. and, in addition, the weather has been very good. I
think this was taken on Dec. 7th. Not a great number of us left who
actually remember Pearl Harbor. I do. I will be glad to recount for ant one
interested.

Japs hit Clark field about 7 hours after hitting Pearl Harbor. Jim was at
Clark field.

The home health nurse comes twice/week. The one on left is Bethany. I have
known her through church since she was knee high. Michele on the right,
newly moved here from Reno where she worked at nursing for 20 years. She
likes it here.

We got the electric fuel pump hooked up properly . We also got the
headlights rigged properly. However, it is obvious e have a problem. Know
what it is?

As all know, the Studebaker has had clutch problems all along. While David
was here, he pulled the clutch release mechanism out . I have rigged up a
test stand for it.
The next step is data collection on the vehicle/ Stay tuned.

There is a lot of fast growing. invasive Alder around here. I cut a number
of large trees and sawed the wood up firewood length a couple of years
back. It has been stacked drying since then. Wayne isists it will not burn
well at all. I tested a piece. Sure enough even with a piece of Oak under
it burning was poor.

Today, with the help of a couple of migrant WWs, we threw all the Alder in
the bush pond and loaded firewood to bring in for the next weeks supply.

Alan built a ramp for use when GSH was being trundled around in a
wheelchair.I have been debating taking it out. As can be seen she prefers
to crawl through the railing.

My little pointy pepper is doing well.

When we went down the drive today there were 8 deer in the garden spot
beside the driveway.
