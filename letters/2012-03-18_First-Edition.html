March 18, 2012 · Sunday

First Edition !

11 photographs·538 words

I went in to visit the heart Dr. He said an abnormal EKG is normal after multiple bypass surgery and to come back in one year. He did say my treadmill stress test results were exceptional.

It has been so warm and sunny I had to get out and play in the dirt (since Katy is not here to do it for me). I planted peas, onions, carrots, radishes and beets in the upper garden. Intend to hold off on potatoes for another week at least.

The regular AT work day was back to Hughes Gap. One thing was different this week. A reporter for the Kingsport paper went along. She took a lot of pictures and talked with several people. I suppose an article will appear by and by (keep an eye out Vanessa).

She was about frazzled when we got finished for the day. In the gray sweat shirt.

The ramp season is almost here. ! Where we are digging runs through a ramp patch. I collected a sack full. However they are quite small. I gave them to Dave and Carol as they were so pathetic at not having recognized as we were digging. Probably big enough to harvest in a couple more weeks.

We have about 130 miles of the AT. The Tuesday group does all the heavy work on the entire section. However, all of our part is divided out in short 5-10 mile chunks for light work, weed mowing etc. One day this week I went with Jim Foster to cut blowdowns on his section and another day I went with Dave Dunham.

This tree was near a camp site so I sawed a lot up for firewood. When Meredith gets here we will go back to dig ramps and I will saw up the rest of the log.

Not a lot of progress on the '37 this week. Too much outdoor work but I did do some metal repair work and did take the glass to glass shop to get new windows and windshied cut.
As three days of do-gooder was not enough, I took the tractor with blade to the church Friday to spred gravel and level the parking lot annex. It turned out rather well. I did not see anyone or tell anyone but several people mentioned it to me today. It did turn out well.

The peach tree is in full bloom. This year, I am going to (at least I intend to) spray it every week in hopes of harvesting a good crop. Should be about Summer Camp time.

I noticed a bulge in the Miata paing just in front of right rear wheel well. Cleaned it out to bare metal, cut and installed a patch and covered with my expensive metal epoxy. It is not noticible at all.

Romance is in the air. I think this is the house which Allison built. I have decided to name the birds doing and inspection Katy and Ross.

To cap off the week:

While we were out cavorting work resumed on the long delayed bench and table project.

I am well aware that other members of the family have had all sorts of adventures. I wait with suspended anticipation.
