Knee

0 photographs·129 words

It is not bad at all. I have damaged the Meniscus on the inside of my right
knee. Dr. said he could give me a shot which would last up to a year or he can
go in and remove the damage if pain gets to be excessive.
So, be careful with it, ease back into running, aspirin and hour before
running, plenty of stretching , avoid hills and if it recurs either the shot or
the surgery.

Better if I would run some and bike some maybe cut back to every other day.
. I plan to hit the road tomorrow and see how it goes. One day at a time.
