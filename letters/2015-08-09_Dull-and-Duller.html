August 9, 2015 · Sunday

Dull and Duller

5 photographs·493 words

While others were cavorting around the world, climbing mountains,seeing
sights and wonders, we were right here in River City plodding along.

Heavy rain predicted to Thursday so Carl moved work day to Friday. Friday
we had an appointment to take the cat to get a 1,00 mile checkup. So, I
stayed home from work so WE could take the cat to vet. I bet none of the
kids can remember me taking off from work so WE could take him/her to the dr.
It should be noted that this is the same cat which gnawed on my arm and
sent me off to the dr. not too long ago.

I made a couple of runs to Erwin. Herb continues to improve as far as I can
tell. The most exciting thing there was the Carolina reeper Peppers. I got
seeds from Ebay. Herb took to greenhouse at voc. school to sprout. He kept
some plants, I kept one, Laura has one and Linda a couple. Trying to cover
my bets. His are looking good, better than mine or Laura's. Not sure about
Linda's. I can hardly wait, better get your orders in early.

GSH has been sending New Cadet Coulson a postcard or letter from time to
time. She has a misc. collection of postage stamps of various
denominations. Most interesting watching her work through to make it come
out even all the while fussing about the price of postage. Perhaps he is
worth it.

I did get the tractor tie rod replaced. To do so, I borrowed a 3/4" NF20
tap from Pat. PBDD picked the tap up when she made the delivery run of
Dwight to Seymour Saturday. We made a run out to collect the tap taking the
dogs along to meet the new member of the family. That went OK.

I could have not got the tie rod installed without use of the tap. I do not
understand what had happened to the threads but I had to re tap several
times to free threads enough to install the new part.

Also got the Alfa put back together and moved it back into cold storage.
Like the Boll Weavel, it is still looking for a home. The shop is now all
cleaned up awaiting the next project. I think it will be the "53 Studebaker
and try to tie up some loose ends which do not suit me. Nothing real major
I hope.

I think I posted a picture of the old house Pat and Karen Loven purchased.
They have decided to tear off the tacked on additions and get Anita's buddy
Mike to do some repair work on the log structure. We went by to check their
progress. Stay tuned.

I meant to mention, after Dave and Carol moved into their new home, they
had a small chest type freezer left over. Carol offered to me and I
accepted.Any one in need/want of a small chest type freezer?
