December 23, 2012 · Sunday

Winter Time !

8 photographs·426 words

I cannot locate my copy of "Anarchist Voices". Anyone know where it is?

Anyone besides me use the "Family Tree Maker" program? I am trying to
figure how to make all my accumulated family record stuff available to
all (at least all who want it).

I made my follow up visit to the skin Dr. She told me to quit picking
at the spot on top of my head and it would heal over. Froze a few
small ones and said come in if anything pops up otherwise come back in
a year.

Larry Kidd is amazing. After 3-4 weeks in a coma and another 3-4 weeks
inert he is awake and alert. He can talk when they cap off his
ventilator and can move his head very well and his feet a bit. But,
appears to have all his marbles and able to talk and joke freely.

The big , new monitor/tv is up and running. Christopher ran it through
some sort of set-up program. Now, we get all sorts of stations both
analogue and digital but still no PBS. In addition, LLLL came for a
visit. While here we tried out the new scanner by scanning some
pictures going back to Temple Hill elementary school in the late '40s.
We/she also made a copy of all the family pictures etc. onto a new
external hard drive so I can start editing as soon as the spirit moves
me.

A fair amount of progress on the '37 this week.
Back window is installed, Temp. fuel tank installed. Body filling is
pretty much complete. Installed a battery and found not getting power
through the coil. Waiting for more expert help.

AT work was at Little Rock Knob. There is a very steep, rocky section
on the trail there. We are going to relocate the trail out and back to
by-pass that section. The skipped section is about .1 mile long, the
by-pass probably .4 miles. This week, we were roughing out the by-pass
in this case through Laurel thickets all the way. I use a chain saw
and cut all the big stuff.
The by pass rejoins the trail right at the best view site on Little
Rock Know so we cleared the area there making a nice site for hikers
to break .

We got a bit of snow making it seem like Winter and setting the stage
for Christmas. Cookies also help. After much searching, we harvested a
Charlie Brown Christmas. It is now in place but not yet properly
decorated. Maybe later today.
