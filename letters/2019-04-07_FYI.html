Must be a family record for us. Additions and/or corrections?

Aaron and Chelsea were in for a visit prior to his upcoming (this week)
deployment back to Afghanistan. I got to talk with him a bit about how
things are organized there. Aaron is just the way one would picture a
highly trained, experienced, Sergent. Wonder what the chances are that Adam
will get sent to serve with him?
It was fun getting to visit and watch Chelsea and the kids. I suspect she
would have made a formidable Sergent also had she chosen to stay in service.

It has been reported that we have a new GGC. We have not yet performed an
inspection.
We did get a pictorial report on Utah GGC at church this morning. Sort of
round about but delivered with obvious delight.

One of my 1/2 cousins died. I had got to know her well while working on
the family history project. She was 87. Several children and grandchildren
but only one son and several grandchildren and friends at the funeral. I
did get to talk with the son. He is career military and is a Sergent ,looks
and sounds the part.
I did take a picture of a picture of Gertrude and her husband made when she
was 20. He died when she was 43.

A friend gave us some old, mouldy hay which we used to spread over the
water damaged areas out front.

I got it seeded just before the rains came down.

We got to the track about every day, I have been doing 5 miles most every
day. GSH likes to stretch out.

At other times I get some work out of her.

The workers continued in the thermal storage area. Putting paneling around
all the glass appears quite tedious and labor intensive.

I took the '63 for several drive way laps. Runs very well but smokes a lot.
Got price quotes on Exhaust system and decided to try to find a complete
package to order. No luck so far.

Perhaps more exciting, we unloaded the parts Daytona from the trailer. As
near to a perfect sequential vortex as I have been caught in for quite a
long time.

Grandson Kenneth sent a file with his thesis. I have been reading and
proofreading. Thanks to the Great Courses class I took and several lessons
by Kenneth, I am able to follow and understand the thesis. But, the math is
all Greek to me.

Martha just informed me that Lil' sister is back in the confines of her own
home. But, dog is still being boarded for fear of his being a tripping
hazard.
