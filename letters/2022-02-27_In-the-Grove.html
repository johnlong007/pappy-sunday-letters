February 27, 2022 · Sunday

In the Grove

13 photographs·271 words

Or maybe a rut.

We did a wee bit of do-gooder work this week. Farmer Lisa, and her willing
workers, had not been able to get Herb's pole saw running. I was able to
accomplish that task without much trouble. We talked about some of the
things she would like to do at her home place this Summer.

Friend Don Haas had cut down a wild cherry tree which he offered free to a
good home. Two nice logs which he even loaded.

Back home, I unloaded and positioned the logs ready for the sawmill crew.

The sawmill crew and off the street spectators decided to do other things
first. Perhaps admire the Bobcat operator?

While they were pondering PBBD and I moved the last of this year's firewood
up to the station just outside the shop dorr.

We also prepared a site for slabs to be deposited.

The rains came dow. So far, so good on the retaining basin.

GSH fell and skinned a place on her forehead last week. Now, it has caused
a black eye. I have had several over the years, a first for her.

The home health nurse checked rather carefully. I think she does a really
good job.

Spring is a week closer. This week I am going to walk up and check for
signs of ramps emerging.

And some folks think she is going to school.

So far, no progress on the Imperial. However, the cat likes it.
