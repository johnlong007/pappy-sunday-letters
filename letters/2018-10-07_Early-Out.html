October 7, 2018 · Sunday

Early Out

16 photographs·458 words

I took the car in for 20K maintenance Monday. Told the service man it would
sometime hesitate before moving during routine pulling out. he brought in
service manager to schedule a machine check-up. Service manager asked me if
I was mashing brake rather than throttle as they are interconnected. After
I recovered my decorum, it occurred to me that they have not designed for
left foot braking.
GSH purchased a '55 Chrysler convertible right before I graduated in '58.
First automatic I drove. I figured I might as well do it right and learn ed
left foot braking. 60 years and have never mixed up but may happen any day
now.

After moving the '63 inside to do valve guide seals I decided might as well
give it a brake system overhaul. It still had operating brakes but front
wheel were doing all the work.

For some unknown reason, I decided to get the Bobcat out . Found out it had
a flat tire, Same one which has been leaking for a long time. I may have to
give up and buy a new tire and wheel.

We worked a lot cleaning up the brush and trees We had cut down behind the
barn. Adding to brush pond. Plan is to get the crawler out pretty soon and
level then smash again.

Concrete cracks. It seamed about a good time as any to try to seal cracks
in the landing as Winter is coming which will compound the problem. I tried
three different sealing materials. We shall see which, if any work. Never
worked before but may this time.

The truck has a load of pre-cut firewood which is free to good home.

Due to a series of events no AT work Thursday, But, we did get a free lunch
in the private park. I continue to be impressed by Anita's barn.

Friday, PBBD was over so we had another picnic.

I asked Wayne to come over and inspect the roof on the buildings that we
got repair bids to help decide to have the work done or ignore for a while
longer.
He brought a trainee.
Pretty much decided we need to press on with roof repair.

The Friday hikers came by Friday after hiking.Bob Harvey is the sole leader
since Collins is off romancing. Bod wanted to check for fruit and nuts.

The quince tree is blooming. Momma made and sold quince jelly at the Apple
festival. Now. I am told it is not a Quince tree at all.

Persimmon tree is loaded. Katy?
