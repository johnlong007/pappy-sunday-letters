June 3, 2012 · Sunday

Memorial Day

9 photographs·607 words

Seems like it was Decoration Day when I was young. Anyway, I spent a lot
of time thinking and reading this week.

I have only only known one person who died in combat. Lewis Callahan , a
cousin who was bit older than I, dropped out of school, went into the
service and was killed in Korea in 1952. I looked up his marker when we
were in Honolulu and again on the marker in South Korea last Summer.

I know many of Herb's friends died in combat, probably Dwight knew several,
how about other family members? Aaron is in the midst know.

I know little about my father's service record, more about my uncles, a bit
about Papaw Garland and nothing about J.L.Buchanan.

More about Ed than any of the others. If you have an opportunity, go online
and read about the 32nd Division in WW2 .He joined the unit in Australia
early 1943 and served with them until 1945. I heard the story of July 10th,
1944 many times.

We had a family event on Monday hosted by Wayne and his kids. It was great!

Part of what made me ponder so much was Rachel asking me questions about
how the cold war had impacted my life. If anyone is curious, I will forward
the response I sent to her. No answer yet on what grade she got.

For AT work, we went to the Over mountain shelter to relocate the outhouse
(or, as the British would say, "a loo with a view"). We had too many
willing workers so I left with Jim Foster to walk to the top of the
mountain clearing water bars. At lunch, we saw a fauna.

How does it go? A thing of beauty is a joy forever? You can decide if the
facility is a thing of beauty.

On the way home, while driving through downtown Hampton, I saw a '63
Chrysler . I stopped and talked with the owner. He has a somewhat ratty
garage but he showed me pictures of another '63 300 conv. which he claimed
to have restored. It looked like ours did while new. He said to bring ours
over and let him look it over.

Normally, I do not get into future plans. However, this week is an
exception. GSH has left me (again). Mowing and gardening are all caught up
(thanks mostly to the new mowing machine). The Blountville old tractor
owners event is in two weeks. So, I intend to work on getting old cars and
tractors running during free time until she returns. My plan is to start
with the easy ones. Toward that end, I brought THE CAR out today. Even
after sitting for at least two months, it started right up and purrs like
Allison's cat.

Come by or check in next week to see how the project is going.

While I was gone to take GSH to start the great adventure, the refurbished
dining room table moved itself into position.

Lest anyone is concerned about my diet in the absence of the cook, I am
including a picture of Sunday dinner. How could anyone ask for anything
more?

I have a picture of a box turtle guarding the blue berries and a picture of
the dog playing soccer but believe I will hold those for now.

One note, the swimming pool is open and is clear and sparkling. Feel free
to come swim.

CAUTION: I do not wash laundry (if I run out of clean socks or underwear I
will go buy more), GSH is not here. Bring your own towel or plan to air dry!
