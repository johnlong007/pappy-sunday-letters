May 1, 2022 · Sunday

Mud Wrestling

10 photographs·333 words

What could be more fun than playing in the mud. The plan is get the
retaining basin on a constant grade from the top of the dam to the bottom
of deepest spot. Toward that end I used the tractor and the scoop to move
quite a bit. The scoop sideboards work well for increased capacity but
dumping clean is an ongoing problem. Stay tuned.

Along the way I came to doubt that the tractor electrical system is
working. Brought in a couple of consultants and confirmed , sure enough,
not charging. Stay tuned.

For some time it has appeared the mower electrical is not working as
designed. The dynamic duo checked and confirmed. We all agree, we miss and
need Dwight.

My brother Herb, accumulated a lot of items including a large quantity of
concrete blocks. With farmer Lisa's permission, I collected 50 blocks.
General plan is for the foundation for permanent site for the sawmill.

Alan and Catherine have been coming over in the evening to entertain GSH
and gave her a break from me.

LLLL came to town Friday and spent the day Saturday. She is really good at
entertaining her mother. She and PBBD took the car in to Wallace Nissa for
wheel alignment. Wallace used up their third strike.

For any interested parties, it can be seen that the ramps are beginning to
turn yellow. They will start to die down and become very difficult to find
as other plants grow around.
Not a bit sure I am going to be able to make a ramp run this year. However,
Jim Foster (a distant cousin from Clearbranch and fellow trail maintainer)
deliver a mess of ramps to us.. To be consumed.

Usually one evening/week we go over to the Long residence for dinner and a
show. Usually perch on the porch a bit and look out over Blountville.
