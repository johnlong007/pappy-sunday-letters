February 11, 2018 · Sunday

Old Dogs-New Tricks

11 photographs·416 words

The new car has a lot of electronic gadgetry. I am trying to master some of
them without getting into information overload. We usually go to the track
for about an hour around noon.
One day this week when I pulled in and parked GSH said she wanted to drive
the car. She took a few laps in the parking lot. Allowed it was too
complicated with which I agreed. I told her if she had to go somewhere take
the Miata, no thinking required.

Next biggest event was my trip to cardiologist. Routine checkup. He said my
aortic stenos is noisy valve is getting noisier. I told him I have it under
control and am going to stall until they are good at inserting new valves
without surgery. He said "good plan, come back in six months". Never
mentioned pacemaker this time.

On the home front, The experts tried to install the seat covers GSH made
long ago on the seat cushions I procured long ago, It was interesting to
watch but frustrating for workers.

Frustration therapy involves apple pie.

​​Seat cover project is going to be continued.
Some have shown concern about the old folks at home scooting up and down
the marble steps. To reduce, or perhaps worsen, their concern ponder this:

​​I had not seen Anita for so long I thought she had left town, so, I
invited her over for breakfast one morning. Made pancakes. My fourth try
with each preceding try worse failure than one before. This time I got it r
ight. Also got caught up on her part of our family.

​Speaking of new tricks. Wayne told me to try soaking walnuts in hot water
for a few hours would make meat retrieval intact much better. Sure enough,
it does. We cracked a couple more gallon (of nuts)​. I still have a lot of
walnuts left to be cracked.

AT work Friday. The crew I worked with extracted and relocated a big, heavy
rock to make a wet weather creek step over. Or more correctly, one side of
a step over. We also rebuilt the incline trail coming down the hill to the
creek. It was a great day to be out and a fun group of old codgers with
whom to work.

​Today, Wayne came over, borrowed the truck to poach a load of firewood and
complained that some of the wood was not very good.

​The rains came down and the floods came up !
