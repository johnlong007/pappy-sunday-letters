May 15, 2016 · Sunday

Unto These Hills

13 photographs·404 words

The mountains sure are pretty this time of year and everywhere one looks,
there they are !

Four trips out on the AT this week. One to Erwin and one to Reservoir road.

Mundane things first. I cleaned the hydraulic cylinders real well, Cut a
thin piece of flashing for a patch, coated with JB weld and attached with
hose clamps. The cylinders are installed but not yet tested. Stay tuned.

Also installed the new starter motor for the lawn mower. A $90 miscue.
Removed the toggle switch and will use a jumper hot wire until a proper
switch is available.

Speaking of problems, I went to Erwin to aid and abet Lisa as she slides
into the routine headaches of farm maintenance. Just the usual things none
of which seem to perturb her overly . Flat tires, pressure washer will not
wash nothing unusual. I have promised Suz I will scrape her driveway. Going
to have to get on with that task. Stopped by to give Aunt Flo a sack of
ramps while there. She was properly appreciative.

At home, I intend to plant one row (only) with tomatoes and peppers. maybe
a cucumber. Laura gave me a bunch of stunted tomato plants which are in the
ground and looking sickly.

Two trips to the mountains involved ramp harvesting.

Another trip was fence building . A group of through hiker alumni
accumulate at the site we are fence building each year during trail days.
They call their group "Riff Raff". That appears a fitting title. We just
went on with our fence building.

Back home, with much difficulty we sort of got the cherry tree covered. It
still needs some finish work.

I went with Pat Loven to help on his section of the trail. It goes through
the area which was burned over in the recent forest fires. It did not
appear to have done very serious damage.

GSH's strawberries are beginning to ripen.

Stephenohad another birthday ! His family had an outing more or less in his
owner.

Also, Diane and Dwight had an event. I suppose they also had birthdays at
some time. All the activities involved eating and watching motorcycle
repair efforts. The eating was successful.

I am sure some family members are scattered hither and yon around the world
for over 80 days. Are any of them doing anything suitable for sharing with
the old folks at home?
