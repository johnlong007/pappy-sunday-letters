From: Lick, Wendy Wilkinson
To: Kingsport Community Band members
Sent: Fri, Dec 9, 2011 8:27 am
Subject: Performance December 15 and your new officers, for Kingsport Community Band

Please be there for our performance NEXT THURSDAY, December 15, at Asbury
Place's Baysmont Campus.

Time: Downbeat at 6:30 PM at the Assisted Living wing. Arrive by 6:15 for
set-up and warm-up.
Dress: Dress Christmas-y. If you don't have anything festive, you can default
to dark slacks with a light (or red or green) shirt.
Directions from Stone Drive in Kingsport (easy): Head west. Turn left onto
Netherland Inn Road. Take the first left onto Netherland Lane. Baysmont will
be on your left.
Directions from Stone Drive in Kingsport (harder): Head west. Turn left
directly onto Netherland Lane, and Baysmont will be on your right. If you reach
Netherland Inn Road, you've gone too far, and follow the "easy" directions.
Address for your GPS: 100 Netherland Lane, Kingsport, TN. Phone: 245-0360.
Other: Bring your own stand if you have one!!! Also bring your music folder!!!

Program lineup (full program for Assisted Living then again for Independents)

* It Came Upon A Midnight Clear
* A Christmas Festival
* A Holly Jolly Christmas
* Angels We Have Heard on High
* Christmas Pops Medley
* Christmas Folio-Pack (omit the last piece)
* Sleigh Ride

Other:

1.
If you plan to pay your $50 annual dues but haven't yet, please get your check
to Jim Finney (trombone) or Blick (horn). Dues are optional but encouraged if
you can afford it.

2.
Congratulations to the officers and new board member who were duly elected by a
quorum of band members at our rehearsal 12/8/2011. The current year's officers
will continue in their role for one more year:

President: Theron Blickenstaff (horn)
Vice President: Amy Crawford (oboe)
Treasurer: Jim Finney (trombone)
Secretary: Wendy Lick (horn)

Dr. Michael McDonough (trombone) will be our new Board Member and will serve a
three-year term, taking over for Frances Cottrell.

Pat Buchanan (trumpet) will continue as a Board Member for two years, and Mary
Cunningham (flute) will continue as a Board Member for one year.

Woodwinds, you are gradually ceding power to the brass. Some of you might want
to step forward to be officers next December!

3.
It's not definite until Scot says so, but we think we will not have rehearsals
for the rest of December, after next week's performance. Listen for an
announcement Thursday and/or watch your email for confirmation of that and for
the date we will start back in January.

Wendy Lick
Secretary
Kingsport Community Band
wlick@eastman.com
