Nothing Can Go Wrong !

6 photographs·510 words

Kenneth give me another tutorial on gmail and moved my mailing list. So,
here we go again !

Monday Kenneth went with me to Erwin to check on Herb and his wards. All his
wards were in school except these.

Well, I cannot get the cursor to go below the picture so guess I will press
on as is.

We also visited his corn patch while in Erwin.

Then a trip to Temple Hill to check in on the ole home place:

Finally back to Blountville to spend the day trying to determine why John's
car quit. However, if anyone would like to make a contribution to barefoot
children in Appalachia they can route to me for disposition.

No AT work Tuesday. But a door opened up to keep me from having an idle day.
The VW has a parking brake light which shows on instrument panel when the
brake is on. If you drive the car with the light on it has a maddening beep.
It went on. I spent all day Tuesday taking the interior apart then putting
back together. Turns out the switch snaps into a housing and had fallen out.
Fortunately, GSH still has nimble flying fingers.

I mention raiding Herb's corn patch. Meanwhile, back at the ranch, Laura was
having visitors to her petite patch. Ricky raccoon was enticed into
temporary confinement with marsh mellows. The next night, Pogo tried the
same menu with the same results. Both, in accordance with sytate
instruction, were "dispatched". I forgot my camera and did not get a picture
of Pogo which is unfortunate as he was full grown, at least nine pounds in
my headlights.

We went to LLLL's on Friday, Saturday I ran a weedeater. Sarah drove the
tractor and mowed with the rotary mower. GSH's step father once told me that
when she was driving if a brick wall appeared in front of her she would
drive right through it. I took a picture of Sarah on the tractor and that is
just the way she looks.

I also have pictures of Rebecca with and without her chickens and Adam
playing soccer with the dog. Oh, we took the dogs along. A real adventure
going down but it worked out very well while we were there and on the way
home. There is hope.

I have about finished Winter firewood here and have started running the weed
eater on all the steep hillside above the soccer field. I would like to
employ some appropriate size helpers for love and/or money to pick up
sticks, brush and rocks where I am mowing.

I think I will quit and see if this will go not sure what size will fly. If
anyone really wants to see pictures of the other things I mentioned let me
know.

We checked off quite a number of things which did not cause the quitting.
For a follow up I suppose anyone interested should check with John. I have
not heard.
