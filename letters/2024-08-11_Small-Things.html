Small Things

0 photographs·170 words

Momma had a lot of small things, and those are up for good homes now for
the next process round.

I'll attach a list of the subject items for consideration. There may be
other things to add; if you think of something, let me know.

Please review, and think about what you remember, and respond with a list
of the top three things you would be interested in having.
If you know of anything you gave to Momma and you want that gift back,
please respond with that information as soon as possible.
There are some things on the list that are actually a category of several
things so if you want a particular one of those say so, but if an example
of the category is what you want, then say so on that as well.

Sooner is better than later! Coming by for pickup is encouraged. If you
can't come by, then e-mail as soon as possible with as much information as
possible.

Lots of good memories.
Diane
