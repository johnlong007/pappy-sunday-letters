December 24, 2017 · Sunday

Nutcracker Suite

8 photographs·249 words

​Actually, this was not the big event for the week but I could not resist.

Of course Kenneth had the big announcement to make. He flew into TRI this
evening and stopped by on the way home. I showed him the ring I gave GSH in
June 1954. He seemed quite pleased at the apparent similarity.

A big event for me was the Winter Solstice. I can tell the days are getting
longer already.

Bob ordered a temperature, 5 remotes, measuring/recording device. Jonathan
came by and got it set up measuring 5 positions in the soon to be remodeled
pool room. This will be the "before" part. Device is on top of PC.

​​ Once more, I did not make AT work. Too much going on to make get away.

We did harvest and install the Christmas tree.

​And here it is ready for ripping and shredding !

​GSH is on the right, It is clear she is the favored person. No idea why it
is sideways.​

We went to The Barter with SS class one day to see "White Christmas". I was
lost the whole play.

Tried again to sharpen chain on saw and cut up some downed trees. Did
better sharpening but still not very good.

Stitches are to be removed on the 24th.

​Time to get ready for Christmas. A lot less noise around here than in the
long ago. Looking forward to tomorrow and rest of Christmas season and
events. (Whatever that might be.) ​
