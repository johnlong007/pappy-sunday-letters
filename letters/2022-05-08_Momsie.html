Momsie

13 photographs·375 words

I miss my Mother.
When I was growing up, someone would be standing at the door of the church
on Mother's Day giving each adult either a white flower or a red flower.
Any church do that today?

My earliest memory of my mother is sitting on the arm of her chair (Jimmy
on the other side) while she read us a Bible story. I am pretty sure Sis
got the bible story book. Halbert's ? We would clamor for our favorites,
usually blood and gore I suspect.

GSH had a TLC week. Fed her ramps and eggs one morning.

Went to the track 2 or 3 times but only one lap for her.

Strawberry shortcake while in the swing to day thanks to PBBD who also
brought us white roses.

Played in the dirt (mud) a lot this week. Used our siphon to get the
retained water quite low.

Then, the rains came down and the water camee up. Will start over tomorrow.

My tractor has three warning lights. Due to time and neglect none are
working properly. So overdue maintenance was begun.
.

Diane ordered new bulbs and Alan explained the entire project to his
Mother. She listened intently !

A & C got back in to operation on the sawmill. Sawed up a big Knotty Pine
which may be used to make Oak flooring .

Cousin Jim (and wife Jan) brought us an even bigger mess of ramps. We took
some tp Dave and Carol. Laura helped prep and eat.

We took the nipped off roots and planted above the big ramp patch. Some
future occupant should have plenty of local, organic ramps.

I now have a deer , rabbit and groundhog proof tomato spot. Laura has given
me some plants but I was afraid to plant on Sunday remembering my
Grandmother wisdom.

Everyone know what is pictured here? Anyone?

The entire family appreciates the moving pictures. I see it go on at 7 and
off at 9. Usually flip through everything first thing so see if anything
new has been posted. Thanks for posting things.
