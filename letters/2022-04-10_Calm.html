April 10, 2022 · Sunday

Calm

12 photographs·396 words

I have the negatives for all the pictures I have taken since 1955. The
actual prints are in boxes or books mostly. Some rolls of film which I
developed never got printed at all.
After starting at ECC I switched a lot of my picture taking over to
slides. All the slides have been digitized by Alison and are available to
interested parties.
Looking for cooped up work, I decided to digitize all the negatives and
have been chipping away. The pile beside the box have been processed, the
ones in the box are waiting in line.
I due time the results will also be available to interested parties.

It has been a fun project and has stirred many memories to come out of
storage..

The oter big time eater has been the drain line. High tech help showed up
and looked around with a tiny camera. The conclusion seems to be there is a
dry well under the walk and it is clogged with roots.

Not to be outdone, the low tech worker drove a 4''
pipe in alongside the drain line for 3' and concluded there is something
there.

A tire went down on the Imperial.The bead was off the shoulder on the one
side. I cleaned, sealed and inflated. Found the tire had been penetrated by
a piece of metal. After a trip to town to get a plug kit I repaieed and
inflated. Found it was now leaking on the other side at the bead. With much
difficulty I got that bead off the shoulder, cleaned, sealed and inflated.

Wheel back on the car and the car moved out of the way to await its fate.
Stay tuned.

Most weeks on Friday morning, we go with Sanford for breakfast at the
Burger Bar in Elizabethton. All the Spring Chickens are his relatives
and/or high school classmates. The young man is an ex seal Sanford's
daughters significant other.

Typical lunch. For those into exotic dining.

Allison once said Max did not recognize buttercups.

I sure am glad Allison's Max let us borrow his name. It was not demanded by
our dog.

The cat misses the dog. That has been a surprise to me. I had nevver seen
that before.
