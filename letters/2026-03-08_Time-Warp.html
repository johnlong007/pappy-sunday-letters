Time Warp

11 photographs·223 words

More and more I appreciate how fortunate I am to know my GC. But, I don't
believe in luck. Statistics yes, luck no. Summer Camp was a lot of fun and
exceeded all my expectations.

It's hard to believe my youngest grandson is a parent and a homeowner.

Paul, my oldest GGC is a teen now.
Getting to meet the GGC as they come along.

Linda is a grandmother. She showed up looking for breakfast and lugging her
grandchild.

Alan has been feeding his parent(s) once a week for several years. This
week Rachel and her children were stars of the show.

We were invited to see Janet in concert.

The wildlife sanctuary has all sorts of creatures.

The regulars.

The folks at the cancer center make it as pleasant as could possibly be.
This was my first infusion nurse, June 2023; I was expected to live a few
months.

We worked on splitting the beech pieces. Another couple of nice days
should suffice. Now, next winter's firewood has started drying.

Also worked on the '37 nose cone. It's pretty much ready to install once
the engine is back in place.

One of the very few times she surprised me.
