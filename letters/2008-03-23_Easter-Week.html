March 23, 2008 · Sunday

Easter Week

9 photographs·139 words

A little bit of a number of things:
It was cold and windy on the mountain Tuesday. But, that inspires the
workers to work.
The trail maintaining crew has two 40" mowers. They are in good shape but
not the correct tool for our work. We are getting them prepared for sell.
Kenneth and John play for Central.
At long last the big bare created by burning a mountain of piled up logs has
been prepared for seeding. Perhaps even seeded.
In the meantime, floor work was continuing inside on the floors.
Barn raising did not go very well due to high wind. However, eating (ribs)
and parachute playing exceeded expectations.
While taking pictures of the flowers around this place, a wooded egg was
found.
