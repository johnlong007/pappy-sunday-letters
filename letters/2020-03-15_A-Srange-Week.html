March 15, 2020 · Sunday

A Srange Week

13 photographs·345 words

GSH had a birthday this week. She is now the same age as I. For a variety
of perplexing reasons, there was no single family party.

But, we went out to lunch on her birthday. No cake.

Anita had us over for supper with cake and fine dining.

Diane had us for supper the next night, more cake, more fine dining.

Laura had us over tonight for Sunday night at the movies PLUS more cake,
more fine dining.

No progress on cars or tractors this week. However, the scatter brush has
mostly been moved to the brush pond to await gradual return to mother
earth. GSH keeps wanting to burn brush pond and I keep refusing.

But a lot of time and effort into removing the root ball from the big Oak
tree. While the tree was on our property, it fell across the fence onto the
neighbors property.
I need/hope to get rid of the root ball in order to drag it the trunk back
onto our side. The scattered chunks each represent an hour or more of
hammer and wedges.

Fortunately, an occasional willing worker comes by. Last time this one came
by he went back to West Point with an M-1 thumb. This time he will have an
impressive hand covered with ruptured blisters.

Another willing worker. This one with direct supervision.
Perhaps Gwendolyn can critique his form?

When all else fails.

I also worked on jacking the trunk up to facilitate sewing and dragging
the trunk project. A friend with a 26 inch saw has promised to come help.
We shall see.

We were getting a bit concerned about the snake due to no obvious source of
food and water. So, Wayne did a catch and release. Snake was last seen
heading under the smoke house. Hope he gets on with groundhogs.

The ramp crop is looking good. They are spreading around on the hill.
