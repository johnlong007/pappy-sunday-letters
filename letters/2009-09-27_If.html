September 27, 2009 · Sunday

If

10 photographs·402 words

It has been a year since Hal had his seizure and brain tumor removal. He is
in between treatment and is feeling the best he has since this all
started. But, he goes in tomorrow to have a port installed getting ready for the
next series.
Dave and Pat Edwards are back home after about 12,000 miles on the road.
Lifestyle of the R & F. I expect I will make a run over their way before too
long.


Low for the week. We had downpours of rain and have a leak in the roof.
Contractor came and looked. Promised to be back tomorrow and said he will find
and fix.

In a frenzie of locking the barn door, I installed brackets on the trailer
fenders. This time I do not believe they will fall off.

On a happier note, I retrieved the Studebaker engine and have it ready to
install. Also, I went to see JW and was able to get most (but not all) of
the missing parts.

Meredith had a birthday. But, she was too busy studying to join in the
party.

The Friday hike was in Warriors Path Park. We hiked over 8 miles on the
bike trails.

Fall Creek runs through the park. Turns out there are falls but not very
impressive.

I think the Friday hikers are falling apart. Turns out most of the group
had gone to Grayson Highlands without bothering to tell Collins.

Friday was homecoming night for UCHS. We went over to watch the parade. I
was quite impressed bytheir AF ROTC unit. Bigger than their band and looked
like mostly girls to me. Herb says it is a very good pogram and gives a
jump start for people going into college ROTC or into the AF. Erwin girls are
not dumb.

Kaylee and the sheriff brought up the end of the parade.

One of my AT work buddies gave me some strawberry plants.

Wayne brought his kids over Sunday PM. The pool water is still at 84 and
the water still clean and clear. D & D , back from hobnobbing with R & F,
came by also. Dwight checked the poo and pronounced it perfect. He has even
stopped the leaks in the plumbing. Then he fiddled around with the
Studebaker. For the week, in addition to rounding up the parts I did get the body
securely bolted to the chassis.
