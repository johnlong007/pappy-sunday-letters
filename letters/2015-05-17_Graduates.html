May 17, 2015 · Sunday

Graduates

10 photographs·269 words

While some were covering themselves with honor and their parents were
basking in reflected glory we were muddling on.

It is clear I assign no $ value to my time. The 885 tractor tires came full
of salt water. Over the years some leaks out through the valve cores and
causes the wheel to rust away. I spent all day Monday breaking down the
rusted front wheel and all day Tuesday rebuilding it. Worked pretty good
except the tube I installed leaks and the tire went flat . Fortunately the
front wheels on the MM are identical so tractor is back in operation.

As some may know, the ramp season is upon us. I hope all have their fill
as the season is about over.

Fortunately, the ramp season overlaps the strawberry season.

My other brother Jimmy came by. He had an engine noise and wanted my
advice. I should have sent him to Rachel.

We went over to Erwin to deliver ramps and check on Herb and his farm
issues. A tree is leaning over his goat house is problem one.

Problem 2 is aged hoses on the lift cylinder of his backhoe. I hope Dale
will be able to advise as to replacement.

For several weeks our AT crew has been preparing for two big work days at
Doll Flats. Today was day one with 120 or so hikers recruited . It went
really well. Every thing planned for today was accomplished. Including
feeding them Supper. GSH helped by making four pans of brownies.

Tomorrow we report for work at 6:30. Time to call it a day.
