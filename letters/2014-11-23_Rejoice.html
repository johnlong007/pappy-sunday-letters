November 23, 2014 · Sunday

Rejoice

8 photographs·376 words

It is reported that the homeless little ole lady has procured lodging ! Big
relocation planned for early Jan. Stay tuned.

The HB02 treatment on Herb's face is making progress in helping healing.
Slow progress. Looks like another 20 or so sessions.

For my part I picked the coldest day of the year to replace the water pump
on the tractor. Fortunately I was able to capture a willing worker to help
with reassembly of the big heavy parts. I filled with anti freeze and got
it running with no sign of leaks. This week, I intend to get back to wood
splitting.

For the first time I can remember, it was cold enough one day to stop me
from running around. But, only for one day.

However, the cold did keep me indoor and working on the LBT. I spent an
awful lot of time getting the door and window latches operational. Believe
all is functional now. I want to get it running and put it away to await
painting next Summer.

It was cold when we back to do AT work. We are trying to move the trail up
on the rock face about four feet or so above the water level. Most of the
crew gathered rocks and locust logs to build up the trail base. I chipped
away on the rock face. I am willing to wager my part will be there when the
rocks and logs go on down the river. Anyway, this project is going to last
all Winter.

Laura told me the cat will find the most comfortable bed in the house.

After lunch with the good ole boys Wed. I brought lunch home to GSH.

Saturday I went with Dwight to Seymour to check on Pat and the race car.
They are having a BIG chevy 675 cid with expected over 1,200 HP engine
built. They have a dummy installed in the car for fitting. Note the
carburetors. Two very large 4 barrel carbs each sawed in have and moved
about an inch back between the halves. A plate on the sawed surface to seal
it.

There was a lot of discussion, speculating and calculator inputting
concerning transmission gearing and differential gearing. Big shakedown
should be about March.
