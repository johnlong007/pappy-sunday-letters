Shock and Awe

13 photographs·367 words

When I was in high school sometimes I would walk to town at lunch with
friends. Wwe would often go the lunch counter at the drugstore (It is still
in operation !) where I would get a real, tasty hamburger for 35 cents.
For scale, I was getting paid $1/hour for farm work.

This week at the Long's for dinner and a show night, Joe showed a picture
of a $30 hamburger which Maria had purchased. I suppose she would send a
picture upon request.

I was able to get the shift into neutral on the '63 and got the car
running. Could not get it in gear so I shut down .put in gear and hit
starter. Should have had shop door open, almost ran through it. Clutch is
still stuck, working on Plan B.

Not yet able to get '55 running. still no spark at the plugs and no fuel at
the carbs. Electric fuel pump installed but not yet operating.

On a brighter side ! When we let A & C take the LBC ; so many know and,
love the condition was keep it running forever.

A & C come over in the evening for a dinner and movie time.

It looks like the '37 is going to have the workshop tied up for a spell. To
have a secure spot for the '55, a metal carport has been procured and
erection has been started.

The deer do like the apples. Several times/ pay they come by to check.

PBBD also likes the apples, she picked three buckets full. Both the apples
and apple pie which followed have disappeared.

Charlotte and Fish came by.

I did quite a bit of earth shuffling. I need a method to spread the dirt
evenly. Do want to get it seeded with 2-3 weeks.

The '37 engine is being extracted for an overhaul. Easier and better to
remove the radiator rather than risk damage.

Farmer Lisa also has a wildlife refuge. Not only deer to eat the apples but
also bears.
