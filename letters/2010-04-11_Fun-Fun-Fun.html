April 11, 2010 · Sunday

Fun, Fun, Fun

10 photographs·551 words

Behold ! Paint, a silk purse in the making. Dwight painted the area,
well, most of the areas, where seals, gaskets and glass are to be installed. We
have about decided to do all that work before the final overall paint job.
It will require masking this route but avoids scratching the final paint
while installing widows etc.

Trail work was fun. Hiked 10 miles clearing blowdows. I made it ok but age
and mileage are beginning to show or, at least be felt.

We removed one which was perhaps the trickiest I have had to deal with. The
top of a very large, very high Oak tree had broken clean. It fell down and
stood upside down over the trail supported by three branches, like a
tripod. The broken, upside down piece was perhaps 25 feet high and about 20
inches at the thick part.
Fortunately, there was a large, maybe 48 inch Oak close to the trail. We
cleared all the brush from under the inverted blowdown, tied a rope as high
up as we could get it, stood behind the large Oak and pulled the upside down
tree over.
It skinned down decide the tree we were hunkering behind and hit with a
great thump.

We went to visit the Edwards in Raleigh Wed-Sat. Also got to see Beth. The
time was spent helping Dave work on the '65 Plymouth, visiting cousin
Joseph Leonard Davis and going to the Charlotte auto event.

Progress was made on the Plymouth. It was running really poor when I got
there. By doing a lot of testing, adjusting, conferring with Dwight and
making test runs, we got it about 90% OK. It still wants to lurch during severe
acceleration.

About a year or os back, Sis and I went to visit our cousin. The son of one
of Daddy's 1/2 sisters. He went into infantry at age 16 in 1938, switched
to Airborne after WW@ and served there through Korea and Vietnam retiring
as a Sergeant Major.
It was a delight to talk with him. Neither his daughter nor D-I-L were
there this time so there were no distractions.

This is a copy of a printer picture he had in his room. I will ask his
daughter to get a good copy for me.

Then we went to the flee market.
Aaron, can you identify this car? Hint, I know where you can find one just
like it.

Can you see the asking price on this bellhousing? $800, I have one like it
out here in the shop (plus a bunch more).
Asking price on the long rams was $4,000.

For human interest, here is Dave taking it all in. Actually, he spent quite
a bit of money. The box under his arm is a lighted, fluorescent clock
"Mopar" for his hobby room. I kept trying to remember the name of the song it
about the woman who was going to convert house into a bar.

Back home, peach, Redbud and cherry trees are in full bloom. Designing,
fabricating and installing a cherry tree cover must be moved up on the list.

Wisconsin persons. Moving Summer Camp is not looking good. All have been
surveyed. All want you here but complications arise in any direction. try
threats and bribery on your director.
