The property is great. It will be a great place to raise little willing

​Top of the ridge.

On the home front, Diane and GSH made progress on the '63. Started the

​I made progress on passenger side of the '49 but nothing worth a picture.

Well worth a picture, the AT crew assembled a bridge over the less than
troubled water at Shook branch. A fine crew 19 of the workers turned out
and all worked together. A fun crew, Everyone just does what they see that
needs to be done and fits their skills.
The trail crosses the bridge the goes up a steep , sharp drop off hill. I
and some others worked on building steps up the hill.

​
Two trips to Dr.s this week. One for a follow up MRI, another for a visit
to neurologist. A return visit for testing is scheduled this week. Stay
tuned.
