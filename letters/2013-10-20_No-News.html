October 20, 2013 · Sunday

No News

8 photographs·412 words

Well, not quite no news.

I went back to dermatologist to get my head examined. She said "I believe
we got it this time!". I have had a multitude of skin cancers (not as many
as Herb). Probably this one was the most serious I have had, at least it
seemed that way to me.

Dwight came over to aid in hooking up the connecting rods on the MM.

In addition to being able to examine the innards of the MM it can be seen
that the top of my head where she worked is as normal as it gets.

We did run into a snag on the MM. The exhaust valves are a bit too long.
Part of an exercise called "making do and getting by". I am confident the
issue will be resolved with no further capital expenditure this week.

On the domestic front. GSH continues to work on domesticating the cat. She
saw it out on the dam this week and went out to commune . She soon returned
to tell me he had a baby chipmunk and was playing with it. Ha !

Speaking of GSH, she had one eye renovated this week. As far as I can tell
the procedure went ok. The other eye is to be repaired in a like manner in
2-3 weeks I believe.

As you can see, I have continued to wait on her hand and foot which now
includes eye drops four timed/day.

Turkeys, deer and Gwendolyn have cleaned out the chestnuts. The garden
still has potatoes, tomatoes, carrots, beets, turnips and sweet potatoes.
Apples are all harvested but a lot of pears are left on the tree. The deer
get 'em as soon as they fall.

It seems a number of family members were directly are indirectly involved
in football this weekend. I suppose all are pretty well satisfied except
any Clemson supporters.

Friday evening I went with George and Sanford to Onida for a funeral. The
mother of a friend. The deceased lady was 94. Her husband was there for
the service. He is 97. Twenty years older than I am and still going strong.

On the way down, I took some pictures for the benefit of those who may not
remember what the mountains look like.
