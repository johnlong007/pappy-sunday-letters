January 31, 2010 · Sunday

Snow News

6 photographs·222 words

A car report. I took the windows in need of replacement to the local glass
shop (just done the hill from the Longs). No word yet on when they will be
ready; In the meantime, we took the heavy duty sewing machine to the
repairman. It is now back, material has been purchased and production will
started any time now. Stay tuned.
The Instrument panel is in and connected. The heater is in but not
connected although the firewall holes have been cut. I sorted through all the
chrome. As near as I can tell, two pieces are missing. I called JW and asked him
to hunt as they will be very difficult (and expensive) to replace. I am
not optimistic.

A before and after picture for trail work. We worked above Temple Hill on a
place called "No Business". It was cold but we got another 4 miles of
trail cleared of blowdowns.

Dave has been having problems so we did not do church do-ggoder work. But,
GSH went with me to cut up a downed Bradford Pear tree for a friend. I
guess that counts.

GSH went by to check on Hal and Sue. They plan to continue Chemo a couple
more weeks, do another MRI and decide what, if anything, to do next.
