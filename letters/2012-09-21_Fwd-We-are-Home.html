Dear Bikers from the Main Tour of August 26-September 7.
It was such a joy to meet each one of you. That's why it was so
disappointing that we missed out on the rest of the ride. However, we are
so very thankful that Rex is on the mend. Our insurance company sent a
male nurse to accompany him on the flights home. We all arrived safely
last night, Thursday, September 20.

A big lesson during this frightening and painful experience is this: When
troubles come, angels appear on the pathway to support. Wonderful people
at the hospital and the hotel were so very kind and went out of their way
to help. We had to wait a week after Rex was discharged from the
hospital. He was instructed to rest and come back the following Friday for
another scan. That one was very encouraging; healing was happening. Then
we waited for the "fit to fly" form and did the paper chase to obtain new
airline tickets home.

Each day we went out for short walks around Schweinfurt. Sometimes we rode
a round trip on a city bus; cheap way to see the city. One day we even
took the train for a 30 minute ride to Kitzigen. Just had to see what you
saw. Around the corner from the hotel is a huge bookstore with an
extensive English section. So finding good books, playing cards, and
exploring new eating places helped. One evening we took a taxi to a large
biergarten where a German band was playing American country music. No beer
allowed for Rex but we laughed, sang along and enjoyed seeing whole
families with kids dancing. Another evening we walked to a protestant
church to hear an organ and flute concert. We especially enjoyed the
young child who tried to direct the performers.

Thank you for your card messages and friendship. Happy Trails to all of
you.
Arlene Raudenbush
