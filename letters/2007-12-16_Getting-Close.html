December 16, 2007 · Sunday

Getting Close

5 photographs·224 words

Tuesday we worked on a bridge on the AT. We cut a locust which was about 200
feet up the hill and dragged it down to site. Here it is being manhandled
into position. It will take several more trips to have a completed bridge.

Puppy Dog at work.

Turning the "pond" into a soccer field has started. This week, Dwight
decided to keep both feet on the ground, confining himself to helpful advice. Such
as,
keep it in gear while going down steep hills" and "always fasten your seat
belt".

Community band gave a public program at the mall this PM. Notice little
white haired lady on back row.

The Christmas program at the Blountville prbys church had only ONE of the J
& A Long's on stage. I missed Katy.

We also went to a very good program at First Baptist in KPT. Laura and
Allison both played and played well. I was up in the balcony and did not take a
picture.

I did get to Erwin to check on both Herb and Aunt G. Herb and Tootsie were
doing fine. Aunt G was a bit down. She said she was scheduled for another three
weeks there. It would be good to try to visit or send a card or both.

A pretty good week.
