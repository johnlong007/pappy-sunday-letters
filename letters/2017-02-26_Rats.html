February 26, 2017 · Sunday

Rats !

9 photographs·318 words

My contacts whit the medical world have gone up exponentially in the last
few years. Concurrently, my confidence has evolved into an inverse
relationship.

Tuesday I took GSH in for a stress test (an official Bruce Protocol stress
test, not the kind I administer daily). It went well. Wed. some person
called, said the equipment had malfunctioned and test must be repeated. We
went back in at Thursday noon, back through the same 23 pages of consent
and understanding forms. Once more, the actual treadmill part went fine.
But, have to wait for the Dr. to actually explain. Union rules you know.
Stay tuned.

The week did have some fun parts. Beth and her brood were at home for the
week and came by several times.

​The weather was great all week.

​I got the driver door on the '49 more or less completed. Did more work on
the floor pans and am ready to move to the passenger side.

​The regular AT work day was Thursday but stress test took priority. So,
Friday we went with Kim and Pat Loven to remove the blowdown I noted last
week. About a 6-1/2 mile in and out hike. But, time well spent as hikers
are coming out in numbers and the trail was seriously blocked.

​On the way out we met a couple of hikers eating lunch at the shelter. The
lady shared gingerbread muffins with us.

​Also on the way out GSH stubed her toe and did a nose dive. So, for bloody
picture for the week :

​
Friday night was the annual dinner meeting for the actual working
maintainers. I was asked about the various grandchildren who have gone out
with us over the years.

I did get my drag bottom plows out and set up for garden plowing. Just
cannot decide that I really want to plant a garden. Where has all the fun
gone?
