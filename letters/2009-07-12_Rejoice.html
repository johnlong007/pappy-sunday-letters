July 12, 2009 · Sunday

Rejoice !

3 photographs·295 words

I was mistaken! The momma turkey and both (frying size) turklets have been
spotted several times recently.

Summer camp has come on gone. The pertinent data:

!. I had to tell, one person, one time, to remove elbow from table.
2. On three occasion( two people), I detected, and called attention to, a
superfluous "like".
3. On one occasion a person tried (without success) to leave a (small)
quantify of food on a plate.
4. I had to tell two persons, two times each, to close mouth while chewing.

Not bad for 21 kids for a five days.

I slipped off to see the Dr. on Monday to get stitches removed from arm. It
was a squeamish(sp?) cell carcinoma. I will go back next week to find out
if he thinks it is all removed.

On the way home;
I checked in on Hal and Sue. He is about 1/2 way through the current chemo
cycle and is getting pretty discouraged." Frazzled all the time."

I have two brothers and one sister. My brother Jimmy has three children,
Dan, Ellen and David. Jimmy goes to church with us at CHCC. I talked with him
this morning before SS.

Ellen is getting married next Sunday. The person she is going to marry has
three grown children and a former wife.They are buying a house half way
between their respective workplaces in a suburb of Chicago. Jimmy is not going
to the wedding. He smelled as though he had been smoking.

Today I timed my running for one mile, 12 minutes. No problem at all. I go
back to St. Thomas in about 6 weeks. I intend to be down to nine minutes by
then.
