Once More With Feeling

9 photographs·318 words

I expect all have heard about the flooding in the lake district. Here is a
picture showing the Cocker river going through Cockermouth. The house with
green shutters is the one where we visited while doing the walk across
England and whre Bill Murdoch lived while we were over there. Several of you
have been there.

I visited Hal and Sue this week. Things are pretty much unchanged which is
about as good as one can ask for.

My running went a bit better than last week but still not as good as a
while back. Alas.

This is last weeks do-gooder helper in action. We were asked to cut up a
tree which had broke and fell down. Turns out it had three trunks. But, we
got it.

When we go to the track to run, the dogs like to go along. As can be seen,
there are not adverse to togetherness. They get along better than most
sibblings I do believe. Maybe it is age and maturity.

In an effort to make sure the barn door is securely locked and to help
provide for domestic tranquility, I put water seal on both chimneys. It only
took a couple of hours. I had been stalling for a long time. Next Summer, I
am going to put on Summer Camp list.
