March 28, 2010 · Sunday

Sloooooooooow

8 photographs·429 words

My do-gooder buddy Dave has almost dropped off the radar due to health
issues and church issues. I told him that if he wants to start a new helping
hands wherever he light I will be glad to work along as before.
No AT work and no Friday hike for various conflict of interest reasons. I
like life to be somewhat routine.

On a more cheerful note, no trips to the medical world this week, at least
not for us. Furthermore, Friday I got back to sewing:

and Saturday got serious:

Saturday was a fun outing. A person with whom we used to go to church had a
big Oak fall across his back yard. It is about 100 yards from where Wayne
lives as the child walks and carries a load of wood. further by truck.
Wayne has sawed a bunch, we sawed more. Now all that is left is splitting and
retrieving. Perfect work for releasing frustration and avoiding the Devil's
workshop.

Aunt G was buried Monday. Flo is the last female left, Jim the last man.
It put me to thinking I had better get moving on filling in some gaps on the
story I collected from Jim about his years in The guerrillas of Mindanao.
Flo said he has lost some of his speaking ability so I am preparing a list
of questions to send in hopes of written responses. We shall see. I re-read
several books about the Pacific ww2 operations covering both Edd's and
Jim's service. Both are real heroes in my opinion. One always wonders what
would I have done had I been in those shoes. I am sure Herb knows better than I.

Flo has a housefull of family artifacts with only two grandchildren, poor
thing. I took a picture of a plate Aunt Mae painted.

Perhaps the most exciting news of all is that I went back to my favorite
junk yard. I asked about the Spitfire. It has been left there for years but
is not his. He is not sure what the intention of the owner is. But, he said
I can send Charlie over to look, talk and lust.

Even more exciting I traded the parts '53 Plymouth for the missing pieces
on the Studebaker. The trunk latch JW lost and the tail light lens unnamed
children broke. I dragged the car out front yesterday. He promised to come
get it this week.

See if you can identify the further signs of spring right here in River
City. Some in the woods and some in the garden
