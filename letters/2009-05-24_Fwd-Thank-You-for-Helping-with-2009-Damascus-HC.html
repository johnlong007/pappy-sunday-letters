Fwd: Thank You for Helping with 2009 Damascus HC

0 photographs·355 words

Thank you for making Hard Core successful again this year. This is
the largest maintenance program with hikers on the AT. It could not
happen without you! Overall, it was very successful on several
fronts:
a.. Hikers and club members went away smiling and friends
b.. 4500 feet of new trail was opened with half being north of
Wilbur Dam Rd on Sunday and other half between Grassy Ridge and Stan
Murray Shelter on Monday
c.. There were no serious injuries
d.. Many hikers had their first exposure to trail maintenance and
some indicated they will be looking for more opportunities
The most trail we ever dug with Hard Core previously was 4000 feet and
this year was overall the most difficult we have ever built with this
group.

Without you Club Members and Guests, acting as trail leaders and as
critical support, this event would crumble rapidly. Bob does a good
job in limiting participation to about 50 new hikers and 50 returning
hikers, otherwise we could probably recruit 200 if we could handle
them. You 29 contributed about 500 hours to the total approximate
2300 hours. 110 hikers participated. We picked up some hikers on
Monday that arrived on their thru hike at Kincora on Sunday evening.
