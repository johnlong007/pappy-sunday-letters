August 16, 2009 · Sunday

Better Late

10 photographs·317 words

Hiking club members sign up to "maintain" sections of the AT. They do light
work such as mowing and trash collecting. Monday, GSH and I went with Dave
Dunham and Carol to do routine maintenance on their section. A double
header as Monday is the normal do-gooder day. They have the section around the
end of Watuga Lake so trash is a serious problem. We collected several
large bags full of trash. A lot of Miller Lite bottles.

Plenty of work for GSH this week. I got the BobCat stuck and had to be
extracted. It was a frustrating week as far as lawn mowing went. Both the rider
and push mower are out of order.

Aunt G had her 91 birthday. She is in the facility in Erwin. Still alert
and mobile.

The Tuesday AT work day was on Thursday. It was a long hard, hot day. I
made it very well but at a slow speed.

The critters are working on the tomatoes so I decided it was time to take
serious action. The ground hog weighed 14 pounds . I turned it loose to
visit with friends and family previously released on parole on a nearby farm
Did not weigh the possum.

Saturday was the requisite trip to Seymour to admire the race car. The
data collection system was installed and confirmed to be working, at least
partially. Field testing is planed for Friday night. However, since the
transmission came back with us, running Friday does not seam to be a sure thing.

B & L had a family event to make due note of eldest son returning to
school.A fine ,well fed, time was had by all (or at least by me).

The week was wrapped up by a trip to the local ball park for a Sunday night
double header. A trip to the ball park apparently requires an on site
meal.
