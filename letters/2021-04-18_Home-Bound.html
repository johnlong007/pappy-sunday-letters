April 18, 2021 · Sunday

Home Bound

14 photographs·489 words

Wilburn Bowman died just a few weeks back. Her brother, JR died this week
with the funeral today. Their mother Prude Tipton, was the daughter of one
of my fathers 1/2 sisters. Celia Buchanan. My father lived with Celia when
he came across the mountain looking for work after being discharged in
1927. The year JR was born. Prude's husband was Ray Bowman. His mother was
Hannah a sister to Mommy's mother. Got that?

Daddy said they wanted him to be Ray Jr. but wrote "Junior Ray? hence the
JR Bowman. Like all the Bowman's, he was a good man. Hard as a rock but a
good man.

This week I confirmed Allison was correct about another of her admonitions.
don't use a toothpick on a temporary crown. I am trying to hold out until
she comes to town but not sure my substitute dental assistant will let me
stall much longer.

While I was adding food to the woodpecker feeding post, a little furry
fledgling came up and starter pecking away right in front of my nose.

We are having a lot of customers.

However, they do not always confine themselves to the feeding post. here,
WW is filling woodpecker holes in the siding.

Shop organizing work continues. Faster on bad weather days. We have
procured containers of various sizes and shapes. Getting rid of the milk
jugs. I suppose sometime in the future this work will be appreciated.

Work continued on preparing the Imperial for relocation. Below is my tire
changing station along with my supervisor.. I think I have 3 tires/wheels
ready now.

The car is all blocked up and brakes on all four wheels have been freed.
Drum brakes always rust tight when a car is parked for years.

The replacement for the broken steering knuckle arrived and is installed.
During reassembly, it was found that the steering rod end was damaged when
the knuckle failed. A replacement part is on order. PBBD is busy learning
all sorts of new, mechanical engineering skills.

from time to time I get called into service to work on a gas powered lawn
mower . I now have a fiendishly clever plan. I have a couple of push, reel
type mower. Getting one ready for service which will be available as a
loaner. More ME skill building.

Fiday, the signs were right for a scouting out ramp run. The white shirt is
the back of a ramp field inspector.

We only harvested a few with intent to return in 2-3 weeks. But, the few
were put to good use. I believe these were even better than in fried
potatoes.Scrambled eggs added a bit later.

The steps on the floor level change have been a growing concern.

But no longer a concern.
