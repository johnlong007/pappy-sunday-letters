Re: wardrobe

0 photographs·134 words

Their bedroom did not have a closet. The one Jimmy and Sis slept in had the
only closet in the house. Perhaps it was intended to be the "master"
bedroom.
Somewhere along the way he built that closet. It sat just inside their
bedroom on the left.

Daddy smoked until he was about 50. I always gave him a can of Prince
Albert for Christmas. It came in cans about the size of a coffee can.

He smoked Camels before filters came in. When he decided to quit, he took
a partial pack out of pocket and lay it up on the shelf in that closet. It
was still there when Momma died. To my knowledge, he never told anyone he
was quitting. I suppose Momma knew but no one else. He just quit.
