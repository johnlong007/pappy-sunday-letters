January 4, 2015 · Sunday

First Things First

9 photographs·502 words

I heard not a word from my Lil' sister this week

I did hear from Herb "sicker than a dog". Do not believe it was related to
face issues. Tomorrow we are going to Vanderbilt to get their perspective
on things.

As almost the whole world knows by now, when all the family was here the
septic system failed. Fortunately, we had some world class experts to
diagnose the problem. Unfortunately as it turned out later, they were all
wrong.

Wayne contacted Joe the tank pumper to come out at 8:30 Wed. morning.

So, Monday I worked on the LBT passenger door.

Tuesday I went out to do AT work. Same place, same activity, for me that
meant break big chunks of rock loose from the rock face and call in the
young (relatively speaking) guns to ease the chunks down into position in
the trail. The last time I tried to control a rock descending I ended up
with a broken toe. Not a good move. The toe no longer will bend and still
causes foot pain. Hence the locking the barn door caution.

Today, I went with Kenneth, Anita and GSH to survey the work site. we also
walked on up the river a short distance. Notice no ice or snow.

I took a picture of the work site as we left and promised to send an update
to Kenneth from the same spot after next work outing.

Back to the septic system. Joe the pumper showed up but we could not locate
the tank so he left. GSH then launched and intensive search effort and
found the system sketch. He had been looking within a foot of the location
but I threw him off by saying I thought it was elsewhere. Anyway, after he
left, Wayne and I dug around and found the discharge line.
Spent New Years day digging in the mud, blood and gore (at least it smelled
that way) across the front yard.Ditto Thursday and Friday. Determined the
discharge line was not blocked over to edge of driveway. Saturday rented a
rotor rooter and determined the line was not blocked all the way to the
flexible line which I assume is connected to field bed.

Unfortunately, that is at an unknown depth and location so have to figure a
way to locate where to dig to determine source of problem. Stay tuned for
next chapter of this exciting mystery story.

It does fit in with my off stated dictum of "I do not do anything I don't
want to do". Does everyone see how? Anyone?

For the first time I can remember, I did not sort out , at least in my
head, my objectives for the coming year. Amazing how blocked plumbing will
distract a person. I will try to get that done soon as I believe it helps
get there if one knows where one is trying to go.

Guess what the little dog was doing.

Anyone else have near as much fun? Anywhere?
