September 8, 2013 · Sunday

Hear Ye, Hear Ye

11 photographs·501 words

At the start of the year, I had several things on the list for '13. Now, I
have worked it down to only one biggy remaining. This week I got fitted
with hearing aids. So far, it is a mixed blessing. I can hear everything.

One of the things I could hear on the way home from Costco was the brake
dragging on the VW.

While we were in Knoxville, we stopped by to see Sue Tabor. She is a very
nice assisted living facility. Mary Alice stops by several times/week and
Nancy takes her to church on Sunday. When we went in, she was sitting in a
rocking chair going through a book of old pictures.
We got her to talk through all of them. I took a couple and took a picture
of the picture.

Made in 51 one when George was stationed in Alabama.

AT work was at the Overmountain shelter. While most of the crew was working
on the shelter (The red barn) I went on to the top of Yellow Mountain to
clean water bars. It was foggy, windy and chilly but quite pretty.

Saturday I went with Karen Loven to watch Pat do cowboy action shooting. It
was great. Aaron would love it, maybe Jake also. Ross ? Benjamin and Julie?
If you have not watched on u-tube, do so.

There were about 20 participants. Each fired 5 rounds from each
six-shooter, 10 from a rifle ant four from a shotgun at each of five
stations. It is all about speed with accuracy being a minor element.

It seems apparent that this is a sport for participants not spectators.

Saturday night Anita and Joe fed us Brots roasted on an open fire. It was
the first real test of my hearing. I could hear Anita !

We have had a fine year for fruit. (but it looks like no persimmons).
Today, Laura came over and we squeezed a couple of batches of apples. It
reminded me of the moonshiners "shall we drink it now or let it age until
after supper".

Still do not have the crawler running. Maybe this week. But, got the '37
out and it fired right up. Ran rough but after a few minutes it smoothed
right out. I have heard that Meredith wants a small pickup truck with
manual transmission. Meredith, come get it !

Thanksgiving.

Listening to the discussion about children coming in from school hither and
yon, I am inclined to think it would be better to have a family event in
the Blountville area on Friday or Saturday. Linda, check that out please.

I did get news from some (but not all) far away places with strange
sounding names. Some included pictures. Thanks.
