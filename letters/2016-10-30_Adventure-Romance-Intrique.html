October 30, 2016 · Sunday

Adventure-Romance-Intrique

14 photographs·265 words

Were totally missing around here this week.

The overall plan for the week was to get all the operating equipment into
dry storage. With the aid and assistance of GSH the plan was executed.
Somewhat to my surprise we ended up with enough room for one more small
tractor, maybe 8N size. I may decide to reclaim it.

I skipped Thursday AT work but we went with the Friday hikers to walk on
the Tweetsie Trail in Johnson city. A nice outing on a perfect day. I
checked GSH's Fitbit before we came home. Over 17,000 steps. But , when we
started to sync it came up zero. I worked and fumed but could never
correct or figure out what went wrong.

We also made a run to Erwin to check on Aunt Flo and another run to check
on Lisa's gate. All are still standing.

Mike and Anita are renovating a log cabin for Karen and Pat Loven. We
stopped by to check on that project. I was and am impressed.

The leaves are pretty this year. People are driving and walking for miles
to see the colors. We walked around the house today.

Bob took the vw in to get a timing belt installed along with various other
items. I hope/expect it to be good to go for another 50,000 or so now. All
I need is somewhere to go.

Seems to me that some grandchildren are a bit lax in updats, Remember, "as
you sew so shall" . Perhaps I should start trying to get
various spouses into the swing of things.
