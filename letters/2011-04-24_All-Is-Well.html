April 24, 2011 · Sunday

All Is Well

12 photographs·390 words

Sanford has one child. A 43 year old single Mom. After breakfast yesterday
he asked me if we wanted to go to Barter yesterday PM. He said he told his
daughter they were not going to dye eggs, She was crushed.
I knew just how she felt. Yesterday it appeared that we would have no
arrival of the Easter Bunny for the first time ever.But, the day was saved !!!!



Back in River City, things were pretty normal. AT work was on Roan
Mountain. We roughed out some relocations to be dug in conjunction with Damascus
Trail Days.

This time of year, The North Bounders are going through. Probably 25-30/day
I would guess. This yellow stopped and ate lunch with us. He went away
well fed.

For those who have not been to "Cloudland", the top of Roan , a typical
view.

Back on the ranch, mowing season is well underway. Once it starts there is
no relief. This week it was string trimmer, push mower, Momma's old rider
and tractor mower. I do think the Kroger dog will learn to be a tractor dog
by and by. So far, she has too much energy to be still very long.

Next year, for Christmas, I believe I should procure some woodworking
equipment for GSH+
Perhaps a wood plane and some sort of work bench with a vise built into it.
.
She is pretty good with other woodworking tools. Perhaps I can loan her out
to the Alaskan contingent?

Back on the road, Allison's fan club made a trip to Huntsville to see and
hear her (and others) in concert. They had a big picture poster in the
lobby with Allison front and center. I will insert it followed by a live and in
person picture.

Saturday, D & D were looking for skilled labor for castle wall
construction. Since they could not find any, GSH+ and were called into action.

Sunday was Easter. A really great day. I hope the extended family members
will send pictures and reports of their Easter experiences.
LLLL, Alan,Vanessa/Aaron, Charlie, Katy, Christopher, Allison.

+ GSH, a good Baptist expression for the role of the wife. Graciously
Submissive Helpmate.

If any of the girls have trouble working out the role they should contact
my GSH sister for advice.It is obvious she has trained Laura well.
