Fall Fell

10 photographs·262 words

Wiring on the trailer continued off and on this week. Tail lights, brake
lights, signal lights and brakes. I put it all together and Wayne came over
today for the testing. The brakes on the front axle work. The brakes on the
rear axle engage but their is a short. Right now my inclination is punt and
go with single axle brakes.

I mentioned last week that GSH was hobbled. I figured I had better get her
cured as that had really hurt her trade in value . Anyway, The word from
the Dr. was "If it hurts, don't do it" (or something like that. The leaves
are coming down in massive quantities. GSH had been keeping them blown out
of yard and off driveway. I went out Friday, picked up the leaf blower and
suddenly realized how she had strained her hip. The Leaf Blower ! A heavy
cantilevered load.
Now I feel much better know cause of the problem and things to avoid in
near future. It is already causing much less pain. (Still grouchy and short
fused.)

Anita had a bit of sawing she wanted done, We got there just in time to see
farmer Long make a hay delivery.

​​Access trails which connect to the AT are "blue trails". For the last 5
years we have worked on the blue trail at Backbone rock. This week, we
completed the project.
