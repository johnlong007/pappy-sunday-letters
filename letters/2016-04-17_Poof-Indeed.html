April 17, 2016 · Sunday

Poof Indeed

10 photographs·620 words

My plan for the week was get brakes on the fire engine. Well, it did not
all work out as planned. I did get all the wheel cylinders honed,
adequately I hope, also all the wheel cylinder kits and a new master
cylinder are here. So, all we need is a new plan. But, not this week . The
coming week is booked wall to wall.

Progress on the '63 was not a lot different. Sanford and I went to a parts
store in Elizabethton where I procured a selection of blind fastener clips
to determine if they will work when reinstalling the moulding. It was a fun
trip. Since attaching the moulding is the last thing to be done it may be
a bit premature to be clip hunting but the signs were right.

In more exciting news, LLLL came to town. She brought with her a new (to
me) incredible device and got all my data lists phone numbers etc
transferred to it. Now, it is to be determined whether two old dogs can
learn any new tricks or not. It is an old one of hers and already a few
generation out of date I believe. Just like us.

While she was here, she got the new, pink, Fitbit set up for me. I could
hardly wait to give it a good workout then proceeded to forget to put it on
for my running around. Maybe tomorrow.

Monday GSH and I walked out our section of the AT to remove a blowdown we
had observed last trip. It was a lovely day for a hike.

Tuesday I went out to the hillside to get Dwight for a Dr. visit. I
stopped to take a picture of the poplar tress on the back part of the
property.

I have been debating whether to pull the engine from the '63 or not. At
least the engine compartment must be cleaned and painted. I took the hood
off to better ponder. And noted the heater control valve had been removed
somewhere in antiquity. Went on ebay and found one listed for $485. Then I
dug through my inventory of parts from flea market visit at some time in
the past.

Away back in Buchanan days in Buladean, the Buchanan boys would cross the
mountains to find wives. Sometimes Davis boys would cross the mountains and
bring wives back. So, in my family record work there are lot of Davis
people. One of those I met a few years back. Jackie Davis Allen. GSH and I
visited them in their mountain (second) home a couple years back. She
writes and paints. I got a note from her about a meet the author night at
Buchanan County Library in Grundy. It was a fun evening . I bought her book
and would be happy to share with any or all.

There a lot of hikers passing through our section now. Pat Loven and I went
out with Kim to cut blowdowns on her section on Friday. We cut quite a
number. Also met a lot of hikers. Turns out the night before most of the
ones we met had stayed at the Watauga Lake shelter. The section had been
closed to overnight camping for the last two years due to aggressive bear
activity. They had encountered the aggressive bear and all wanted to tell
us about the experience. The bear got bigger and taller with each hiker.
The last one had watched it shake a pack off the bear pole. He reported it
could reach about 10 feet and weighed at least 500 pounds.

On the way out, we spotted a green field. I intend to go back in about two
weeks.
