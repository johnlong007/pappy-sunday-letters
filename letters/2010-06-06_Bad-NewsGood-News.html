June 6, 2010 · Sunday

Bad News/Good News

7 photographs·412 words

I did not take many pictures this week.

The Miata gets a new timing belt each 60,000 miles. We take it to Bill
Gatton in Bristol where it costs about $400, about equal pats and labor.
This time it was almost $700 with all the increase in Labor. It has made me
really PO'ed. They have lost a customer and made an enemy in one swoop.

The Farmall MD rear tire has had a slow leak for years. When I parked it
last year I propped it up to keep it from settling on a flat and ruining the
tire. Saturday Charlie came over to help me start getting ready for the
Blountville tractor event two weeks from now. The prop had sunk into the
ground letting the tractor weight set on the flat. The tire will not hold air.
We took it off the tractor but I cannot get it broken loose from the rim.
So, it looks as though I will have to take it to be repaired. Rats.

We did move the Mack out of the barn. As soon as I get a couple of good.
high output batteries it will be ready to start. The Farmall "H" started
right up. We did not try the MD due to the flat. We dragged the '53 Plymouth
around w/o getting it started but finally decided it has no fuel I procured 5
gallons on 100% gasoline today but did not get around to trying again.

I went to Erwin to visit Herb and one of his friends out of the past. While
there, we went down to check on the Studebaker paint job. It has some runs
and will be in the shop a few more days.

I picked about 1/2 gallon of cherries today which were converted into one
of the best pies I have eaten. No pictures but you can imagine.

The Summer Camp gardens are coming along.

The garden down front should have things to harvest (and to weed). The
garden near the house should be ready to weed and the upper garden ready to
plant.
Planting corn, bugging potatoes.

AT work was fun. We went back to Hughes Gap to do some touch up work. Two
weeks ago ramps were abundant and easy to find. This time I could not find
any. The ramps die down and the weeds grow up.
