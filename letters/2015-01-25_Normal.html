January 25, 2015 · Sunday

Normal ?

9 photographs·302 words

Lil' Sister called . She is settling in to her new abode and life is good.

Herb heard from the folks at Vanderbilt. He is scheduled for "pre op" om
Feb.11. Operation on Feb. 25. HBO2 treatment is on hold until after the
operation.

For good news, the septic system is back in operation . Just in time.

I had not driven the Miata for some time. When I did drive it I quickly
concluded we had a problem and called in ace mechanic.

As some know, a house is not a home it is an ongoing project for GSH. Also,
as some know, over the years I have been subjected to various arrows of
scorn for carrying various auto items into the house for repair.

For work on the AT this week it was back to the blue trail near Hampton.
The trail had one section about 150 feet long on a fairly steep slope. The
slope now has 26 stone steps. It seemed a lot like busy work to me as I
preferred the slope.

The big lesson for this week was that I should have cleaned the fireplace
chimney last Fall before putting the fireplace back in operation. A
secondary lesson was that a dry chemical fire extinguisher will not
extinguish a chimney fire at least when trying to work through a fireplace
insert. However ! It will fill the entire house with a heavy coat of fine
,yellow powdery dust.

The repeat lesson is that when I get in a hurry I hurt myself.

A few years back, to make her woodworking easier and recognizing it was
likely to occur inside the house, Santa brought her a nice, woodworking
bench complete with clamps and vice.

It also doubles as a coat rack if anyone is missing a coat.
