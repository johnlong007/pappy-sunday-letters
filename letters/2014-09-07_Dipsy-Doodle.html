September 7, 2014 · Sunday

Dipsy Doodle

9 photographs·343 words

Neither GSH nor I had a Dr. appt. this week. However, Herb had several
culminating with the conclusion that he needs to go to Duke for further
work. Stay tuned.

I went with Jim Foster to cut downed tress on his section of trail. We ate
breakfast at a local place in Unicoi. Cousin Todd and his wife Kim where
there. We talked a bit , enough to determine that Jim is related to both .
They left before we did and upon leaving found they had paid our bill!

Meredith knows this section of trail well but no ramps this time of year. I
carried the club saw (20" bar) and all the safety gear.

We met three hikers who had come down from Madison Wis. for section hiking.
Had quite a good conversation with them since we had recently visited in
their home town. One of the men, and his son, we from the Dominican
Republic originally. Made me want to go there for a visit. They had
mised the turnoff for the shelter and had to spent the night out (it
rained) with no water.

We wold them about the next water source (not far) and that we were going
to renew the turn off sign.

Mechanic work was so-so.

I put new front brake rotors on the vw and cured shimmy during braking.

Wayne helped with starting the MM. Found we had to use full choke to keep
it running. Also determined that the radiator leaks and person standing
beside engine will get sozzled with expensive anti-freeze.

The LBT carburetor and the MM carb are now back from carb shop and
installed on respective vehicles. Stay tuned.

Thinking I can almost see the finish line, I started taking parts from the
parts van in order to choose the better pieces to put on the LBT.
After many fits and starts, the '63 fired up and sounds quite good. But,
some indication that valve guide seals may be required.​​
​All the animals are doing what animals do.

Anyone there?
