Adventures

14 photographs·237 words

I think I mentioned being contacted by a 3rd cousin once removed? Today I
met with Mike (Aunt Flo's son) and the "new" cousin and his grandmother in
Erwin. We talked at length then Mike was taking them on to his Mom's house
to see the furniture made by Ervin Robertson.
We will enter the details into the family tree program for anyone who is
interested.

My other big adventure was sticking my head into a wasp nest. Set a new
over 80 record in the 10 yard dash. Many stings but glad wasps do not chase
one as hornets do.

Bob and Laura put together a family event. Most , maybe all, those who
could not attend were missed.

I am using the correct color paint from spray cans do do all the 63 parts
which will not be exposed to weathering. Car is ready to paint when the
signs are right.

A lot of effort trying to get the clutch functioning properly on the case.
Looks like it should be replaced.

LLLL and David were here for the events. I got a bit of usage from David.

Wayne and Thomas stopped by to lend a hand on the clutch job.

Reward for many laps at the track.
