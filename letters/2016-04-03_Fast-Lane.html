April 3, 2016 · Sunday

Fast Lane

9 photographs·395 words

Up until 1962 Chrysler had the emergency brake as a band around a drum
around the drive shaft. A system completely separate from the brake system.
In 1963, that was dropped and Chrysler started using the much less reliable
system utilizing the rear wheel brake shoes.
It that time it quit being an emergency brake and became a (not very good)
parking brake.
The parking brake on our '63 did not work for years before we quit driving
it. But, with a manual transmission, a parking brake is not really needed.
Anyway, as part of the back like new program this week was focused on
getting the parking brake operational.

I spent two full days working on it. Fortunately I have a good supply of
spare parts and parts cars. First I found the assembly Wayne had removed
from the parts
convertible, then I removed the assembly from the SC swamp car Herb and I
procured. Turns out the problem was the end swaged on the actuating cable
had come off. I decided it would be easier to swap out the entire assembly
than to replace the cable. So, after two full days it is now back in
operation. I did check on line. I could have purchased a new cable for $30.
It appears my time is valued at about $2/hour.

As part of the '63 project, the trunk floor is now finished and the gas
tank back in place with undercoating over most of the underneath.

A bit of work on the fire engine. Finally got the brake master cylinder out
and cleaned along with the wheel cylinders.

We did trail work at the same place this week. The trail passes behind
Temple Hill, so , far those who have only seen it from the Shallow Ford
side, I shall include a picture from back side.

In more exciting news, we had a swarm of flying ants in my little corner of
the house. They came out from under the baseboard, I sent a picture to
Meredith who identified for me. Now, the question is, how to get rid of the
colony.

In almost as exciting news, Microsoft snookered me into downloading windows
10. I am struggling with my learning curve especially in dealing with
pictures.

Wayne spent two days applying another layer of fiberglass matting on the
pool. Maybe by and by.
