August 25, 2013 · Sunday

Feeling Old

9 photographs·532 words

Today is Diane's birthday. She was born a few days after I graduated from
UT. I baled the two of them out of UT hospital, took to Erwin and left with
my parents, Alan was already there , and headed cross country to Seattle.
I had withdrawn the last of our life saving to bale them out, no insurance
of course.As I remember, I had less than $100 to get me to Seattle.

Watching the kids get settled in to college and others starting families
and careers as brought home to me how quickly it all goes by.

Enough of that!

I am sure everyone remembers that my overall objective for the week was to
get the crawler running.Old Chinese saying " a picture is worth more than
ten thousand words".

On a high(er) note , Charlie came by with a couple of his Yankee friends.

A mechanical and Aero engineer and Charlie. They rummaged around amongst
the cars, trucks and tractors. I offered to let them take one each home. No
takers. I am really interested in making a giant sweep and visiting ALL
the non-resident grandchildren. I hope they are interested in having me
visit.

As the various grandchildren have been leaving various nests to test their
wings, some have requested a last meal. So far, none have requested my
specialty, hamburger with pork and beans.

For exciting local news, We had a group of kids from Wright State in to
work on the AT Tuesday. A small group so I only had two but they were
really good, fun workers. We got a lot done, more than our share.

Brian decided we needed to put a locust log beside the trail to keep it
from sloughing off. He cut a locust while Pat and I tugged on it with a
vine (from behind a big tree). As it started to fall, I decided to get
further away , turned to run and a limb smacked me across the bridge of my
nose. Mangled my specs, bloodied my nose and addled me.

After I got home GSH cleaned it good and applied tie strips with lots of
kind words . It is about well now. At church Wed,. night (Day after it
happened) the preacher asked what happened. I told him "she was slicing
cucumbers with a butcher knife when I came up and pinched her on the
backside, she turned and sliced my nose."

Friday we went back to Costco for hearing test. It was interesting and very
through. I believe I shall go ahead and procure the ones tester
recommended. I will have 90 days after delivery to return for full refund
with no questions if not fully satisfied.

Sunday we went to Shallow Ford Baptist Church for homecoming. It was great.
But, a lot of my family and friends are getting older and as song goes,
others have "gone home"

I almost forgot, Laura and Anita got in a lake outing.
