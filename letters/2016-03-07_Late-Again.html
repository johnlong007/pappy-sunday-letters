March 7, 2016 · Monday

Late Again

9 photographs·183 words

I went over to check on Herb last Monday. He is not doing well.

The fellow who has been "working" on his 300J for many years has the shell
primed. He brought it out for Herb to see. It was not a much of a rejoicing
event.

Herb and I bought a little IH crawler tractor from Don Hass many years ago.
It seems obvious Herb is not going to be using it soon and I have a project
so I commissioned Don to go haul it over here for me. It was difficult to
start but did get it running. However, after he got it here and started to
unload it quit. One more project but at least it should be warm this week.

Anita and I went to Switzerland to 1. visit Kenneth 2. see the LHC 3. go
to church with Kenneth.

It was a fine visit and I am really glad we went. Anita has pictures and I
have pictures.

It has been a long, long day so I am about ready to stop right here.
