April 19, 2020 · Sunday

Still Stumped

12 photographs·452 words

No time to get bored !

I hear reports of people going stir crazy from being confined. I can help?

I think it was Sunday night the rains came down and the floods came up.
That is the second time this year the retaining pond has filled and
overflowed. My intention is to move the dam over to edge of soccer field
and make basin about 10X bigger.

All I need is time and machine operators. Loggers, loggers would be helpful
also, and mechanics, maybe a gardner or so.

Last week we had moved a lot of the decayed leaves beside the drive down to
the eroded places. Mother nature moved them on down the field.

For do-gooder work we went to Erwin to aid and abet Farmer's daughter Lisa.
Some time back I had sawed up a cherry tree which fell at lower edge of the
hayfield. I wanted to get it moved to the brush pile in time for the hay to
recover from trafficking. I left three logs which I intend to go back and
claim for my collection. Stay tuned.

Wayne borrowed Bob's 20" saw. The main trunk is 32' long and is now
disconnected from balance of trunk and limbs. Extracting thelogs is still
in the talking about and thinking about phase.

Looking on along logs across Albert's field. Fianlly got to talkwith him,
as I expected he said take my time and do whatever I wanted with the wood.

As usual we went on a Sunday PM inspection tour of the kingdom. As usual
our comfort animals went along.

Somewhat to my dismay. when we worked our way up to edge of the woods we
found a jumbled mess of more downed trees. I think 5.

One a big Poplar and one other big Oak I did not clamber up to inspect the
root balls.

Th field in front of the barn is covered with buttercups.

I am trying to work out a plan to make a serious ramp run. In the meantime,
back at the ranch, the ones here are doing well. I have planted roots in
various places around for the last few years. Never harvested any here.
(Wonder if MA knows anything about ramps?)
The bunch pictured would be all part of a single root stem from 2-3 years
ago.

A little indoor work on the '37. PBBD helped F & I a piece of trim on the
other side of the grill. We are unsure whether this is going to be
declared good enough or try again.
