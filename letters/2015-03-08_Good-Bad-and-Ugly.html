March 8, 2015 · Sunday

Good, Bad and Ugly

5 photographs·456 words

I went with her to see Dr. Sago. She was very pleased at the work which had
been done and more than pleased with his rebounding. She did give him quite
a lecture about proper eating, drinking and walking, lots of walking!!!

Little sister found out her new and improved house did not have storm
windows and the North wind blew right throug . So, she has had windows
installed. A good thing otherwise heating and cooling bills would bankrupt
her.

No AT work this week as it was cold and more snow. But, we did have the
maintainers dinner.

While penned up with the cold, GSH has been working on the income tax. A
rough cut has been made. I suppose it is good in a perverse fashion that we
are going to have to pony up a whole lot (more). Less good news that we owe
TN (a non-income tax state) a bundle also.

While she and the cat were working on the tax reports, I have been moving
and sorting information from the old PC to the new one. Now that I have
about got it under control, I have figured out a filing system. If anyone
is interested I would like to run it by to get an opinion. I am trying to
get all the family pictures and stories in a form such that if anyone ever
wants to peruse it will be in a logical order.

One category is "Summer Camp". I thought that might be of interest to some
of the participants. I have entered all the materials I can find by year.
Diane has added what information she has. Now, I want to get it to LLLL so
she can add anything more. I am pretty sure some things (pictures) I
entered are at the wrong year so will need help moving things around.
Allison is making a first pass almost as we write (at least I left a copy
with her).

I did a bit more on the LBT. Finally got a new brake master cylinder
installed and put the newly covered drivers seat in (protected by a trash
bag).

In the Spring a young mans thoughts turn to--gardening !

Bob donated a bunch of sawdust and shavings to the cause. We hope that will
help lead to a bumper BB crop.

GGC number one had a birthday. GSH went to the party but I stayed home to
tend the critters. While tending I swept, dusted and mopped. Did a good job.

When I went to Erwin I looked at the mountains all snow covered and thought
about how fortunate I am.

For those who do not update for fear of no news worth recounting, Take
heart.
