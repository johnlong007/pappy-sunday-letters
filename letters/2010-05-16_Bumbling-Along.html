May 16, 2010 · Sunday

Bumbling Along

9 photographs·215 words

We finally got back into the do-gooder business. Working on a shower
stall for a LOW.

Dave has finally got an answer as to why he has been feeling lousy so much.
A tick borne disease akin to Lyme disease. It will take months of
anti-biotics to subdue. Til then, he is going to be limited. Also, they have
decided to switch to another church. Too liberal at CHCC with a female worship
leader.

We worked on the AT one day laying out switchbacks for a relocation at
Hughes Gap. Actually, I mostly harvested ramps.

This weekend is trail days in Damascus. We always recruit about 100 hikers
to work Sunday and Monday. In return, they get a patch and a meal Sunday
night and Monday night.
GSH has helped with the meal for the last few years including furnishing 1
gallon of strawberries for desert.
I could not find the place for the dinner this evening. We went, we hunted,
we came home. We disposed of the strawberries.

In other news, We covered the cherry tree, worked in the garden and
collected a load of wood/brush for a LOW neighbor.

In other exciting news:
Travelers returned from Alaska, My dog got a new bed and I visited the car
in the (pre) paint shop.
