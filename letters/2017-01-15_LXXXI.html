January 15, 2017 · Sunday

LXXXI

5 photographs·376 words

Not a good week.

The fat dog got sick, threw up all night and died in the morning.

Then later in the week I threw out a old, hard Christmas roll. Later saw
little dog with it. Still later thought it might have raisins in it.
Getting her stomach purged and neutralized did not make her happy. They
found nothing . But, she is fine today and I am glad we took her in.

Wed. GSH developed a saggy right side face and droopy eye. Could not get
through to Dr. so took her to emergency room. They said Bell's Palsy. It
not seems pretty clear there is more to it. Going to try again to get her
in to see GP to get a more complete answer.

Diane has been coming over to help with various tasks. This week, they put
a zipper in the red coat. They Chrysler parts man has promised shipment of
'63 parts this coming week. Will be glad to see that get underway.

​
I did not go work on the AT this week.

I did do my running about every day. It is not going well. I thought I was
making progress but went to the track today and was disappointed in the
times. Too disappointed to relay.

I suppose all know the news about Meredith? They cam by Saturday PM for a
guided tour. Turns out Connor is working at Tractor Supply while awaiting
the opening of other doors. Not wanting to miss an opportunity I asked him
to see if he can swap out Dwight's old tanks for current tanks.

​I did a bit of work on the '63 and started body work on the "49. One word
of advice, when trying to drive out a ground off bolt, look at the part of
the punch one plans to hit rather than at the part pressed against the bolt.

​Undaunted (but bruised) the work continued. Replaced the rusted out metal
on one fender, Plan to do the better, quicker, less painful job on the
other side. Stay tuned.

​This is going to be a better week. Really would like to go see Sis Tuesday
but looks like cannot make that happen.

And to all a good night.
