September 20, 2013 · Friday

Moan & Mown

8 photographs·428 words

Monday morning started ok but fits and starts since then.

I took the rig out to the hillside for annual mowing. The mower was making
a racket so I decided to the tough parts first.

Went over the hill and mowed the part down in the low parts.
Came back and mowed the top of the hill beside the driveway going to Dave
Bowers house.
Then the parts on either side of the big hill and finally the swamp right
down the hill behind the apartment annex.

Made a couple of swipes on the big hill when the mower made a loud racket
and began to jump violently. It had thrown a blade. Never did find it but
sure glad it did not get a tractor tire on the way to wherever it went.

Finished the whole job then using Dwight's '4 foot mower. Took longer so
had to finish on Tuesday.

Pretty much got all the mowing done around here. Depends on when the frost
arrives. All the woolly worms I have seen have been brown meaning light
Winter. For confirmation, while mowing the hillside I wiped out a hornets
nest. They sure were mad but are not able to find the target when driving
the tractor. Anyway, the nest was no more than a foot above ground.

For the first time in a spell I went to Erwin. Had a ham biscuit at
McD'swith Herb then went to Flo's house. She has a lot of family
letters,
clippings etc. which I would like to copy. But, she will not allow out of
her sight. May have to send an agent to copy. Better not wait too long as
she is 86 I believe.

Our garden is winding down but it has been a good year.

Went to GP dr. this week and had my annual, 1,000 mile check up.Pronounced
me ok for another few miles. On the way home, I stopped at Laura's to
transport HER cat out here to be a barn cat. I tried to pick it up. A
mistake. Scratched one hand and bit the other. Bled like a stuck hog.

Read on line about cat bites and decided to go see Dr.

Got a shot, sewn up and anti-biotics. There must be a moral in that story
somewhere.

We are going out of town for a few days. Stay tuned.
