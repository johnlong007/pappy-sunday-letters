June 17, 2012 · Sunday

P.S.

0 photographs·354 words

The Peru contingent did come home.

I headed for LLLL's Wed. afternoon to go with Dale to pick them up at 11PM
in Nashville.

As I was going across Monteagle on I-24 the VW quit going. At this point, I
should inject that I purchased the VW because I figured the Miata could
not/would not live forever. Anyway, the VW quit going . Fortunately,
Charlie responded to my call for help.

My consultant says it is in the transaxle/transmission and is bad news.
Stay tuned.

In order to get something accomplished, Charlie was enlisted to help
extract the '63. I checked it out today and expect to have it running
tomorrow.

While I was foraging through old pictures in Mom's scrapbook, I found this
one with the '37 in the background. That was made in Granny Buchanan's
front yard up Bean's Creek in Buladean. The fine young person in last weeks
picture appears to be what? Maybe 10? Jimmy is still bigger than I. That
did not last very long.

When we cut weeds and cleared water bars last week we encountered a large
downed tree. So, we went back to clear it this week. All these years and I
never stuck a saw. But, Jim brought the club tool kit with wedges and a
pole axe. I stuck the saw twice. Probably that confirms people taking more
risks when they have safety equipment. At least for me. We did clear the
trail and met several intere4sting hikers.

Blountville old tractor owners show was Friday and Saturday this week. It
went well. I did not take any of the old cars we had extracted and did not
get any old tractors running. So, I took a load of old farm equipment on
the trailer. It worked out well. Old folks love to tell their grandchildren
how they used to have one just like that and what it was used for. Almost
always in the very difficult circumstances.

As we were leaving church this morning someone came running up to GSH to
tell her good she looked in the pretty dress. I thought so also.
