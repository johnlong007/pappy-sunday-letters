March 26, 2017 · Sunday

Who Killed Cock Robin

10 photographs·358 words

Many will appreciate my trials and tribulations with the cherry tree.
Covering, uncovering, frost, ants,birds and occasionally harvesting. All
worthwhile because she can indeed, bake a cherry pie. At least she could.
It appears I used the wrong spray, wrong sprayer, failed to clean sprayer
or some other brain fade act but I have killed the cherry tree and part of
the grape vines. Rats.

On the other hand, for the first time in my life I washed a load of clothes
in the washing machine. (lest you write my off as a male chauvinistic pig,
I hasten to add, GSH has yet to change oil in one of our cars.)

Even better news, the dead dish washing machine has been replaced ! Not
with just any machine but a one button machine. I may be able to learn to load
it.

​ No work on the '63 this week. A fair amount of work on the '49. Anita had
glass shop cut new glass for the wind wing windows. GSH washed all the
glass to help determine what else must be replaced.

No Dr. appts this week. The home therapy work continued. Flexibility and
mobility are coming right along.

​
Thursday was AT work day. The hiking season is upon us. Have not met any
through hikers who started in Ga. (400 miles) yet but a number of section
hikers. I carried a hand saw and a mattock type tool and cleared several
blowdowns while walking about 7 miles.

​Did have lunch in a lovely location by a babbling brook.

​I have not spotted any ramps yet but have not visited favorite spot
either. The ones planted here are coming right along. It is clear they are
spreading.

I got the mower out and checked it over. Could never keep this place beat
into submission without it. I need to clear all the downed limbs from the
wooded parts of the place before the weeds grow up. Anyone looking for work?

​We have a lot of periwinkle , it is in full bloom.

​Spring is upon us. How about around the rest of the family?
