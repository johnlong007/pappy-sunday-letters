April 12, 2009 · Sunday

Easter et al

15 photographs·459 words

I did have tests run to determine if I have restricted blood flow to the
upper extrimity. They listened and looked at the heart/chest area and the
neck area. Said my Dr. would pass on the results to me in due time. I suppose
it was not too bad as , unlike Alan, they did not cart me off to roto root
or turn the monitor so I could not see it. The stress test had to be
delayed as Dr. had failed to specify with or w/o drug induced heart rate
increase.So, stay tuned.
Being grandfatherly inclined, I watched a track meet featuring Maria and a
ball game featuring Benjamin.
Maria did not get a good start in the hurdles, stumbled on the first hurdle
and did not finish well, But, she (and her team) won the relay and she ran
off with the 800 meter.

Benjamin had a good game. He is the leadoff hitter and got on 3/4 I think.

AT work was delayed due to bad weather. When we did go therer was still
quits a bit of snow.Over a foot in some places but mostly only a few inches of
wet slushy stuff. A group from ETSU is coming to work on a Saturday
pretty soon. I went through and cut all the big trees and brush where they will
be building trail . The rest of the group was digging a re;ocation. We are
meeting a lot of North bound through hikers now.

Aunt G spent as few days in the hospital In Erwin. I do not think it was
too serious and the plan was to move her into the LTC center today. Since
Easter was approaching, I took my most prized artifact with me when I went to
see her. She recognized it ! Said it came from her grandmother and that she
had always had several nest eggs.

Charlie brought his South Korean roommate home with him for Easter. I am
not sure how much he enjoyed his visit.

Ask the Summer Campers what they see in this picture.

Saturday was egg dying.

The mowing machine is finished except for patching leaky tubes and mounting
tires on the wheels. Herb brought the leaky tubes and tires over. Now, I
wish I had taken the wheels to him.

A half hearted attempt was made to start the Cletrac. But fuel problems
then ignition problems foiled the attempt. Fortunately GSH was available to
drag it back into starting position waiting another day when Dwight is
available to try again. I was going to let Charlie start it but thought of
baseball (or something) got the best of him.
