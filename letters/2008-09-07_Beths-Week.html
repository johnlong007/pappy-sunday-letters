September 7, 2008 · Sunday

Beth's Week

7 photographs·296 words

We have deer,many deer. A mother with twins included the Quince tree in the
turn around on her daily route. Right after I made this picture a Ground Hog
showed up, sat back and ate a Quince. But, I was too slow to get a
picture.Also had a flock of turkeys wander through but I missed them also.

Tuesday we worked on the AT. Eight of the regulars and 20 students from
Wright State in Ohio. They built a relocation trail around an eroded up and down
section of trail. We walked in about two miles to get to the section. As I
was coming out down a fairly steep hill, carrying a load of tools on my
shoulder, I tripped on a root and took a face down fall. Blooded my arm and my
already sore knee. But, no one saw it and I was ever so appreciative of being able
to get up and press on. Bloody but undaunted.

Some time back, I noted that a THP officer had explained the law to me
concerning open wheels on trailers. Herb's trailer now has fenders . Tomorrow
morning I get to make an appearance in sessions court to find out if locking the
barn door really works

All week long the family has been focused on the WEDDING. The rehearsal was
Friday evening followed by the rehearsal dinner catered by Diane, Laura and
supporting staff. I thought it went practically perfect. I took a picture of
the sky looking out over Blountville after the rehearsal.

Final preparations.

Wedding celebration, really well done.

Sunday morning, some decided a lake outing was in order before dispersing.
