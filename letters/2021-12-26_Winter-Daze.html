December 26, 2021 · Sunday

Winter Daze

9 photographs·311 words

I can already tell the difference !

First things first, Effort continued to try to free the stuck engine in the
'65. I rigged up a binder rig to apply a lot of force to the power handle.
It works but will take about 3 more weeks. So now planning to go with Plan
C as soon as David arrives. Stay tuned.

We like our fire place and shop stove. HGowever, they have to be fed and
one needs to look a year ahead. One warm day we loaded up the chain saws
and harvested a load of next Winter wood. About 3 more loads will be needed.

S-I-L Bob installed an electric fireplace on the now bedroom side. My
backup plan is to replace the wood fireplace when I get to far gone to deal
with fire wood. Who knows, could be any day .

About 1946 my grandfather purchased anelectric train which he set up and
opereated for a few days.

Then it showed up at our house and eventually came to me. While we lived in
Seattle in 1959 we refurbished it for out TWO children. This week PBBD
dug it out and our clever ,talented D-I-L got it operating.

Wieh Christmas time upon us, family members started to come in from far
and, in some cases, near. We were pleased.

Sure enough, two well stuffed stockings !

And a package all the way from Utah ! BTW, the pepper was taste tested
tonight by a panel of experts (me and Alan) and pronounced just right.

The modern age boggles me in many ways.
Gwendolyn from Portland
Charlotte discretely displaying her new adornment.
Jonathan with an adornment apperaring live from Toronto.
Maria from a Rocky Mountain High.

Ponder that.
