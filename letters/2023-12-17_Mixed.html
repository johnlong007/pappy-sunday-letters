December 17, 2023 · Sunday

Mixed

12 photographs·299 words

This picture was taken 1- 1/2 years ago when we got the 55 out. The plan
was to spend a few days prepping it then drive it all Summer'

These pictures were made yesterday when we gave up in despair.

As far as we made it. I think I retarded the timing too much. To be
continued.

We pulled the slightly damaged fender off the truck.Wayne located a
replacement.

As soon as the replacement is painted, it will be ready to install. Nothing
can go wrong.

In a lull between tasks, we shoveled several years of accumulation of ashes
from the incinerator.

A delightful interlude.

I worked on the AT with Pat Loven for several years. He had a section from
White Rock to Dennis Cove. Due to age and mileage, he has given up his
section. We took his Jeep up the tower access road just for old time (and
timers) sake. Then we came back out, drove over Buck mountain and on to
Dennis Cove.

A fine day out, we stopped at McDs in Hampton. I paid $10/ for two chicken
sandwiches. I am still in shock.

Graduation time.

While I was fumbling with the camera, Diane got a good shot.

In other exciting news, we have a newly minted RN in the family. I hope Sis
was watching.

The sawyers were back on the job. Note differences in cold tolerance.

My mother, every year, would order a big tin of Christmas Candy from Sears.
She put it out before Christmas . and gradually loosened the restrictions
as the day drew closer.
I still delight in being able to pick up two pieces.
