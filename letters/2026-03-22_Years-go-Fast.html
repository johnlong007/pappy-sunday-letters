Years go Fast

15 photographs·377 words

My mother liked to tell me, "Days go slow, years go fast."

Probably the high point of the week, Mary wanted ask about life when some
old person was young. She picked me. I was pleased, and glad to oblige.
This is the first time any child, GC or GGC has shown interest beyond the
theoretical and painless.

75 years since I attended school here.

Granny Lewis Creek flows just beside Temple Hill School. In the early
Spring, Horny Heads come up South Indian Creek and into GL Creek to spawn.
I told the Mary and Suz about playing in the creek at recess and grabbling
horny heads (fish not boys) .

We had lunch at the "lower place." Round Mountain is Temple Hill. A Forest
Service lookout tower was there. Now it's gone. I think all our kids have
been there before it was torn down.

My parents would be dismayed. I am dismayed. You can't go home again.

On the other hand, color me delighted. Rebecca has caught a trout. I think.
G & R came by, Diane mentioned the deck table her mother had assembled
needed repair. On their first day off, the work crew arrived, and the table
is like new.

Mr. and Mizz Mallard dropped in. Janet and Mathis spend time and money
looking for them.

She worked on the nose cone's finishing touches; I mostly worked on Cub.

I told Alan that being bothered by not being able to do things is balanced
by having kids willing (and able) to do things for me.

Worth more than a thousand words.

I am no longer able to swing 5 gallon containers around. But, I know
someone who can.

Cub in progress. Someone had put a plug in cross-threaded, a needless,
bothersome error. Like most errors

Full works on Monday morning. We asked the UVA doctor. Is the IDNIFA
working? He said, "You are still alive."

Sunday PM buddy Dave Edwards came by. I told him the GOB's used to talk
about girls, guns and cars.
