September 18, 2022 · Sunday

Family and Friends

12 photographs·405 words

All know we went with the Friday hikers for years. Not much hiking anymore
but we still keep in touch somewhat. A couple of the remnants came by to
check on us (and the fruit and nut crop) . Bob Harvey is one of the most
interesting people I have known. I never came back from a hike without
having 2 or 3 items to go on line and learn more because of things he had
mentioned. Lee (AKA Daisy) is a survivor, she has had more than her share
of issues but would not know by being a casual observer. She was one of our
group which "did" Mt. Elbert.

Guess what he came prepared to play on his new, hand made, custom made
guitar. Charlie? Color me delighted and honored.

Moved outside and got the full program. My sister would have been pleased.

Finish work continued on the retaining basin PBBD is spreading fertilizer
with a bit more grass seed included.

Some washing had occurred so we procured more of the seed sprouting mat .
Also rigged up watering hoses and wet the mat to hasten sprouting.

A & C made progress on the metal carport before heading North to visit
their grandchild at a remote site. The plan is to have it ready to park the
'55 by the time we get it running.With the help of John and Wayne, the car
is now ready for a test drive. I worked with John to determine that the
fuel pump is functional. Then Wayne said he believed the ignition
switch was erratic. We put a jumper wire on and the engine fired right
up.I took a picture with Wayne admiring the running engine but muffed it.

I bet several have found memories of this truck. 1996 pushing 300 K , looks
good and runs good.

Speaking of fond memories, how about the best car ever built !

We took the rogue in for 85K service this week.Do not expect it to come
close to the car or truck service record.

More memories. Fond?

I went in for Dr. Sago visit this week. She dug out a live one. My first
was in 1960 while we lived in Seattle.. Many more since then, I never took
them seriously until recently
