February 17, 2008 · Sunday

Week 7

7 photographs·215 words

A varied week, mostly good.
For my do-gooder work Monday, I ran a chain saw cutting a new trail through
the woods at Bays Mtn. Park. Saw some turkeys which reminded me of some young
men.
Tuesday was AT work. We refurbished a shelter and built rock steps up to the
shelter. I rolled (uphill) and carried big rocks.
Wednesday I went to the knee Dr. Not sure what he said but what I heard was
he could give me a shot which might last a year or he can go in and repair
the damage. I asked how about I go back to running and if it gives out he can
go in and repair it. He said OK. So, that is the plan.
Finally got the Alfa ready to go back on the road. Also put in a few hours
clearing brush at Blountville over the course of the week..
Thursday I went to the eyelid Dr. I will be having eye lids refurbished in
march. I can hardly wait to see how the world looks.
Friday I went with Diane and Vanessa to visit Aaron, came back Sunday. A
fine visit. Got another reminder of how small the world is.
