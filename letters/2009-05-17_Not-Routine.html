May 17, 2009 · Sunday

Not Routine

13 photographs·552 words

Monday was the day for the heart catherization. Not a bad experience. The
assisting nurse was a lady we go to church with. She likes hot pepper and we
eat with her family sometimes on Wed. night.When they started the sedative
I told her it felt like my high school football days when one of my
buddies would put Atomic Balm in my supporter. Outside of that since they did not
install a stent recovery has been a non issue. I did take her a mess of
ramps for supper Wed. night.
Oh, the Dr. said I have 70-85% blockage in all the heart arteries. Should
stop running and all vigorous exercise and get in line for multiple by-pass
surgery. The alternative is a heart attack. Also have to carry
nitroglycerine at all times. I am currently scheduled to talk with a heart surgeon
Friday morning.

Two band concerts this week. Central and DB.
For do-gooder work, Dwight aided and abetted in getting a riding lawn mower
going for a LOW. I forgot the camera so no pictures of that. She was
grateful and told the SS class today so no credit in the here after.
Christopher came over one day to help me piddle around. We got the M' Cycle
out and goy it running. Last year, I had tried to get the "H" running to
take to Blountville tractor event. I did not get it started and put it away
not running when parked. That bugs me.
Christopher drove and I dragged the "H" up on the landing. Then, Dwight
came over and got it running. It had both fuel and electrical problems. After
getting it going, I drained all the fuel from both tanks and Christopher
helped move it into the shop. I want to spruce it up for the tractor show.
Oh, I forgot to mention, Wayne came by and DROVE the Cletrac into the shop.
It also needs some detail work to be ready for the tractor show.

Hal was doing much better when I stopped by to see him. But, Saturday
morning he fell out of bed, hit a sharp corner and cut his eye. Five stitches. I
told Sue to hold a fist by his eye but she is a failure at a fist and at
looking mean.

GSH has undertaken to mulch and cover the BB's. First, I got to mow around
them.

Flag Pond got 6 1/2 inches of rain Thursday night. So, I went over to see
the creek and to check on Aunt G's house. I really wanted to see if the
creek had washed the bank away in front of the house Rusty had built. Creek was
still almost full when I got there but no damage that I could see.


This weekend was "Trail Days" in Damascus. Hundreds of past and present AT
hikers converge for the weekend. I took a picture of their parade Saturday.
We recruit 100 for special work days Today and tomorrow. They get a patch
and a free supper. GSH helped feed them. I worked on the AT.
I almost left out, A trip to a friends house to harvest a load of pre-cut
firewood for Wayne.
