April 1, 2018 · Sunday

He is Risen

15 photographs·383 words

Family Easter tradition is pictures after church. Preferably in front of
Spring flowers.
So, here we go.

​So far, I have received one other input.

I expect many others will be forthcoming. If not, I may post a picture of
me pouting. If anyone has an egg dying picture ​I would be interested .

This week has gone poof. I thought after I retired (20 years ago) I would
sit by pool and relax. Still waiting. While on the subject, no work on the
TDBG project but contractor said he planned to start replacing the front
wall this coming week.

Monday we made a trip to see Beth and family. Watching the kids makes it
all come back to me. Actually not all but all the good parts.

​The food reminds me. I have tried making pancakes several times. The last
time was such a flop I had to fling the whole mess and eat cereal for
breakfast. This week, I did better, much better.

​The slab drama continued. collected the remainder, unloaded and started
sewing the whole lot. To be continued.

​

​

​Some time back I mentioned trying to deal with wild onions by snipping
tops and spraying with weed and grass spray. It has not worked well, should
have consulted and expert. So far I have rather large dead places
surrounding sick wild onions all round and about.

PBBD brought a woodpecker feeding station over. That has gone well for
Downy and red breasted but so far no Woody.

​
Quite a bit of work on the 63 this week. A seam split for a couple of
inches in the newly installed seat cover. After much pondering and
preparing, the entire seat assembly was put into a large trash bag. Hooked
to a shop Vac and collapsed. The torn seam sealed off as for an appendix
removal operation and PBBD did surgery using curved needles. It worked.

​I suppose automobile steering wheels of the 60s were made of Eastman Tenite.
Anyway, with time, they crack. Sending off and getting rebuilt is $400. Doing
it oneself is interesting. Made my first pass at it. May or may not try to
improve a bit.

One final note/picture. We got a very nice Easter card. Can anyone guess
who it was from?
