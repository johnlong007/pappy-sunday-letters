So Far, So Good

13 photographs·304 words

The times they are a changin !

Today, I went to bfst at Perkins, then early service and stayed for SS.
First time in a spell. Katy was here to help PBBD get things moving.

Don and Nancy brough a box of tomatoes. Linda fixed BLTC. Maybe the best I
ever had. PBBD says I am taking an anti-viral pill. So, I gobbled tomatoes
with no restraint and have not yet got a fever blister. Perhaps there is a
good side to this trial.

Another good side is family and friends jump in to help. I am not able to
hike the 5 gallon cans and pour in fuel.

Adam has been out cavorting around the globe. He gave us a travelogue on
visit to Korea, Japan and Mongolia. I enjoyed it thoroughly. Second best to
actually going along.

Maria has been doing her globe trotting inside the lower 48 for a while.
She flew in and came by to check in. Linda and Dale were i town also, Dale
to help Sarah on her house I think

I had not seen this sight for a while and figured I might not see again so
grabbed device to record.

One of our visitors asked if the dog and the cat get along. They also
provide escort service when we go check the mail.

The dog loves to chase the deer. Look closely. I have timed the dog as we
come up the driveway, about 25MPH. The deer go more than twice as fast it
appears watching them depart.

And I thought she came to town just to visit us !

Looking good,
