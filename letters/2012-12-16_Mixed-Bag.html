Mixed Bag

10 photographs·339 words

As regular readers know, I have been giving Katy helpful tips on what
is expected of a GSH. This week, in response to all have enquired
about the proper role of the Lord and Master, i will include one
picture which should capture the essence.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, GSH has embarked on a couple of new
projects. I have no idea what her plan is for the "front yard". The
fence" around the circle is to make it easier to go around without
turning too tight onto her island. We shall see.

Re: Larry Kidd. Now off the ventilator, still on everything else.
Moved into a regular room. Cannot move anything other than his head.
But, I believe he is going top survive. It will be interesting to see
how long recovery takes.

GSH reported Miata brakes were making a noise. Fortunately, Wayne came
by and more or less offered to install new shoes. Turns out the manual
was correct in noting drain and replace fluid or the caliper will
corrode and stick.

Dwight pronounced the old Samsung TV/Monitor DOA. So, Bob ordered a
new, bigger, Toshiba TV/Monitor. I rented a jr. geek for the afternoon
to hook up the old copier, the new scanner and the new TV/Monitor.
Everything is working now except me and the TV.

How about a pretty evening picture and an identify that bird picture?

For AT work, we were roughing out a relocation near Chestoa. Once
more, I was the chain saw operator. In case you are interested in
meaningful usage of your tax dollars for you, before we can touch
anything several experts in various fields must examine the proposed
route. One, is a biologist, in this case marking trees which we must
not touch lest we disturb the resident bat population. Note the ribbon
on the dead tree trunk. Do not disturb!

A bit more progress on the '37 but I moved it over to get The Miata
inside for brake work to be followed by the VW soon.
