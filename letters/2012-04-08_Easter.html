April 8, 2012 · Sunday

Easter

9 photographs·537 words

This picture is my GGM, Mary Jane Parks Howell. I am not sure if the kid in
background is me or Jimmy. She died when I was four. She lived in the
little log house just pass Granny and Papaw Garland.

She gave me my wooden egg.

So, I have had this egg for over 70 years. I remember taking it to church
for Easter egg fights. I suppose on Easter Sunday afternoon and I also
remember taking it to school. Sort of a wonder I ever kept track of it for
all these years.

I took it to church with me today but never got the chance to have show and
tell.

We went to SS then went to First Prbys in KPT for their Easter service.
John, Benj and a couple of other young men played as a brass quartet along
with their organ all through the service. It was a great service and the
music was superb. Glad I went. It was soaring and uplifting, I liked the
form, structure and ritual of the service. I am afraid we are going to far
on the "contemporary".

If anyone would like to report on where they were for Easter service and
what it was like, I would be interested, Aaron, Beth, Katy, Allison, Janet,
Rachel . Everyone get the message or do I need to keep listing?

Of course, I would like to tell you I have spent the week waiting on Laura
hand and foot. Actually, I believe I will tell you that and see if anyone
believes me.

Truth be known, GSH did more waiting than I did. However, I did get a
chance to spring into action Saturday.

For AT work I went with Jim and Kim to repair the shelter roof on Jim's
section. It just so happened that near here is a fine ramp patch. We dug a
couple of sacks full. The ramps are still small but we hope tasty. GSH is
preparing eggs with ramps along with sausage and biscuits as I am
composing. Sure smells good.

We got back from working early so we went to Erwin. Herb had called to tell
me the mountain land next to ours had been sold and a young man has started
building a road and is planning to build a house. I stopped to talk with
him. He said he is going to get it surveyed. I asked that he keep me
informed so I can come walk the line after the survey is under way. Stay
tuned.

Our property is just to the right in this picture. I came home, dug out a
copy of our deed and sent it to Dan Saxon who does my surveying.

While digging out the deed, I found a couple of letters Granny Garland had
received from a family member in California. A relative of her father,
Joseph Alexander Howell. He is the one I learned almost nothing about while
digging for the family roots. So, now I have a new lead to pursue.

Charlie had company for Easter.
