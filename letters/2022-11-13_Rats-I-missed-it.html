November 13, 2022 · Sunday

Rats, I missed it.

16 photographs·355 words

The high point of the week ! I made GSH laugh a bit. Getting her dressed
and prattling along. I said "we are so good we should get jobs at the ole
folks home. I will undress little ole women and you can undress little ole
men''.

Fran came to visit . A delight , I like her and her family. We each got all
caught up. She and Ron are modifying their house so her Mom can come live
right adjoining them.

Progress on the 55 brake system. The rear brakes system is all back
together and parts are here for the front wheels. Nothing can go wrong.

You will remember the 37 was running with all systems working when
PBBD took over. The latest is taking the headlights off the nose piece. The
mountingbrackets are each broken and she wants to improve on my grill
installation. I think I shall get niece voc. school teacher Farme Lisa to
sign her up for MIG and TIG welding in Spring.

We split some firewood. I am distressed. I run down way too quickly. Too
much soft living or getting too old. Maybe both.

She doesn't bleed near as profusely as I do.

We cleaned up the tomato patch and cut down the rab apple tree. The plan is
to call in a landscaping pro to help improve the site.

The dog had a standoff with a deer (look close). Then the deer stepped
toward the dog. She went into full speed retreat. Today, a deer ran by and
she chased it at high speed.

Leaf raking, blowing and sweeping season is almost over.

The retaining basin is looking good. The deer will love it this Winter.

There is one spot where PBBD spread some really cheap seed that has her
worried that it contained thistle.

Aid and comfort

More aid and comfort

Even more

The Lion and the lamb are getting along fine.
