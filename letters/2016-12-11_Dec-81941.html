December 11, 2016 · Sunday

Dec. 8,1941

12 photographs·457 words

​Momma's little brother Jim, was stationed in Hawaii until shortly before
the war. He was in the army air core and was shipped to the Philippines,
Clark Field, along with a shipment of 17 Disassembled B-17s.

The Japanese occupation of the Philippines occurred between 1942 and 1945,
when the Empire of Japan occupied the Commonwealth of the Philippines
during World War II. The invasion of the Philippines started on 8 December
1941, ten hours after the attack on *Pearl Harbor*.
The attack on Clark Field started right after the attack at Pearl Harbor.
Jim told me he was on the runway, under a truck when the attack was going
on. He had a 45 and would shoot at the pilots. He could see them looking
out and laughing during the attack.
Get a copy of The book "They fought Alone" if you care to read about his
next four years. He came out alive, as a captain, with a Silver Star and TB.

​I remember Pearl Harbor. It was a big event in the family because we knew
Jim was there. I bet Lil' Sister remembers it better than I.

Jim died a couple of years back. A fine man. I talked with him several
times and have his remembrances in my family history.

I am proud of our family and those have served and continue to serve;

​

Makes my notes even more trivial.

Wayne and his helper got the brakes on Herb's hand off car repaired. Going
to look for a new home before long.

It turned cold late in the week and I put in some time on the '63 but no
pictures.
I sanded on the '49, it has paint under rust at some places.

​GSH continued her leaf blowin' in the wind.

​The Christmas season frenzy continued !

​AT work day was more of the same. I went with three others to finish
replacing rotten timbers in the Laurel Fork bridge. The timbers go across
the walkway and have a brace holding the railing. My job was to take the
bolts out of the brace and replace in the new timbers. I had to stretch
across the walkway and reach out to remove bolts, drill holes in
replacement and replace bolts. It led to very sore ribs.

​

GSH commanded me to cut her hair. Under duress, I did just that ! No after
pictures.

​Tuesday I spent the day at the hospital getting a battery of heart testing
done. A lot of nodding and whispering but they told me nothing. I wore the
monitor for 24 hours but did not run during that time (AT work on Wed.) so
no "events". Back in for consultation Tuesday.
