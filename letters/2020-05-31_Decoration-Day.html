May 31, 2020 · Sunday

Decoration Day

16 photographs·413 words

Meredith had asked about visiting the family cemetery last Sunday. We
delayed due to poor weather. But, today was really nice.

We went to Beans Creek where my father grew up and drove to the old home
place. My grandmother and her son Frank lived in the house pictured below
until 1953.

Not sure when I took this picture. The property was all sold for taxes at
some time. Not a trace left now.

Then we toured the Beans Creek cemetery. I had not been there since Sis and
I went to Cricket's funeral. Maybe she will tell you the story.

My grandfather, his first wife and the baby with whom she died in
childbirth are all on the first row Along with my grandmother and other
family members.

Next we are going to the cemetery in Shallow Ford. Stay tuned.

On the home place this week, one of the maple trees on line out front split

I dragged it to brush pond and sawed in to firewood size. Told Wayne he
could have it. We shall see.

Since the Giant Oak is sort of under control I decided to start thinking
about the tangled mess above the grapevine arbor.
The part which is of most concern is an Oak caught in another Oak. I do not
like working under a leaner.
Wayne is standing on a downed Poplar.

Look close and can see I have a chain attached to the leaner. Could not
break it loose. Finally with much trepidation I got under and in
between the whole mess and sawed the Oak trunk about 4 feet up. Still could
not budge. To be continued.

Early in the week I decided to do some M & R on the tractor, Started with
pressure washing and installing a brand new battery.

You will note Wayne is Multi tasking. He installed a new tach cable.

The willing workers taking a break. Well deserved we trust. Maybe.

GSH's sister Nancy came by for a really nice visit. I caught up on all her
family. She is 80 and believes that is indeed old.

We have a few strawberries coming in each day. So far, no more than GSH can
handle.

LLLL came by for a visit . All tuckered out it seems.
