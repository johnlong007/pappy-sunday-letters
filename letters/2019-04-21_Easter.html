April 21, 2019 · Sunday

Easter

9 photographs·300 words

I suppose by this time everyone has found their Easter basket and
gorged on chocolate
bunny?

I spent a lot of time this week reading the thesis. I believe two people,
each named Kenneth, who have read it cover to cover. I also got to listen
, and see charts and graphs, of his thesis defense. Due to circumstances
beyond my control will not be able to attend the graduation.

Also getting short stories fromAlf to read. East Germany was a different
world.

Worked a little bit on the 63. Adjusted brakes and steering. Working to
check sources for exhaust system installation before finishing that part.
Local outfits gave me sticker shock.

Continued to try to get fuel system working on the IH crawler. This time I
got the other arm but will only provide picture upon request.

The new dog is doing well. He likes to ride with me on anything. Tractor,
mower and Bobcat.

We went on a ramp checking run. The patch I know which involves the least
hiking. Ramps can be seen in this picture at edge of bushes where GSH is
looking. They need a couple more weeks.

Anita and Joe had an event for various family members. I got to see the
newest GGC.

Rains came down and floods came up, again. Looks like we will start over on
repairing flood damage.

Most of the week was really nice and warm. First time GSH has donned her
serious track outfit this season. I have been trying for 5 miles every day.
When we had Spike, he would trot around 4 laps then quit. GSH walks until I
do 3 miles then starts wandering here and there.
