Continuing Education

12 photographs·410 words

The you tube video is for Rachel and any other aspiring banjo players. If
anyone has a video of her performance with Janet I would really like a copy.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qo5OsvoDcpY

It is good to have family and friends.

Bob Harvey and his stepdaughter came for a visit Saturday. I failed to get
a picture. Bob should have reminded me. He was the one who always insisted
on a group picture on the Friday hikes.
I read recently that for an old person to be secure and happy they need,
good genes, enough money and a good daughter.
I do believe he has nailed it.

Kim is hiking in Florida, at my request, she sends a picture each day. I
appreciate right much.

I do believe Spring is on the way.

Two weeks ago a storm came through. Uprooted two big trees and blew top
from several others. The pines all fell away from our driveway and the big
Oak over into the neighbors pasture. None in the way at all.
Now, after two weeks work, I have a couple of pine logs in the way out
front and the walnut field littered with limbs such that we must remove
before time to mow. All in the way.

Sometimes I wonder about me.

Weather was cooler this week GSH being mostly skin and bones, bundles up to go
out. But, out she goes.

My other willing worker is much more willing, but less worker. He is quick
learn and knows what "stay here" means.

Some work on the '63 trim. GSH polishing.

PBBD installing letters where my fumble hands could not go.

Got the letters front and rear installed. A bit concerning about how
securely but I have spares if any come off.

Did not attempt the side emblem. Wayne checked and said he believes he can
install, Cannot afford to lose one of these as not easily if at all
replaceable.

We walked around today. When we bought this place there were several apple
trees up on the hill. All But one have died of old age. One just fell down
and the other (in background) is leaning low.

Coming back from the walk I looked at the house. It looks very large, It is
very large for a little ole couple and their little pets.
