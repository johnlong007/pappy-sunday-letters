April 22, 2012 · Sunday

One Down

8 photographs·429 words

Rejoice, after many weeks, months, Years ? the table / benches project has
been completed, put into service and a new project undertaken.

Too ensure no loss of momentum or onset of boredom, a project to renew the
"old" dining room table is now underway. First, it had to be peeled from
the support structure. Other updates to follow.

Some progress was made on my current silk purse project. Sealed beam
headlights did not come into use until after the war. Before then, the
headlights used bulbs with parabolic reflectors and a glass cover. I have
been carrying on an internal debate about converting the '37 to sealed beam
lights. While pondering, I decided to clean and repairing one of the
housings. The glass cover fell out onto the concrete shop floor. I did make
quite a bit of progress fitting the old grill into the grill housing (at
least I think so). Not sure what to do about the missing chrome ring to
surround the grill.

The new and improved roll cage has been tested along with the addition of a
seat belt and found to be a big step forward. I am still considering the
tire situation and trying to decide how many should be replaced. I tried it
out while we finished collecting all the sawed wood and brush around the
big oak tree. Soon, the log will be sold or converted into chunks to be
split (by unnamed willing workers) for firewood.

Laura required very little hand holding this week , at least by me.
However, one of her children took time off from her academic pursuits (or
whatever she was pursuing) to come administer TLC.

We have two dogs. One of them dearly loves squeaky toys. He can rip and
shred in seconds to get to the squeaker. GSH loves to insert the squeaker
into what she hopes will be indestructible shrouding. So far, dog is ahead
by 12 to zero.

The Kingsport Community Band mostly plays for local old folks homes for the
broke down and stove up. But, again this year, they performed a Sunday
afternoon concert at the Natural Tunnel sate park in Va. A very cold, windy
day but a good, well attended concert. Perhaps you can pick out a familiar
head.

Dave had is operation so, no do-gooder. Carl had a special ASU work day on
Saturday so no AT work. Plenty to do on the home place !
