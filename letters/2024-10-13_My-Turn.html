October 13, 2024 · Sunday

My Turn

11 photographs·310 words

Aunt Flo died, best I can tell, I am now the patriarch. No church or
funeral home service, A brief, very well done, service at cemetery right
beside Bud's marker.
It was not sad, The sun came up and went down over Mike and Gary, they were
both there.
Many other family and friends, Glad I got to go.
I had been to visit her a couple of weeks back. The last thing she told me
was that when she died she hoped to go quick.

Mike.

I started off the week in my increasingly normal manner.

We are getting all caught up on M & R for the homeplace.. Now have new and
improved gutters all around.

The spike tooth harrow has been a long running but really fun and
interesting project. Now, with Wayne's help it is mounted in a nice
sheltered highly visible location.

Beth and her WW's were in town, J & A fed us supper one evening. Diane got
a bit of work out of some of them.

I went with Alan to the barn and gave enough helpful encouragement for him
to get the crawler running. He still has more things in mind before
actually putting it to work I believe.

Jim Foster came by. He is a remote cousin. He is also an active AT trail
maintainer and the only supplier of ramps I have left. For a period of time
he was a lay Minister. Katy knows him, maybe others, Maria?

A & C are doing their part to help me build up my RBC.

Meredith came by to entertain me with her performing child and ,I think, to
root through some stuff Daine has dug out. A fun visit.
