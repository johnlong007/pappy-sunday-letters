November 3, 2019 · Sunday

Fell Back

13 photographs·367 words

My usual practice is up at 6:30. come down and let dog and cat out, fix a
cup of coffee then park in the corner and get ready to face the day.
Monday, looking out window I saw a flock of turkeys on the edge of the
soccer field. Several Toms were strutting around just like men at the gym.
Females were ignoring them just like females at the gym.

Early in the week It was still warm here. Since the Rogue car went into the
shop for mailbox repair, we drove the '63 to track. First road trip.
Came back and put on jack stands under the canopy. Added several items on
the "to do" list.

As part of my plan to find a new home for the Ford tractor I am going to
put my old rotary mower on as a package deal. The mower needed some body
work which I have pretty well completed.
However, during the process my air compressor died. Pump locked up. Only
30 years old. A new pump is on order.

While I was working away sawing, heating and beating, GSH cleaned out the
tractor tool box. First time since new in '79. Probably parts from 20 shear
bolts was most common item.

Rejoice, pool room project tile work is complete. At least the tile people
think it is complete. Not sure if Wayne signed off.

I suppose all know Beth is the designated person in charge of the brass
kettle and operation thereof. She mustered the forces , planned and
executed an apple butter event.

She scheduled it for 7AM until 7PM. Turned out to be right on the money if
biscuits and apple butter supper were included.

I did not check the roll but know for sure family attended from far and
wide. As a bonus, Jake split the seats in to chunks . Aaron would have
thrived.
