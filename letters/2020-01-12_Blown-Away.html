January 12, 2020 · Sunday

Blown Away

12 photographs·370 words

Many , many years ago, LLLL and her mother planted white pine seedlings
along side the driveway. Too many, too close together.
Over the years the numbers have been reduced first by errant lawn mowers,
most recently by a windstorm.
Last night a brief but intense storm passed through. The top 1/2 blew out
off several of the pines but only one was uprooted.

One above is closest to house. One below is near drive entrance.

Also, a rather large Oak tree blew down. It is on our property but fell
across fence and onto neighbors property.

I do not have a chain saw big enough to handle it. Stay tuned.

There are still a lot of odds 'n ends to do to the '63. Trunk, hood and
door seals included on list. To keep it as dry as possible. I moved PU over
and brought into shop.

But moved it back out in the pretty weather to get some things done. Oil
and filter, rear shocks and good cleaning. I have been creatively avoiding
buffing as am concerned about damaging the paint. I need an expert.

Speaking of expert. Everyone recognize the expert and know the rest of the
story?

Soon it will be time to put the trim, letters and emblems back on the '63.
(After buffing). I have trim and letters from 3 cars so will be picking the
best. GSH lept at the chance to polish the letters.

The emblems remain misplaced. We must find as are almost irreplaceable. I
cannot find any advertised for sale.

The pond we worked on for Lisa is now full and overflowing. Moving on to
the next project. The Bamboo Herb planted by the pond is well along toward
taking the place.
We attacked it with loppers before and chainsaw this week.

It is going to take 2 or 3 more sessions to get the patch down to the size
she wants.

If anyone wants poles to stick beans they should contact Lisa.

It was 74 degrees here yesterday. Ice age delayed by one more day.
