Flora and Fauna

11 photographs·261 words

Wayne had to remove a downed tree from one of his TLC houses lots. What he
really wanted to do was show off his Mech. Engr. skills. Learned by osmosis.

In further skills display, he moved the Ash logs to the sawmill.

and helped convert into knotty pine lumber.

Everyone know what this fruit is?

This is my overwintered pepper.

Tomatoes were thriving until we discovered the blight had found them.

So we dug up the only two showing signs of blight and relocated. They look
pretty wilted but may survive the move.

In the finest barn door locking tradition we obtained blight spray and gave
all a through spraying to be repeated every 3 days from here to the end.

PBBD says no day is wasted if you learn something. This week, I learned box
turtles like water.
I also learned what "gaslighting " means in the modern vernacular (see
Stockholm syndrome).
and that trying to install ear plugs without removing hearing aids does not
end well.

When we came in this afternoon, 5 squirrels were under the bird feeder. I
miss Max.
Later there were three deer in raspberry patch under the walnut tree. They
moved up the hill picking apples as they went , past another berry patch
going under the cherry tree (where we got zero cherries this year) and
finished off at the blueberry bush. Sometimes I think we have overdone the
wildlife refuge.
