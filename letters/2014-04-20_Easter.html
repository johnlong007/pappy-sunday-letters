Easter

12 photographs·203 words

Some have posted Easter pictures (some have not).

​

​
I wonder what happened to the person(s) who moved to Utah. Anyone hear
anything? Perhaps that is the edge and they fell over. Not surprised we
hear nothing from California as that is part of another world.

some years back we opened a wheelchair accessible section of AT on what we
call the Osbourn Farm site. It is on top of the mountain near Pleasant
Valley. We had installed quite an elaborate entry to let hikers in and keep
cattle from getting out. Some of the posts and railings had become broken
and rotten so work day was to repair and replace.

​

​Water was running down the trail and eroding the ramp so an effort was
made to build a diversionary ramp.

​

​As best I can tell, GSH spent her week making a large silk purse.

Over the years, I have planted ramp roots here and there on the hill out
front. I do believe they are at last showing signs of some spreading.
