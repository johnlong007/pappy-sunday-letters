February 16, 2014 · Sunday

Snow News

7 photographs·487 words

As some know, I do not give up easily. My long delayed desire for a cross
country bike ride( or car tour as second choice) got a much needed boost
this week. A check/relief station is being opened in Salt Lake City.

Some other things must fall into place but every bit helps.

One thing which I need for ant serious travel plans is a house/dog/cat
sitter. If I offered some quantity of love and/or money seems like one or
more of the responsible grand children who are going to be tied to area
this summer might be interested.

We had another kind on snow this week. While waiting for it to melt I
fabricated and installed brake lines on the LBT. I tried to learn how to
make a double flair in the brake tubing by watching utube videos. It did
not work, my flared fitting leaked. I did learn that;
1. Silicone brake fluid is expensive
2. Silicone brake fluid on a concrete floor is slick.

I also learned that trying to stop leaks at a brass fitting by over
tightening a steel fitting will strip the brass threads. Fortunately,
having a parts vehicle in the drive and a willing worked who showed up in
the nick of time took care of the problem.

One of the justifications for purchasing this vehicle was to obtain a
proper fuel tank. Notice the dent? It was trying to correct some such
problem which led to the demise of the original fuel tank along with
Wayne's eyebrows.

I decided to hold off on further brake line work until a received some
proper flair training and spent Saturday taking excess play (commonly know
as "slop") out of the shift linkage. Wonder how many gear changes were made
in 500K miles.

There was no AT outing this week. But, weather is projected to be much
better this week with work scheduled for Thursday.

I made a run to Erwin to check on Herb. Tuesday with have him 1/2 way
through his radiation treatments. He looks tired but face was not as
swollen as before and not showing burned at all.

We talked about the fire engine. He said If I can round up some willing
workers we will bring it over here and get it cleaned up and checked out
this summer.

GSH has completed knitting a lovely. wool sweater which is available to a
properly appreciative person. (GSH says it is scratchy). Let her know.
