September 15, 2013 · Sunday

Fruit 'n Nuts

13 photographs·506 words

Once more, I will start off with a report on the progress with the crawler.

I will add that this week I brought in the big guns for aid and assistance.
A fellow from the tractor club spent almost 1/2 day "helping" and Dwight
spent at least a couple of hours. It is now positioned for one more roll
down the hill. If that fails, it is back to the shed and try again next
year. Or the year after. Or the year after that.

A number of family members checked in from near and far. I like that, their
lives are much more interesting than mine. I did make one discovery. I
mentioned a couple weeks back about getting new specs. It has been somewhat
disappointing that the new glasses did not help much. I found out why, my
eyelids are sagging again ! Rats.
How long since I had them jacked up 5-6-7 years? I am going to try tape or
maybe toothpicks this time.

Speaking of broke down and stove up, the hearing aids are doing fine. I was
scheduled to go back for a check-up to resolve any issues but canceled it
and told them I would reschedule if any problems arise.

AT work was fun this week. Several of the willing workers went to Grassy
Ridge and walked i about three miles doing basic renovation. That includes
all kinds of repair work.
I will include a before and after picture of a repair stretch of trail and
a water bar (made of rocks) installation.

The readers of "Hiker" magazine voted Grassy Ridge the most scenic spot on
the AT. It was somewhat of an overcast day so No spectacular pictures.
However, just a reminder for those who are far away and/or in flat places.

In case anyone is interested, there are plenty of huckleberries up there.
Also plenty of signs that the bears like huckleberries also.

While I was out having fun mowing around here GSH was busy making apple pies

And canning grape juice.

On the way back from watching Pat shoot last Saturday, we stopped at an
Amish operated store just off I-81 at exit 23 "Yoder's" I believe.
GSHpurchased some Lebanon Bologna. She has been alternating with my
BLTC sandwiches.

Mostly here at the home place the time was spent mowing getting ready to
lay by for Winter.

Some apples and grapes remain to be harvested. The fig trees have a few
figs, And Chinquapins were loaded but no one harvested (Benj!). The turkeys
do enjoy.

The plan all along has been to turn the place into a wildlife preserve. It
is about there.

Can anyone top that for the lifestyle of the dull and mundane?
