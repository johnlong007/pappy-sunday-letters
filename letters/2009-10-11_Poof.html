October 11, 2009 · Sunday

Poof

5 photographs·190 words

It would be a stretch to see how I made the world a better place this week.
But, I will try.

Progress was made on the Studebaker. Fortunately, Dwight seems to enjoy
this part of the process. The steering, brakes and fuel systems go in first.
So far. Most of the parts and pieces have come from foraging in the storage
building. Remember, I only want it to look correct from outside.

Outside activities were:
1. mowing the hillside
2. Seeding the gardens with Winter Rye
3. and a few odds and ends

No pictures of any of these but I did take a picture of the turkeys
harvesting the Rye (a bit prematurely I might note.)

Friday and Saturday I made a run to Northern Va. A cousin lives in a resort
there (Wintergreen , anyone want to go ski?)

I collected some new information about my GGF. The one who disapeared for
11 years .

Sunday, D & D came over. We worked on the Studebaker a bit then I made
sandwiches for the willing workers (and support team.)

This week we hope will be a bit more"normal".
