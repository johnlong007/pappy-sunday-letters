September 4, 2022 · Sunday

Gone Home

9 photographs·335 words

We went to her funeral in Union City. She had the event planned and paid
for. Like her life, it was well organized and efficient. I will miss her.

One of our SS class members was a high school classmate with Sis. During
class today word came in from his daughter that he has died,

In other news, Dan said David is now brother Jimmy's manager and David will
be movin Jimmy back to the home place. Stay tuned.'

We got a note from our friends in UK who visited us a few times. Andy and
Joan Langford. Joan has died some time back
We worked on the retaining basin,

Picked up rocks. I picked up rocks and threw in a sled drawn by our horse
Prince when we were clearing the place Farmer Lisa has.
Then moved up to the "37 PU with Herb as driver.
Then the '49 Dodge which I drove.
That creek bank must have been lined two feet deep in rocks.

I want to side toward the house to seed well and look good. PBBD procured a
seeding mat and rolled it out.

She also seeded the entire area. Now we need rain, time and fertilizer.

LLLL and Dale came up for baby viewing. While here, I snabbed Dale to help
with a couple of tasks. First we were able to get the '63 transmission
unjammed. The clutch is still stuck but I do have a plan.
After that, he checked the electrical. We were able to get it running but
it was not pumping gas so more work will be required. But no serious issues
so far.

A trail working friend, Ted Mowery, brought us a quantity of peppers and
tomatoes.

Max and Allison came to town for show and tell.

A picture of my tomato patch. Looks like we will have pumpkins unless the
deer get hungry.

Fall !
