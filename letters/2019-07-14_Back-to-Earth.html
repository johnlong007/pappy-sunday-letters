July 14, 2019 · Sunday

Back to Earth

10 photographs·554 words

Our SS class is mostly old folks. A lot of ailments. At the start of class
teacher goes over all the old and new aches, pains and problems people are
willing (some eager) to share. After going over the concerns teacher said
'Getting old is not for the faint of heart".

Last week for my first time I experienced a UTI. John took me to a clinic
where the young Dr. picked right up and prescribed 5 days of anti-biotics.
Monday I went in to regular Dr. here and told him I believe problem was
solved. Now, I am not so sure.
Also got a letter from Dr. Sago(skin) saying last one she removed was a
live one, It is also in the face area which I have learn ed may be serious.

Not to be outdone, we took the dog to the vet to get first serious check
up. All well except he has fleas. Seems fleas have learn ed to live with
advantage. So, for those who do not believe in evolution. Keep scratching.
We made a trip to procure dog food, cat food and people food. Spent a lot
more on the animals than the owners.

A lot of time collecting pool pump information this week.

I think it is like the great molasses tank failure we studied in
metallurgy. At conclusion of testimony the judge said "it is clear 1/2 the
experts are wrong".
The project is to be continued, stay tuned.

An invited pump expert.

Expert brought his little ole wife along.

As most people do, Andrew was having a birthday. Today I believe. I
remember well when he was born. They lived in Rocky Mount at the time. I
got in truck, went to Erwin, got Mom, took her to RM. Visited LLLL and
child in hospital and drove back to Erwin, non-stop. After she got out
Momma said "it would have been allright if you had stopped once".
Anyway, with several birds in my sights, I loaded all the pumps and motors
in the car and we made a run to L & D. Got no aid or comfort on the motors.
I wonder if Benj knows how much of a furor he has stirred up? Nurse Rebecca
undertook to get blood sample from David for testing. I am not at all sure
what was going on but am pretty sure she got her sample. Also pretty sure
she enjoyed extracting.

Wayne worked on the house gutter drainage system and in the pool room
getting the filter emptied prior to removing to make way for the tile floor.

I mowed the circle again this week. The crab apple, quince (or something)
trees are loaded along with the other apple trees.

One evening, I witnessed this blessed event on our doorpost. What is it?

I have not heard from Sis in a while so I sent a note to Martha. She said
all is well, back in church, teaching SS and driving around Union City. I
suppose the street traffic peels back like the Red Sea as they used to for
Sherrill.

Look close, she is humming "I'm a little green frog-------". We have at
least three.
