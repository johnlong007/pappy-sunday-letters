March 18, 2018 · Sunday

Continued Unchanged

18 photographs·353 words

I did not run a 1/2 marathon (although I have in the distant past). I did
not do AT work. Does helping Dave Edwards with his car count as do-gooder
even if I enjoy it?

On the other hand, GSH and I go to the same GP Dr. and go into exam room
together. Each got an OK stamp on the back side and told to come back in
six months. The nurse did tell GSH she is underweight and should gain 4
pounds. Ha ! My weight is right on target. If everything were that easy.

GSH is now as old as I am once more. We had a very nice family birthday
dinner at CB.

The big project for the week was work on the '63. Mark Coates gave me a
person to call to get the windshield installed. He came right out and
removed the old cracked windshield.

​Then we spent a lot of time prepping the site and cleaning the old seal to
install replacement windshield.

The new seat cover on one of the new seat cushions split a seam for a
couple of inches. After much hand wringing a plan has been devised, and
tested, to shrink the cushion to get enough slack to allow repair. Stay
tuned for actual repair work.

​

We spent a lot of time and effort polishing tarnished and/or pitted chrome.

​

Also spent some effort taking chrome pieces off to allow cleaning. Note
steering wheel restoration is also underway.

​
​Meanwhile The TDBG project moved right along. The wall alongside the pool
shielding the unofficial dressing room is gone and supporting the upstairs
hall and roof is gone. It has been replaced by a structure of which Dwight
would be proud. I believe it would easily support a medium size coal train
goind down the hall.

​I believe work will soon start on replacing the trusses and the front wall.
PBBD made a stack cake. Really good. Wayne did his part to demolish but I
failed to record.

​
