March 3, 2024 · Sunday

Tongue Tied.

11 photographs·303 words

Just about every week when I have lab work done they tell me "don't get
sick and don't bleed. One evening this week, my tongue had a blowout and
began to bleed profusely. Diane got the leak plugged, called NP the next
morning then dragged me in for platelets.

As I am sure you can imagine, everything around here reminds me of
Patrice.. Some things more than others. Her flowers are way up high on the
list.

Anta had a birthday. She has to wait longer than most. In turn, she gets to
have a bigger event than most. The children came in from far and wide to
help her make hay. I mentioned to John that in clearing clutter, Diane
dragged out a box of my old papers, some of which went back to my time in
Huntsville. S & J came by and I was able to give him some papers from my
work days there.

Wayne came by to aid and abet his sister in working on engine assembly for
her truck
Mark delivered the freshly painted fender for the red truck. Installation
to follow soon (I hope).

Anita's 16 birthday event at the church.

Rachel came by with A & Cs incredibly cute grandchild and her little
brother. Eliza took right after the bouncing machine.

Once more, Meredith came by to help me with the PC. Look what happened
while she was here !

Any and every day, I can walk around here, inside or out and see things
which should be done.' PBBD records then we work them off and add new ones.

We got 4 quarter logs ready at the sawmill with four more waiting.
