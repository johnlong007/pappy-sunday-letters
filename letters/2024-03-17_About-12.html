March 17, 2024 · Sunday

About 1/2

15 photographs·364 words

With a lot of help from Diane, I sawed the last of the Winter slab pile
remains into firewood length. Saturday, WW's showed up !

While Paul and nephew James were splitting the firewood into burning
size,Diane was teaching Mary how to stack wood as passed down from her
Uncle Kenneth.

She picked right up, such that it took three of us to keep her supplied.

Meanwhile, the slitters were busy splitting. Diane had started Paul with a
sledge hammer and wedge. He swung the hammer full circle, just like I have
seen his Dad swing.

Back to sawing the slab pile, I can handle the sawing pretty well but dire
not try to move around while using the saw. My feet get tangled.

The kindling end of this winter's with plenty left over in the stack that
Mary built. The firewood bay is now full.

Somewhere along the way, Beth took time and appeared interested while I
told her about the house where I grew up near Erwin. Mom left the place to
Jimmy. BTW, Dan said Jimmy has TB and has been put in isolation at the VA
in Johnson City.

One day, Sarah brought us a loaf of pumping bread and also invited us over
for dinner one evening. We went, it was very good. We also got a guided
tour of the place.

The pear tree thinks Spring has arrived. The ramps also, but, ramps can
stand a freeze, pear blossoms cannot.

Remember the smooth cheek. Monday I got hauled in for blood testing and a
lecture by the NP. This picture is Bailey getting set to install this rig
in my port.

After the sampling the NP said go to the dentist . Period. We went Tuesday
to my family dentist, he said the tooth is old, brittle, will have to come
out but it will not be easy. Signed us for a tooth surgeon. Thursday.

Patterns are sorted, organized and waiting for good homes.

Saturday evening !!!

68/4/10
