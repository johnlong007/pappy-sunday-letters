November 21, 2021 · Sunday

At Last

8 photographs·451 words

The word from Lil Sister is she passed the last pre-op test and is now
tentatively scheduled for artificial Aortic valve installation on Dec.
15th. I am glad and hope it will go as well as mine did.

As I have mentioned before, Herb liked to collect Chrysler cars. Often
quite large cars belonging to the widows of Erwin upper crust. Over the
years we have mentioned to move a few to various places, like the Kidney
foundation.
I haver had my eye on his '65 Imperial for a long time and have been
actively preparing it to bring over here for months. With Wayne's able
assistance we moved it here on Saturday. It will be a challenge as it had a
carburetor fire doing a to be determined among of under hood damage.
Even getting the doors open is proving difficult. Stay tuned.

Today I went to Perkins for breakfast. When the waitress (Connie) came to
take our order I told her she should bring me a muffin for Pat but this
time put it on my bill.
She informed me in no uncertain terms that It was from Connie for Pat and
I should tell her to get well and they miss seeing her.

I skipped the early service and came back home with the muffin. PBBD had
GSH all ready to go and we went to SS. First time in a couple of months she
has made it. We went in the social break are before SS and quite a number
of folks stopped by with encouraging words.
SS friends are right at the top of the list.

Home from ss, dressed for outdoor fun and lunch ready !

Later we went out to see the Big Boys Toys !

One of the headlights on the '37 quit working on low beam. The original
headlights had a polished reflector and a two element bayonet bulb. With a
fair amount of difficulty, I had installed modern sealed beam lights but
had cement in place. In a classic example of jumping to solution, I
disassembled the headlight rig. Then upon bench testing found the unit was
OK. Turns out a wire connector under the hood had become disconnected.
How to make a simple job difficult. Tomorrow I will put it all back
together.

Got a note from our class of '54 organizer.

We have recently lost two more classmates. Merle Lynn Hensley Woodruff
died Oct. 26, 2021. She suffered a massive stroke and never regained
consciousness. Patsy Frye Westberry also died Nov. 9, 2021. She
also suffered a massive stroke and never regained consciousness.
