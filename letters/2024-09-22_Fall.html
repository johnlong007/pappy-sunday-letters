September 22, 2024 · Sunday

Fall

12 photographs·259 words

Patrice quit driving when she could not find her way home. I assured her do
not be concerned because I would take her wherever she wanted to go.
One day when we were at Central where I was running and she was walking
laps, she got in the car, went out in the parking lot and turned several
laps. "Just wanted to see if I can still drive". That was her last drive.
With her permission, I let Alan have her car with the provision that he
keep it " running forever".

Best car ever.

Piddler on the roof. Diane had her eyes replaced and is a bit unsteady. I
am a whole lot unsteady, age and mileage.

Four trips around the world in 6 years.

Although somewhat limited in my work endurance, I still like to operate the
BobCat.

Moved the Hickory chunks across the drive and stacked them without getting
off. Diane put the cover over the wood. This wood will be for Winter of 26.

Dark glasses required. My role has been eye drops as scheduled.

We planted three tomato plants. Yellow, Red and Mr. Stripey. Mr Stipey was
late coming in but did well.

Brush thinning continued with miles to go before she rests.

The never ending joys of home M&R.

A lot of time and effort into spike tooth harrow refurbishing. Teeth are
printed. One coat on bottom. Stay tuned.
