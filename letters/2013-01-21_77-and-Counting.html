January 21, 2013 · Monday

77 ! (and Counting)

12 photographs·267 words

Reading the notes from various children and grandchildren who are
scattered far and wide makes my week seem pretty humdrum. But I can
report that not only did I pass another birthday but also had three
birthday events. I suppose someone keeps trying to make up for the
time GSH was in Dallas on my birthday and I did not hear boo from
anyone !

It snowed one day. We had been getting a lot of rain then one
afternoon it switched to big, soft snowflakes and withing a few hours
we had 5 inches of snow.

No AT work so I decided to do a quick and easy task on the '37 and
install the outside door handles. I wrestled with the task for two
days. The handle has a square shaft about 3 inches long which goes
into a blind hole. I could not get it to work. I finally gave up and
cut a hole in the inner door panel. Still could not get it to work
Then I removed the entire door latch mechanism and was able to discern
the problem. The handles are not correct for this vehicle. They are
about 1/2 inch too short. I cut the end off a square shank screwdriver
to use. It works quit well but is a bit ugly.

A select group traveled to Union City to visit relatives. While there
we toured the new theme park which is under construction. Also toured
a M'cycle facility.

On the way home we made a stop in Dechard to burn brush and build a footlog.

All is well.
