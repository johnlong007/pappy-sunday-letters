May 16, 2021 · Sunday

Mundane

10 photographs·392 words

Strikes my funny bone when I get an update which starts out with "nothing
-------". My father used to say "you boys don't know what tired is". I
feel the same way you kids don't know what' nothing going on around here
is".

Elsewhere, Steven graduated with a masters in math. He sent a link for us
to study his Thesis. GSH, the cat and the dog were helping me wade through
with varying degrees of interest.

It seems Alan drove off and left the LBC in Wisconsin. Wayne has been
conscripted to go procure the car and perhaps other items. He has been
doing some prep work on the truck. GSH likes to supervise.

Sarah has asked to borrow the engine hoist. Naturally, it was stored behind
other items. It did provide a reason to start the '63. Fired right up, sure
sound good. Also required moving the '57 out a bit but just moved it the
old fashioned way.

Progress ! Herb's Imperial had been left parked in one spot for at least
five years. Tires had sunk into the ground, cracked the sidewalls and
allowing the wheels to be rusted. I have wrestled with tires and wheels for
several weeks off and on. Now, the car has freed brakes, inflated tires and
securely attached wheels. Tractor driver Lisa dragged it out into the open
area where it can be accessed with a car trailer . By and by.

I understand it had a carburettor fire. I am really interested in
determining the damage and what will be required to bring the engine back
to life. By and by.

Trimming the junipers at PBBD's house continued. Just a bit more work
remains but will require use of the Pruning pole.

We had visitors from the frozen North today. They were able (and willing)
to watch their home church service.

After fried potatoes and ramps for lunch, the crew ventured out to
check progress on truck prepping.

A bit of progress on the mower. The new starter arrived to replace the one
I burned to a crisp. Wayne installed it and found it works fine. But, the
new tie rod end has not arrived so the mower remains out of commission.r
