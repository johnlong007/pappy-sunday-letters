May 8, 2016 · Sunday

Mind Over Matter

8 photographs·345 words

Only two funerals this week and each person older than I ! May have a few
miles left after all.

No work on the fire engine this week. Dwight is still waiting to get parts
for second try on the yoke repair. So, I might as well wait to finish the
brakes.

Some interesting events on the '63. I have about finished body work and
sanding the blue paint off the exterior. Have not even started cleaning
inside engine compartment or trunk. Those are going to be difficult.

But, for some unknown reason, I decided to put the top up. After prolonged
struggles involving the top switch and filling the fluid reservoir, the
motor ran and the pump pumped but the top did not go up. A quick
look revealed the reason, the fluid was being pumped out in the floor of
the car. Pulling the cylinders I found each had corroded. Then I retrieved
the parts car cylinders and found the cylinders intact but the piston rods
corroded. I await Divine guidance. Or educated opinions. Or random
suggestions.

This was the GGC week. Beth came by and helped with lawn mowing. With such
a good start, I was able to get the mowing done for this week. But, it does
not stay done

Later in the week, we made a trip to hear Beth (and others) in concert. A
reception after the concert to prepare us for the ride home. En route down,
we stopped in Erwin to check on Lisa . She is taking to farming like a pro.
She should get a video company to come capture the story.

Friday, we went back to work on the AT. All the essentials were in place.
Cold, wet and miserable. Everyone needs to drive up and check the place
out. Easy drive, easy walk and great views. Unless the fog and rain have
moved in. Always like some mountain views for those who can only remember.

Today was/is Mother's day. If your Mom is alive, be grateful, if not, be
grateful for the memories.
