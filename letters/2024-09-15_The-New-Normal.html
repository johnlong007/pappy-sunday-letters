The New Normal

14 photographs·232 words

Lest we forget

Monday morning.

Worked a lot on new skill development this week.. In her spare time, she
had the lens replaced in one eye. To be continued.

Meanwhile, I worked on solving the world's problems.

I did get a chance to play a bit, No front view at all. Drive by feel.
The hickory log was in the way of mowing . Bobcat would carry but not lift
it.

when I was young, chinquapins were common. Chestnuts were still available
but only at higher elevation. We sometimes stopped on top of the iron
mountain gap on our way to see Granny Buchanan . We would collect large
quantities of chestnuts.

The smaller hickory log is in progress, The sawyer decided it is really
tough and closed up shop to get a new freshly sharpened blade.

At the Friday night feast (Saturday this week) we decided our burgers were
better than the world famous restaurant.

Main project was the spike tooth harrow.. Significant progress was made.

We go to the track everyday. Friday night a pay per view football game was
underway so we walked all the way around the property. The highlight was
finding a penny. Patrice would have beeen delighted.
