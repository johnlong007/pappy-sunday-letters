April 12, 2020 · Sunday

He is Risen

17 photographs·538 words

It has been a very busy week here in seclusionville.

Quite a number of my Easter RSVP. I may decide to be proud after all.

I finally got a chance to talk with my neighbor Albert about the tree which
fell onto his property. As I expected he said to go ahead and do as I saw
fit.

My current plan is to rig a pulley system and drag it back. I have one
pulley block and knew Herb had another. Made a trip to Erwin and did find
it. While there, Lisa took a picture.

A big poplar tree fell recently. Up in the hill in the woods where we have
had previous experience, right Benj? This time I dragged out before sawing
into long logs which are now safely ensconced in bush pond, log yard.

I am also glad to report I had a socially responsible WW show up to cut my
hair. While here, she more or less offered to help with cleaning downed
pines from the hill out front.

Thinking the fireplace season is over, we worked to neaten and consolidate
the various woodpiles. Under the last piece of wood we found a resident.
Most be a relative of the one Wayne moved from house recently.
While mowing up near grapevines I saw another much larger but managed to
stop without causing bodily harm.

News Flash !
It fired right up ! If you will notice in second picture the fan is a blur.
No noise, no smoke, smoothed right out. If David decided the Studebaker is
more than he can digest, I may offer a swap. The '37 is a lot simpler.

Traditions ! Go watch Fiddler on the Roof.
Two here 1. dyeing eggs 2. biting ears from stale MM bunnies

We made a run to Dennis Cove to check on the most assessable ramp site of
which I am aware. Found few, small and picked over.
In a couple or 3 more weeks will have to make a run to the secret patch.

Daryl Hyder, Friend from church had 90the BD Friday. His daughters moved
him out on the front lawn and invited people to come by and wave. We did.
I read in WSJ that old men with heart issues and high blood pressure are 4X
as likely to die from THE virus as same age but without those issues. Looks
like I am in the highest endangered species group.

After Albert said go ahead on log, I went out his side to look it over. WW
went along.

The plan is put roller poles under it,rig the pulley blocks with 100' of
cable (10 feet apart will take 60 feet) and jerk it right out. Stay tuned.

Several things to note in this picture.
1. no down pines. Last time that occurred was in Summer camp years ago. I
think John led that effort.
2. Groundhog hostel.

Easter dinner must proceed.
But, with carefully calculated social distancing.

I suppose this is our official Easter picture.
