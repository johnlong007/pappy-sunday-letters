February 27, 2011 · Sunday

Continuation

6 photographs·503 words

When I retired I made a 10 year plan of how I wanted to spend my time,
things to do, places to go. It went well. I checked off most, but not all, the
items on the 10 year plan.
Since I was still somewhat active after the 10 years, I made a 5 year
extension and am now 1/2 way through it.
One of the items on both lists was to bike across the US. The problem was
and is, I cannot (as in unwilling) do it by myself. I believe I need a sag
wagon driver and a person to ride along with me. I figure about 7 weeks
would be required. If I cannot get it put together this year, I intend to give
up as gracefully as I can manage.

Two more weeks before DST and work stops on the silk purse project. This
week, after many mis-steps I got windshield wipers installed. The electrical
man has not hooked up the wiper motor but there are no plans to get out in
the rain. Just in case, I will rain-X the windshield before venturing
forth.

I also finished installing paneling on the interior walls. GSH has offered
to do the upholstery. But, if I could find a shop willing to do the work,
I would be interested.

AT work was more of the same. Working with Paul Benfield and Richard
Carter to clear rocks from the trail. These are serious rocks that the original
trail builders thought were too tough to remove.We went to the maintainers
social event!I got another patch for my collection.

For all those who enjoy using George's dock, You owe me one.

Closer to home, the tractor club people came after the Farmall "C". During
Summer Camp we took one wheel from it and installed on the Case tractor. I
sold it with the stipulation that I kept the other tire and wheel. They
took it with the wheel mounted but brought it back the next day. I would like
to get the wheel mounted and the case running and painted during camp this
year.

Spring is almost upon us ! Birds are singing and flowers are blooming. I
checked the ramps yesterday, they are up about 1/2 inch. It is also the time
for tending fruit trees. I did the lime-sulphur spray of about everything
(including me and the dogs) yesterday.

The VW has always had some quirky warning lights. We got a recall notice to
bring it in to check the glo plugs and the control module. They replaced
all the glo plugs and the module. So far, no quirky warning light. I was
impressed as it has 130,000 miles.

The dogs are coming right along. I went up on the hill to start trimming a
downed tree yesterday. Yappy went along and stayed in the vicinity the
whole time I was out. Later I took her for a tractor ride. I believe she will
be a pretty good dog by and by.
