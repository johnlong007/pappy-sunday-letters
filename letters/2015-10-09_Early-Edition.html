October 9, 2015 · Friday

Early Edition

6 photographs·199 words

About 2PM today I will be heading out for Brazil. Should be home next
Saturday evening. Til then, GSH and the animals will be in charge here.

The big event for the week was taking the dogs with us and going to visit
Linda and Rebbecca. We admired her animals, got her garden ready to seed
and got the go cart running. But not in operation.

On Wednesday, Suz took Herb to Vanderbilt. He told me the experts will
have a meeting of their expert minds and come up with a recommendation for
course of action by early next week.

The VW has a shake or shimmy during braking. I have replaced brake rotors
but it has only gotten worse. I took the front brake assembly on each whell
apart and greased all metal rubbing parts. It did not help a bit. What next?

For the bummer of the week, GSH made an apple pie for me to take to Herb.
When I got it out of the car, I dropped it. He did report that his big dog
found the remnants to be tasty.

There is a Facebook page for the Brazil trip.CHCC 2015 Brazil Trip

Stay tuned.
