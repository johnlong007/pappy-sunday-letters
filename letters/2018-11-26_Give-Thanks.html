November 26, 2018 · Monday

Give Thanks

12 photographs·241 words

Carl had an AT outing this week but I did not make it.

It looked like the signs might be right to put a coat of primer on the '63.
So, I decided to give it one more going over with fine grid sanding.
Decided there is still a lot of work to be done.

The big event for the week was Thanksgiving. We made a run to Linda's via
Beth's place. Got to see the "old" and new.

Then on to Dechard to feast and visit.

I sure am glad we live in a part of the country where God and Country are
still Important. Adam is winding up at West Point he seems quite pleased
with events.

Chelsea has finished her military career. Still has a few souvenirs , Aaron
is on active duty but not getting shot at as much now.

I believe they have decided this one is also a keeper.

Thanksgiving dinner at Linda's was great in many ways.

Some traveled a long ways to join in and to give more family members the
opportunity.

I have not seen any visual proof but I suppose Katy and Maria prepared a
Utah feast.

Saturday was the big event at Diane's.

Today, we decided it was time to admit Winter is coming.
