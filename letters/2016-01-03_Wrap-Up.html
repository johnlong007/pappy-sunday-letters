January 3, 2016 · Sunday

Wrap Up

9 photographs·342 words

I was going to compile a long ,thoughtful, meaningful summary of the year.
But, I am running late and want to watch the football game so short and
sweet.

Good news is that the tractor is back in operation.

If you look at the picture between the two fuel filters you can see a bolt
sticking up. There was a vent bolt in that location but it was stripped so
I replaced with a bolt. Turns out I had screwed it in too far blocking the
fuel flow. At least three days of frustration. But, back in operation.

In even more exciting news perhaps, the LBT has a coating of primer. Now it
is time to put it away until next Summer when (we hope) it will get a paint
job. Along with the '37 pu perhaps.

No progress on the fire engine. Dwight has the worn part in for repair. I
tried to drag the FE back into the barn but that did not go well. Stay
tuned.

The Fitbit is working great. To make sure GSH gets her steps, we have been
going to the track. This has brought to my attention just how slow my
"running" had become. Now, I am really working on trying to pick up the
pace.

One night, we went to an ETSU ball game with Dave and Carol. It was a fun
outing. The thing which most impressed me was the slim. trim cheerleaders.
Did not realize there are that many slim girls in East Tn.

New Years Day was fun, started out here then moved to D & D.

For the first time in several weeks, I went out for AT work. The work was
on the steps going up the hill from Laurel Falls. While we were there
working a group of intrepid hikers came through. This week we are going
back to the same place in case any more hikers are interested.

Checked in on Herb several times this week. He is in JC Medical Center and
is not doing well.
