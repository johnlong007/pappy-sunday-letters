August 8, 2010 · Sunday

Funny World

10 photographs·464 words

Monday is do-gooder day. Larry Miller asked me to help him remove a downed
tree on the property of one of the Friday Hikers.She lives in Va. I told
Dave and we all agreed to meet at McD's at 8 AM Monday.
Larry wanted to drive so we left Dave's truck here.
Dave told Larry we would follow him as did not know the way. I told him not
to run off and leave me.
We went out on I-81 heading North. Larry took off. I drove at 72 and never
saw him.We went about 30 miles, turned around and came home. Stay
tuned,there may be a sequel.

AT work Tuesday went better. Seven of the old codgers went back to finish
the access road clearing started last week. Management decided a large stump
in the road needed to be removed. It took about three hours but we got it.

Acting upon Diane's recommendation, we procurred a sqeeky toy for the new
dog. So far, I have taken more pictures of this dog than I did of Alan when
he was cute.

Another big event for the week was moving tractors back into the dry. Some
we removed during Summer Camp are still outside.
With the help of GSH the MM is back inside. The Farmall H started right up
so I could drive it into the barn. But, when I switched it to Kerosene, it
was putting out a lot of white smoke and developed little power. It appears
I am going to have to make it run hotter but will have to get the tenp
gage in working order first. Stay tuned.

Friday night was drag race car practice night. You will remember (after I
remind you) that the target is a 5 second 1/8 mile.
Friday night, Dwight, Pat, Jack (number 2 advisor after Dwight) and a full
complement of fans and admirers, gathered to watch.

A lot of pondering is mandated.

Notice the little spitting image of PJ.

Final pass was 5.04 seconds and 144+ MPH. All were delighted and plan to go
to the real competition next Saturday night. No more practice, now the
real thing.

Saturday, W&L left their kids here for a day of fun and frolic (by W&L,
not the kids). In addition, Benj came over to learn to drive before school
driver training starts tomorrow.

The kids did various things, picked 5 gallons of crab apples which we all
hope will reappear as crab apple jelly.
They also picked about three gallons of beans.
The big endeavor was cleaning on the Case SC in preparation for painting .
No painting yet, no pictures.

It looks like a fine grape year, They are almost ripe, not sure what is to
be done with the harvest. Suggestions?
