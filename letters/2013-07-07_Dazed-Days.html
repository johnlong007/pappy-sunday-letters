July 7, 2013 · Sunday

Dazed Days

15 photographs·656 words

The trip to the Philippines wore me down. It was almost two days portal to
portal coming and going with a 12 hour time shift. That must be the reason
I have been in a fog all week. Could not have anything to do with getting
old.

I forgot to include a picture of my knic knacks. Anyone know what they are?

I did go out to work on the AT one day. Of all the things we do my least
favorite is mowing weeds. The least favorite of weed mowing is in the
wilderness areas where no power tools are allowed. So, I spent the day
mowing with my least favorite mowing tool a swing scythe.

Looking out over the lake I was surprised to note how much the hills were I
had been in the Philippines last week resembled the hills here.

On the home front I spent a lot of time wrestling with the mower. The power
steering has quit. I now know how to get to the steering box but decided to
not take it out just now. Once it is removed the unit will be useless until
replaced. Now, it is just difficult to steer.

Fun things happened on the 4th.

Willing workers showed up to get the '37 out and take it for a spin. It did
fine but ran a bit rough. I hope to run it some more this week to see if it
will smooth out. It appears that in spite of my best efforts (our best is
none too good) the brake master cylinder leaks and truck has no brakes.
That was a concern to Benj causing him to decline the opportunity to drive
it. Maybe this week now that he has adjusted the emergency brake.

Of course, no 4th would be complete without the Blountville parade. Since
many have marched in the parade with the Central band, I will include a
picture of the current edition.

Also, no 4th parade would be complete without the local politicians !

The day was completed with grilled brots at B & L's.

The delayed 4th celebration was at the hillside on Saturday.

Although it was a bright and sunny day, the children insisted on fireworks:

John lit something off, hid behind me while i got scorched by an errant
whirlygig.

As all should know by now, Benj is soon going off to Texas A & M. I hope he
will go through the Corps program. In hopes of encouraging him, I gave him
a run through of the manual of arms and told him he can have my M1
Garandto take along.

Notice the casing flying through the air. I believe he likes the weapon.

Dwight has purchased a pontoon boat. Maybe even a ship. I can hardly wait
for the floating parties to begin.

Dwight made ribs for the event. The ribs were enjoyed by all.

My many health plagued friend Dave Dunham lives out in the Reservoir Road
direction so we stopped by to check on him on the way home. He has been
having back problems and has purchased a machine to allow him to hang down
side up.

In other news, The Raspberries are going into full swing. I have plenty of
potatoes in the garden free to good home . Corn should be ready soon. I
pulled and delivered a quantity of beets to Meredith but not sure of the
disposition (Meredith or the beets).

I have got a note from Wayne in Alaska (Going great now) but have not heard
anything from Amara.
