August 4, 2024 · Sunday

9 Months

16 photographs·313 words

It is important and satisfying to me to have Patricia's things go
where they are wanted and will have special meaning. Diane has been working
on soft goods for some time, this week we took a big step forward by
placing some significant items in the hands of our children. There are
quite a number of items, large and small, to be parceled out. Stay tuned.

The smartest thing I did this week was define the problem before
implementing the solution for the Bobcat hydraulic leaks.

Fortunately, a hydraulic tubing expert arrived and proved to be a willing
worker.

Hard to believe but this was my first time to meet Mettilynn and the first
time for Zack to meet me. I was delighted with the child and his Mom.

Bring 'em up right, even to the second and third generation.

Wayne procured replacement hoses and Alan lept at the chance to install.

I took it for a test run by stirring the compost pile. It seems to be
composting nicely.

For another good deed, Alan gave a tutorial on proper use of a wood plane.
His ace student picked right up on it.

The rain slacked off long enough for me to do a shakedown on the lawn mower.

The feast or famine rain has led to blossom end blight in the tomato crop.

Just in case the tomatoes get almost ripe, the deer are waiting.

The flowers popped right through the gravel. Our little garden is in the
foreground. The dense ,green patch in center is the flowers from the seed
sent to us in appreciation of Patricia's donation.

Grapes are starting to get ripe.

Finches, hummingbird..
