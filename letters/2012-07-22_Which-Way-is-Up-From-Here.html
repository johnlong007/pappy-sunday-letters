July 22, 2012 · Sunday

Which Way is Up From Here ?

7 photographs·390 words

Some unnamed person or persons are rejoicing in Mudville this week. Not
one, but two loads of stuff went out never to return. Wayne took a load of
about 50 tires to be dispatched into some worthy, stimulating sinkhole (at
a nominal charge of $86). He also removed a fair size load of scrap metal
for which he received some nominal reward. I had my camera with me to
record the historic event but got so excited I failed to take a picture.

Perhaps less exciting, Tuesday for AT work day the willing workers (plus
GSH) went back up on Carver's Gap to inspect the gravel project and to
decide where to install steps and water bars at a special work session next
weekend. I suppose I could try to recruit some willing workers from Katy's
event but that might be mistake.

Remember the giant Oak log? I had sawed it into two big saw logs. The base
is large and straight but with some rot showing. The second log large and
straight but had been shorn of large limbs. With the help of one willing
worker the second log is now in firewood length. The base log has had the
defective part removed. It is now about 34 inches in diameter and 12' in
length. If no one shows up wanting to take it to the sawmill, it will also
turn into firewood very soon. Probably next week.

As most will know, LLLL, got a wake up call this week. I decided to go
check on her and her goats. I can send a picture of the goats upon request.
After I got back home, I discovered Adam and David had come along with me.
I can see that wood being split ! Maybe some others will decide they need
meaningful work in the great outdoors?

While waiting to get started tomorrow, David decided to jump in front of
Meredith in the "teach me to knit" line. We shall see.

Saturday, a group of old folks from church went to Knoxville for the paddle
wheel boat river ride. It was fun. I would recommend to those who have not
tried it.

We rode down and back with our new (and improved) minister. Turns out he is
the grandson of the man who was our high school principal at UCHS.
