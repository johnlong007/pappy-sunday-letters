Walnuts Galore

10 photographs·248 words

PBBD is talking about going into walnut manufacturing but needs a marketing
agent. Experience has taught that the walnut must be thoroughly cleaned in
order to cure properly.

A lot of variation in the harvest this year. I assume everyone looked up
the meaning of the subject last week ? If not, do. One of the trees up on
the hill above the grape vines bore well. I took GSH and a bucket to
collect the harvest. She went on a sit down strike.

Farmer Lisa has been gathering nuts also. . I mentioned that the easiest
method for hull removal is to spread the walnuts in the grass and drive
over them,. Stay tuned

The local leaves are lovely on the trees, less so on the roof and on the
lawn.

It seems the blight season has abated and the tomato plants are thriving.
Our best return on investment has been the pumpings. Paid 15cents for a
pack of seed and harvested 3 punkins.

Up until today, we had harvested one tomato.

Not only do deer and groundhogs like acorns,

Sunday morning breakfast at Perkins. Connie was back from vacation. Note
the to go box. A muffin just like the one David has.

To Erwin to give Aunt Flo a first hand report on trip to see Sis off and to
pick up the walnuts already mentioned.
