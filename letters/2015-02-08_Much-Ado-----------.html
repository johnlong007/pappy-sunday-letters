February 8, 2015 · Sunday

Much Ado ----------

8 photographs·342 words

This week GSH used up a bunch of time on her silk purse project. I, on the
other hand, spent my time chasing a wild goose (gooses?). For some reason,
I decided the LBT needed a matched set of wheels. Now, it has various odds
and ends selected solely based on the trait of staying inflated. So, I took
me down to the barn and brought back a load of tires and wheels.
Then I spent a lot of time breaking them down and repairing slow leaks. I
now have a quantity of tires, wheels and trash ,metal and rubber.

It may be of interest to know that I am doing a fair amount of meal
preparation. GSH gets so wrapped up in her projects that she goes right
along past meal time. I know when I get back from running around (11:30) @
12 it is time for lunch. Fortunately, we have the makings to keep it
simple.

One day, I went to the indoor shooting range with a couple of friends. She
is retired military and has quite a collection of pieces to utilize. She
gave me a checkout in the LC9-ct. I can shoot it very well but have a
difficult time operating it because of my poorly functioning hands. It was
fun.

AT work was at Chestoa. A section of trail quite near the RR track tends to
be submerged in water after any significant rainfall. We relocated about
150 feet over a few feet closer to the RR track and a few feet up on the
grade.

Saturday, we sponsored a father/son (and willing worker) togetherness day
involving putting new brake hardware on the '53 Plymouth.

Today we made a run to Erwin so I could show GSH the trail relocation and
we could drop in on Herb. It worked out well as one of his neighbors (male)
showed up with a cake while we were there.

His open wound is healing well.

I did get a note from Sis. She lives.

Anyone beat that for exciting?
