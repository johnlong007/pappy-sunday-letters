Fwd: Fw: See Historic Blountville's exciting events June 17-20!

0 photographs·626 words

June 10, 2010

Sullivan County Civil War Days
will begin with a kickoff event entitled "Call
to Arms" at 7 p.m. on Thursday, June 17, 2010 at the Sullivan County
Courthouse. The living history event is hosted by the Sullivan County
Historical and Genealogical Society. Saturday's festivities will host a
series of events from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. in downtown Blountville including
military drills; period music; Campfire Ghost Stories by Stacey Allen McGee
of Appalachian Ghost Walks; "Tales of the Civil War" by Dr. Nancy Hamblen
Acuff; "Sullivan County and the Civil War", by Historian Bud Phillips;
African-American historian and activist H. K. Edgerton; David Chaltas who
will portray General Robert E. Lee; the 50th Virginia Regimental Band;
Period Sutler with wares; Re-enactment on the Great Stage Road in downtown
Blountville; tours of the Old Deery Inn and Museum and more. Sunday's events
will include an old-time camp-meeting worship service at 10 a.m. and a
re-enactment at 1 p.m.

Arrive with an appetite for history and good
food! "Raggedy Ann's" will have breakfast items on hand for purchase, and
"Just the Good Stuff" will offer pulled pork barbeque, French Fries, Cole
slaw, chicken tenders, and Premium hand-dipped Italian ice in 8-plus
flavors.

Sullivan County Civil War Days is sponsored by
the Sons of the Confederate Veterans and the Sullivan County Department of
Archives and Tourism in conjunction with:
a.. Vaughn's Brigade, East TN Sons of
Confederate Veterans
b.. Lt. Robert D. Powell Camp #1817, Sons of
Confederate Veterans
c.. Col. John S. Mosby Camp #1409, Sons of
Confederate Veterans
d.. James Keeling Camp #52, Sons of
Confederate Veterans
e.. Gen Alfred E. Jackson Camp #2159, Sons
of Confederate Veterans
f.. 19th TN Volunteer Infantry Regiment, Co.
B Re-enactors
g.. 12th TN Cavalry Battalion, Co. B
Re-enactors
h.. 50th VA Infantry Regiment, Co. H
Re-enactors
i.. 59th TN Infantry, Co. F Re-enactors
j.. 1st East Tennessee Artillery
k.. Sullivan County Historical and
Genealogical Society
l.. Sullivan County Historic Preservation
Association

The general public is cordially invited to
this special event which is sure to be enjoyed by young and old alike!
There is no charge for this educational event. For further information,
please call (423) 323-4660, or visit historicsullivan.com for complete
details.

17th Annual Sullivan County Antique Tractor
Show

Featuring International Harvester, McCormick
Deering & Farmall Tractors, Engines & Equipment!Sponsored by: Sullivan
County Antique Tractor and Engine Association

Wanna see some old-time tractors? The grounds
of Blountville Middle School will be the scene of the 17th Annual Sullivan
County Antique Tractor and Engine Show beginning Friday, June 18 continuing
into Saturday, June 19. Registration of Tractors & Engines will be from 8
a.m. to 8 p.m. on Friday, with an Antique Tractor Parade beginning at 1:30
p.m. Friday's events will also include working demonstrations of engines
from the past. Satuday's events will begin with an Antique Tractor parade
at 10 a.m., followed by a Kiddie Pedal Tractor Pull & Race at 11:00 a.m.
Other event on Saturday will include Tractor Olympics (for men, women and
children); Ransom McCoy Band & Stoney Creek Cloggers; Antique Tractor Awards
; and demonstrations throughout the day. The event will include craft
vendors, food vendors, parts vendors, antique tractors and hit-and-miss
engines, antique garden tractors, antique cars and trucks, farm-related
items, homemade ice cream and toy tractor vendors.

While in historic Blountville for the weekend,
be sure to include lots of time for Sullivan County Civil War Days taking
place in the historic district of downtown Blountville!
