August 16, 2020 · Sunday

Churched !

12 photographs·432 words

Not really, does anyone know what it means to be "churched" ? I never knew
of it happening at Shallow Ford but was led to believe it had occurred. Sis
might know.

I our case it just means we went back to live service at our church today.
We did ignore the rules but no one seemed to mind. SS teacher said SS will
resume in two weeks. Masks unless one has a "good reason". Ha !

PBBD usually comes over after work Tuesday to tend to the ole folks at
home. This time, she whipped out dried beef gravy and real biscuits with
imported biscuit mix. Not many meals better than B & G with real tomatoes.

After we get a couple more family members settle in locally we may be to
eat out every night. We went to Long's Thursday night and after supper
waddled out on the porch to see a rainbow over Blountville.

GSH usually watches Star Trek from 8-10 each week night. Then, from 10-11 I
catch up on things. The pets help me.

One or more deer visit our apple trees about every night and sometimes in
the morning. Ellis Road now has a "deer crossing" sign posted.

My big objective for the week was get the dead pine down. Did not get it
done, maybe this week. I cut the limbs as far up as I was willing to go,
actually a bit more as do not like working from a ladder. Rule 2 is plan
your escape route, hard to do while up a ladder.
Now I am debating using Bobcat bucket to go higher on the limbs or go ahead
and cut tree as is. Stay tuned.

I got the car in for 60K routine maintenance. Still good on tires and
brakes after so many miles. LLLL did well on her car selection.
Did a bit more work on the '37 nose. Ready to go for a spin as soon as I
can get the '63 out of the way.

Sunday dinner ! Our tomatoes are coming along but slow. The one you see
here was poached from Laura.

Did a fair amount of mowing this week. While I had a willing worker
available, we consolidated the 5 woodpiles into four. I still have no
plan as to disposal. I would like to relocate and stack neatly. Need help,
quite a bit of help.

Dog loves to tree squirrels.
