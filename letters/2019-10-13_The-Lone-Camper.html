October 13, 2019 · Sunday

The Lone Camper

14 photographs·312 words

David got to drive the Studebaker when he visited last Summer. I believe
that brought him back this year. The first project we undertook was
clearing wheels and tires from car storage bldg.
Came across an original "53 Studebaker tire and wheel. Guess we can use for
a spare.

Loaded most, but not all, the tires and wheels and moved to Ag bldg. More
work is needed.

Adam was sent to retrieve David (way before we worked through the list).
Studebaker fired right up after sitting for over a year but hyd clutch had
bled down. Took some effort to get it to function properly.

Did not get much work out of Adam but GM seemed to appreciate his aid and
comfort.

Just in the nick of time, more willing workers showed up !

I spent the rest of the week in shuffling car parts.
We have several Hemi engines. Three are partially disassembled. However, I
believe most of major parts are in hand.

Also have an extensive transmission collection. The one without lid is the
original for the '63. It ate first gear. Replaced with a unit from a '62
from Housers junk yard.

A place popped up on my right arm. PBBD sent a picture to Dr. Sago who said
not a good one come in to get it removed.

I did.

Turns out I had a matched set on my arms , one more on my neck and a bonus
pick on my back. The ones on my arm were new and different. They rise up
and erupt, painful.

Sunday dinner

Sunday supper. In honor of Christopher being back in town. He and Charlie
spent the evening catching up I think.
