April 26, 2009 · Sunday

Much Ado

10 photographs·523 words

I think Mini is a good name for A small female horselet.

Ramps, Monday Herb and I went to the Iron Mountain Gap, walked North on the
AT for about a mile and harvested ramps (in the rain) .
Latter in the week, I took Dave Dunham to Dennis Cove and harvested ramps
so he could take a mess to his dad who lives in Ohio

We got back into the do-gooder business by replacing shingles on a roof for
a lady from church. She two children, one a very young baby. Her husband
recently ditched her so she is trying to get the house ready for sale. We
have a bit of a backlog of work but, as noted, Dave has gone to Ohio to visit
his parents. His Dad is in the short rows.
Question, do all the kids know what "in the short rows" means and where it
came from? Another question, how many of the kids know the meaning and
origin of "the handwriting on the wall"?

Another big event for the week was the Cletrac (Diane, note spelling, a
contraction of The Cleveland Tractor Company).
It is now, once more, running well. The spark is created by a "magneto". It
is a device which has a coil and internal magnets which, when rotating
create a spark it just the right instant to fire the plugs .Getting it
installed correctly is quite tricky as it is spring loaded and wants to rotate.
Apparently it was installed one notch off as it required wiring the spark
plugs one position early to make it run.

The Central High Prom was Friday night. Kenneth asked if he could use the
Alfa to square his date in an appropriate style. Getting it in operation was
a three step process:
1. Dwight
2. Tags
3. Insurance
I told him he could drive it this summer until school time if he so
desired. I also told him to be prepared for some unforeseen events.
After he left, I told GSH I thought would be a sight if he and date, all
dressed up, had to get out and push it out of the road. I have not heard a
report, with or without pictures,

AT work was fun. Bob had captured a bunch of through hikers, too many for
the work we had planned. So, Dave and I went off with Kim and refurbished a
stretch of trail. We worked hard and did good.

Thursday and Friday I worked on the registration desk at the Gray old
tractor show. The number of full size tractors was down (like last year) but the
number of small tractors and amount of "stuff" was up quite a bit. I took
almost no pictures and none worth sending.

Today was music day. GSH played at Baptist church this morning, Played
with Kingsport Community Band at Natural Tunnel this afternoon then back to
First Baptist this evening. All good programs. Attendance at the evening
program was disappointing considering the quality of the program.
