February 1, 2015 · Sunday

Due Cause

9 photographs·512 words

Many have heard me and other members of the family refer to "Dr. Sago". I
have been going with Herb on various medical sojourns sort of being his
Aaron. This week, I went with him for a visit with Dr. Sago, and, with her
permission, I took a picture.
He had brought a copy of all his biopsy results, after looking the whole
packet over she put in a call to the people at Vanderbilt to clarify just
what is going to happen and when it is to take place. Current schedule is
Feb. 11 for pre-op and Feb. 25 for operation.

The trip to Dr. was on same day as AT work so I missed that. The crew
worked at Chestoa. I just got a note saying this week will be back to the
same place this Wed. So, unless something more important come up I should
have pictures for next edition.

All I heard from my Lil' sister is that she is in a tizzy getting settled
in to her new domicile. I should think if anyone wants to send her a kind
word it would be appreciated. Not everyone has an 85 year old relative .

I did get one good deed in this week. I have mentioned Pat Loven several
times. He has become quite active in AT work which is good as Dave Dunhan
has dropped out. Anyway, Pat is not a certified sawyer. He asked me to come
advise and consent for him to cut a dead tree located in the corner of his
house lot. He did not want the tree to fall backward and smash a car on the
road. Falling it in the desired direction it needed to not smash his fence.
Fortunately the tree was pretty vertical. Seldom have you seen such a proud
woodsman.

Chain saw certification for AT work requires refresher each year and
re-testing each three years. I have been chief sawyer for about 10 years
and will be coming up time to re up this year. I told Carl I believe it is
time to step aside and told Pat it is time he stepped up. So, my intent is
to let my certification lapse.

At home her in River City, Wayne came over and finished the clutch
installation in the Miata (also giving up my mechanic certification). It is
now back on the road.

He also came back with Jonathan in tow. Jonathan wants to emulate Charlie
and bring an old car back to life. We shall see.

I worked on front and rear bumper for the LBT most all week. Each are beat
into bloody submission, primed and installed.

While she was not being a mechanics aide, GSH was engaged in another silk
purse project. People are going to come from miles around to view this
project when completed.

In case anyone is wondering about the lions and the lambs in our household.

I hear indirect reports of various family members doing various exciting
things. I need to keep searching for the missing link I suppose.
