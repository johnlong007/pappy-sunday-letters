May 19, 2013 · Sunday

Rejoice

8 photographs·413 words

Last week, Kenneth and Christopher graduated from college. This week, Maria
graduated from Central HS.
I have heard reports more will be graduating in the weeks to come. I am
glad I get to share.

A pretty normal week for me.
Tuesday we delivered a LOT of handtools to a site quite near the AT for the
work crews coming on on Sunday and Monday.

We also did a bit of trail rehab work while we were there. These folks are
carrying a long locust to be a side log.

Benjamin was in his final band concert at DB. Unlike the Central concert
where I failed to get a good picture of Maria, I got a pretty good shot
here. Perhaps an eagle eye can spot him?

Spare time activities were mowing and taking Dwight to Seymour. But, since
the home place looks about like it did after the last mowing and I did not
stay for the Friday night races, I have no pictures of either.

However ! I did get to sneak a peek at the newest family member !

On a less joyous note, after hours and days of work I finally got the
drivers door window installed and functioning in the '37. The last act was
to install the inner retaining frame around the glass. After installing it,
I went back to admire my handiwork one more time.

The glass had cracked !

Turns out the top of the door is bowed out about 1/4 inch. I suspect
someone has closed the door with something caught between it and the frame
(Like Herb's fingers). Anyway, I loosened the retaining frame and put a
foam filler in the gap planning to leave it for the time being. I hope to
get the truch primed in time to take to Bltv for our old tractor show June
15.

The Demascus Trail Days were Friday and Saturday. Our folks recruited about
100 hikers to come work Sunday and Monday on the projects we have been
prepping for the last couple of months.

I did not work today but delivered food and cooks to the site for the
workers to eat supper. They barely got down from the mountains when the
rain hit .

Tomorrow, I get to be a team leader !
