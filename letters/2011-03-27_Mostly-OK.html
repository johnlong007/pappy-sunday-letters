March 27, 2011 · Sunday

Mostly OK

7 photographs·146 words

I am tempted to send this on with pictures only and let each person figure
out what is going on, and where.

In case you are wondering, The dogs are fine. They are coming right along.
I may decide to have a "Big Dog" and a "Little Dog" as he is a bit over
twice her weight. But, she has him intimidated. He keeps telling me he had a
good life until we brought that yappy female here.

Car work progressed also. I have all but two pieces of bright work
installed (just a little British slang there). GSH is working on installing carpet
padding. Maybe next week I will include a picture.

Dave has mostly dropped out on me so he was not involved in Monday's
do-gooder project.I was out of town Tuesday so no AT work.

But, Spring is here and all is well.
