June 26, 2016 · Sunday

Run Down

12 photographs·424 words

After GSH got a Fitbit for Christmas we have been making trips to the track
so she can walk while I shuffle around. It soon became apparent that I have
been getting slower and slooower in my "running around". So, I decided to
speed up. Then I found when I try to speed up, I get tight chest and light
headed. Finally decided to go see the Dr.

He poked, prodded, listened, looked, X-rayed and had me do breathing tests.
Then he said "you are getting old." Also have a touch of COPD.

Today we went to the track at noon. I did 12 laps in 42 minutes . I have to
start real slow and speed up very gradually, a few seconds per lap. I can
tell when I am increasing too rapidly and back off for a few seconds until
it passes.

Any of you still running at 80&1/2 keep all this in mind.

On the flip side, Monday we went out and hiked 10 miles on the AT. I
offered to help Kim mow weeds on her section. We hiked in 5 miles to the
shelter then mowed weeds all the way back out. GSH snipped things which
needed snipping. She got 33,000 steps on her Fitbit.

Trail goes by Nick Grindstaff marker. Everyone should walk out and check on
Nick.

For supper, we had chipped beef gravy and did not have enough people to eat
it all,

For the regular work day on Thursday, we went back to the Beauty Spot and I
worked by myself rebuilding trail while the others wrestled rocks to build
steps.

The forest service person we have been working with has been promoted. We
had an outing for him after quitting time. He is a good guy and I expect to
keep in touch. He promised me that when forest service goes back in on Rich
mountain he would let me know. I have been wanting to all the way up Mill
creek for years.

Friday night was the long awaited shake down run of the new and improved
drag car. No problems and nothing broke. However, it was slower than
expected . Plans were immediately formulated for improvement !

Saturday, I went over to check on Suz's driveway. After much pondering and
discussing choices, I think she has decided to get an estimate on getting
it paved.

A lot of time mowing, a bit of time gardening and some time sanding on the
'63. Now, the week is gone and I am even older.speed.
