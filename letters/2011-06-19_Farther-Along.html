June 19, 2011 · Sunday

Farther Along

10 photographs·413 words

One of my AT work friends who had a rather traumatic childhood asked me
about my father this week. It made me do a lot of reflecting on how fortunate
I have been in my life.
I suppose I am most thankful for the parents I had (and all that means) and
second for the girl I married (and all that means).

Does do-gooder work count when it is inside the family? Wayne says he has
all Summer to get the roof on. With the help he is able to muster, it may
take all Summer.

AT was hard work. I went with a friend to walk his section and clear water
bars. Turns out there were blow downs which had not been reported. The only
saw we had was a small fold out job.

We hiked from Greasy Creek Gap to Hughes Gap. Ed Oliver picked us up there.
While waiting, he entertained a group of Through hikers.

The Blountville Tractor Show was Friday and Saturday. John came over
Thursday PM to help start tractors. We dragged three out as he wanted to take
the John Deere. It was difficult to start but we got it running but strange
behavior from the hand clutch.

The little Allis Chalmers however started easily. So Friday evening we
headed out to Blountville in a procession. John on the JD, Me on the A-C and
GSH bringing up the rear in the truck with the blinkers on.
John pulled out on main road (Joe informed me it is 126 not Memorial Blvd.)
me right behind and GSH tagging along.
He shifted into neutral to go to a road gear and could not get it into any
gear. So, there we are all three in the main road and all three at a dead
stop.
We did get out of the road with no damage or injuries and I hope John
learned a lesson in staying cool under stress.
We did not get the tractors to Blountville. But, I ended up being glad. It
rained Saturday about 2 PM, a horizontal downpour.

The rain brought the event to a sudden full stop. The pull scheduled for
Saturday evening has been rescheduled for next Saturday at 4 PM. I have to
work the gate. I least I have promised to do so.

After 2 years, we are getting close.

I had hoped to get it going but did not quite make it (did not even try).
Stay tuned.
