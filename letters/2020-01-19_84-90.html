January 19, 2020 · Sunday

84 & 90

11 photographs·327 words

Well, my Lil Sister who said she was getting old on 70th BD just passed 90.
Without slowing down as she went by best I can tell.

People came in from far and wide to join the celebration.

While we were there, she showed us a birth announcement she had just
received. A namesake ! She was very pleased.

On the 15 th, Anita delivered a BD cake to her ancient and decrepit father.
It had four corners ! I ate 'em all and almost all the in between.

Before all the partying started. We worked on delimbing the downed pine
tree and broken tops from pine trees. Actually, a lot of time was spent on
chain saw M & R. May decide to go electric. Til then I did most of delimbing
with the my axe. It always starts no matter how cold.
I did saw above the root ball on the big downed tree. Root ball went about
1/2 way back down.

I wanted to drag the trunk closer to driveway but it dug too much in so had
to jack it up first.

Many years ago, my mother asked what I wanted for Christmas. I told her a
big hydraulic jack.She turned it over to Dwight. I have a 30 ton jack.
Second time I have used it.

Now trunk is where I want it. Hope some person will have use for the log.
Not very good for firewood.

I got all the others delimbed. Thinking about buying a PTO driven wood
chipper.

The experts say old folks should do mental and physical exercise. We try.
PBBD procured some wire puzzles for us. I just fiddle with one until it
comes apart.

Gsh wants to figure them out. gets quite aggravated. I turn off hearing
aides.

My remaining willing worker.
