Age (ed) !

11 photographs·328 words

I think we have more family birthdays in January than any other month.

For years we went to wherever Donna and Henry were to celebrate birthdays
in mid-January. For the last few years we have gone to Union City to share
with my Lil' sister. My mother made cakes with hard icing. Of course both
Sis and I think that is the proper and correct way it is to be done.
Fortunately, willing helpers within the family have stepped up and I always
take a hard icing cake when going for a birthday visit.

One year we met in the Detroit airport and sat on the floor to eat our hard
icing cake. I think on the way to Ecuador probably. Not quite as novel
this year.

Martha brought in a very timely and tasty lunch,then we ate cake.

I really appreciate all the family who made this possible.
Laura made the cake
A & C shuttled us back and forth to Dechard
LLLL did the same for Union City
Diane and Anita did the good fairy bit to the house.
Diane provided TLC for all our animals wild and domesticated.

Guess it takes a lot to keep the ole folks ticking.

Meanwhile, I am told there is a sawmill waiting for assembly. Stay tuned.

With ill weather predicted, we decided to restock the handy wool supply.

I had remembered stacking wood in the barn behind the fire engine.. Part
was left in big sections. Not wanting to split it myself, I offered it to
Wayne.

All during the Union City trip we got dare predictions about the storm
approaching. But, we made it home Saturday night. Picture below early
Sunday morning.

Martha sent us home with 7 baskets of leftovers.

GSH's home health nurse.

Recognize anyone? Newly minted nurse in 1952 .

Elise
