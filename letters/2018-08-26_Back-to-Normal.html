Back to Normal

14 photographs·269 words

For some time we have been going to the track almost every day. I was able
to enlist help in procuring "suitable" walking attire for GSH.
\

But she still tends to rearrange for temperature control.

Both Wayne and Diane had birthdays this week ( Hint, I graduated 3 days
before she was born, I graduated from UT 60 years back).

Everyone know what this nut is?

The mower is useful for all sorts of things. GSH is also.

We lost the grape crop because of more pressing events.

Identify species?

Thin slices of sharp cheese under the lettuce.

After This place got covered with trees I did not have much use for sickle
bar mower so brother Herb had it for the last few years, I brought it back
home and spent Friday doing M & R , plan to check it out here this week and
if all works well take it to hillside to mow out there.

GSH has been bugging me about the mounting brush pond for years. I have
kept promising her that I would smosh it by and by. I learn ed from
watching Dwight's ride to cinch the seat belt down tight.

Finally got it done.

PBBD had her birthday Saturday, went right on without us.
Sunday after lapping the track we had a birthday dinner. The cupcake with
one candle is in front of the salt shaker.

Oh when will he ever learn?
