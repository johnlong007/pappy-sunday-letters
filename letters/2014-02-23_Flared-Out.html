February 23, 2014 · Sunday

Flared Out

12 photographs·377 words

I tried to rework some of my double flared tubing (my English major told me
if I had flared with flair they would not have leaked).

Still got leaks !

But, I did make a lot of progress this week. I used various tools including
a pneumatic needle gun to remove rust, paint, grit and undercoating from
mostly underneath the LBT.

You can get a feel for how dirty the job is. Also, to sort of protect my
tender head I try to keep padding in place. Speaking of head:

I will go back to the Dr. in a couple of weeks to see how I am doing.
GSH is working on a knitting project (in addition to tending to her cat).

When LLLL was last here she showed me how to open Hulu. We soon found one
can view the Inspector Morse series. So:

Now if she can knit while hand feeding her cat.
The cat sleeps (or at least spends the nights) in the shop.

We finally had a work day on the AT. Last Summer, when we had so much rain
the AT around the end of Watauga Lake was submerged causing hikers a lot of
problems. Thursday we went out and dug a high water trail about 1/4 mile
long and maybe 1/2 mile back form the lake. It went well except for a large
blowdown in the path. It will require a return trip with chainsaw.

You might be interested in seeing the willing workers. A flock of spring
chickens.

Friday night we had the "Maintainers " event at the ECC cabin.

GSH got a patch for 250 hours and I got re

upped on my chainsaw certification.

Today, we went out to the hillside to ponder possible building site on top
of the hill.

We went on over and down behind in case one wanted to build completely
isolated.

And last of all, for the benefit of those who live in colder climes;
