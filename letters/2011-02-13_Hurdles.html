But before we get to that, I want to pose another caption creation contest.
To liven interest I am going to offer a first (and only) prize of a trip
to Burger King in Blountville. Entrees will be judged on originality,
creativity and aptness of thought.

The big news for the week is that for the first time in several years, I
ran in a 5 K race. The race was put on by Linda's church. The route was a
short uphill, a long downhill U-turn and back. A rather difficult course
especially since I had not run it before. I ran with Linda (Sarah ran off and
left us). We finished in 34 minutes and change. All in all not bad. Sarah was
instructed/requested to take our picture as we finished the coarse. So,
far, no picture has developed.

While there, Linda gave me lessons in using the new electronic device.
Then, Laura came over today and gave me another session. The race did not make
me feel my age. The lessons did.

Dave is out in Colorado living the lifestyle of the rich and famous. At
home, the weather prohibited AT work, so, work continued unabated on the silk
purse project.

Getting the 1/4 windows adjusted has been a major effort. They are now
positioned to open and close to the proper position but further work will be
needed to get smooth operation.

GSH is not getting active on the interior work. That starts with fitting
panels which will be covered with upholstery material in due time.

On the subject of GSH, I have been subverted as some unnamed person has
slid a Christmas present request in front of the car project.

As some may remember, part of my 5-year extension on my 10 year plan was to
get rid of at least one car and one tractor each year. At the Monday night
meeting of the Gray Old tractor Owners club I was approached about selling
a tractor to the club to be superficially fixed up and used for display
purposes each year in show advertising. After much soul searching I agreed to
let them have the parts Farmall C. When they came after it, they also made
a pitch for the little A-C (Charlie's).

Sorry bout that Charlie.
