January 30, 2011 · Sunday

Almost Ditto

5 photographs·333 words

Perhaps I should make a list of all the things we did not do this week. A
lot more variety than what we did.

No ice fishing.
No skiing
No tripping the light fantastic at the Ball
I did not even go to the opera
Of course, no bounding out and going "in to work"

I did manage to get out of bed each morning and move to the vertical
position w/o too much difficulty.No trips to the Dr., planned or unplanned.

I made trip to Erwin to see Herb and his charges and he came over here one
day. Makes me tired thinking about chasing a teenager.

I think we are a little ole couple with their dogs.

I suppose everyone has heard about Yappy. Not everyone has seen (or heard)
her live.
I believe she is going to be OK in time.

I do not intend to let her out of the house without being on a leash until
she has been to the vet for some serious work. We have been price shopping.
The pound gave us a $35 coupon to take to vet and get Junior repaired
(sounds better than fixed). It still cost over $200 with all the extra charges
and fees. I do hope to avoid that this time.
Aside from the dog, the good news is Dave and I were able to spent a day on
do-gooder work. Just like the good book mandate this was for the little
ole widow woman . We did several projects around the house mostly sealing to
good down on air leakage.

Pretty much the rest of the week was either installing glass in the silk
purse

Or splitting wood

I hope the former wood splitters recognize the wood pile and the ease with
which it is being dispatched.
We did have the one needy family come by looking to replenish their wood
supple so the children would not have to go to bed cold. We did not discuss
hungry.
