August 11, 2024 · Sunday

Sands of Time

13 photographs·375 words

Monday we went to Erwin (to pick up the relined clutch disc in JC) to visit
my Aunt Flo. The last of the sisters. She is the center of family
information (gossip) but deaf as a post.

Nephew Dan came by one day. We had a very good session, It is
distressing that the three kids are not doing well handling my brother
Jimmy's affairs. Dan sent me a picture of their group with Aunt Adele. Does
it look like one familiar?

Another day, Dave Edwards brought youngest daughter Katy by. I well
remember when Katy was born. Gave her a copy of Go Dog Go. We went to
Rochester when she got married. Now, she has a dear departed husband and
three grown kids all raised in Lake Wobegon best I can tell.

An important story here, My mother had fever blisters. I had fever
blisters, Diane has fever blisters. Various things set 'em off. Tomatoes
are one of those things. Do Hass has been supplying us with tomatoes, Diane
has fever blisters and I do not !!!. Diane says it is due to the antiviral
drugs I am taking.

In addition to telling me don't get sick and don't bleed, I need to eat
more protein. A &C are doing their part.

The crew got the Bobcat back in service just in time to give it a serious
workout. In this scene, Cathering is loading the Walnut log which Alan is
prepped to saw so Bob can make knotty pine furniture. Diane and Catherine
each have MSME degrees. They put it all to use here.

Stay tuned.

More MSME work.

More MSME work. When Wayne put the new steering cylinder on the mower ,he
had to use the old rod end. It fell apart, Diane cobbled it together so I
could get the mower back to the house, now, we awit Wayne and a new
replacement rod end.

Invasive weeds and grasses are taking the place. With the mower out of
commission, any port in a storm.

I thoroughly enjoyed getting to play on the Bobcat.
