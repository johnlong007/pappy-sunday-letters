I am still struggling with gmail so will stick with AOL for the time being. But, since I am not good on this PC I am going to try text in one dispatch and pictures in another to follow. So, if nothing follows, don't be surprised.

First the high point of the week ! I got an update from Vanessa showing bits of her life in the frozen North. Makes me want to go visit !

Second, speaking of going to visit, select family members went to visit Kenneth at his work site in Oar Ridge. We got to meet his Prof, and the two Dr's for whom he works as well as touring the place. I toured Oar Ridge in 1946 or 47. I remember putting a dime in a machine and getting it back out with a pressed around frame with inscription. More exciting, when held in front of a gieger counter it caused it to go wild counting gigers. I have no idea what happened to my coin but , I got on ebay after coming home and found they are avaiable and I remembered correctly. ( That was a LONG time ago.)

Third, at last tomatoes are getting ripe and we had the first BLTC sandwich(s) of the season.

Sunday I did AT work. On Aug 8-11 we are going to be putting gravel on the trail from Carner's Gap to Round Bald. Today we worked on getting the trail access ready for the transport vehicles.

Wayne brought some of his kids over for swimming and for firearms safety training. Found out the pool had turned green while Dwight's attention was elsewhere. But, using his transmitted instruction it was converted to clear and sparkling again in just a couple of days. I do like the salt system.

Saturday, Dave and Pat Edwards came by. Dave drove the car and took it through downtown Blountville. He pronounced himself delighted and asked if he can borrow for his high school reunion first weekend in Sept. I told him he could and I would try to have mufflers installed before then. I am not sure he really cares about the mufflers but he did mention it is difficult to steer.
Katy and Chad have three children (maybe 11,9,7). They are in the process of adopting a three year old girl from an orphanage in The Congo. She has to go for two weeks. Dave said he cannot go with her. I told him I would if so desired. We shall see.

Did I mention Allison came over and painted the porch ceiling. It look much better. I perch here in this corner and look right at that ceiling. It has been bugging me for years ! A great improvement for which I grateful. Perhaps not eternally but grateful.

On the other hand, While smashing two old car gas tanks with the Bobcat, I got two punctures in one tire. It is not jacked up outside the sho door awaiting further treatment.
Saturday, with Charlie's advice and consent, we set out to replace the clutch assembly in the VW. I had read an on-line procedure which said take the olf one out and put a new one in. After we got started and it became apparent there were some obstacles, I found another on-line procedure (21 pages) which gave all the details including the special tools needed. We put it back together and decided to ponder further. Stay tuned.

Having failed in that endeavor I decided to attempt to hook up the power sterring in the Studebaker. I soon found the gear box fitting threads are completely stripped and gone. Another frustrating failure. However, Dwight in a fit of strategic thinking has claimed a steering rack assembly from his number one son's future race car project which we believe will fit. Once more, stay tuned.

I finally got started splitting the giant log which I previously dragged down and split (for no know reason). It did not take long to find the next week link in the splitter mechanism. The pusher head failed. I have been trying to use sow's ears all along but this time I may have to get a part fabricated. Once more, stay tuned.

So, here we go to dispatch this and on to pictures!
