March 25, 2018 · Sunday

Busy, Busy, Busy

20 photographs·396 words

A lot of activity. a lot of pictures. Trying to encourage sending pictures
to the Lil' Ole folks at home.

Work on the pool room moves right along. I can almost feel the warm water.

I have mentioned my most willing, willing worker several times.

More or less willing workers helped on the car project. The heater
temperature control valve is a complex item located inside the heater box
inside car cabin. When the valve leaks, anti-freeze runs out in the cabin
floor. It stinks.
I had removed the leaky valve from the '63 years ago, But, also years ago,
I had found a NOS valve at a flea market. Just recently I saw a site listed
on club web site to rebuild the valve for $250.

​Wayne installed my NOS valve for me. I had removed glove box but job
requires octopus arms and nimble fingers. Note willing helper.

​​GSH doing her imitation of a willing worker did a lot of Chrome polishing.

​

PBBD installed wind lace around the doors and applied sealer around the
newly installed chrome trim.

​A professional, the one who removed cracked windshield, returned to
install the replacement.
Many years ago, some of us drove to Ft. Lewis near Seattle (as I remember)
to watch Wayne complete some sort of military training. On the way home, we
came through Whitehall Montana and picked up a windshield I had arranged to
purchase. I have forgotten the price but the windshield is unique to '63/64
Chrysler convertible.
David Crane is the installer. He is good (but not cheap). We prepped the
site, cleaned the old seal, cleaned the windshield.

​

​
Some years back I helped Anita procure a load of used tin roofing which she
stacked by her building at foot of hill . It was stolen in broad daylight.
Some of my tin roofing has been disappearing, today I caught culprits red
handed.

​

Anita's buddy Mike has been harvesting timber on the Long property. He had
it sawed and offered me first shot at the slabs.

​
For the gardeners in our family. Look at my over Wintered potted pepper !

​And for the love birds, snowbirds.

I am also pleased to announce that several picked up on the ramps. It
appears they are also alive and multiplying although less rapidly that some.

​​
​That's All Folks !
