July 29, 2012 · Sunday

Mini Camp

9 photographs·163 words

Monday Charlie helped do routine maintenance on the truck, oil, filter,
grease job and tire rotation.

The other big news this week is that I had willing workers (besides the
dogs) all week.

Adam and David were here, almost like the days when it was Aaron and Beth.
Perhaps we have another one or two willing workers out there?

Guess what, we weeded the garden. Split wood and mowed.

We dragged the unfinished Case tractor into place expecting Janet to be
here Saturday. Perhaps Alan would like to tow it back to Wisconsin.

In related news, I went to Union City Friday to pick up my little sister.
While there we went out to Reelfoot Lake to eat fish and chips with Amy and
Martha. It was a fun outing, Amy and her spouse Kevin are planning to raise
the worlds first perfect child so Amy was eager for some helpful hints.

Oh yes, now I remember there was something else this week!
