20/20:
Not my foresight, not my hindsight.
But New Year I find to be a good (as any) time to take stock.

The best way to ensure keeping a resolution is to tell people. Enlist help,
avoid embarrassment.
So, here goes my resolution.

Stand up straight and keep shoulders back

What is wrong with these pictures?

Grandson Kenneth visited from Geneva. He brought GSH some Swiss Chocolate
and supervised the opening.
Overcoming her urge to save it, she ate one piece.

six days later, before going to church, she ate another piece.

When brother died, his treasured car was in an unfinished reconstruction
stage. A former student who now has own business "wanted" to finish the job
for love or money.
He came got the car out of storage, took to his shop, made cost estimate,
put James (herb's son) in shock, brought back and unloaded in driveway :
charged $1,000.

We went over Monday and moved it back into storage. Difficult to do as no
steering and incline into storage bldg.

Grandson Adam on his way back to West Point to become an officer and a
gentleman came by and cleaned more of my Christmas presents.
Here is picture from last stop when I was teaching him the manual of arms
for an M1.

I was notified the '63 was ready to retrieve. Since there are still many
tasks both large and small to complete, we moved the '37 sideways in shop
to make room inside.

Brought the car home Sunday. Installer had a broken top bow welded and
tacked on an extra $100 to his inflated price.

Grin and bear it.

Top looks great, just like new.

We ate supper with Diane Friday night. Both her cat and dog suffer from ADD.

Unlike our well adjusted animals.
