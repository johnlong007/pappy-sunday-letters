September 6, 2015 · Sunday

Non-Routine Routine

8 photographs·447 words

Thursday was go with Herb to Dermatology Associates to get the latest
squamous carcinomas removed from his right cheek. I do believe Dr. Thomas
decided he had better go deep and wide this time. I watched during the
sewing up part but did not take a picture.
We were assured his eye would be swollen shut for some extended period of
time but did not occur. So far it seems to be progressing better than (I)
expected.

The other big event was harvesting the Holly tree but you already know
about that. Jimmy came out to lend aid and comfort. He has just finished
his 11/29 and can now get the breath analyzer removed from his vehicle. I
suggested he leave it but he assured me he has sworn off for sure this
time. Stay tuned.

You are not aware that Because GSH worked so hard and complained so little
that we stopped at DQ so she could have a banana split for lunch.

I took a container of THE peppers to church dinner Wed. evening. A number
of people decided to have a try.Responses were similar to what David had
when he tried one. I have eaten 1/2 of one. Nose running, sweating and
crying but stopped before hiccups. I believe we will have enough for
everyone to have at least one.

I did sanding on the LBT to get it ready to prime, now waiting for my
painter .

Purchased a new high pressure hose( $51) for Studebaker power steering and
induced Joe to install it. Also worked on adjusting clutch travel and am
ready to give it another trial run.

This morning I looked out window and saw three deer under the crab apple
tree. Later, I went out and determined they had eaten GSH's new strawberry
plants down to nubs. Venison anyone?

Stink bugs are beginning to show up around the windows. I detected the odor
of one yesterday and found it was trapped in a spider web built between the
glass and the window screen in a utility room window. GSH and I watched
with interest and pleasure while the spider wrapped it for Christmas
delivery. I did not realize anything could cope with stink bugs. Maybe
someone can deduce spider species?

Anyone for Paw Paws?

We did get in another session of sawing up the downed maple at Don's house.
Not quite finished sawing but next time I am going to take the truck and
bring a load home.

I watched some of Auburn and Alabama ball games looking for band members I
could recognize but no joy.

Has anyone figured out how far apart we are scattered E/W and N/S?
