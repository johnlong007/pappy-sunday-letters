July 27, 2025 · Sunday

Goals

16 photographs·366 words

Number 1 for the week : get the second DB mower on display. Failed again.
Last week Alan the welder installed a hitch on the mower tongue.

Yesterday he installed one on the lawn mower. Now I should be able to
transport to display site and perhaps snabble a couple of strong WWs to
move it into position. Stay tuned.

Second goal for the week was to catch up on mowing the place. I mow over 2
acres.What with the rain and other issues I had got behind. The steep bank
beside the driveway is difficult. It has many years of accumulation of
leaves and pine needles. It also has limbs and sticks which cause problems.

Anita drafted a couple of workers. A bit early to pronounce them as
willing. She is the right person to teach them the way they should go. I
should have let her use the Summer Camp whistle which Beth used to keep
things moving.

For swim time, they did appear willing !

We have a lot of groundhogs. This one was spotted going up the hill at
West end of the house.

I called in friend Pat Loven the bounty hunter to reduce the population.

Wayne had spotted a hole going under the house foundation.

He filled the hole with concrete. Stay tuned

The big Walnut logs which were dragged down to the soccer field are bugging
Wayne. He moved one to the sawmill.

A delight for the week was Max and Allison coming by with noodniks.
I can still see Allison and her siblings bouncing on this same Wonder Horse.

Have to start on the right path early.

Allison also claimed some knick knacks while they were here. Know what she
has here?

Don Hass came by after lunch on Wed. with produce wife Nancy sent,

We have many squirrels. This picture is Blackbeard the Squirrell. He mostly
ignores us. He (or she ,or it) has learned to walk the high wire to get the
woodpecker log.
