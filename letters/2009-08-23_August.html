August 23, 2009 · Sunday

August

6 photographs·300 words

Momma used to have an event in August to celebrate August birthdays. This
year, Anita managed the August birthday event. Our family sure is good at
putting together a fine event with very little advance notice.
You may note the event included a fair amount of swimming in the new and
improved salt water pool. A couple of mermaids even joined in the
festivities.

Moving right along now, another possum ventured into the trap. Under the
heading that they are scavengers and don't really hurt anything, I turned it
loose in the drive. The next night, something tore down my Mr. Stripey
tomato plant and destroyed the large, ripe tomatoes. Next time I catch a possum
it will be going for a one way ride.
Another critter has been getting in the garden and working on the corn.
Herb said Coons like marsh mellows even better than corn. Sure enough, I
believe he is right.
Progressing through the critters, a bears) has been picnicking in Herb's
garden at frequent intervals. Bears take it much more and cleaner than the
other critters.

For a change of pace, I helped Dale pull the engine from Linda's
tractor.One of the counterweights is broken off the crankshaft. I have asked for a
picture when Dale pulls the crankshaft out of the block.

For AT work, we met in Hampton before starting the days work. Quite a bit
of time and brain power went into planning. About 100 ASU students are due
this coming Saturday. They will be digging a relocation of the AT for about
1/2 mile around the end of Watuga Lake.

My progress is slow but I think forward. About the only time I notice
anything out of the ordinary is when I am running. Up hills are slow and
sometimes walking.
