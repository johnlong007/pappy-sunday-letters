November 27, 2011 · Sunday

Thanks

8 photographs·349 words

It was a a really good Thanksgiving. I have much for which I am thankful.

This week.

1. John's funeral

2. Table sanding continues and continues.

3. Power steering leaks continue and continue, and continue.

4. Combination Thanksgiving !

John's funeral was at a Society of Friends church. We learned that the Quakers have a large presence in Greenboro. It was a first for me going to a Quaker service. They have prolonged periods of quite. I told one of our Elders at church today that all our leadership should visit a Quaker service.
Teressa appeared to be in a state of suspended disbelief. We are going to go back over and visit with her for a longer period quite soon. Maybe this week.
Both her boys, Brian and Jeff were at the service.

It was decided that the finish on the table was not satisfactory, I spent a lot of time sanding off the finish. Bob has ordered some new and improved finish to be applied in the event I do get my part completed. Filling defects in the benches was continued.

Dwight installed new seals in the mixed and matched steering rack which I then put back into the car to go for a test run. The power steering seems to be working well. The gas gage showed life (but not fully). Gear shift linkage needs further adjustment and there is a PS leak. But, it is close enough to send to town to get the mufflers installed. I need a volunteer.

Thursday was pie production/consumption

followed by apple peeling and slide viewing.

Thanksgiving/Apple Butter production!

The crack of dawn.

Willing worker/willing watchers

Final assembly!

I took a lot of pictures but none of the giant meal spread out in Linda's garage. Someone else must supply those. I had purchased Winter Banana, Rome Beauty and Stayman Winesap apples. It would have been better had we been able to make the run earlier rather than storing the apples . Maybe next year. The blend seemed to work well. At least from the cooking standpoint and initial taste tests.
