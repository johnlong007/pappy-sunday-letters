March 28, 2021 · Sunday

Washed Out

15 photographs·531 words

I bet Bob remembers !

My mother did not cook on Sunday night. We foraged. I ate milk and bread.
"Bread" meant corn bread, not biscuits, muffins or light bread. Much later,
I ofter went into the plant on Sunday evening then had bread and milk after
I got home. Tonight, since we did not have regular Sunday night movies at
Laura's, I made cornbread and had bread and milk. A vanishing art I bet !
Anyone in the family still eat milk and bread? A bunch of city slickers?

The big, new project is cleaning out and organizing the shop. With the help
of PBBD we moved out a truck load of stuff which Wayne made disappear. It
appears cleaning out will be easier and quicker than organizing. The main
task for Diane is to keep me from deciding "that's good enough".

Another trip to Erwin. Took the sprayer full of Round Up and sprayed the
invasive plant life in the pond. The pond has a deep layer of muck in the
bottom. I need waders or a boat to get where I can do a good job. While
there I used her tractor to consolidate the brush pile and run the tiller
over her garden spot again.

Many, maybe all, will
be glad to know the rams are doing well. Much slower spreading than at the
higher elevation but it is clear they are spreading. Iplanted tese in a row
2 or 3 years back.

Some may rember the year "we" dug all the bulbs up in the circle. I plowed
three furrows around the edge of the woods and planted the bulbs we dug up.
You can see the end of one row in this picture. Now, the plants in the
circle are back more than ever. I will post pictures as they bloom

Saturday night the rains came down and the floods came up. But ! No roof
leaks detected. We bounded out at 7:45 to got to Perkins then church and
encountered an obstacle. After pondering the choices and went back to
house, got a chainsaw and in my Sunday clothes and pouring rain sawed
enough to get through.
We hurried breakfast but got to church on time.
I am through wearing a mask. We have been inoculated and tested, can
neither give nor receive, have no interest in virtue signaling so , no more
mask. But will not go anywhere I am not welcome.
Only saw one other person w/o a mask at church but somebody has to be first.

When we came back from church the brush pond was almost full of water. It
must be over a sinkhole as it goes down fast.

Willing workers helped clear more bush out of the driveway in PM.

Retaining basin was filled and overflowing. When it dries up I intend to
make the dam a lot higher and maybe double the capacity.

Pear tree

For those living in the bright lights of the big city, ponder this.
