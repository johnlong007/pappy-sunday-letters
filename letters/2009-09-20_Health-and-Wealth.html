September 20, 2009 · Sunday

Health and Wealth

8 photographs·413 words

there was a special crew working on the our section of the AT all week So,
I stayed away. Since Dave was working, we also skipped do-gooder.

I did get to see Hal. He is getting a bit despondent.

The Studebaker has moved to the top of my list of projects. Dwight came
over one evening and worked of determining front coil spring requirements. I
went by to take JW a list on the parts I could tell are missing. He was not
there so I left the list on shop door and a note that I would be back next
Wed. I intend to keep pestering in hopes he will find at least some parts.

As you can see, Diane was eager to pitch in and help.

There were also other willing workers waiting for an opportunity to
participate.

I took the apple butter equipment to Herb and went with him to get apples
from high school classmate (Genevieve). To see the results of Herb's apple
butter run, check his Facebook page.

While there, we walked around the place . Herb showed me the results of the
bears raiding his chestnuts trees.

My appointment at St. Thomas was Friday PM. We went to linda's Friday
morning taking the Ford tractor to her.

I hope this will not bring the repair work on their Case to a halt. I did
get to see the broken crankshaft. Hard to believe the tractor wouls still
run.

The Dr. visit was fine. Said I am doing "great" and in the 99th percentile
for recovery process .I will go back in four months for a bunch of testing
to find out if Dr. is right about how I am doing.

Dale's Dad had his 90 th birthday Saturday. His kids had a big reception at
the church. It was really good. He is a remarkable person. I did not get
any pictures at the event. Maybe Linda will send some.

After the Saturday event, I raided Linda's garden.

After we got back, I took a picture of the trailer.

Note two things, the fenders (a previous story) , are held in place with
bungee cords. Also. one of the ramps is missing (it is in the truck bed). I
tell you having one of the ramps fall off while running in traffic on the
Interstate will bump your pulse right up.

When the ramp dropped down I pulled onto the shoulder and ramp stayed off
the highway.
