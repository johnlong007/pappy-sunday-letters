June 22, 2014 · Sunday

Solstice!

13 photographs·596 words

I could just hear the people in the cars passing us saying "Look at that
old codger, she is young enough to be his grand daughter".

Some may have heard, Rachel purchased a car. Not just any cay, a Miata.
But, she needed checked out for driving a car with a manual transmission. A
first for both of us. At the same time, Maria wanted to get her ticket
punched for Interstate, big town (and pouring down rain) driving.

It was really fun and I am delighted that I get called on and trusted with
such pearls of great price.

As a bonus, I got to meet Andrew.

Apparently, fish are abundant in Alabama.

When I left off last week as I remember, the "H" had a flat tire, the MD
would not run on Diesel fuel and I had not tried to trouble shoot the A-C.

Any Summer Campers remember working on the Case SC? We painted it red. It
had a shredded tire. I took tires from a tractor I sold to install on the
SC, Then I had one tire and wheel left over. Turns out, it was a 38" wheel
with an 11.2 tire. Just what I needed for the "H". Everyone follow that?

​
The aspiring mechanics and engineers in the family will note and remember,
"Don't force it , get a bigger hammer (or longer cheater as it where)"

Anyway, the H is tucked away warm and dry ready for next years event. So is
the crawler.

​

The fuel system on the MD was a challenge. As one might guess, I did not
really fully understand all the events which had to take place in the
correct order to make the fuel switch. Also, while sitting upstarted for a
few years, it seems the high pressure fuel system had lost its prime. After
many tries I finally got the system primed, bleed, and running on diesel
fuel. I certainly do like the machine and wish I had some really heavy work
for it to do.

Next was the A-C. It has a slow leak tire and a blocked fuel system so it
will not run more than a few seconds. So, the report will have to wait
until later. Stay tuned.

​
D & D sponsored another lake outing.

​
​Diana took a lot of bird pictures which she may or may not share.

Gwendolyn is working on making the perfect white cake with hard icing. She
brought latest try over so I could eat the corner pieces and tell her to
keep trying. Wonder how long I can milk this?

​
​ GSH went to the local softball game one evening. Storm light and noise
show all around. Joe and Barry were working the concession stand. They make
a fine hot dog .

​
​ Speaking of hot dogs, the only exercise fat dog gets is playing soccer. I
purchased a lightweight ball for him and pumped it up as tight as I could
get it. Lasted two days.
I would like a basketball size, smooth surface very firm but not too heavy
ball if anyone runs across one. As soon as ball leaks a bit he bites and
ruins.

​
The little dog and the cat have signed a truce. Fat dog has not yet
conceded.

​I wonder if Christopher and Charlie got home, Maria did.
I am sure Beth and Katy are out there somewhere as I got notes from each.

Nothing very exciting going on concerning health and wealth but happiness
is doing well.
