April 15, 2012 · Sunday

Going On

9 photographs·483 words

Buddy Dave Dunham has more health issues that a dog has fleas. This week he
went in to VA and had a shoulder rotator cuff repaired. As soon as it gets
well he is scheduled to get the other side . Guess he moves from do-gooder
work to visiting halt, lame and afflicted side.

On the home front several big events. 27.4 degrees one morning which I
expect will severly impact the fruit and nut harvest. We did keep the
strawberries covered.

Remember when I helped Anita salvage a big load of roofing tin from a job
she and Mike did? We stacked the salvaged tin outside her fence down at the
foot of the hill by the horse pasture. Thursday night it was stolen. Aaron,
to heck with the nuts over there, come home and take care of the nuts here.

AT work was fun but very cold and windy. Back to Hughes gap. This time we
put in a "blue blaze" trail running off the AT to a water source.

These are pictures made from opposite ends of the blue trail. Bob Peoples
is the person in the ratty brown jacket. GSH asked as I was heading out if
anyone else dresses as ratty as I do. I told her maybe Bob .

How about my efforts to build a roll cage on the mower? I made a trial run
on the steep hill just behind the house and determined the mower slides
down the hill sideways when trying to mow back and forth. So, I decided i
need a roll cage and a seat belt. Of course, being one for making do and
getting by, I am trying to make it from existing bits and pieces.

GSH tended to Laura and worked on her table (and the taxes).

I believe a shall attach three table pictures. Painting another coat
sanding it off (once more)
and what we believe to be the finished product

I have slowed down on the '37 with the advent of the mowing season. But,
did make some effort, progress I hope. I believe I have found a way to
mount the battery. It goes right below the drivers feet. However, I had to
cobble up a mounting fixture. More work will be required.

I also determined that the new grill I purchased many years ago does not
fit the shell very well. It is too wide and too short. I spent 1/2 day
wrestling with it before making that determination. Then I went back to the
original grill. It is going to be quite a project to make it fit decent.
Also, the chrome molding pieces are missing. Rats.

de a run down to see LLLL and talk about travel.
