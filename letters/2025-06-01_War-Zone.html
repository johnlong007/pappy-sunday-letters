June 1, 2025 · Sunday

War Zone

15 photographs·279 words

The squirrels have been in control of the ground and the air. This week,
the air war has been taken from them. WW"s stung a clothes from the maple
tree to the house and hung a log feeder in the middle. So far, no squirrel
has mastered a high wire act. Stay tuned.

Along the same line, Meredith recommended a for sure feeder to thwart the
squirrells.

I have not seen a squirrel on it yet. Stay tuned.

Usually there are 4 on the ground ignoring me, sometimes up to 7. I read
age, size and attitude determine the dominant one. Cant see his teeth to
judge age but clearly dominate.

Farmer Lisa's daughter works at Scott farm during the Summer. Farmer Lisa
brought us some strawberries and some of Ellas hand made ice cream.

First harvest from our garden !

Now about 34 days until harvest time. Stay tuned !

We pretty much disassembled the mowing machine to clean and paint the parts.

Small Diameter tires are tough to mount.

But, we got it. The tool box rusted away requiring rebuilding.

New drive tires are on order. The wheels have been cleaned and painted
.Helper got the hubs ready.

The heavy equipment mechanics had fun.

A view of the inside of the crawler transmission.

We went on tour of the newest eggs in the basket nest. There is little
chance they will run out of things to do.

Monday 7:30 AM ,a holiday just me and the NP.
