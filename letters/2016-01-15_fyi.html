If your name is John or Mary, you now have the ultimate comeback for anyone
who pokes fun at your intelligence.

According to a MooseRoots.com study
<http://names.mooseroots.com/stories/9751/genius-baby-names> that looked at
the most common names of geniuses, John and Mary took the top male and
female spots, followed by Robert and Elizabeth.

*SEE ALSO: Study suggests drinking soda piles on fat around internal organs
<http://www.aol.com/article/2016/01/14/study-suggests-drinking-soda-piles-on-fat-around-internal-organs/21296798/>*

The study's author said in a press release
<http://names.mooseroots.com/stories/9751/genius-baby-names>: "Every parent
wants his child to be happy and achieve success during her lifetime. Giving
your little one a distinguished name is a great way to put her on the right
path from the very beginning."

The study looked at nearly 15,000 names of geniuses in various fields like
philosophy, mathematics and music to identify the most common first names.

You can see the full list at MooseRoots.com
<http://names.mooseroots.com/stories/9751/genius-baby-names#Intro>.
