Same ol, same ol

10 photographs·187 words

I went with Herb to see Dr. Sago . So far, so good.

Went with Anita to visit Beth and deliver one of the chests GSH had
refinished. A fine visit ! I like their house and property. It reminds me a
lot of the place Linda and Dale have complete with woods and creek.

In addition, they have a detached garage apartment which would be perfect
for a homeless Lil' ol lady.

I spent a lot of time working on the LBT. I am mixing and matching doors
and windows from the parts vehicle. I did reach new heights of
inefficiency in fabricating parts to make the door latches functional. Good
thing I am not on any one's payroll.

Linda came up for a visit Friday night and Saturday. Notable for two
reasons, she helped a whole lot with some PC issues and with incredible
device issues. Perhaps more important she spurred GSH to resume pie
production !

Tonight, Laura made hard rolls and brats, Very tasty. Allison appeared
wearing a new winter wrap. She is warm, it would appear some poor
unfortunate critters are cold.
