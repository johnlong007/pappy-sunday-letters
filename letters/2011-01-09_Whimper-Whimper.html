January 9, 2011 · Sunday

Whimper, Whimper

4 photographs·213 words

I have felt lousy for the last three weeks, A nagging, hacking cough.At
least the backache went away.

We did get back out on the AT for a workday. 12 old (but not angry) men. We
worked on a relocation near Watuga lake. The trail runs up and down hill
back about 1/4 mile from the lake . We are doing a relocation so the trail
will follow along the shoreline back about 50-75 feet from the edge of the
lake. It will make a very nice section.

The other project for the week has been the car project. While JW had the
car, he had it stripped down for sandblasting. He had all the door and
window mechanism removed so all I got was a pile of pieces with no guidance on
how it all fit together.
Knowing GSH is much more painstaking (read persnickty) than I am, I have
recruited her to help with the reassembly.
In addition to a pile of parts, we have a box of rubber seals and gaskets.
The car has a vent window, a door glass and a quarter window on each side.
We got the rubber fitted into the quarter window and have pretty much
figured out how the assembly goes into the car door.
