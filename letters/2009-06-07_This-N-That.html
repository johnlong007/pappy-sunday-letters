June 7, 2009 · Sunday

This 'N That

7 photographs·705 words

Last week, I had report ready to go, one of my better efforts I might add,
and the PC ate it. This week, I am going to start earlier and include fewer
pictures.
The major theme for the week was trying to get old tractors running in
preparation for the Blountville event on June 19 & 20. Last year I waithed
until the day before and only got one ready to go.This year I hope to have
several ready and able willing workers to take them in the likely event that I
am out of action
Monday is the normal do-gooder day. Does helping ones brother count. It
should. It also gave me an opportunity to Practice my chain saw skills (or
lack of same). Herb had (past tense) a couple of White Pine trees on the creek
bank behind his house. One large and one real large. He wanted both cut.
Both were tilted in exactly the direction they could not be allowed to fall.
Fortunately, with plenty of equipment and some amount of planning, we
brought them down with a minimum of damage.

For bonus points, I procured several White Walnut (Butternut) trees from a
friend then shared with a good friend of GSH. We even delivered to her in
the Lamar community.

In other good news, Sue Ketron is doing very well (Linda, you need to talk
with Sue or send her a note, she said she was not going to have
reconstructive surgery but I think she really would like to and needs encouraged). Hal
was in first day of another cycle but was doing much better than last
time. Also latest MRI had not detected any increased growth.

In even more exciting news, June 5Th marked our 54 th year. Wow, that is a
long time. We celebrated by going to CB. (I have cut back on trips to BK).
I hope GSH has decided that she has me honed to perfection and will no
longer need to strive for continual improvement. We shall see. We also
celebrated by dragging the Ford tractor up the driveway. We no longer have to go
out in the road as trailer lot is vacant. I told tractor driver to make a
wide swing across the open area. She cut quite short, ran under the maple tree
and broke off a large limb. I was relieved and surprised as I knew the
muffler and exhaust manifold were doomed when I saw it coming. As you can see,
I still have areas to work on in progressing her toward perfection.

One of the more fun projects was taking the headlight off the parts tractor
Farmall "C" and modifying them to go on the Cletrac. I have no picture
this time but will by and by so please note. Gwendolyn left w/o completing the
Cletrac painting so guess I will have to unless I get an offer from a
willing worker.

Saturday was another trip to Seymour to work on the drag car. I mostly give
helpful advice. Apparent progress has slowed as the structure is all done
but a bunch of details remain. They pulled the engine to install the
special slingshot torque converter, put the assembly back in and worked on
details while Dwight completed the electrical. Electrical is very complex. It is
all computer controlled. It appears that the driver punches in the various
starting and shifting RPM, holds the throttle wide open, presses a button
and holds on. The first trial runs are wishfully scheduled for July 4. Stay
tuned.

Lets see, We dot the little A-C running which was overhauled last Winter.
It is still in the barn. Dragged the Ford up here and got it running. I
intend to put it back in the barn tomorrow. Charlie dragged his zero cost A-C
up to get it running. Stay tuned. I fiddled with the Farmall
H" all week trying to get it so it will operate on duel fuel mode. So far,
it will not operate on any fuel or mode.
More progress on the Cletracv.

Tis the season !

First pie at 10:15 tonight. Ha!
