Aligning Ducks

15 photographs·208 words

Monday morning, bright and early. Lab checks were low so I got the works,
Blood and platelets. Maybe from a family member !

Later in the week we had company, just back from a trip to Ghana.
Best I can tell she has a couple of keepers.

Anyone get a big, heart shaped, box of chocolates? These are left over from
years past.

It took no time at all to master the wonder horse.

Dave Edwards came by. Alan kept him entertained.

The scooter. The rear wheels are not correct. So far, we have not been able
to find the correct ones.

The cider press.

Frame assembled, waiting for the basket.

We did get two items finished and ready for transport to the staging area.

We collected knick knacks and doo dads from all over the world. Each has a
story. Diane is going through the cabinet and taking notes from my memory
collection. Stay tuned.

Meals on wheels continue to be kind to us.

While the deliveries were here, they hit the crawler a couple of sound
licks.
