While collecting information for my family history project, I
became acquainted with Ruby Duncan Miller. She was born in 1900 , and went
to school in a little school house which was located in the field beside
Herb's house.

Mom went with me several times on trips to listen and learn.

I went to visit Ruby one day, her daughter met me at the door, said Mom
died and handed me a video tape she had of her mom telling about her life.

In due time, I was able to get Jonathan to digitize the tape and copy to a
memory stick.

In recent times various people have suggested it would be meaningful if I
would consolidate all the information I have collected.Last night, in
thinking about that as a project I started to look for the memory
stick.Then the tape.

We hunted. High and low. Cannot find the video tape or the memory stick.

I may have loaned one or both to some family member. At least I hope so.

Can anyone help ?
