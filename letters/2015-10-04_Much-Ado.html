October 4, 2015 · Sunday

Much Ado

8 photographs·216 words

I finally got a note from Lil Sister. Much to my surprise, she also has
some aches and pains but is muddling along. Those who know her from Summer
Camp bathroom sanitation training might consider sending her a kind word.

Speaking of aches and pains, I went in with Herb to see Dr. Sago. As near
as I can tell, the medical community is at a loss as to plan F (I believe
is next). Stay tuned.

If anyone is interested in my woes and rumors of woes let me know and I
will send a list.

The fellow from Wheels of YesteryearSC called wanting me to name a price on
the '55. I sent an email price and have heard no more.

It rained a lot this week. In between rains, and sometimes during, we
split and stacked wood, mowed all around the place and sanded on the LBT.
It is now masked and ready for a coat of primer.

The weather looked miserable Thursday so I cut AT work.

I watch as much Auburn and Alabama football as I can hoping to catch a
glimpse of some famous family members. No luck so far, perhaps someone will
post something on Facebook.

I wonder of TTU football games are on TV in Utah? Or Switzerland?
