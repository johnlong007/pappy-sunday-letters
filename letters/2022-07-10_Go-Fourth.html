Go Fourth

13 photographs·200 words

https://phys.org/news/2022-07-human-lifespan-due-contributions-elders.html

Once again we sat on the Baptist church front lawn and watched the 4th
parade. No Central band.What year did we take trailer during Summer Camp?

I was very glad to see a lot of people waving a lot of flags.

GSH got to be on the community band float when they were in the Kingsport
parade. Joe this year in Blountville.

This picture says a lot to me.

Of course family outings are an integral part of the fourth. The family is
so dispersed that many could not make it. Maybe next year.

Bring up a fawn in the way it should go.

PBBD is spending more time with the old folks now that she has departed
ECC. She continued working on the '37 but only under close supervision.

The flex brake line from the frame to the rear axle housing broke. The
replacement she procured required some mounting modification.

I remain convinced the Little Blue Car is the best vehicle ever.

I did finish welding the scraper blade and the subsoiler.
