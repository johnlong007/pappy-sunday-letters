September 5, 2011 · Monday

Third try

5 photographs·281 words

I went to Dr. Monday and had the live one on my neck grubbed. If anyone
really wants to see a close up picture just let me know. I went to the Dr.
recommended by Herb and by Diane. I was very favorably impressed.
She cut out three and froze several others. Talking all the time.

Most ,but not all, my week was spent running the weedeater on the hill above
the soccer field. It really looks good but needs sticks and stones removed.

GSH spent most of her leisure time working on the table project.

Where the AT crosses Roan Mountain it runs down the backbone of the mountain
for about one mile. The trail is steep, rocky and entangled with roots. We
are doing relocation's which will zig zag all the way down making the trail
about two miles but much easier to hike. Tuesday, we had a group of willing
workers from Wright State in Ohio.

We did get in a lake outing.

I was going to include a picture of each of the tree skiers but since they
look very similar and it is getting late I will leave it with one.

The critters had a good week (mostly). I did get a picture of Pogo's brother
which is available upon request. I uncovered quite a number of turtles of
varioue sizes during the mowing along with one snake and one bull frog which
I inadvertnly bisected.

The two dogs are sort of coming along. Little dog loves to snatch items and
run away. She can make very short work of gloves or seat belts.
