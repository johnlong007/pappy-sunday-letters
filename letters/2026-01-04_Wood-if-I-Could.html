Wood if I Could

15 photographs·389 words

Growing up on the farm burning wood, one learns to plan ahead. It looks
like we have enough dry wood to get through this Winter without burning the
furniture.
But, next Winter is another question. Fortunately, providence and Wayne
stepped in to provide a potential solution. A large Ash tree blew down and
a much larger Beech blew down.
This week, Wayne cut the Beech off at the root ball and, working under
close supervision, cut the trunk into manageable lengths.

The Ash from last week.

Not everyone has a delicate blossom daughter skilled with a peevey.

My call for wood splitters was answered.

It has been quite a while since I got a chance to use the Bobcat. It was
fun !

Not only did Wayne saw to firewood length he helped with moving.

The beach is tough. What you see is one section split into pieces. I hold
wedges and Diane does the wook.

Aaron and his crew came to town. It was a fine visit. I got to watch the
kids and talk with each a bit. Aaron got to release his repressed
frustrations.

Our family Dr. made a house call. Diane grilled her as to what the NP write
up from Monday said and meant.
I remain in the dark.

Linda came to town to get checked out on being a grandmother.

Linda had a birthday. I got up at 6 to send her a Happy Birthday note.
Then, I noticed it was Jan. 4.
My new year's resolution was to not make any mistakes. Blew it right off.

Prince charming and Sleeping Beauty have been with us all week. They come
and go like ships in the night. No waves created.

Allison and Max came by. I opened the Summer Camp folder so Allison could
start teaching Max and her little ones how tough she had it as a child.

All seemed duly impressed.

I like to watch the little ones try the jumper. This one got the hang of it
in record time. Must have been the instructors.

This picture goes into my "bring up a child in the way they should go
folder".
