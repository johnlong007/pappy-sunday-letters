December 15, 2019 · Sunday

Ups and Downs

9 photographs·305 words

Curch service Wed. night was the children decorating pre-fabed gingerbread
houses. It was better than a three ring circus.

Then Thursday night the Blountville Christmas tree lighting service. Barry
Staubus gave a fine talk. I know it was fine ,even thought i could not hear
a word , because it was brief and people applauded.

Saturday was the Blountvile Christmas parade. Many family members have been
active participants. This year was cold and wet but not miserable.

We were huddled together under GSM's red coat at our regular spot. A young
mother with two boys, maybe 7 and 9 was nearby. Of course, no candy was
tossed our way. After while, one of the boys picked up a mini-snickers and
brought to GSH. Later, when we started to leave stiff from cold and
sitting, both boys came over to steady us off the slope. Without beiing prodded
by Mom as best I could tell.

The high point of the week.

Our family pill pusher looks after the dogs pill prescription. Max was
more willing to take his heart worm medicine than some other members of the
family down her pills.

Quite a bit of time trying to get the '37 ready for painter. I do not want
to get embarrassed again.

Most of my effort was on the grill/nosecone.

Bummer for the week was on '63 work.
Monday I went over to agree on top selection and give him advance payment.
He gave me a receipt which I did not look at until Friday. He had entered a
price 10% higher than originally stated.

I try to deal only with workers who are honest and competent. That sure
sent up a red flag about the honest part.
