October 8, 2017 · Sunday

An Apple Each Day

13 photographs·367 words

The apple event in Erwin has turned into quite a production. We went over
Friday and found yard sales going on for miles as we approached town. We
did not go into Erwin but instead visited my Aunt Flo who lives about a
mile out.

She hates having her picture made so I snuck a picture while she was making
a REAL pot of coffee.

Then we walked down the street to a yard sale.

​Flo had her 90th birthday last week, Still alert in mind and body. The
person selling stuff in the picture said his mom died and left a house ful
of stuff he wanted to get rid of. He had a lot of Southern Pottery ware.

​https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Ridge_(dishware)

Flo had worked as a painter at the pottery after high school. She said she
painted "leaves"

I asked if she painted the ones on this plate and she said no, hers were
better than that. Anyway, thinking some persons might be interested in
pieces which may or may not have been painted by their great aunt I bought
a box of pieces.

​Not much else to report this week. Too dry to do much mowing. No AT work.
I did wash and wax the truck. Washed it all and waxed the cab.

​Some places on the bed need a bit of touch up so waiting to see if PBBD
will take care of that for me.​

We bought this place from Dale Laughlin. His brother Albert is next door
neighbor (the one who allowed Frisbee games). A good neighbor. Dale died
recently and Albert came into possession of his farm implements. None of
which he needed as he had his own. I am pondering purchasing some of his
duplicate items. Might take items and one of Herb's tractors and leave at
hillside. Thanking about it.

​We are also pondering updating the pool room. The pool was never designed
for swimming. It was energy storage for the house. To upgrade into a
serious swimming pool it needs to be able to maintain 82 degrees in the
room and in the water.
That is going to require some accurate calculations and a fair amount of $$.
