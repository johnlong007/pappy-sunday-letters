January 17, 2021 · Sunday

Tradition !

9 photographs·318 words

Donna's birthday was Jan. 13th, mine the 15th. For about 25 years we went
to visit them for our birthdays. Some will remember the visits.
Philadelphia, Tampa.Minneapolis, crown point, Indianapolis.

My sister's birthday is the 17th. In recent years we have been going to
meet her for our shared birthday . Always taking along a cake with hard
icing. One year we met and ate in the Detroit airport on our way to a
mission trip somewhere.

I hare to give up on anything ! This year, because of the virus I wimped
out on going to visit Sis. Rats, I am well aware there is no guarantee of
tomorrow.

GSH and I got our first round of shots this week.

Why on earth would anyone want to celebrate being 85?

Laura made her usual cake with hard icing for me to deliver and share with
Sis. Delivered to PBBD's house for the party, joined by Sarah and whats his
name.

Meredith came by here with a grapefruit poppy seed cake with caution I
should not eat or might drop dead.

In addition, I got notes from children and grandchildren. A visit from
George Thorpe, AT fellow worker. Invitation for breakfast from Syble,
friend since 1965.

For those who think 85 or 91 is old, we went to see Aunt Flo, will be 94 in
Sept. I had not been to visit for a while due to virus but got a birthday
card from her which said "come on over". we went.

Mu only meaningful work was on the '37 windshield frame. I delivered the
glass to get a new one cut and worked a lot on rebuilding the rotted frame.
A lot remains to be done.

Still waiting !

This profile picture is NOT a deer.

R & R.
