January 11, 2015 · Sunday

One Down

9 photographs·338 words

Monday was off to Vanderbilt with Herb. We stopped to check on Lisa and
the children. Today I was telling the breakfast boys that Lisa and James
have 6 children 10 and under. GSH reminded me that they are not the first.
Lisa had prepared a fine lunch which I rather enjoyed.
On the way home Herb kept telling me we should have asked for some banana
pudding to go. I agreed.

The trip to Vanderbilt went very well. When Herb brought up the issues
which had stymied the process at Duke, The Dr. just asserted "we can handle
that" without any hesitation. He wants a bunch of biopsies of the site
before starting to operate. A road map. Due to distance and travel time, it
was decided to get the biopsies in Johnson City. That work is scheduled for
tomorrow. Stay tuned.

On Tuesday, Carl scheduled another AT work day at the Hampton Blue trail.
This time, Benj went along and spent the day making small rocks out of big
ones. Seems like Tennessee used to have a school for teaching that subject
but don't guess it is taught at A & M.

It was cold Wed. I dug ditch trying to find the septic system discharge
line. Same for Thursday. No success. But, I am glad I can still do hard
work even if futile. I have officially given up and turned the project over
to family contractor to get the system back in operation.

Some time back, one of the Grandchildren, I think Maria, asked what was my
most interesting experience this past year. I promised to think about it.
That has led to reviewing the whole year. Today while I was running around
it came to me

Watching the Grandchildren fish !

I think when I get old I am going to write a couple of books. Maybe more.

"Tips for making child rearing easier "

and

"Fishing for Trout"

Finally an opportunity to compare and contrast. Which (if either) is the
real me?
