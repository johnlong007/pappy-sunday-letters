May 11, 2014 · Sunday

Mothers, Daughters, Wives

12 photographs·567 words

Where has the week gone? Poof.

Herb got released from hospital and seems to be doing well. It appears the
stent has helped his circulation and he will be able to do more while
getting tired less.

​

Dwight had a handicap accessible van brought in for inspection. I crawled
around under it and took pictures but have not heard of any decisions.

​

I worked with Sanford to get Sue Ketron in contact with a potential buyer
for Hal's car. It has been parked in a shed for several years and really
looked ratty. Now I wish I had let him leave it here. Oh well, even my
hindsight is not 20/20.

Wayne started to go retrieve his oldest child and found the vehicle would
not crank. I got the truck out and when to tow him (and the vehicle) home.
Tried to take pictures of him crawling around under it in the Food City
parking lot using my smart (er than me) phone but failed. So, no pictures
of that less than joyous event. You will just have to imagine it.

I did work on the LBT just a bit this week. Started doing some body filling
to smooth out the many patches I installed. Would sort of like to get it
running by and by.

GSH painted the burning deck. Looks much better. Two more trips to procure
paint and I believe she will have it.l

​

​
I made two trips to the hills this week. One to dig a few ramps and to
retrieve my ramp digging tool. Found it. For work day, Carl sent me to walk
from Carvers Gap to Hughes Gap and cut blowdowns along the way. It just so
happened that that section of the trail passes through some prime ramp
patches.

​

I took all the requisite PPE along to wear!

​But then it was so hot I took off my long sleeve shirt forgetting that is
part of the outfit. Rats.

One day I gathered brush along the driveway and threw in the brush pond.
Those who have participated might be interested to know it is not nearly as
much fun when working by oneself.

​Fortunately, I still have one willing worker.

​
For some time the A/C on the vw has been decaying. Today, in return for not
taking his picture under the vehicle, I took a picture beside a different
one.

​
Linda came to visit her mother, In return, her mother fed her supper and I
fed her breakfast. It was fun.

​
Pat Loves lives out close to the lake. His wife has a horse and a donkey.
They have five acres but recently purchased 25 A adjoining. With the
purchase, they got an old house and a few sheds. I went out to walk over
the place with them. The house is a real delight. It is a single room log
house which has had structures tacked on all around.

​
I told Karen that Anita could help her see just what needed to be done and
how to do it. Stay tuned.

In other exciting news, Meredith is all set to purchase a car (pending
passing final inspection tomorrow morning). She took me for a spin. One
thing to which I can attest. The brakes will stand it on its nose !

​Did all the mothers get breakfast in bed?
