December 4, 2011 · Sunday

Wee Pause

7 photographs·362 words

Almost nothing to report this week but I shall press bravely onward.

Monday it was back to the skin Dr. for my 1,000 mile checkup. The good news is she kept telling me how much better I look than my brother. Of course, in this case at least, beauty is only skin deep. She zapped quite a number on my face, neck, ears, hands, arms, chest and back. Sun lovers, we need to talk. No pictures from that event.

The major activity around here this week was sanding the finish from THE TABLE. A major endeavor but I believe it is ready for finish to be applied (again). The finish Bob ordered has arrived but it appears that application will be delayed until after the holidays. Multi coats over multi days and all at moderate temp. outside .

On the human interest side, the dogs ahave learned to watch for my return when I go to run (or shuffle as the case may be). They have not learned to wait outside by the ramp but perhaps that will come in due time.

They have also learned to relax and help me while I am surfing.

A couple of big social events this week. We went with Dave and Carol to Christmas Light program at Natural Tunnel. For those who have not gone, I recommend it. It cost $5 to ride down into the canyon on the lift but no charge to walk down (better have a light !). Two trains went through while we were there. There is no cost to you local entertainment of quite variable perceived quality.

I took a picture looking out and up toward the sky. It would be interesting to see what one would get with a really good camera.

The other really big event was the Blountville old tractor owners Christmas dinner. It was nice, Several children two of whom have mental and thus physical handicaps. They fit right into our crowd.

We did not get back across the mountain to visit Teressa, I did pave a bit more road. Anyway, the intend is to go as soon as I hear the mat is still out.
