April 12, 2026 · Sunday

Sorta Normal

13 photographs·205 words

Hospital stay drained me. Had to get a refill on about everything. Nurses
are really good to me. Had to go to CC on Monday and again on Thursday.

Good deed for the week. I got Mark Coates to look at Herb's '63 J. He
promised to come up with some recommendations and pass them directly to
Farmer Lisa.

While there, I looked over Dad's 8N and the original implements which I had
used.

Behold ! A load of ready to split firewood appeared.

Wedge holder.

Willing worker.

I was sent home from the hospital with several days of infection infusions
remaining, along with a trainer WW.

Friday, home health nurse Claudia arrived to take out the temporary port in
my left arm.

Parked in an empty classroom, waiting for SS when Janet Sells showed up
with a cup of coffee for me.

Goldfinches decided it is time to be irresistible.

Friends and family.

No work on the '37 PU, but progress on the cub. Repair work on the battery
box.

Almost ready to start reassembly of the cultivator.
