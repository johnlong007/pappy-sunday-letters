November 8, 2020 · Sunday

Life Goes On

13 photographs·375 words

I spite of elections and virus.

We got a note from SS teacher saying a person in the class Sunday had
tested positive for virus. Later , we got a note telling us to stay away
from church service today. My inclination was to ignore the directive but
not wanting to be a stumbling block we complied. But, not as a happy
camper. Takes a lot out of the special of Sunday.

Last week I included a picture of GSH and asked who could identify
location. No one responded, so, Here is a hint.

But, to keep the theme, how about this site?

The Animal Farm.
When we come in the drive after dark this is the usual sight. Know what
animal is departing?

However, when we come up drive in the daytime: waiting for the laggards.

We have a lot of groundhogs. The dog loves to harass them. It appears he
caught one too far from the burrow. I bet the groundhog was not aware how
fast he could climb a tree.

We went to Erwin to give farmer Lisa a hand with some mowing. She does well
in the wide open spaces but a bit shy about tricky spots. The part between
the branch and the road has been allowed to grow up for last few years. I
mowed it.
My audience watched in rapt attention from across the field.

We stopped by Sarah's demolition project a couple of times. Here Sarah and
PBBD are working in the second floor construction site. We mainly helped by
eating lunch with them.

At breakfast with the GOB's a few years back one (who liked to talk)
allowed anyone over 65 had no business up on the roof. Guess that gets me
and Santa.

We spend a couple more days sorting and storing parts.

The steering well in the '57 is in very poor condition. However he sent
along a spare which is not as bad. Reconditioning a vintage sterring wheel
will run upwards of 1K. GSH is polishing the first round of building back
on the practice wheel.
