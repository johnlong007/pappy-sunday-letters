March 24, 2013 · Sunday

Spring !

15 photographs·627 words

Timing of the garden plowing was good. Although the ground was very
wet when plowed we have had several hard freezes since then so it is
going to work up very well, Soon time to plant potatoes. Perhaps on
Good Friday which, as I remember, is when we planted in the days of my
youth.

On the other side of that coin, It has been mostly too cold for my
taste. So, as before I spent a lot of time indoors chipping and
scraping on the MM. The project is mostly at a standstill waiting on
the engine man. Come to think of it, so is the '37 but a different
engine man. I did go ahead and paint one front wheel with some of the
leftover Case SC paint.

On Tuesday I went to the family Dr. for 1,000 mile checkup. As part of
that they checked BP and found it to be 130/70, so, guess the pills
are working. The only other thing they found was that I am getting
old. Given the choices, I suppose that is a good sign.

For AT work this week, Carl sent a bunch of us on top of a rock
outcropping above Dennis Cove looking out over Hampton. He wants to
cut a trail across a bare rock. Four of us worked all day and maybe
got the trail 6 inches wide. It needs to be about 2 feet wide. The
rock is quartzite and just chips. It will not split unless a hole or
slot is drilled for a starting place. It was really cold with a cold
North wind. I had heavy gloves and two packs of hand warmer. Still got
cold to the bone.

Erwin Formation - White, vitreous quartzite, massive, with interbeds
of dark-green silty and sandy shale, minor siltstone, and very
fine-grained sandstone. Thickness 1,000 to 1,500 feet.

D & D decided their pool had outlived its useful life and took it
down, They wanted to get rid of the sand base and GSH wanted it for
her latest outdoor project, perhaps the final solution to the problem
area right in front of the swing.

In more exciting news, Christopher came in to visit during Spring
break. His Dad promised to send him back in a family car, so he came
by and extracted the Daytona from storage.
However, Bob being Bob chose to send him off into the sunset driving
the beamer. Clearly, the Daytona did not measure up (or Charlie has
started a chain reaction) as Bob went out and procured a NOS Miata the
next day !

In case anyone is curious about the drag car in process, the
cage/chassis is back from being powder coated and final wiring, tubing
etc is under way. Launch is 2-3 weeks away according to usually
optimistic sources.

Saturday evening, Maria came by with a fresh fish. I am not yet sure
of the species as did not get to examine closely. Pretty sure not a
Carp. I will keep watch. After she left, I noticed some new items on
my to-do list. I may need help in implementation.Suggestions are
solicited.

For some time, it has been apparent that one of the drive way pine
trees was dying and was leaning. I have been having quite an internal
debate as to how to make it fall doing the least possible damage.
Overnight, the issue was resolved.

Maria, Gwendolyn and Benjamin have all been participating in a special
youth orchestra for several years. Today, they had their final event
with the orchestra at a Milligan concert. It was good.

On my way home while coming through Elizabethton, I spotted an item
which might be of interest to the newest car collector (beside Bob)
family member.
