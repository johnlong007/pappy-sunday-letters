August 15, 2010 · Sunday

Dog Days of Summer

12 photographs·249 words

Many suggestions for a name for the New Dog have been received. I am
leaning toward "Fatty" as in "all right Fatty up in the back where you belong".
But, GSH said no so I may try "Porky" due to appetite and potential.

Most of the discretionary time this week was spent moving saw logs from
Blountville annex to the pending sawmill location.

Tuesday AT work day was canceled so I went Huckleberry picking at Grayson
Highland with Kim. About a two hour drive (each way) with five hours spent
berry picking in the hot, hot sun.



As you may remember, I opened floor for suggestions on useful disposal of
a bountiful grape crop. Turns out I counted chickens which had not hatched.

In case anyone is interested, here is a picture of the Mulberry tree limb
which was entangled in the cherry tree leading to my hand injury. The short
stump is from the Mulberry.



In case the city dwellers have forgotten life on the farm.

Friday we took the day off, first one in a long time, and drove across the
mountain through Hot Springs (where Granny Garland went to school 1900-1905
more or less) and into the park to see Elk and other sights. It was
raining so all we saw was mist and fog but did stop and visit Herb's garden on
the way home.

I have a watermelon patch out front. Seems to be coming along. I think this
one is promised to someone.
