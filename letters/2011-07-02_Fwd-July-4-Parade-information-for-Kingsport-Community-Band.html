From: Lick, Wendy Wilkinson
To: Kingsport Community Band members
Sent: Fri, Jul 1, 2011 12:19 pm
Subject: July 4 Parade information for Kingsport Community Band

Our parade position is number 65, and we'll be on the same float as last year.
I'll attempt to attach a photo so you'll see what the float looks like. It
should be waiting for us right in front of the Renaissance Center, which is on
Center Street not far from the intersection with Wilcox.

If anyone needs to ask a parade official for help in finding the float, remember
that it is the float of Marcum's Pharmacy and Berry's Pharmacy. Scot (director)
will have his cell phone, (423) 571-5739.

At 8:30 AM Monday we will meet at the Kingsport Towne Center (formerly known as
the Fort Henry Mall), in the outside parking lot near Belk. This is the parking
lot alongside Memorial Boulevard, close to the intersection with Fort Henry
Drive. We will carpool over to the vicinity of the Renaissance Center by
loading into a few of the larger vehicles. The drivers will drop off their
passengers as near the float as practical and entrust their instruments and
chairs to them (if desired) then go seek parking. There should be some spaces
within a few blocks at that time of day.

We will play Battle Hymn of the Republic - the marching version.

WHAT TO BRING:
* your instrument
* your music (in flip-folder with lyre if appropriate for your instrument
- there will be no stands but sometimes flute players tape their music onto
another person's back)
* a hat
* sunglasses
* sunscreen
* plenty of water (!!!)
* smallish folding chair (except tubas and trombones will sit on benches,
maybe a few others)

The parade starts at 10 AM. We'll be sitting around waiting in the sun for
quite some time, then there's the parade itself, so you really do need to take
care of yourself. Sunstroke could ruin your whole day.

Dress comfortably. Shorts are fine. Red, white, and blue (or any combination
thereof) would be ideal. Most people will probably wear a white shirt since
that's what we're used to.

The parade ends at Memorial Park near Dobyns-Bennett. Our float will continue
on and drop us off at our waiting cars at the mall parking lot. A few of us
will stay to take our intrepid carpool drivers back to their cars.

We will have a guest drummer, who will play during Battle Hymn and will fill in
with a cadence during the times we're not playing. His girlfriend also will
join us on clarinet. We hope our newest trombone player, Michael McDonough,
will be with us too. (Welcome!)

Traditionally we take up some money to tip the driver of the float. We all
appreciate a smooth ride! If you want to pitch in, bring a little cash.
Totally optional.

The weather forecast is calling for scattered thunderstorms. I'll seek
direction from Scot as to what we should do in the event of bad weather. He has
already said we won't perform in the parade if it's raining (would ruin some of
the woodwind instruments), but I don't know what to say about "scattered" storms
that might or might not make an appearance at the parade site. Might want to
check your email Sunday in case I know more by then.

The parade will be our last time together for the summer. We will not rehearse
again until after the Kingsport schools open up in the fall. That first
rehearsal will be August 18. Practice to keep your chops up - Scot is putting
together a fun and technically demanding program on the theme of "Americana" for
us to perform in the fall.

You'll probably hear from me a couple more times through the summer. We might
try to get a few volunteers to help organize our new music storage cabinets a
little at a time, and you'll hear more from me about choosing a logo and the
birth of our website.

See you Monday!

Wendy Lick
Secretary
Kingsport Community Band
wlick@eastman.com
