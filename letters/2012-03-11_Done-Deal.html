March 11, 2012 · Sunday

Done Deal

8 photographs·431 words

Sue's tractor is gone !

I Worked with Dom Hass to get it running. He listed on Craigs list and referred the serious ones to me. Several calls so it seemed it would sell easily. I lined up to meet a person noon Saturday and told all the others to call after that. A young father, two kids (maybe 5 &7) and his Dad for moral support. He said they have six acres and want to have a garden. I showed him how things work on an 8N helped load all the equipment and sent him on his way. I think he got a really good deal and perfect match for his needs. Sue was happy, asked what she could do for Don and me. I told her the next time she and Jill ( another widow) go on a cruise they can take us.

AT work was fun. A shelter was located not far from where the AT crosses the highway near Hampton. There have been ongoing problems with people using the shelter for improper purposes. So, it was decided to remove it entirely.
When we arrived to tear it down two hikers were just getting ready to move out.

We ripped off the siding and pulled the building forward to finish demolition

Mostly well seasoned Oak. Several of us worked to pull nails. Then, near quitting time, we learned that Carl only planned to save maybe 1/10 of the wood and burn the remainder. At that time we suggested it would be a lot easier to burn with the nails and comb the ashes with a magnet.

Not sure when we will burn the pile. Would not be a bit surprised if someone else does the deed for us.

GSH had a fine week. Her last week to be younger than I, at least for this year. Although jumping the gun a tad, Anita, John and Joe delivered a birthday cake.

We made a run to Erwin to check on people and things. James and his growing family were there. The goats were there, Herb and his trio of family females were there. A fine visit. With so many subjects to chose from, I took a picture of a blooming tree.

I did not get the '37 out and running with the advent of DST. But, progress continues. I am trying to get it all tacked together to determine that I do indeed have all the bits and pieces (or can procure).

Just to round out the week on a high note!
