November 27, 2016 · Sunday

Thankfully

11 photographs·445 words

A long time ago when I was a child, we got out of school at Thanksgiving.
That is the time we killed hogs. I suppose on Friday. It was always cold.
It took all day and up into the night before the meat was salted down and
the sausage ground.
One year was I was maybe 8 or 9 we were having a Thanksgiving play at
school and I had the role of an Indian. Probably not a speaking part.For
some reason I had a very clear picture of how an Indian should dress. I
suppose from a book as that was long before TV and I doubt I had ever been
to a movie.
Momma did our clothes washing in a kettle heated over an open fire. She dug
out trousers and a short sleeved shirt for me. I insisted it must have long
sleeves and a row of short ribbons on the sleeves and on the trousers. She
cut and sewed to do the whole job. Then heated a kettle of water to dye the
attire light brown.
At least I have a mental picture and another memory proving to me my mother
was a saint.
Running has deteriorated quite rapidly. I have been experiencing light
headed spells with increasing frequency and severity. So, Monday I went to
a cardiologist. A good visit.
He has me scheduled for a battery of tests on Dec. 6. Probably take all
day. Maybe pacemaker, maybe stent, maybe valve job. Stay tuned.

Fo auto work I got the tire for the '49 repaired without getting
decapitated. Split rim wheel.

​With Waynes help, we got Lisa's spare car running. But, more to do as it
has a ruptured Brake line.

A couple of weeks back we sawed up the old wood from the torn down barn. I
sorted out a lot of dirty mostly rotten wood. But, decided to go ahead and
saw it up also as Winter is coming. By the time I got through the saw would
not have cut hot butter.

​On day, we helped shovel out the basement of the log cabin Karen Loven is
getting Anita and Mike to refurbish.

​People come from miles around to watch.

​For the first time I can remember, GSH did not do ant Thanksgiving
cooking. We ate often and well but thanks to Diane, Anita , Linda and Laura.
On Friday After Thanksgiving several went to walk our section of the AT to
do some maintenance issues which had been reported.

​It was a fun outing.

​Saturday morning before departure, LLLL and family filled up on B & G.

​All in all, a fine week.
