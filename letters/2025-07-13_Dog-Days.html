July 13, 2025 · Sunday

Dog Days

12 photographs·296 words

We have a video conference with the UVA Dr. the week before Chemo week.
Monday, it was a Dr. we had not met with. When he came on he was looking
up at a screen I suppose my history.
He said " You are 89. you have been in treatment for two years.And you are
still alive !"

In other good news, the pool work is now complete. Diane pushed it along.

I commissioned a young, agile WW to clean the skylights. Turns out she is
not afraid of heights or hornets.

My pool temp target was 80. We are past that and climbing ! Now, all we
need is swimmers.

A & C bought things at an auction near Knoxville. They went down and
brought back a load of pearls to add to their extensive collection.

The 50 year old tires on the DB mower failed. The wheels have been cleaned,
and painted. New tubes are on hand and new tires on order. Stay tuned.

A problem was uncovered. The hitch had broken and was just stuck together.
Serious repair work is underway . Waiting for a competent metal
worker/welder. stay tuned.

Farmer Lisa and her PBBD came to visit bringing a tasty blackberry cobbler.
Burp !

Master gardener Nancy had Don deliver 3 yellow tomatoes.

Wayne cut up the Walnut trimmings and delivered to the splitting and
stacking site. Now, we need splitting and stacking WW's. Perfect Summer
Camp work.

Linda came for a visit. Today, she went to breakfast at Bondos with me,
David Quillen and several GOB's. Then church and SS. A fine day, she livens
things up.
