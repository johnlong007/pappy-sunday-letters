November 29, 2009 · Sunday

Practically Perfect

7 photographs·309 words

Thanksgiving week and much I am thankful far.

No bad news, just good news and better news.

Dave is pondering what he is going to do (or not do). In the meantime, we
were back on do-gooder duty Monday. We installed hand bars in a shower for
an elderly couple . Only one trip to Lowe's (but two stores) and one trip to
Home Depot. Also, we went to an ETSU ball game with Dave and Carol on Wed.
night as no church service. We stopped at Bob Evans for supper making a
fine night out.No pictures of any of that.

Furthermore, although Dwight (and to an extent I) made progress on the
Studebaker, I am not including pictures. In case anyone is interested, the plan
is to be able to drive it in driveway by Christmas. Since the deer
(usually three) and the turkeys (usually nine) look just like last week, I am not
including their pictures.

I did visit Hal and Sue, no news there which is a good as one can expect.

No AT work as there was a special outing on Saturday (which I skipped).

As you should know by now, JW did not send all the car parts back. So, a
friend and I went foraging foe Studebaker parts one day. We found a
treasure trove on Cripple Creek near Watuga.

However, I decided to give JW one more cahnce. I made another run to his
place and found he had a box of parts for me. Most (but not all) the missing
items.

I think we will be able to make all the needed pieces or get them from the
new found source.

Of course the real event was Thanksgiving day/dinner folloewed by apple
peeling then apple butter making.
Children, grandchildren, spouses, Sis and a guest appearance by Herb and
James (with child).
