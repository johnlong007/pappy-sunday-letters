May 30, 2021 · Sunday

Home Again !

12 photographs·224 words

Max went to the beauty parlor. Summer haircut, teeth cleaned and a bath.

Wayne washed the truck before returning it. Nice clean truck caused me to
notice a place where the paint was badly oxidized. I tried polishing by
hand and made it much better. Then I got the polisher and cut right through
the paint. Rats.

I came into a nice, pop up canopy. Set it up over the mower. The rains came
down, the canopy collected water and collapsed causing three of the joints
to buckle. Time for a couple of MSME's to spring into action.

We have a lot of wildlife in our wildlife park. This morning on the way out
I had to stop in the drive to let the turkey flock go across. It must be
the season as one had his tail feathers spread. I was too slow getting
camera ready to get his picture.

Max much prefers to chase. and tree, squirrels. We have plenty.

Piglet and Rover used to take out Groundhogs. These two do not work as a
team.

A fine Memorial Day event.

We have a lot of this kind of bird. They hop.

Meanwhile, in a far away place.
