March 20, 2011 · Sunday

Romance, Mystery and Intrigue

12 photographs·383 words

Did not happen here !
The major event for the week was wrestling the large log down from the
hill. Wayne helped, GSH helped, Benjamin helped Jonathan sort of helped and
both dogs helped. We finally got it down, tore up a bunch of the small white
pines, broke some maple seedling and gouged a furrow in the hill only to
find out the log was not solid in the center. I should have known.

So after getting it out, we decided to split it in 1/2 and then figure out
what to do with the halves. We put Bob's ripping chain on Dwight's 20" Farm
Boss and cut a full length slot 20" deep.

Then I drove the wedges into the slot. This story is to be continued!

Tuesday was work day on the AT. We went to double Springs shelter to
renovate the shelter, cut some trees endangering the shelter and do trail work
in and out. Cutting one of the trees was interesting, I watched. First a rope
was attached, then a group of willing workers pulled on the rope while
Carl cut the tree. As you might guess, it fell straight toward the pullers
since that was where Carl aimed it. They scattered like Quail.

Closer home. GSH has continued to work on putting padding on the floor of
the car to be under the carpet.

Some years back, Dan found a '63 Chrysler in a junk yard in SC. Herb and I
took a rig down and rescued the car to be a parts car for our soon to be
restored '63's. Of course, the car had been out in the open for many years
and has been out since being brought here. But, I do not like cars left out
in the weather. Having recently emptied some barn space (and before it got
purloined) I got the parts car moved into the warm and dry . Rejoice.

Meredith has expressed and interest in having her own private garden.
This being the first day of Spring, I figured she would want to get started
soon. It remains to be seen if she is willing to share gardening activity
with fellow Summer Campers or not. The space will be expanded as the season
unfolds.

In other signs of Spring.
