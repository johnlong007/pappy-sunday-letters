April 17, 2022 · Sunday

Oops- Easter

7 photographs·148 words

I continued with the negative scanning project this week. I have scanned
about 2K and have am over half.

I spend a lot of time looking and remembering. Also wonder if anyone will
actually ever look at them.

Declared victory on the drain line and refilled the ditch.

Mowed the whole place, at least the part you can see from the house.

Apple and cherry trees are in full bloom. The deer are waiting. Munching
the blue berries while waiting.

We found the smallest of the big logs was too large for the Bobcat. But
managed to drag it down with the tractor and wrestle up onto the sawmill.

Aaron and family were in to visit. They went to church with us today.

Catherine is working to steal my cats heart away.

Mail bounced on first edition to some folks so thought I would try a
different route.
