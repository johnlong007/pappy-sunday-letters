March 6, 2011 · Sunday

Highs and not so Highs

11 photographs·327 words

The roof:
Monday the rains came down and the leaks came up. Same place.

In the meantime, outside we had a lot of runoff.

Tuesday we worked on the AT near Chestoa. The hiking club is really the
hiking and canoeing club. Tuesday we could have used the canoes.

After draining the swamp as best we could, we went on up the trail and
built a cross over for a ditch which the trail crossed. It turned out well.

This was the week for downed trees. Wayne has a fine one at his backdoor. I
helped start sawing it into chunk size.

Several more outings will be required. In the meantime, ,back at the ranch,
work has started on a big tree which fell us on the hill here. I have it
pretty much trimmed and ready to try to figure a way to snake it out. I
measured it as 25 inch dia, 25 feet from the base.

Equally exciting, The Jack family has purchased new furniture and in the
finest family tradition , other family members have claimed the castoffs. It
was striking to me as to how different John looks with his 50's vintage
haircut.

GSH decided that the front lawn needed refurbished. It was being taken over
by moss. We raked off the moss, applied lime, peat moss, grass seed and
matting on the bare places. Then the downpours returned.


In other news, the roof man came over and sealed a siding seam. It did
not leak in the latest down pour so we shall see.

Also, GSH helped drag the crawler up from the barn. It never hit a lick.
Try to start it or drag it back? That is the question.

It snowed Sunday PM. I was covered, wet and cold when I got back from
running, But, no one knew or cared. Alas.

However, later the dogs had a fine time playing in the snow. They are
coming right along.
