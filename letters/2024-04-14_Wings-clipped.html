April 14, 2024 · Sunday

Wings clipped

16 photographs·374 words

My brother Jimmy died this week. I am the last one left. From what I hear,
things are not going well among his children.I guess not uncommon but
difficult to comprehend.

I started the week at the cancer center for Chemo infusion. The folks at
the center are really good to me. Still a funny feeling to be on the
receiving end and I really appreciate the family members who are donating.

A picture of nurse Bailey (HS friend of Meredith) showing me a picture of
her daughter Tilly

Cancer center has a gaggle of do-gooders who scurry around tryin to ply
patients with food and drink. For the first time, we took and ate a
sandwich.

I understand some family members traveled far and wide to view the
eclipse.. I waited out front, it did get a bit like dusk

The truck failed to start. Turned the master tech loose who determined both
batteries are dying of old age.
Today, she went with Wayne to procure two new batteries

The floor in carry all died of old age and exposure.

Now, the implement is stored in a dry spot

See the place under the Ag bldg? That is where I park the tractor.

When I park it. I just held the clutch and brake while shutting off the
engine
I later decided to go mow some more but it would not crank . I got off
in the tight spot which was to the right of the mower, fiddled with wiring,
the engine started, the mower started forward, knocked me down, ran over my
right arm and my right foot..
Diane was there and shut the engine off..

Work continues on the 37 pick up. Nothing broken and no one hurt, so far.

The ramps are looking good. I hope, plan, intend to get back to normal
walking in time to go to the secret Ramp patch within just a very few weeks.

Apple trees which have been pruned and dormant sprayed should look like
this about now.
