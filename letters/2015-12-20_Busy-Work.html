December 20, 2015 · Sunday

Busy Work

9 photographs·299 words

I pretty much stayed close to home this week so I could shuttle back and
forth to Erwin as need arose. Just when it looked like Herb was making
progress moving better with his walker he fell and hurt his knee. Now, it
is swollen and sore and so is he.James came to visit him Saturday and
delivered a new toy.

GSH had a good week. She finished a long term project.

For the last couple of weeks I have been trying to move the Fire Engine
(FE) into the barn. Finally located a willing worker to help and started to
drag it into the barn.

Tractor started cutting out so I stopped the project and on next trip out
purchased two new ($$$) fuel filters. Got them installed after much
fumbling and bumbling then could not get air bled out. So, I disconnected
the high pressure fuel discharge line at the filter. Then, when I tried to
reattach, I cross threaded it. Woe is me. Now waiting for a new fuel pump
and an able bodied (at least nimble fingered) helper to get the project
restarted.

Did spend more time crawling around under the FE. I dug around in the pump
gearbox and fished out a bearing which had come off the input shaft
allowing it to become disconnected . Stay tuned.

GSH has begun to get the house decorated for Christmas. She has several
nativity scenes from around the world. Here is one.

This week the community band had a guest performer for the program at the
mall on Saturday.

Does everyone know we have a family member ensconced at West Point? We do !

I did not go on AT work outing this week. Maybe next week.

Can anyone match or exceed such an exciting week?
