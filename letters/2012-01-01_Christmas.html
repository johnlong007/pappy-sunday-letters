January 1, 2012 · Sunday

Christmas !

8 photographs·389 words

As is our tradition, Christmas at the ole home place was a bit delayed. But, the events were well attended. I believe 37 stockings hung by the chimney with care.

Beth, Jake, Aaron and Vanessa were here in spirit I am sure. Actually, Beth and Jake were here in body late in the day and Vanessa will be arriving soon. Who knows, perhaps Aaron's arrival is just around the corner.

For those who are unsure what they missed:

In case anyone is curious about what Santa brought me, I received a DVD lecture series on Quantum Mechanics. Kenneth and I have been viewing together mostly. After I finish, I will make available to others for a nominal fee. Actually, I may deliver lectures for an even more nominal fee.

Other events did transpire this week. Wayne is rebuilding the engine removed from the car last year during Summer Camp. As can be seen he is working under Dwight's watchful eye. He is also following my advice "don't force it ! Get a bigger hammer"

Momma once told me about helping Daddy install a piston in the Essex. He was under guiding and pulling, she was above "tapping". He kept telling her to "Hit it !". The piston broke ! I asked what did he say, she told me "not a word".

He was really good at not crying about spilled milk. I miss them both.

Christopher is moving right along in ME at Clemson. He is going to Germany for the next semester for continuing co-op training. While he was here, I wanted to make sure he got some practical experience.

Since Charlie is also working on ME, I have paid a lot of attention to his practical training also

As part of my duties to keep an eye on all things mechanical, I went with Dwight to Seymour to help Pat install an engine in the new work in progress car. As you can see, as soon as he installs wheels, it will be ready to run.

James and his family have been visiting Herb all week. It may be that his family views the number of stocking hung with care as a competitive sport!

Decapitated !

The plan was to go visit Teressa today but a brake system failure on the truck aborted the attempt. Stay tuned!
