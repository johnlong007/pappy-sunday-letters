Trip Report:

George, Sanford, Jim Holston and I headed out early Monday in Sanford's
Chrysler van.
Breakfast in Middlesboro at Krystal Burger. It cost too much but made up for
it with poor quality and service.
On to Dayton and the AirForce Museum. Jim is a retired officer so his pass
got us in the gate and we drove right to the door.

We got out about 5 PM, so we headed for DC. Stopped at CB for Supper. It was
OK . Spent night in a Hampton inn in Columbus.
Up at 5:30, pretty good breakfast included then off to DC.
Drove direct to the new air museum at Dullas airport.

Lots of aircraft, lots of engines.All our air wars and all our friends and
enemies well represented.Also a space wing.
We got out at closing, 5:30 PM. We had planned to stay another night and
visit Arlington but Jim had forgotten his pass, so, we headed for home. Supper
at
a steakhouse (forgotten which one) was very good.
Home about 1:30 AM. A fine trip. about 1300 miles at 23 MPG.
