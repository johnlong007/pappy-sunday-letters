December 22, 2019 · Sunday

Joshua 10:13

10 photographs·287 words

Everyone get it?

A little bundle from heaven arrived for Aaron and Chelsea this week. Othr
bundles from other places have also started arriving near here .

Work continues in fits and starts on the '37.

The real issue is when is good enough good enough? The blue is primer. I
sand, fill and spray primer, repeat, then repeat.

We like to get outside and do things. We have "grasshopper" wood stacks
near end of driveway. I decided going over the hill to fetch wood is no fun
in the snow. So, we moved one of the woodpiles out front. About 2 weeks
worth.

What you see was all hay field when we bought this place.

Willing worker in action. This stack is quite handy, even in the rain.

Niece Lisa continues to battle mother nature. The water supply to her pond
has quit supplying water. We saddled our white horse and went over to
check. The water is supplied from creek along edge of property. Inlet
looked ok, outlet plugged with run off mud. Lisa got it open after we came
home. All she needed was a pep talk.

She also needs to decide she is quite capable of operating a back hoe.

One day we went to lunch with some old (literally and figuratively) hiking
buddies at Baysmont. It was fun but we are not tempted to stay.

We did not go to Friday breakfast group due to active hacking and coughing.
However, one of the ladies sent Pat a Christmas shirt and vest. She wore to
church today and got many positive comments.
