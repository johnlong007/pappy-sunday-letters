September 6, 2009 · Sunday

Lost Weak

7 photographs·497 words

Last Sunday night, I developed a sore throat. Over the week, I developed a
head and chest cold which has hung with me all week. Most of the week I
have been sort of frazzled so even less was accomplished than usual. I was
scheduled to go back to St. Francis for a 1,000 mile check up but Linda
rescheduled it for me. I sure am glad as GSH was really sick on Friday the day I
was due to be there.

No do-gooder work, no AT work, no visit the halt lame and afflicted. Maybe
this week will be better.

Kenneth brought the Alfa back with both doors a bit sprung and paint rubbed
off on door and fender. I spent all day Monday getting repairs made and
did a decent job. However, no pictures and even better no recordings of my
muttering.

One tire also had a slow leak. I pulled three nails out and took it down to
Morgan's to get it repaired. It came in second in the record for most
nails. Turns out it had 7 (Their record was 12).

The Alfa is now safely stored in the warm and dry. I am not a bit sure what
the future hold for it. Someone must love an Alfa to own one.

GSH felt about as bad as I did this week. But, she worked on her flowers
and on water sealing her deck.

For my part, I got most of the mowing done this week, woods. hill and
soccer field. I did not get up on the hill but am considering buying a mower to
do that job. Stay tuned.

The big event for the week was going to the drag strip with Dwight and Pat.
In case you have forgotten, The car ran initial 1/8 mile runs in about
5.75 seconds. Pat wanted to get down to 5 (to beat a dragster which comes to
the track and runs 5.04).
So, they installed a NO 2 system which first ran our last trip down and
showed no improvement tho it should have bumped HP from 800 to at least 1,000.

After going back to the shop and pondering, looking at the data and doing
much adjusting and head scratching Friday night the system was ready for
another trial. Pat made three runs.Each a bit better than the one before. I
did not write down the exact numbers but think last run was about 5.4.

I am confident he will get the 5.0. Stay tuned.

The days are getting shorter. It was dark during the runs and no flash
pictures are allowed. So, I got poor pictures. We need a better camera,
photographer or both. Stay tuned.

Around the home place, the fruit and nuts are ready to harvest. Paw Paws,
chestnuts, Hazel nuts and chinquapins. Better get 'em before the critters
beat you to it (Benjamin)

The pool is cleaner and warmer than ever before. Plus, no leaks in the
plumbing.
