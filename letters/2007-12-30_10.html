December 30, 2007 · Sunday

"10"

8 photographs·245 words

On Dec. 27, 2007 I completed 10 years of life in retirement. It has gone by
the fastest of any 10 years of my life. Before I retired, I dragged in a
bunch of old cars and tractors to make sure I did not run out of things to do. I
also sketched out a general plan of how I wanted to spend my time and things I
wanted to get done. Having a plan has served me well, I may not be spending
my time productively but at least I am doing pretty much what I want in the
order I want. I thought at the end of ten years I would be ready to stay
home and (finally) get to work on the cars and tractors. Turns out, I am not
quite ready, so, I have made a five year extension to my ten year plan. Being
painfully aware there is no promise of tomorrow much less five years, I am
ready for the next phase.

The big event for this week was floor mopping. Three times to get ready for
the visit by Saint Nick and once after his visit. No pictures of that! I also
got to take Allison out for driving around a couple of times. All the kids
are learning on manual transmission cars.

I did get a digital camera for Christmas. Now, if I can master the basics.
Captions upon request (maybe).
