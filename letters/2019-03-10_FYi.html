March 10, 2019 · Sunday

FYi

11 photographs·354 words

I feel very fortunate to have been able to get to know our grandchildren
(and their spouses) well. Probably quite a bit better than most.
Now, when I hear directly or roundabout from one of them that they do not
post notes and/or pictures because, in essence, "I lead a boring existence
and no one is interested."

I am.

I asked for opinion on the car bumper last week. All present and voting
said stick with the original plain. bumper. So, it is now back on the car.

Getting it re chromed would be about $800.

The only other progress on '63 was cleaning the interior.

The big event for the week was back to the dentist ! The proximate reason
for the trip was to get me approved as a demonstration project for her show
and tell at board exam next trip down (2 weeks). That objective was
accomplished and I got my approved stamp.

I have a difficult time getting my Fitbit steps during these sojourns.
Laura and GSH have more creative ways than slogging around the track at
Central.

The mighty Mississippi was still running full. At least mostly full.

We leave town through a waterfront storage neighborhood. On the way out we
came up a side street into which a big truck had started to turn. A 4
wheeler must have failed to see him turning or tried to scoot through
before he turned. The car was about 1/4 under the truck and parked beside
his rear wheels. I wonder how that will all be resolved.

Anita took care of the animals while we were gone. But , they did seem glad
to see us.
Cat came to meet GSH when we got home from church. She wore an outfit which
reminded me of high school days.

PBBD has embarked on a program to bring my abuse skin back to like new.
Dr. Rash, told me he needed to dip me into a vat of LN2.

Stay tuned.
