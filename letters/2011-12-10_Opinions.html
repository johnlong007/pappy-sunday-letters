The *Ferrari 308 GT4* and *208 GT4* were
mid-engined<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MR_layout>
 V8 <http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V8>-powered 2+2 cars built by
Ferrari<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari>.
The 308 GT4 was introduced in 1973, supplemented by the 208 GT4 in 1975,
and replaced by the Mondial 8 <http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_Mondial> in
1980 after a production run of 2,826 vehicles. It was sold with
"Dino"<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_Dino> badging
(continuing the Dino brand to differentiate non-V12s) until May 1976, when
all badging was replaced with "Ferrari" badging.
[edit<http://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ferrari_GT4&action=edit§ion=1>
]308 GT4

The *308 GT4 2+2* was a groundbreaking model for Ferrari in several ways:
It was the first production Ferrari to feature the mid-engined V8 layout
that would become the bulk of the company's business in the succeeding
decades, and was the first production Ferrari to feature
Bertone<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gruppo_Bertone> (rather
thanPininfarina <http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pininfarina>) bodywork.
Pininfarina was upset by the decision to give cross-town rival Bertone the
design, considering all they had done for Ferrari.
<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Ferrari_308_GT4_in_London.jpg>

308 GT4 in London <http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London>

The Dino 308 GT4 was introduced at the Paris Motor
Show<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Motor_Show> in
November 1973 and featured angular lines entirely different from its
curvaceous 2-seater brother, the Dino
246<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dino_(car)>,
and later brother, the GTB & GTS<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_308_GTB>.
The styling was controversial at the time, with some journalists comparing
it to the Bertone-designedLancia
Stratos<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancia_Stratos>
 and Lamborghini Urraco <http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamborghini_Urraco>.
The 308 GT4 finally gained the "Prancing Horse" badge in May 1976, which
replaced the Dino badges on the hood, wheels, and the steering wheel.

The chassis was based on the Dino 246 but was stretched for a 2,550 mm
(100.4 in) wheelbase to make room for the second row of seats. The
suspension was fully independent and the V8 was
mountedtransversely<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transverse_engine>
.

The 3.0 L (2927 cc) V8 was integrally joined with the gearbox and produced
250 hp (186 kW) in the European version and 230 hp (172 kW) in the
American; it had an alloy block and heads with dual overhead camshafts. The
induction system had 4 Weber 40 DCNF carburetors.

The 308 GT4 had a total length of 170.1 inches, and a wheelbase of
100.4 inches and weighed 2535 pounds; height was 46.5 inches and width was
70.9 inches.

2,826 308 GT4 coupes were produced between 1973 and 1980. There were 2
series of GT4. The earlier cars featured a twin distributor engine and
foglamps mounted in the front valance. Later cars had a single distributor
engine, with foglamps mounted behind the front grille.
