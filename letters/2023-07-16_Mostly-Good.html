Mostly Good

16 photographs·433 words

I have told many I avoid being "proud". and stick with being pleased.
Except ! in the case of a family member donating blood (or going
to church). This week, I had a lot to be proud of. Most of the pictures are
on the frame.
Being on the receiving end has given me a new perspective.

Monday Wayne took me to UVA for another bone marrow extraction. The
picture is the very nice NP who talked, did the set up then brought in a
real, live Dr. to do the procedure. His first go I think. It was a rather
slow, somewhat uncomfortable procedure but he did get the requisite samples.

Tuesday, on schedule, SF helped remove the packaging.Site is still somewhat
sensitive. MUCH more so than the procedure done at HVCH.

Friday, the Dr. himself called to pass along good news. Now, after a few
days off, I am to go in on Thursday when the control plan will be revealed.
Each contact has ended with "it will return".

About all I have been able to do is drive things. Bobcat, mower, tractor,
Children to distraction.

I moved some big root balls from open area over to the fence row,

Loaded firewood for Wayne. I first tried from the downhill side and stood
the Bobcat on its tail several times. Always a good plan to keep the seat
belt tight.

The big Oak log is ready for the sawmill now in three ten foot logs. Wayne
removed the excess

I dragged the chunk down to the woodyard.

PBBD and I attacked it to split into anvil bases for Charlotte.

We gave up and brought in the big guns. David brought his own admiring
audience.

The axle seal and axle bearing have been replaced but the axle is not yet
back in the car.

LLLL procured some real tomatoes, and we had serious BLTC sandwiches.

About every day we watched the expanded highlights from the bike race .
One family member is there, in person and blending right in with the
natives. Stay tuned.

Also, everyday, we go to the track. Not always with company from hither and
yon.

A few years back, I told my mother these are quince fruit. She made jelly
and sold it at the fair. Reckon God will understand? Anyone know for sure
what it is? Tree is loaded and the deer love em.
