February 21, 2016 · Sunday

Alas

12 photographs·253 words

Herb spent most of the week in the hospital with a urinary tract infection.
This situation is not going well. I did talk with Lil' sister, her long
time neighbor and friend Alice died.

The Studebaker came home from upholstery shop. I think it looks good but
smells awful ! All that crowd smokes. It did not cross my mind. How can I
get rid of the stale smoke odor?

It was a distressing return of the car. The high skill machine shop put the
freeze plugs in backwards. I had cemented the first one which leaked and it
seems to be holding. The one beside it blew out completely and the car came
home on the dolly. It has 8 freeze plugs. All difficult to access and two
impossible without pulling engine.

We got a snow job Monday. Pretty but wet and messy. The birds liked it.

We had an AT work day. Going South from Watagua lake starting up Pond
Mountain. We did trail and switchback repair.

The trail maintainers dinner and award banquet was Friday night. I did
quite a bit of metal repair work on the '63. It is going along very well.
GSH continued to work on the upholstery. She spent a lot of time pondering
the two front bucket seats.

Dave Edwards came by Saturday. I missed getting a picture but did get
caught up on family matters. Seems all is well. But, he is an orphan now
and only passing through.

Speaking of passing through !
