December 11, 2011 · Sunday

Strange Things Done

6 photographs·741 words

First the good news. After wandering in the wilderness for over a year, Dave and Carol have returned to CHCC.

During that time he has fought Lyme and his Mom has died. His Dad is fighting lung cancer and depression from losing wife of 67 years. Dave is going to Ohio tomorrow to take him to the Dr. However, one of these days, we should get back to serious do-gooder work on Mondays. At least I hope we will.

For my own effort at doing good (at least do no harm), I went to Erwin to check on the dogs, goats , daughters (DAR so to speak) and maybe even Herb. No sign of life ,truck gone and the watch dog asleep. So, I came back home. Turns out Kaylee had driven to school in the truck and Herb was out wandering aimlessly.

Since the table is in limbo and the Studebaker is awaiting a driver, I decided to start a new project.

Way back in 1972 we obtained a Honda Trail 90 M'cycle. GSH rode it all around even taking young children with her (in the days before safety rules were invented). But, as happens, the M'cycle died. About 1980, I purchased the Twinstar for her for Christmas (the one Christopher rode until tiring of it). She rode it up and down the driveway, said it is "too Big" which ended her M'cycle moll career.

The trail 90 has knocked all around since then in the ends of various youth leading to a painful death of the engine. By a twist of fate, I came into a 200CC Honda engine which is now more or less installed in the Trail 90 frame. There are a multitude of items with which to deal. However, my electrical consultant (Dwight) says he can handle the electrical and I hope to be able to cobble the rest together. Stay tuned.

We went back to Greensboro to check on Teressa. She is getting things together and goes back to her job tomorrow. I was surprised that John had not left things in very good order. She said he has felt bad for several years and real bad the last couple so the things he normally took care of were not kept up to date. Anyway, she will get it all together. She did say John would have liked me to have THE car.

Everyone knows, I have vowed and declared not to drag another one in unless two go out. So, I need volunteers (with signed parental permission slips for those under 22.).

On a lighter (at least less expensive note), we took the dogs with us on the run. GSH worked harder at tending to two dogs than I ever remember her having to work at tending to many more children. That is even after I built a barrier to keep them out of the front of the car. But, they did behave very well while at her house. She has three large dogs and friend who have dogs so she is well set up to handle the visitors.

Upon return.

The Blountville Christmas parade was Saturday. I/we have been going for a lot of years. A whole lot. This was the first time I ever watched with no family with me. As the parade went along the kids were scurrying around getting candy. Then, A young boy can back and offered me a pepperment ball. I said "thanks that is just the kind I like so he later brought several more. Then, two other kids came and gave me various pieces of candy.

The parade was very good with more entrees than usual.

One item was a 30s something Plymouth coupe. I believe it was like the one Momma said Daddy had when Herb and Sis were little. He traded it to get the 37 pickup we had when I was little. The front of the car is just like the '37 pickup I hope to work on next. It would be nice to make it look so good.

Today, we harvested the fatted Cedar tree from fence row up on the hill in front of the house, but, since it is not decorated. A picture will have to wait. So, to be continued.
