October 6, 2013 · Sunday

Cat House

10 photographs·422 words

That cat has got way more than its fair share of time and attention this
week. It seems to have taken up residence in the smoke house but comes down
about dusk for food and petting. IF, the correct petters are present.

GSH is sitting in the swing now awaiting his arrival.

Some people may be interested in the fruit and nut situation. Katy,
Gwendolyn and perhaps others.

The turkeys and deer are interested in the chestnut crop.

The '37 is now officially ready to be primed. I masked it after going for a
driveway run. Guess I will put it back in the shed after getting a coat of
primer applied.

No AT work this week as the government "shutdown" extends to free labor in
the national forest.

I did got to see Dr. Reeves. 9 years since the prostate operation. Another
zero on the PSA so sure be free and clear on that issue. However, I had a
blood test as part of routine physical a couple of weeks back. Got a letter
yesterday saying I needed to call them to discuss the blood test results.
Who think they are going to tell me I am practically perfect? Ha. Stay
tuned.

Today, we weny for a lake cruise on the HMS D&D. It was fun and
relaxing.Others should try it when (and if) the opportunity arises.

If anyone is interested, we still have plenty of potatoes, free for
digging. Also, the turnip patch is coming right along. If anyone likes
turnip greens the signs are right.

I have been chasing a noise in the VW drive train for several weeks. New
wheel bearings, new brake pads. This week, I installed a new CV joint.

Still not cured. Next, I am going to replace the hub and the wheel bearing.
Stay tuned.

The dogs have not yet been introduced to the cat. As part of the plan, They
have been taking walks while on leashes and attached to their owner. Here
is a picture of the dogs taking GSH for a walk.

In addition to stink bugs, this seems to be a banner year for packsaddles.
I remember them well from pulling fodder in the days of long ago and far
away.

Life goes on at a frantic pace in Blountville.
