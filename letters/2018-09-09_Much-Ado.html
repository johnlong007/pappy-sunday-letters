September 9, 2018 · Sunday

Much Ado

17 photographs·588 words

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gD7IpkiO2fM

Paw Paws are ripe and falling on the ground. Dave Edwards was over to bring
us a pecan pie..

He also brought some car parts with which he wanted advice/opinion.

I gathered some Paw Paws (knowing he is picky, I washed one) and offered it
to him. He asked 14 questions what does it look like, taste like, used for
etc. But would not even taste. He is 67 years old and would not taste a
strange fruit even while GSH was eating one.

The pie was very good. I gave him some good advice and loaned him a tool.

One day we went down and worked at the food pantry at church prepping food
boxes for Monday's distribution. It all came back to me. My job was the
last person to add and item to the box as it came down the line. A large
box of Whitman's Sampler. Soul food.

Labor Day, Monday, Wayne went with me to mow our section of the AT, it was
not bad and we moved right along. If one goes to the TEC hiking and
canoeing web site all the work reports are available.

According to Pat Loven, Monday the signs were right for root crops so we
planted turnips.

Not sure about the signs for trimming bushes but It looked like high time
to me. I set the stepladder up with the legs on the up hill side making a
rather step angle to climb.

Gave the camera to GSH and told her to be ready when I fell. She was not
ready to catch in full flight. I went over backwards body hit followed soon
after by my head. Really rang the chimes. I still have a very sore neck.
Wonder when I will learn, 82 is not adequate.

Thursday was AT work day. The crew went to Carver's gap and worked up the
mountain toward Cloudland.

The trail had suffered badly during recent rain storms. Before we started
Carl gave our safety and watch out rules for the day. Mainly, "do not get
off the trail." At least 3 different sets of watchers, artifacts, flora and
fauna inspectors would be checking to see if we had disturbed any thing,
dead or alive.

I sort of went off by myself and worked on installing a step where water
had cut beneath a tree root. Worked a large rock into place and crushed
small rocks to pack in around it. Now, I have a bit of sore back to go with
sore neck.

When I got home from AT work Thursday, I found supper was ready! Seems
Ross had donated pert of his Winter food supply to us. Unlike Dave, I was
ready willing and able to dig in. My compliments to the Chef whomever it
was.

I has decided to switch hoods on the '63 and the pars car. Wayne came by
and we made the switch.

He helped put both hood and trunk lid on the '63.

Saturday PM we moved the '63 out on landing in order to get it running.

However, when I tried to get it to crank it did not. Now, I have Bob's
multi-meter borrowed and a project for next week.

Aaron, I think this picture is my Uncle Jim's ,can you identify the various
decorations?
