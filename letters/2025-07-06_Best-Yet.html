July 6, 2025 · Sunday

Best Yet

18 photographs·526 words

I am near the end of the recovery cycle from Chemo week. Seems bone marrow
is still working to some extent as NP looked at lab results and told me to
go home, Don't get sick., don't bleed.
Bad news is my hip and upper leg have been giving me fits all week such
that I had to use crutches to get around.

After we got home, we fished the second DB mower out of storage and brought
it up for refurbishing.

One has to take their help where they can find it.

The plan is to try to salvage the old tires but install new tubes. Charlie
wanted to get back in practice so he can teach Tommy in due time.

The wooden pitman rod on each mower was rotten. Bob made replacements.
However we found they were not quite alike with some modification required.
I am not sure that will be a skill Linus finds useful in 15 years or so.

The Independence day parade in Blountville was great.

Easy to see why the South lost.

I hobbled over to my usual seating spot. Plopped down and dropped crutches
beside me. A fellow seated in the shade of the church came over and
offered to let me use his chair ! That made my day !
I have learned over the years that grumpy old men do not entice any candy
tossers,
BUT, Crippled old codgers with crutches get buried !

In addition, as I was leaving, hobbling across the lawn toward the parking
lot, a firm hand took me by the arm. I thought it was Dale. When I looked
after reaching the pavement, it was another person I had never seen!

I sure like Blountville values and culture !

All is as it should be, at least in Blountville.

Family members continue to claim artifacts.

This one must plan to apply for a WPA job.

I had to tell Cliff how to operate a potato spade. Don't spear a potato !
Janet can explain.

The temperature stabilization system has been under conversion to a
swimming pool for quite some time. Diane pushed the project along such that
the pool is now open. Laura made the plunge. I hope many will utilize it. I
had calculated target temp. to result from Wayne's work to be 80F. It is 78
and rising.

Laura had a grand opening !

We do outings with the best ! Alan grilled enough ribs to feed Coxey's
Army. I think Diane gathered 15 baskets of bones after all were gone.

All six of our children were here.
Many of our grandchildren
Many of our GGC.

Several pending GGC.

Even more meaningful event than the helping hands was seeing the little
ones playing in Patricia's frog pond. All is well.

Diane moved the squirrel disco device out near the memorial site. So far,
the deer have not learned to party. Flowers are coming back!
