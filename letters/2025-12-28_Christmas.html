Now

Charlotte is working on a top secret project.

I think I know what it is.

Family and friends came by this week. Farmer Lisa and daughter Ella came
from afar bearing gifts.

A couple of widow ladies from church came by. No casserole.

Mondays are always exciting.

More (a lot more) family came by. Good to get caught up. They grow up fast.

More family. A fine visit. Another Sarah living life in the academic world.

Skill building.

Application of skill and knowledge without getting injured.

A & C did rigging such that I could drag the log out with the tractor.
Now, I need a good sawyer with a good, sharp saw.

Our weekly refilling the wood supply. Furniture soon.

Wayne designed and fabricated a walnut cracking machine. First one gallon
bag has been cracked . About 12 oz. from a 4 # bag. Only 105 bags to go !

We had sticky buns here with B & L, then a serious christmas dinner at
their house.
