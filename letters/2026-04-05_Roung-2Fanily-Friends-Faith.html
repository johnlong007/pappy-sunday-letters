After I had Just got home from 7 days in the hospital

I was told my most likely cause of death would be an infection or
hemorrhaging. I thought I knew what an infection looked like. I was wrong.
It turned out to be an internal issue. After they isolated specific
bacteria and got it under control, they trained Diane in administering the
antibiotic intravenously and sent me home.

They said it will recur; I hope we can learn how to detect it earlier. Not
quite ready to give up yet.

I set goals every week and have planed activities every day.

When help shows up, we put them to work.

Nephew James is in Erwin briefly, he brought Lisa and came for a visit. I
was pleased and honored.

Matty (I think) reminds me very much of my mother.

Queen Anne

Big brother checking on little sister.

One of the men I worked with at ECC had a field of heritage apple trees. He
gave Patricia two grafted seedlings
It looks like a crop this year if there is no late frost. Usually safe
after the 10th.
