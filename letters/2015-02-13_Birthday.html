Birthday

2 photographs·99 words

My father was born on Feb. 13. Just which year I am not quite sure.

Growing up, we were told he was born in 1900.

When Granny Buchanan lived in the garage apartment in 1953-58 she had a big
thick family bible. It had all the birth and death dates in it. Even the
untrained eye could tell some had been modified.

It appears he had to get a birth certificate when applying for wartime work
in the Kaiser shipyards.

Anyway, he would have been either 114 or 115 today.

He was a good man and I miss him.
