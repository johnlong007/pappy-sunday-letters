Days Getting Shorter

12 photographs·290 words

Willie, with a pint of blood for me and a young man beard for the girls.
When I go in I get a bracelet with my ID. At every step they ask my name,
BD and scan the bracelet code.
For infusions, go through that drill then one person will read all the
information from the bag and a different person enters the data into the
machine.

We have the Oliver plow ready to mount on the trophy wall but signs have
not been right yet.

In the meantime. The next project is poised and waiting., It may be an even
bigger endeavor.

We think the final leaf removal on the Ag. Building occured.

As part of that project, the hole in the roof got a temporary patch. We are
considering having a metal roof installed. Some of us are also looking at
the big trees on the hill above and wondering if the roof would be a secure
investment.

Outside work slowed due to weather and other issues. Diane has pretty well
found homes for all Patrisias disposables. Now, we are starting on mine. I
have files, folders and boxes going back to Temple Hill, UCHS, UT, UW, a
lot from Boeing, GDFW and ECC..We made a lot of progress.
I prefer to go through rather than have it all flung into the dumpster
later.

A & C continue to help boost my red blood numbers.. It may not work but
sure tastes good.

B & L had an early practice run for Thanksgiving and invited us.It was a
delightful experience and fine eating also.
