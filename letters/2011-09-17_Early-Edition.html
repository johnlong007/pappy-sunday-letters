Early Edition.

6 photographs·289 words

Testing, testing, 1 2 3
gmail has been frustrating so I am going to try AOL again. We shall see if this goes.

Not a lot to report so I am going to report early. There may be a follow up Monday night, there may not.

We finished sawing the slabs and stacking the wood. We may have enough wood for the Winter. I would like to collect some of the maple we cut last year at Blountville annex but so far have not got there.

It should be obvious here the focus is on the little dog. She is very high energy. Making good progress in several areas and no progress in others.

For the lake outing this week, I did not go but sent my camera with Anita with instructions to take a picture of Laura looking relaxed and waving like the girls on skis at Cyprus Gardens. When I downloaded the pictures she took, this is what I found.

Here they are, the incredible dancing dogs.

GSH spent all her discretionary time on the table project. Here, she is pondering mounting the table legs.

After months of working we finally got fittings cobbled up and installed into the car power steering rack. For the big test, I asked GSH to put aside her table for a minute and look at the PS assembly to see if it was leaking when I started the car. She leaned over to look down at the unit, I started the car and she got hit smack in the face with an erupting geyser of PS fluid.
1/2 of us thought it was really, really funny.
Oh, the PS leaks and did not work. So, back to the drawing board. Again.
