June 29, 2014 · Sunday

Tractors !

9 photographs·317 words

Not much excitement in River City.

Finally got the little Allis C​halmers running and moved it into the barn.

​
​Then time to bring another one to the front and center.

It puffed white smoke but never hit a lick during towing. So, I concluded
fuel but no spark. Dried inside the distributor cap and sanded the points
producing a good spark but now clutch will not engage.

It has a hand operated clutch which is locates inside the belt drive
pulley. I borrowed a puller from George but it is not going to fit. To be
continued.

We did AT work on Thursday going in at Hughes Gap. One of the guys was
having problems with his foot so we lolly gagged along behind while walking
in ti the work site. Came across Bambi right beside the trail.

I think we were actually working at Little Rock Knob. We had cleared a spot
for a scenic overlook some time before. For the benefit of those who think
they now live in scenic spots.

​
​This section of relocation has included a great deal of heavy duty rock
work for steps and switchbacks. I recommend walking through for anyone who
wants to see what we do for fun.

​The crew is not made up of a group of youngsters.

Today I made a run to Erwin to check on Herb , He was in good form.
Daughter Lisa with child Ella were there as was D-I-L Lisa and child
Sophie. He will not get bored this week . His little truck is back from the
paint shop and looks really good.

Tonight we went over to Long house to hear John narrate the pictures from
the hike in Corsica. Maria joined in from time to time. Getting her
impressions of the various cultures she had experienced was fascinating.

All quite on the western front?
