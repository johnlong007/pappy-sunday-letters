September 11, 2016 · Sunday

New Record !

10 photographs·342 words

For the most inefficient week in my life.

Monday morning I broke down the wheel with the flat FE tire. It is of the
lock ring (Widow maker) type but did not take very long to break down.
Found the tube was no good .
Remembered I have a spare while/tire for Big Black Truck. Checked, same
tire size. So, decided to break down and secure tube from it.
Worked on breakdown, Monday, Tuesday, Wed. (AT work Thursday) Finally got
it open...Found the tube was riddled with holes. Made a trip to town for
repair kit. Patched, still leaked.
Saturday, we went to Erwin to check with Lisa about installing a gate at
entrance to her place. While there, I rooted around and found a tube.
Brought it home, installed with considerable effort. I inflated with
considerable trepidation (Widow maker). Still holding air today,
So, tomorrow I will be ready to pick up where I left off last Monday.

I suppose most understand that:
1. I do not give up easily (if at all).
2. I have spent my life making do and getting by, too late to change (even
if I wanted to change).

AT work was back to the same place, I mostly split rocks. Others wrestled
with rocks.

I did take the VW in to get front end aligned. Then, inflated the tries to
max allowable pressure and find it to be doing much better. It still has a
shake on brakeing but not when on a really smooth road. I am beginning to
think it is the steering rack allowing feedback.

GSH has a sore hip, I think too much hiking and to much brush dragging on
side hills. It is very difficult trying to get her to let hip rest but I am
trying.

For my do-gooder efforts, I went out and did blood sugar sampling on Dwight
at lunch time. It is a good thing he has no feeling in his finger.

I missed chance to see some of the grandchildren (and others) this week.
Rats.
