February 9, 2020 · Sunday

Noah

11 photographs·328 words

I seem to remember from my QM days that 7 data points inside one Sigma, in
a row would indicate a stable system. I think the same guideline works well
on making or breaking a habit. I believe I am doing well on my resolution
to stand up straight, shoulders back. I do very well when I stand up and
begin to walk around. Much less well while piddling around.

The weather forecast at beginning of week was for rain, a lot of rain. To
make room in the shop to work I moved the '63 outside.

On my next trip outside, I decided I should cover and protect the new
(expensive) top.

While the good weather was still here, I got the Bobcat out to work on the
downed Oak rootball.

Made progress but still work to do to free it. I do not, as yet, have a
plan for extracting the log from Albert's field.

Then, the rains came down and the floods came up.
The retaining pond retained until it filled up. I now know I should have
started out to rebuild the original dam. No soccer players live here
anymore anyhow.

Next Summer will use that as project when things get slow. Need tractor,
crawler and Bobcat drivers.

So, we moved indoors and got back on the '37 project. I started on the rear
fenders trying to remove dents and scratches. Can you imagine how many
dents a farm truck fender has after 83 years?
Anyway, I procured some blue primer and have the bed exterior in much
better condition.

GSH aids by keeping work area clean and neat. A novelty for me.

Saturday, we had snow flurries. The cat, being pretty smart watched from
inside.

On the other hand, Wayne and Dirty Thomas ventured outside to fend off any
potential invaders.
