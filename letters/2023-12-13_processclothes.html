process/clothes

0 photographs·315 words

Diane again, using Pappy's computer

Several people have expressed an interest in some of Momma's clothes, and I
have sorted through and have everything ready for people to look at & see
what fits & what they specifically want.

The process I was thinking of is -- when people get a chance over the next
~6 weeks (end of January or so) they can come by & try on things, look
through things, and pick out as many items as they might really want. We
can make a list and then after everyone has had a chance to think, we can
decide how to make sure everyone gets something they really want a lot and
as much else as works out. However, when people are looking & selecting, I
think it will really help if everyone tries to pick the smallest things
they want, which will leave at least a few possibilities for those of us
(and we know who we are!) that need the larger items. The size range
covers really small (quite a few things) up through small (lots of things)
and medium (a very few things). There is nothing that will work for anyone
really tall. Well, the scarves & hats, I suppose; not many of those.

For those that do not want clothing items to wear (either for fit issues,
anti-cross-dressing issues, or personal preference in style), I plan on
making memory quilts out of as many items as possible that do not get
selected.

So, no hurry, but anytime over the next while, I now think everything is
reasonably accessible and fairly well organized.

Oh, Momma's wedding dress -- In my opinion, since Anita is the only one
that wore that dress also, it should definitely go to her. And again, if
during this process, you see a gift item you had forgotten about but want
back, speak up!

Diane
