July 21, 2019 · Sunday

The Good,Bad and Ugly

10 photographs·300 words

You can arrange.

A Fair amount of the week was spent in misery. If anyone is interested in
the signs and portends for blood infection to the point of Sepsis, I can
help 'em. We think it is well under control .

An overriding objective for the Summer has been get the '63 painted, or at
least primed. It now has a coat of primer. Actually several coats of primer.

After a lot of time an effort. The final wipe down with cleaner / degrease

Then several thin coats of primer.

A prime(ed) example

Next comes sanding to desired finish. First 220 grit, then 340 grit then
440. Completed vehicle until smooth with each. Anyone who wants to earn
love and/or money is welcome to participate. I think my last sander (for
both ) was Beth. I will be delighted if she should decide to renew her
skill package and would give her raise.

Sanding starts tomorrow. I need help if it is going to finish tomorrow and
GSH ain't.

In between medical runs, we worked on the ole home place.

Everyone knows how good I am at furniture work !

Tomatoes are getting ripe. But, stink bugs are attacking. I hate to spray
but not as much as I hate stink bugs.

Christopher came home ! Bobsey Twins are united, wish Cliff had been here.

Niece Ellen (appearing live from Chicago) brought her DOD.

Everyone recognize these flowers? Know the history for us?

To my surprise, no one came back with an answer as to the event taking
place on our door frame. Shell is still there if anyone wants it.

I am looking for a more relaxed week.
