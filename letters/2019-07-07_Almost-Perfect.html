July 7, 2019 · Sunday

Almost Perfect

10 photographs·226 words

Just as July 4th should be, we had a lot of family things this week.

Also, the contractor completed his part of the pool project and cleaned up
the rest of his remnants. After that, I pressure washed the area, looks
good now.

Wednesday, Carl had AT work day scheduled. I had signed up to go but
canceled when I heard company was coming !

Then I found out they were really coming to have their picture taken with
Max.

Still more company coming. A fine day.

Thursday, the Blountville parade, something old.

and something new! The Kingsport Community Band !

And appearing live and in person, Anita and Maria all the way from Arizona.

Then a find event which was joined by the Snow family.

When I retired, I made a "I want to do" list (maybe a "I want to have
done") list. Either way, jumping out of an airplane was on the list I made
almost 25 years ago, (I worked on plan for 3 years before actual last day
at TEC).
Tried for years to make it happen. Maria took me seriously, passed on to
Savannah and the two made it happen.

Linda and John made pictures and videos, I did not.
