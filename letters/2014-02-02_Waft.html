February 2, 2014 · Sunday

Waft

8 photographs·421 words

I wonder if I just re posted the notes from last week, or the week before,
would anyone notice?

It was cold here with snow most of the week. Just like a lot of places I
suppose. Then Saturday Groun'hog day day came early and I crawled out of my
burrow, blinked a few times and had a fine outside day.
No AT work .

Would you believe that on one of the cold , snowy days I made breakfast!

Older and younger friends and family keep dropping off around me. Monday
night we went to funeral for Sue Tabor and Wednesday night it was to Erwin
for funeral service for Arnie Edwards, father of Todd and Rusty.

My indoors work was almost all scraping and wire brushing on the LBT. I
have two , five gallon buckets filled with dirt and rust scraped from the
frame and body. I have debated with myself the merits of cutting pieces
from the parts vehicle and fitting into rusted out places but have about
decided to fabricate parts to attach over the rusted out spots.

I put a clutch Master cylinder in the Miata last week. That did not solve
the problem, so, with a lot more time and difficulty I installed a slave
cylinder this week. It would have been an easy task for a person with
functioning fingers and without a bruised top knot.

GSH has been complaining that Miata heater was not performing properly so a
person with more nimble fingers showed up and installed a new thermostat. I
can hardly wait for the next cold spell to test it.

Cat food left on the front porch cat tower tends to disappear. GSH decided
to put my anti-bird owl out the to scare off the intruders.

Everyone knows GSH is a math major. She loves figuring geometric patters.

My head site is healing except I keep bumping the wound and making it
bleed. Perhaps trying to be more careful will be the answer.

During the week, we heard from and about family from near and far. From
warm climes to very cold places in the frozen North. Anita posted a picture
of Beth and her sprat Paul. Looked a lot like Anita and her sprat Beth when
we drove to Alaska. The point is, they grow up quickly.
