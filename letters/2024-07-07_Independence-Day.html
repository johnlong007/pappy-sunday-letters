July 7, 2024 · Sunday

Independence Day

15 photographs·369 words

The Blountville Ruritans got it right !!!

Joe has been a big driver in bringing the community band to the parade. I
believe they like it. A lot of red, white and blue, all important to folks
around here.

We had our private feast.

Work continued on the spike tooth harrow.. It appears that all the parts
were hand made including the spikes with square holes cut to fit. I have
not yet found wood to replace the termit destroyed beams.

We went to the home place to hunt for replacement wood beams. While there,
I took pictures of the tractor so 8N owners could see most, but not all,
the draw bar components.

Linda, David and sweet Pea were here for a few days. Kylie is being
introduced to hanging around with our family members.

The RR's have been a failure this year A contingent came hunting but had
little success.

Before they could escape, a couple of WW's were snagged to help organize
the slab pile.

When I get an opportunity, I like to do some work. My orders to not get
sick and not bleed sort of limit me. But, I still try. A & C have started
on the walnut logs. some slabs have been generated. I wanted to keep the
walnut firewood separate from the pine. Walked down to the walnut slab
pile, sawed into firewood length and was so frazzled Diane had to get the
car to shuttle me back up the drive. I really intend to try to build up
some endurance. Stay tuned.

Niece Mari and spouse Eric are deeply involved in SCCA . An event was held
at Bristol track, We went over to watch . Here, you can see Mari run.

Their son Jon is an engineer and also an active duty AF F-16 pilot. Jon
and Eric built a racecar.Mari loves to tell about all.

Meredith had an event. A & C fed us on a different night.

The finch feeder is attracting mostly gold, some house.
