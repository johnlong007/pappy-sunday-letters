October 24, 2021 · Sunday

Mostly Good

8 photographs·291 words

10-15 years ago there were about a dozen friends met at Perkins early
Sunday for breakfast. Today there were two, David Quillen and myself. The
waitress came over and gave me a bag with a " Pineapple upside down muffin
just out of the oven" for GSH. David attends 1st Baptist in Blountville so
he dropped it off here with Diane .

GSH continues to regain mobility. Today for the first time she walked a bit
on the track.

We made a run to the hillside to check on brush clearing. While there, we
sampled a walnut cake.

Persimmons are ripe and falling to the ground. Turkeys like 'em, deer like
'em and I like persimmons. Katy said no more for her.

Hulling the next year walnut crop has been completed and the walnuts are
stored for curing.

Reshaping the retaining basin is about completed. I had scraped the top
layer off to the side and have now started spreading out over the bare
ground.

Max likes to guess which piece of equipment I am heading toward.

Also for the first ime since the fall, we had Sunday night rolls and
Midsomer Murders. All were concerned about the stairs but it went very
well. I sat on the deck drinking coffee to stay awake until bedtime.
Came home and got GSH tucked in but have had a fit trying to move pictures
out of the new camera. HELP !

Took this picture from B & L deck.

David took up woodworking while being confined. He sent me this picture of
a canoe he made to hang in his cabin. He also had built the cabin.
