September 4, 2016 · Sunday

Out of the Past

9 photographs·432 words

Dave Edwards came by for a visit. Turns out he has a friend who owns the
Bemburg derelict plant and the plant site in Elizabethton. For the
youngsters, my father worked there for many years. I think about 1927-1942.
He and Momma live there (Black Bottom) until about 1935, just before I was
born. They purchased the home place property (1-1/2A) from Mary Jane Parks
Howell (the one who gave me the wooden egg) in 1933. Momma told me Daddy
paid her with a $100.00 gold piece.I am told Daddy and Jim Bowman built the
house and other buildings. Momma said they moved in when it only had roof
and outside walls.. After late 1935 Daddy drove to Elizabethton 5 days/week
to work until quitting to go work in the Kaiser shipyard in Vancover,
Washington.

I have no doubt his stories are what caused me to go there after UT.

So, I told Dave I would really like to visit the plant and he lined up a
guided tour Friday PM. I also looked through Momma's scrapbook and found
pictures made when Daddy worked there.

All the rest was ho hum.

A little work on the FE and, I think, a little bit by GSH on the '63. While
trying to get the brakes working on the FE I found the left rear wheel
cylinder (which I had rebuilt) was leaking. Pulling a wheel cylinder
requires pulling axle , bearings and brake drum. Rather than taking a
chance, again< we ordered a new wheel cylinder. It arrived, Wayne brought
it over and installed it as well as rear axle. I still have to finish the
job.
GSY is chipping away at sewing the seam back on around the rear window in
the '63. She should have it by Springtime.

AT work this week was a lot like AT work last week except a storm rolled in
about 2 PM causing us to move out in a cloud of dust.

At home GSH continues to lop limbs from the trees beside the drive. It
looks good but we piled so much brush on the brush pond it was annoying
her.

One thing leads to another (the Sequential Vortex Effect). When we tore
down the old barn we stacked the barn wood down next to tractor storage
barn. After many years, it became obvious the valuable old barn wood was
useless worm wood. Saturday we moved the stack to the brush pond. Unfolding
plan is to get one of the crawlers running and use it to pack the brush
down. Stay tuned.
