May 9, 2010 · Sunday

Things

12 photographs·339 words

I appreciated the update from the frozen North. Since I am restricted to
typing with one good and one wounded finger, I will lean more toward
pictures.

In order to redeem myself for not including a picture of John playing
soccer last week, I am going to include a
picture of Maria not running track.

The mowing season is in full swing. That takes a lot of time but is not
very photogenic except we did go see the Colson family taking the parts of the
bush hog mower I had removed and repaired. Now the mowing season can get
underway there also.

The AT work day was Wed. We worked on Pond mountain preparing for a trail
relocation. Pond mountain is in a wilderness area so no power tools are
allowed. The plan was use a couple of conscripted through hikers to operate a
cross cut saw to remove the blowdowns.

In order to access the AT without having to walk miles, a widow lady
allowed us to go in across her farm. It is a lovely site just above Hampton.

That did not work well at all. So, it ended up that Paul Benfield (the man
with suspenders) and I used the saw to remove the blow downs. It had been
many years but all came back to me.


I was told that this picture is an edible mushroom. Anyone know for sure?

Speaking of flora and fauna, can anyone type the turtle?

David had an almost birthday while we were there. Lest Dwight feel sinned
against, he is not the only one to make his own birthday cake.

back home in river city, the Donna memorial roses are blooming.

On a more mundane note, I started hand therapy and am gaining flexibility
by the day. Stay tuned.

Today was Mother's day. I suppose all the mothers were waited on hand and
foot including starting the day with breakfast in bed?

We did lunch at BK but for dinner were wined and dined by the Jack family.
