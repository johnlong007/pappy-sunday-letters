As most know, we have gone with IBT on bike rides over the years. Anita,
Laura, Beth, Allison, and Meredith have also gone along at various times.

sure he would be OK.

For unknown reasons, I have not been able to access AOL for a while.
Whether I can or will access gmail while we are gone is iffy. So, if anyone
should need or want to contact us, try going via Beth.

Anyway, no updates for a couple of weeks.

Monday I worked with Dave on do-gooder work for the first time in a long
time. It was fun doing lawn work for some aged church members. Dave is
leaving our church (again). He does not approve of females taking an active
role in the worship service. They are moving to First Christian downtown
which, as I understand it, is where old folks go while waiting for God.

I had a long list of to do things for this week. As the week has gone by, I
have had to defer most of the to do. Very few moved onto the done side.

I did go to the skin DR. She removed two Squamous, two Basel cell and 18
(helper was counting) actinic keratosis. I think maybe a record but none
nearly as impressive as the one Herb is dealing with currently.

Does dragging the boat back and forth to the lake so idle persons have
water ski time count as do-gooder? Anyway, some of us wanted to be
reassured that Laura was recovered. Others may have just wanted and outing.

Also took the tractor and mower to Blountville to mow the pasture. But, no
pictures. I did not finish the project as broke all my shear bolts on
submerged objects. Rocks, logs and stumps.

I have located a person to restore the '63. So, I spent time and effort
rounding up the trim along with various parts. He is to call me in about a
month when a slot opens to start work.

GSH has been demonstrating Parkenson's law by spending most of the week
getting our (Katy, please note) clothes packed and ready. The dog decided
he would like to be included in the trip.He had to settle for the Spa.

The garden, corn if you can beat the critters to it. Beans, overdue, due
and coming on. Okra coming on. Tomatoes about gone except up here around
the circle. Potatoes, not a good crop but waiting to be dug.
