December 14, 2025 · Sunday

Getting Close

14 photographs·272 words

A sure sign Santa is on his way, The Blountville Christmas parade. Anchored
by some of our local fashion style setters.

As usual for the last 40 years or so, we set up in front of the Baptist
church. It is not easy to see but Meredith tucked a little one in under my
cast off UT jacket.

As usual here lately, it was decided I needed another unit of borrowed
blood on Monday. Also as usual, Diane was watching over me, Panny is the
nurse who administered blood. Bailey stopped by with her daughter Tilly.

We have mostly moved indoors. Wayne came by and worked with Diane to remove
the leaky seal on the '37 differential input shaft. You may note the rear
bumper with tail light has been installed.

The seal.

Another finished project ! Ready to be put on display. Help needed.

The fireplace circulating fan has been getting noisy. FRiday we took it
out and tried various things to quieten.

The new fan arrived as did Dale. Fan is now installed and is silent.

Wonder Horse and Wonder girl.

Linda wanted to go inspect landscaping work at D & C's house. We did.

With Winter coming I wanted to spread gravel on the drive. I got the gravel
spread but need to mount blade on tractor to even.

Next week, It may be the furniture.

Nephew James sent me this picture, taken just up the creek from Farmer
Lisa's house.
