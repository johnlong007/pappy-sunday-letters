Pram Blanket

0 photographs·202 words

Eastman sent us to live in Workington England in 90-91. We visited various
churches before settling down at a church in the middle of town. Most of
our friends and family who came to visit us got to visit the church and
meet some of the same people.

One lady we met, EV Adams, was especially nice, helpful and interesting.
Her husband had been dead for many years leaving her with a grown daughter,
Esther. I have kept up with Ev, through Esther, over the years. Ev does not
have a computer or cell phone. When Kenneth was born, EV knit a pram
blanket for him and I think for all the rest of our grandchildren after
Kenneth.

Ev is about the same age as I am . I recently got a note from Esther saying
her mom had a fall, broke a hip, had surgery and is at home recuperating. I
would like those who were recipients of her good deed to send her letters.
Real snail mail letters. Include as much personal information and pictures
as you can manage.

She will be grateful, Esther will be grateful and I will be grateful.

Mrs. Ev Adams
38 Elizabeth Street
Workington
CA14408
