October 10, 2021 · Sunday

Still Ticking

9 photographs·270 words

As I remember, we ended last week with the Bobcat dead in the water so to
speak We got it out before the rains came down and the floods came up but
not without misadventures.
We knew it was not getting fuel but throught the pump was air locked so
kept trying to vent and cranking on the engine.
It did not start that way but did melt the positive terminal post right off
my big, expensive battery for the crawler. A temporary fuel supply and
finding the bleed valve got it to fire right up.

Turns out the fuel pickup line inside the tank had broken off. Alan
replaced it with an improved design which will not fail. He said.

The bobcat has been losing hydraulic oil. A close search by WW found a
pinhole in a high pressure line. It is planned to P & I a replacement
forthwith.

Dry weather is predicted for a few days. The pond has been mostly drained
and if the Bobcat is returned to service, earthmoving will be resuled by
mid week.

I addition to leaving you with the Bobcat in the mud last week, you were
left with GSH in a new wheel chair. She has learned to move it around.

More important in my opinion, she is able to stand up and shift from chair
to chair.

Saturday, for the first time in almost 3 weeks, we went for a drive. I need
a training session on helping her into the car.
