Fits 'n Starts

15 photographs·569 words

​
​
In case you might wonder what story this picture tells,

Monday I took GSH to the doctor. Mostly pretty routine but he did say her
bone density is not good and is getting worse. He said while it is unlikely
a bone will break for no reason that if she takes a serious fall it would
be very likely to cause a break. With that word, he more or less persuaded
her to let him prescribe some pills to help with bone density. I have three
prescriptions which are filled by "Express Scrips" Delivered to mail box
and paid on line by credit card.

I, being me, asked Joe to come set up account for GSH so I could order her
prescription. She/we could not find her purse to get her insurance number
from her card. After much dismay I sent a note to PBBD who remembered
having seen GSH put purse in back pocket of her black jeans at lunch
Thursday. Freshly washed jeans were on the clothes line. BTY, while Joe was
at the keyboard, he added names to the distribution. If anyone wants to be
added (or deleted) he is the go to man.

​The wood shuffling has come to a halt. We rescued it form tight stack in
Erwin, pressure washed and leaned on wall to surface dry, now, it is stored
until claimed. However, it has taken the place I could have parked one more
small tractor. Maybe 8N size.

​
Work continued on the '49. I did a fair amount of wire brushing on the bed.
Next, I plan to coat with the rust reactor then it will be ready to bolt on
chassis and have the wood bed installed.Not sure what to use for boards.

​
We also put in another work session helping Pat and Karen with her cabin
project. Most of the trash has been removed. Plan is to go back this week
and shovel out all the loos dirt. Maybe a foot deep now. Notice there is a
square hole in the dirt wall. It goes back about 5 feet. I have no idea
what purpose it served. Any thoughts?

​
Saturday Dwight's son Pat came over to look at the crawler innards with a
boroscope. Gave it a good try but could not tell anything about the
problem. Made the mistake of trying to get tractor running. Failed. Rats.

​

​
Thursday a big group of willing workers turned out for a work day at the
Over mountain shelter. I worked with a crew assigned to relocate the
"facility". A loo with a view. We were digging before 10AM and came off the
mountain after dark at 6 PM. A tough job due to rocky ground. It normally
lasts 3-5 years before having to be relocated again.

​
LLLL being concerned about our propensity to default to BK for food,
prepared a one week menu for us and Laura procured the makings. It has gone
very well. Some items better than others. We had left the stir fry pending.
However, when Joe (and Anita) were here to deal with PC seemed a good time.
All agreed it was a glowing success.

​
Thanksgiving dinner wed. night at CHCC.
Thanksgiving dinner Sunday night at Bltv. Methodist
Friday we went to Elizabethton to look over the East Tennessee Christian
Home and Academy.

Anyone coming this way at Thanksgiving?
