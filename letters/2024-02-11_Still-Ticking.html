February 11, 2024 · Sunday

Still Ticking

16 photographs·468 words

Dan has been keeping me updated about the status of my brother, Jimmy.
Jimmy was born in 1933. Heb in 31, Sis in 1930. Jimmy is currently in the
JC hospital with pneumonia. Stay tuned.

My Monday lab results were best in a while as we near the end of the 8 week
rest cycle. We had a video conference with The UVA Dr. who is managing my
treatments. He was quite up beat. Said something like eat, drink and be
merry.

Trouble had an adventure with a skunk early Monday. Diane started working
on him as I left for chemo. The patients and nurses announced "there is a
polecat in here". I just tried to look casual.

As part of our mental and physical health program, we filled in the machine
dug water line ditches.

We did that !

Look what sticks and stones will do to my aged, fragile body. No external B
& G .

During their years in exile up North, A & C have developed some upscale
skills and tastes. They fed us one day from way up high on the hog.

In order to get a second opinion, Rebecca dropped in to look at my charts
and nod wisely (look close and you can see the treatment cycles).
She brought her person with her. He passed the second reading with flying
colors.

We went to Cosby to visit cousin Fran. She and husband Ron live in a cove
which adjoins the GSM park. The house in front of Fran's face is where
Ron's GP's lived. The other bldg is where they had a store selling local
items.
Their two sons are in the AF and make us have pride in being from the
South.

Adjoins Fran's property.

The signs were right so we split wood. Felt really good to get out and do
something.

I used to do a lot of photographic work. I have a collection of cameras
going back to the one my mother had which I used as a kid (taking pictures
of kids doing silly things).Such as doubling a girl.

My last good film camera was a Pentax I had Herb procure for me on one of
his trips to Japan. I later bought a spare.

I intend to document the history of all my cameras. Just in case there is
any interest.

Wayne and Diane are making serious progress. They opened the engine package
from the machine shop.

Some assembly required. Stay tuned.

Rain came down and floods came up. Today at the track.

Friend David is in Florida for 3 weeks. He takes pictures.
