December 2, 2012 · Sunday

Social Season !

12 photographs·538 words

Last week I mentioned meeting a niece of George Reed. She sent me a picture.

I am sure you are waiting with baited breath to hear what came of the
sows ear. It is now a full fledged, in service, silk purse. GSH does
it again !.

This week Carl split his AT forces to walk various sections of the
trail to clear the trail after the recent snowfall. The crew of
willing workers I went with is shown at rest after we had finished but
before hiking out. Two of us walked in 4 miles to the shelter then
worked our way out meeting the other crew working toward us. Just to
let you know about taste and PC of the AT hikers, I took a picture of
the shelter wall.

A lot of the Grandchildren have time invested in GSH's go around.
Therefore, an update. D & D contributed a load of hand made pine chips
to the effort. It is now mostly asparagus and strawberry.

Speaking of GSH, the local sewing circle meet here this week.

Having paved many miles with good intentions, I made a run to visit
Dave and Pat Edwards. Pat has gone through a series of medical
procedures, Scott fell 25 feet and hit on his feet on a concrete
driveway smashing both feet.

I did not see Scott, he is living with his children in a rented house
and is confined to a wheel chair for a couple more months. Pat said he
is doing fine all things considered.

Pat is also doing well. She has had back pain for years, It finally
got so bad she has work done and now proclaims it is pain free. All
the tumor and cyst issues seem to be under control.

Saturday morning I went with Dave to a "used car lot". His used cars
are old, real old. Also a number of unusual brands. Nash, Stude,
Desoto etc.

I am interested in how many, if any, can identify the car shown in
engine compartment picture.

Lots of Ford ( and other brand) coupes.

Dave has the '65 Plymouth. He found the engine ID numbers and was
delighted to determine engine was produced on his BD.

The old tractor Christmas dinner was today. A bit somber as Debby and
Larry were missing. But, still a nice event. I went to check on Larry
Wed. He could move his head a bit , he looked at me and appeared to
recognize me. He was still on all the life support equipment.

Dwight has officially given up on the TV/Monitor. I have asked Wayne
to procure it for Jonathan to use to deveelop his skil and knowledge
as an aspiring geek. I would also be remiss if I did not note that
Jonathan was able to bring my printer back to life.

Now, most exciting of all, a '37 pickup update (He has several '30's
Plymouts on the lot, no pickups).
Close examination show show, Fenders, seat and a windshield wiper. I
have still not develoed enough courage to check out my wiring but it
is installed.

College children, how about sending a sign from time to time? A wet
sheepskin? A smoke signal?

,
