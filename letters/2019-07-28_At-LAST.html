July 28, 2019 · Sunday

At LAST !

9 photographs·374 words

Allison finished in Memphis with my gold crown in her pocket and me with a
temporary. This week, we made the run down to see Dr. Max and Dr, Allison
in action ! What would have taken hours in Memphis took minutes in Selmer
and now I have (another) gold crown.

All know Sis and Sherill adopted Martha. She was from Selmer and still has
family and friends there. We all met up at dental clinic then went out to
lunch. It was fun. Little sister is doing great.

A bit earlier in the week, Christopher came by before flying off to the
left coast. I wanted to hear about his work, Mary Allison's work and life
in California. He brought me right up to date on all those. I must confess,
her works sounds a lot more interesting. I hope to be able to manage a
visit and field trip.

Also early in the week, Wayne arranged to get a section of gutter replaced.
I had damaged it with the mower as I remember. He has caught up on a lot of
my neglected M & R.

Car work consisted of sanding on the '63. GSH being the only willing worker
to show up. The first round is done but two more grades to go. I have asked
Dwight's son Pat to come do the spraying. He has not said no. Stay tuned.

Also, a bit of work on the spare '63. It would not crank. Pulled the
starter and found it to be frozen. Got it freed and lubricated then got
stronger hands than mind to put it back into place.

I started work on creating a holding pond to slow the flood waters using
tractor and box blade.

Then decided to get serious and got Bobcat. Found it had a flat from
roofing nail punctures. Bob has ordered a new tire at my request. It also
is leaking hydraulic fluid. As Lisa has discovered, it is always something.
Gonna take a bit to get Bobcat in operation. Will try it a while then fire
up the crawler. Ha !

Passengers from shed up.
