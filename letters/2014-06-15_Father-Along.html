Father Along

18 photographs·384 words

Here is a picture of my Grandfather with his second family children. I
would guess the picture was about 1906 or so judging from my father's size.
He is the one with the "X" .

My father was a good man. He could fix anything and do anything.

My take on people is we are born with disposition and learn behaviors.
Aptitudes and propensities are hard wired.

I read the attached article this morning and liked it.
http://www.foxnews
.com/opinion/2014/06/14/father-day-to-all-good-dads-out-there-thank/

What a week. John helped me work on starting tractors for the BIG tractor
show at Blountville. We ran into many challenges.
We tried to start the crawler by drifting:

​
It did not start although John let it roll down into the walnut woods.

We left it overnight and got a heavy rain.

​
Eventually figured out it had water in the distributor.

​
That worked so we loaded the crawler and the drag plows on the trailer to
go to show.

​Who would have thought GSH would take a picture while driving !

The H started right up ! Note the tractor dog.

But, the MD would not start !

​Finally got it running on gas but gave up on the Diesel issue. Tractor is
now parked in position to try again.
Did not get the little A-C to hit a lick and never got back to trying to
trouble shoot.

We moved the club trailer to the show site:

​And John drove the H, well almost all the way. He had a flat and ran out
of gas just before turning in to the show site.

​
​
The show went off well. Many folks sat around in chairs borrowed from a
local church.

​
​The kiddie events went over well. I do remember some family children
participating.

​

After the show we sponsor a tractor pull. The club keeps gate return. For
the first year ever, we took in a bit more than it cost to sponsor the
pull. Probably because of the gate collectors and money counter

​Getting everything home and all sorted out was an adventure in itself.
But, it did get done.

On Thursday, we went for a ride in a big ole Buick and stopped at a country
food place.

​
​

Four.
