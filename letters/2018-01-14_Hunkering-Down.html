January 14, 2018 · Sunday

Hunkering Down

9 photographs·495 words

Mostly good news this week.

The family got a new addition and promise of another !

Aaron and Chelsea conspired to bring PBBD a grandson. She is quite pleased,
we believe the parents are also.

Kenneth and Emily set wedding date. Good reason to believe they are
pleased. Anyway, I am, believing her to be a keeper and being stuck with
Kenneth.

One do-gooder project for the week. My friend David Quillen took GSH and I
to Erwin to inspect the roof on Herb's (Lisa's) house. He found several
proximate causes of leaks, none serious problems. Mostly need some tender
loving M & R. he said about 8 hours by qualified person. I ran it by Wayne,
He is pondering.

Looking at the pictures I took this week, one would think all we do is
collect firewood to get ready for one more week. That would be pretty much
correct.
We brought another batch up from the barn.

​Deciding there is not enough left of the wood in the barn to make it
through this Winter, we dug into the stack out front to get down to the
year old wood.

​Then, to start getting ready for next Winter, we harvested a load from
around the soccer field. It is still in the truck as snow is predicted and
truck will do much better with a load than Miata will do empty. But only if
I must go in the snow !

​Carl had AT work scheduled for Thursday and I had signed up but he
canceled due to predicted bad weather. The same reason we were rounding up
wood for this week.
As most know, I hulled bushels of black walnuts and maybe a bushel of
butter nut walnuts. GSH and I cracked maybe a gallon this week. I am about
ready to quit on that job. Anyone want to make easy money I have a cracker
machine, and a lot of walnuts. I see cracked black walnuts advertised at
$20/qt.

We are about recovered from cold/flu bug. But, GSH has ear problem messing
up her balance and her disposition. So, PBBD sprung (sprang?) into action
to help with the ear.

​​Getting ready fro the predicted bad weather, my GSH and I moved the '49
Dodge into the shed. There it is destined to remain until some other person
drags or drives it out.
Actually, I would like to set up a paint shop this Summer and paint the
'37. the LBT. the '49 and the '63 Chrysler. We shall see.

Running has been going well. I think the cold weather has helped. I have
been doing 3-5 miles/day. Pretty slow but not getting light headed. In
training for the 26.2. Did get one offer to aid and abet. It must include a
flat course. Prefer not round and round.

So far, the house project is more tearing down than building greater.

​​GSH likes to relax and watch Columbo. Dog likes to help.
