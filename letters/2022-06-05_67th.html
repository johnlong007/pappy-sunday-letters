That was then

Recognize anyone?Dr. Webb, Stepfather and Donna have gone home.

Now is now.

Breakfast was country ham and a Perkins muffin furnished by Connie our
waitress and hand delivered by friend David Quillen.

Lunch was shrimp, coleslaw and Dr. Pepper. GSH favorites

And for supper dessert, a cake made delivered and partly consumed by son
Alan

In other big news ! Wayne was able to procure and adapter to go from BSP
thread to NPT, install an oil pressure switch (from parts bin) and get the
oil pressure warning light back in operation.

PBBD helped move the '37 out, added a bit of fuel in the temporary tank and
took it for a spin. She likes it. Coming right along.

For the sun lovers, I went back to Dr. Sago to follow up on the last visit.
She zapped at least a dozen. Neck, head, back arms and ears. While she was
freezing my ears I remember telling her she could not make my brother
flench. I was not about to flench

I didn't need to be concerned that GSH could consume it all before
melting. Linda peeled my head to make it easier for Dr. Sago

Prepping the next Oak sawlog.

Logging helper in training. Learning to handle a Peevey. Actually, I
believe she is using a cant hook.
