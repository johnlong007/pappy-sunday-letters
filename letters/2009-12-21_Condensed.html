Katy did an exceptional job both planning and executing.

We made it far enough inside the Arctic circle to be within the 24 hours
of darkness.THE OBJECTIVE OF THE TRIP ! (Actual Solstice is about 12:30 PM
today I believe).

It was not dark at the circle at noon. It was dusk for about 2 hours then
dark for 22. I expect it would be full dark for 24 if one went on to the
North Pole.

It was cold all the time we were in Finland , around zero. Not a lot of
snow but the snow was dry and compacted on the roads and streets. All cars
have good, studded snow tires on all four wheels. The drivers seem to ignore
the snow covered roads.

I will have a slide show for anyone interested.
