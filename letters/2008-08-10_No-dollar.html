August 10, 2008 · Sunday

No dollar

8 photographs·229 words

The fruit trees are loaded this year. We have a deer which has been hanging
around for some time. The other morning it was cleaning up the crab apples.
Then, it sauntered across the driveway to have tomato salad for dessert. Pat
when out to chase it off with her dog under her arm. The dog never saw the
deer. The deer trotted off before she got close enough to throw her dog at it.

I changed oil in the tractor. Did not seat the filter properly and pumped
one gallon of expensive oil out before I could get it shut down.

We went to Linda's Thursday night to tend to the kids on Friday and to empty
the rented storage trailer. It can be seen in the background as the
cheerful, willing workers are eating breakfast before starting to move.

The moving went well. We got it all emptied by noon and took the kids to
McD's as their reward.
For the afternoon, I mowed.
Saturday, I mowed the hillside. On the way home, a THP officer brought me up
to date on all the rules concerning trailers.

Saturday evening, Maria made BLT sandwiches for us. I did not have my
camera, so I made another for Sunday lunch.
