May 1, 2016 · Sunday

Bout Time

9 photographs·314 words

After she passed 80, my Lil' Sister started whimpering about her friends
dying. Now I understand. It has been right at every day for the last few
weeks. Some older, some younger. Could get to be depressing.

On the other end of the spectrum, I worked with LLLL to bring The Car back
to a legal status so Andrew could use it to transport a princess to the
ball.

Meanwhile, back in River City, i worked on my same ole same ole.

A lot of sanding on the '63. I like being able to see what color it was in
a previous life. Also finished the undercoating and am getting close on
body repair work.

At the same time, GSH is making progress on the seat covers/rebuilding.

I also worked on the fire engine brake components. I think everything is
ready to install as soon as the signs are right.

AT work was on Friday. I have mentioned the handicapped accessible trail
section. We are building a fence across the open farm to keep cattle being
run on the open field from getting on the trail The fence will be woven 36"
with one strand of barb wire. Ten metal posts, ten feet apart then four
locust posts. It seems clear our group also has worker/watcher issues.

Again this year, the community band performed at the Exchange Place Farm
Day event. It was a lovely morning , a lot of people out and a lot of
vendors showing their wares. I was tempted on the tomato and pepper plants
but decided it was too early for planting.

I made a run to Erwin Saturday to check on Lisa and her little brother.
James was in to help celebrate Ella's 12th birthday and to have fun on the
farm. All the signs I see indicate Lisa is getting things sorted out and
will be OK.
