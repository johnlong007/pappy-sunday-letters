August 6, 2017 · Sunday

Varied

12 photographs·450 words

For those who have kept up with the saga of the 8N, it was left in the care
of Jake in a non-operable condition. He recently sent out a picture of skid
marks on his driveway pavement due to dragging it around with his fine new
AWD truck.

Deciding to take the matter into my own hands and wanting to go visit Beth
and the kids, GSH, Dave and Pat Edwards and I went to Oar Ridge Friday.

It was good to visit Beth and the kids. However, Suzanne squalled when I
held her-briefly, real briefly.

I pulled the plugs from the tractor and rocked it in 4th gear. Nothing.
Backed the starter out about 1/4 inch and rocked it, engine turned. I quit
right there. Charlie?

I believe next step would be pull starter out and see if the interference
point is visible. Otherwise, shim it a bit and see if that avoids the
problem.

On the home front, I found a correct size tire for the truck which Wayne
helped me mount and carried off the old one. Reckon people thought that was
a spare for his MC ?

​​

Work continued on the tilt trailer. The brake and signal lights are now
working. The side marker lights are to be replaced.

​

A brake-away kit must be procured and installed. On Saturday. GSH and I
made a run to Alabama (without Suzanna or my banjo on my knee) and returned
with two mobile home axles with brake assemblies on each. This week, will
find out if the twain shall meet.

AT work day was the same ole same ole. Getting ready for ASU in two weeks.
Last week, I tripped and fell on my face. Since my section is ready for the
students, I spent the day removing tripping hazards from all the section.
There are plenty left, I need a strong helper armed with a sledge hammer.

Our log ramp will be by passed. It has been very popular.

​One of the guys knows mushrooms. He feed me some "chicken of the Woods". I
ate it.

​One morning I looked out the window and saw two turkeys with a covey of
tuklets, frying size. By the time I got the camera, the cat had spotted and
decided to have one for breakfast. They flew.

​Just in case those scattered around in far away places with strange
sounding names would like to know some of what they are missing:

​No trips to the Dr, this week, Not a lot of blood letting and running
around has gone a bit better. I discovered the a cup of coffee just before
running reduces onset of light headed.
