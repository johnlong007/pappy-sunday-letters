November 5, 2017 · Sunday

Quo Vadis

11 photographs·454 words

At the beginning of this year I announced to resolution :

Run a 2 minute lap leading to an 8 minute mile

Get my eyes opened

I have long since given up on the 2 minute lap. Since getting out with GSH
to go walk on the track I have about doubled my "running" distance. I now
do 3-5 miles about every day. But. it is not running due to light headed
(Aortic Stenosis I think) i have to start off with about 5 minute laps and
by the time i have 2-3 miles I get down to 3 minute laps. Have not been
able to get much beyond that but am grateful for what I can do.

Early in the year I went in to Eye Dr. Got exam and referral to get
eyebrows lifted. Referral dr. said go back and tell them to complete the
exam, vertical acuity I believe. then come back, $40 please.
Monday I went back. $40. When they asked if I had gone to referral dr I
told them with emphasis added. They did another eye exam. I told him to go
ahead with eyebrow lifting. Sent me to another room for vertical acuity
test. Tested said you must schedule another ($40) appointment as cannot do
with dilated eyes. Due to go back in this Wed.
Clearly the medical profession does not value my time, only my money.

The new undertaking this week was helping Dave Edwards with his car. The
'65 Plymouth I let him have years ago. I delivered it to Rochester NY.

We worked on two days and got the brake lines in. He now wants to replace
master cylinder and shocks.

​​Finally got back out for AT work. Roan Mountain got a very heavy rainfall
last week. The report was that the trail we have worked on so many time
going to Jane Bald at Carvers Gap suffered severe washing. I wanted no part
of carrying the same gravel up that mountain for the third time so I went
with a shelter painting crew. Being the tallest one in the crew, I got
assigned the rafters.

​Fall is upon us and just in time leaves are turning and falling.

​also, the last years pine needles are falling.

​
Once more, a flock of turkeys have taken up residence in the Beech trees
behind the house.

​Saturday morning Wayne brought some visiting German teachers over just in
time to see the local Turkeys.
​

I have not started a new project for a while. So, I think I will undertake
one. What can I make from this piece of equipment?

​And finally, another bonus pick​.

Anyone else got anything to report?​ Hither, thither or yon?
