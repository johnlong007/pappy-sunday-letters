The plan for this week was 1. get the ford tractor running properly 2. Get
the parts '63 running and back in storage and 3. get the '37 running and
into the shop.

I left last weeks notes with a carb for the ford on order. It came in with
a broken parts but supplier promised to send a replacement without asking
for a return of the carb. Wayne brought carb with broken part over which I
decided to install. At that time I found the carb is not correct for this
tractor. Now, we have two incorrect carbs and a replacement (which we
believe will be correct on order. Stay tuned.

The blue '63 was procured as a parts source for the '63 being restored.
After taking such parts as needed, I put the car all back together with
best parts available. It that time it was to be driven back into storage to
await its fate. We never got it running but did put it back into storage by
force and wiles.

Pat owes me another couple of paint jobs in return for the MD. Last time
when I thought the '63 was ready to paint I learn ed after the fact it was
not even up to my low standards.
So, although I had thought the 37 PU was ready I now know better and
brought for storage to get it really ready to be painted. Had intended to
get it running en route but gave up on that part.
Sanford driving, dog supervising.

A bit of effort required for last part of the trip.

In our spare time, we harvested some next winter firewood.

Beginning of the floor tile work.

The 'convertible is running good. Fuel gage is not working. Wanted to go
with escort to gas station but did not get it done. Stay tuned.

Today, GSH, PBBD and I went to see Pat 's farm. In high shool vo ag they
told us the only way to be a farmer is inherit or marry.

His wife's ole home place has been a working farm for almost 200 years.
During that time a lot of buildings and a lot of old equipment have
evolved. A couple of highlights. I want to go back for extended tour.

All is well
