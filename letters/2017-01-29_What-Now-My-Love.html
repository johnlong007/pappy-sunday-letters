What Now My Love

8 photographs·323 words

Do you remember a couple of weeks back when I included a picture of my
left thumb ? Just to keep a balanced perspective:

​Another selfie.

On Jan. 11, I took GSH in to the Indian Path hospital ER because something
was amiss. The ER doctors on duty sprang into action and concluded she had
"Bell's Palsy". After getting home and reading up on the subject followed
by observing that something was wrong with her right side I conferred with
children and got an appointment to see Dr. Asst with our GP. He listened
and looked then scheduled and mri. We had that done on Monday the 23 and
got the results on Wed. the 26th.

So much for "rush to the ER" if you suspect a stroke. She had a stroke. Now
on blood thinners and a couple more pills wit therapy to follow I believe.
It has left her with some impairment. Stay tuned.

But, just so you will know she is being well taken care of:

​And, so you will know she is still being useful:

​For various reasons I did not do AT work this week. I did spent time
fiddling with the '49 Dodge. It has a lot of rusty metal. I pressure washed
the painted with "rust reformer". Hope that stops it in its tracks as I am
covering rather than cutting out panels. When I do metal work I wonder if
my father would be surprised, pleased or embarrassed. Probably the latter.

Wayne is working with a prospective contractor to get the downstairs
facility converted into a first class bathroom suitable for an elderly
couple with limited mobility. So far work has been confined to planning but
we think the go/no go day is getting close.

Note the cap.

​

​The only other event was my running report. Nothing to write home about.
Two weeks from yesterday. Why on earth did I agree to run ?
