February 21, 2010 · Sunday

Contrast

4 photographs·185 words

The weather was really bad early in the week and really nice by late.
Mostly I stayed in the house during the bad part and sanded on the car. Filled,
primed and sanded, repeat as necessary.

I did make a run to the secret junk yard on Cripple Creek Saturday. It
looks as though I will be able to get the parts JW lost (or broke). I told the
owner, Tex, that I will come back this Saturday with measurements for the
needed parts.

Of course, Anita pays no meaningful attention to the weather. She thought
the signs were right for giving John a chainsaw operation lesson. of
course, the signs were also right for fence building.

Once more, no AT work this week. I offered to go clear a section but Carl
could not find anyone to go with me. Probably just as well. He has already
canceled work for this week also.

Hal died. The whole process was pretty much what had been projected at the
beginning. He did rather well until about a month back. Since then it was a
rapid decline.
