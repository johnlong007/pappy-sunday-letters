Two Years

10 photographs·298 words

The last of our GGC Patricia got to meet. Quite a number have been added to
the family since then. I have been able to meet and get the mandatory GGF
picture with most but not all. I really like the Aura frame and apppreciate
getting to see the new ones as they grow. I also like to see pictures of
all the not so young doing things they do. Keep at it.

Our memory garden. I hope things work out so this place can stay in the
family for a few generations.

Sarah and Mickey had an outing loosely connected to child church baptism.

Both my father and my Grandfather were Free will Baptist since childhood.
After 40 or 50 years of being actively involved in the Shallow Ford
Missionary Baptist church, they agreed to take the plunge.

I am not sure it is official unless performed in South Indian Creek.

We have a number of past, and present members of the family who have
served our country. I feel honored and bask in reflected glory. Thank you
for your service.

Family and friends drop in. All know I am running on borrowed time and
borrowed blood. Sure glad I donated.

High point fr this week.

Christmas 1957 I believe. The day care where Alan stayed had a wonder
horse, he liked it. At the time. Patricia was making about $150/month. The
horse cost about $25. Turns out to have been one of our best purchases.

The AC is about ready to move into the museum. We are fiddling around
waiting to decide about our next project.

Maple and walnut leaves are down. Oak just started.
