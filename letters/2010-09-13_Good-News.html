September 13, 2010 · Monday

Good News !

8 photographs·376 words

If you believe no news is good news.

Since fellow do-gooder Dave had to go back to Mo for a follow up visit on
his Lyme disease this week, I spent Monday doing blow down cutting on a
section of trail.
Dave got a pretty good report. He is making progress and may have it under
control in another six months or so.

For the Tuesday regular work day, we repaired and painted the shelter at
Yellow Mountain gap. Normally know as "The Red Barn" shelter. This shelter is
the only one on our section which has a toilet facility. As the British
would say "A Loo with a view".

Yellow mountain is visible on top of the left side.

Using a forest service road, we can drive to this site so we took in a
bunch of equipment including a generator and paint sprayer. It made me think I
should get my old barn painted.

Work has resumed (at a snail's pace) on the Studebaker project .

Work has started at a somewhat higher pace on a log splitter project.
This project is to fabricate a log splitter to be tractor mounted as I have
given up on procuring manual log splitters. Pictures this week maybe .

Work continued on moving firewood from Blountville over here but no more
gymnastics.

I did uncover a critter.

The race car got more than two trips to Knoxville this week.One to deliver
a new TIG welder (required for the new race car project) and one to put the
old car through an outing.

As you may (or may not) remember, Pat was determined to get his time down
to 5 seconds.
Well, as happens with many goals, once he got there, he moved the target.
He made it to the final round in his bracket and lost by .01 seconds, 4.93
to his 4.94. It was the best race of the event.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, The Summer Camp garden has potatoes, beans,
gourds and tiny tomatoes free for the pickings.

I sort of keep up with various adventures by checking on Facebook. Katy
gave me a brief tutorial. However, I do appreciate the various reports which
come in from the outlying provinces from time to time.
