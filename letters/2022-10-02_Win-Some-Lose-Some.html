October 2, 2022 · Sunday

Win Some, Lose Some

13 photographs·270 words

The fight continues with noise in the Nissan Rogue. Time (Diane) and
money(Me) spent this week trying to chase and pin down source. Stay tuned.

The clutch froze in the "63 while not being used. I thought we had it freed
but turns out I was mistaken. We let it roll out of the storage bldg. only
to find it is still stuck.Next step involves a tow on the asphalt road but
I need a driver in order to tow around to the house.

.

No work on the 55 or the 37 this week.

However :

Various critters like the persimmons. The tree behind the house is loaded.
May ship some to Katy.

One of God's creatures.

I think only the squirrels work on the walnuts.

Before drying,the hull must be removed. A rather messy job.

The deer like most all the fruit and nuts we provide. There is a large Oak
tree (with acorns) at each end of the house.

Sort of fun watching them open chestnut burrs. The turkeys pick up their
scraps.

Last year, I had one tomato which the deer got just before it got ripe.
This year we put up a fence, sprayed for blight and harvested one tomato! A
mister Stripey from seedling provided by Laura.

Sarah was in town and came by to see the ole folks at home. Charmed her GM
into swallowing a pill !

First fire in the fireplace for the season.
