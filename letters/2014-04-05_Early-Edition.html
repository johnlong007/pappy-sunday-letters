April 5, 2014 · Saturday

Early Edition

11 photographs·446 words

Tomorrow morning, bright and early the trip to visit the Jack family circus
is scheduled to begin. Sunday night with Linda and Monday night with my
little sister. Reckon she will be surprised?

This week was mostly the same ole same ole.

I went with Sanford to visit his sister. She is home but with hospice care.
I can tell the decline with each visit but she and her husband, Dick, are
class people.

Of course, I am not about to take her picture but I did the next best thing
and took pictures of a couple of their cars. They have more. She has 70
Cadillac convertible.

They take such good care of thing that a friend, with no children, gave
them the Buick. No strings attached.

Their only son died just as he was finishing his PhD work. Dick has his
motorcycle but seldom rides it.

I asked Dick where I could obtain a piece of sheet metal for the LBT. He
sent me to a shop in JC. The shop foreman was really helpful, cut the piece
to size and broke bottom edge to attach to LBT.

I spent a lot of time cutting and fitting but it is now attached.

I let caution get the best of me and attached with counter sunk pop rivets
rather than welding. It also has a coating of the metallic epoxy along the
back edge. The body work is now pretty much done . Serious sanding and
filling are next up.

Don Hass, a fellow engineer, declared he had never met a tractor engineer
could not get running. He came and loaded up the little crawler to take
home and attack at his leisure. Stay tuned.

Also, as part of permanent inventory reduction, I let a couple of surplus
engines go:

He wanted the Chrysler straight eight but I refused.

We got in another pretty good day of AT work. Went in to Dennis Cove and
worked on trail rehabilitation out toward the falls. I shall include a
(almost) before and after picture for the work I did.
Pretty Tough digging because of the roots.

And just a reminder of what others are missing.

Cat report;

GSH is letting the cat have the run of the house at night. The little dog
is learning to co-exist with cat but fatty still wants to chase him and he
would rather run than fight.
