August 14, 2022 · Sunday

Toiling On

11 photographs·385 words

AS everyone IS aware Charlotte went off into the world in search of
adventure this Summer. She gave a family overview of her trip . I hope to
get an opportunity for further viewing. Once upon a time I was young and
good looking. Well, young anyway.

I bought this mower a few years back. It has been one of my better
purchases. Even with it I am still letting the property boundaries
encroach. The first Summer of use, I burned up a couple of expensive
belts. I finally learned to avoid jamming blades and burning the belt,
keep the mower running at high RPM. Then it mostly chopes limbs into
splinters. But not always. I recently finished off the belt which has
lasted 3 seasons.

The reason I went ahead with new belt installation was to permit mowing the
hill above the retaining basin. I sort of hoped David would come mow for me
. But, no such luck.

I finally decided which bumpers for the '55 to get rechromed. Sanford
stopped by the plating place where they quoted $1,000 per. I decided to
hold up on that item and leave for the next owner.

Work continued on the 37 PU brakes. We are about to punt on this front
wheel cylinder.

A & C often come over in the evening to help get GSH settled for the night.
Fixing supper is a bonus. I believe this night was a contribution by Sarah.

PBBD helps often during the day. Ham (donated by Sue Ketron) and tomato
sandwich.

Anita was over one morning covering for me while I ran around the block.
She came in, eating an apple and announcing they are about ripe. Came home
after church today and found PBBD had also decided they were ready to eat.

Not to be outdone,

We have a lot of woodpeckers.

I can and do operate the new washing machine . Had sheets on the line
waiting for the sunrise.

The guard cat stays on duty while I put up or take down washing.

FYI, we do appreciate the pictures many (but not all !) are posting on the
new device. Keep em coming.
