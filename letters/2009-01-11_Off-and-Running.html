January 11, 2009 · Sunday

Off and Running

11 photographs·278 words

Rain, a lot of rain. So the plan for the week involved a lot of staying
indoors and fiddling with various workshop projects. My Do-gooder partner had a
knee operation so we are on hold with that for a spell. AT work got rained out.
The first shop project was getting the sand (bead) blaster back in operation.

Another important event was in helping our oldest granddaughter celebrate
her birthday.
January is a big month for family birthdays. I am sure we will be getting a
lot of pictures and reports.

The rain filled the upper pond. And more

Having almost completed the last six projects. GSH started at least two new
ones.

She also found time to try out the new, all edge pieces pan. It works!

She also went on the Friday hike for the first time in a spell.

Dwight and his son Pat are building a drag racing car from the ground up.I
have been to check in on the project a few times. I went again Saturday. It is
most impressive. They installed the steering mechanism this time around.

\
To celebrate Wayne's being able to Take in solid food. The local family
members gathered in Saturday evening for B & G. A fine (filling) time was had by
all.

It may be of interest that my sister now has a digital camera and is able to
take and transmit pictures. I expect her to file regular reports in the
future.

If Rachel does not get back in gear, I am going to delete mail heading
toward the frozen North.
