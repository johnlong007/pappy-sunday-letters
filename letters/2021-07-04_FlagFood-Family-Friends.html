July 4, 2021 · Sunday

Flag,Food, Family, Friends

10 photographs·416 words

The older I get and the more I see our country divided By those I see as
takers rather than givers, the more I appreciate our flag and all it stands
for. I remember in the first 8 grades of school the day was started by
standing at attention, hand over heart and reciting the pledge.

Not only do we live in the Bible Belt, but also in Appalachia submerged in
Appalachian culture. Chief among the values is family. Are you aware that
within the family it is possible to shun (for certain behaviors) but cannot
disown, no matter what.

We made a trip to Erwin to check in on family there. Farmer Lisa gave us
some of her gold plated blueberries. She also told me a bit about how her
Dad's 1963J became seen as a problem rather than a treasure. Caused me
sadness.

Another feast, another tie that binds.

More family, more fun.
Mari, Eric and son Jonathan were in town for SCCA races at Bristol. We went
over to watch Mari run Friday and Saturday. The finals were Sunday but my
Bible Belt training sent me to church. But, keeping one toe in the water, I
stayed in touch via the device.

The note pasted below came from Mari after the final results were in. She
said she improved every run but not enough for the podium

Eric won; Jon second. Old age and treachery over youth and exuberance
today! 🤣

A little bit of work was at least started. Alan and Wayne decided the
tractor needed a brake job. Shoes are on order.

In my days, cars were important. In my psychology studies we learned that
ood if you only had one thing go go on to understand a man, look at his
choice of automobile.
In "63, Herb took delivery of a new "300J"
I wanted a letter car but convertible was not offered as letter car so I
ordered a custom "300" with the J engine etc. Each of us kept our 300s and
viewed as our treasured possessions.

Alas, the sentiments are non-transferable.

The long range plan was a wild life sanctuary . Good progress but it has
been overdone on the Gron' hog part. Anyway, Birds like Polk berries so I
leave the Polk plants. Since this one tends to sprawl out into drive, we
tied it up.
