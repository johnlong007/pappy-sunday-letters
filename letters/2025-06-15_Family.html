Family

17 photographs·332 words

the goal for this week was get the mower painted and re-assembled. Here, a
willing worker is busy prepping for painting.

Parts is parts. Made from scratch tool box. Bob handmade tall grain
deflector installed on a repaired swathboard.

Mostly re-assembled and painting well along.

Alan has a lot of tools including a pipe bending toll.

He and C have fabricated a replacement brush guard and are in process
installing it.

ayne had a different goal. He has been fretting the truck clutch for years.

Dropped transmission ,pulled bellhousing and clutch assembly Clutc disc,
the wearing part, almost gone. But not quite ! No pressure plate or
flywheel damage.

Dinner at the job site.

Back ready for next 300K miles.

I hope and expect a number of GC are glad they learned driving starting in
this vehicle. All that learning to pull out on a hill without using a
parking brake by slipping the clutch ! Clutch survived as did I.

Linda was talking about teaching her school kids a geometry lesson
which made me think of my days in the TN, forestry camp in 1951. Dug out my
hand made timber stick and showed her how to operate. No batteries required.

J & S came by with their turnip termite.John is allowed to hold and
feed but only under close adult supervision.

A & M stopped in for a visit . I got to talk with each and was able to get
her to enter family tree information in the file. She has been warned, you
get one, you get them all !

A fine day. Maria and Chris also came by. I got to catch up on life in the
fast lane. Anita gave me a pearl.

Then, proving the bring them up right saying !
