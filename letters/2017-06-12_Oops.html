June 12, 2017 · Monday

Oops

13 photographs·422 words

I hear rumors of much more interesting things going on in the lives of
various members of the family than are going on here. Same ole same ole.

Mowing season is in full swing. The plan has been (and continues to be)
that I would get the place in such a wooded situation that when I could not
tend to it it would not need tending. The time is getting closer. However
the new (for us) climb a tree mower has delayed the onset.

​
​
Aaron (and Beth) will remember mowing here with string trimmer. I did that
for years but now use the mower. Do have to be a bit carefull as it is only
stable up and down, turn sideways and it will slide.

I mowed the hill behind the house with string trimmer. I am going to have
to quit that as it has caused problems with my knee. I do not want knee
problems!

​
​Not much car work this week. I tried to start the '49 with no success. No
spark at the points, I am afraid to try to put points and condensor in with
my fumbly fingers. May have to stay tuned on that one. Did do one more
cardboard pattern fitting on the floor. I think we are ready to start
cutting on the red carped Lisa (sort of) gave me.

​
​The maple tree beside the Ag. building died. I suspect I damaged it at the
base while mowing some time in the past. Anyway, I cut it down. Not wanting
it to fall in the wrong place, I tied it off on the Bobcat. Notched and
sawed about 1/2 way then pulled it right on target. Now GSH will have a
good supply of brush to drag to brush pond.

​
​Did get out for AT work. Same kind of thing there. Only a few hikers now
and they are South bound flip-floppers. Here are my before and after
handiwork pictures.

​
​Remember the strawberries we planted last week? Doing great!

​
​The peppers and tomatoes in the garden are less great. ​The groundhog has
decided to help prune the tomatoes and cucumbers I planted on turn around.
Let the battle begin. I put up cages and netting.

​
This morning a couple of deer were helping with the June apple drop.

​
​The other critters and creatures are going along for the ride.

Aaron left home.
