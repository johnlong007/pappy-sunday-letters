April 13, 2025 · Sunday

Rites of Spring

13 photographs·271 words

The mowing season is now underway. It will get serious from here on
until first frost

We set out to have a nature preserve. GroundHogs and squirrels have
exceeded expectations.

Look close, A pileated woodpecker at the base of this tree. Just uphill
from the Ag building.

Turkeys galore. Time for eggs to be hatching little turklets.

Lots of these. Red belly I think.

Just out front. Bambi and friend.

I think Jim Foster told mutual AT friends it is ok to come visit. Ed has
had to drop out but Carl has been working 3 days a week on the AT. Our
section is now open before the big influx of Northbounders are due.
😊

We finally got started hanging hand tools on the Ag building. Sanford got
trapped.

Dave Edwards came by. He uses me for a sounding board (perhaps a clanging
cymbal).

New project for the week The parts are all cleaned now waiting for the
painter, was a push mower.

Growing up on the farm, my father told us what work he wanted done. We were
expected to get it done .period. I learned to make do and get by. Mostly
that has served me well.

We have three crosscut saws. One handle was missing. I found one on Ebay
for $49.

I fabricated the one shown in the middle below from a piece of a broken
hoe handle and a threaded rod

The cider press has gone to Wisconsin.
