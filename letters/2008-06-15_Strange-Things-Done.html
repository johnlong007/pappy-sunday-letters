June 15, 2008 · Sunday

Strange Things Done

11 photographs·356 words

Most of the week was spent getting ready for the Blountville old tractor
(and old owners) show. Charlie helped me drag out cars and tractors to get
ready. Friday was the day set aside to take tractors. I always work the
registration desk just inside the entry gate so I had to be there at 7 each morning and
stay until about 7 PM.

I did get to the outhouse project on Tuesday, after my final (at least my
intent) visit to the dentist. I am glad to report the siding is finished with
a bit of high ladder trim work left to be done. I am going to leave that for
the younger and more agile generation.

I stopped by the ECC recreation building and took the exam to quality to use
the fitness equipment. For the first time in recorded history, ALL my
parameters were inside the "normal" limits! (My cholesterol has always run high but
ratio and "good" are always OK.)

The number of tractors at the show was down, just like at Gray. We did have
a lot of engines and a number of vendors. Visitor attendance was good on
Saturday and it was a lot cooler than Friday. However, the rains came down just
when the children's events were scheduled to start bring an end to the whole
thing.

This afternoon, I put some of the tractors, the ones I could get running,
back in the barn. Also fixed the flat(s) on the little A-C and did a bit of
mowing with it. It really works. I was afraid to do much as had only put a
little gas in it.

One day, I went out to the lake and took a ride in a friend's speedboat.
Along with George and Dwight 6500 RPM should be pushing 80MPH, very rough, noisy
ride. Then Laura and kids showed up. So, George took the kids for a ride.

For some time I have been concerned that the Miata will not last forever. It
has 236,000 miles. So, we have a back-up.
