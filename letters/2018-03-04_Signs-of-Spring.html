March 4, 2018 · Sunday

Signs of Spring

15 photographs·373 words

I killed a house fly. Grannie Garland would say one fly in May is worth 50
in August.

Did anything newsworthy occur here this week? Not really other than the fly.

After the heart Dr, said my heart valve is getting louder he scheduled me
for an echo gram. Had that done but heard no report. Stay tuned.

Work is well underway on the pool room project, The ultimate objective is
to upgrade appearance, renovate structurally and raise room and water
temperature significantly. I have collected room temperature for last
couple of months . Will see results next Winter.

​The hood has been off the 63 for a long time. I started work on the hood
to get it into good shape to put back on the car. Of course, it will come
off again when painting time arrives.

AT work was back on this week. A lovely day cutting and peeling locust . We
use locust for side logs and steps. It lasts much longer if it is peeled.
We are helping clear land procured by some sort of AT related group. The
site is just across Yellow Mountain from the Overmountain (Red Barn)
shelter. Wonder if Katy knows where that is?

​On the home front, we are being over run by wild onions. I read that they
just shed Round Up but cut off stems and spray may work So, we attacked the
ones in front of house. We shall see what happens.

As most know, we do light M & R on the AT section from highway 91 to the
double springs shelter. When I was out Tuesday Carl told me a blowdown was
reported on the section. Since through hiker season is upon us I figured we
had better get on the job.
Saturday a group of willing workers lept into action.

​

​By the way, this is a section of trail Andrew helped build. Wonder if he
can recognize?

Diane had her eyelids nipped and tucked.

While GSH was hand holding, I skinned around the original property we
procured in 1966. Planted trees in 67 and 68.

Part of it I had not been on since we planted trees.

​

Is anyone out there?

If so, got pictures?
