April 2, 2017 · Sunday

Spring (Once More)

14 photographs·345 words

We have been losing a lot of friends and family the last few months. "Gone
Home". Sometimes it gets a bit discouraging. But, this week we went to
visit Beth and her family. It was a refreshing trip. Even better than
prowling around in a junkyard. Carl did not have AT work scheduled this
week but I was able to round up my own trail building crew.

​
Also able to get the 8N back to almost where the adventure started last
time. Now, ready to wait for Charlie.

​At home, I gave up on planting a garden and seeded the front field in
grass. No corn, no wedding flowers, no turnips. Just grass.

​Killing the cherry tree really bothers me. As an act of penance, i
purchased two seedlings and planted them. should produce in 2-3 years we
hope.

​I have been keeping in touch with Niece Lisa the tenant farmer, In
accordance with her understanding of her fathers wishes, I retrieved two of
Herbs (non-running) tractors.

In addition to not running, one has no seat and no steering. So, front end
must be picked up to move the tractor (and no driver required, most
uncomfortable seating). Bobcat handled easily but the much punctured tire
went flat.

​Took me 1/2 day to get it repaired.

​For do-gooder work, we took the truck out to the Loven cabin and loaded
slabs to supply Dave Dunham with wood for his outdoor firepit.

​In other exciting news ! work continues on the '63 seats.

​For Sunday lunch today, we ate by the pool and had a gospel quartet
serenade us.

​We walked all around the place this afternoon.
Several ramp patches are looking good.
The "dead" cherry tree is struggling for life.
The well pruned grapes are budding out.
Pear tree is full green but not yet blooming.
One skinny asparagus shoot showing.
Nothing showing on chestnuts or chinquapins.
Crab apples in full bloom and swarming with honey bees, More than I have
seen for years.

​
Life is good. Glad we made it this far.
