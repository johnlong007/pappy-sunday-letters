June 21, 2020 · Sunday

Bout Half

11 photographs·357 words

My father was a good man. The picture below was made when he was about 30
and they lived in Black Bottom in Elizabethton. I talked with a lot of
people while collecting information for my family history. The only
critical thing I heard was he never rested.
He supported his family and a good many others on each side of the family
through the depression.
I miss him

Flo said the car had a rumble seat and he gave them rides.

I think I was 21 when this picture was made. We were camping in Cades Cove.
GSH's mom gave us the scooter. It is still here. Not getting much usage.
John, note shirt.

Farmer Lisa has all sorts of woes. Her pond is being over run by some
invasive species. We went over and I sprayed with Roundup (after
reading all the cautiouns). Then it rained that evening. Have not been
back to see how it worked.

On a somewhat related topic, Wayne has worked on the Ford dump truck off
and on all week. As of today he has it running. Still have to bleed brakes
before giving it a trial run.

My mower got a flat tire. I pulled wheel, removed tire from wheel, patched
and remounted.
Four times.
Purchased and installed new tire.
Belt broke, but, I keep a spare.

Spare was wrong length. My mower is 5 foot, belt was for 6. New belt is on
order.

Coming in one night had company.

I had uncovered the strawberries to be able to pick. Deer ate all the
plants.
I need a deer hunter.

I am goal oriented, I need to have a plan for short and long time. If I
start a day without a plan I will have a headache in no time.
Goal for the week was saw the woodpile into firewood pieces.

Thanks to some unusually willing and capable helpers( in town for Ms.
Long's funeral} the woodpile is pretty much beat into submission.
