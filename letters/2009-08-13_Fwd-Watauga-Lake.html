Fwd: Watauga Lake

Hi Dave and Carol,

I wanted to let you know of some activities that have occurred since your maintenance trip around Watauga Lake on Monday.

On Tuesday, I received a report from the Appalachian Trail Conservancy about an encounter a camper had with a bear early Tuesday morning. Here's the meat of the report.

at 2:45 a.m. today Phillips was awakened by a bear "pouncing" on and ripping into his tent. He got out to see what had happened - the bear backed off to about 15 yards away, but did not leave. He and another hiker also tenting there decided to pack up and leave. He had food in the tent. He reports that the area has a lot of trash.
A current ATC volunteer who stayed at the shelter June 24 says a bear visited the shelter that night. He and another hiker were able to chase it off. They picked up trash and cleaned up the area the next day.

Bob Peoples spoke to the hiker either Tuesday or Wednesday and I spoke to Bob yesterday. The incident occurred at one of the campsites near Shook Branch. I forwarded the report to the Forest Service on Tuesday. They posted notices of the activity and guidelines for food storage, etc. at the shelter and at a gate near Shook Branch where a side trail leads to the AT yesterday. Because of the earlier incident at the shelter, their original notice highlighted the shelter area more than the campsites. Bob told me that there have been other encounters and they are concentrated at the campsites, not at the shelter. The Forest Service has modified the notice to highlight the campsites more and I'll pick those copies up for posting this afternoon.

I walked from Shook Branch Recreation Area to see the signs and the campsites yesterday evening. The Trail in that stretch is the best I've ever seen it look, especially since this has been a good growing season year. Your crew did a super job cleaning up the trash at the campsites. I want to post at least one of the modified notices right at the campsite, which is likely to get some use and some trash during race week next week, and move the one posted at the road to along the Trail. Most of all though, I want to commend the two of you, Ken, Pat, and Paul for doing such a good job on this section.

Thanks, and if I hear anything more I'll keep you posted.

Joe
