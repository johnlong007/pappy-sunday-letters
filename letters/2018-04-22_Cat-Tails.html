Cat Tails

19 photographs·350 words

The cat is settling right in. Little dog resents and gets in his face
barking at every opportunity.

But mostly is resigned .

We are starting to try to train the cat to go outdoors to rest.

For those who wish they were here.

​​

On our first trip to Alaska, I put a new round of tires on a set of chromed
wheels taken from the wrecked GTX I had purchased. I put the wheel covers
from the '63 Chrysler on the wheels. Somewhere in the Yukon territory, one
wheel cover flew off. We hunted until driven to retreat by the mosquitoes.
Later, crossing a rough bridge in heavy traffic in Cleveland another one
flew off. Did not bother to stop that time.

​
Now, trying to bring '63 back to life.
Found four of the covers on Ebay. Two good, one fair and one rough.
This week I rooted around and found the original wheels. If you look close,
you can see a green tint in the center.

Bob is ordering new tires.

​
Wood harvesting continues. Don't want to be like the grasshopper.
(Actually, don't really want to be an ant either.) We got the rest of the
wood from Jim Holston front yard.

​​

Also went to check the residue from one of the trees Mike had harvested on
the Long property.

​Decided right off I need a new chain and maybe a new saw,

​
GSH pays no attention to me when I try to get her to act her age.

​​He carried the saw, I did the sawing. A fine plan. Can just see the saw
moving along.

​
Met a lot of hikers.Some with dogs.

​​
​Work on the house continued. The front wall is no more. I believe the new
structure could handle a 747 .

Did I mention Collins Chew has a new lady Love. I like her.
Saturday , we went to a meeting of a poetry club at her invitation. It was
interesting and fun. She read three of her compositions and Colling apperaed
to listen.
