A Ramp in Time

9 photographs·260 words

I have been trying for 2-3 weeks to make a serious ramp run. The best site
I know about is about an hour away and somewhat difficult access.
Sure enough, the May Apples have completely hidden the ramps which have
turned yellow and died down.

However having been down this road before and knowing where to look, (and
crawling around on hands and knees) I found plenty of full grown ramps.
Two bags full. Now to dole out to needy family members.

1.

Early in the week, we took Carol Dunham to Dennis Cove for a ramp run.
There, the patch is only 1/4 mile walk.

On a more practical note.

Out of this exercise. we got a quart of strawberries.

I am determined to get the crawler running. The previous pictured IH man
suggested installing an electric fuel pump ahead of the existing pump.
After many fits and starts, I found a working pump and jury rigged an
installation.

Have not yet tried to determine if it will work. Stray tuned.

The Bamboo harvest is now stored in the newly re-roofed barn to cure.

I believe it was 1982 when GSH got a M'cycle from Santa.
It has served many masters over the years, Aaah the stories it could tell.
Anyway, it is back home where Wayne has visions of bring back to life (like
more or less new I hope) .

Perhaps the coming week will be more exciting ?
