April 9, 2023 · Sunday

Easter

11 photographs·353 words

Early this week I went through many saved pictures picking out Easter
pictures from years gone by. I came across the one below from about 1982 I
believe. Thinking our young and good looking Easter picture days are
limited , I decided we should have a remake. Diane is not an
incredible optimist. However she is an incredible get it done person.

Diane fished out the dress, did repair and conjured up a matching jacket .
She also procured a proper shirt for me in spite of my snide remarks about
they/them wearing pink I did my part by coaching GSH all week and waving
the dress and telling her how much fun it will be.

Easter dinner magically appeared after which with much fanfare we donned
the dress and adjourned to the driveway where I had parked the car.

Success, beyond my expectations !

For a bonus, we came back in and ate cake provided by Anita! It was clear
that Patricia enjoyed it with a smile I had not seen in a long, long time!

Now about that car, a 1955 Chrysler 300. As you may recall I dragged it out
of storage several months back thinking one weekend would have it in normal
operation. Ha !

Here, Alan and PBBD are working on the fuel level transmitter. To be
continued.

Not to be outdone, Wayne is installing the power brake booster he rebuilt.

PBBD is trying to decide which knock off wheel hub covers to use with the
powder coated wheels.

The power steering worked, the power brakes worked. I drove the car out for
pictures and later Wayne turned a few fast laps.

The tree leaning beside the drive fell. We sawed it up. PBBD decided to try
her hand at axe work. I think a few more lessons may be in order.

We (mostly she) put down a grass mat to try to heal some bare spots.

To all who posted and emailed Easter pictures, Thanks.
