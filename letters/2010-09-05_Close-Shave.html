September 5, 2010 · Sunday

Close Shave

7 photographs·440 words

This week we have been going to Blountville as opportunity permitted to do
more brush cutting and to bring a load of firewood back.

Soon after I made this picture, GSH stepped backward and tripped. The scene
unfolded before me in slow motion. I thought this is going to be much
worse than my accident she will break her neck or her back or both, As she was
falling backward over the side of the truck!
She squatted down with her backside out over the side, grabbed the edge of
the truck bed with each hand beside her and did a slow motion "skin the
cat" over the side and hit the ground on her feet !

How long does it take to fall eight feet? Less than 1/2 second I would
think. During that time I went from total despair to astounded, delighted and
relieved. Sure makes me appreciate her even more.

On another close shave note, I see all those dashing young men on TV with a
3 day beard so I decided to give it a go. I figured I would find out if it
made me irresistible. I found out.

For AT work a group came in from Wright State University. They come each
year. All in all, the best student workers we get. They worked on a
relocation at Hughes Gap which we roughed out last week. (I am going back tomorrow
to cut blow downs if any Jr. workers would like to go).

My dog is old, deaf, can't see well, stiff and sometimes grouchy, all just
like me.In addition, we had several of his teeth pulled so he has trouble
picking up his food.

GSH asked how long I intend to hand feed him. Guess what I told her.
For the other critter report:

Dwight spent most of his Saturday working on projects here.
Charlie, the long weed eater is running. I stuck the muffler on with one
retaining screw and tied a piece of wire around the body. Someone needs to
reclaim it.
Also, the Alfa lives again. The fuel leak was caused by a cracked line
between the tank and the fuel pump. Another victim of Ethanol? It took a while
but is now back in operation (as soon as we get a tag on it. If anyone in
the family wanted this car I would make them a real deal.
We also worked on the Studebaker. Miles to go. Stay tuned.

I am sure there were other adventures but I believe I will close for now.
It would be good to hear from the remote locales from time to time.
