April 13, 2015 · Monday

Part 2-Alf

9 photographs·549 words

Various members of the family have gone with us on trips with IBT. On the
bike trips, there will be an IBT leader and a sag wagon driver. Several
years back, our sag wagon driver was Alf. I got to know him pretty well and
found him to be a really interesting person, One of the most knowledgeable
of people of the world ofanyone I have met.
Alf was born in East Germany the in 1957 so he spent 30 years under the
Russian/ East German system. He served in the East German Army and has lots
of funny stories to tell.His Dad was a high person in Hitler's circle but
died in an accident. His step father was also well connected but also died
but of natural causes (all as best I could understand). Now, his mother
lives in an apartment near Munich near her other son and with her cat. She
is 94.

Alf arrived Friday noon. We went to Erwin and ate lunch at Clinchfield d
rug lunch counter. Then we went to Herb's and spent the afternoon "working"
on the fire engine.
Friday night, we had soup beans and cornbread. It was a bit like the
wedding feast.

Saturday, AT work was at Doll Flats. Go to Elk Park and turn right onto
forest service roads and go to the top on the mountain. on the way to Elk
Park, we stopped at Bojangles (per Alf's request) for breakfast. Alf seemed
to have a fine day. Several of the guys served in the military, several of
those spent time in Germany and Alf likes to talk.

When a person works one day with us, they get a patch to sew on their pack.
Alf seemed rather pleased with his.

Saturday evening, Sanford came by and took us to Elizabethton to the
regular Saturday evening (5-8) cruse in. We stopped at Pal's for a hot dog
for supper. He liked the big, old cars and told us about the East German
two cycle cars.

Alf grew up in East Germany where no religion was favored. He said he is
not religious and had never been to a church service. He did not want to go
to church with us. I assured him it would be OK if he just went out of
curiosity and sort of talked through what to expect.he went with us. He
does know a lot about various religions and goes to a place in California
to meditate.

Alf likes to rock climb. Serious rock climbing which he has done all over
the world including El Capitan. Saturday, we had a sweet thing who is a
writer for Elizabethton newspaper out with us. Apparently she is a rock
climber of some degree and got Alf convinced he needed to check out
Grandfather Mountain. So, Sunday PM we went to Linville Falls and
Grandfather mountain (stopping for Sunday dinner at McD's). Then, back to D
& D's for supper.I told Diane we had seen more sights than food.

Today, we sent Alf on his way back to Germany. The IBT trip we usually go
on. this year will be the last week in August , first of Sept. For this
one, Sue will be leading and Alf driving sag wagon. Anyone want to go?
