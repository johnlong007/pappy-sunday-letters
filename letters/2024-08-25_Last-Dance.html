August 25, 2024 · Sunday

Last Dance

9 photographs·248 words

One year ago yesterday since the last event in which I was sure Patrice
recognized me.

I am grateful for all who have stepped up to donate blood. Never even
thought about being on the receiving end.

Does this look familiar to any recent donor?

I perked up somewhat after a refill on Monday.

However, they failed to tell me "don't bleed" and I got a nosebleed
Wednesday evening. Fortunately my home health nurse has all the supplies
needed to bring it under control.. Labs tomorrow will be interesting. Stay
tuned.

In other exciting news, Diane continued working in the circle. The Patricia
memorial flowers are looking good.

Once more, the washing machine drain clogged leading to an indoor flood.
Our own calvary came to the rescue!. Cleared the drain and laid out a plan
for next time.

Once I get on the tractor I can still do OK. Moved some brush from behind
the house to the brush pond.

Fran came for a visit. She and husband Ron took an extended Western trip
along with a number of close family members.. She took us through a
hodgepodge show and promised Ron is preparing an organized condensed
version. We promised to come to her house soon to see his presentation.

One day, it was so pretty we decided to eat out. We were joined by one cat
and one frog.
