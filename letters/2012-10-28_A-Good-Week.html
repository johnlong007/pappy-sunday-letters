October 28, 2012 · Sunday

A Good Week

13 photographs·350 words

http://www.state.tn.us/environment/na/natareas/virgin/

For the past few years we have been going on a back packing trip with Kim
and Jim. Usually 2-3 nights. This year we went to Virgin Falls down near
LLLL's.
It was a lovely place and perfect weather.
It is a bout a 10 mile loop passing four major falls. In each case, the
water comes out of a cave under a cliff, goes over and undercut rock rim,
falls freely and then disappears into another underground passage.
Since it was a short hike and weight was not an issue, I carried Bratwurst,
buns, Root Beer and a pack of marshmallows. How is that for roughing it ?
We made the trip in Kim's Lexus SUV. It started jumping out of gear as we
were coming home and quit driving as we turned in here. It is now parked
here.

We of the walnut trees on the Blountville annex property has very large
walnuts. Anita brought over a box full one day recently. We decided to go
hunt for more. We did find another bucket full but I decided the barn is
more scenic.I would like to see the blacksmith shop.

Friday we went on the hike to Grassy Ridge. Grassy Ridge is on top of the
ridge at Carver's Gap across from Cloudland. It is regarded as probably the
most scenic spot on the AT.

Rejoice ! Charlie came over Saturday morning. He had told me he was coming
so I had help needed projects lined up. All will be delighted to know that
the parking brake on the Miata is now functional. Also, he removed, cleaned
and gapped the plugs in the Miata, it should be good for another 100,000
miles.

Sunday afternoon we went with David and Edna Quillen to see the "cabin" he
has built overlooking the river near Damascus. Just across the river from
the creeper trail. It is exactly the way I picture a cabin should be. One
big room with enclosed bedroom and an open 1/2 loft. Full basement and a
covered deck on two sided looking out over the river.
