Trail Daze

6 photographs·204 words

Well, my poor ole me comment about my one and only willing worker brought
another worker out of the woodwork.

​
This paticular WW also expressed an interest in gardening so I prepped a
site. Thanks to Herb I now have a few tomato plants out . I am going to
turn the rest of the site over to WW. In addition I have promised the
garden down front after she gets this one all planted. Stay tuned.

​

I have the metal repair work pretty much finished on the LBT. I would
really like to get a coat of primer applied but first I have to do all the
filling and sanding. I worked on that quite a bit this week.

GSH has started to make a mini skirt out of a LOL dress. I think she is
going to cut a splice. Stay tuned.

​

​WW brought in a string of fish from school. We went hiking in to Laurel
Fork Gorge Saturday.

​This weekend was Trail Days at Damascus, Va. Our trail crew recruits about
199 hikers for two days work Sunday and Monday. In return they get supper
Sunday and Monday night a patch and a tee shirt.
