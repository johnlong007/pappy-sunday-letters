August 22, 2010 · Sunday

Good Riddance

8 photographs·604 words

Monday, I should have stayed in bed, all week.

Major discretionary time activity for the week was working at the
Blountville annex aka "Spring Park". The saw logs have all been safely relocated so
the remaining work is to relocate the fire wood and saw it into usable size
or saw it first then relocate.

It rained, se we decided to saw the wood first. I decided to sharpen my
saw. After sharpening, it would not cut at all. Later, Dwight took one look
and said I had used the wrong file." What's that noise?"

Another major Blountville activity was continued cleanup of the front
corner.

A close look will reveal a patch of uncut weeds beside the road on the left
side. There is a reason why it remains uncut. As I was working along the
road with the weed eater I stirred up a yellow jackets nest and received a
number of stings on my right arm, shoulder and face. Not to be deterred, I
departed then returned a safe distance away and started mowing along the
bank from the field side. This time, I stirred up a very big swarm of yellow
jackets from another nest . They promptly chewed up the same parts of my
anatomy. Stay tuned !

Tuesday was AT day. At Doll Flats the trail runs along the edge of an open
field, enters the woods makes a 90 degree turn goes about fifty yards and
makes another 90 degree turn.The management decided the trail should go
direct across the field cutting out the two turns. I took the weed eater and
cut a trail in the grass across the field. Later, another willing worker
decided he could do better and cut a parallel path across the field. We debated
as to how to label the new section as a passing lane or have a north lane
and a South lane.

We had finished work when the rains arrived.

For the good news, Dwight found a person (and son) to install the
Studebaker windshield and rear glass.

The windshield went in place in about one hour.

The rear window took all afternoon, a lot of persuasion and is now
strapped in place. It must have distorted while out for so long. Tomorrow, we
will find out if it is going to stay at all.


Take a close look at this picture and guess what happened.

Dwight has procured a new video game to play with his son Pat. It is
called, "beat the light".
It is gradually being revealed that some of the competition at the drag
strip gets off the line quicker than Pat, So, a practice light assembly has
been obtained. Dwight and Pat exercised their switch thumbs this afternoon.
The object is to release the switch ASAP after the yellow light goes on
turning on the green light. But, too early and the red light goes on.
Both father and son were about 1/2 second most of the time. Dwight red
lighted more than Pat.

After leaving Pat, we visited all the old Brewer home places as well as
grandparents then came back through Dandridge and viewed the Bush plant.
Catherine, it occurs to me that some of your kids might enjoy employment ?

The major frustration for the week was
1. A weed eater which will not start
2. A leaf blower which will not start
3. A (new) lawn mower which will not start

The good news is, none of these things really matter.

It might be noted that some members of the extended family are quite
derelict in their status reporting.
