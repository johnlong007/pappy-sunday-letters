December 6, 2009 · Sunday

Continuing On, Literally

6 photographs·250 words

Where shell I start? The big news for the week is that this is the
beginning of our social season. Today, the Blountville old tractor owners had
their annual Christmas dinner.

As the name implies, this is not a bunch of spring chickens.

Since I stayed home from AT work Tuesday to entertain my Thanksgiving
leftover sister, I went with the Friday hikers.

Not much younger than the old tractor owners but a bit more mobile I
believe.

Progress continued with steps forward and less forward on the Silk Purse
project. It should be noted that people are coming in from miles around to
admire the work in progress and the workers in action. Parking break is
installed, fuel tank is installed. Stay tuned.


We have been keeping a fire in the shop stove for the last week. It keeps
the shop warm and is a bit easier to manage than the fireplace insert. It
has not yet been decided what to do next week while I am gone. One thing I
have decided is that we are going through a lot of wood. May have to saw some
more.

My running went better this week.

Hal is having sort of a rough time of it. He had a port installed before
starting the latest chemo series. But, it is a two week schedule and port
has failed each time. They wear him out trying to get it in operation. He
looked frazzled when I went by to see him.
