May 24, 2015 · Sunday

Continued

12 photographs·225 words

Monday work continued on the AT project at Doll Flats. At the end of last
year I did not renew my chainsaw certification deciding to leave that to
the kids. However, through various circumstances I have become the rock
mover and splitter for our section. Monday Carl gave me a couple of willing
workers and asked that we go over the whole section and remove any
immovable objects. It was sort of fun.

The plan was work until 5:30. About 4 it started to thunder so I collected
my tools and headed down off the mountain. I got most of the way down
before the downpour hit.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, GSH started a new silk purse.

Upon the recommendation of top management and lacking any willing workers
at the ole home place, I decided to organise my overhand garage and storage
site.

We had company from time to time!

Also had a fairly good supply of strawberries.

Friday evening, in the absence of our own picker and grinner, we went to
Blountville for the jam session. It was great and I recommend highly.

Saturday morning was DB graduation for Wayne's oldest son ,Saturday evening
was picnic in the park for same son.

In between, Dan and his blushing bride came by along with Beth, Jake and
sprats.

All in all, a fine week.
