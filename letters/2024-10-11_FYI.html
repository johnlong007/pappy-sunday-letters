Flora Smith Obituary
Flora Garland Smith

September 27, 1927 -

October 8, 2024

ERWIN - Flora Garland Smith, age 97, of Erwin, passed away on Tuesday,
October 08, 2024, at the Johnson City Medical Center. She was born on
September 27, 1927, in Erwin to the late Conway Garland and Nancy Howell
Garland.

In addition to her parents, she was preceded in death by her husband
Charles W. Smith in 1999; three brothers and four sisters.

Flo is survived by her two sons: Gary Smith and Michael Smith and their
wives, as well as by several nieces and nephews, grandchildren,
great-grandchildren, and great-great-grandchildren.

Flo was a kind-hearted and generous woman, beloved by all who knew her. She
was a lifetime member of Shallowford Baptist Church.

A private family graveside service will be held in Evergreen Cemetery with
Pastor Zach Smith officiating.

In lieu of flowers, the family request donations be made in memory of Flora
to: Shallow Ford Baptist Church, 315 Old Highway Road, Erwin, Tennessee
37650.

Online condolences, photos and memories may be shared with the Smith family
at www.ledfordfuneralhomes.com.

Robert Ledford Funeral Home, 720 Ohio Avenue, Erwin is privileged to serve
the Smith family. (423) 743-1380.
