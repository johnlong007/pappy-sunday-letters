June 30, 2024 · Sunday

So So

12 photographs·343 words

Anyone remember this M'cycle ? Wayne came wheeling in on it.

Who put the most miles on it? Who used to get their license?

It was a Christmas present for Patrice in 1982. Until then, she only rode
the Trail 90. She rode the Twin star once and said "It's too big" and
never again rode it.
The Trail 90 has a new engine but has never been put back in operation.

Family is important. I publish my Sunday night notes in hopes of keeping
the family tied together.

Plan A called for delivery of the implements we had reconditioned to
their new home last Sunday Now, we are working on plan B.

Til then, the implements have a new parking spot.

I have a morbid fear of idle hands. I retrieved a spike tooth harrow from
storage and moved it down for M & R. Some Summer campers will remember it,
some rode it while preparing the upper garden. It was in first class
condition then. But, I did not properly store it leaving weather and
termites to cause serious damage. Some woodworking family members may be
called in for help in restoration.

I am about to decide it was all hand made.,including the spikes.

The sawyers got started on Bob's Walnut logs. I believe this plank
would make a fine thing. Not sure what.

After Patrice built the deck she decided to build a little pool. So, she
built on. It has sort of turned into frog habitat so PBBD put it back in
operation. Wayne worked on the pump electrical .

Pool now back in operation. Look close and you will note one frog stayed
home.

Gold finches came in from the cold. A few, but not many, house finches also.

My grandfather used to sit by his "Sang" patch for hours. I wondered what
he was thinking. I fluff the cat and watch the birds.
