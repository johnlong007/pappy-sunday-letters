Father

13 photographs·385 words

I would guess my father was about 8 when this picture was taken (X). The
only parent practice I tried to do differently than he did with me was to
allow the child to have and express an opinion.
My father did not tolerate what he regarded as disrespect.

I was 20 when Alan was born. June 16 ,1956 (time has flown by !)

We had moved back to Erwin for the Summer . I went to ETSU for the Summer
to knock off a number of non-technical classes.

This week we continued on our mission to drag in old farm implements and
restore them to at least operating condition.

The drag disc I had purchased many years ago and parked in my own fence row
is almost ready. However, a few parts were nissin so we went to see Farmer
Lisa and try to find the disk I had used in my early 50s days on the farm.
($10 to plow and disc a garden).

We found it, Herb had parked it right on the edge of the creek bank, now
submerged in a thicket.

I had heard that a For Sale sign had been posted at the corner of the
property next to ours across the road from the old home place. Just in case
anyone is interested. It is on the left side, Herb's is on the right of our
property.

We are refurbishing a carry all, One i purchased from Albert.

The seal in the mower gearbox failed.

Fortunately, the family handyman installed a new one. Now back in operation.

Patrice's white Walnut tree overhangs the house and causes moss growth on
the shingles.. The willing workers trimmed a bit with more to go.

Diane had cleaned the bushes growing across the drive in our Pawpaw
thicket. That brush along with the white Walnut trimming made a load.

While the mice are away----. We went out on RR to press in a drawbar pin
for the disc in progress. One of the things we observed on the Erwin field
trip

The first step in restoring an implement is rust removal. Fortunately help
is at hand.
