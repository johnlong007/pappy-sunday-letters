June

12 photographs·174 words

A lot like last week.
Tires are mounted, wheels painted.

Wheels installed and final cleaning/sanding under way.

I assume everyone knows what a swath board is. When mowing in tall grass or
grain, if the board is not high enough an arm is attached to extend height
. The extension was missing so Bob made one. You can see when final
assembly occurs. Stay tuned.

Armed and dangerous !

A lot of time and effort went into trying to extract the logs and limbs
from the Walnut blowdown,

Moderate success but severe pain in my back from distortion.

I hope to delay further logging until cavalry arrives.

Another fine outing on RR.

For those with memories of Blountville school.

Our trees are alive with the sounds of birds. A lot of birds of many
species. This one was humming.

I am not sure how the deer missed during Winter foraging.
