Growing Old

14 photographs·356 words

As the kids were growing up I always tried to take a picture of each on
their BD and at Easter. As the family has multiplied and spread out I have
encouraged sending me an Easter picture which I can put in my file.

It seemed like a good plan to collect Halloween costume pictures and keep
those also. I received several quite good ones this year but some I
expected were missing. I sent John a note of complaint and told him he
(they) is too young to be old. Others came to mind also.

Dog has been here for a full week. She is fitting in well and is mostly
tolerated by the cat.

Half of our children have been out tending to their grandchildren this
week.Anita came by to feed the birds and liven the place up before leaving
town to inspect her newest grandson.

One day A & C invited us out to watch Alan put new brake rotors on his
vehicle and for an outdoor lunch.

Once again, Sanford came by Friday morning with breakfast from the Burger
Bar.

Wayne stopped by and helped his sister pulle the engine out of the 37

Diane pressure washed and hug the engine out to dry.

We did determine why the left front wheel brake on the 55 was not engaging,
The flex line had collapsed internally.

Now the mighty Oak is shedding leaves.Another week should bring end to leaf
blowing season.

The big project for the week was splitting the large difficult chunks of
wood.

We could have put the log splitter on the tractor and may do that yet. But,
for now, we did it the old fashioned way.

If any willing workers show at Thanksgiving, opportunity awaits.

I understand Meredith and Conor went to California in search of Bigfoot.

The home health nurses come by on Tuesday and Saturday, This one is
Darlene, I like her. GSH was checking her hair.
