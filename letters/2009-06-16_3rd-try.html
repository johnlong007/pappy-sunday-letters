June 16, 2009 · Tuesday

3rd try

8 photographs·477 words

The week started pretty normal. We had a couple of do-ggoder projects.
Trimming shrubs for an elderly couple from church family then repairing a
warped walkway for a church widow. We had put the walkway in about a year ago.
The sun had caused one end to pull up making a tripping hazard.

I did not get to go on AT work Tuesday but Christopher , Charlie, Benjamin
and I think Kenneth went.
The feedback I got from Paul and Dave was that they did well. I will quit
there without asking more questions.

The next adventures involved the cherry crop. The crop was less than 1/2
normal this year but still tasty when properly processed.

The Blountville Old Tractor Owners show is this coming Friday and Saturday.
I hope to get some willing workers to get some tractors running and to the
site. Toward that end, I have been piddling with various tractors
sometimes aided by GSH et al. The BIG event was getting headlights on the Cletrac.

The Ford has been an aggravation. Last week, we dragged it up and got it
running. But, when
I tried to move it to the shed it would not start. GSH and willing
assistant helped move it behind the storage building. Today I am going to drag it
back up and see if Dwight can help solve the problem or problems.
Tuesday we went to St. Thomas for pre-admitting, spent the night with LLLL
then back Wed. to be sliced and diced. I was hoping to get out Saturday but
they kept me until Monday, Apparently I had a heart blip sometime along
the way plus the long ride home deterred them.


I have a bunch of studying to do. But, I took the classes, read the
literature and listened to the experts at St. Thomas.

What I believe so far is my problem was not caused by the usual factors.
The things I ate were basically good and exercise much better than normal. It
appears than falling to eat adequate amounts of fruit and vegetables
leading to being deficient in vitamins and minerals
(Magnesium and Potassium) caused hardening of my heart arteries.The Dr,
by-passed 5 blocked arteries.

Most of the people there had obesity and diabetes issues causing their
heart disease. The experts attributed that to diet and exercise issue. Junk
food, TV and video games.

Linda took a picture right after I was brought out of the operating room
and while still zonked. I suppose she will release to those interested in due
time. I will include a couple for general interest.

St. Francis is big, efficient and well equipped for mass production on
heart repair work. I have no idea whether my procedure warranted the trouble
involved in going there.
