May 25, 2014 · Sunday

Try Aagain

13 photographs·417 words

We left off last Sunday with about 100 willing workers waiting to hit the
mountain again early Monday. About 60 actually showed up so instead of
getting to supervise I got to work?
We were completing something like a five year project to open
15 zig zags up the side of Roan Mountain. We id all the relocation leaving
maybe 50 feet at each end until the very last thing.
Carl sent me to finish section 15 where it ties in to the old trail/

​
​ So if you walk down from the top you can cross this section.
We were almost 5PM getting out so the WWs were about frazzled.

​
​On the way home in the VW the temp gage started to fluctuate but never did
peg so I kept moving along.

​
​ I went to Advance to buy hose and anti freeze, while talking to me, the
clerk answered the phone. I picked up the ruptured hose and left. He came
running after me explaining it is company policy to answer and tell caller
to hole. I told him it is my policy to leave and go elsewhere. I did end up
getting Bob to procure the antifreeze for me at his personal repair shop.
Only $30/gallon instead of the $50 O'reilly wanted.

Meanwhile, back in river city, GSH continued painting her deck under the
watchful eyes of her fans.

​
​
Followed by lunch:

​
​Followed by R &R with the lion and the lamb.

​
​
I finally got the rotary mower back together. New blades and secured on the
hub along with a couple of tasteful patches.

​
​

​
​
I kept watching for Maria to come up the drive to continua garden planting.
Instead, it appears she sent in the second team.

​
​I did make a ramp run. Herb had to go to skin Dr. and stopped by on his
way home. GSH whipped out breakfast complete wit scrambled eggs and
