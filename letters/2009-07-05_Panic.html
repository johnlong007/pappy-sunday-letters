July 5, 2009 · Sunday

Panic

5 photographs·399 words

This week has been spent in preparation for Summer Camp.

Mostly that meant running the water line from the shed to the pool to fill
the pool with solar energy (Al Gore approved) pre-heated water. We bought
500 feet of new 1 1/4 inch black pipe then added a couple of hundred feet of
old pre-used one inch pipe. That was a mistake, the old pipe kept spring
leaks and we had to keep cutting and splicing. I hope I learned at least one
lesson.

The next big project was trying to clear out some of the tractors which got
dragged up here in preparation for the Blountville tractor show. Of
course, having sat for two weeks, starting was a problem. Finally got the JD put
away in the shed and several others scattered around the hill above the
shed waiting to be moved into the dry.

Also tried to get the whole place mowed.

Thursday was my appt. with Dr, Shuman at St. Thomas. We went to LLLL's Wed.
evening, spent the night and on to Nashville early and home afterwards.
Next will be an appointment with Dr. Boyd (the heart Dr. I first met with) in
a couple of months. I was cleared to go back to running and have done so
the last couple of days. Slow, very slow, I can scarce wait until I feel so
much better than I have for years.

Not much celebrating on July 4. GSH was on the community band float in the
KPT parade but I did not see the parade. We went to the Blountville parade
which was well done and well attended. I got a picture of the Central band
with John on the front row. By the time I found Maria she was past so
missed my picture there. Maybe next year.

Anyway, I can include a picture of her horse.Lots of critters around the
place this week, but, no pictures. I flushed a red fox while I was mowing up
on the hill where we tore down the old barn. Also flushed a fawn which had
a bed under the pear tree while I was mowing our Thursday picnic site. We
had a momma turkey with two little turklets but both disappeared one day
then she gave up and left. Very sad.
