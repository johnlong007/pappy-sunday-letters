Just Older

7 photographs·228 words

For obscure reasons, I was digging through the boxes of pictures and
scrapbooks my mother lift in my care. The cat has been helping.

I found a stack of pictures which Mom had taken and decided to scan so they
could be made available to all interested parties.

Below is a picture of the homeplace as it was before electricity and
running water.To the right was a screened in porch with a well and a pump.
Beyond that a small no heat room where the boys slept on strawticks.

Andrew and Rachel came to visit Saturday and brought EJ with them. Watching
her grandparents interact brought to mind this picture from Mom's
collection. Anyone look familiar?

A & C have been coming over some evenings to be helpful.

Saturday the task was get the sawmill assembled.

I understand it is now assembled and loosely bolted together. No glitches
so far. I understand Aaln has a plan for putting it into service. I don't.

It was chilly today!

Ant old timer Chrysler person there is /was no such thing as a "C300". The
correct designation is "Chrysler 300". After 199 55 letters were added
starting with "B". Herb's was a "J". My 1955 Chrysler 300 is now in the
Virginia Transportation Museum.
