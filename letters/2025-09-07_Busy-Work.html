September 7, 2025 · Sunday

Busy Work

18 photographs·346 words

My father had at least three careers.

Military from about 1915-1926.
Bemburg in Elizabethton from 1927 _1943
Metalworking first Liberty Lumber in Erwin then his own shop 1945-1965

He could make any metal item from sheet metal, Gutters, hoods, buckets
(Laura has one)
He used soldering for all his work. I watched but never tried using an
iron and a blowtorch.

I have worked the last couple of weeks cleaning some of his blowtorches.

Got one going yesterday. First time I had tried for maybe 65 years.

Getting hot.

All but one cleaned

Soldering iron in position.

Still plugging away on the hickory.

Note that I can only work sitting .

Needed to spread gravel so I got the Bobcat out and stirred the chip pile
while I was on the Bobcat. Composting very well best I can tell

Just in a couple of low spots.

Katy's hornet nest is growing nicely. They catch and eat insects. Flies.
They used to try to pick freckles from my arms while I was milking. Do not
disturb!

I get free recitals daily.

Found one more item to prep and hang on display. The work bed is missing.
Intent is to fabricate a replacement.

Trying to stretch the tires enough to mount without pinching the tubes.
Need a sunny day.

The small fuel tank is cleaned and installed. As soon as the line to carb
is installed, we will give it a try at starting.

Cleaning and painting is well along.

Toe shoe for cytter bar also missing . Fabrication is waiting on Wayne the
welder. He has more pressing issues.

Both Sarah and Meredith have used and expressed appreciation for the pool.
I never gave that particular usage in mind but am delighted it has been
useful.

This pictured squirrel lost some fur someway a while back. He seems to be
regrowing.
