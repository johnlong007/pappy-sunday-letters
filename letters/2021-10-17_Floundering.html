October 17, 2021 · Sunday

Floundering

7 photographs·210 words

We made a run to Erwin this week. Partly to check on farmer Lisa but mostly
to get GSH back in the riding around mode. FYI the hill in the far
background is Temple Hill

We believe the fuel and hydraulic problems on the Bobcat have been
resolved.in this picture Wayne is moving dirt and GSH is observing. Another
dry week and retaining basin should be ready to try for another season.

Our almost resident mechanic finished gapping and cleaning the 392 plugs.
Any worth his/her salt mechanic knows that old Hemis do not have gaskets on
the spark plugs. The plug seal tube serves as the gasket. Leaving the
plug gasket on causes oil leaks. Ace mechanic removed the plug gaskets.
Car seems to be running very well.

Also under a watchful eye, A&C tried operating my walnut cracking machine
on walnuts saved from last season. Alan was pleased with results and
Catherine whipped out a brownie with walnuts added.

Walnuts are like firewood, one must harvest a year ahead. The kindly
landlady gathered several buckets of walnuts and carried up on the landing.
There, they await tromping and stomping to remove the hulls .
