September 27, 2020 · Sunday

Fall all Over

13 photographs·312 words

When I went in to see GP last week he looked at a place on my neck and said
looked like Basel Cell to him. I asked if he could do it, he said yes and
scheduled appointment. He made a much bigger production than Dr.Sago does
but no co-pay. Have to go back Friday to get stitches out.For the first
time in recorded history, we got the full battery of ole folks shots, Flu,
pneumonia and shingles.

Monday I unloaded the trailer loaded with wood from last week. All in the
first stack in this picture. I am thankful I can still do things like that.
Which reminds me, time to go jump out of another airplane? Savannah?

With the newly unloaded trailer, we went to Erwin and collected the
lawnmowers previously dragged out into the open. I talked with one of my
tractor club friends upon return. He works on small engines and promised he
would come check'em out. To be continued.

A couple of the riders look pretty good. No idea as to engine condition.

I always work better under close supervision.

Herb's best friend?

We rolled the '37 out with intent to get it running. But, found it had no
spark at the plugs and I had no non-E fuel.

So we moved it back in, dried distributor, sanded points and now have
spark at distributor. Also procured fuel. Stay tuned.

Wayne came over hunting for Alfa bits. While here we checked the pear tree.
Loaded except lower limbs and very few on ground . Wonder why?

Shook the paw paw trees. end of season crop.

The rains came down so I checked the retaining basin. So far, so good.

Fall !
