Into Swamp

tI have never been bad about second guessing myself (or others). But,
sometimes after a painful experience I do like to look back to see what has
been learned and what should be done different next time.

In that line of thought, the restricted washing machine drain is still
restricted. Seems pretty evident the project should have started with the
snake to locate obstruction.

Stay tuned for the next thrilling chapter.

Most of the week it was cold so we stayed in. I cracked walnuts. I have a
box, maybe 2 bushel of walnuts. A walnut cracker and all the walnut kernels
I expect to need. It appears they are priced at about $15/lb. Anyone want
to get rich?

Bob and Laura worked on converting the ill fated darkroom into a full
functioning closet.
.

The rains came down and the retaining basin filled up. Close to top , the It
appears a couple of wayfaring travelers thought it looked inviting.

It has been a few years since we had gravel delivered for the driveway. The
cost has more than doubled.

Due to the low overhead in the drive they cannot spread. But, we have a
bobcat and a tractor !

A highlight of the week ! Meredith came by to talk about the
bathroom upgrade project and to deliver some cup cakes to the ole folk at
home. Fortunately we were able to feed her lunch sine a couple of friends
from church (Don and Nancy) had done their version of meals on wheels.

Another highlight !

The family circus at rest. Things are going well with the home health care.
They are working on my hand and neck pain. Making real progress reducing
pain in each. Working on balance issues with GSH and tell me that
improvements are being made. I have had no problems related to my valve job
A couple of week before I go back for check up. I did get picked back up to
a trot for my track outing.
