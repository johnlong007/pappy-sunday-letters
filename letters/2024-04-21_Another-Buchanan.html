Another Buchanan

13 photographs·235 words

In other news, Jim Foster brought us a mess of ramps. Jim is a distant
cousin and also another direct descendent from John Alden. What I
mostly remember from Jim was on a hiking trip with Patrice and others on
The Old Settlers trail in the smokies. We were sitting around the campfire
when Jim asked me "do you tease her all the time?"

Jim promised me he would go with us this week to a patch near the AT. Stay
tuned.

Also in the news, our resident bird enticer has lured Woody up close to the
house.

I have not tried the track since my ankle mishap. For a trial run we made 3
laps today. You may also note it is Dogwood Winter in Blountville.

Nurse Rebecca came by . She cheerfully demonstrated her skill by clipping
the dogs toenails. Ignored the other old dog in need.

Team continued work on the 37. The crankshaft drive pulley is back from the
shop and already installed.

Aunt Flo does like ramps and likes to be remembered. We split with her.

Speaking of remembering, Meredith brought us some tomato plants to feed the
deer and groundhogs.

glad I can still operator tractor

And Bobcat
