Friday Hikers,

The hike of Friday, Nov. 16, was a three-mile loop on a mountain that's on
the north shore of Watauga Lake, a drive of several miles from the
Hampton-Mountain City highway. Brian Paley suggested this hike and he was
the de facto leader, being a near resident and familiar with the
territory. He led us on a walk that went by some old homesteads that are
now reverting to their natural state, leaving foundations, walls and
trenches for us to study and guess at as to what their functions had been.
After we passed the old settlements, we got to some serious climbing, and
after reaching the crest, we had a pleasant walk on a ridge. Then came the
part where we stopped short*:* a STEEP descent. Thinking ahead, Brian had
brought a rope that he strung from the top on down far enough to let us get
down safely, and from there on it was was much easier going.

On our return to the parking area, which I see from an ancient TVA map was
once known as Farthing, we followed Brian to his house, which was just
across the lake from our hike, but a long way around. When we arrived we
found that his wife, Beverly, had vats of steaming chili, salad and garlic
toast waiting for us, with hot cider to drink. It was a delicious meal.
Also, it's a rare Friday-hike lunch that's eaten in such a luxurious
setting.

Another bit of history: the part of Watauga Lake that the Paleys live on
covers up the bed of the railroad that went from Elizabethton to Mountain
City. Just about across from their house was a minor sidetrack named
Eastman, which was where wood for Tennessee Eastman was loaded on the cars.

The hikers were Olin Babb, Lee Bockman, Pat and Ken Buchanan, Jerry Case,
Carol and Dave Dunham, Judith Foster, Bob Harvey, Sally Hoover, Carol Idol,
Patty Jo Nachman, Brian Paley and Susie Seiler. Also with us were Brian's
friends and neighbors Terry Wallace and Ralph Watkins. Another of Brian's
friends who was with us was Reuben Potter, who works for the Forest Service
out of the Unicoi office.

Because of the Thanksgiving holiday I won't be able to lead a hike on the
23rd, but anyone who would like to can get in touch with me and I'll get
out the notice. As for Nov. 30, I don't have anything in mind right now,
so send in your requests.

Bob Harvey
