November 1, 2020 · Sunday

Rains Came Down

11 photographs·345 words

But the floods did not come up !
The retaining basin was less than 1/2 full. No flooding down through walnut
woods.

And the pine needles came down.

We spent quite a bit of time sorting nuts, bolts, screws and washers which
came with the '57.
Glass and trim parts we stored in the shop.

Anyone make a good guess where we were when I took this picture?

Farmer niece Lisa hints around at needing a bit of uncle's help from time
to time. Got a note saying (even after I had hauled off a load of dead
lawnmowers) she had two mowers which she could not get to start and run.
So, off we went on a mission of mercy. Turns out when we arrived she had
one running and all the other needed was a new air cleaner filter element.

Then the fun part. My brother collected stuff. A lot of stuff. His
garage is full as well as some other buildings. Lisa has been sorting and
sometimes picks out things she thinks I could use. She also askes what some
things are. Mostly, I can identify as I had similar tastes as Herb.

This pictured item I could not.

The picture below is the items she had picked out for me.
A delightful trip !

Sunday is a day for fun, family,friends and relaxing. Today. we got all
with one whack.We scheduled "start the '57" for Sunday PM.
People came from miles around to join in. It takes a lot of helpful
suggestions to get a car which has not been run for a long time started.

Here, Wayne is taking his Mom for a spin around the block. I think I am
really going to like this addition to the fleet. Number 23 I think.

We got a number of notes/pictures this week . I appreciate it very much.

Turned the heat pump on for the first time .
