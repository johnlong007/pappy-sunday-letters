January 8, 2012 · Sunday

A new Start

10 photographs·635 words

Stan's SS class has been very helpful to me. It has given me a new ,deeper appreciation of each day and a new resolve to rejoice and be glad in it. I know trauma's will come but they will be dealt with as they occur.One does not come out of a trauma unchanged, The choice is better or worse and I am resolved to come out better, no matter what.. That is my new years resolution. We shall see how it plays out.

A fine start to the new year. We have four tagged and insured vehicles in the drive. For one day, all four were out of comission ! A new record and well above average. An opportunity to rejoice !

The truck brake line to rear wheels had rusted through. I spent all day Monday cleaning the rear drums and debating what to do about the brakes. Finally decided to by-bass the entire rusty line and on Saturday I installed a new line all the way front to back.

It is believed that Charlie has been able to get the shift linkage correctly installed on the Studebaker . However, the new clutch master cylinder and new clutch slavew cylinder had lost all the expensive silicone brake fluid. Although the system has been refilled (without even hunting for the source of the leak), the system has not been bled so still unable to take car to get exhaust system installed. Continue to stay tuned !

For do-gooder work, Dave Dunham brought his truck over. The power window on the passenger side has been inoperable for quite some time. I located a somewhat detailed procedure on line. Turns out the somewhat detailed procedure was only somewhat accurate. At the end of the procedure it was noted that a skilled mechanic should do the job in about one hour. It took the two of us about 6 hours. However, we got it working and did not break or damage any parts of the door.

Dave has been feeling somewhat better so he went out on AT work. About one dozen folks went so Carl divided the group. We all went to Spivey Gap. He left a group there to work and the rest of us drove a forest service road to No Business knob. He had a report from a hiker that the trail near No Business was almost impassable and very dangerous. Here is a picture of the trail section noted, before and after (or during).

Charlie drove the Miata last weekend. Sunday night on his way home from Cookeville, a heater hose blew. His rescue and removal from the interstate is a fine story. Ask him. But, Monday Joe had to go bring the Miata home on the Dolly. First time for it as best I remember.
Got to it Late in the week to replace the blown hose. $3 for 3 feet of 5.8 hose. I still need to change oil and filter in the car before sending it back on the road.

The tree is down ! Dave is also a certified sawyer. I induced him to come help cut the tree with his main assignment to keep me from getting hurt.

Dwight procurred a 26" bar for his big saw . With a new chain, the saw really makes the chips fly. After we got a proper notch in the tree and started to saw, we soon found the core was rotten. A rotten core is a tricky situatin in a tree (or a person). However, we managed and made it fall on planned target.

Sunday, Sanford and I went to Grensboro to retrieve THE CAR. Now, it is here, still stored in the warm, dry ,enclosed trailer, still attached to the truck. Anyone want to make an offer. FOB ?
