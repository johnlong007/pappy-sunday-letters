August 9, 2009 · Sunday

All ok

7 photographs·244 words

At last we got back to do-ggoder work. Pressure washed Sue Tabor's house.

Went by to see Hal but he was not available. Sue sent me a note saying
treatment would start week after next.

We went to L& D . Chased the kids while the parents were off doing what
parents do. I took the valve cover off the tractor engine and found everything
looked good. But, when Dale put it all back together, he found the engine
had seized. To be continued.

Made a run to Erwin to take Anita and retrieve hay and fodder. Is that
do-ggoder, child care or horse care?

W & L left most of their kids here for a couple of days. A fine time was
had by all (well by some) stacking wood and doing other outdoor activities.
Fortunately, Dwight has got the pool switched over to a salt water system so
they were willing to check it out.

Friday night was the boys night out. Went with Dwight for the test run of
Pat's car with the new and improved nitrous oxide system. Pat was looking
to cut time (for 1/8 mile) from about 6 seconds to about 5. It did not
happen. However, after testing and analysis of the available data, it is
believed the gas had water in it which froze in the metering orifice and cut offf
gas supply. The next field trial will be in two weeks. Stay tuned.
