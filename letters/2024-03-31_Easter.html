Easter

5 months today.

I went to early service at CCCC, then we both went to Bltv to hear the long
family musicians live!

Farmer Lisa and daughter Ella procured and delivered a waterpik at my
request. Now, I need to get to family dentist to get teeth cleaned. The
Thrush episode has not been fun.

The truck is back in service.

I do have intentions of doing several more M & R things. Stay tuned.

The first task was responding to Meredith's call to haul a tree. Not quite
what I had pictured.

Hawthorn, it seems to me British hedgerows were Hawthorn,is that correct?.

Spring ! Time to get the mower back in service. I would not come close to
keeping this place in shape without this mower.

WW's stepped in and cleaned up from the fallen tree.l Alan has mostly sawed
the timber.

A & C had us out one night for the best Beast feed yet.

We went on a survey to check the various patches of ramps.

As best I can tell, Maria rode in from Santa Fe so she could visit us.

We have redbuds.

While here, Maria went through some of GM's clothes on the free to good
home shelf.

PBBD is starting periwinkle in the front yard. It is doing well beside the
walk.
