Janet

13 photographs·340 words

Family and other friends came from hither and yon last week to help Janet
celebrate a special day. Her birthday last Saturday.

While we were all assembled, she had wedding event on Sunday.

It was a fine, well planned and well executed event. I had met Mathis
several times before but never really got to know him. I was and am
impressed. All the signs and portends point to a well balanced team.

Does everyone remember the "girl next door"? She is an honorary Buchanan.

​Saturday, while some un-named person was working in the garage to dig out
a car for transport back to Tn., other named persons were busy assembling
flower things for the wedding.

​I think Wayne was taking notes in case he has to advise and consent to
wedding preparation sometime. I would never have guessed some things which
had to be decorated.

​It appears other persons were there taking notes also. Just in case I
suppose.

​What could be more admiring that a mother gazing at the handiwork of her
firstborn child.

​
I know ! a daughter of said child.

​I heard several comments about the Ark of the covenant and consequences of
stumbling. Ark or not I suspect getting struck dead could have occurred.
