December 21, 2008 · Sunday

Ado

7 photographs·377 words

No trail work, no do-gooder work this week. Everything is sort of in limbo
until after the first of the year. I moved the refurbished plows up on the hill
to await their grand unveiling at the spring tractor show. But, since that
was fun and went well, I moved an old mower which is in even rougher shape up
on the front row and started work on refurbishing.

We did get to do a bit of visiting with the halt, lame and afflicted this
week. Hal finished his combined radiation and chemo series. To celebrate, GSH
made an apple pie for him.

He will go back to the Dr. in a couple of weeks (after brain swelling goes
down) for an MRI and to decide what to do next. Later in the week, Pat and Dave
Edwards came by. We went with them and with Hal and Sue to Ridgewoods. Dave
is slowly getting his strength back but still has many concerns with which he
is dealing.

Dave and Carol have season tickets to ETSU basketball. Dave's cousin and
wife also have tickets. One night we were invited to go when the cousin's
tickets were not being used. The first time I have been to a basketball game in a
long, long time. It was a fun evening especially sine we ate out before the
game.

We got a card from friends in the UK. It included a small Union Jack with
the suggestion I could put it in my knick knack cabinet. So, here it is.

As most know, family is nice. Herb donated a Christmas tree and his daughter
delivered to us. I promised GSH that as soon as the tree was properly
decorated at least one present would appear under the tree. As you can see, a
veritable frenzie of decorating ensued.

Speaking of halt, lame and afflicted, Wayne has recovered physically (if not
mentally) sufficient to procure a replacement Motorcycle. We went to claim
the prize Saturday and here he is, his first venture since the accident.

He did make it home OK.

All in all, a lot to be thankful for.
