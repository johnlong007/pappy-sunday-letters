August 14, 2011 · Sunday

High Aspirations

11 photographs·220 words

Monday I met David Qillen at McD's at 5:30 AM. We took his tractor to Carver's Gap, went to ATC office in Asheville to pick up a small gravel hauler, on to rental place outside Asheville for a larger hauler and were back to the gap at 12 noon.
Ran a double shift until Wed. when the job was completed. So, Thursday PM we returned the equipment.

If you want to check the work go to Roan Mountain then walk up to the Round Bald.

Friday I got the log splitter back in operation. With my Jr. helper we split the left over from last year.

Saturday with an older model helper we split the remaining usable part of the big stacks of wood. Unfortunately, dry rot had calimed most of it. I dumped that part in the brush pond.

Having some confidence in the equipment, I moved on to the big maple log. So far, so good.

I heard a report of trouble in River City with the washing machine. Wanting to prove I am equal opportunity employer, I called in my assistant to solve the problem. To be continued.

Last year, the coons ate our grapes. The grapes are getting ripe so I set the live trap. Went up to check today and found something getting the grapes.
