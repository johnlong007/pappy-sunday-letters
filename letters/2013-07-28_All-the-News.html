July 28, 2013 · Sunday

All the News

9 photographs·708 words

While others were cavorting hither and yon, back in River City we were
settling deeper into our ruts.

We got a bit of do-gooder work done for an aged couple from church who are
no longer able to attend. I watch these things and pay more attention than
I did not too many years back. They are about 80 and have no family in the
area but lots of friends. Even though Dave and Carol have moved from our
church to the one downtown (the old folks church) we still get together to
help the widows, orphans and old folks.

AT work was back to the bridge at Dennis Cove. This time I was able to get
David Quillen to go along. He is a very talented general contractor and I
was pretty sure he would have some good suggestions on how to replace the
center section.

As usual for this time of year, I spent a lot of time mowing. Going happily
about my business when the mower started squirting hydraulic fluid. Found a
hose to the power steering had a hole. I could have cut the hose, installed
a short piece of tubing and a couple of clamps and been back in business.
But, deciding to "do it right" I took the hose out . It was a difficult
slow, messy task. Took it to the hose building shop to get a new one.
Figured about $30. It was $69. So much for do it right.
Then I had to put it all back together, even slower, messier and more
difficult.

The picture reminds me, I went back in for another chemo shot in the place
on top of my head. Dr. Sago said it is making progress. I have one more
round and if that does not work may have to amputate.

Another lake outing this week. I hear rumors of selling the ski boat. I am
not in favor. Of course, I hardly ever want to get rid of any on my stuff.

I proved once more that hope is not a good plan. The '37 has been parked
with the battery disconnected for 2-3 weeks. I figured no way it would run
down in that short a time. So, rolled it out and down the drive. Never hit
a lick. GSH towed it back up. Never hit a lick. Checked the battery. 3
volts. Put it on the charger for a couple of hours and tried again. Fired
right up. Still running pretty rough but hope to get my tune up man
involved by and by.

Did I mention that a couple of weeks back I went in to get new glasses. Not
much change in vision but the old specs are badly scratched. Anyway, this
week I went to Costco in Farragut for a free hearing exam with the intent
to procure a hearing aid. My left ear is loaded with ear wax and I must get
it flushed before the testing can be accomplished. So, stay tuned.

However, on the way home, we stopped by to see Sue Tabor. She has about
healed from all the damage incurred in resetting her hip and is back in her
little apartment.
Anyone going through Knoxville with a few minutes to spare should drop in,
it is easy off and on from I-40.

Saturday, Wayne came over with his children.

I learned that Stephen now has a drivers learners permit and is ready to
spring into learning. So, today we took the truck and went to Central.But,
hard to believe, I failed to take my camera ! I will do better next time or
you can just review the ones who preceded him. Next time we will go for the
drink cans.

Does anyone know anyone who would like a quantity of crab apples or
quince(s?) . The trees are loaded.

GSH's Christmas wish card is like the widow's meal box. This time, she made
a dress for Maria.

Hard to believe Maria is off to college tomorrow.
