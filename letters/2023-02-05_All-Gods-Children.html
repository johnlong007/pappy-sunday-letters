February 5, 2023 · Sunday

All God's Children

14 photographs·519 words

As part of the process to determine why my system is not producing adequate
white blood cells, I had an echocardiogram this week. Since the technician
did not panic, I assume it was not too serious. Follow up with Dr. by and
by.

GSH had a setback this week. It appears to be another mini-stroke.She lost
more mobility making it more difficult to take care of routine maintenance.
I appreciate all the helpers ; more every day.

Our one outside spigot quit working. Our local, on-call, handyman came to
make a repair. Easy to see he does not meet minimum requirements to be
called a plumber. He did pronounce it had not frozen but had broken from
fatigue failure.Come to think of it, that may be my problem also.

LLLL came in for the weekend. Always a delight. I hear little from her
brood so I have to try to catch up through her. The mother of A & C's
incredibly cute grandchild also came to visit. Do you wonder what those two
are watching?

Laura and Bob usually come by on Sunday evening. Tonight, Bob installed
non-skid pads on the ramp. Also, Anita showed up to dr. the cat. More
later. I hear little from Laura's children and hear even less through her.

In contrast, when we go to the Long's for dinner and a show we usually get
well caught up with the possible exception of anyone living in a
foreign country.

We continue to go to the track on days which are close to decent.

PBBD helps a lot. Mostly she seems to enjoy it. I hope she does not tire
before we finish a couple of car projects.

A & C come over in the evenings through the week. Alan watches a movie with
his Mom while C spoils the cat and I quiz her about her children and EJ.

The cat was out one evening after dark. I noticed his wound first thing and
Anita, our cat expert, (we need a family vet), took him to the vet for M &
R.

What Catherine does well, among other things.

I was struck by the similar look on these two faces.
Disdain?

A new seal for the 55 axle was obtained and installed in the backing plate
but not in the car. Round 3 in the battle with tires, tubes ans wheels was
fought. Remember, you want to win the war but may well lose some battles
enroute.

Break between SS and church worship service,

I keep forgetting to mention, We sorted stacks of magazines from the 50's
and 60s. Cars, farming, popular science ets. Things of interest. If anyone
wants to dig through and make a few carefully considered selections I would
be glad to accommodate. Perhaps with war stories included. ((When i left
Boeing for GD/FW), the group gave me a Playboy subscription).

Parts sorting continued. Know what these are ? Pearls as it were.
