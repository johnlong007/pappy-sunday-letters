February 28, 2016 · Sunday

Ponder This

9 photographs·310 words

If a tree falls on the strictly agricultural building when no one is
listening will it still make a noise?

One of the GOB's at Saturday morning breakfast informed me that a
person over 65 had no business on a roof. Reckon what he thinks of an over
80 on the roof welding a chain saw? The easy parts are done. Do not know
how I am going to get the trunk down without damaging me and/or the
building. Stay tuned.

On more important news, Herb is not doing well. It does not look good at
all.

That makes everything else even more trivial than usual. But, trivial goes
on.

Wayne came and put an expanding copper freeze plus in the Studebaker block.
He had an apprentice helper along this time. All seemed OK during the test
run. His usual helper was disappointed. So far she has not been allowed in
the newly upholstered interior.

AT work was moved to Saturday and I did not participate. More important
things to do, like helping the Long family celebrate a birthday and
listening to the VonLong family singers. No pictures from me but the
patriarch took several.

I made good progress on the '63. The body repair work is almost complete.
Of course, sanding and smoothing is the big challenge for me. A lot of my
scarcest commodity is requires. Patience. GSH is plugging along on the
front seat upholstery. She had cut the pieces out years ago and piecing
together appears to be a challenge. Like spreading car parts out and coming
back years later.

A thoughtful person gave us 4 little containers of dried beast at
Christmas. We finally got around to making chipped beef gravy (SOS). it was
even better than I remembered.

And a final bonus picture !

Aaron, do you get this report? We think about you.
