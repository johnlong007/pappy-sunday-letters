April 25, 2021 · Sunday

On Getting Old

9 photographs·275 words

I gave up on getting ducks in a row to go see Dr. Allison to get crown back
in place. Went in to see Family dentist. Told him my granddaughter had
worked her way through dental school on my teeth. He said his school used a
lot of dummies. Guess what I said to that.
He inspected her work and was very complementary. Glad I went.

PBBD brought stay at Home health people in to enroll us. I am considering
what ,if anything, to do. Right now what I would really like is to be able
to go to the track for an hour every day.

Work continued on sorting bits and pieces in the shop. Note the washer
collection. Good progress is being made but a long ways to go.

One containers is "ask Wayne what this is".

The walnut trees on one side of the drive shade the pine trees on the other
side. The bottom limbs which get little sun die and fall . We went down the
line breaking off as many as we could reach with the pruning tool.

Back on the car retrieval project. Making slow, painful progress. I have
wheels ready for another try as soon as can work in another trip.

Herb had sawed walnuts in rows in one part of the field. Now there are
seedlings in rows. Lisa was not confident enough to mow between the rows .
Next time it will be her turn.

PBBD restocks our pantry on Saturday. This time she brought fersh
strawberries.
