We are in Kathmandu at a great little hotel in the heart of the crazy
tourist shop district. The hotel has been great and the people
unbelievable. They helped us arrange our hike and will provide guides and
transportation to Chitwan (the hotel doubles as a travel agency). We're
outside waiting for food now, listening to the incessant honking of horns
and motorcycles on the street below. So far it has definitely been the
culture shock I hoped for. Kathmandu is crazy. The roads are pure chaos and
the air pollution is so thick you can instantly feel it. We spent today
organizing things and getting permits: good thing we decided to cut our own
passport pictures, they had scissors and a glue stick at the trekking pass
agency to make your own pass! Tomorrow we leave to begin trekking, so we
won't be checking in for awhile.

Hope all is well at home.

Kenneth
