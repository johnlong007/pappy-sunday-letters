December 16, 2018 · Sunday

Snowbound

9 photographs·347 words

It snowed last Sunday. Activities ground to a halt.

I cleverly parked the car backed up on the ramp entrance with the tractor
behind it. All I had to do was move the car out, back it past ramp entrance
and let the tractor start by drifting across the clean, dry place where car
had been parked. After a lot of button pushing, I found the one which
locked it in AWD and eased down on the throttle. Did not move an inch !
Had to shovel out behind all four wheels.

We got our steps in every day. Walking the first few days. Ran in about 6
inches of snow with my Lands End boots one day. GSH always likes to take
the road less traveled.

One day we met a person carrying a dog. Takes all kind.

I assume all have kept up with the broken deck window saga? Wayne delivered
the second donor / parts car window to the shop and in the interest of
preserving domestic tranquility brought the remainder here. Displaying hope
exceeding wisdom, he tried to bring it up the drive behind the truck. Not
only would it not come, I could not drag it with the tractor. We had to
unload and drag it backwards back down to pond to get out of the way.

All that close supervision wore the supervisors to a nub.

Nothing like TLC to get the day started right,

Saturday with the snow about gone we took a tour to assess damage. A few
broken limbs (trees, not people) but nothing serious.

Almost no work on the car or anything else this week. I did de-clutter two
rooms.

Sunday pretty much spent the day at church with rescheduled Christmas
programs.

The fruit I pictured last week was what we called "a ground apricot". It is
really a passion fruit and upon reading up it appears to make one healthy,
wealthy and wise. besides, butterflies like the blossoms.
