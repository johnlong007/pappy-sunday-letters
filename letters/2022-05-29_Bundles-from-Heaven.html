Bundle(s) from Heaven

10 photographs·197 words

Looks just like her Mom.

Amazing how quickly a baby can become a little person.

An officer and a Gentleman.

Some work on the tractor but no progress this week. Stay tuned.

Some work on the retaining basin. Drained the water to a low level and
scraped the bottom. Again.

Then the rains came down and the pond came up. Again.
You should get PBBD to tell what happened to the drain line.

A &C took the night off.

I am not quite sure what we were celebrating but we had a fine event with
family coming in from miles around.

I think we have found the cause of the cats skinned head. A raccoon is
showing up often under the bird feeder. I set the live trap this evening
and watched him go part way in the back out 4 times. I had baited with
small marshmallows which went through onto the grass below so he could
reach under and get, i think. I will go to plan B tomorrow. May have to
send for Andrew.
