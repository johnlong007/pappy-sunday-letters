January 20, 2014 · Monday

Belated

6 photographs·600 words

I had a birthday this week and my little (older) sister had a birthday this
week.

In my spare indoor time, I worked on the LBT. This time I removed a front
and a rear wheel cylinder. I will have to get all new brake parts and I
like to have the old ones in hand to make sure the replacements are correct.

It is to be seen that quite a bit of body work is going to be required. But
first the brakes, then get it running, then think about body work. At least
that is the plan.

We did AT work Tuesday , it was cold and wet on top of Low Gap. I walked
all the way though to US 91 with Tim and Jim clearing blowdowns and water
bars. 49 water bars and several blowdowns. Not sure what all the other
folks did. I got it about right on dressing for this outing. Kept on of the
chemical hand warmers in each pocket for hiking and put into each glove
while digging. Worked pretty well.

This week was medical week. Seem to be having a lot of those.Routine stop
at Dermatologist to get head checked (doing fine, stitches out this Friday)
and a bunch of "pre cancers" zapped with LN2. Also had to go by lab and get
a blood sample drawn. The high point was Thursday going in to get an ultra
sound run checking for possible hernia. Will hear this week. I can scarce
wait.

Wed. was birthday. Friend from Many years at ECC called and we got together
at Pop's to talk for a couple of hours. She has an adopted child who
graduated from DB last year. He dropped out of ETSU and is back living at
home doing nothing meaningful. I gave her my most encouraging words and (I
hope) helpful advice.

But what I really liked was when she told me she and others think our kids
are "brilliant and successful, and we never had any problems". I made NO
attempt to set her straight.

Wed. evening we skipped of church and had soup beans and cole slaw along
with birthday cake. It was delicious. And, as Aunt G used to say "God will
understand".

Friday was my Lil' Sisters birthday. I decided to wait until Saturday to go
visit in order to give others a chance to go.So, while I was waiting, I put
a clutch master cylinder in the vw with no problem. Then, I put a brake
light in with a great deal of difficulty. &^*$%# German engineers.

Saturday morning, I took the remnants of my birthday cake plus a
supplementary cake made by Anita, went to Dechard to get Linda, Rebecca and
David and zipped on to Union City.

Sis was in better form than her usual fine form. She fed us soup and corn
muffins for supper. Did not get to see Martha or Amy as they had been in
and auto accident earlier in the day. Neither hurt but shaken and stirred.
She thinks the bug is totalled.

Sunday morning was funny. The dog slept with Rebecca and David. Snarled and
snapped Sis when she tried to oust him for morning health walk.

We went to church Sunday morning then back to Dechard Sunday PM.
Monday AM we cut brush. I gave Andrew and short course in safely and
effectively operating a chain saw then hit the road for home.
