September 17, 2017 · Sunday

Broke Down & Stove Up

14 photographs·426 words

Anyone know the story behind that expression I use?

One night I dreamed I had a pity party and no one came.

I did go in to see GP Dr. one day for a routine check-up. He said keep
breathing and come back i six months. GSH has come up lame. A hip pain has
been bothering her for several days. I would say made her extra grouchy but
she might get feedback.

No work on the trailer wiring by me. However, Wayne and his remaining male
helper did show up to get the plug which connects wiring to towing vehicle
wired. Dwight could have done the job blind folded.

I spent a lot of time mowing around the place. But, main project was sawing
and stacking the big maple log I had dragged out last week. It is now sawed
and stacked along with quite a bit of other wood which has not been split.
I am in hope that apple butter day could also turn into wood splitting day.

​
One full day was spent converting apples into juice (think Rocky Top). The
Yellow Jackets loved it.

​

​

​​
As most know, My home growing up was next house down road from
grandparents. In between there was a gully and in there was a big, old
apple tree. A "Jim Ray Sweet ". Herb had a couple of trees grafter and I
have one. The apple has a very unique taste which always brings back
memories. I harvested a few this week if anyone wants a taste. Must
remember to ask Lisa if she had a harvest also.

While out and about, I found various fruit and nuts ready to harvest. Can
everyone identify?

​ My pepper patch is doing very well. But, while I was making a survey I
discovered somewhat to my surprise that some critter will eat hot pepper
plants. I hope Laure has back up peppers.

​For some time I have been laying off to get a load of gravel delivered.
Today, the gravel arrived. Filled ground hog holes down front and built up
where drive had sunk near house. At least one item off list for the week.

​A hiking friend, Bob Harvey came by yesterday, Almost every time anyone
comes I have to go down the line on the GC pictures like a police line-up.
To simplify the process, PBBD made name tags to put with the pictures.​.
Too bad I also have to explaining the out of date ones.

Finally, an award winning cat.
