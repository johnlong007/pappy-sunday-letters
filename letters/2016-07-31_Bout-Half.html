July 31, 2016 · Sunday

Bout Half

11 photographs·470 words

Since I dropped the pills from the quack Dr. my "running" has been
improving. But slowly and with difficulty. We have been going to Central at
noon every other day. Black asphalt and mid-90's. Anyway, Wed. I did lap 4
at 2:08 and lap 8 at 2:09. This week, I am aiming for a 2.00 lap.

Since those BP pills were dropped, I have been checking BP each day. It
continues to be very average.

Niece Cathy has an event this week and I was invited ! I hope and intend to
stay in contact with all Herb's family.

Laura got tired of my complaining about my vision and send me in for an eye
exam. Sure, enough, my forehead is sagging. I go back for more consultation
Tuesday. I guess I might as well get it fixed. He also said I have
cataracts which will need removal by and by.Should I have tat done before I
get new specs?

Work continued on the same ole projects. As you may remember, the hydraulic
cylinders for the '63 top were each leaking. My repair seems to have worked
as I filled the system and raised the top! Diane came over one day and
worked with GSH on the new seat covers. I have ordered new cushions so
there is hope for getting the project moving along.

I hate to admit it but I think a Chem E may have spotted the problem with
the Mack transmission. I was able to procure drawing from Mack. Don Hass
looked at the drawing and looked at the transmission and announced that we
had the bearing on the wrong side of the retaining ridge. Joe has taken the
splined shaft to the machine shop to get the flutes (or is it flytes) built
up and to press the bearing on. Stay tuned.

Last week there was a major downpour of rain at Cross Mountain where our
handicap accessible section of trail is located. The rain washed out the
entrance. So, Thursday the crew worked to repair and improve drainage. We
shall see. Note the ditch filled with newly hand made crushed rock.

GSH has been keeping busy on the home front on her sewing projects in
addition she has been using her Christmas 3X loppers to clip the bottom
limbs from every tree and bush on the place. Need to fire up the crawler
and smash the brush pond.

I got in a couple of days on the FE. The brakes are now almost complete for
hardware but have not been filled and checked out. Stay tuned!

There are a number of family members from whom we have not heard in quite a
while. Perhaps they sailed to close to the edge and fell off or soared to
high and melted their wings.

Who knows?
