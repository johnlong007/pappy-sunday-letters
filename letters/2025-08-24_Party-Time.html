August 24, 2025 · Sunday

Party Time

12 photographs·300 words

Some folks have come as you are parties, we have come if you can and want
to parties.Before and after birthdays !

Alan provided another giant filled to overflowing platter of pulled pork.

Family from far and wide.

Some splashed in the hot spring.

I think we have peaked out for this season. Beyond my expectations.

This event and picture brings a flood of memories. Patricia was always a
good sport . I put her in dead cars and on dead tractors to be dragged up .
down and around. She made faces, gestures but never refused outright. Diane
has learned well.

The plow is ready for display so it was time for the next project. This
A-C is it. Running when parked maybe 15 years ago.

The UVA Dr. asked what I do to stay busy.

As I remember the tractor had a severe shimmy at road speed. Manual says
take a shim out of the steering gear. It took 3 tries but we got it done.
Probably a while before we have a road test.

It is a 1941 model. It still has a crank but also an electric starter but
no lights. Ignition by a magneto is the same as a lawn mower.

No spark so far. Charlie?

Janet made a pick from the cabinet, a parrot from Peru. I also gave a
little sample bottle from a distillery visit in Scotland with my mother.
Jimmy made my mothers sample evaporate

For some time we have seen 2 does and 3 fawns . One evening, no does, 3
fawns, one holding a foreleg folded up.

Our wildlife, and some friends, love em !
