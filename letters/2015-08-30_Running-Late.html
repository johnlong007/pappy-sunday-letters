Running Late

While Benj was here we went to the farm of a friend and cut down a big
maple tree. This week I went back and started to saw it into firewood. It
is going to take another session to finish and will then have to figure out
how to transport it home. It is on the backside of a big hill. Sort of like
out at the hillside.

For AT work, we had 25 students from Wright State for a day. They come each
year and are always top notch. I worked with George and Pat and a student
named Sarah to rebuild about 75 feet of trail.

At home, we had 10T of gravel dumped at entrance to drive.

I did a lot of sanding on the LBT. Getting ready for a coat of primer.
Maybe this week.

Friday we went to see LLLL. It was a good trip. I showed David a trick for
starting the leaf blower.

GSH showed Rebecca hot to make a pie crust.

And I helped LLLL get their log splitter back in operation.

Wayne came over today to help me look over various vehicles.
1. The VW has a new noise. He thinks it is frame noise, maybe a motor mount.
2, Studebaker clutch is not functioning properly. Dwight thinks return
spring may have come off.
3. LBT carburetor accelerator pump is not functioning properly. Maybe
ethanol induced.

Went with Herb on one trip to heart Dr. this week.He sounded very
concerned. Asked several questions and said come back in three months.
