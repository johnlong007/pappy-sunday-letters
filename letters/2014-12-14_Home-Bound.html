December 14, 2014 · Sunday

Home Bound

7 photographs·478 words

My last day at ECC was Dec 27,1997. When I left, I had a plan worked out
for how I intended to spend my time. One thing I had picked was the
creation of a "pictorial family history". My objective was to turn the
three generations in my family and GSH family into real people. The project
has extended in time and scope.
For the first 3 -4 years I was averaging one day/week. I traveled all over
the US talking to relatives and taking pictures.
GSH gave me a family tree maker program for the PC. I entered a lot of data
as I went along but most of the pictures I took and/or copied I parked in a
folder labeled "Ancestors". At the beginning of last week the file
contained over 1,000 pictures.

This week working 2-3 hours in the morning and 2-3 in the evening I have
been clearing out our ancestors. I am moving every picture to the persons
page. It has been a major project.I have not added notes on all my talking
to the individuals pages.I may need help with that. In addition, I have
quite a number of interviews which were taped and typed. Not sure how to
handle those. Stay tuned.

Anyway, anyone who is interested can check progress any time.

In my spare time, I cut AT work to go to Erwin to check on Herb. The
radiation on his face did a lot of damage and dealing with the damage is
causing a lot of time, trouble and pain. He does not yet know what the next
steps in the process will be.

I did not hear from my little homeless sister this week. perhaps she does
not have Internet service under the interstate bridge. She does have a
birthday coming up on Jan. 17. Intend to go visit then if not earlier to
help relocate her before it gets really cold under the bridge.

The other endeavor was sanding on the LBT. I also fitted the drivers side
door and started work on passenger side ft door. I found it had suffered
some damage in an earlier life and am working to repair.

Gsh finished recovering the seats for the LBT. The dog approves. I am not
going to reinstall for some time yet with the sure and certain knowledge
that I would get them dirty . Next she worked on Christmas stockings for
Jake and Paul. Of course, she had to start with a basket of sow's ears. I
figure at $10/hour those would be $500 stockings.

Dogs, cat, turkeys and deer are fine. This year the roosting resident flock
has about 15 turkeys in it. They wander up the hill from the barn toward
the beech trees at sunset. The little dog loves to go make them scatter in
all directions when opportunity arises.
