Remission

7 photographs·172 words

My view for most of the week Person in Black is Willie

But, to prove I am making progress I spread two bags of fertilizer by hand.
I will take another picture in 2 or 3 or 4 weeks when we can see how
evenly i did spreading.

We lost one lens from GSH glasses (found ;ater by Catherine. In the
meantime we procured some Jane Cool glasses. By the way, we made it to the
track everyday.

I lost one of my hearing aides. Dave Edwards took the other and made a trip
to Cosco where he procured a new, no charge, replacement.

We have a supply of Paw Paws but not many customers, Perhaps we need to
raise our prices.

Horn worms foune my tomato plants. There are two in this picture.

Wayne made a bit of progress on the 55. We may make it before the
snowfall.

PBBD has decided to take up metal working.
