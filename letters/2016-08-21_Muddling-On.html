Muddling On

14 photographs·284 words

Seeing the college class rings suddenly appearing, I decided to drag out my
HS class ring. We cannot find it. Last I remember it was on GSH's dresser
along with some other trinkets (her class ring, dragon from Indonesia,
et al).If
anyone has a clue let me know.

Prepare to rejoice ! The fire engine is primed to start ! Temporary gravity
feed fuel system installed, ignition checked out, engine turns just waiting
for right moments. We even have our own Budweiser dog.

Progress on the '63 also,. New carpet in rear and seat, installed. GSH is
working on repairing the rear window seam bead. We have a new rear window
which is to be installed.

Of course, she also gets to be involved with the FE. We were checking out
the electrical and gave up. I have disconnected everything except the
starter.

We have a friend at church who has an indoor pool. We took Wayne out to
look at it Tuesday evening so he could get a target picture in mind.

Trail work was back near the Beauty Spot. I pretty much worked by myself
renovating a section of trail See the bag hanging in tree on left? I worked
from it back . Two before and after pictures.

In the meantime, back at the ranch, the home place could use some M & R.

Today, our youngest son is 1/2 century old. LC in Latin or just L?

Grandchildren and some children teachers are going back to school. Been
there and done that.

PBBD did a training class for care and feeding of Dwight. I am going back
to retake class before going on duty. Anyone want me to take pictures?
