June 12, 2016 · Sunday

Just Rewards

11 photographs·563 words

Since Dave Dunham got miffed and left church we have not been doing our
Monday do-gooder. Then the friends I used to visit on Wed. died so have not
done much visiting the halt, lame and afflicted.

That left AT work as my main do-gooder activity. That is rewarding and fun
getting to meet all the (strange) people hiking the trail Noth bounders,
South bounders, flip floppers and section hikers.

But this week, I had the most rewarding event of all. I went to Temple Hill
elementary school for grades 1-7. My uncle Howard Garland was the principal
at Unicoi elementary on the far end of the county. He lived next door with
his parents and drove to Unicoi each day (In a '49 DeSota). It was decided
, without my input) that I should attend Unicoi for my 8th grade education.
Meanwhile, at Unicoi elementary, Mack Jones was the custodian. His
daughter, Shelby, came to school with him. She arrived early and stayed
late, I arrived early and stayed late. We became good friends. The man she
was to marry later was also in our 8th grade class and we all went through
high school together. Since then, we have all checked in every five years
at the class reunion. A couple of years back, I heard he was ill and
visited him in the hospital. He died.
Recently, I heard Shelby was in long term care at hospital in Erwin. So,
Saturday afternoon we went to see her.They brought her out to the patio
where we talked for hours. We stayed until she got tired of us. She was
highly satisfying to visit. I intend to go back weekly but only for a few
minutes. If anyone knows her, drop by.

Early in the week, I went to Erwin to visit Aunt Flo. I took the Cemeteries
of Unicoi County book with me and went through the Tinker Cemetery names
(The ones I had taken marker pictures last week) so I could get the
connection correct.

For home work, I crawled around under the fire engine and sanded on the
'63. Could not get the drive installed in the FE pump gearbox so I finnaly
pulled the rear housing off. It appears that the drive shaft must go back
to the machine shop. Our knowledge gap is lessened but still remains.

Each front fender on the '63 has required filling, Not rusted but
distorted. I got each in pretty good shape then sprayed with my expensive
paint knowing full well I will have to sand it all off.

AT work was a double header, Thursday the group went to Roan Mtn. Knowing
there will be thousands of people there in the coming weeks. Carl decided
we need to get the trail in top shape. We worked to get the trail in shape
and worked to make people stay on the trail. A cutoff had been established
close to the start up the hill. I worked with three other to block it.

Friday, "we" walked "our" section to cut blowdowns and weeds. It was a fine
outing.

After we got back the willing workers checked the cherry tree. Very few
this year. But, enough to make a pie.

I have heard that after 'THE" wedding our family scattered to the four
winds. I wonder if they have settled back to the ground?
