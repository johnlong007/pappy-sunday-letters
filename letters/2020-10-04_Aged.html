October 4, 2020 · Sunday

Aged

12 photographs·428 words

I got the new hearing aides from Costco. They are really good. But, I am
overwhelmed by noise. They are set just for me and are to work on
automatic. I am supposed to acclimate for 3 weeks to get used to the racket
then go back to see if I want them turned up, down or left as is.

My own personal barber came by to trim my locks. All well and good except
for an unplanned event. Some, are all, remember Kenneth and the air hose? I
got a reminder. The air hose dangles about 4 feet below the reel. I removed
chuck from hose then open the valve at the tank. The berserk hose with a
connector on the end whacked me on the back of my head. Rattled my cage.
No way to take a picture but later I was asked why my head was bloody.

Got the biopsy report on latest excise.squamous, scheduled to see Dr. Sago
Friday for further work.

Two of our Friday hiking friends came by to share in the paw paws. Bob
Harvey and Lee Bockman. Really good to see each.

Way back when, we butchered pigs. One part which was separated we called "
middlings", some called "side meat". I recently learned middlings are now
"pork belly" and are a Select item. On our first trip to Cosco we were
requested by Laura to procure a pork belly. I did.

After some procedures, she and Bob have turned it into quite tasty bacon.
For the trip to Costco this week, she ordered, and I delivered, four pork
bellies.

Working with PBBD as an auto apprentice, we rolled the '37 out and fired it
up. Only ran for a few minutes as I want to check everything over before
going on tour. But, sounded good.
