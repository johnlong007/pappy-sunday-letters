I'll tell you how I did! I got four 1st place, boys and girls won 1st over all. We were going against Colonial Heights, Ketren, North, Sullivan, and Bluff City. One girl who was also doing hurdles had a starter bock, and sprung out of it in a flash. She went over the first hurdle before anyone. But while I was just catching up, she knocked over a hurdle and fell. I took first. Our 3200 has lapped the other teams every time and we did so again. The there was the 800( half mile). In the first meet, a girl from North beat me by 2 seconds,(though I won the next meet.) I was against her again. She took off sprinting at the very beginning. The last half a lap I caught up and we sprinted neck and neck, her screaming the whole time. At the last few yards, I pulled ahead and she stopped. My time was 2:38, over ten seconds faster than my previous time. The other girl laid down and didn't get up. Before the mile relay, our team went down and talked to the girl. She demandingly asked me how much I ran a day, if I worked out, and what sports I did. She had won high jump, plays on the boys football team, and says she runs three miles a day. She is big and muscular, and claims she has never lost that race before. We went on to do the 1600, and placed first. We don't have a meet next week because of TCAP. But the week after that we have one at South. Hurdles will probably start around 4:00 or so.
Maria
