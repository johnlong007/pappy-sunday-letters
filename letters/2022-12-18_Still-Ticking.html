December 18, 2022 · Sunday

Still Ticking

11 photographs·255 words

During one of the wood splitting outings, I stepped on a moving piece,
foot tilted sideways and caused very positive, immediate and sincere pain.

Friday I got in the truck to move some stuff and found I could not operate
the throttle or brake without a recurrence of the same pain level. PBBD
allowed it was time to get foot checked. We did.
No obvious breakes, not yet sure of how much damage.

My lead off hit for the week was a trip to Dr. Sago. No real live ones this
time but she zapped a few on my head (newly peeled by PBBD)

On a happier note, our church friends delivered a care package.

And sent a delegation to sing to us.

We went back after the injury and finished splitting the wood. Next step
(when I can both step and operate the truck) will be sawing some more wood
to split.

Even happier, we now have a Christmas tree. With lights.

Wayne and PBBD took the '37 engine to the machine shop this week.
I started to take the 55 for a few laps in the driveway but the power
steering pump froze, burned the drive belt and disrupted my plans.

If we really need food I know where to find it. An amazing machine which
turns grass into meat.

Best planned and executed trip I have ever taken. December 18,2009
