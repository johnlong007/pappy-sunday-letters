September 1, 2013 · Sunday

Green, Green Grass of Home

9 photographs·479 words

Anyone want to know how it went with the crawler this week?

But there is hope for life after death. Or, at least after a long
hibernation. Tomorrow opens a new day and renewed optimism.

The Tuesday AT crew went to backbone rock where we had the ASU students
recently. Just a bunch of the old and decrepit this week. We went all the
way (about 2 miles) to the upper end to do serious rock work. Would you
believe they made me wear my hard hat !

We got quite a bit done. Short, almost flat places followed by rock steps.
Pretty rewarding work in a perverse fashion.

This year, we raised a few watermelons. Not many but quite tasty.

GSH has developed a green thumb. She went to some sort of gardening seminar
in ealy Spring. They gave a her a tiny tomato plant. We nursed, pampered
and transplanted it. Finally it has ripe tomatoes. Strangs looking ripe
tomatoes.

She also has fine grape crop in one of the driveway bushes. The ones on
grape arbor are now mostly turned into juice. It would appear these are
ripe for plucking.

It has been a fine year for apples. We have several loaded trees. One small
tree out in the side yeard is a Red Delicious which my mother gave us. The
apples are much better than store bought . It appears that the resident
ground hogs who live under the ground hog hotel (smoke house) agree.

Thursday we took Laura and went to visit Linda and various other family
members. As best I could tell, a fine time was had by all.

Some spent Saturday studying, some spent the day watching football games,
and a select few spent the day cutting brush. Would like to include a
picture but it is on my incredible device and I do not have the skill level
to get from there to here.

All our liability insurance came due on August 20th. We dropper the Ferrari
and the Studebaker. Anyone need a car or two? Along the same line, we
delivered the boat to it's owner. He has proclaimed an intent to find it a
new and happy home. It will be missed.

The pontoon boat went out on another moonlight dinner cruise but I missed
it. Maybe next time.

Much discussion about serious topics. Including, but not limited to, what
and where will Thanksgiving occur this year.

Several of out grandchildren actually went to football games yesterday.
One, I hear, marched in a halftime program. Wish I could have been there.

I bet the weather in Alaska is beautiful. Maybe in Wisconsin, who knows.
