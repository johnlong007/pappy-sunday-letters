Free at Last

0 photographs·87 words

I did not believe it possible for a 90 year old to spend a week in a
hospital and come out alive.

When the cancer center folks I should go. I told Diane, "I aint going", I
went

Three things got me through alive.

1. Diane who did not allow me to whimper.

2. Family and friends who kept visiting

3. A HVCH staff that exceeded all my expectations.

Now, I am home, bloody but undaunted.

I have a number of hospital visit pictures available on request.
