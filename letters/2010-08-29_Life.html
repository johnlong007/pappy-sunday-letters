August 29, 2010 · Sunday

Life

6 photographs·398 words

Just when I think I am doing pretty good by being able to go on line, take
digital pictures, download and include in my updates, I find I am the last
one left using email.

Pore ole me.

But a fine week. Some time back the SS teacher was doing "gifts". All the
standard SS questions and answers.

I told him mine are weed eating and tractor driving. So, this week I have
been honing my skills. Glad I did not include chain sawing as my reputation
has taken a hit since then.

My fellow do-gooder, Dave is having a bad year. This week, his youngest
sister died. She had a very difficult and troubled life but still a blow.
Anyway, no church do-gooder this week.

But I did AT work. We were having about 100 ASU students in Saturday so had
to go lay out a relocation for them. I was gone and have not yet seen a
report on how it went actually building the trail.

We are not a bunch of spring chickens!

In sort of do-gooder, George gave me the opportunity to help reattach the
ramp to the floating dock. I figured better to take his picture than try to
get him to take mine. None the carefully engineered lifting rig.

Some might think the jam and jelly inquiry was to aid and abet in
distribution. That was incidental, it was really an opportunity to apply math
skills. Six kids and 7 flavors. Something like 6 factorial

\
A jam and jelly testing session.

Friday, we took Dwight to Seymour and dropped off for a weekend of
dragging. We went on to LLLL's for a weekend of mowing and sawing. Picked Dwight
up on the way home today. If I understand correctly all the hurdles have been
mastered and we are now looking for new challenges/opportunities.

Closer to home, in spite of weed eater still on the injured reserve list,
the push mower came to life so I could finish whipping the walnut woods into
shape.

Although I have no inquires at all, I am sure some are wondering about the
dog with no name.
It is coming along very well learning to be an outdoor dog.GSH is trying
"Jr." I do not care for that much preferring "Dog" I may decide to start
pushing Rowdy" if he does not settle down a bit.
