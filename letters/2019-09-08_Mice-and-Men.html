September 8, 2019 · Sunday

Mice and Men

10 photographs·396 words

In spite of my none to good best efforts, I have not been able to get the
dash lights in the '63 working. Monday, Labor Day, Wayne brought his only
remaining helper over to give a hand. They checked the compression and
found nos 1 & 2 cylinders to be waw low. Maybe a stuck ring.

I think he finished reinstalling instrument panel today. Tomorrow I plan
to start the car and check things out. At the advice of Pat (Brewer) I
added a bit of Seafoam into each cylinder in the faint hope it will "solve"
the smoking problem.

Indoor work continued on the other '63. I finished repair around rear
window and am ready to paint as soon as I can find " CC1". paint.

Niece Lisa has been having a bit of trouble getting her tractor started
along with a tasteful selection of other issues which go along with the
life of a farmer. We went over to gave aid and comfort and in one case a
bit of muscle.

I believe the tractor starting has a reprieve but a new battery purchase is
on the horizon.

While there, I looked over her used vehicle selection to see if there is
anything with which I might be able to help her . I like this one .

Wayne has spent a bit of his Summer doing M & R on the ole home place.
Saturday he put shields over the gutters curing our clogged gutters from
here on.

Speaking of clogged gutters, leaf falling has begun along with leaf
blowing. I dug the leaves from GSH's frog pond. Many small frogs.

Herb had my sickle bar mower for several years, After I brought back, it
has been parked unused. I did not mow the hillside last year expecting the
imminent arrival of a replacement mower person. Still not having seen the
whites of replacement persons eyes, and being offended by the uncontrolled
vegetation, I decided to go mow again. But, this time using the sickle bar
mower.

The PLAN:
Put on tractor Thursday
M & R and transport to hillside Friday
Mow Saturday.

After blood, sweat and bad words (thought, not said). The unit is loaded
and ready for transport tomorrow.
Stay tuned.
