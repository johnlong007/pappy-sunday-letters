July 26, 2015 · Sunday

End of an Era?

10 photographs·426 words

Tonight in church while I should have been pondering the hereafter, I
pondered the LBC. It left to start a new life today with GSH holding back
tears.

It had about 11,000 miles when we purchased in 1997 as I remember. It now
has 281,000. I figured about 10,000 gallons of gasoline. Capital cost of
about 3.5 cents /mile, gas about 5 cents/mile and M & R of about 1.5 so
total operating cost of about 10 cents/mile.Alan has promised to do his
best to make it live forever.

Herb had no adventures at all this week as best I can tell. I am pretty
sure that he much prefers no adventures. I was over a couple of times.
Seems he is getting back up to speed a bit more each time I see him.

GSH and I had a talk with our financial planning person. So far it looks
like our ducks are in a row such that we can join the ranks of those
waiting for God.

A big social week (for us), we had dinner with friends one night, a SS
outing one night and family outing with D & D one evening.

AT work was really fun this week. We built a bridge, at least a long way
toward building a bridge. Some finish work remains. We mined and relocated
two large rocks, one I estimate at 1,500 pounds and one at 1,000 pounds to
use for end supports. Then cut a locust tree into two logs and positioned
atop the rocks.

A lot of progress on the truck this week. Wayne and I took it to Dodge
dealer in JC. They recognized the problem and priced repair at $600 parts
and $300 labor.We went back to get the truck. The mechanic said that by
depressing throttle while cranking it will start as is. Sure enough, it
will. I want to understand what is going on and correct the problem. Stay
tuned.

Just to check it out, we used it to collect the white walnut tree which had
fallen.

Back at the ranch, Wayne got serious about lining the water reservoir wit
fiberglass. I am really optimistic that this is going to be a real
solution, stay tuned.

I took a picture of the stickers on the rear of a van at the SS party. A
couple of not named family members are missing out on a tasteful bumper
sticker opportunity.

Finally, just for Allison.

And for all the Summer campers who worked on the circle over the years
(Cliff et al).
