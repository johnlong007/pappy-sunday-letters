October 31, 2010 · Sunday

A Fun Week

12 photographs·584 words

Do the Summer Camp workers remember the tractor we worked on? It had a flat
tire . I took the shredded tire off the rim a while back and have been
pondering what to do for months. I did not want to spend $400 on tube and tire
for a tractor which is not worth much more than $400. I finally decided
what to do and Monday I executed the plan. I have a parts tractor in the shed
which has good tires but wheel size of 36" rather than 38" needed for the
Case.

The Case is back in the shed, it is lopsided so the other wheel will need
to be installed and painting has been left for willing workers. Clearly a
silk purse of the first order.

I continue to get inquires about the adjustment process for the new dog.

Is a picture worth more than 10,000 words? We went to see D & L Friday
night. Stopped at McD's to get apple pies and a cup of coffee. Knowing the dogs
would expect a snibble, I procures a McBurger for GSH to eat and share
with them. They ate the whole thing. On the way home Saturday night, I stopped
and got one just for them.

I had a 1,000 mile check-up this week along with a physical exam and a
bunch of bodily fluid testing. All the results were in spec. The Dr. had added
and highlighted a note "cholesterol amazing". (I must add that I am know
taking a BP pill and a cholesterol pill). Anyway, to celebrate I procured
Brats, buns potato chips and various trimming and had a called meeting of
available and interested tonight. I failed to take my camera along but Diane
had her (replacement) camera so stay tuned. I can just feel my cholesterol
surging back to my normal.

The place really looks good as a result of all the mowing. In the morning
I sit here in my corner and watch sunshine on the hill above the soccer
field. In order that all can share, I went out early one morning and took some
pictures.

To get the mowing all laid by for the winter, I took the rig out to
reservoir road (Without getting reeled in by the THP, this year) and mowed.
Keeping it mowed sure makes it go better. It only took six mowing hours.


A before, lunch time and after picture. As I was mowing the last part a
flock of turkeys flew over. Must have been 30 or so. They flew over the house,
over me and landed in the woods shown in the picture above.

While at Linda's, I watched David, Rebecca and Andrew play soccer. Adam and
Sarah are being officials (for fun and profit) as well as playing (for fun
only).

Of course, the main project was getting the garden cover crop planted. Not
much of a problem except Linda wanted it smooth enough to mow with the push
mower next spring.

I got back on the Studebaker a bit. As it gets colder I am slower getting
going and out in the morning. So, I installed the trunk latch and the rubber
seal around the trunk latch one morning. More progress this week I hope.

No AT work or do-gooder work this week. We did drop Dwight off in Knoxville
Friday evening to work on the car but I did not see any point in showing a
picture of him leaving with Pat.
