February 28, 2010 · Sunday

close call

7 photographs·253 words

Dave and I worked together to cut a big tree. We thought it through,
planned it out and cut it w/o incident.

later in the week I cut a tree for Anita. I glanced at it, knew which way
it would fall and cut it. instead of falling, the trunk kicked backward 12
feet ,jerking the saw out of my hand faster than I could turn loose of it,
then the tree fell down. A large limb hit my head as the trunk fell.
looking at the site I now see I had overlooked a limb which was caught in
the adjacent tree. that limbed made the tree rotate before falling.

I was working between the stump and the cluster of trees. most fortunate to
be alive with only skinned fingers.

on to other news.

Mark came to look at the car and talk about painting it. the short story
about two months and over 5K. the new plan is to get Pat to do the job.
Probably take it to Seymour. we spent all week filling ,smoothing and sanding.
so, this week I think it really is ready to prime.

critters, mostly deer, 10 were out in the lower garden Rye patch when we
left for church tonight

this week, I was the halt, lame and afflicted needing visitation. We did
have a social event,the annual maintainers dinner. Most embarrassing with my
newly busted hand. I got a patch for over 1,000 hours worked.+


one finger, one hand typing, left at that.
