Just So Stories

Health ,wealth and happiness. I went in to see Dr. Reeves for my belated
PSA check. Another zero. He said it is now very unlikely (below 10%) that I
will have a recurrence and if it does recur it will likely be slow growing
with no treatment prescribed. On a related topic, my daily run (actually
more like a shuffle) has been going well since I have increased my water
intake markedly. I drink at least 24 oz. in the hour before I go run.

I went with Bob to retrieve a load of wood being kiln dried. That kept the
truck tied up most of the week so we did not retrieve ant more firewood.
