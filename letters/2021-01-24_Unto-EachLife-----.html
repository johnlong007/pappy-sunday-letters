January 24, 2021 · Sunday

Unto EachLife ----

9 photographs·352 words

Everyone knows about my G'mother Buchanan who always greeted me with "the
reason I am so broke down and stove up is because I worked so hard and had
so many children', (Died at 107).

I do believe I am beginning to share the sentiment.

This week, the home health care people descended upon us. Young, energetic,
cheerful and I expect competent. One gave me some neck exercises which are
already helping. Another checked out GSH's mobility and allowed they can
help with balance issues.More visits to follow this week. Stay tuned just
in case you make it to old age.

Way back when I decided no car in unless two go out. The '57 came in a
while back and this is the second one primed to go out. Waiting on the
prime mover.

The '37 windshield came in. I have worked a lot trying to repair the frame
to avoid a repeat. PBBD cut a piece of cardboard same size. We are going to
get everything in order before cementing the windshield into the frame.
Since the unit swings in and out, we also have to fit a seal around the
outside of the frame.

A skiff of snow one morning. As usual, GSH did two laps while I did 8.

Did I mention the box of prints Donna had entrusted to us? I was able to
contact Fran and enlist her help in identifying many of the subjects. All
that we have been able to identify have been scanned. Now I want to find
another relative on GSH's side to make another cut at the project.

It has slowly dawned on me that I really enjoy having a fireplace and a
fire. We have enough firewood prepped to last a long time (picked words
carefully). But I get frazzled carrying up the hill. So, plan B.

Today being Sunday, we went for a walk at the park. Not quite sure what PBBD
was describing to Wayne. You can guess I suppose.
