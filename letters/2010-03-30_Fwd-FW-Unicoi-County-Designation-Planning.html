Great news! The second week of May is National Tourism Week and Unicoi County Chamber is interested in partnering with ATC to kick off the week with a trails day. In order to promote the idea of being a tourist in your own community, the chamber would like to host a series of events highlighting trails, hunting/fishing (Tuesday), parks (Wed), rafting, etc...
So please mark your calendars for May 10th because we would like to plan a designation ceremony for unveiling Unicoi County as an official Appalachian Trail Community. We will also be planning some hikes and volunteer opportunities.

Further details will be coming soon, but I wanted to get it on everyone's calendars.

Looking forward to another successful event!
