October 5, 2014 · Sunday

Part 2

6 photographs·273 words

Some may wonder how I am doing cooking in the absence of GSH. Fear not, I
have it under control.

For AT work day we finally got serious about building the bridge. All along
we have building a super highway in to the bridge site in order to be able
to move 20 foot/400 pound timbers (9 of them) to the site. When the big
moving day arrived, a bunch of strapping young forest service workers
showed up to help. You will note how they actually transported the timbers.
The trolleys did not last long. Back to brute force.
My job was to walk the plank out to the next pier and tow the timber across
the span.

Friday we had our 60 year high school class reunion. It was both fun and
depressing. We had about 135 in our class. About 1/3 were at the reunion,
1/3 are dead and 1/3 did not attend but are alive as best we know. For
those who remember Aunt Donna's husband Henry, he is on right edge of the
first picture.

We had to leave before the meal but did get there early and got to talk
with all we cared to engage.At the last reunion, we were all 73, this time
we are all 78. Several of our friends who were still slim, trim and active
last time are no longer that way, Some stooped, some with canes, some
walkers, some not quite all there. Also, 9 have died in the last 5 years.
It makes it come home sharply how fortunate GSH and I are being well aware
that this two shall pass.
