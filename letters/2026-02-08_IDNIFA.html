IDNIFA

11 photographs·200 words

Not quite the same ol Monday

Before starting me on the poison pill, they took baseline data. All the way
from IQ to shoe size

High price, do not touch the pill. 10:15 on Monday and the same time every
day from here to eternity.

Then the blood.

In the real world, the Olympics have started. We have a trainee.

Either restock the firewood or burn the furniture.

A few things stand out as satisfying to me. Getting to work with children
or grandchildren. Family members getting along with one another and cousins
liking and helping each other top the list.

R & R with family ain't bad either.

It was mostly cold, so we mostly worked in the shop. I had done a pretty
good job on mechanical work on the '37, but a really poor job on body work.

One of my SS class members had a 90th BD event at church. Blue dress. I
suggested we do something scandalous enough to make the news but not enough
to get our tickets to heaven canceled.
