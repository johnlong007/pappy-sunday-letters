I arrived at Chung-Ang University yesterday at 11.
I've been assigned a "buddy" through the school whose job is to help
me get around, he couldn't meet but that was alright I just met up
with Jae.
Seeing Jae was really nice, he helped me get into the dorm, set up my
bank account and many other things. We got lunch with another guy
who was at Tech last semester (Byungjoo) it was really nice. Coming
here and knowing people has been extremly extremly nice. Everyone
has been so helpful and a friendly face is the best thing in the world to see.

Jae is studying for his architecure license exam, mother knows what
I'm talking about. It seems like memorizing lots of building codes.
So anyway he left around 3 and I just unpacked in my room.

Around 6 my roommate came in, he is a sophomore form University of
Kentucky and is actually half Korean! His mom is Korean, so he was met
at the airport by a couple of his uncles. I'm
quite jealous of his Korean, he's pretty dang good. I think he can
help me a lot, as he's way easier to understand than the natives.
He dressess and acts a lot like a high school prep. He's 19 and
extremly excited about the 18 year old driking age here.
He's a really nice guy and I like him quite a bit. He wanted to talk
for a long time last night so we got to know each other pretty well.

Today, I met Jae again for lunch and then went to orientation and met my buddy.
He's quite a talker but really nice and super helpfull. he spent
3rd-5th grade in India, his english is quite quite good. His major is
international buisness.
Orientation was about as expected I guess.

After that we hung out for quite a while and got some things done.

Around 5:30 me and him and one of his friends (a girl in his major)
went out to eat.
We had pork and veggies stired up in a spicy sauce and "pajon" flat
eggie dough with onions and seafood. (one of my favorites).

We sat, talked, ate, and drank makoli (rice wine, most of you that
tried it didn't like it) for almost 3 hours.
It was really nice.

Tomorrow... Class!
