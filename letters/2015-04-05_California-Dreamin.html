April 5, 2015 · Sunday

California Dreamin'

7 photographs·110 words

In case you were wondering I did not post notes last week . We were in
California.

It was a fine trip. I had been wanting to visit GSH's neice Mari for quite
some time .

She lives in Pasadena with a house as far back as houses are allowed on the
side of the mountains.

Stayed there one night then a couple of hundred miles up the coast to
Eric's Dad's house, then a couple hundred more up the Big Sur to Monteray.

Back to Mari's to watch them drive their race car at the old El Toro
airbase.

Huntington estate, church at Church of the Valley then home.
