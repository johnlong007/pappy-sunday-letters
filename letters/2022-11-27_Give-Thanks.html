November 27, 2022 · Sunday

Give Thanks

13 photographs·351 words

For family and friends.

Lots of gmail and Aura frame pictures this week. Thanks, we really do enjoy
and appreciate .

It all came back. I think it was 1957 when GSH baked a Spam for our
Thanksgiving dinner.

I mentioned being distraught at my lack of stamina. Read a long article
about exercise. It was touting several outings/day to elevate pulse rate.
So, that fits right in with wood splitting. Using Aunt G's rationale that
God will understand, we went down and split wood for about 30 minutes today.
Then since it was such a pretty,warm day we headed to the track for a few
slow laps.

Earlier in the week, Gwendolyn, who came from Portland for the event,
Brought her fan club to come watch her form splitting wood.

Her little brother decided he should join in. I fed him some tough ones
which he breezed right through.

Not to be outdone.

We got offers for Thanksgiving dinner but decided we would stay home with
each other. While waiting we went down to the woodpile to raise the pulse
rate. While there, meals on wheels showed up with dinner for two.
Fortunately, the drive was blocked and they were held to pay toll.

After a decent interval, we came to the house, ate a fine dinner of select
leftovers followed by pecan and pumpkin pies.

Friday, PBBD put on a full multi course, multi generation official day
after dinner. I took lots of pictures.

Then back to work. The '63 runs good and looks good. We have the
brakes assembled in the 55 but not properly bled and adjusted so I
refrained from another brake picture. Stay tuned.

High skilled low cost expertise showed up to start serious disassembly of
the 37 engine.

Then, for the first time in months, Alan fired up the sawmill. Catherine
demonstrated she could start and operate the Bobcat after it had been idle
the same time period.
