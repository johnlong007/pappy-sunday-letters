January 18, 2015 · Sunday

Older

11 photographs·598 words

January is a big month for birthdays in this branch of the family. This
year, Instead of three cakes like last year I got part of one cake.
Delivered the part remaining to my Lil' sister who, I am happy to report,
is no longer homeless.

First things first, Herb went in to have multiple biopsies from the wound
on his face. No results passed along yet. Stay tuned. Also took Collins to
JC for results of his biopsies. More work to be done.

Benj came over and helped with the LBT project . Some may remember that the
passenger vent window latch was broken for many years before we retired the
vehicle. I worked over 12 hours trying to replace the window with one from
the parts vehicle. Could not get it to work for reasons I will be glad to
explain to any interested party. Finally gave up. We found the latch in the
LBT parts pile and Benj reassemble it. I think if no high force is applied
it it will work ok.

Benj and I spent a fair amount of time trying to get a door release latch
properly installed. Wayne rode by on his white horse and slipped it right
into place.

I went off to do AT work Tuesday, back to the same place doing about the
same kind of work. Busting rocks. I did not take the camera as it stayed
here to record the exciting events going on at the ole home place. Septic
system renovation !

The diggers started at the tank where the line was uncovered, followed it
across drive, down over the hill and to the place where it was attached to
the field lines.
Sure enough, it was blocked at first field line, so , they switched over to
the second line. I suppose in another 25 or 30 years it will have to be
switched to third line unless the tank is kept properly pumped in the
future. You may note that the drain field connection was way below the
current ground level.

Friday GSH and I took the truck, went through Cookeville to pick up Adam
and on to Union City to help move Lil' Sister and her household belongings
into her new abode.

Adam was a fine addition to the team. He is fun, funny and helpful. In
addition to moving a bunch of stuff, he got Sis's PC all set up and in
operation.

The move was much smoother and quicker than I expected. The "Townhouse" she
moved into is only a few blocks away so we shuttled easily without have to
pack or wrap carefully. Martha and her friends showed up with skill and
equipment. Everything was in place by early afternoon so we headed for home.

On the way down, i noticed that the truck was not performing well and
suspected a fuel filter problem. However under the heading of hope spring
eternal, I did not take any corrective action believing (or as it turned
out wishfully thinking) we would make it home OK.

We did not. On the good side, a Saturday night rollback ride is not nearly
as expensive in Harriman as in Chicago. Also, GSH has been wishing for a
restful night out. Once more, Wayne to the rescue.Found us, fixed the
problem and got us on the road by checkout time at the motel.

While we were gone, John took care of the animals under the watchful eye of
his mother I am told. They seemed glad for us to be home.

Raise up your children---------"
