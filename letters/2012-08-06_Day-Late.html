August 6, 2012 · Monday

Day Late

7 photographs·257 words

Travelers in, travelers out.

My little sister was here for the wedding. Monday we ventured to Erwin to
check on the goats, Kaylee, Aunt Flo and Herb. All appear to be doing
well.Then Tuesday she was returned ,COD, to Cookeville to meet up with
Martha.

At the other end of the week, it was off to Atlanta as Johnny came marching
home. A sort of fun trip as it gave me a chance to catch up on his life in
the field.

We had a downpour. Most rain in shortest time since living here.

Ignoring the biblical dictum to "take no thought of the morrow", serious
wood splitting was undertaken. This week, , if my willing worker(s) show
up, wood splitting is to continue. In addition , the big log is going to
become firewood. I hate that but such is life. I may cut one section off so
Anita can make a bowl or perhaps a bathtub.

Car report, well, Charlie brought the Studebaker home before he skipped
town for greener pastures. I have not looked in the strictly agricultural
building to see if he stored his little car. The last word I had on THE CAR
was that Joe may well have headed for the border driving it.

The case tractor is now painted. It needs a bit of reassembly then it will
be time to attempt to get it running. I have not yet decided if I will
attempt that or just put it back in storage as is. One more almost
completed project.
