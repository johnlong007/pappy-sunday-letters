December 21, 2014 · Sunday

Happy Solstice !

10 photographs·404 words

On the health, wealth and happiness front, Benj and Meredith, Gwendolyn,
Rachel and Janet, John and Maria, Adam and Sarah are all home for the
holidays.
Others are beginning to filter in from hither and yon.

I suppose everyone knows by now that Beth and Jake continue to both
multiply and divide.All well on that account I am told by a highly biased
person of interest.

Herb continues to gain input from various sources with various opinions. It
now appears that we will be dropping on on the experts at Vanderbilt as
soon as appointments can be scheduled. Stay tuned.

Little sister finally reported in. We offered to go help her move in before
the end of this month but she has decided to go see the bright lights of
the big city first so it seems moving day will be quite close to her
birthday. Many members of her fan club may wish to participate. Not
everyone gets to the milestone she is approaching . Even fewer arrive with
most of their marbles.

On the home front I almost always get inquires about the various members of
the animal kingdom. So, I will start with a picture of GSH and her willing
worker followed by my helper.

As you may have deduced, it was time to move another load of wood up closer
to the house. Sure enough, three weeks have gone by and GSH had got over
the back pain if not the neck. But, to be careful, this time she got to
work on the upper side of the woodpile.

For AT work we were back at the beauty Spot where the temp was below
freezing all day Since trail digging looks about the same every week, I
will include the view from lunch spot instead.

In even more exciting news, Joe went with me to the Long estate to harvest
a fine, fatty Christmas tree..

Speaking (or writing) of Christmas the Community Band did a concert at KPT
mall Saturday morning. It was good, even better than usual. I understand
they brought in at least one ringer. See if you can pick him out.

I spent a lot of time trying to get the passenger door on the LBT to fit
more or less properly. About ready to declare victory and retreat.

I also continued to scan pictures into the PC. Sort of interesting to watch
my/our lives flash by.
