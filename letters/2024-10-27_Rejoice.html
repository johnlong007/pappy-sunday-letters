October 27, 2024 · Sunday

Rejoice !

11 photographs·263 words

After 3 months living life in the fast lane, The snake came home dragging
his tail behind him. As I understand charlotte rejoiced and fed him a
fatted rat !

In other exciting news, David and Kylie embarked on their adventure. In
my Pep talk ,I told them to exceed our 68 year record. When she said that
sounds like a long time I assured her when she looks back she will be
astounded at how the time flew by. My mother would say "days go slow, years
go fast".

Does anyone besides me remember Kenneth sitting on the cabinet on popcorn
night at Summer camp ? He liked to put the "buttery" stuff on the popcorn.

https://cms.cern/news/working-w-mass-no-sprint-it-marathon

Meanwhile back here in River City, PBBD has mastered concrete mixing and
finishing ! After 4 years of struggling one step at a time, the drain line
is repaired . Period.

The wedding event brought willing workers in from far and wide. Adam has
transitioned from boy toys to big boy toys without missing a beat.

Adam can also split wood.

Not to be outdone, much to my surprise, EE's can aslo split wood.

Alan is working to help with my low red blood counts. In a most delightful
way.

A & C have worked for weeks getting the crawler ready for active duty. Best
I can tell it is close and they are pleased.

Thanksgiving dinner on the hoof.

I voted.
