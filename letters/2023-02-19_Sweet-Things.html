February 19, 2023 · Sunday

Sweet Things

13 photographs·350 words

Charlie brought their new addition to the family back to Blountville for
inspection. It was good to get caught up. John and Savannah showed up so I
also got caught up on life in the Huntsville fast lane.

I always have goals for things to do, get done, day,week,year
One for this week was get the car washed.. I could not persuade PBBD to run
it through a car wash place.

Another was to harvest the large white pine which fell on Albert's
property. I promised him I would take care of it.

Fortunately our local sawmill owner/o[perator wanted the timber. The piece
in work here produced several nice 4 by 4's.

The tree had forked at ground level. Albert said I could have the other
trunk. TBD. It was a bit tricky getting the trunk cleared with no damage or
injury.

Remember the 55 generator in the shop? Turns out there was no issue with
the generator. No charge at the shop. But, the local mechanic set up to
test the PS pump and found it to freeze up upon spinning. So, always
something.
Fortunately I have a spare. Mix and match is the next thing to try.

I watched a you tube video of an overhaul of one of these Eaton pumps.
First I want to try getting the spare in operation before tearing into this
one. Perhaps I can persuade Wayne to have a go .

After many fits and starts, we have 2 tires mounted and seemingly holding
pressure. Three more on standby at the ready.

Another project, the retaining basin is intended to catch heavy rainfall to
prevent erosion. To keep the retained water from killing our carefully
cultivated grass, we are getting set up to bleed the retained water as fast
as possible. Toward that end, a second, larger, drain line is now
operational. Let the rains come down!

Just around the corner !

Know what these are?
