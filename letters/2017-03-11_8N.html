It was headed in to shop and would not crank

Tried to rock loose, failed

Pushed it out and got it loose.

Cranks but did not start.

Rolled down drive hill, hit a few licks then nothing.

Spark at points, spark at plugs, fuel runs out when carb drain opened.

Brushed soot from plugs. Soot but not wet or oily.

Never hit a lick.

Then started locking rear wheels when trying to drift start in 4th. But
would still crank with starter.

Left it parked by drive about 1/2 way down hill and came home.

Puzzled. One, two three separate problems?
