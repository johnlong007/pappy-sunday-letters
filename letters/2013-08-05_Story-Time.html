August 5, 2013 · Monday

Story Time

11 photographs·390 words

I admire people who can tell stories. Aunt G could and my sister can. Diane
can write stories and I have overheard the girls telling about their Mom.
Never about me, nothing funny there.

last week, GSH and I went through Dechard to pick up Rebecca then on to
Union City to pick up my Sister whom all know and love from Summer Camp and
for other reasons. The four of us then drove to Wisconsin to visit the
extended family members domiciled there.

When I am driving I want my navigator (singular) to tell me the next turn,
how far and which direction to turn. I do not want it stated as a multiple
choice and as part of a interactive discussion. I am now convinced that the
probability of a missed turn is something like the total number of choices
to be made raised to the power of the number of contributors. In our case,
three.

GSH had an out of date GPS unit which neither she nor Rebecca knew how to
use leading to much loud commentary around possible things to try. My
sister (who could well play the role of Rumpol's wife) had an out of date
road Atlas. I am sure we made more wrong turns on this trip than I had made
in the previous 58 years of travel with GSH.

Going up we missed a turn near Bloomington and ended up going through
Chicago during five PM traffic and on city streets.
Coming back, we missed a turn near Bloomington and ended going through St.
Louis (at least Rebecca got to see the arch).

Going up had about 70 miles extra, coming back about 120.

But, we got there, we got home and I had a fine time.

Trail work was water bar clearing , weed mowing, stinging weeds and poison
Ivy. We saw a lot of places where bears had been feeding. They dig apart
rotten logs and turn rocks over.

The skiers were back in action one day. This time Allison joined the outing.

Another time we went out on the lake in the pontoon boat. I think it is
really going to be fun for picnics. It would even work for overnight trips.

I think I shall just attach a selections of pictures. Captions upon
request. Or, add your own.
