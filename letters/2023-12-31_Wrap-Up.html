Wrap Up

7 photographs·290 words

I have been pondering Christmas past presents. Two stand out as really
surprising me. The first in 1957, when the end was insight at UT. I worked
in the UT clinic, one of the Dr.s talked about the car he had traded in for
a new Olds. Patrice, unknown to me, borrowed money from my parents. Bought
the car, stored it with one of her workmates who delivered it outside our
door early Christmas morning. Surprised is an understatement.

All will recognize the second surprise. No loan this time . However the
folks from whom she bought it brought it out of her hiding place and parked
it outside our door ealy Christam. They gave me a 15 minute training
session ,now, some over at East Tn Rent All still remember it.

Family members and allies came in from far, far away to join in for an
after Christmas event. Round 2. This person came with David. The two went
out for a self defense training session.. I was highly impressed.

Seems like more tiny ones than usual. Really fun events. I sorta tried to
stay out of most commotion and use a lot of hand cleaner. So far, no sign
of having caught a cold.

Rumor has it that there will be even more tiny ones at the next event.

After the ball is over. We are left with peace, quiet, and our pampered
pets.

This picture is for your brain twister. Those who have access to one of the
6 children. ADALLW, ask what they can remember about all the stories
covered in this one picture.

It ended with Patrice reading Bartleby The Scrivener.
