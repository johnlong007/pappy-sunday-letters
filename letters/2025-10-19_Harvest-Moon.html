October 19, 2025 · Sunday

Harvest Moon

17 photographs·411 words

Before the apple butter event last week I procured apples at Jarrets in
Burbank. None left now. I checked Friday and found Va. Beauty apples are
now coming in. I plan to go over this Friday and procure one bushel. Does
anyone want a bushel of their own? $26.

Walnut harvesting continued. Lots of walnuts, in current vernacular, tons
of walnuts.

Hull, wash, spread to dry.

Hang in the shop to cure.

Now we have over 100 gallons in process. There are a lot on the ground
(tons?) free to good home. Pick your own.

While the sawmill was in operation the slabs were stacked on logs in the
brush pond, covered and left to dry. The apple butter process used some of
our split slabs which we need for the fireplace. I captured a willing
worker and got the remaining slabs sawed to fireplace length.

The stock has now been replenished and enough left to stack in inventory
when the signs are right.

In the search for process improvement, Diane is working on a different
method for harvesting walnut kernels intact.

In my efforts to teach her everything she needs to know to keep this place
running, she did a cleaning job on the A_C tractor carburetor.

Carb is installed and ready for a test run.

Friends from the past show up from time to time. This picture is Jim
Foster, several know him. while we were on a hiking camping trip on "the
old settlers" trail in the smokies He asked me " do you tease her all the
time?"

WWs came to tackle the Hickory wood splitting. Gwendolyn will be pleased to
know they saved some for her.

Fiddler on the roof getting ready for predicted rains.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jm5ZV1zetA0 Wayne sons listen up.

My father installed coal burning furnaces . He did not like cleaning heavy
soot deposits. During my high school years, I got the task.

We are settled into a grove, Bob and Laura dinner and show Sunday night.
Joe and Anita dinner and Long family show Tuesday. A & C Friday Feast. All
under the making hay heading.

Next to soup beans, salt cured ham with biscuit and eggs must be my
favorite food. I missed both while living away from here.
