January 6, 2013 · Sunday

Looking Fore and Aft

4 photographs·387 words

That is for Jake.

The big news for this year is from Alaska and Norfolk. GSH and I know
nothing about being GGPs. We will need helpful suggestions from
involved and/or interested parties.

Each year on Jan 1, I record mileage on all our cars and hours on
tractors. I can provide data going back a long way upon request. It is
apparent that we are slowing down. Our driving mileage was running
50-60 thousand/year. It dropped sharply when fuel prices started up
and continued to drop last year. Only 32,950 on the fleet here. Of
course, that does not count miles I put on other vehicles.

I have taken a good look at my original 10 year plan and the follow up
(just completed) five year addition. Probably about 85% completion on
the first and 75% on the second. I am giving up on a couple of items
which were on both and adding several new ones. No chance of running
out of things to do and places to go. Time and energy sooner or later.

GSH is a Luddite. She has had serious go a-rounds with ECU on line
banking and with Eastman's medical $ assistance plan. It is safe to
assume that when GSH is unhappy everyone is unhappy. No pictures of
any of that!

Jan. is birthday month for most of the important members of the
family. We have been helping with celebrations but leaving the picture
taking to the owner/operators. However, the parties are not yet over
so stay tuned.

I made essentially no progress on the '37 this week. But, Wayne worked
on the stately sedan which had appeared in the shop and took it
elsewhere. Also, the Miata is now supplied with a fine new battery
which I expect will last a long time.

As some may have noticed we were not able to get our act together to
have Summer Camp in 2012. One result was PJ's incinerator did not get
cleaned and renovated. But, after having been highly motivated by GSH
(and giving up on willing workers appearing on the horizon), I did
the job alone.

Perhaps even more exciting, I completed reading two new, Christmas
present, books.

No AT work this week. I decided it was too cold for my hand when the
crew went out Saturday.
