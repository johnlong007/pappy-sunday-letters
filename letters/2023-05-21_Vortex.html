May 21, 2023 · Sunday

Vortex

14 photographs·392 words

Most of the family know that for me working outdoors with a child,
grandchild or even with GSH is my notion of about as good as life gets.
This week, I had a fine time watching PBBD disassemble the next engine
block for the '37.
As best I remember this engine came from a WW2 military Dodge PU which I
bought at auction a good many years ago, We still have one more engine in
storage. The engine had not run in at least 60 years and was/is stuck tight.

The rule is. don't force it, get a longer pry bar. I think they are
extracting the valves.

Later in the week, rod bearings and piston removal.

Saturday, crank, bellhousing, and flywheel came out via the backdoor so to
speak. 2 valves, 2 pistons, camshaft and oil pump remain. Stay tuned.

I sleep on the side away from the bathroom being more mobile than GSH. The
distance from her knees to the edge of the bed was the space she left for
me.

Tuesday night Anita, Joe and Katy provided dinner and a show. I have a
project underway which will require adding text to some pictures I have
scanned. I can do that on Gmail but want to be able to just do it as a
document. I snabbled Katy who then enlisted helpers but no success by
supper time. Stay tuned.

After last week's diagnosis, PBBD was able to get an appointment with a
hematologist at the Oncology clinic in Norton. Looks like all the simple
answers are unlikely and further drilling is required.

I think the helper Diane located and brought in on a mutual trial basis is
fitting well.

We like to sit outside and watch the birds. I saw this one taking a bath
and took a picture without asking permission.

The woodpecker watched the scene and decided to think it over.

The three tomato plants which we gave so much TLC are not looking very
healthy. Fortunately (perhaps) Laura brought her excess which I planted
with no TLC at all.

The coon is ahead 2 - 0 on stealing bait and springing trap.

Everyone is doing their thing.
