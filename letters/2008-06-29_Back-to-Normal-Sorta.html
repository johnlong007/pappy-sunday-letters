June 29, 2008 · Sunday

Back to Normal (Sorta)

9 photographs·375 words

I really, really like the long days in June. But, I know we have past the
1/2 way mark and now the days will start getting shorter.
My first cousin Doral died. He was one of Hubert's kids. Hubert did not rise
up the socio-ecomomic ladder as far as Daddy and all the other family.
Hubert had a bunch of kids. Some of them appear to have climbed the ladder pretty
well.. Doral did not make it very high.
I went to his wake/receiving and met a number of his children whom I had
not seen in years and a number of his grandchildren. I collected quite a bit of
information to enter into the family tree record and have been promised more.
Stay tuned.

I used the new vehicle to take Sis back 1/2 way home. Laura also drove it to
go visit Allison and Linda this weekend. I will have a report on fuel
consumption pretty soon.

A friend asked me to help with his section of the AT. I took Benjamin along.
He got to mow weeds and clear sediment from water bars. I got to carry a
chainsaw and cut blowdowns.After we got home, he produced one of the best pies
yet.

When I made my run to Erwin, I took pictures of both Aunt G and Herbert's
goats (to replace the ones I erased along with Benj's first pie). All looked
about like the originals to me.

On the home front, I did get the flat on the Farmall H repaired, plowed the
garden and tied up my tomatoes. I estimate two more weeks before I have ripe
tomatoes.

I wrangled and invitation for lunch from D & D. Then, Sunday evening, I went
to a block party with Hal and Sue Ketron and other long time friends. It
slid right in between rain showers. Pat got back in time for dessert which I
suppose might not have been and accident. I have more than made up for little
food while cook was gone.

Tomorrow, the cycle starts over with meeting Dave at 7:30 at McD's before
heading out for do-gooder work.

Life in the fast lane.
