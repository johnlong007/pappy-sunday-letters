April 21, 2013 · Sunday

Humdrum-Continued

10 photographs·428 words

Pretty much like last week except I went to the skin Dr. I had a live one
on my arm and a recurring one on top of my head. She carved the one out of
my arm but had to drill and shoot the one on top of my head. It has been a
week almost and I can still feel my pulse.

Oh, I just remember, we did something else different, we went to the
handgun carry training class. We went to the class in Erwin with a young
lady instructor, a former student of Herb's and a former military person.
She was very good. She made the classroom part interesting and the range
part safe. We all passed.

I started sanding on the '37. I want to get it in shape to apply a coat of
primer ASAP. A long time back I had coated the bed interior with body
filler. Rust stain was showing through the filler in several places mainly
around the seams. So, I had to grind it out.

Also had to put the VW front spindles back in and repair a steering tie rod
end on the mower. Nothing exciting there
.
For AT work, I went with Jim Foster to clear his section and renew all the
paint blazes. We met a bunch of through hikers. One "Beacon" was very
interested and got very enthused when we offered to let him paint a blaze.
The section has a lot or ramps. Acres and acres. I dug enough for potatoes
and ramps for supper. They are still small but looks like a good year.

The community band had a concert at Baysmont. I went along as usual. I
worked with a number of the folks and even more of the husbands of widows
there. If you look at the back row in the picture, you can spot the top of
Joe's and GSH's heads.
It was a good concert.

My uncle JIm (died last Sept.) was in the Philippines throughout WW2. one
of the men with whom he escaped into the jungle was Kenneth Bayley. Mr.
Bayley came to visit Jim after in war in maybe 47 or 48. I met him and
asked for his autograph in a book I had. He laughed, signed and said the
only time he had ever been asked for an autograph. I wish I still had it
but no idea what happened to the book.
I have copied a couple of pages out of "They Fought Alone" and am attaching
in case anyone is interested.
