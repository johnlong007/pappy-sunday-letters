Some previous wood splitters left without finishing the Hickory. Since it
is dry and clean burning we brought it up to get ready for the fireplace

When Wayne needs to unwind from a day at DB, splitting Hickory will do the

Elm. Notice the wedges in place.

I got some mowing done and we also used the mower to drag bundles of brush
to the brush pond.

Looks like a bumper harvest year. But, no apples, zero.

has expanded to need more home sites. Wayne and Diane filled the entrance
