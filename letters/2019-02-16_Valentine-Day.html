February 16, 2019 · Saturday

Valentine Day ?

7 photographs·201 words

Different strokes for different folks.

No AT work. However, the big social event of the season was the maintainers
banquet Friday evening. We are signed up for the same section as before. I
will be looking for willing workers as before.

No work on the '63. Quite a bit on the sewing machine.

The dog is doing fine. He went to vet. Monday to be tutored. Barking
soprano now. Events do not seem to have reduced his energy level at all.
He sleeps in a cage, also he stays in it when we go out for any time. It is
metal. In hopes that we can do some traveling and take him along we
procured a fabric cage. Hope to get a chance to try out on an overnight
trip pretty soon. We are going back to Memphis tomorrow but Allison
rejected him.

He and the cat are adjusting very well.

Weather moderated a bit so we got our track time in OK . Number 1 daughter
comes over to check on and make sure we are taking our pills. Sometimes GSH
take a bit of persuading.
