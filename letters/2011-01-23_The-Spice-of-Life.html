January 23, 2011 · Sunday

The Spice of Life

9 photographs·388 words

My buddy do-gooder Dave Dunham has moved his church membership to Boone's
Creek. But, I have promised him that if he can get a "helping hands" program
going I will continue to participate. So, we are scheduled to go back to
work tomorrow. Stay tuned.

In the meantime, he came over here a couple of days this week to help on
the car project.

Monday was a school holiday so, a couple of willing workers showed up to
help split wood. Notice the new and improved splitter. This time I plan to
split all the easy stuff (when we get a warm day) before really putting it to
the test.

As one might guess looking at the snow and the clothing, it has been cold
this week. So, I worked on the silk purse most all week. The quarter windows
are more complex that the door and vent windows. In addition, they require
a weather seal box which GSH rebuilt. We finally got the window in with
only a bit of paint damage. But, it is only installed loosely and will
require proper adjustment. It has several adjustments so it will be complicated.
To be continued.

The other day, I was looking out and saw the Miata out front. The tires are
almost slick and we have more cars than drivers so there it sets. Sort of
sad. Has not even had the hard top installed. No one loves it anymore.

This week I had a voice form the past. Many of the family have met Syble in
the distant past. I worked with her for years. After she left ECC, she
hired on at Delta and has been a stewardess for the last 10 years. She calls
every few years on my birthday and we go out for lunch. It was fun, she
caught me up on all the folks she has kept up with and I did the same for the
ones I know about.

Linda called to let us know someone had taken in a lost dog and she had
assured them we would take it if the owner could not be located. We drove down
and picked it up on Friday.

We are open to suggestions (at least I am) for a new name. I am a believer
that pet names should be descriptive.
