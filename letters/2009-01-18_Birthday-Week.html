January 18, 2009 · Sunday

Birthday Week

5 photographs·330 words

It has been cold here most of the week. Cold enough to make me delay the
birthday bash to Indy. Fortunately, I have a good indoor project going with work
on the crawler (Cletrac) tractor.
Merton Dibble had a blood clot so Lisa was on number 1 daughter duty, Wayne
was at school so we had the kids a good part of a couple of days. It worked
out well for me Gwendolyn and Thomas made fine mechanic helpers

As a bonus, Charlie came by just as I needed help putting the engine into
the chassis.

It was not all work and no play. I did go by and check on Hal. On Wed. he
had the first of 21 consecutive days of home chemo by pill. They have reduced
his steroid dosage so he his less active but still ok mentally. I read the
report from his recent MRI. It was, of course, medical jargon. But, as best I
could tell, it said no new growth, no infection and no fluid build up. I read it
as saying he is doing as well as could be hoped for.
Syble, a girl I worked with for many (many) years called and offered to buy
my lunch for my birthday. We met at Pop's where I had a bowl of beans. We
talked for a couple of hours about people we worked with long ago and far away.
The first time I had seen her in years.



Sunday morning I went out in the dark, started the car and backed out. When
I tried to stop, the car went right on down the ramp. We had freezing rain
during the night.We went on to breakfast then to church. Early service had been
canceled so we came home then went back to SS.
Since most of the week was on the Cletrac. I will include a picture as of
right now. Stay tuned.
