Cat Tales

14 photographs·353 words

Wed. I went to cardiologist for my 6 months checkup. Thanks to exercise.
clean living , cheerful disposition and a handful of pills 2X/day,
everything is right down the middle. Should be able to delay the Aortic
valve replacement for a good while yet.

Several weeks back we dragged the Farmall H and the MD out of the barn to
take to the Blountville tractor event. Could not get either to run. A
couple of weeks back we got the H running and put it back in the barn.

This week, with the help of Don Haas, the MD is also tucked away.

That space in between is for the blue ford.

We struggled with it all week and continue to lose ground. Fuel and
electrical problems.

Look at these two pictures and guess what is going on.

We did have an AT work day.It involved a lot of GOB's, a lot of pickup
trucks, a lot of milling around and not much productive output. I did peel
locust logs for a couple of hours.

Various children are quite helpful with GSH and my menus. I was informed I
could make DIY ham biscuits. Sure enough.

The cat decided to try to enhance his menu also.

Sarah and Trevor stopped by Sunday. Sarah wanted to see the Studebaker she
had heard about from someone. While there, she announced that she heard a
cat. They ended up leaving town with three kittens.

I determined that it is possible to get the mower on a hill so steep the
gravity feed to the fuel injection system does not feed and engine quits.
Unfortunately when it happened is was up against a tree. Unfortunately my
tractor has been in shop for 6 weeks waiting for a 1/2 day clutch
replacement . Had to drag it back up the hill from behind tree the old
fashioned way.

I understand Wayne intends to rescue another car and a load of M'cycles. In
checking out the dolly he determined a wheel bearing needed replaced. Much
better in the driveway than beside the interstate.

Saturday night PBBD sponsored a party.
