November 1, 2015 · Sunday

Oh Dear

7 photographs·456 words

The British do not teach their children "Uh O", they use "Oh Dear".

It has been called to my attention, not too gently I might add, that I had
used "Dipsy Doodle" in a previous epistle. I shall repent in sack cloth and
ashes. I suppose one more sign of incipient dementia.

A normal week in River City. However. normal is still a bit strange.

Did quite a bit of shuffling around with my big brother. Many miles and
many Dr. visits. One visit was to see our mutual Dr. Sago. She zapped a few
for Herb, gave him lots of kind words and promised to fill out some forms
for him. Rather than another Herb picture, how about one of what he sees
while sitting on front porch fidgeting?

I did get word from Union City that Lil' Sister is alive and well. Herb and
I plan to make a run to see her in a few weeks.

Zapped a few for me, dug one out for a biopsy and got me caught up on her
daughters math adventures in learning with Cathy Moffitt (worlds best
middle school math teacher).

A Monday outing to the hillside and a Friday outing finished the mowing
project for this year. I failed to get a finished photo.

I let wishful thinking and hope springing eternal get the better of good
judgement and drove the backwards freeze plug pack in, put JB weld around
the edges (knowing full well no leak has ever been sealed from the
outside), poured in another gallon of anti freeze and drove it three laps
up and down drive today. No leak so far. I am ready for Charlie to come
back and take it for another test run. It sure will move when punched ! No
picture as photographer rode along. Fussing.

AT work was a continuation of last weeks inventorying only made interesting
because this week Joe Morris helped. Joe owns two Ace Hardware stores and
is a full time character.

Rain is predicted all this coming week so it was decided that the leaves
should be moved from house and lawn before the rains came down and floods
come up.

Others may find it difficult to believe that GSH has taken to blowing
leaves hither and yon.

We decided to leave our carpeted drive as is.

One of the Saturday morning GOBs is all knowing Larry the motor mouth. He
gave a dissertation some time back on all the dumb, unsafe things old (over
65) people do. One item listed was getting up on the roof.

For Halloween, we went to Burger King. Did anyone get dressed up?

Kenneth ? Katy ? Aaron? Others? Post some pictures.

Anyway, we muddle on.
