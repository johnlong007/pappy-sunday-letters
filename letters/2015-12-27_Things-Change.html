December 27, 2015 · Sunday

Things Change

11 photographs·98 words

I think we hit our peak number of stockings hung by the chimney with care
at 38 as best I remember. This year we reverted to 1955 level.

In addition to Winter arriving this week, Christmas arrived, right on
schedule.

I made several trips to Erwin, on one trip, Herb insisted I start his
backhoe. I did as instructed.

I got one Early Christmas present this year.

The more or less official Christmas was Saturday and Sunday at D & D's. Not
a lot of present shuffling but a whole lot of eating, Talking and "banging
and clanging".
