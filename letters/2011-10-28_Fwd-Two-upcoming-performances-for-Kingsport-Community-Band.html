From: Lick, Wendy Wilkinson
To: Kingsport Community Band members
Sent: Fri, Oct 28, 2011 5:14 am
Subject: Two upcoming performances for Kingsport Community Band

You already know about the upcoming performance on Saturday, November 19, at the
Kingsport Towne Center (formerly known as the Fort Henry Mall), with downbeat at
9:00 am. We will be playing as part of a walk for St Jude's hospital (info
here: http://www.kingsportchamber.org/calendar.php?id=755). It will be an
especially important performance for the community and for us, and we need
everyone there who can possibly come. Scot suggests we arrive at the concert
site by 8:45. Our music lineup will include peppy Christmas music and some
marches, all chosen to help the walkers keep up their pace. The mall will
provide chairs, and Scot has advised them we need chairs without arms, but we
need to supply our own music stands. Scot has some fold-up stands, but if you
have your own, please bring it.

The second performance, we talked about for the first time last night. We will
play at the Asbury Center's Baysmont campus on Thursday evening, December 15.
This is the retirement community and assisted living center on Netherland Lane,
just off Netherland Inn Road. Normally we play an abbreviated program at the
assisted living wing around 6:30 followed by the full program for the
independent living residents. As with the mall, Baysmont will provide chairs,
but we need to bring our own stands.

Clarinets, where are you? Howard had to carry your entire section by himself
last night, and while Howard is an outstanding clarinet player, he can't quite
handle playing chords by himself. Come on back in so we can hear more of your
section's sweet sound!

Finally, a reminder for those who, like me, intend to pay the $50 voluntary dues
but haven't done it yet. There isn't a hard deadline; Jim Finney (trombone) or
Theron "Blick" Blickenstaff (horn) will accept your check at any rehearsal.
Again, the dues are voluntary and do not apply to students, players just giving
us a tryout, players who just help us out by performing with us occasionally, or
anyone who doesn't feel they can do it. No questions asked.

Wendy Lick
Secretary
Kingsport Community Band
wlick@eastman.com
