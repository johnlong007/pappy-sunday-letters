April 23, 2017 · Sunday

Glad Tidings !

12 photographs·373 words

We got a wedding invitation from Rachel (and Andrew). I am delighted.
Getting old, broke down and stove up is depressing, Watching the
grandchildren graduate and go out into the world to seek their fortunes is
rejuvenating.

More good news is that I was wrong ! I did not kill the cherry tree.

​It must have been sand bagging. Meanwhile, back at the ranch, the new
trees are coming right along. Looks like a bumper crop.

​Speaking of bumper crops, last year the raspberry patch was so dense the
crop was retarded and picking was difficult. So, I cut trails through the
patch. This week, with the help of GSH, we picked up limbs from the hill so
I can mow it and I mowed around the berry patch. It is looking very good.
Do the Summer camper graduates remember the jam sessions? More may be in
order.

.

​​In other news, since it rained most all week it seemed a good time to
pressure wash the deck. As soon as it dries out and the signs are right it
will need repainting and sealing.

​As near as we got to do gooder was helping PBBD prep the Porshe for
finding a new home.

​We spent quite a bit of time working on the '49 interior. I have about
done all the body work I intend to do. Not sure whether I will try to put a
coat of primer on it or not.

When we dating in high school GSH was just learning to drive and I let her
drive a twin to this vehicle.

​

​68 years of Dodge trucks. Sounds like three generations. Which do you like
best?

​

Dr. Sago sent me a note and said I best come in for a 1,000 mile check-up.
(Think about that a Dr. who cares .
about her patients!).

Have anyone seen a "stuck hog"? Know what it means. I have not only seen
but have done the sticking. Anyway, I suppose due to the plavix, I bled
like a stuck hog, Had to explain to Dr. Sago,. So, if anyone wants to see a
picture of blood and gore, I have one.

All in all, a quiet week. How bout elsewhere?
