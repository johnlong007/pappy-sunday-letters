March 13, 2011 · Sunday

Still Ticking

12 photographs·324 words

I could make another long list of exciting things I did not do this week.
However, I shall stick with the mundane I did do.

AT work continued at Chestoa. The trail goes up over a rocky ridge for
about 1 mile. We are working to smooth and level to remove tripping hazards.
Here is a before and after involving a fixed rock outcropping.

The Studebaker has not been forgotten. I have stuck most of the chrome back
on having decided I do not want to spend 5-7 K to have it refinished at
this time. GSH has started on the interior but has been diverted by sewing
Christmas presents.

The trip to Seymour was to run a dyno test on the new and improved engine
for the new and improved car to be built. As best I can tell the engine HP
met expectations at 800. Fine tuning by moving the moveable parameters
around proved to be interesting but it appears everything was real close to
optimum to start the day.

For those who think they have clever pets, can your's top this talent?

I bet Jake cannot brush his teeth.

Linda and David came to visit Herb's goats.

While they were here, we had a birthday party.

In other exciting outdoor news, I decided it was time to attack the rock
outcropping Alan left in the driveway.

and most exciting of all, work started to get the big downed tree moved.
Wayne, GSH and the dogs helped with the project.
The tree was cut into three large sections, We were able to get two dragged
out but had to stop before really getting serious about the remaining (and
much larger) section.


The tree work is to be continued.

I decided to not get distracted with making the crawler run so with the
help of GSH it went back into the shed. Perhaps "we" can get it running for
the Blountville tractor show.
