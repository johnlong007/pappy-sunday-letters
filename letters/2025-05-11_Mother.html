May 11, 2025 · Sunday

Mother

15 photographs·313 words

My mother was probably about 32 when this picture was made. Clearly, I had
been for a bike ride.

A lot of graduations this week. Aaron interrupted his schooling to go
defend our country. All those between Aaron and Thomas have completed one
college degree. Thomas is now on duty defending us. His turn will come for
college I expect.

I got a note from Cousin Jim asking if I wanted to go dig ramps. I said yes
but am afraid I could not make it up the first hill. He then said he would
go on and harvest for me.

He did ! And delivered !

Poor Laura, she is a masster at cooking a ramp fest but cannot eat potatoes.

I have always believed Alan does not like tomatoes because we never fed
tomatoes to him when he was small. Merdith is not making that mistake.

I have a picture of Linda getting Chemo and eating an eclair. I can't match
that but do the best I can with what I have to work with.

We have finished hand and horse tools . Next target is an old David Bradley
mowing machine.

In case you are wondering, This skill is the reason Diane when to school
all those years.

Cat is gone, dog is gone, Critters are taking over the place. We are on
round four with the squirrels and have lost all rounds so far.

Linda has told me that David knows how to convert Squirrels into gravy.
Perhaps I can borrow him for a few days when the new graduates move to town.

We continue to eat high on the hog with A &C.

Tooth M & R.
