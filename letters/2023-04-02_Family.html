April 2, 2023 · Sunday

Family

15 photographs·485 words

No Dr. visits for me. My running is going better . I have not been drinking
(coffee) except in the early morning. CNA came two days. I believe she is
going to fit in better than I hoped. Probably ask her to go to three days
before long. credit PBBD.

Herb's son James came by. I had failed to officially invite his family.
Rats; would like to have seen them. They are a delightful group. At least
they were the last time I saw them, but the girls are full scale teens now.

Work continued on the '55. This time under the supervision of Anita and GSH.

The best '55 news is all the windows are now working ,slow but working.
Speedometer is not.

I think I mentioned the trunk seal. A new seal is in hand. Work continued
on site preparation. Stay tuned.

Linda came in for the weekend and lured Katy and her noodnik over for a
brief targeted visit.

We were all out working away when visitors arrived without being baited ! I
think the high point of the week was little EJ was dancing around . GSH
waved and smiled at her. I did not get a picture. Maybe next time.

We make it to the track for 8 laps most every day. Sunday,PBBD went off to
the art show to show her skill so Alan and Catherine filled in. Three PM is
afternoon pill time. Before we left for the track I told Catherine we
needed a pill and a plan. She had both.

I got the mower out of hibernation. One tire has been slow leaking for a
long time. This time. I think we got it.

Wayne checked the '55 speedometer failure and determined the dial had
frozen, twisting the cable off in the head. Fortunately, we have a spare.
PBBD cleaned it, when Wayne returns we hope he will check the operation and
swap it out. Stay tuned.
These 150 MPH units are hig $ pearls.

Less joyful was my attempt to save time freeing the stuck window by not
removing the door panel for ease of access. We broke the glass.
Fortunately, we have a spare. Stay tuned.

LLLL came in for a visit. I knew school teachers were not really high paid
but was not aware of just how bad it is. I did tell her that a big
milestone in my life was not having to wear patched clothes attending high
school.

As best I can tell, she has put Dale to work playing for donations to raise
funds.

I am aware our grandchildren are dealing with rain, snow, wind and perhaps
gloom of night.
Just a reminder of days gone by.
