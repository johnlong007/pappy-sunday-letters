March 22, 2009 · Sunday

Biking the Yucatan

10 photographs·400 words

Overall, the trip exceeded my expectations. It was very much different from
previous IBT bike trips in Europe. Generally we covered less distance but
tougher riding conditions.
It was hot
Strong headwinds most of the time
Off road bikes with knobby tires not suited for asphalt
The group was delightful
The locals were very friendly
The Spring Break kids were tamer than one would expect
the sights and sites were spectular
The group members assembled at the Cancun airport Saturday March 14 for a
van ride to the starting point at Izamel. I think 18 people out of which we
already knew 5 from previous outings.

On Sunday we went to our first Mayan site Chichen Itza.A local guide gave us
a tour. Apparently there are thousands of sites on the peninsula only a few
of which have been uncovered and of those only small parts are open for
touring.

On the bike ride to the site, I was riding sweep and got to use my recent
Red Cross triaining when one of our riders became dehydrated. A passing driver
saw what was happening, came back and took Steve to catch the escort van. It
all worked out fine with a bit of rest and a lot of shade and water.

Lunch was fruit and peanut butter/jelly sandwiches beside the road.
Breakfast and supper were lots of fruit and local fare almost all very good.

The whole area is limestone with scrub vegetation. A lot of sink holes, some
filled with water and connected to underground rivers. We went swimming and
snorkeling in some of the sink holes.

At one site, we got to climb to the top of a pyramid.

Another time, another place, we saw A cow with a crumpled horn and spent a
lot of time trying to remember the rhyme

No trip would be complete without a visit to Cozumel to see the Spring Break
kids and to snorkel amidist the multi colored fishes. No actual pictures as
all the photographers were in the water.

In Tolum, we took kayaks in to the swamps to see various swamp creatures.

I took about 250 pictures. If anyone really wants to see more, I will be
glad to accommodate them after I have had a chance to sort and edit to a
reasonable number.
