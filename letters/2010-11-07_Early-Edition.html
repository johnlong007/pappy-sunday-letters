November 7, 2010 · Sunday

Early Edition

10 photographs·304 words

First, daylight saving time has ended for this year. Second, our new (but
not improved) preacher has killed off Sunday night services. So the early
edition.

I think we should have a "write a caption" contest.


Use the reply all function, then we will have a peoples choice winner and
perhaps a second place also.

We did AT work Tuesday. It was good because for the first time in several
months Dave went out. In addition, since county schools were out, Jonathan
went with us.We were able to drive very close to the AT were we wanted to
work. Also, the trail passes through an abandoned apple orchard.

Jonathan filled his backpack with apples.He also got a patch for his days
work. Later in the week the apples reappeared in a different form

Monday I ran the disc over the three garden spots and seeded with Rye.
Then, it turned cold late in the week so I stayed indoors and kindled a fire in
the shop stove.
I have decided to delay a decision on what to do about the pitted "bright
work" on the Studebaker. In the meantime I have been installing it on the
car. That way I can make sure it all fits and I have the required fasteners.
Plus, it lets me delay starting on the interior work which I am dreading.

GSH has gone back into professional counseling services :

Having lost my helpers and since my little flying fingers have been reduced
to fumbling fingers I recruited a passer by to help install the retaining
bolts in the grill.

I did learn something new this week. Hairy woodpeckers like persimmons.

Looking closely, one should be able to spot three family musicians in this
picture. (A forth was on stage but not in view for this picture. (Sorry
Meredith).
