September 20, 2015 · Sunday

Wood

11 photographs·266 words

Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday I started each day by going to Don's place
and getting a load of wood.

Monday and Tuesday afternoon we unloaded and split wood. Wed. we stopped by
Coates farm to see Mark and ended up trimming a downed tree and sawing wood.

I asked Mark to retrieve the '55 from the museum and get it in condition to
sell. He promised to do so withing a few weeks.

Thursday was AT work. We put in a series of steps and landings up a rather
steep section of trail

Friday I went with Herb to visit various Dr's. He learned nothing on three
visits as far as I could tell. We did stop at Stihl shop in Unicoi to pick
up his saw. Learned they don't fix anything just replace parts. Met Lisa at
last Dr. stop and transferred Herb and the saw into her care.

I learned that I would have been smarter to start wearing safety toe shoes
years ago.

Saturday was a great day !

Various wood splitters showed up. I missed getting a picture of Gwendolyn
and her helpers. I did note from sledge hammer examination that someone has
taken a mighty swing and overshot. I do hope that was one of the trainees.

Of course, the highlight of the day was getting to see all three GGC at one
time. Makes me want to start over with Summer Camp. Pretty close to size we
started with way back when.

Wonder what is going on elsewhere?

Switzerland, Wisconsin, Alabama, California, Utah, Texas, Kentucky, New
York Tennessee. Miss anywhere?
