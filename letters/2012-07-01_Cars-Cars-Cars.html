July 1, 2012 · Sunday

Cars, Cars, Cars

9 photographs·363 words

I decided to get all the cars running, checked out and put back in storage.
Easier said than done.

The '55 Chrysler started right up (after I replaced the rotor I had left
out when I sanded the points). Charlie got to drive it several laps. But,
the power windows were not working. After much effort and evil thoughts I
have all except passenger window working.

Laura's Daytona battery was low. So, we stuck another battery in and drove
the car down to wash and check tire pressure. Recharged the battery and put
the car back away sort of covered.

No progress on the '53 Studebaker. Waiting for a fuel pump.

The Alfa also started right up. It also has a window not operating. I am
not doing well on it. It seems to be grounded back through the wiring
rather than at the motor.

Monday I finished planting the front garden, corn, beans, okra and punkins.
None of it will matter if the rains keep holding off. Maria came over and
tended to the wedding flower patch.

AT work day was weed mowing in the wilderness area above Wilbur dam. No
power tools allowed. I mowed the left side, Pat Loven did the right while
the rest of the crew worked on the shelter and the water supply.

Finally got to Erwin to walk the property line. As near as I can tell, the
surveyor had it just right following the deed calls up the ridge. Herb,
Jimmy and I had put in a barbed wire fence many years ago, maybe 1946. We
nailed it to trees where possible. The line follows the fence about 1/4
mile then goes along top of the ridge another 1/4 mile. It was well marked
and easy to find until we got to the national forest markers. Then, I did
not find any clear mark where our property line intersects the US line.

If anyone wants to go walk the line go ahead while the markings are easy to
see.

It was hot all week and records, all time hot on Saturday. The inside temp
did not get above 85 and cools right off at night.
