December 1, 2024 · Sunday

The new Normal

17 photographs·243 words

Family, faith,friends.
So far, so good.

For the last 2 or 3 weeks we have been going through 70 years worth of
accumulated stuff.. So far, mostly from our trips all over the world. I
sure am glad we went.

As the weather turned colder, we moved inside and fired up the shop stove.

First cultivator is restored and ready to go on display.

Second cultivator is in parts. Rusty parts. Handles missing.

Thanksgiving week. We got company. Jonathan came and went. He missed
hearing me tell Charlotte the original meaning of "getting into the short
rows". and what "balk" means when cultivating.

Mari, Eric and their preacher stopped by after depositing a truck load of
goods from their church to a local sister. church . I don't remember
what Mari was studying. .

Another visitor. First time I have seen this one in several years.

Old people (me) need a warm place. We are going right through the wood out
front. However, plenty left to replenish.

Laura let us come join in for her Thanksgiving dinner. As usual, it was
great.

Saturday, A & C took us to have another go at Thanksgiving with Rachel,
Andrew, Cliff, Andrea and various noodniks. It was a delightful outing.
