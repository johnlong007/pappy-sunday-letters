Picture

0 photographs·100 words

The picture of you cutting a limb with a chainsaw while on a ladder put me
in shock.

Probably the worst thing that happened to me at ECC came about because a
man (about like you) on a Saturday chose to cut a limb just like you did.

It did not break clean, the top swung around, taking the ladder out from
under him. He fell and broke back. He spent the last 50 years in a
wheelchair before dying recently.

If you want to know the rest of the story, stop by and ask me to tell you.

Pappy
