We just got in from a hard days work.

Each year at Damascus "Trail Days" we recruit about 100 hikers for Sunday,
Monday and Tuesday work on the AT. Official name is "Hardcore". We aim for
50 new recruits and 50 return folks. Trail Days is reunion time for hikers
from years apst. They come in from all over the world. By not paying
attention to my scheduling, I can only work Sunday, today, this year. GSH
always helps with feeding the tired, hungry hikers at the end of the day
Sunday.

We were doing a relocation away up on Pond Mountain, a long steep walk in.
The relocation was split into several section, each about 100 feet long and
each to be assigned a number of hikers under the direction of a club
member. I did not sign up for supervision. I did go ahead with the first
folks to put up signs and direct hikers/workers to their assigned location.

Then I went off to help Kim with her assigned section (12). We came up
short on workers and she only had one worker , "Virginia from Georgia". I
started digging and Kim started training virginia. She said two rules,
"have fun and don't get hurt". Then she turned around, walked behind me
tripped, fell down the hill backward hitting a tree. When I started to help
her up she allowed her shoulder was hurt. We put her arm in a homemade
sling and sent Virginia to walk her out.

I ended up doing the entire section by myself. Late in the day some of the
others finished their sections and came helped me do the first section. So,
all the work got completed and the relocation opened for business.

Dinner was good.

Turns out, the shoulder is broken. Dave and Carol were there. Dave directed
traffic and Carol helped with dinner. Dave is recovering from rotator cuff
surgery.

Earlier in the week, we went with Carol to trim, inspect and pick up trash
on her section of trail. It goes around the end of the lake to the shelter.
I ran the string trimmer and cut poison Ivy.
