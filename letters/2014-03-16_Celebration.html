March 16, 2014 · Sunday

Celebration

13 photographs·665 words

This week GSH celebrated the 39 th anniversary of her 39th birthday! It was
a pretty low key event. However, other family notables had rather higher
profile events.

This party had plenty of cakes and goodies for two or more birthdays.

BC/BS sent another Dr. by to check on us. The good news is we both passed
the quickie Dementia test. I asked him why this home health check is being
done. He said the government requires it of companies covering people on
Medicare. Pages and pages of questions, same questions that the last one
asked. Next time I am going to give different answers.
A nice gentleman, grew up on a farm in Kansas driving a MM. He liked Mine.
He is now retired but works two weeks/year to keep license valid.
Interesting and easy pocket money.

GSH has high blood pressure and
it pegs when Dr. suggests taking pills. This dr. said more exercise and
less stress. She could not think of any stress rather than me. So, a
program is under way to introduce more exercise. Katy, we need you !

We did go out for a day on the AT. Where we went up near Indian Grave
Gap, the AT zig zags up the hill. When we started down, we found a deep
trail about two foot wide had been cut straight down the hill crossing the
AT several times.

Carl tried to call Forest Service ranger to notify them. He could not get
through. So, after he got home he sent a note. Carl is normally very
diplomatic and leans over backwards to avoid offending. But, believing the
ditch to be the work of some hill runners or the like he allowed it was
pretty dumb and a certain source of uncontrolled erosion as well as
confusing to hikers on the AT.

Answer came back, it was dug by the forest service people who are going to
do a controlled burn.

Gwendolyn was home for break.

Benj was also home. He came over one day to help on the LBT. He removed the
gas tank from the parts vehicle. I intend to get it installed this week. He
also got to practice using self tapping screws and pop rivets.

Herb came over one day after going in for his 500 mile checkup on face
work. Not content to have worked over the right side of his face with
radiation, she attacked his left cheek with knives and possibly forks.

This week he will go back in to see if more work on right side is required.

On his way, he brought me a stack of metal computer cabinet panels which
are just right for making patches for the LBT. So, I have started making
patches. I am still going to have to find one big piece to patch the entire
lower strip along the right side.

Saturday, I went to Maryville and Seymour with Dwight. Wayne sent a note
saying he has broken a car part so I thought I was going back to pull a
part but he got it resolved.

The pictured treasure (other then Dwight) was manufactured in Finland in
1944. Today I read the history of Finland during WW2. It is well worth
reading. It would be fascinating to know the history of this piece and the
hands which have held it.

Then we stopped by to check on the pool program progress.

Looked like fine work to me . It also appeared to me that Pat would have
preferred to be playing with his race cars.

When we got home from church tonight, the turkeys were just heading up the
hill to roost.
