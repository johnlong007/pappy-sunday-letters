March 30, 2014 · Sunday

Taps

12 photographs·620 words

This week saw the passing of another ww2 vet and the final chapter in a
long and well lived life. Mr. Long will be missed by all who knew him.

I have another friend (Sanford's sister) who is now in hospice care. We
went to visit her Monday and plan to go again tomorrow. She is truly an
amazing person.

On the health front, I went over to check on Herb. His face seems to be
healing nicely but the verdict is not yet in as to whether follow up
treatment is going to be required.

Some time back, Laura sent me a "23andme" kit. I went through the whole Q&A
process, prepared my sample and turned over to the USPS.. Before long I
will get an answer as to just where my genes came from. I may or may not
share the information depending on the answers then get.

Who remembers our first trip to Alaska in the LBT? We drove 800 miles on
gravel and dirt road on the way up (more on the way back). The windshield
got cracked but the only failure we had was the wires going to right rear
tail light broke due to an overload of mud. This week I took out the
taillight and found the wire I had spliced in to repair the break.

I do not remember where I got it but the wire is clearly just a cheap cord
for a lamp or something. Anyway, I stuck my arm down in the tail light
opening to extract the wire and gave it a jerk.

Not enough what mother nature does to me. I have to add my own efforts.

As usual, GSH has been actively involved in helping with the project.

She used her nimble fingers to take the "Sportsman" emblem off. The target
for this week is to replace the big rusted out lower right side. If I
cannot find a piece of sheet metal large enough the plan is to cut out a
panel on the parts vehicle. I really do not want to do that. I sort of hope
to store it and sometime out there in the future one of the heirs or
assignees can do a body transplant on the LBT.

Mostly this week GSH worked on the income tax. She did it all on paper
(without even a calculator) then did it on Turbo Tax and kept going round
and round until they matched. Then, she did the same thing for the state
tax. By the way, if you think Tn. does not have a state income tax you are
wrong.

Several family members were in town this week. I did get a picture of Ross
and Katy making a somewhat delayed departure.

Which reminds me, can anyone identify the child in this picture?

AT work got back underway with walking to White Rocks with Pat Loven. We
cleared blowdowns and clipped Laurel. For the benefit of those who live in
the flatlands. I did take a couple of pictures.

If you look close you can see the south end of a north bound Ruffled
Grouse. We saw several but they were too quick for me.

Wayne came over today. So, I enlisted him to remove the fuel fill line from
the parts vehicle, I was afraid I would do in my other arm if I tried.

Finally, a flora quiz. Identify the pictured plant!
