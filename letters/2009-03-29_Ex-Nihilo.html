March 29, 2009 · Sunday

Ex Nihilo

8 photographs·173 words

I had it ready to go when the computer ate it.
Spread dirt leveled and re seeded soccer field.
Herb and Sis gave Pat five rose bushed for a Donna memorial. With the help
of willing workers, they are now planted.
AT work involved a lot of walking carrying a chainsaw in order to cut five
reported blow downs. The Northbound through hikers are arriving. Met several
including two from UK and one Australian.
Tax time in Tennessee. Because of the house work withdrawals, we are getting
hard hit.
Romance is in the air.
Linda came to visit during Spring break. Herb had donated some fresh sausage
so B&G were the order of the day.
I piddled with the mowing machine all week .I am trying to make the parts
mower operational. It is missing four pieces. I removed the needed parts from
the complete mower to use as patterns and am in the process of making silk
purses.
