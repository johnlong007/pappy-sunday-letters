Four Walls

10 photographs·203 words

>From this picture one would think Monday morning. One would be wrong.
Friday morning ! Two units of blood.

Along with the start of IDNIFA we received many warnings about the system's
potential overreaction, which could lead to fatal results. While splitting
wood, I encountered some, but not all, of the possible indicators. Wound up
getting an EKG and two units of blood. A really interesting practice run.

On Saturday, we had another medical event. For those in the medical field,
you should be aware that low platelets and hot peppers can cause serious
mouth blood blisters and bleeding.

Into the real world, we split wood. Wet beech is difficult but indications
are that cured beech is more difficult. We are making good progress.

Loading the last of this winter's dried firewood.

When this storage is empty it will be time for the furniture.

The tractor has had a leaking PS cylinder for years, and the leak has
worsened. Wayne decided to fix it properly.

The forestry products team has been collecting sap by the gallon.

Signs of Spring: Turkeys.

And other signs.
