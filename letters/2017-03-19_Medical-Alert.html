March 19, 2017 · Sunday

Medical Alert

6 photographs·313 words

I had an appt. with our GP on Tu. Got in on time, All my signs and portends
are better than should be expected.

I gave him a pep talk about getting run all over the place having testing
done on GSH and no one tying it all together. He assured me he would
examine her file and be ready for her routine visit on Wed. (the next day.)

Wed. appt. at 11, arrived at 10:45, left at 11:45 w/o seeing dr.

Diane called and got appt to see NP on Friday. Almost same story but NP
came in 5 before time I had set as leaving time. 55 minutes late.

Glad we stayed, she was really good, went over all the reports in detail.

Welcome to socialized medicine, control the cost by limiting the supply.

On the home front,

It was mostly cold this week and I mostly stayed inside. No A work and not
much on the '49.

​
Diane and GSH made progress on the '63 seats.

I suppose the bathroom people made progress, they hammered and banged all
week.
Friday the Decatur branch of the family came through, just in time for
picking and grinning.

​
I highly recommend checking this out on Friday evening, good people, good
music.

One evening Anita had a social event. Good people but no music.

Know what they are consuming?

​We did make a run to Erwin to check on niece Lisa. Best I can tell she is
doing fine. A somewhat traumatic week for all of us as one year since her
Dad died. I think she is pressing on pretty much as he would want.

Another night we went to the Durham's to eat supper and watch ETSU play in
NCAA.

​
​We got a couple of updates from extended limbs and branches. We do
appreciate hearing form each and all.
