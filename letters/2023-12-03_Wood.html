December 3, 2023 · Sunday

Wood

13 photographs·362 words

Monday I went in for labs as usual. Results were OK. Later in the week we
talked with the UVA dr. he expressed satisfaction with lab results and said
he is going to extend the recovery period from 5 to 7 weeks between chemo
series.

He also said I should be able to take an extended trip about Springtime.
Stay tuned.

Back in the real world, we had a pile of sawed up firewood to split.Foe
some reason we decided to bring up to the ramp to split it.

While we were busy splitting away, Maria showed up with a potential WW.. I
judged from various signs and portends that he had not done a lot of wood
splitting before. But, he appeared willing and a rather quick learner. Not
a bit flustered working under Maria's critical eyeing.

When I went out to run around, I noticed a power company crew had cut down
one of our big white pine trees. They had just chunked up the log. Had I
known it could have been a saw log and saved us a lot of effort. I did tell
them I would take care of the chunks and they could just dum the chips
right along under the remaining trees.

Not only did they dump that load but also two more loads from along Ellis
Road.

I moved the chunks down to our wide splitting/stacking area beside the
driveway.

The captive wood splitter lept into action.

All split in half.

Pine is not the first choice for firewood. Low heat content and quick
burning. but, it should do for the shop stove. Only needs 6 months to dry
rather than a full year for the Oak

We completely filled one section.

The old man and the saw.

While we were busily engaged, the sawmiller arrived.

To the dismay of PBBD, she found by reverse engineering that she has a
water leak at the almost empty house on Reservoir Road. Her renter came to
launch exploratory searching. TBC.
