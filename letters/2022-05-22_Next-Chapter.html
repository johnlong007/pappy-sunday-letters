It has been a long time since we graduated from UCHS. Even longer for Jimmy
and Sis. Dont think we have anyone in the family in process now . Maybe for
the first time since mid-30s.

Family members graduating from far and further. I think Jonathan flew under
the radar.

Closer to home, Momma left the home place she and my father built in 1935
to brother Jimmy. He is getting old, broke down and stove up. He has the
good fortune to have three children who work together to look after him.
Saturday, We went to Erwin to see him off to the old folks home in
Morristown and to take a perhaps final look at the home place.

I took a lot of pictures, inside and outside. If anyone wants a narrated
tour I can provide. It was indeed a walk down memory lane. Mostly, but not
all, good memories.
Some time, in the 70s as I remember, I hired George Tabor to install a heat
pump A/C system in the house. I dont think either Mom or Dad ever really
like it.
But looking back brings up one of my few regrets. Momma always wanted a
fireplace and a warm spot. I regret not providing for her.

Work continued on the tractor instruments. Stay tuned.

Work also continued on the '37. My contribution is by not helping. PBBD is
doing a much better level of fit and finish than I settle for.

I fear the ramp season has slipped by. Meredith came over but
circumstances did not permit. So, we finished off the last of those Jim
Foster had provided. She also brought my Fitbit back to life after having
been dead for several months.

we spend a lot of time outside bird watching.

Wildlife has become brazen since Max left. Mostly it is OK but the crows
are coming in and stripping the feeders. Stay tuned.

Not on the saame level or schedule as California tomatoes but doing well
and critter proof ! Maybe.

Quite a bit of work on the retaining basin. I had planned to make the deep
part bigger and deeper, however, indications are that I may have hit solid
rock. We shall see, or hear, or feel, or despair.
