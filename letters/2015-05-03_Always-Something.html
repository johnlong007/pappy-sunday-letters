Always Something

4 photographs·282 words

Last week i had an Echogram. This week, the Dr. called and said I have an
appointment with cardiologist on Friday May 8th. Siad I showed signs of
Aorta constriction. Stay tuned.

Herb had various Dr. appts this week. Looks like his infections are healed
. Next he is scheduled to get a plan from Vanderbilt Dr. for radiation and
Chemo. I sat in on one dr. visit this week. He said the chemo will have
some side effects but nothing like the kind we have seen while dealing with
serious internal issues.

Tomorrow is Herb's birthday. His kids put on an event for he and Ella today
(almost shared BDs). It was a fun event. A number of folks showed up most
of whom thought I was Herb. I refrained from doing anything to overly cause
embarassment to Herb.

On the home front outside of mowing, The rear shocks are now installed ,
and LBT has been operated for long enough to come up to temperature, So
far, so good. Next step is to tow it to muffler shop and get exhaust system
installed. GSH is working on replacing the noise and head shield inside the
engine compartment. But, will not install until after exhaust system.

Then time to get it primed, It does look a bit ratty now.

The dog loves to play soccer. AT Wal Mart, I paid $2.50 for a big ball
which I did not think he could bite. He loved it. Rolled all over at high
speed across soccer field and into the mud hole. Then he killed it and
skinned it out. Sure would like to find one about that size but much more
durable.
