May 25, 2025 · Sunday

Well, Bless Your Heart !

16 photographs·439 words

A number of high points this week. I got to ride shotgun with Merdith on a
trip to bring home a load of silver. Just about everything which happened
made me remember Patrice and think she would be pleased.

Alan planned and executed a Bar-B-Q event. Come if you want to sort of
happening. I think the best part was Rachel brought me a wet kid she had
fished out of Patricia's frog pond. I am pretty sure her kids will have no
gender doubts.

Along the same line, Nephew James came by with his three youngest. Watching
them run, play and be rowdy was a delight. James got me all caught up on
things in his domain.

Linda sent me a picture which includes her in full academic regalia. Looks
like school is out for Summer break .

Coming up on two years being at the cancer center early Monday to get a
blood test and treated as seems necessary. I continue to appreciate those
family members who donate.

I stay busy but do mostly sit down work. This week, a lot of work on the
mowing machine.

Don and Sanford stopped by after GOB lunch on Wed. I tried to get them
involved but all I got was free opinions.

We have a lot of birds ! A lot of woodpeckers. Woody has been
coming closer. He was working on a stump beside the Oak at the ramp
entrance.

Friday is breakfast with Sanford and his high school family at the Burger
Hut in Elizabethton. Patrice went along for her last 2 or 3 years and the
waitress ,Sharon, was always kind, polite and thoughtful with her. Now, she
always comes over and gives me a hug . I told her I would bring her home
but doubt my daughter would let me keep her.

Wayne checked the operation of the solar powered lawn mower before
hanging on display. It works.

For those who remember Blountville High and Jr. high.

Diane continued in mortal combat with the squirrels. She bought a device
which flashes light and emits high frequency sound. They loved it, had 7 on
and under the feeder in no time. Sort of a disco thing .

She also has a water AR 15

The mower has rubber ties. Both shot. We are cleaning the wheels while I
try to find some low cost 600-16 tires.

You can turn green with envy !
