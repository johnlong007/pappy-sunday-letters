February 19, 2012 · Sunday

Bewitched, Bothered and Bewildered

8 photographs·361 words

Not much going on in Lake Wobegon this week. The weather caused us to postpone working on the tractor which would not run and caused a rescheduling of AT work day. So, no do-gooder got done, at least by me. Perhaps This coming week will compensate.

But, neither rain, sleet, snow nor gloom of night kept Valentine Day from coming in like a lamb.

However better than the Valentines was the delivery person ! GSH has been working on a cape project for 2-3 months. She had recently became discouraged, disgruntled and dismayed and had packed the almost finished cape away never to be seen again, at least not in this life.
Then, Anita showed up and asked about the cape insisting that it be dragged out and displayed. It was.

Her reaction was so encouraging that GSH completed the cape (late Saturday night and early Sunday morning) and wore it to church today. I failed to get a full dress picture but did get a sort of reenactment.

She has been working on another long running project which may be nearing completion. It is one of the Christmas request items I believe. I also understand that she has another request waiting in line.

The weather was not bad all the time. One day we got out and peeled bark from the big Oak log and rounded up more of the pieces of firewood. Splitting and stacking may be delayed until Summer Camp.

I spent a lot of time grinding rust from the '37 pickup and then painting the exposed parts.

Later in the week, helpers arrived to move the cab back and expose the rusted out metal places. It is to be hoped (and expected) that serious repair work wil occur this coming week.

I intended to move THE CAR into the storage building this week. Made it far enough to get the Alfa out and moved down here to the house and pulled the little blue truck out into the weather. Then I stalled out.

Two empty bays and vehicles, both running and inert scattered all around.

So, plenty of opportunity to do more and better this coming week.
