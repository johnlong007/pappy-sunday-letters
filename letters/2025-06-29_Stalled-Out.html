Stalled Out

13 photographs·241 words

There is a new name written up in glory'

After many weeks of work, the DB mowing machine is in position for mounting.

Up on stands, in the dry and ready for drive by viewing.

The pool people pushed to get the pool filled last weekend. The electrical
people came Monday to run new power lines. Said pool people would come to
start the system and finish the job.We waited and watched all week.

Diane called on Thursday. She was extremely annoyed with them. Still
waiting and watching.

Eric and Mari stopped by for a brief visit on their way home. I got caught
up on their children's adventures growing up.

We got new belts for the mower just in time to get sort of caught up.

A lot of progress was made on moving the Walnut logs to the sawmill.

This picture is to show how far behind I had dropped.

Somewhere along, i cracked the mower deck. Alan leapt at the opportunity to
practice new skills.

Meanwhile, the ME's set to work trouble shooting various issues with the
mower operation. Stay tuned

Friday, we had a hamburger feast with A & C, today, we had leftovers with a
Mr. Stripey from our own plant !

Confirmed, again, she can bake a cherry pie.
