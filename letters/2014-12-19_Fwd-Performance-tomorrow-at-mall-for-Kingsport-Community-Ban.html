*Attention **Dr. Garrett and any other trombone players* who have played
with us a lot in the past or are good at sight reading: For our
performance tomorrow morning, we have some people who can’t be there
and we really
could use a couple more trombone players!!!! You have probably played much
of our music lineup before, and you can always “play just the white notes”
in the ones you aren’t familiar with.

Here are the details for tomorrow:

*Time:* *downbeat at **10:**00 **A**M* – please arrive no later than *9:45**
AM* to reduce Scot’s stress.
*Location*: Sears Court, at the stage. The nearest entrance is next to
Piccadilly Cafeteria. (*map*
<http://www.kingsporttowncenter.com/pages/main/documents/MallDirectoryinstalled4-13-11.pdf>
)
*Dress: *Wear “Christmas” colors if practical.
*Directions* Mall address is 2101 Fort Henry Drive. If you need driving
directions, let me know.
*Other: Bring your own stand if you have one!!! AND remember your music
folder!*

The music lineup:

The Christmas Song (Chestnuts Roasting…)
The Wonderful World of Christmas
The Christmas Suite
Victor Herbert Overture (see “ * ” below)
Three Carols for Christmas
A Jingle Bells Fantasy
Sleigh Ride

* In the Victor Herbert Overture, we will play only measure 64 to the end.
Measures 64 and 65 we will play twice to better set us up for March of the
Toys.

Also, we once again forgot to hold officer elections at our first rehearsal
in December, and now we are out of rehearsals for this year. So our
current officers will continue to serve until we can sort things out in
January. If you are interested in becoming an officer or board member,
reply to this note.

Wendy Lick (horn)
