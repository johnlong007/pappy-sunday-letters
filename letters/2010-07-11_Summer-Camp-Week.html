Summer Camp Week

3 photographs·53 words

Pictures must wait for the official album.

This year we ran Tuesday through Saturday. I always try to work through
what went well and what needs improvement.

The weather was really hot and dry.

The garden to be harvested worked great.

No one got hurt.

Some missed, some left early, some arrived late.
