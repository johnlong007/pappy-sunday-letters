January 3, 2021 · Sunday

Still Ticking

13 photographs·312 words

It turns out that when we cut the dead pines for farmer Lisa we missed one
. If you look close the orange hard hat is my Stihl hat, Wayne has a white
one. We think the job is taken care of for a while now.

The windshield job in the 37 is not going well. Pretty obvious why the
swing out windshields were abandoned after a few years. Sealing and fitting
is proving difficult. I did manage to recruit one new helper.

Less interesting but more worthwhile was trying to find the source of the
fountain of water coming through the ceiling. We have had the roof replaced
2 0r 3 times and always end up with leaks before any reasonable time.during
the recent snow the leak rate increased.
Using my brute force "do something" approach, I put a cover over the area
where I believe the leak source is located. A lot of rain since then and
no drips inside. Stay tuned.

Three pieces so far !

Holiday week ! Children, grandchildren and (John) great grandchildren to be
welcomed and or admired.

I had some world class Aunts. Like cousins, aunts are very important
people, more so than parents in some aspects.

Another event , this time on Reservoir road.

Sarah put together a birthday event for her Mom. Actual birthday was on a
Sunday morning while we lived in Fort Worth. I really liked Fort Worth and
Texas but not GDFW.

We helped Donna relocate when she broke up housekeeping. She gave me a box
of pictures. I solicited help in trying to get names and perhaps some
information about as many as possible. My panel of experts at work.

Guess the significance.
