83 ! Good Grief !

A really big week.

I have sorely missed the Little Dog. Want and intend to get another Pom but
in the meantime, back at the ranch:

In other almost as exciting, I had another dang birthday. 83, difficult for
me to believe and do not comprehend. Wish I knew how one is supposed to
feel at 83. I suppose I could ask Lil' sister.

Dave and Pat Edwards asked us to their place for birthday breakfast (and
car consultation). Then last night various family members had a pizza party
here.

Sort of difficult to see the family pictures above the windows but they
were examined and (mostly) admired by all those present and voting. While
the married section is right up to date, their is one missing in the GGC
collection.

Does everyone know when the atomic bomb was dropped on Japan bring WW2 to
an end? I was in elementary school then and not yet well versed in particle
physics. Neverthe less in 1947 a buss load of students from Temple Hill
made a trip to Oak Ridge to visit the newly opened museum. As part of the
museum, they had a machine with a coin slide insert into which one could
place a dime. Push it in pull out at some time and get a coin in a frame.
Said coin would then bring a conveniently placed Geiger counter into a
spasm of counting Geigers'.

I did the coin thing and kept it for many, many years. But, at some point
it got left behind. Having recited this pitiful tale to Kenneth and his
thought full Mom during our much later tour of said museum . Thoughtful Mom
led thought full spouse to secure one for a very meaningful birthday
present.

Now, as all know everything in my knick knack case has a story (even if I
have forgotten some of them) so all will know the story of the irradiated (
sp?) dime.

Willing workers are getting scarce around here. Trash has been accumulating
on Ellis Road. Some time back, a sign appeared which has helped much more
than I expected but trash still accumulates. So, conscripting the nearest
person I could find, we picked up on both sides. Our neighbors must be
getting older. Only one beer bottle, two beer cans, 5 soft drink cand and a
multitude of fast food refuse. Interesting to note the soft drink
containers were all super size.

We did a bit more work on the '63. The moulding clips are now installed one
each side. Plus a bit more work on the seat installation. The backs of the
front seat await Diane for installation.

Wayne has been working with a roofer to get a new metal roof installed on
the barn and barn annex. He came by to bring me up to date and I asked that
we make one final inspection to convince ourselves the barn is worth
spending money on a roof rather than tear down and build greater . Te use
and awl to check basic structure which seems sound. The support posts for
annex behind are rotted below ground level. But, overall the plan is press
bravely onward.
