April 25, 2010 · Sunday

All the New News

12 photographs·333 words

In spite of popular requests I have decided to give an update on my hand. I
can fold it enough to hold a hand tool . No problem holding the chain saw.
It does not work well for small thinks, trying to button my shirt. It have
been working more without gloves heading toward getting it toughened. I go
back to the hand Dr. Thursday so will find out how I am doing.

The prom dresses were finished and put into service. Obviously well worth
all the effort.

More progress, sort of, on the car. The first time we got it running it was
leaking oil, anti-freeze and PS fluid.
I think the anti-freeze leaks are corrected. The PS is disconnected pending
technical consultations and nothing has been done about the oil leak, Stay
tuned.
It would not shift gears easily. Dwight adjusted on the shifter wit no
success so I took it out today. Only a minor glitch we believe.
We did move it outside and run for a prolonged period yesterday. It starts
easily and sounds great. There is hope of getting it to look like the
picture by June one but interior work will likely remain.

AT work was routine. We were digging a minor relocation. We moved the trail
about four feet and put in a series of steps. It can be seen this is not a
crew of spring chickens.

The Gray old tractor owners event was TFSS this week. Our Blountiville club
always handles registration of arriving tractors for the show. I did the
registration Thursday and Friday. The show was in conjunction with the
national Allis Chalmers club event this year. A lot of A-C tractors from all
over the US and Canada.

Willing workers attacked the garden just before the rain Saturday evening.
Then Katy (willing we assume) planted more things today. We hope the things
they planted will be ready to harvest during summer camp. Probably ready
to weed and hoe long before then.
