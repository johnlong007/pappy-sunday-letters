August 11, 2013 · Sunday

Dr. Who

9 photographs·562 words

Back in to Dr. Sago for the "final" round of chemo shots. She looked at
site. Pronounced "it is not working" and she was going to go back to drill
and shoot.

She cut and dug another one out of my arm and froze several more before
sending me on my way bloody but undaunted. I go back Wed. week to get
stiches out and further assessmant.

Then I had to go right in to get my ears rooto rooted so I can go back this
coming Friday for a hearing test.

To cap the day off, I got two new sets of glasses. clear and shade
changing. I should soon be ready for another 100,000 miles.

Laura and kids went to the lake for a final pre-school outing. I understand
a fine time was had by all, at least all on the outing.

The Bobcat has a hydraulic leak. I hunted for it at length without success.
It did give me a reason to give it a good washing and cleaning. Wayne came
over and helped in the pursuit. No luck.
After he left, I lowered the bucket and tilted the Bobcat back about 30
degrees. When I checked later, sure enough, fluid was running out. So,
tomorrow back on the search for the source of the leak.

My major source of frustration for the week was a leaking tire on the
mower. I must have taken off and replaced the wheel 1/2 dozen times.
Plugged it and filled with goop. Still it leaked. I finally took it off the
rim. Some time previously, some one had put a tube inside the tubeless
tire. All the way inside, stem and all. A tube which was much too
small for the tire. Stay tuned.

Friday night State Street in Bristol was blocked off for an old car cruise
in. I went with Sanford. We walked all up and down while he told me "I used
to have one of those". Finally I asked just how many cars he had owned.
Today, he sent me a list. 50 cars. He said he had only made money on one of
them . I only saw one vehicle I liked.

When I was about 12-13, a local kid, Bill Hughes" got a new one of these. I
would have given my eye teeth for a chance to ride it but under no
circumstances would I have dared.
But, Leonard Bowman did and wrecked it. I wonder what his Dad had to say
about that.

GSH spent a lot of time on peaches. She has had the best crop ever for her
trees. A lot of the early ones were scruffy little things which she
salvaged in bits and pieces. Now, they are getting better but she is about
burned out.

Driving continued with Stephen, all but Benj will recognize this picture.

Speaking of Benj he came by to eat a hearty meal.

GSH and I went over to check on D & D. Actually, I checked on Roscoe (old
and bony) and the birds.

I have heard rumors, (unfounded perhaps) that some people were at fun
places doing fun things.
