March 16, 2025 · Sunday

Lest We Forget

17 photographs·426 words

Almost 100 Mondays at 8AM. This young lady is Kylie, easy for me to
remember. She has 16 year old and two under 6. I asked her to fill me up
with hot, young blood.

Folks around here never talk about dying. They say "if something happens
to". In the unlikely event that something happens to me, I have
been trying to get a lot of loose ends tied up. One long standing issue has
been getting the stone faced chimneys waterproofed. That task has been
completed.

__

In the good ole days, Summer Camp took care of the smoke house clutter. No
more.

The tools in good shape got hung back on the walls. The ones in need of M &
R were brought down for inspection and TLC.

Below are the ones processed this week. If any of the new home owners have
a need and think they have operating skill, they need to put in a request
to Diane.

The crawler crew actually turned the corner this week. At least on one
side.

Ephraim Place is a spot on top of Unaka Mountain overlooking the
Nolichucky River across from Lost Cove. I have been there a number of
times. Bob Harvey mentioned it during our last visit . I could not find any
information on how the place came to that name, we went back so I could
ask him. For the first time in all these years I have known Bob, he said "I
don't know".

Diane recently sent out a shopping list for our travel mementoes. Beth came
by looking for artifacts from our trip to Ecuador and Bolivia.

Mike has been sending me copies of William Parks business records going
back to Civil war days. All the copies are being entered in the family
record file. As part of clearing out his Mom's place, he and Gary picked
out some books of interest for Diane and me.

Farmer Lisa and Ella delivered.

Patricia arranged to buy this property. Worked with Alan to build this
house. Planted trees and flowers. Everything inside and outside brings back
good memories.

I planted the ramps ! Well, actually, for the last couple of years, Jim
Foster has supplied the ramps and Diane has planted the root nubs.

Saturday, we went to Asheviile to visit the rich and famous arm of the
family.
