November 30, 2014 · Sunday

Being Thankful

9 photographs·665 words

We all made it through the year.
In the new digital age, GSH and I can keep up with most of the family and
many of their adventures.

It was a great Thanksgiving. Although we did not have any event here at the
house, we did get invited to Laura's and got reports on most of the other
family members. Then Saturday Anita had another event. I was not quite sure
what we were celebrating but we did a good job..

Since I leave the picture taking in others home to others, (mostly) no
Thanksgiving pictures included but picture us being very thankful

Nothing new on my soon to be homeless sister. Herb did get the biopsy
results from our trip to Duke testing. He is continuing the HPO2 treatment
with healing progress and will be deciding by and by what other actions to
take.

Everyone knows we got the LBT running. Best guess is it had been about 34
years. It has a spin on oil filter. I thought we replaced it when Benj was
here but must have been mistaken. Anyway, when the engine started, the
gasket seal on the filter blew out spraying oil in a radial direction.
Since I was ripping down the drive with no brakes I went on down, made a
sweeping uturn and brought it back up.

It is now back in the warm and dry ready to resume where I left off on fit
and finish. At least we know it will run when needed.

Speaking of warm and dry, Charlie came home for the celebration. He came
over Friday to help me do what I do. We split wood. Brought it up and
Charlie stacked. It was really not a very high stack this time.

Wayne brought the '63 down a while back so I could scope out the project.
He parked it under the canopy. Now, the snow season is upon us so time to
move the car and take down the canopy.(Also time to put hardtop on the Miata
)

Charlie took the truck with dolly attached and left here Sunday. Plan was
to relocate the Spitfire to home in Va. No report yet.

It is sort of a tight fit in the shop with two but I mainly want to sand on
the '63 to get a better feel for the metal work required. Some time back
the truck was leaking grease on a rear axle seal. I decided to check it
before sending Charlie off into the sunrise. Looked ok. Hope he made it.

Looking back, I find more cat pictures on file than anything or anyone
else. But, get more requests for dog pictures. I go to breakfast early Sat
morning with some GOBs. On the way home, I stop at McD's and get a country
ham biscuit and a parfait for GSH. All the animals await my arrival.

When Laura presented us with the cat, she warned me it would find the most
comfortable place in the house to rest. GSH has undertaken to recover the
seats for the LBT. The cat is helping and checking comfort level.

Tuesday was AT work day. Not too cold but pretty windy on the Beauty Spot
where we were working. A small group with the mission to lay out a
relocation on some steep parts of the trail. Since I preferred to work,
Carl sent a few of us off to remove "garlands". Not our relatives, a
different kind of garland of which I was unaware.
Turns out where the trail goes along the side of a hill, it will gradually
get moved down to create a sag between trees. Then it looks like a garland
of flowers. I took an after picture, Look close and you can see where the
old trail had sagged down.
I also took a picture from where I was sitting beside the trail just for
benefit of the family flatlanders.

All in all, a fine ,normal, week.
