Surprise !

15 photographs·310 words

Guess where I woke up Monday morning.Pint of blood, two of Platelets.
Thanks donors !

This picture reminded me of the time long ago when I did something to
offend Hazel. She chased me with a broom. I climbed the hemlock tree in
front of house. So, Herb offered to throw the broom at me. It missed, came
down and hit her. She cried, I laughed.

Pretty good harvest this year makes me miss Patricia. The apple trees were
hers.

In Workington the ex-pat wives often went to Mitchells auction house in
Cockermouth. Patrice purchased this piece of furniture there. They
delivered.

Mostly really hot but lovely days. Fine car washing weather.

Our resident landscaping lady has been working wonders. This past
week cutting back bushes .

My role was relocating the truck on demand.

For Summer Campers who remember the old fashion way of unloading the
trailer, we do not have the muscle for that, A long rope across the bed
prior to loading ,a slip knot after loading, tied off to a tree. No strain !

Not a complete crop failure on our 3 tomato plants.

The Studebaker driver door doesn't close easily. We spent the better part
of one day getting the seat moved and attached much more security than
before.We then found it was still not quite correct. The door panel
upholstery is too thick. Stay tuned.

Saturday night, the rains came down and the driveway gravel relocated. When
I came out to go to Perkins for breakfast, I saw the condition , stopped
and sent a note to PBBD. While I was off being pious, she was home
shoveling our ox out of the ditch.
