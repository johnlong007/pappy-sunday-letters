October 25, 2015 · Sunday

Dipsy Doodle

12 photographs·330 words

I have heard it said there are three things belonging to a mountain person
which must be hands off. Wife, boundary markers and his ax. In some order.
I put an ax out for wood splitting during apple butter production. When I
went to round it up and put away afterwards, I found it and my "good" ax.
On the farm we always used a double bidded ax. One edge kept really sharp
and the other less sharp. If there was a chance of hitting something to
dull the ax, The less sharp side was used.
One can glance at the attached picture and see that each blade has been
stuck into the ground. A capital offense.

Sometime back, Beth expressed a desire to make a run of apple butter this
year and proceeded to make it happen. My mother would be proud and pleased.

Not a lot to apple butter production.
Peel apples
Cook apples
Eat apple butter

Oh, some clean up is expected.

In other news, Charlie took the Studebaker for a shake down run. It failed.
A freeze plug came loose and poured out all the anti freeze. It is going to
be very difficult to replace due to location and a welded exhaust system.
Stay tuned.

I went to Vanderbilt with Herb. It appears he is going to wait six weeks,
have another MRI and decide where to go from there. We stopped by to see
James and Lisa but no one home.

For AT work I helped do an inventory of trail assets from Cloudland to
Carvers Gap. It was interesting work for the first 15 minutes.

Finally got the rig back out to the hillside and got a bit of mowing done.
It will take another 8-10 hours to finish with rain predicted all week.

Friday a group of intrepid hikers walked to the pinnacle at Unicoi. It was
a fine outing.

Supper time here.

Like to hear news from those near and far.
