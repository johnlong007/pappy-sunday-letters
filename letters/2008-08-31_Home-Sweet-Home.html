August 31, 2008 · Sunday

Home Sweet Home

6 photographs·250 words

We flew to Basel Switzerland on August 14 and got back home late on the 29
Th.
The flights both ways were OK. I could see no cut backs in services. In
fact, I think the in-flight food continues to improve. Both ways we went through
CDG in Paris. It was confirmed as the poorest designed and operated airport in
the world.
We went into Basel a couple of days before start of the group bike ride. The
hotel provided and unlimited tram/buss pass for our time there. So, we spent
our days riding and walking around the city and visiting museums.
On Sunday when the group arrived, we rode a bus to a small town nearby then
Monday went on to the town where the tour was to start.
The ride was along a river, mostly on bike paths, some gravel some paved.
Usually we would get a lecture by some local expert once or twice each day. The
banking system, social system, defense, Red Cross, Industry,etc.

Note house and barn are connected.

If anyone is interested, I made about 300 pictures and will be glad to put
on a show.

After we got back, I spent Saturday mowing here.

It is good to be home. The dogs were glad to see me.

Sunday PM we assembled at D & Ds for an event.

Anita has taught Meredith to learn by osmosis.
