Why would anyone want to go to Holland to see tulips?

Can you identify this tree?

For those who need to learn about the birds and the bees.

Another sign of Spring is the potatoes are planted. But, nothing more.
Maybe next week.

Another good event was doing all the routine maintenance work on the
truck. I kept waiting for willing workers looking to learn auto
maintenance but decided that was not working at all.
Oil, filters, grease job and tire rotation.

For AT work, we went to the top of Pond Mountain and cut locust trees
to use for siderails and water bars. Since Pond Mtn. is in a wilderness
area, no power tools are allowed. I thought I was through with cross
cut saws a long time back. But, I was not. None of these folks knew how
to use one. Some thought they did.

On the race car report, I took Dwight down Friday so they could
practice Friday night and compete Saturday night. Dwight stopped by
tonight and gave me a report. Something about hope springs eternal.
Anyway, here is a picture of the race car for next season.

I understand they did have a successful sojurn to the track in the
long, long rig and used it to stay Friday night on the grounds.

Here in River City, mowing has been started, but before that, brush
collecting started. It is hard to get good help and poor help comes at
a high cost.

Dp-Gooder buddy Dave came by and talked a few hours one day. He is not
doing well at all.
