> Time for an update. I haven't done one all summer and it is really very
> overdue. Way too much stuff has happened to me over the summer to fit in
> one update but I will do my best. Sorry if you get left out.
>
> This summer I worked at Eastman doing Quality control testing in the
> Cellulose Esters lab. It sounds harder than it is. My job was to make
> little bottles of plastic and test them for color and haze percentages and
> viscosities. I learned a few things, chiefly that I did not want to be
> doing that for the rest of my life. It is a terrific summer job but I'd
> like something more intellectually stimulating (and something that doesn't
> require me to work nights!) My coworkers seemed fond of me and I liked most
> of them. They had a feast on my last day but I suspect that I was a
> convenient excuse for feasting. I earned a bit of money and work experience
> and I'm very glad I did it, even though it made my summer fly by.
>
> School has started back up again. I have been off since December and I'm
> definitely a bit rusty. I'm taking Statics, Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics
> 1, Figure Drawing, and Business Ethics. It's going to be a very busy
> semester but I feel like I can handle it. I'm also participating in my
> service sorority, Omega Phi Alpha, and mentoring HS students for one of my
> scholarships.
>
> I moved in last Saturday and have nested in my room, with the help of Aunt
> Linda and Cousin Sarah. I am trying to convince Sarah to go to Auburn but
> am not sure if it's going to work. Everything is perfectly organized now
> and I'm afraid it's all downhill from here. I am rooming with two of my
> roommates from last semester and one other girl. I like all of them and I
> like living here. Life is good. Rachel is across the hall from me, which
> was unplanned but great. I'm actually typing this on her computer since I
> forgot my laptop charger, along with some other essential items.
>
> Classes started pretty quickly- none of this "sign forms and syllabi for
> the first week" stuff you get in HS. I have homework in everything already,
> and none of my textbooks have arrived yet. I've managed to borrow what I
> need but I'd prefer t o have my own. I have fluids with a professor who
> seems really promising. She's a great lecturer and really clear about what
> she wants from us, although I really need to brush up on my physics. I'm
> taking Statics as an elective ("Sure, it's an elective, but none of our
> students have ever taken it"- my advisor) and it is all physics. It really
> doesn't look that hard, however, and that professor does a good job and
> seems easygoing. I'm in a class with a bunch of Aerospace and Materials
> people. He does videos in addition to his lectures. I like videos because
> you can watch them for as long as you like, although this is difficult if
> you forgot your laptop charger. Thermodynamics is taught by a guy who is
> about 80 years old and teaches as a hobby. He had some lucrative boiler
> technology patent, apparently, and donates his salary to the university.
