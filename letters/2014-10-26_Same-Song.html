October 26, 2014 · Sunday

Same Song

14 photographs·465 words

Had a four month "no pill" test going to see if I can keep cholesterol down
w/o pills. I cannot, back on the pills.

Herb started hyperbaric O2 treatment Friday. Scheduled to go on for four
weeks. Should help heal the wounds from the radiation treatment.

Lil' sister called. She has sold her house and must be out in 60 days.
Looking at some nice sites under the interstate bridges I believe. If
anyone has a spare basement room they can contact her directly.

We finally finished the bridge and did the touch-up work. South bounders
are coming through now. Many came through while we were working . Most
stopped to admire the bridge. The workers slid a big rock down into thr
trail just past the bridge then called me to come break it down to a size
they could move. Finally found something I can do well: Bust rocks. I took
a picture after it was reduced by over 1/2 and they had rolled remainder
off the trail.

Katy might like to know that this year we have bumper persimmon crop. The
Turkeys like them and are keeping all cleaned up from the ground. I can
attest that plucking direct from the tree is not a good idea.

City schools were in break this week . During his break, Wayne came over
and installed new shocks on the VW. We finally gave up on finding Bob's
errant spring compressor and purchased a new one. So, anyone installing
shocks should contact Wayne to determine rental rates.

It got cold one evening. so for the first time this year, we had a fire in
the fireplace. Have not yet turned the heat on in our new A/C system but
expect it will not be too long now.

Although it was cold, we did not get frost so I did not pluck the peppers.
I hear Christopher has a pepper patch so I took a picture in order to
compare crops.

We rolled the LBT out and tried to get it running. It has spark and fuel
but hit not a lick. Held hand over carb inlet while cranking engine. Could
feel it pulsing a bit but no suction. Rolled it back in and will pull the
valve covers to see what is not going on. Stay tuned. Since I thought the
LBT truck was under control it was time to start another project. "63 fired
right up and Wayne moved it down here. I intend to sand all the rusty areas
then decide how to get repair work done. Stay tuned.

With the help of GSH, we moved the JD into Winter hibernation. With the
BobCat, I moved the MM into a warm, dry Winter slot. In appreciation, I
made Sunday dinner.

Anyone out there?
