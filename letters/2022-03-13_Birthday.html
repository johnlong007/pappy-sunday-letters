March 13, 2022 · Sunday

Birthday

9 photographs·199 words

Now, we are the same age ,again.

One week ago today it was warm weather for taking pets for a walk.

Yesterday, it was rather cold.

About a month back Dr. Sago zapped several sites . One on my lip kept
coming back after she zapped it so I went in Wed. for that one and several
others. She zapped it again and said come back in a month and if not
stopped she will grub it.

Of and on all week PBBD has been working on the '37. Trying to make a
beautiful body.

Today, we got a visit from EJ and her parents.

I have been struggling with trying to open the rear door on the 65 Imperial
. Dragged Andrew into the effort today. No success. Rachel suggested more
WD 40 and a bigger hammer.

I have spent a lot of time on this turkey. Herb thought it was a basket
case and 6 years out in the weather has not helped it any at all. About
time to decide what next.

The door lock.

Far away places.
