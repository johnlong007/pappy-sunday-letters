March 6, 2022 · Sunday

Oops !

12 photographs·377 words

I continued to fiddle with the Imperial this week. I decided to try getting
the engine to turn by engaging the starter. The starter motor would run
but not engage the ring gear. So, I pulled, cleaned and reinstalled the
starter. A five band aid operation.

I have not yet tried the newly cleaned starter. But, I do have a rig set up
on the crank pulley bolt to apply a preload on the system. Stay tuned.

Along the same line, I asked Mark Coates to come by and see if he could
open the car doors. He said he would bring his burglar tools and come back.
While here, he showed me a picture of the 55 newly placed in the Va.Museum
of Transportation. Maybe somebody can get by to check it out.

LLLL has been looking to put us in a new car.She sent me down to the dealer
to get a price on a new one with trade in. Sticker shock.

These pictures are stacks of lumber. Pine, Oak and Cherry.

This picture is a pile of slabs.

Slabs are cut up and used for a pleasant fire in a fireplace.

Slabs are also used in the kitchen for cook stove fuel. There are several
steps involved between a pile of green slabs and a woodbox on the back
porch filled with dry cut to size cook stove firewood. As the youngest, all
chores migrated to me ,and stayed there.Keeping the woodbox filled was one
task. There were others I disliked much more. Milking.

My mother had a top of the line, wood burning cook stove. I purchased one
just like it some years back. It has damper controls, two warmer
compartments.four eyes, and oven and a tank to heat water for Saturday
night bath.
I have been waiting all this time for some family members to need it for
their kitchen.

Sawmill actually in operation.

Cherry I believe, 5 or 6 $ per board foot.

Retaining basin water has gone down faster than I expected this time.

Not only do our children like each other, Our grandchildren do also.
