It is obvious to me that the smartest thing I ever did was latch onto this
one . In light of other things happening this week, others might agree !
Without her, people and events would have been different.

I will leave reporting on other significant events to others.

Birthday presence !

We are getting close to enough wood for next Winter's needs. Probably one
more section will do it.

Because of the cold snap, we had to dig into the strategic wood reserve.
The WW informes me that dry Oak is much tougher to split than green.

Struggle with 55 brake issues continues.Wayne more or less agreed to see if
he could get the PB booster working. A rebuilt one is available for $800.
Stay tuned.

Garden work continues. The radishes are peeking above ground. Beets and
turnips planted.

Two or three years back this was a row of ramp sets. The ramps do seem to
be alive and spreading. Slowly.

Pear tree loaded with blooms. Not sure what this cold spell will do. The
Cherry tree is dead.

The drain lines are keeping runoff level contained. PBBD overseeded the
grass part. It does seem we have a low spot which will require a bit of
fill.
