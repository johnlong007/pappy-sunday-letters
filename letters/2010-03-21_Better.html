March 21, 2010 · Sunday

Better

7 photographs·200 words

After being cooped up for two weeks, I went out on AT work twice this week.

No progress at all on the car. It is ready for painting. I am in hopes Herb
will be able to get into the new paint booth at the voc. school before
long. If not, we think Dwight can pull rank on Pat to get him to paint it.
I did go back to the junk yard on Cripple Creek to check on a windshield. I
believe the windshield he has (in a '57) would fit. But, it has small
scraches and I do nor want to start that way.I did find:


On the home front things went along. Indoors and out. (Tax time in
Tennessee!)

Meredith has not driven a manual shift car for so long I was afraid she had
foegotten how, so, we we had a refresher class. I am happy to report she
had no problem at all. Note the unerring aim.

We have a lot of deer about, A few turkeys wandering around (I assume
nesting females) but, like the fingers, I will only include pictures upon
request.

As all know, Aunt G died. We will all miss her.
