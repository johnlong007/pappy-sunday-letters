Subject: Two things for Kingsport Community Band
To: Kingsport Community Band Members

Just a couple quick comments for the band. First, tomorrow evening’s
rehearsal is our LAST ONE before our December 3 performance at Baysmont, so
please try your best to be there. (Next Thursday is Thanksgiving Day, and
we never rehearse on Thanksgiving.) Scot will finalize the lineup at this
rehearsal, and we may even sight read O Holy Night if we’ve got it in our
library or Gate City’s.

Second, if you are a current, active band member, you should have received
an e-newsletter from our NEW BAND WEBSITE!!!!!! If you’re an active member
and you didn’t get it, add KingsportBand@gmail.com to your safe senders
list, and you should get future ones. But also please send me an email (to
wendylick@gmail.com or KingsportBand@gmail.com) so I can make sure I’ve got
you in the site’s contact list. I may have missed a few.

The new website is at http://kingsportband.wix.com/kcband. Check it out,
and share it with your friends! If you have a Facebook account, “like” our
website and ask your Facebook friends to do the same. Let’s get that
number of likes up, and businesses will be more amenable to sponsoring us
financially and appearing on our “Sponsors” page.

I welcome any suggestions to improve the website; some of your suggestions
may even be within my meager ability to implement. J

Wendy Lick (horn)

Secretary, Kingsport Community Band

wendylick@gmail.com
