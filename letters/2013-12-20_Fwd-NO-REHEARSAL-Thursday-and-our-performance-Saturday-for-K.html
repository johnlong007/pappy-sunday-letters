To: Kingsport Community Band members

There will be NO REHEARSAL this Thursday, December 19, because Scot has to
be in Virginia and can’t bilocate. So the next time we see each other will
be THIS SATURDAY, December 21, at the performance in the mall.

Saturday morning, *December 21*: at the Kingsport Town Center (the business
entity formerly known as the Fort Henry Mall).

*Time:* Downbeat at 11:00 am, arrive by *10:40 AM*.

*Dress: *Typically we wear dark colored pants with white shirts, but this
time dress Christmas-y. Green, red, silver, gold, and sometimes purple are
thought of as Christmas colors.

*Directions* The mall is at the intersection of Fort Henry Drive (route 36)
and Memorial Boulevard. The closest entrance is the one between JC Penney
and Belk in the upper parking lot, sort of facing toward Memorial Blvd. We
will be near Hibbett Sports. The mall has hand trucks, if you need wheels
to get your heavy instrument to the performance location.

*Other: Bring your own stand if you have one!!! *

Here is a link to a map of the mall.

http://www.kingsporttowncenter.com/pages/main/documents/kingsport3a.pdf

See you there!
