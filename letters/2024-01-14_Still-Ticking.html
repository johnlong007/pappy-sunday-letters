January 14, 2024 · Sunday

Still Ticking

13 photographs·266 words

Tomorrow, I may be old but not today!

Monday starts off with a visit to the cancer center for lab work. Usually
not many there when I arrive.. I had a platelet infusion scheduled but
after getting the lab result I got a good, serious, don't get sick and
don't bleed lecture.

Business as usual on Ellis Road. Serious cleaning of parts underway.

Wayne often comes up Wed. to give us a hand. This week, they actually put
some of the pickup body back together. Progress ! Stay tuned.

In less joyous news, we found the exhaust manifold had one bolt hole tab
broken. Not a bit uncommon when over tightening while trying to stop an
exhaust leak rather than do it right.
Fortunately, we have a spare in inventory. Unfortunately, it will require a
lot of prep work.

Prep work started.

With cold weather predicted, we decided to restock the firewood. Running
the fire place and the shop stove digs into inventory.

The truck is still out of service.

The corner has been turned on repairing the leaky water supply line at RR
estate.

Visitors from the frozen north and from the wild West were in ET for a
visit. They came by just to see me. At least they acted that way I was
pleased to see them. The family continues to grow!!

A test !!
