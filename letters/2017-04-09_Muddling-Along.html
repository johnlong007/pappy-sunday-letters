April 9, 2017 · Sunday

Muddling Along

12 photographs·317 words

We had NO dr. visits this week.

The mowing season is here. I have worked for 20 years to get this place in
shape so when I became unable to tend it no tending would be required. But
I am not ready to punt yet. It looks so good when I get everything trimmed.

​ Of course, Spring also brings on battles with various insects.

​Most of my non-mowing time was spent working on one of the tractors which
I have in custody. It now becomes clear to me that Herb enjoyed rounding up
and rescuing odd cars, truck and tractors.
For my part, I like bring old cars, trucks and tractors back to life.

Neither of us much cared for the finished products. The satisfaction was
and for me is in the journey. Anyway, one of the tractors I hauled in last
week is now running and parked in the nice, dry shed. It took several tries
to work out the problems of long term non-running and parked outside.
Fortunately, I do have a more or less GSH.

Wayne came by to lend some much needed advice (but no help).

​No AT work this week but we did go to the annual banquet. At this one, the
workers get a chevron showing hours (rounded down) during the previous year.

​Diane took a day of vacation Friday and worked on the '63 seat project.
Looking good so far.

​The home health person continued to drop by to lead rehab sessions.

​And to share in pre-Easter dining prepared by LLLL

​For do-gooder, I sawed the load of slabs for Dave Dunham. Now, all he has
to do is harvest.

​For downers, one of the neighborhood kids with whom I grew up died. Also,
a fellow I worked with in Kodel for many years.
This time I may go visit Charlie to get rejuvenated.

Is it Spring anywhere else?
