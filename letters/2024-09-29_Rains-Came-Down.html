Rains Came Down

10 photographs·316 words

I suppose everyone saw the shots of the people starding on top of the
hospital ? That is it to the right in this picture. The
Sanday Bottom cemeteries is one of the oldest in the region.My GGF was
buried there about 1920. The cemetery was abandoned due to flooding in the
area, I rented some jailbirds , Momma fed them and we worked to clean it up
of trees and brush a few years back. Later, some great minds bought the
property and built the hospital. While we were working, Anita said "leave
that tree: so people can sit under it while visiting." That is the tree you
can see all alone.

About the same time, South Indian Creek was at flood stage. Farmer Lisa
sent me a picture taken from her front porch looking across the lawn, pond
and driveway.

Meanwhile, back at the track.

Not much damage here. One tree down, one leaner and a lot of debree to
collect .

On Monday they usually leave my blood sampling plumbing installed until
test results are posted in case I need a transfusion. Monday I squeaked by
so the nurse ripped out the tubing. They all know me now and like to kid me.

Robert Harvey, an "old" Friday hiking friend, had his 96th birthday by
breaking his knee cap. He is in rehab now . I expect he will soon be out
and about.

Other not so old friends came by. Bob and Anna sherrill with her
daughter Lisa..

The washing machine drain is a continuing problem. I believe it will
require cutting out a section of the walkway and replacing the damaged
section. Stay tuned.

The acorns and chestnuts are plentiful this year. Deer . turkeys and wayne
are our best consumers,
