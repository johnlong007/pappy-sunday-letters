February 7, 2010 · Sunday

All Is Well

6 photographs·287 words

Almost all, Hal is in the hospital. Clearly in the short rows.

Dave is having bad reactions to BP medicine so he is not doing much and the
weather stopped AT work.

The good news is I muddled along on the Studebaker.

My willing worker usually stays outside at the door waiting for me to come
out. He hardly ever comes into the shop. This time, he came in and found a
place to curl up and keep an eye on things.
I put the heather in three times. The first time I built a box to rest it
on. Then I built a frame to hang it on the firewall. Finally, I hung it from
the newly installed cross member. I also installed a piece of firewall
padding from the Summer Camp Alfa dismantling.
GSH has sort of agreed to reupholster seats for the project. I had dug a
couple out of the shed but decided to switch them out .Redoing will still
be required.

I made trip to Erwin to check on aunt G.She was in good form, looked good
and sounded good.

For the first time in a while, we made a trip to Seymour . As I am sure you
will remember, we made many trips to install the wiring in the dragster.
This trip, Dwight and Pat ripped all the wiring out. I am sure this made
perfect sense to them. Stay tuned.
Another continuing drama involves John and an axle CV joint failure. He
brought the cat over one day while school was out. A cold ,wet miserable day.
John and I struggled along until the cal very arrived at which time it came
right apart.
Stay tuned for installation report.
