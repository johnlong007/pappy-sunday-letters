October 21, 2012 · Sunday

Zip

13 photographs·376 words

An exciting week in the lovely hills of East Tn.

The week starting with a bang when I got the opportunity to transport
Vanessa to the airport. She was heading out to Anchorage to rejoin Aaron
reported to already be there waiting with open arms (we assume). I told
Vanessa that I plan to come spend the month of December with them. I
figured when I admit I really want to spend a few days it will be such a
relief that she will be glad to see me.

Anyway, turns out when she started to get on the airplane they told her she
had so much luggage they would have to get a bigger airplane and she should
come back on Tuesday. So, I went back to the airport, collected her and the
luggage and returned her to the starting point on reservoir road. I did
hear later that she was able to get herself, and her luggage, transported
to Alaska.

I did not take to the airport on Tuesday because duty called, that is AT
work. Back to Cloudland, back to splitting rocks. I shall attempt to
include a picture of a large rock split into two haves and later positioned
in the trail as a step.

When we go off, we leave the dogs in the house. When we come home, I come
open the door to let the dogs out and they run to the car to greet GSH. One
day, she was away when I came home. Jr. dog sat and watched the empty car
until I felt sorry for him and lured him back into the house. Clearly, he
is her dog.

Linda and family came visiting Thursday evening. That led to a lot of good
things.

1. Dale got the lift on my tractor working w/o surgery.

2. Finished harvesting peanuts and sweet potatoes.

3. proved that GSH can still bake perfect biscuits.
and !!!

4. LLLL took THE CAR home with her.

On Tuesday, Mary Cunningham went with us to work. Mary is 86 I believe.
At the maintainers dinner Friday night, Bob Miller talked about some of the
most memorable hikes he had been on, Bob is 92.
