March 20, 2016 · Sunday

Pressing On

9 photographs·306 words

It has been a while since I posted a what do GSH's do picture for Katy.

Did not touch the '63 again this week. Did touch the Fire Engine but not
with a magic touch. The drive line part Dwight had machined would not bolt
up. Back to the drawing board.

Got out to work on the AT Thursday. We worked at Temple Hill gap. An old
Forest service road comes in from Chestoa. Some may remember riding with
Clifton in his Jeep all the way to the lookout tower coming in that way. A
trail comes in from near the school at Temple hill. That is the way other
will remember walking in. Now, a Forest service road comes in from near
Spivey Gap. I rode with Joe Morris who took his all wheel drive PU all the
way to the AT.

I finally got the flat front tire on the Case tractor fixed and was able to
get the drive scraped. Then I got the Bobcat out and found it had a flat
tire also. It is easy to jack up using the bucket. I did find that the
wheel assembly is heavy. After removing lug bolts I wrapped hands around
the top of the tire and pulled assembly off hub. It dropped and smashed my
chain sawed fingers. It hurt. Still hurts.

With all this power equipment available, I am thinking of trying again for
a small pond at the upper part of the soccer field. Anyone want to have a
go at that project?

Finally a quiz for Meredith, what time is it ?

The Final service for my brother was Wednesday. I thought his kids did a
really good job planning. The VA service was especially well done . The
young minister from Shallow Ford was perfect. I miss my brother.
