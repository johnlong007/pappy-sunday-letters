Poof !

Day is gone, week is gone, Summer going,

Farmer Lisa lost the wheels on her lawn mower and another mower would not
run. So, like our dentist, we made a house call. While there I noted the
pond needs dregged. She has a backhoe but needs and operator.

We brought both mowers back here. willing workers went into action. Mower
did not.

Oh, moving one of the mowers on her back deck disturbed the resident .

This was medical week for us. Each had six month check up with GP. Told me
I am getting old. PBBD went in with GSH so not sure what he told her.

I went in to see Dr. Sago. . Grubbed one on my arm and zapped several here
and there.

One morning went I went to door to let the dog out I osaw 8 rodents under
the bird feeder and one up the woodpecker post. Seems I remember David
knows how to harvest and serve?

Finally got a picture of the twin fawns. I assume they are getting fat on
mom's milk made from my pepper and tomato plants.

other members of the family.
