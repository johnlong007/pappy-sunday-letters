August 18, 2024 · Sunday

Family, Friends,Faith

13 photographs·272 words

Dave and Pat came by along with his brother Phil and wife Maggie. I have
known them for a long time. They did missionary work in other countries. We
stayed with them while they lived in Singapore. Used their home as a base
to go visit other countries.

Don came by and left a box of tomatoes. No fever blisters yet !

Sanford came by (as usual) on Friday to get breakfast in Elizabethton.

Joe and Anits sponsored dinner and a show one night.

Chemo week. If you have to spend the day looking across the room, you might
as well enjoy the view. Bailey and Beth.

Bailey, classmate with Meredith at DB. Daughter "Tilly" is not yet 2.

Meals on wheels.. Sarah wanted to rummage through posted selections.
Several have been claiming various artifacts. I am quite pleased with how
that process is going.

Wayne replaced the worn rod end. I took advantage of the next bright sunny
day to get caught up on mowing and to renew my genuine "redneck"
credentials.

The washing machine drain clogged causing local flooding. The problem has
not yet been solved or resolved.Plan B is in effect. Stay tuned.

Benj was in town. We went to visit and watch children play on the floor.

Later, another GGC arrived.

Ever see anyone sitting down while operating a chainsaw?

After months of prep work, some of the 8N implements were dispatched to
their new owner.

Dinner and a show.(For some,not all.
