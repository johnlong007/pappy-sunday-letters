April 18, 2010 · Sunday

Continuing On

5 photographs·204 words

Happy birthday to Dwight. Working on a race car is a real father son
project. It requires a lot of serious pondering.

The other big event for the week has been the prom dress.

Work has continued on the car. Dwight painted all the pillars and posts so
we could install the glass and all the seals etc. Then Herb brought the voc
school instructor over to look it over. He said better to paint it first.
I am going to deliver it to him on April 29.
Before then, I hope we can get it to running good, exhaust system installed
and make sure all the glass and trim are ready to fit with no problems. I
am still hoping to have it looking like the picture on June 1.

Tuesday was AT work day. It just so happens that we hiked through a ramp
patch on the way out.

We also went on the Friday Hike. It was a wildflower outing up the Doe
river gorge to the old tress le.

Another do-gooder event was being nice to Dave Dunham. He came over here to
learn to drive the Bobcat. While here, I let him help cut wild cherry
trees for Anita.
