March 16, 2008 · Sunday

And again

7 photographs·234 words

The eyes are doing fine, I can see better which I appreciate while reading.
The stitches are to come out early tomorrow morning.
Dave and I cut downed trees on 8 miles of trail while Bruce and Bill cleaned
up trash which red neck locals throw on the trail near Elk Park.
I combined my trip to Erwin to visit Herb with watching Benj play baseball
at Erwin. He pitched two inning and did it very well.
Big event was getting the Mack running after a long hibernation. Note it is
my graciously submissive helpmate who is doing the honors.
For outside work, I kept moving dirt on the soccer field. I thought it was
pretty close until the Saturday downpours when I could see how far I have to go
to get it level. I am going to let it dry for a few days before resuming.
In the meantime, I am working on the little crawler. I have resolved not to
take it all apart and spread pieces everywhere. I intend to take one item
apart, clean, paint the parts which will be hidden from view and reassemble as I
go. Witness the seat mounting support.
For my critter report, A happily cohabiting Carolina Wren couple are setting
up housekeeping in Allison Bluebird house just outside my window.
