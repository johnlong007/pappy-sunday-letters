A reminder...please be there for tomorrow morning's performance at the mall,
playing for the walk for St Jude's Hospital! This is a highly visible and
important public appearance for the band, and as Scot reminded us at last
night's rehearsal, St Jude's is an enormously worthy organization that has
helped seriously sick kids at his school and many others from our region.
You'll be doing your part to help them keep helping kids.

Details are repeated below. The only new thing I heard last night was that
since it's so early, all mall entrances might not be open yet when you arrive.
If you can't get in at the most convenient entrance, someone mentioned that the
one near Picadilly should work. I have a message in with Amy Boyles to ask her
what the best entrance will be, especially for those with large instruments or
walking difficulties. I'll send another email later today if I hear back from
her.

Oh, another new thing, we MIGHT repeat A Christmas Festival after we've run
through the whole program, but Scot will ASK us before deciding whether we will
play it the second time.

Bottom line, be at the Fort Henry Mall (Kingsport Town Center), lower level of
Belk Court (in front of the inside entrance to Belk's) by 8:45. We'll warm up
then, with downbeat at 9:00. Bring yourself, your instrument, your music
folder, and a stand if you have one. And don't forget your pre-Christmas cheer!

Wendy

For our November 19 performance during the walk for St Jude's...

Location: We'll be on the lower level of the Kingsport Town Center (formerly the
Fort Henry Mall) near Belk, downbeat at 9:00 am. Try to be there by 8:45.

What to wear: Dark pants, light colored top. You can wear a sweater or jacket
if you think you might be cold. We'll be inside.

What to bring: a folding music stand if you have one. (Scot has extras if you
don't have one.) And your instrument of course.

Also please bring your music folder with you after this Thursday's rehearsal, so
one person won't have to transport so many folders from the band room Saturday.

Following is the program. We may repeat some of them to fill the time (~45
minutes), but we won't repeat the first two.

* It Came Upon A Midnight Clear
* A Christmas Festival
* A Holly Jolly Christmas
* Angels We Have Heard on High
* Christmas Pops Medley
* Christmas Folio-Pack (omit the last piece)
* Sleigh Ride

On another subject, for those like me, who intend to pay the $50 annual dues but
haven't done it yet, please pay as soon as you can. We're blessed with a nice
band room to practice in and many decades' accumulation of sheet music, but we
still have expenses, and the dues are what we use to meet those expenses. Once
again, the dues aren't mandatory, and no questions asked if you don't pay dues,
but if you can pay, please do. Make your check payable to Kingsport Community
Band and give it to our Treasurer, Jim Finney (trombone) or to our President,
Theron "Blick" Blickenstaff (French horn).

Wendy Lick
Secretary
Kingsport Community Band
wlick@eastman.com
