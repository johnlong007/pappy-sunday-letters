Last week I covered the sewing machine in some detail and more or less
promised a progress report. The ebay drive belt from treadle to pulley

and discovered the manual I had downloaded and printed was not for this
machine. Tired again and have printed out manual for a "66". Expert
seamstress to return and try again on check out. Stay tuned (again).

Finally gave up om PBBD installing the seat back panels and did it myself.

and easier on passenger seat.

forward to blood and gore.

Did start the car and let it run a good while. Mildly encouraged as smoking
was not excessive,

I am getting close to putting car on the road again (that could be a song),
got to looking at rear bumper and decided I should dig through inventory
for one with bumper guards. The guards were optional and I did not order
with the car. However, it now has a front bumper with guards.
I brought a bumper down cleaned and straightened in order to decide.
Decided I do not like as it is somewhat rusty. Could take the guards off
and put on original bumper but that requires drilling two holes in the
bumper. Open to opinions. Original bumper is shown in last picture.

Cypress siding arrived for the pool room project.

As you may remember, last year the deer ate the strawberry vines just
before harvest time. This year we left them covered all Winter. Looked like
time to weed and reposition cover. When one battles mother nature are
mother natures creatures one frequently loses. Try one more time.

Dave Edwards, out rich and famous friend, has the '65 Plymouth I let his

was damaged. Rest looks really good.
I loaned the car to him for his 40 year high school reunion. He wants to
take it again next time.
