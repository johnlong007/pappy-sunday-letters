November 12, 2017 · Sunday

Better

15 photographs·354 words

PBBD told Dr. Sago I had a live one so she had me come in for scraping and
burning along with sliceing and diceing. The live one was not, it was just
and agitated place. But, she found several others one semi-serious.
I am pretty sure it is not going to be a skin cancer which does me in, she
will not let that happen again.

I also went in to eye place to get vertical acuity checked. Now they seen
to Medicare to get approval and notice to GP to suspend blood thinner. If
both fall into place will get eyes opened on Dec.15 I believe. Did not hit
me up for another $40 so I did not get to use my prepared remarks. Rats.

Dave Edwards came over one day and helped me move sawed wood which was
stacked in barn beside the BBT up onto the tier poles. It is the wood I
retrieved from Herb's place. Anita is checking to see if she can find a use
for it, maybe Mike.

The next day, I went back to Dave's place to finish the brake job on the
'65. We also installed shock absorbers. Not sure what is next.

Back on the ranch, worked at hulling a lot of black and white walnuts and
spread them out to dry.

I searching for walnuts we found a few pecans. First time ever.

​
The leaf blowing continued

AT was a tough work day. Back to Carver's Gap to move gravel back up the
mountain. It was cold, windy, wet and foggy.

​
Saturday Charlene Stonecipher had a birthday party for George Reid.
They have no children, about 25 acres, 7 horses and other items.

leaking. So, we fabricated a lid. Now to get it installed.
