November 24, 2024 · Sunday

Second Try

17 photographs·396 words

We went to Lindas' for a Thanksgiving dinner, a week early. I have been
having plenty of opportunities to ponder the many things for which I am
thankful. Perhaps I will document some before the season ends this week.

I think it is all built on the time, place, family culture into which I was
born.

Squeaked by Monday with no blood added. The usual don't get sick, don't
bleed delivered with even more than usual conviction. Guess what , my nose
started to bleed. Just a bit. I declined other choices and my ever ready
home health person lept into action.

We dragged the cultivator inside and began to dismantle it. I think
I probably spent more full days walking behind a horse and a 5 foot
cultivator than any other single farm activity.I was doing that by the time
I was 12. After my father bought the 8N ,herb and I went together and
bought a cultivator for the tractor.

After disassembly, I spent a full day wire brush cleaning the metal parts.
The single tree wood got sanded.

The resident artist started painting the parts. My painting skills are
adequate for barns and outhouses.

Wayne came by and helped with cider press work. I believe it will be ready
by apple blossom time.

We were more or less invited to go to D & Ls for an early family
Thanksgiving dinner. Much dithering about when to go and when to come back
home. Then work up to a skif of snow..

We got all loaded up and went on. ,got there early enough for me to get out
and wander around the pasture. Donkeys and goats.

I had Billy Whiskers books when I was a kid. This goat looks just like the
one on the cover of the books.( Look at one on ebay) I feel a bit sorry
for kids now.

The big children amused each other by playing pickleball. Adams' golden
retrievers did their part. Rebeccas' little dog joined right in.

After eating too much, Adam conducted a demonstration using old, new and in
between. arms. He is quite good.

Just in case someone needs a project.
