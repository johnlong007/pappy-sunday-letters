April 26, 2015 · Sunday

Show Bound

6 photographs·354 words

Talk about "poof".

Monday I went in to get an Echo Gram. It was quite interesting as the young
lady running the rig told me all about what she was doing and what she was
looking for. I watched the little valves fluttering and the heart
pulsating. Some faceless person will read the images, pass the word along
to me GM who will, in turn, let me know the results. So, stay tuned.

Ramp season is here. Since I knew I would not be able to harvest ramps on
Thursdays AT work day, I made a special run to Iron Mtn Gap (Meredith knows
it well) with Kim on Tuesday. Took my camera to take a picture of the acres
of ramps, but, alas, when I started to take picture all I got was "charge
battery". Just imagine the sight.Did get a sack of ramps.

Wed-Th-Fri and Sat were all taken up with the Gray old tractor show. The
Blountville club members have handled tractor registration for Gray since
the show began 22 years back. However, it has dwindled down to me with
cameo appearances by one or two other members. Fortunately, I have been
able to get Sanford involved. He knows a lot of the participants and loves
to talk with all of them. So, he worked with me all the way through.

First a picture of my office. See if you can read my list of answers to FAQ.

Today we went to Erwin to take Herb ramps and check on his well being.He is
doing fine but plans for further treatments are not progressing smoothly.

If anyone has stink bug problems I am making progress on entrapment.

Last week I posted a picture of my one out of ten seeds pepper plant. Here
is a picture of Herb's 6 out of six. Katy, want to take one back when you
visit?

Speaking of Katy, some time back she helped me lubricate the Miata speedo
cable. I enlisted a couple of willing workers for the same job . But, no
success. Apparently it takes small nimble fingers .

No work on LBT.
