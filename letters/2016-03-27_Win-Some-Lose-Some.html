March 27, 2016 · Sunday

Win Some/ Lose Some

11 photographs·284 words

Which would you prefer, the winners or the losers?

Got the rear brake drum off the fire engine, got the clutch linkage and
pump shift linkage working. Dwight is still pondering the short splined
shaft and I have not been able to get the brake master cylinder off. It has
rusted solid or I have missed a attachment bolt.

PBBD came over and helped GSH sort out pieces previously cut to F & I seat
covers in the '63. I did some work on the trunk floor pan but not enough to
be able to install the fuel tank. Stay tuned.

Thursday is regular AT work day. Carl moved it to Wed. this week. I had
appointment with Dr. Sago Thursday. It was a painful trip. First time I had
been in without my brother in a long time. Dr. zapped several, face, ears,
lip, arms and back. The only one to watch is on lip.

After I got home from Dr., we went and walked the section of the trail I
signed us up for . The first one mile or so is our only handicap accessible
section. There is a bench at the end of that part, for there on, it is
through the woods. About a six hour round trip from here if no major
problems are encountered.

Wayne and Jonathan came by to start the '53. It did not start. It was fun
watching a son try to bring up his son In the way he should go.

Today is Easter, no eggs (except my wooden egg) no baskets. I will attach
our pictures. Would be pleased to get pictures from other . (Already got
pictures from one thoughtful grandchild).
