October 3, 2021 · Sunday

Last Dance

6 photographs·212 words

I guess all wedding vows include the "Until death do us part" vow. For the
first 66 years it has been sort of abstract. Now, we have signed up
for home health care which is literally until death parts us.

Since it is open ended, my plan is to keep on going at the highest possible
speed until "the end". I will do my best to get GSH back walking in a few
weeks.

The good folks delivered a wheelchair. GSH is not to put much weight on the
cracked foot and minimum steps until further notice. But, now i can roll
her around to provide close supervision.

I had changed fluid in the tractor transmission a few weeks back. The lift
quit working. Examination revealed water. A lot of water. So drained and
refilled and now have the tractor perked in a dry place

Family members arrived to welcome Sarah in from California. GSH also got
cards, letters ,drawings and candy from various GC and GGC. All appreciated.

Lt. Fuzz was here and proved quite helpful

Autographed , original drawings!

The Bobcat is now parked in the lowest part of the pond with rain projected
this week. Stay tuned.
