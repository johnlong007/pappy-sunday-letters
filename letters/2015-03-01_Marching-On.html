March 1, 2015 · Sunday

Marching On

8 photographs·462 words

Cold and snow bound this week so I tried to continue my hibernation.

No AT work but I did get out long enough to check on Herb at Vanderbilt.
Daughter Lisa took him down Tuesday, operation on Wed. and brought him back
home today.

Dr said cancer was in eye and bone but not in brain. He removed the
affected cheek bone and his eye but was able to leave brain alone. He
built the face back up, put a graft over the entire area and sewed it in
place.

when it became apparent that Lisa and James had the Vanderbilt situation in
hand I headed for home but could not stay ahead of advancing snowstorm so I
stopped over in Cookeville. I had a fine time there ! I found the apartment
OK but no one was home. So, I sent notes to all $) of the grandchildren to
see who, if anyone, would come rescue me.

Maria and "her fish" (her quote) Showed up first. We went out to eat. Ben
(fish) passed his first test with flying colors. Cleaned up not only his
plate but mine and Maria's as well. Then we went back to the apartment
where John and girlfriend appeared and I was interested observer until
sacking out on the couch.

Thursday bright and early John left and soon after I left to walk to the
campus. I set in the cafeteria and drank free coffee until time for Maria's
concert. It was very good and I am glad I got to see and hear it.

Left town without getting to see Adam or Gwen. Maybe next time.

One Thursday while we were assembling to work on the "blue" trail at
Hampton, A reporter from Elizabethton Star showed up and took our picture.
Too bad Benj was not there that day.She was a cute thing only about 40
years younger than the lot of us.

On the home front, I have been hand feeding GSH. Really getting good at the
lunch bit. Mostly I have been doing treadmill then fixing lunch while she
does treadmill.

Anita had here tri annual non'birthday event. It was a fine event and Joe
took many pictures. An important part was Beth, Jake and children were in
town so Paul got to check out the silk purse brought to life by GSH. I told
Beth if she wanted/needed it at her house I would deliver. Stay tuned.

GSH has started on the income tax. The cat has decided to help her.

We had hoped to be able to go see/hear Janet concert but could not get it
worked out. So, next best thing, I will put this in mail and watch live
streamed. Hope some who are there will wave for us!
