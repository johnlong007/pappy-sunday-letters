January 16, 2011 · Sunday

Snow Bound

10 photographs·418 words

It has been cold with several inches of snow on the ground. Fortunately,
after all our good Summer Camp workers (and a home made wood splitter) we
have a good supply of firewood. We have kept a fire in fireplace and in shop
stove so we have been warm and dry.

So, the car project moved to front and center. I spent the week installing
the door vent windows and the door window glass. The biggest problem was JW
had removed everything and all I had was a pile of parts. But, turns out
all (well, almost) all the parts were there and I was eventually able to
piece it all together.

The car is a hardtop with no door post. It has rear quarter windows on each
side which retract and roll up. They retract into a sheet metal housing
which was originally lined with a soft material and a felt strip.GSH has been
busy working on relining the housing.

Once more, I have a pile of parts to work with. Stay tuned.

I suppose everyone is concerned about the animals ? Do not be concerned
about Junior dog. He is also warm and dry.

The outside animals are also well. The dear have saved the flowers in the
go around until they really need them.

The turkeys are interesting. They go to roost and come down from roosting
within a couple of minutes of local sunrise and sunset times.

To go up in the trees, they walk way up the hill then take a running go
downhill before launching into flight. In the morning, they stir around a few
seconds, flex their wings then glide down.

Saturday was my birthday. 75 years completed. Most folks do not get that
far. Ro celebrate, I went with Dwight to Seymour to deliver race car parts
and to admire Pat's new motor home.

Actually, the birthday celebration started Friday night when Anita and
Maria showed up at 11 PM with a cake (with hard icing). Turns out Maria was
going off on an adventure Saturday and wanted to make sure I was not ignored
(this year).

As it worked out, the birthday was well covered on Saturday. After we got
back from Seymour Laura and Meredith showed up with another cake with hard
icing and Anita and John appeared with a giant chicken pot pie. ( i.e. a
large pot pie I assume a normal size chicken was included.)

All in all, plenty to be grateful and thankful about.
