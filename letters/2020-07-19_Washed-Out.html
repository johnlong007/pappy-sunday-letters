Washed Out

7 photographs·375 words

When we moved to Knoxville in 1955, we bought a wringer type washer (I have
one in Ag Bldg. now). Paid $25 as I remember. After Alan was born and GSH
was working full time I did a lot of washing (and drying and diaper
folding).

The machine went with us to Seattle and into our little house where Anita
was born. We must have been the only people with a clothes line. Went I
went out on test flights I could always spot our house by the diapers on
the line.

Then I junked the wringer washer and bought a well used ABC washer.

It went into storage while we were in Huntsville then followed us to New
Orleans and later Texas. Then it started giving trouble and when I came
home from work I heard "the washing machine".
Over time I could tear that thing apart and rebuild with my eyes closed.

Back to TN in 65 we left it behind and bought a new washer. I told GSH I
never want to come home from work and hear "washing machine " again.

In recent times, we have been having to wash about every day. Monday I
loaded the machine, pushed the button and nothing happened. A fault code
showed. F DL. Looked on line and found door lock. Looked on you tube and
found door lock switch, remove and replace. That looked easy.

Removed switch, Wayne fiddled with it and ordered a new one. He then said
there is a long list of other things which can cause that defect code.

Sure enough, there is. None of the items appeared defective and now waiting
for the new switch.

My intent was to finish splitting the wood pile during the week. It is
now done except for a dozen or so pieces I do not want to risk loading by
myself.

About mid week I did enlist GSH to throw the wood on pile as it fell off
the splitter. A big help. It was really hot out in the noon day sun.

Break time.

I have no idea what I am going to do with all that firewood.
