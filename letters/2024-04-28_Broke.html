Broke

13 photographs·387 words

when we went to Urgent Care two week ago they looked at xrays and said
Severe sprain. Don't put weight on it.
Two weeks later when I went back the crack had opened revealing a clear
break.
Orders ,4 weeks with no weight , another three to heal, unless I do further
damage which will require surgery.

Trying to stay busy and active without doing further damage.

My father bought a new 8N in 1952 . I never had to ask parents for money
after that. I did Dad's work then worked for others. Flat rate $10 to plow
and disc a garden. Dad never drove the tractor very much.
Herb took possession after he finished his Service time. I am unsure how
much he used it but he eventually had it restored. BUT ! They painted it
the wrong color.

Fast forward: I purchased an 8N and had it here for several years. Patrice
loved it. I took to Linda while her tractor was out of commission, Passed
on to Beth until Jake went upscale the passed on to Aaron.
Now look ! I love it!

1958

Spring is upon us. PBBD moved the plants out into the light of day.

Another sign of Spring. Can all identify?

Back when the folks at church were offering help, I told him I have Family,
friends and faith , nothing more needed.

All were on duty this week.

One day, we went ramp harvesting with Jim Foster.

Work went well on the '37. Head is now installed.

One evening, Joe and Anita delivered a meal on wheels. Wayne and Lisa were
here to put the ramp back into place.

Another evening Alan and Catherine let us join in singing Happy Birthday.

Remember the giant downed Oak trees from the last few years? All
converted to firewood or knotty pine furniture.

At church this morning, Debby Roberts asked if I had a knee scooter. Her
husband had finished with his. After lunch, She showed up with the scooter.
PBBD took her on a tour of family.

Debby is a Chem E., long ago, I hired her as the first female engineer in
Kodel
