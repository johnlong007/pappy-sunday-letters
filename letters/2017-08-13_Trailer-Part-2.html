August 13, 2017 · Sunday

Trailer-Part 2

12 photographs·365 words

I had one Dr. appt. this week. Heart person. He stamped my ticket for
another six months.

Pretty much the entire week was spent working to make the flat bed, tilt
trailer road legal and road worthy. As best I can tell, all the requisite
lighting is installed and functioning properly. Side lights,

brake lights, running lights and signal lights.I should go out in the dark
and take a picture.

The big effort was removing the non-braking axle.;

Then I had to get it loaded in the truck:

​Along with the recently acquired axle with brakes assemblies.

​Easy to see they are not the same length, I delivered both to local
welding shop with instructions to make the axle with brakes fit the spring
shackles where I removed the axle without brakes. promised ready by
Tuesday. So, stay tuned.

GSH helped with the lights.

​And water sealing the deck. Some will remember having done that job before.

Proving she is multi talented, GSH was back on the sewing machine when not
aiding and abetting. Her complicated machine gave up the ghost but old one
seems to be working fine. About time, all my pants need repair work,

​A requirement for road legal is a battery powered break away unit to
engage electric trailer brakes in the event and trailer comes loose from
towing vehicle. Since that job required connecting 5 color coded wires I
called for help.

​In other news, there is a good crop of apples here this year. Plenty if
person or persons would like to make apple juice . Not a very good picture
but one I made early one morning of a ground hog sitting under the tree in
the circle eating and apple.

​We went to Erwin today to check on the farmers daughter. She is doing well
but needs get certified to operate a back hoe. The musk rats are taking
over her pond. Maybe Dale can take a day off and dredge it for
her.;;Neighbors are working on the supply creek and filling her pond with
sediment (mud).
