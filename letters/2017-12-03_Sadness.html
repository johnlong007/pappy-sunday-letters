December 3, 2017 · Sunday

Sadness

12 photographs·224 words

Our cat died. We had him a bit over four years. I did not realize how
attached I had become until he was gone. Unsure what happened. I knew he
was losing weight and had been hacking and wheezing but did not know it was
serious.

On the home place it was the same ole same ole.

GSH blew leaves a lot
:
​

​
​I gave the cut off saw a coating of rust reformer and moved it out of the
way.

More walnut hulling. But, I am finished with that job. The nutcracker is on
order. I am expecting some will be interested in cracking black walnuts. I
have maybe 50 gallons of hulled nuts. Don says maybe 20% yield so several
gallons of walnut kernels to be harvested.

​AT work was back at Carver's Gap.

​I worked with George to retrieve gravel which had washed off trail and
refurbish trail. Before and after:

​I have been told persons my age and mileage should not be cavorting on the
roof. Fortunately a chimney sweep came by today. Just in time as weather is
expected to turn cold this coming Wed. May have to kindle fire in shop
stove and move indoors.

​Turns out chimney sweep is multi skilled.

A BLTC sandwich for lunch. The last garden tomato of the season. Alas.
