December 28, 2014 · Sunday

Christmas Edition

8 photographs·184 words

Carl scheduled an AT work day Tuesday of this week. It was decided that I
would rather stay home to mop floors and wash windows. So, that is just
what I did.

I did make a couple of trips to Erwin, One to go to lunch with Herb and one
to check in on James and his ever expanding family. Both Herb and James
seem to be pressing bravely onward.

No news from Lil' sister.

I did a fair amount of sanding on the LBT this week. Enough to make my aged
hands hurt. Dwight donated an air compressor to the cause so I should be
able to use the pneumatic tools more this week.

The night before Christmas for the Lil" ole folks at home is not what it
used to be.

However, Santa outdid himself this year.

GSH also prepared a fine Christmas feast.

Some family members were not among those present this year. For their
benefit, I shall attach a few pictures to which they can add their own
captions.

I wonder what Christmas was like in other states and country?
