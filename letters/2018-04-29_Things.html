A full week

Went Poof

Carry over from last week was sawing the tree Mike had cut in the Long hay
field into firewood. We made a good bit of progress on cleaning up there.

​
All I got done on the 63 was completing cleaning the original wheels.

​
One was bent a bit, But, by heating and beating I got it back in shape.

​​

For the last 25 years The Blountville old tractor owners club has handled
registration for the Gray show. For the last 3 years it has been pretty
much Sanford and me. This year we recruited a couple more helpers.

​Each year the event seems to have fewer tractors and more flea market type
vendors.

​Saturday, I went with Meredith to harvest ramps, A good outing but still a
bit early.

​ Sunday, we went to TTU to hear Maria toot her own horn. Also a good
outing, glad we went.

The building project seems to be moving along.
