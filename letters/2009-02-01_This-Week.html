February 1, 2009 · Sunday

This Week

8 photographs·393 words

Everyone will be delighted to see the entire family, more or less, pitching
in on the Cletrac.

Sometime back I got a lead on a new contact for the family project. Sis flew
in Monday. Wed. we drove to Fayetteville NC to meet with a 1/2 first cousin.
Joseph Leonard Buchanan, named after our mutual Grandfather. "Joe" knew our
father well and showed me his infantry div. badge saying it was the same one
our father served in. I also met his daughter and got the name of the person
in their family who is working on the family tree "stuff". Since she is a
descendent of Grandfather Joe's first family, that is the area where the contact
(Jackie) has been working. We have been exchanging notes. By and by, we will
have to get together.

While we were down that way, we spent the night with Beth. It was a real fun
visit, at least
for me. Since it was pouring down rain, we did not go out on the town. But,
Beth fed us well. Then, I got to talk with her about her class work and
planning for her thesis. Jake was hardly mentioned.
Thursday morning, we stopped by to see Dave and Pat. As usual, Pat fed us
well. Probably due to being so well fed, I promised Dave I would come back for
two days "before long" and help him get the '65 Plymouth running.

.
Saturday, I took Gwendolyn (who came over thinking she was going to be a
tractor mechanic) with me to help D & D repair the burned out fence. As you can
see, the Valley Girl also joined in proving that she can indeed learn new
skills. I was quite pleased. Besides, we got the fence repaired in better shape
than before the fire.

Sunday, John came over for the second round of driving lessons. As can be
seen, we did more than drive. We harvested carrots. There were and are plenty if
anyone wants garden fresh carrots.

Donna is in the hospital after having a heart attack Saturday morning. She
is on life support. The girls are going to meet with the doctors tomorrow at
1 PM to decide what course of action to follow.
