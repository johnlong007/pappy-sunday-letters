May 10, 2020 · Sunday

In Seclusion

9 photographs·323 words

Life is about the same for us except for not being able to pop into the
stores anytime and more important, keeping Sunday special.

Friends and family have been great about the store popping and Wayne's zoom
class helps a lot on SS. Our church is reopening next Sunday but old (over
65 Ha!) are invited to stay away.

Made this picture at Perkins this morning. I suggested to our SS teacher
that we could have class at Perkin's.

We went to Erwin one day I had put boom pole on the tractor to load the
logs. Took it off and put Lisa to reinstalling mower. Clearly she is
willing. Also clear to me she needs more practice.

For those who care, this picture is a young Paw Paw tree. It is in full
bloom. I had two but LLLL gave her Mom some heavy duty loppers which she
use to cut one of my trees before I stopped her.

Mose of the week was spent mud wrestling the Oak logs to get them off
Albert's property. All the logs have been extracted. A lot of brush and
firewood left to clean up.

Finally decided to bring in the big guns for the job.

I have been doing a lot of sawing. Some on dirty wood which is really tough
on saw chains. Sanford has a contact to get chains sharpened and is willing
to pick up and deliver.
He is NOT willing to get dirty. No mud wrestling.

This picture is the opening from our property into Albert's. At least a
foot deep mud.

Same place but before I dragged the last two logs through.

This is over in Albert's field looking toward our side. I promised to clean
up all the remains of the tree. That is number one project for coming week.
