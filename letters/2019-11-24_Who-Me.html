November 24, 2019 · Sunday

Who, Me?

9 photographs·361 words

My grandmother Buchanan lived on Bean's Creek in Buladean N.C. We all got
in the '37 Plymouth PU and went to visit on Sunday about once a month as I
remember. Mom, Dad and Hazel in the cab. Three boys in the back. Standing
behind cab are sitting at rear with legs dangling down. Road was gravel
from Unicoi on over Iron mountain. We had to for ford the creek three times.
This picture is Mom and Dad standing in front of their tool etc. storage
building. I suppose you can look at clothes and make a guess at date

This picture is turned about 90 degrees from one above. Looking across road
and creek to the barn . Me ,maybe 10 or 11, Mom and Jimmy. Clearly style
has always been important to me.

Style shows in this picture. We went back to the Long farm and harvested
more firewood. Just wanted Jake, and maybe Gwendolyn to see I really can
split wood the hard way.

But only split into chunks I could load in truck, Then back to the easy
way. GSH operates the valve while I load and unload the wood.

Most of the week was indoor in the shop fiddling with the 37 grill and
hood. I am torn over trying to upgrade the original Grill or use the better
condition reproduction. Stay tuned.

The Oak tree beside ramp started to shed and shed it did.

Less than willing worked always debates with self, in or out.

Today, Wayne and Thomas delivered and installed the '63 fuel tank pick up.
Now, the fuel gage is working. Plan to take car to get a new top installed
as soon as installer is ready.
GSH and I installed the one now on car. It is not the original color
(white) and has a tear.

I have the trim from 3 cars . Plan to select the best to install on the '63
but not until the top is finished. GSH spent shop time polishing the trim.
It is high dollar items.
