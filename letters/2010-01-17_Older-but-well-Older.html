January 17, 2010 · Sunday

Older but, well Older

5 photographs·154 words

A fine week.AT work was fun. No do-gooder work planned Monday, no AT work
scheduled for Tuesday so we cut blowdowns on the AT Monday then cut up a
downed tree for a fried as we were coming home.

In order to check operation of the chainsaw (and to get out of the house
after working on the car all week) my team of willing workers (GSH and Atlas)
sawed up a big log down by the pond.
No car pictures but progress was made.

Quite a number of January birthdays in the family. A couple on the 15th and
one on the 17th.
A party was held to celebrate some of the birthdays.

A smaller, private, cake sampling party was held late in the evening.

Once more, my sister proved she is a person of discriminating taste who is
not only thoughtful but generous. Consider the birthday present she passed
on to me.
