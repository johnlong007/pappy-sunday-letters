November 18, 2018 · Sunday

Testing, Testing 1-2-3

16 photographs·572 words

Did anyone notice I missed sending out a note last Sunday?

A lot of things have happened since then.
Beth added ,divided and multiplied.
Adam got his first choice assignment
Sarah accepted employment offer at ECC
I visited my Lil' Sister
And went to see Dr. Allison !

Most know my mantra has been "clean body and clean teeth". It now appears I
was a bit lacking on the teeth part. That leaves me worried about the other
parts.
Allison seemed quite pleased to determine that my gums and teeth were in
need of M & R. Also, my situation was one of the hurdles she must get over
to become certified. So, she did all the "before" part during this visit
and I am to return for the "after' in a few weeks.
I suppose I should be delighted to be a failure. Stay tuned.

For the benefit of Katy and Ross, Here is a picture of a small snow. A
skiff.

The roof is already in need of at least one more clearing.

I spent a lot of time cleaning around the storage building. The roofers
have been told to get on with the job.

Local inspection team checked my work.

Why would anyone want to leave anywhere else?

Linda and GSH planted the pines along drive on the left. Beth and I planted
a row of Oaks on the right. I later added pines.

We visited Lil'sister in Union City. She is doing well.
She tied getting shots in her aged and aching knee but according to latest
word from Martha has decided to get on with replacement about the first of
the year.
Her little dog is coming right along. Makes me miss "Little Dog" all the
more.

We spent two intense nights with the Bradley's.

On the home front, at the suggestion of John, the mower is now painted Vol
Orange. Actually , the nearest thing I kad in hand was School bus yellow.

I got more brake work done on the '63. After twisting a line in two, in a
very difficult access place, I finally got it all together filled and bled.
Sometime soon expect to take it out and run it some more,

It is not obvious but cat has become more social . In this picture he is
helping me watch the concert in which Maria was playing. We liked it.

Wayne has decided to hang on to the parts car for a spell. I decided a good
place to park it was on the trailer. After much effort, I used to come
along and got it dragged up to front wheels on the trailer. I then decided
to back the trailer further up under the car. My scotches moved and car
rolled off backwards.

It was much easier loading the next day with the help of Wayne. However,
when I towed it around to park out of sight and out of mind, the tractor
ran out of fuel. For all Diesel owners (Gwendolyn) that is not a good thing
to let happen.

Saturday morning Wayne, Diane and Laura were here in various vehicles in
addition to the resident fleet. It is obvious the cat is a cat of
discriminating taste.
