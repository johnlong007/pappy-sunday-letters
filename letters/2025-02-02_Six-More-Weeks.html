Six More Weeks !

13 photographs·507 words

Monday morning ! After 85 Monday mornings in a row, the cancer center
people mostly don't bother to activate the device they just give me a "come
hither" . This is Bailey, she did my first Chemo a long time back. She went
to DB with Meredith and has a young daughter she calls "Tilly".

Start of Chemo week, 12th cycle. For those who are into
statistical quality control, I am now a "rare event".
She is holding someone's donated blood while they check and cross check. I
am humbled and appreciative of all the family (and others) who have and
continue to donate..

As promised this week the lay off plow was to be finished.

It was all homemade so I fit right in by making the cross piece from a
broken axe handle.

Sure enough , Wayne moved it into the waiting area to be mounted on display
on the end of the Ag. building. So far, only the spike tooth harrow is on
display.. When the signs are right, , weather and willing workers all the
completed items are to be mounted for display.

Alan and Catherine after long , intense, struggles have mastered starting
the crawler. Now, they have moved on to correcting the steering problem..
It seems the first step involved driving out a pin which locks the ends of
the track together. Stay tuned.

As the lay off plow moved out, the triple shovel plow moved up. New handles
arrived.. Now, we are waiting for Bob to offer insights and guidance for
next steps. Stay tuned.

Since I was small my mother chastened me, don't wipe your nose on your
sleeve. Patricia picked right up with the same results. Diane took over
with an even more serious urging, "you will make your nose bleed".
I did and it did.
Internet wisdom lists blood hemorrhaging as the most probable cause of
death for my situation. Had Diane not been here to manage me, over my
objections. I would have been a goner.
As it was, we spent the day waiting (patiently) in various medical
facilities. That is a spit cup for blood.

Finally got to see an ex military physician assistant who knew what to do
and how to do it.

So, after two miserable days, Diane removed the balloons and the bleeding
did not rusume. Looks like I am going to witness the resurrection
lilies come back to life.

I have a number of children, GC and GGC (increasing exponentially) . Often
someone will ask if I know all their names. I can
display righteous indignation ! I practice on the Aura frame. So, thanks to
those who make entries I know names of all, some I have not yet met. (but
hope and expect to meet). It also helps me be a bit aware of what our far
flung family members are doing.
Thanks
