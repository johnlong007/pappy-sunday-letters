Mixed Bag

8 photographs·541 words

I cannot tell much change in Herb's condition this week. Lisa had an MRI
made. She will get a copy sent to the experts at Vanderbilt to detemine
what is to be done next.

Cold and snowy all week so no AT work. Just below is a paragraph from the
hiking club site.

Within our 134 miles of Appalachian Trail there are numerous opportunities
to volunteer. This distance has been broken into 36 sections of typically
four miles each. An individual, couple, or even small group of friends will
adopt and care for their piece of the Trail. These ‘Maintainers’ will visit
several times throughout the year to repaint blazes, remove tree branches
and blowdowns blocking the path, haul out trash, clean waterbars, etc. For
more information, please contact Kim Peters at atmaint@tehcc.org.

For the first time since I left ECC (Dec. 27, 1997 ), I have signed up for
an ongoing duty. I have agreed to look after a section of trail North
along the crest of the mountain where the road crosses over into Shady
Valley. I hope and expect many members, perhaps all, of the family will
want to participate as time goes by.

Did I mention the treadmill quit? It did. Very poor timing as we have been
using it daily while cooped up. GSH Fitbit steps have taken a hit. The same
day, the little TV/Tape player quit. It was used to provide diversion while
walking the treadmill. I rolled the treadmill over, open all the covers ,
blew out a couple clouds of fine dust, adjusted belts and it seems to have
come alive, Unfortunately, those more or less same tricks did not work on
the TV/Tape unit. So, I am in the market for a replacement.

LLLL came in Friday night for a visit. We were supposed to run a 5K in
Winchester on Saturday but wimped out. It was fine visit however.

Anita went with us to Erwin today. That worked out as she got to catch up
on life with Lisa and Suzanne. It started snowing after we got home. I
walked on the tradmill while GSH went out for a walk in the falling snow .

Oh, Anita and I have flight tickets to go visit Kenneth and CERN the first
week in March !!!!!

I spent most all week on the '63 . GSH is pondering the upholstery, it is

I have about finished cleaning underneath. Also got the gas tank treated
with the rust reactor.

deer and turkeys.
