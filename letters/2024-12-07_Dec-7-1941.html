Dec 7, 1941

2 photographs·143 words

I was just short of five years old. My mother took me next door to her
parents house where her parents and sisters were all in a state of concern
while the radio was going full blast.

By that time congress was declaring a state of war because Japan warplanes
had attacked Pearl Harbor and the family knew son/brother Jim was there
in the midst of it.

Later it was learned he was actually at Clark Field in the Philippines when
the attack occurred, ~8 hours later there.

It was years before they heard from him.

In 1945 he was returned to the US and checked into Letterman Hospital.
Second from left in the photo below.

Many years later, as part of my work on a pictorial family history project,
I interviewed him in Jacksonville Fl. Attached.
