July 29, 2018 · Sunday

Fast Lane

16 photographs·408 words

As I noted last time, Wayne got back from Wi. with a car and two M'cycles.

All are now safely ensconced in the warm and dry. Looks like room for
another car or two and maybe 3 M'cycles.

I decided there was no point in letting the Studebaker stay outside so we
moved it back into storage. Running fine. Needs to be driven and a few odds
'n ends finished.

The current active projects are still on the front row. I intend to find a
new home for the Ford tractor before long.

For those who like to go out to the Blountville ball park. Seems like some
even played if years past. What could be more traditional. Besides Joe
makes a fine hot dog.

Several know Collins Chew. His wife of 62 years dies 2-3 years back. His
interest in geology put him in contact with a ETSU geology person. She is
from Houston. Previously married but no children. One cousin who lives in
Salt Lake City. Younger.
All these people are some of Collins children.grandchildren and GGC. Both
she and Collins are only children. I have been having a lot of fun advising
her on how to fit into the clan.

While I was on top of Unaka mountain doing AT work Thursday and having 6
peanut butter crackers for lunch. Laura sent this picture to me.

Lunch time, Tim is standing. He said nice things about David , I worked by
myself mostly on the trail leading up to section where Tim and David did
rock work last week.

Friday we went to Erwin. Stopped by to see Aunt Flo and to get some
information about a 3rd cousin in Okla who contacted through 23andme. Flo
has two pieces of furniture made by persons grandfather. I sent a picture
to her.

The cemetery we cleared is growing over again.

Yesterday, I cleared three paths through lower Raspberry patch. Intend to
get on up the hill.

A picture of the bookcase made by persons grandfather. Pappaw had a full
set of Zane Gray books and other western books in it. I about wore them
out. Flo took possession of more items than all others put together.

Resurrection lilies have popped up. We took some from old place on top of
hill across from Jess and Joyce, moved to Shadowtown road then here.

Lisa sent this picture to me. Part of the good part of Farm life.
