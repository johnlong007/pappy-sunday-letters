June 5, 2011 · Sunday

56 and Counting !

9 photographs·244 words

Monday and Friday both involved lawn work for D & D. Progress is being
made but miles to go before I rest.

AT work was fun this week. Started at Cloudland and worked downhill
roughing out relocations all the way to Hughes Gap.

On the home front, I finished planting the front garden. More corn and a
row of beans. There should be plenty of weeding to do during Summer Camp.
Which reminds me.
July 4-8, I would like to know who plans to be here.



Just in case anyone is interested, I usually refuel the truck and the VW
when each has a bit over 600 miles on the tank. This week, Each had 630 miles
showing . It took $50 to fill the VW and $120 to fill the truck. Now I
remember why I cut back on driving the truck!

Home schooling continues here.

I delivered the lonesome travelers to the airport to go meet up with the
advance party in Amsterdam. Apparently there was a slip somewhere as
Allison's Facebook picture had a caption "lost in Amsterdam". The whole world
wonders where they are.

Before she left, GSH finished the carpet installation in the Studebaker. As
soon as I get a load of junk dispatched to China, I will be able to get
the car out of the shop and take it to get the mufflers installed.

Lest anyone should worry about my diet in the absence of a cook.
