August 2, 2015 · Sunday

Strikes Three

7 photographs·397 words

Off and on all week long I fiddled with the power window on the Alfa. I can
make it work by hotwiring at the connector inside the door. I cannot make
it work at the same wires at the switch location. I took the book out to
see local electrical guru this evening and got a tip on where to look next.
Stay tuned.

After a mere 36 years, a tie rod end broke on the case tractor. I figured a
30 minute job to replace it, screw the old one out and a new one in.
Started the job at 10 Saturday morning. Worked right on past 11:30 without
slowing down. Called GSH in about 12 to hold my make shift removal
together. Worked through lunch. Gave up about 2 and took entire tie rod
assembly off. (Epilogue, I stopped by Wayne's house this afternoon to
admire Jonathan's progress on the '53 and Gwendolyn"s progress on the MC.)
While there, Wayne was able to get the broken tie rod end removed.Stay
tuned.

My hearing has gone from bad to worse in fairly recent times. Tonight,
Charlotte was trying to ask me a question. I could not understand a word
she said. Normally, I just nod and smile when people talk and I do not here
but I really wanted to understand what she was saying. Her voice must match
the deadest spot in my loss of hearing frequency range.She repeated three
times and finally Wayne stepped in to save me. Most distressing.

But not all was glum, despair and misery .

Monday we made a run to Erwin to check on Herb and collect the wood he had
downed in his goat pen. There are still some big chunks left for which I
shall need a stronger helper.

I made another run over later in the week to raid his corn and tomato
patch. Various members of the family were appreciative. It seems to me he
is getting stronger by the day.

For AT work we went back to Cross Mountain. Benj went along this time. The
crew he and I were with walked in a couple of miles to install run offs and
water bars on a steep section of trail. A perfect day to be out in the
mountains. But, there is a lot of work left to be done where we were
working.
