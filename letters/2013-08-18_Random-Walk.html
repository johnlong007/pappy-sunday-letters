August 18, 2013 · Sunday

Random Walk

9 photographs·594 words

The week started with an interesting event.
Our insurance provider is BC/BS of TN. We got a call from BC/BS asking
permission to send a doctor to our home to do a health assessment . All in
the name of improving our health, wealth and happiness. All my GOB friends
received the same call. All were deeply skeptical (as were we).

But, we decided to let the doctor come make the house call. It was
interesting. My opinion after the visit was that it is an attempt to try to
reduce fraud and waste by the doctors providing the services (and charging
BC/BS).

The good news is that he did a number of little quickie tests and
measurements. One was a memory assessment. WE BOTH PASSED ! So, knowing
nothing about the accuracy and precision of the test but assuming it is
valid or would being used, it appears that we are withing the "normal"
range for our age and mileage. Who know what tomorrow will bring.

Less exciting Was spending a lot of time trying to find and fix the
hydraulic oil leak on the Bobcat.

Finally traced it back and found on the supply line from the reservoir to
the pump. A low pressure line. It will be very difficult to remove and
repair. So, I coated it with beeswax. It will do fine unless the oil gets
hot. Stay tuned.

School started this week here and there. However, one willing worker is
moving into the ranks of higher education pursuits which will not start
until next week. Even after hearing that Beth had done her undergraduate
work in the same manner she was able to control her enthusiasm.

For the third time this season we got a downpour sufficient to relocate our
driveway gravel.

The only thing different this time was that it occurred between 11:30 and
12 noon. The same time a schedule driven person goes out to shuffle around
the neighborhood. It was surprising to me as the week went by to hear from
quite a few people who had seen my drowned rat performance.

It has been a bumper year for fruits. It would appear that there will be
enough apples to make apple juice if anyone is interested in so doing.
Also, it has been probably the best peach crop ever for GSH and a good crop
of grapes is coming in now.

On Friday, Carl arranged to bring in about 100 ASU students to help with a
trail relocation at backbone rock near Shady Valley. He divided them into
groups of ten each group to do a section about 100 feet long. There is
quite a bit of variation in performance of students from different schools
and withing schools. The group assigned to me this year was the best I have
ever had from any school. 9 girls and one boy. They all worked, stayed
spread out, helped each other and stayed on task. They finished their
section early and moved to help on another section. It was a really good
experience for me.(Not a one had a device , other groups I am told did not
go nearly as well).

For reasons not at all evident, it seemed like starting the little OC-3
crawler would be a good idea.

Note the tow chain. It did not start. Stay tuned.
