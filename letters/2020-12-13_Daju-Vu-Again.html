December 13, 2020 · Sunday

Daju Vu-Again

11 photographs·441 words

I have had several Interactions with Dr.s rIecently. I believe my blood
pressure goes up exponentially with the time I am left in isolation room
after scheduled appointment time. Late wait has been running about one
hour. If mentioned, they act affronted and offer a trite excuse.
My resolve is take a book and not try to solve their problems (or make mine
worse).

The valve replacement process is underway. If you want to know what I heard
ask me. If you want to know what the Dr. said, ask Laura.

I believe valve job will be Feb for at least one night in hospital.

On a much brighter note, Below is a picture of our cats paw. Vets are much
more sensitive to customer needs. I suppose few cats have insurance. $ 200,
well spent.

A bit of work on the '37. While we had it out, rain came and seals leaked
around the windshield. So, installing a MDGB seal is now underway.

For entertainment, I dragged four of the lawnmowers up to determine
condition. I got each to run on Ether with little difficulty. If anyone
needs a mower, come and get it. Otherwise they are bound for crusher with
the next load.
Pretty soon I hope

The shop stove and living room fireplaces are back in service. Since I have
been warned about doing too strenuous activities, I enlisted a WW to help
move a wood supply closer to the house.
I burn misc. stuff in the shop stove.

We stacked it neath the ole Oak tree.
GSH is our photographer. I was pleased to see this picture as I am
standing almost straight.

Fire place wood is well seasoned from the stacks down front. The wood I
have been lugging up the hill. Now stacked just across the drive. We moved
enough for a week or so.

More people at Perkins for breakfast this morning but fewer at church for
the early service. I expect a lot of churches will go bankrupt before this
is over.

No work on Sunday, we went for a walk on the Greenbelt.

Steven is home for end of semester. Wayne brought him up to admire the new
pearl. He is a bit stoic such that I could not tell if he was falling in
love.
No other cars sound like the old Chrysler Hemis. Still in love after all
these years.
Note ! LLLL said David and Dale are getting close on the Studebaker (sister
engine to the '57).
