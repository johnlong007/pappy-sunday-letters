Fully Depreciated

8 photographs·247 words

On Dec. 27, 1997 I retired after 32 years at ECC (5 at BA and 2 at GD). My
last day I got a "Certificate of Depreciation, fully depreciated with no
residua value".

Wow, and after 20 years 2017 was the first year with no trip outside the
US. I am thinking about 2018 resolutions and will probably post next week.
Making public greatly improved odds of accomplishing.

Big event for the week was catching the sick bug, Laid me low fora coupler
of days. Then I passed it on to GSH and it hit her even harder.

After a lot of testing the Dr. proclaimed she was in fine shape but running
a high temperature. Prescribed Tylenol and liquids.

Did I mention Christmas. Santa left GSH things to keep her warm and active.
She especially liked her new earmuffs.

​She also got a tablet with many mentally stimulating game loaded. So far,
I have mastered one.

I got heavy duty work pants which I but to use right away.

​

In other news, Meredith and Conner are now blissfully married and started
on their first 62 years.

Sunday night quite a number of family gathered here for a post Christmas
party. While it is good to see children and grandchildren there are with us
always and can be taken for granted (unless they do not show up!) It is
especially good to see the addition, Anyway a fine event which is to be
continued tomorrow.
