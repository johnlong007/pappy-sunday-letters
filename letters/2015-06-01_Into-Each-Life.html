June 1, 2015 · Monday

Into Each Life

11 photographs·502 words

Monday was Memorial Day. As everyone knows, that was Decoration Day many
years ago. On Decoration Day, people of the community would gather, do M &
R on the church/community cemetery then on Sunday have church and dinner on
the ground.
So, on Monday I planned to go to Erwin, get Herb, and go walk around the
community cemetery where many of our relatives were buried. But, instead I
went to visit him in the hospital.
He made a trip to Lowe's in JC while driving his little truck. Had a spell
with heart and had an accident. Jumping ahead, on Friday the doctor did a
procedure to rewire his heart electrical system and sent him home
Saturday.Automatic driving license suspension for six months.His dog was
glad to see him.

Since there was no big event going on here or at church, we had our own
Memorial Day celebration at home.

To occupy my idle hands, I procured a load of barn yard fertilizer from a
friend. Leaving me with tasks I thought had been left on the far, We used
the load to put on newly renovated strawberries as well as BBs .

For some time, the truck has been slow starting after being left parked for
a spell. The last time that happened, it was eventually found that a
flexible fuel return line was cracked. So, (almost) willing worker was
recruited to swap out the line. It did not resolve the issue so still slow
starting. One important advance however, I did learn the trick to starting
the truck while drifting. After all these years.

A friend from church asked to go with him on an AT hike. So Friday Bob
Harvey (85), Collins Chew(83) GSH and I went in and out of Laurel Fork
gorge. About 8-9 mile round trip with a lot of steps to climb. We all made
it.

Another day, on my way to visit Herb I dropped a load of intrepid hikers at
Indian Grave Gap to head them toward Maine.

It appears they gave up as I saw some of them at the Friday night P & G
event. Not quite sure who the person with both cap and skin art is.

To cap things off, the water system failed, this time, Wayne the plumber
arrived to discover the pressure regulator had failed. But, correcting that
problem caused the kitchen sink faucet to cease functioning. Correcting
that problem caused a short term water spout dousing all concerns. Note wet
spot on ceiling.i

Maria has borrowed "The Miata" in order to go cavorting on the beach with
other cavorters. Kenneth should be at Cern by now starting a new adventure.
John is closer to home but still turning a new page to start a new
chapter.Katy is running up and down mountains while Beth and Aaron each
have children at the perfect age.Christopher has gone to California w/o his
banjo, no report on that trip.Probably things are going on in WI. I like
our family.
