January 15, 2012 · Sunday

Older and ? Older !

6 photographs·403 words

Dave has been gone to Ark, to the Lyme clinic most of this week so my do-gooder efforts were minimal. But, I did go to our GP on Monday for a routine 1,000 mile check up. Turns out I am the one they use to define average.
The St. Thomas people asked me to come back for a battery of tests as a follow up on their work. I declined. Talked it over with the GP.I told him that My system gave warning the last time and I figured it would again if serious blockage occurs.

Tuesday we worked on the AT. It was a fun day with about a dozen willing workers. Pretty much a self managed team. This time we went to Hughes Gap working on a relocation of a steep section about one mile long.

For some reason, when we come to a rock to bust or a stump to grub, I get called into action.

A lot of discretionary time went into bleeding the clutch on the Studebaker and sawing limbs from the Oak tree. Fortunately, GSH was willing to aid and abet on each project. Well, she did not actually saw limbs but did gather brush. We finally got the clutch bled and adjusted. I drove the car up and down drive going through all the gear changes w/o a hitch. I believe at long last it is ready for the next step, the exhaust system.

The weather had turned colder by Saturday so we moved indoor. During work on the Studebaker, a lot of small parts, nuts, bolts, screws and various clips had been accumulated in the shop. Since Meredith was not here to sort the parts for me, I enlisted GSH once more.

Various parties have inquired about the status of THE CAR. Well. it is still right where I stopped upon our return last Sunday.

We do have a title and a tag but I do not want to unload until I have a place to keep it warm and dry. Or, at least dry.

Just in case anyone is interested, here is the current status of the oak tree. The remaining log is about 30 feet long
and 46 inches at the base.

A fine birthday party for Thomas and me. It was good to have Vanessa back in town. I am going to start working to teach her to be a country girl.
