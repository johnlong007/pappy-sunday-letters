December 14, 2008 · Sunday

Silk Purse

7 photographs·320 words

Monday the contractor finished the job, loaded up and departed. We had to do
a bit of negotiating on the final cost of the pool plywood cost and
installation cost but it all worked out.
The weather was sort of rough so I mostly stayed close to the house. During
the time when I could not get slide film and before I started taking digital
pictures, I used print film for a number of years/trips. I have been scanning
in the negatives for those trips. I cannot very well show that activity but
it did consume a lot of morning and evening time.
GSH has been chained to the sewing machine for some time.

We have been going right through our winter supply of firewood. In order to
replenish (and to get out of the house) I went up on the hill behind the house
and salvaged some downed trees.

Of course, the wood must be slit and stacked. For that, one needs willing
workers.

Tis the Christmas season. That, of course, means the Blountville Christmas
parade.For the first time in 40 years, I went by myself. I sat where I always
do and looked as pathetic as usual. But, not much candy came my way. I needed
a helper, Charlotte maybe. She can really look forlorn.Fof those who have
been there and done that, Central band is included here.

This is the big social season for us. Not only did we go to the Gray Old
Tractor owners dinner a while back, we also went to the Blountville Old Tractor
owners dinner today!

GSH not only plays in the church orchestra, she also plays in The Kingsport
Community Band.The group played in the mall this afternoon. They looked good
and sounded good.

All is well.

P.S. Turkey pictures upon request.
