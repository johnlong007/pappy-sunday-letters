My time for 13 laps on the Central track today. A tiny bit better than it
sounds as I spiraled in and out on the 6 lanes so some extra distance.
Intend to try again next Sunday.

Took GSH in to see GP Monday. He has scheduled her for an MRI tomorrow.

We stayed busy this past week. Cut a couple of pine trees which the pine
beetles had infected. GSH will drag the limbs to brush pond.

Hit a couple more licks on the '49 truck. It has a lot of rusty metal.

We also made a visit to the Karen and Pat Loven cabin project.

ON Thursday rater than going with the regular AT work group we went with
Kim to walk her section and remove reported blowdowns. Pat Loven went
along. He carries all the requisite apparel and dons it before sawing.
Walked in about 3 1/2 miles and removes several large blowdowns. Ate lunch
at the Nick Grindstaff marker . His story is posted on line if anyone cares
to peruse.

Back at the ranch it was decided the signs were right to weed the
strawberry patches.

The remaining pets are being well cared for.

Pat was visiting PBBD Sunday PM. I went over to confer with him about
security systems and other items. While there, he showed me a new toy which
is manufactured in Knoxville tn. I told him he does not look a bit
intimidating . I bet Aaron is laughing.

Just got a pictorial report from Adam ! Delighted. But, he is going to have
to practice looking intimidating.

Some family persons had birthdays this week.
