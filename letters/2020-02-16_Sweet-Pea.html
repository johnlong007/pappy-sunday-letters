February 16, 2020 · Sunday

Sweet Pea

10 photographs·315 words

Valentine day brings back a lot of memories. Grammer school and the
valentine box. Hits and misses.

We had an old folks event at church. It was well planned and well executed.
A person came and played and sang 50s mushy songs. He took requests. I
asked for "I'm a Lonesome Polecat".

Here is my valentine in aher natural habita. Coming up for a break after
dragging brush.

Another brush outing on an even colder day. Notice her willing workers. One
leading, one lagging. We really like our animals.

Niece Lisa sent a note about the battery in her car. I promised to sally
over and teach her how to replace.
Mistake ! Should have sent her to parts store.

My fumble fingers dropped parts into irretrievable places. But, she did get
a free lesson and battery is installed.
0
Dave Edwards graduated Bones Creek HS in '65. He borrowed my '65 Plymouth
for a class reunion and finally I sold it to him. I told him it works, it
runs, it is solid, drive and enjoy it. If you want one that has been
restored to perfect, buy it and let someone else take the hit.

He is retired now and restoring it to perfect. Much time and many $$. But,
it appears a great deal of satisfaction.

The carpentry crew is getting set to work on upstairs bathroom. First step
was installing a door at the end of the hall. Since the pool room access
was closed off during that work there was no fire escape in that direction.
There is now.

Work continues on the '37. Sooner or later I will have to deal with the
front grill problems. Stalling around by starting at the rear.

Brush dragging continued as weather permitted.
