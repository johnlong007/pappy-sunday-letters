Summer

And the days are getting shorter !

We continued to do the finishing touches on various farm implements.

The birds watched us, we watched the birds and the cat watched it all.
Diane decided we had more birds than were getting fed properly so she put
up a finch feeder and another site for woodpeckers.

It is very satisfying to me for family members to come and find clothes
Patrice had bought or made that they like and will use.

I pretty much put crutches aside this week. Walked at track about every
day. just 2 or 3 laps.

A & C came home !

Did Linda send everyone the cat cartoon ?
