March 22, 2015 · Sunday

Sprung

9 photographs·313 words

A lot of scurring around getting ready for Spring and getting ready to be
gone for a week.

I was told on good authority that Friday was the preferred day to plant
potatoes. It was wet Friday so we dropped the potatoes and I went back and
covered today.

A much more significant event was delivering the bed (aka silk purse) to
new home. New owner seemed less than overwhelmed. However, the cat gave it
a stamp of approval.

I did get a four generation picture .

I do like for the college kids to come home for all good reasons.

For the first time in four weeks, we made it out for AT work. Actually we
worked on a "blue" trail at backbone rock. That is the place we were
working when I let the rock roll onto my toe.

Probably the most interesting thing about the outing was George brought his
Step son's, step son along. A quite loquacious young man who did some work.

I worked some on the LBT, Took the valve covers and oil pan off to clean
carefully then put back on with the old gaskets. Plan to flush the engine
then remove again. Also took the water pump off pouring a large amount of
anti-freeze on me and the floor. Spent some effort trying to decipher
engine codes in order to procure a correct water pump.

Wayne and Jonathan came over and got the '53 running very well. I hope to
see tail lights quite soon.

GSH has started to make a new cover for my truck. But, no picture of that
yet so will insert a cat picture instead.

All in all, a good week. Oh, income tax is roughed out. Sine we still owe
will run through again and mail on last possible day.

My confidence in the IRS is in the negative numbers.
