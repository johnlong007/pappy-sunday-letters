December 25, 2022 · Sunday

Changing Times

8 photographs·340 words

My uncle Ed was born on Christmas day in 1917. He was drafted into the army
in 1942 and along with a lot of drafted boys from T, was sent to Australia
as replacements for a National Guard Unit from Wisconsin. There is a
museum I never got to visit.Many a late night sitting around the fire on a
fishing trip he told me about crossing the Lemon river in Layte on his
birthday in 1944.

http://www.32nd-division.org/history/ww2/32ww2-10.html

Early Christmas morning.

The sticky bun tradition came to our family from Granny B, not sure if it
was the German or the English side passed to her. Anyway, GSH slept late
today . Just as I was about to roust her up B & L showed up wearing white
hats.

Later, PBBD showed up, by then GSH was back in her chair. I noted a puzzled
look.Spot the problem?

My foot and leg have been in literal pain this week. Wed. I will be going
to our GP for a routine checkup. Perhaps he will get something under way to
improve things. Laura took me to see Dr.. Ponder the cardiologist this
week. He decided to fiddle with my prescriptions a bit and have me do the
treadmill thing soon.No sign of an immediate demise. I think. Maybe.

With cold weather predicted, we sealed around the shop roll up door. For
the last few days I have kept a fire in the shop stove and the fireplace.
Eating right in to all that wood we have been splitting.

Also having to fill the woodpecker feed station 3 times per day.

No work on the '37. Diane did remove and disassemble the PS unit from the
55. I think we are going to try to mix and match parts from inventory,
flush the system and try again

Several Christmas pictures posted, several came by Maria from far, far away.

Thanks you all and to all a good night.
