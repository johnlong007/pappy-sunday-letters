August 28, 2022 · Sunday

Local Vortex

14 photographs·280 words

As it became apparent to me that the '37PU was going to be in the shop for
a spell, I decided to procure a metal shelter to make place for the '55.

A & C more or less offered to manage erection of the shelter they worked on
footings this week.

Alan also pointed out that the landing had cracks in the concrete which
could cause problems.

First thing was a trip to Lowes to get new grinder wheels to allow grinding
out the cracks followed by concrete crack filler

Of course, the cracks must be clean and free of debris hence the pressure
washer.

The car had a layer of grime so washing was the logical thing to do. Really
improved appearance.

While the rig was set up and a willing worker available, the pool room deck
and windows fit right in'

It occurred to me that if anyone wanted to do a family pin up calendar
this picture might work. Or perhaps a poster for multi tasking.

The difficult side of the footing is now in place and locked down.

If anyone thought the 37 was going to be on the road soon , such is not the
case. Getting close to time to pull the engine.

A fair amount of work on the retaining basin. Intent is to get it seeded
before next scheduled rainfall. Still need to pick up rocks.

Birdwatching for relaxing time.

I also look at the picture frame. Several added new pics this week. Thanks.
