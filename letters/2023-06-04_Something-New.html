June 4, 2023 · Sunday

Something New

13 photographs·336 words

I have donated blood a lot of times over the years.Tuesday in a historic
first for me, I was on the receiving end. I think it has made a marginal
difference in my endurance but nothing drastic.

We continue to go to the track most every day and do 8-10 laps. This week
we were fortunate to be joined by visitors from hither and yon.

Last week, the dog went berserk about finding a box turtle. That was
nothing compared to her excitement yesterday when she announced a new
arrival. Just behind the house.

It is getting serious with the coons. Friday night one dug uo our best
tomato plant, Again ! Si set the trap and watched as darkness arrived.
Raccoon climbed post, ate all the woodpecker food, walked by and sniffed
the trap and kept shuffling.

Stay tuned, this battle is not yet over.

Two or three (or more) years ago I retrieved two rather large cherry logs
from Farmer Lisa. They passed the statute of limitations.

I have told willing workers a number of times there is only one sit down
job on the farm Tractor driving and I've got it. Guess what, my infirmities
have led to another sitdown job.

What and who for the cherry logs is no longer an issue.

The Long children had announced an event for this weekend so several
migrated back home. Charlie stopped by at the same time the Saturday home
nurse was here.

In order to extract the second Flathead engine for the '37 we had rolled
the Alfa outside.. I do not like to leave cars outside so with adequate
help we got it moved back into the storage facility.

The long (get it ?) awaited event has arrived.I understand a Most adorable
child contest was being held but we had to leave before the winners were
announced.
