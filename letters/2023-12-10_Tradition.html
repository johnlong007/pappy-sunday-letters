December 10, 2023 · Sunday

Tradition

9 photographs·210 words

For many years we have gone to view the Blountville Christmas parade from
our spot on the bank in front of the Baptist church'

We did again this year but for the first time ever we took chairs.

Perhaps you will recognize some of the paraders.

Our big kid was able to elbow children out of the way to snabble candy

Last week I mentioned the power company tree trimmers cut down one of our
big pine trees. I told them it would be OK to dump the chips along under
the training trees and other hip as they continued along Ellis Road.

They did.

Diane decided she wanted a load to put around her blueberries'

Since the Bobcat bucket was not on the Bobcat, we decided to load the old
fashioned way. That was a mistake.

Note the fiddler on the roof? She does like the new electric blower.
Cutting the pine tree and limbs left the barn stored pearls in open sight.
So, we diced to block the view.

In working to reduce clutter, we collected a quantity of scrap wood. After
the rains, the signs were right for burning.
