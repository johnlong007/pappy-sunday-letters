November 10, 2024 · Sunday

Chemo

13 photographs·300 words

Chemo week.

It all started in June of 1923. A week of chemo, a pint of much appreciated
blood this week, lab work every monday for several weeks then repeat. They
almost all know my name and all are nice to me.
Mostly I remember their children's names and ask to see current pictures

It is all exponentially better than what LLLL went through.

I asked if this was this witch's brew and did she stir it up. The chemo
bag, I had already finished blood.

I suppose they have cross words but I have never seen or heard any sign.

Meanwhile, the leaves come down. We made it to the track every day and got
some things done in the afternoon. Leaves, a lot of leaves.

I held the ladder. Leaves on the ag building roof. Diane also found a hole
in the roof. Always SOMETHING.

Car storage building. Almost new roof, no damage found.

Barn lean to. Does have some damage from a downed tree. On the list for
this week.

Barn front, notice leaves are aligned with tractor repair man. They did
come down. Anyone remember handing tobacco and having to avoid stepping on
the walnut seedling?

A & C have made great progress on the crawler. More to be done. stay tuned.
Note it is running.

The Oliver 40 has a new paint job ! Painter is waiting for the correct size
stencils to arrive in order to finish the job. Stay tuned.

I can still drive the tractor. A couple of pine trees came down recently.
Both are now at the sawmill waiting for sawyer to decide their fate.
