May 27, 2012 · Sunday

Cars, cars, cars

9 photographs·195 words

We left at 5;30 AM Monday for the Spring Chrysler 300 meet in Chattanooga.
A fine event, we got home about midnight Wed. The dogs were glad to be
rescued Thursday morning.

Chrysler 300s were produced from '55 through mid '60s. The club has peaked
out in numbers and is on the decline as the members are dying off faster
than new ones are added.

But, it was fun. We went to the Coker museum. The Holmes wrecker towing
museum and Honest Charlie restoration shop. All well worth visiting.

After we came home I watched while Anita and John installed the beam into
which I had cut compound, complex angles.

Saturday, Wayne brought a pool cleaning crew. I was amazed that Sunday
morning the pool was clear and sparkling.

Also Saturday, I went with Charlie to the Long family farm to check on the
sawmill engine. W discovered that metal thieves have been at work. Aaron,
we need you.

Anyone know where to find a '54 just like the one pictured?

The race is on so I am going back to viewing.

A&/or C, did you video the proceeding last Sunday? Would like to see.
