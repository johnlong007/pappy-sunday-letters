I have spent my whole life making do and getting by. Now, for the last few
years, I have been trying to do a few things right (for a change). I am
quite accustomed to things I have cobbled together failing but when I try
to do something right and it fails I am disappointed and quite often
annoyed.
All that leading around to the sagging forehead story.
The Garland ancestors all experience sagging eyelids etc. I had my eyelids
jacked up a few years back. However, gravity has remained at work and for
the last 2-3 years my eyebrows have been sinking. After much internal
debate I decided it was time to get it done right.
Monday went to see Dr. Seaton and spent 1/2 day getting tested, examined
and lectured. Took the exm reports and went to see Dr. Hudleston to get the
real work done. He said The test results were not complete and I had to
have a "visual acuity exam taped and untaped " "Pay on the way out and come
back as soon as you get the testing done. Watch out for the doorknob".

Now what?

This week, my running goal was a single lap at 2.00. I got close, 2:02. In
doing so I have pulled a muscle and am going to have to back off for some
time. Rats !

For good news, the splined shaft is back from the machine shop, it looks
good and I believe it is going to work. Don is coming over in the morning
to help me put the drive train back together. Stay tuned.
The brakes are all buttoned up and silicone fluid has been added but no
attempt to pressurize or leak check yet.I also did quite a bit of cleaning
on the engine and the drive train. Nothing can go wrong.

We went to the ballgame at Blountville a couple of evenings. Not so much to
watch the games as to walk on the track and eat Ruritan hot dogs. Hooters
it ain't.

When we came in from church Wed. evening Bambi and family were waiting for
us.

GSH has continued brush snipping and dragging this week. She has also kept
fussing about the brush pile needing packed down. We did get the wood David
helped split stacked to dry.

The garden is doing well. Tomatoes galore, Peppers and some cucumbers. I
fixed tuna salad for GSH one day for lunch. Found the pictured tomato
today, Any guess as to what chomped it ?

Do you remember what JW kept the Studebaker 7 years before I finally
brought it home and finished it myself? "John" has had Herb's '63 for more
than 7 years. I kept pestering Herb to bring it home and get someone else
to finish the job. He would not. I went over today and helped James and
Lisa clear space in the storage building for the car and (I hope) the
parts. Stay tuned.

Last night, I went with Dwight to watch Pat and the dragster. It went well
while we were there but I hear he lost in semi-final after we had headed
home.

No AT work
