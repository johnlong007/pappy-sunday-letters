July 15, 2012 · Sunday

Rain,Sleet, Snow and Gloom of Night

10 photographs·420 words

The good news, almost nothing to report about cars this week.

I worked on the AT.

GSH worked on shower/gift for Katy

I went to Erwin to check on Herb, Kaylee and the goats.

Did I mention last week that Charlie brought the sawmill engine to store in
warm and dry until he can get it running? He did, this week, he pulled the
spark plugs. No attempt to determine if it is frozen yet, stay tuned.

I could attach a picture of the goats, all fat and happy. However, I think
I shall attach a picture of my brother and his ward instead. It all comes
back to me.

Next in some order of importance, Charlie extracting a spark plug.

What next, Katy !

GSH working on her present, can you decode? She is also working on the
wedding dress but I shall save that for a surprise.

My sort of contribution is the flower garden. Actually, all I have done so
far is tell Anita and Maria they could plant one.

Speaking of gardens, it is that time again.

The big project for the week was spreading 110 tons of gravel on the AT
from Carvers Gap up to the top of Round Bald.

Early (5AM) Monday, I went with David Quillen to pick up two rentals
tracked skid steer gravel movers. David had his front loader on the trailer.

Monday was a nice day mostly. We had 1/2 mile of trail to do. Starting at
the top and working down. The trail circles around through a wooded section
which includes three switchbacks. I spent the day smoothing and packing the
switchbacks as the vehicles tore up the trail.

Tuesday started off pretty but turned misty with some rain. The switchbacks
got really messy causing me to have to work a lot harder.

Wed, it started misty, then rainy then a downpour. By Wed, the gravel were
spread past the switchbacks, David was driving one vehicle all along, the
person driving the other one got cold and turned it over to me about 10 AM.
When the 2 PM shift arrive we had about 30 minutes of work left but I was
ready to quit. Wet to the skin for hours and cold to the bone. I turned it
over to Bill Murdock and left.

Thursday morning we took the rental equipment back to Asheville.

Friday I spent the day mowing around here .

Another week in the fast lane.

Anything going on anywhere else in the world?
