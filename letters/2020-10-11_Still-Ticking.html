October 11, 2020 · Sunday

Still Ticking

15 photographs·334 words

My to do list for Monday was "mow everything". I pretty much finished on
Wednesday. I am gradually losing ground on how much of the place I can keep
beat into submission. But, the mower has been a real work, time, effort
saver. The picture is the steep hill behind the house which I can mow when
the ground is dry (and I don't get turned sideways).

It appears that the retaining basin may be working. So, I decided to go
ahead and try to repair some of the erosion damage in the walnut field.

Filled with the topsoil I had dragged aside for the retaining dam.
Seeded with tall fescue and covered with leaves I had shredded with the
mower.

One of the Summer Camp gardens had been left with ridges from potato rows.
Janet will remember. I decided to try to smooth it out and reseed. Same
plan for leaves except shredded on site.

PBBD was over one evening so we rolled the '37 out and fired it up. It is
running pretty rough so will need to try to clean the ignition system
before next effort. Also the water pump is leaking.

Fall nut season is in full swing. Chestnut trees are loaded.

The chestnut burrs fall on the ground, the deer paw the burrs open and
chomp the chestnuts and the turkeys pick up the crumbs.

Persimmons, Katy?

On Friday morning we often go to the Burger Bar in Elizabethton for
breakfast with Sanford, meeting his friends there. They were making apple
butter on an upscale format Friday when we stopped by. Beth, note gas
burner and comfortable conditions.

I was more interested in checking his B-I-Ls car collection.

Today, some people went on a mushroon harvest sojurn. Check with Wayne for
more details (and to check on his health),
