December 15, 2013 · Sunday

A Fine Week

7 photographs·467 words

Family report. I got a note from my sister, she had been out spending the
day with Martha, Amy and was planning a James event. I went over to see
Herb, Lisa and Ella were somewhere about and Kaylee was due in any minute
from her first semester in college.

The Squamous spot on my head appears to be alive if not well. I am
scheduled to go in on Jan 3rd for a "Mohs" procedure. Herb has had a lot of
trouble with places on his cheek and is also having to deal with some
recurring issues. I include this information to let all know to keep a. out
of the sun and b. an eye on spots which do not heal .

For my part, most know there is nothing I enjoy more than spending the day
working with children and/or grandchildren. It is best of all when doing
something I want to do and helper is a willing worker.

I wonder what Mr. Brown would think of these pictures.

As you can see, Benj went out for work day on the AT. We went to backbone
rock and completed the zig zag relocation from parking lot to the AT. A
blue trail which is now open for business, go try it !

After getting how from the AT day, Benj decided to help his grandmother not
only make B & G but also an apple pie. Both turned out excellent.

Linda's Philippine friends are Belinda and Jeremy Lu. I traveled with them
to Luzon this Summer and stayed with her Mom while hunting/hiking the Villa
Verde Trail. I saw on Facebook that Belinda's Mom was in for a visit so I
took me down to see her. It was a good visit. I told her that if she would
find a guide to go with me over the rest of the trail I would come back.
Jeremy's Dad grew up in Mindanao but none of them will even talk about
going there because of the Moros.

Report from the cat house: this week we have been bringing the cat into the
hose through the doors at one end while taking the dogs outside through the
doors at the other end. Not quite sure what we will try next.

When I went to see Herb, I "borrowed" the front bumper from his old Dodge
PU truck. Turns out it looks like it was made for the rear of the '37.

Otherwise, I did not do much on the MM or the '37 this week. Maybe this
week.

Anyone heard from Va.? Do we still have family there?
