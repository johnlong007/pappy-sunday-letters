July 27, 2008 · Sunday

Sorta Normal

7 photographs·367 words

A fair amount of time was spent looking over Charlie's shoulder while he
took something apart this week.
The first item is the engine from Herb's 1950 (about) Massey Harris "30"
tractor. Dwight has promised to oversee the overhaul of this engine. Charlie is
to do the disassembly and reassembly. He did the first part and the block has
been delivered to the machine shop. Getting it done before he returns to
school will be a challenge.
He also replaced the front drive hub in the family car. Once more under
Dwight's supervision. It is not clear to me why the front wheel had not come off
the car.
Work also continued on stripping the (maybe) useful parts from the parts car
Fiat. The hulk is now ready to go to scrap.
For AT work, we did a significant relocation. Where the trail crosses the
road at the Iron Mountain Gap, going north the trail makes a steep climb up an
old logging road than back down on the other side of the hill. We relocated
the trail away from the road and went around the side of the hill on a more or
less constant grade.
Summer Camp leftovers have been collected and await claiming by rightful
owners.
Summer is here. Tomatoes, potatoes, beans, peppers, onions, Cucumbers (and
cornbread).
I captures a couple of willing workers to help me finish splitting wood on
Friday. Meanwhile, back at the house, Christopher and the GM started work on
installing leather seat covers on the Miata seats. They did not finish the job.
For reasons not quite obvious to the untrained eye, they left the Porsche
here departing in the Alfa. Mystique I guess.

Friday evening and most of Saturday I went to a Buchanan reunion held in
Jonesboro. I picked up a little bit of new information and found out how the
extended family is doing.

Sunday, I delivered Janet to LutherRock Camp in Newland, NC. We got there on
schedule at 3:30 PM. The Wisconsin contingent was expected but had not
arrived. I pusher her out, signed her in and left.
