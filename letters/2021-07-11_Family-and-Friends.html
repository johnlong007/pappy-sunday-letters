July 11, 2021 · Sunday

Family and Friends

12 photographs·369 words

Out of town family members showed up over the weekend and converged here
Monday morning. It seems there were rumors of berry picking but turns
out to be a bit early. Fortunately I had some blueberries which farmer Lisa
had donated to a needy family so I was able to avoid mutiny by bribery.

A few blackberries were close enough for the Utah contingent

Turns out one person came all the way from Alabama to pose on our fire
engine.

Monday evening the July 4th celebration continued for the new arrivals. My
big success was making a video clip of this scene. Available on request.

Our family sure is good at eating events !

While Jake was in town I asked him to come check out my new toys. He is
pretty good. Next I want to procure suitable holsters.

Bring up a child in the way he should go .

Family excels at unscheduled events.

I just finished reading a book "First Steps".
Writer traces bipedal walking from way back (to avoid offending young earth
or creationist) to the present.
One entire chapter about babies learning to walk. So, I found watching LUCY
monkey walk fascinating. We had to do that in football warmup drills but
had to rotate body completely without touching the ground except hand and
feet. Can anyone do that?

Work on the Alfa clutch problem continued. A new master and new slave (Ok
to use those terms? ) cylinders are now installed but will have to face the
welder one more time about the actuating arm.

After a long exile, the Studebaker is back home. Obviously I turned it over
to David with more issues than he was able to handle and still go to
school. So, back for an indefinite pit stop.

Maria came by for a visit. I have been trying to get her to produce some
proof that she is actually going to school in Colorado. She does seem to be
making hay while the sun is shining.

I bet his Italian buddies are rejoicing today.
