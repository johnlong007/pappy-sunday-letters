May 7, 2017 · Sunday

All is Well

11 photographs·304 words

Mowing season is in full swing and while one can get caught up (sort of)
you can never get ahead. I mow with tractor, rider, push mower and string
trimmer.
I have also undertaken a clutter reduction program. That will take from
here on out. I am well pleased that the place is
a pretty fair wildlife sanctuary

.
We walked all around this afternoon. Fig tree has come alive once more.
Cherry tree has lots of small green cherries. I really would like to come
up with a way to get a cover over it without damage.
Grapes are looking quite good and all the nut trees are in full bloom. The
pear tree looks good but has almost no small pears.

​
GSH picked two ripe strawberries. The cold spell is holding the berries
back but they look OK.

​
And just for looks.

​
Charlie was not the only one to get a bundle from Heaven. We got two.
Andrew and his friend Colt came to visit.
We did a number of things.
One was to discover the new glow plugs I had obtained for the crawler do
not fit.

​Colt said his Dad is a truck driver so we let Colt check out the Mack
collection.

​We also made a ramp run the fruits of which they had for breakfast Sunday.

​Saturday, we did routine maintenance on our section of the trail. Trying
to get a jump start on weed cutting. Benj and Wayne will appreciate how
much better results are obtained with a string trimmer.

​It was cold crossing the open field but fun talking with the Chicago
hikers we met at the shelter.

I have seen pictures of the new Long arrival. Welcome to the clan.

I have heard graduations are also taking place but cannot confirm.
