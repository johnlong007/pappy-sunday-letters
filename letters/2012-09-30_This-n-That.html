September 30, 2012 · Sunday

This 'n That

10 photographs·406 words

I got a report on the Alpaca babies today. One is doing fine. The other is
being bottle fed, so far, so good.

I took Vanessa to Vanderbilt Monday. Left here at 5:30 AM and got back at
10:30 PM. Stopped in Cookeville for supper with resident scholars. Is
family still do-gooder?

Tuesday was back to the AT. Carl had a report of a large blowdown somewhere
between Low Gap and the Osbourne Farm. He sent me to cut it out and inspect
the rest of the trail. The Forest Service people are insisting we wear hard
hats at all time when doing AT work. We have learned that they pick up most
infractions form the pictures people take and publish. I wanted a picture
showing my chain saw pack but figured I best have a hard hat.

Anita also reported a downed tree blocking the side entrance to the
Blountville Annex. We got it cut out but left a part dangling from a sky
hook.

At home, work continued on putting pine needles around the blue berry
bushes. I believe that project is laid by now.

While I was gone Monday, 20 ton of gravel got dumped in the driveway.
Spreading the gravel was a fun project. If I should have to go back to work
for a living I would like to be a heavy equipment operator.

I finished work with the string trimmer on the steep hill. Still have some
rider mowing to do in the pine woods. When I went up the hill I found
something had been after one of the Yellow Jacket nests. Hard to believe a
skunk could do that much digging but have not seen any bears around here.

Wayne came over Sunday PM. We started the Studebaker and brought it here to
the house. There a few things I want to address before storing it long
term. It fired right up and really sounds good ! Real good. I like it.

Then we started the '63. Had to drag it around a few laps I had drained the
fuel tank and put in 5 gallons of premium no ethanol but it needed the
remnants of the old stuff cleared out. Once we got it going it also sounds
good. I think it will be the next project.

Finally started THE CAR and brought it down to admire. It also fired right
up. It needs a good home.
