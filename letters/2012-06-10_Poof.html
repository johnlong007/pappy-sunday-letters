June 10, 2012 · Sunday

Poof !

11 photographs·485 words

In case you are wondering how my plan to start cars and tractors worked
out, the answer is, not very well.

The '53 Plymouth is manual transmission so I hooked the tractor to it and
dragged it out of the shed and around to be headed down toward the barn.
Put a battery in, primed the carburetor, let it roll and it started right
up , sounded good so I brought it on around. Now I have three parked out
front.THE CAR, the '53 Studebaker and the '53 Plymouth. As GSH left me
(again) in spite of it being our anniversary, I had no tow tractor operator
so I did not try to start any tractors.

For AT work, this time of the year we are mostly busy trying to keep the
trail in good shape for heavy usage. That means weed cutting, water bar
cleaning and blow down removal.
The Tuesday crew scattered in all directions. Three of us (Jim Foster and
Pat Loven) went to Greasy Creek. The other two cut weeds while I cleaned
water bars.

Last week, I included a fauna picture. This week, I shall attach both flora
and fauna pictures.

While the cat is away, I have been clearing out a lot of stuff. In the
process, I have been digging out a number of pictures from the days of yore.
Here is a (sort of touched up) picture of my GGM (of wooded egg fame) as a
young newly wed and another of her as a widow with her two somewhat elderly
daughters. Probably made in the late 30s.

Just to balance the books, I believe I shall include a picture of my GM
Buchanan along with some of her grandchildren. Looking at the fine young
boy in the white suit and guessing him to be about three, I would say
probably taken in 1939.

Since I have no report from Laura, I will send out mine for her. She had
the new (and improved?). Pectoral repair work completed and seems to be
feeling well and glad it is behind her (even thought it is really in front).

I cooked a big pot of soup beans and invited various friends to come join
me for supper. It was just like the wedding feast except I did not go out
on the high ways and by ways.

Katy, if your thesis person starts beating up on your work, Tell him I am
coming with your Mom to have a talk with him.

I took green onions to church for the carry in dinner wed. Very few were
eaten but that was OK as Kim and I finished them off. A lady in the church
who knew I had been abandoned by my spouse came scurrying up as arrived to
tell me she had made enough to cover for me.

So far I have only made one meal at BK.
