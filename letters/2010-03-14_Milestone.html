Milestone

10 photographs·136 words

I think I shall do things in order of importance this week.

The car is running!

I spent most of the week fabricating interior panels from thin paneling. I
had a couple of willing helpers. Well, helpers.

The interior is about fitted except for inside roof.

GSH has offered to do the upholstery work. Toward that end, we took the
sewing machine to former voc. school teacher friend of Herbs for a tutorial on
machine adjustment and operation.

While there, we went by to check on Aunt G. She is doing well. Shuffles all
around. She is getting shorter and more stooped but still alert and funny.
I picked up a few more family file facts.

Signs of Spring abound:

Oh, we had a birthday !
