July 9, 2023 · Sunday

In the Grove

14 photographs·365 words

Maria reminded me. four years ago this week. This jump was the last item I
got checked off my to-do list compiled before I left ECC.
I sure am glad we did it and would be happy to do a repeat.

John came in for the week and brought Paul along with him. When John
offered two willing workers, I gave him two items which were bugging me.
Limbs fall from the trees onto the hill behind the house such that I cannot
mow. Also, PBBD had dragged a lot of brush from the pine trees down to the
edge of the drive. I asked if they would move to the brush pond.

They got both tasks done !

Progress on the 55. The trunk seal is now glued in place but not yet leak
tested. Stay tuned.

In other 55 news, The rear axle has an inner and an outer oil seal. PBBD
reeplaced the outer seal twice but oil still leaking. Replacing the
ineer seal, requires pulling the axle.
WQayne to the rescue, he fabricated a puller and extracted the akle, then
used my seal puller and got the inner seal out. A new seal is in hand and
awaiting installation.

Anyone remember when the very large Oak fell , bringing several other trees
along? Been a while during the time all has been cleared except for the
trunk which Alan has claimed.
The Log diameter was such that a long bar saw was required.
Fortunately both a saw and an operator arrived. Log is now 3;10.5 foot
logs. Next step is sawmill transport. Stay tuned.

No equipment or workers damaged.

The sawmill operators continued converting last weeks log into quarter
sawn boards.

The sawmill helpers cleared out and stacked slabs.

.

In spite of our best efforts, GSH is getting skinny. We make the track for
8 laps most days. I had to go into treatment center a couple of days, SF is
a big help especially for those days.

Always time for some recreation.
