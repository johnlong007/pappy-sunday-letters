March 1, 2009 · Sunday

Sorta Normal

14 photographs·392 words

The local AT workers got a request from Colorado for certified sawyers to
come help with cutting blow downs at high elevations in August this year. I
thought that might be fun but upon checking I found that my certification is
expiring .Being certified also requires the Red Cross first Aid etc. courses
which were expiring at the same time. So, I went to Kingsport and got current on
the Red Cross part this week. I will re up on the chain saw part in March.



Big news this week is that the tile project is finished. That is 100% not
almost but finished.

For my do-ggoder work I helped Anita with cross bracing the corner posts on
her fencing project. The bad news is I did one corner upside down and
backwards and have to go re-do it tomorrow. Stay tuned.

For the first time in a spell, I made a run to Erwin. Herb and I went to see
Aunt G. She was in good, maybe even fine form. I should have come home and
entered the latest family stories in the file but did not and have now
forgotten. She told a couple of new ones. Perhaps Herb will remember.

The AT crew went back to Laurel Falls. It had quite a bit of ice and was
even prettier than before a couple of weeks back.

There were two large rock ledges in the trail. We converted one ledge into
steps. The other one is on the schedule for this week.

On Thursday we went back to Buckeye Falls to get a really good picture of
the Falls early in the morning with sunshine on the falls. Two things went
wrong, no one brought a really good camera and we had a really difficult time
finding the falls. However the falls were still there right where we left them
and very pretty with lots of ice.

Another adventure was going to Seymour to work on the drag racing car. It is
coming right along.

It is to be noted that drag race car drivers are a rugged lot.

To end the week, I think I will include a picture of my willing workers
awaiting my return when I went out running around today.
