December 25, 2016 · Sunday

Christmas !

13 photographs·223 words

Today is actually, officially Christmas day. It is only 4 PM and we have
already received pictures from several family members. I like it.

A lot of water under the bridge in the two weeks since my last notes.

I did not go for AT trail work , however, I took Carl, GSH and the truck to
get a load of timbers to be used in bridge construction on the AT. Carl had
arranged the purchase from Bowman Construction Company.Two of the Bowman's
were there and helped load the timber. Ronnie ( the one I gave a sack full
of cats) and Doug.

​We took the timbers to the Watauga work center to unload.The first bridge
is to be built at shook branch .

​Thursday, we left for Hawaii for Benj's trip. Flew to Honolulu then on to
Hilo

​I have a lot of pictures for anyone wanting a showing.

After we got back I had the previously reported two stent worth procedure.
Dr. told me to take it easy for a few days before back to serious running.
So, today we went to the track and I walked 10 laps at a four minute clip.
So far, so good.
