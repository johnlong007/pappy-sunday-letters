March 4, 2012 · Sunday

Humdrum

7 photographs·383 words

Several good things happened !

All the family members scattered from here to there seem to be doing well. Aaron is still wherever he is. Katy is somewhere else as is Christopher. Kenneth is visiting Katy and Charlie has gone West (actually Northwest). I have heard Cliff is. Beth appeared live and in person for the birthday bash.
Facebook gives me occasional peeks at others about whom I care.

Anita became a teenager. I do not usually take birthday pictures away from our house but made an exception for this relatively rare event.

Linda came for a visit (with Rebecca and her friend)

The man who renovated our house sent a crew out to repair the leak damaged living room ceiling.
No pictures but I will take some of me crying if it leaks again.

I went in for 1,000 mile follow up stress test and did well (I think). Our long time GP is retiring so I figured I had better get checked out before he checked out. I will go in later this month to go over the test results on his last day at work.

Continued to make good progress on the '37 pickup. Most of the sanding and filling is done for the cab and the front fenders. As soon as I can get three willing workers I want to put the bed back on the chassis.

A fine AT work day . I took quite a few pictures where we continued to build trail at Hughes Gap. We are adding about one mile to the trail up the steep part of the mountain. I believe I shall include a picture of our crew after we had finished our days work.

For opportunity for improvement, Dwight could not get a spark from the M'cycle ignition system. My words of wisdom for future project managers are:

1. Don't ever take anything apart until you are ready to put it back together forthwith. The '37 has been in pieces for at least 20 years.

2. Trying to fit an engine into anything other than its correct chassis is asking for trouble.

Finally, GSH aka Grandmother, has been chipping away at Christmas Wish List Items. She delivered one such item today !

Anyone suffering from ennui can send me a follow up.
