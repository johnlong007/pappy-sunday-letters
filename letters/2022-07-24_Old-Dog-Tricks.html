Old Dog Tricks

8 photographs·275 words

This week, I watched the day's highlight video of the Tour De France each
day. While I was in the UK I learned to appreciate Formule 1 racing. Now,
due to Kenneth, I am learning about particle physics and tour De France.
The terms and names baffled me about equally in each arena. I need a
dictionary of frequently used terms.

For the last 2 or 3 years on Sunday night GSH has been watching British
murder shows on Sunday night with B & L while I wander about. For the last
year or so. A & C have been coming over in the evening and Alan narates his
Mom through Midsomer Murders.

Last night due to the allure of a child and/ or grandchild, we were left
alone. For the first time, I watched one of the programs all the way
through with gsh! It was interesting and went well.

Anita arranged for her hairdresser to make a housecall.

Wayne got a new supply of caulking and finished sealing about the edge of
the solar energy storage container.

He also obtained a vacuum actuated dent puller and had a pass on the Rogue
dent. He was short on time and did not complete the job but it looks
promising.

I have 3 front and 3 rear bumpers for the '55. Work continued on cleaning
up in preparation for getting one each replated.

We have a lot of birds. They tend to come to the feeder in groups, 4 or 5
times/day.

PBBD bird feeder.

Red blooded american boy.
