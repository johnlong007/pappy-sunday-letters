June 16, 2013 · Sunday

Which Way is Up From Here

11 photographs·632 words

Long ago and far away when I worked for Boeing in Renton I took a paper
clip and a wooden ice cream spoon to make an "up-meter" which I mounted on
my cubicle wall. Later, some of my friends hung a child's sand bucket on
the wall it cubicle entrance with notice to donate to the "Send Buchanan
back to Tennessee Fund". I guess it must have worked. Do people still do
things like that in the workplace?

Actually a pretty quite week in River City.

Strawberries have come and gone. Cherries have come and gone. Black
raspberries are coming in and red raspberries are looking good. I have been
planting four little short rows of corn every couple of weeks which should
start maturing in mid-July.

We continue to get above normal rainfall. The weather projections have
caused Carl to move AT work to Wed. here lately messing up my regular lunch
with the GOBs.

We did get out Wed. The weather was projected for mid 80s. We went to the
top of Roan mountain to repair boggy places in the zig zags we have
recently installed. When we got to the top it was cold, misty and windy
with temp around 50. When we left about 3PM it was up to 68. When we came
through JC it was 90. I spent the day busting rocks. As I remember, the
state used to run a school at which they taught people how to make small
rocks from big ones.

No one is using the pool. Rebecca coned me into refilling it and getting it
in operation. D &D came over to check it out. She went in once ! If no one
is going to use it I am going to put it back in mothballs.

The Blountville tractor event was Saturday. Friday, Laura and others went
to the lake , afterwards, Christopher came over and helped me get the
Studebaker going and drag the JD out into start position.

The '53 fired right up . I drove it to the event on Saturday. So, for a
years tags and insurance it has made two trips to Blountville. One for
Charlie and one for me. I decided not to try the JD so it is still 1/2 way
up the hill hooked to the tractor. TBD.

The tractor event went well. For the first time in several years we did not
get an afternoon thunderstorm. The club hires a tractor pull club to come
in and put on a tractor pull the evening of our show. We pay them $850 and
get to collect and keep the gate . When we get an afternoon rain it cuts or
one year, eliminates the gate but we still have to pay. This year was a
fine year (by our standards) we collected $705 . Guess we need to put on
more shows. The people who normally help me at the gate were elsewhere so I
commandeered GSH to work. She spent all her time flipping through the take
like Scrooge McDuck. But we still had fun.

Today, I gleaned the cherry tree for one final pie. Too late to feed to D &
D tonight so one day late tomorrow.

It was good hearing from the kids from far away places with strange
sounding names today as well as some not so far away and not so strange
sounding.

This coming Friday, I am flying out of TRI on my way to Luzon. I want to
retrace the route Ed took with the 32INF Div . Th primary focus will be the
Villa Verde trail. Anyone who is interested can find plenty of information
on line. I will be back on July 7 if all goes as planned.
