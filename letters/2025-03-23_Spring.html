March 23, 2025 · Sunday

Spring

15 photographs·245 words

In all these years, I have only harvested one ramp from our site. All the
rest have come from the hills. That one was for Savannah.

Anyone remember planting the bulbs?

I hope and expect it is evident how much I appreciate bringing the little
ones by.

Patricia would love this scene.

Getting ready to break out of the bubble and join the" money but no time,
leaving the time but no money life".

Sarah provided something to look forward to after Cancer Center release for
good behavior.

I think Jim Foster told some of the AT workers it was OK to come by.
Really good to get caught up. They each have health issues and are no
longer active AT workers.

Family members from all over the known world are stopping by to rummage
through the listed artifacts. I really like seeing items going into the
open hands.

Linda can explain her choices. Perhaps in detail.

Linda got caught in" you can't stay and watch".

Much to my sorrow, the cat is away.

Not sure what Diane has in mind here. All these we have refurbished, more
to go.

The smokehouse floor has been undermined by groundhogs. The roof separated
, local handyman did a field repair.

Work continues on our combined memory park.
