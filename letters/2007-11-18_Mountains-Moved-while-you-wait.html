November 18, 2007 · Sunday

Mountains Moved, while you wait

6 photographs·216 words

One of these days I am going to take a picture of one of our do-gooder
projects. Of course, getting ones reward in the here would prevent any reward in
the hereafter so maybe I will not. We did several projects on Monday and
Wednesday.
Tuesday, I picked up Sis and Betty in Sevierville to go on the grand tour.
We stopped to see Flo and then took her with us to see Aunt G. Aunt G has been
having hip pain for a couple of weeks. Finally went to Dr. and found out the
bone was shattered. They pined it all back together. Too flimsy to replace.
After leaving Aunt G we ate lunch at Clinchfield Drugstore, saw Cathy and her
daughter Allison there. Then we drove past Rock Creek park up to Indian Grave
Gap on the AT. Quite pretty view on the way back. Took old road to Unicoi,
then to Limestone Cove, across Simerly Creek Road to Hampton and back via 19E.
Thursday and Friday I worked here. Saturday we all worked on the siding (and
the ribs) Sunday we finished the siding, no more ribs to be found. Benjamin
dug holes in the shale for storage building footings. So goes life in the fast
lane.
