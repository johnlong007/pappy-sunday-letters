June 13, 2010 · Sunday

$$$$

12 photographs·422 words

I think I shall start the report by doing all my whining. I took the slow
leak MD tractor tire to get it repaired. $92 for a tractor to drive to
Blountville once/year. Something is wrong with this plan.

This was the week of the cherry pie. It was also the week I found a pit and
went to the dentist. $175 for 30 minutes work.

Dwight procured a windshield for the car. $xxx, ordered a full set of
weather stripping $xxx. Brought it home from being painted $xxx. I assured GSH
that all the serious money is now spent. But, I forgot the re chroming is
yet to do.

Now the news.

Do-gooder is sort of back in operation. Monday we put in shower grab
handles for LOW and installed a cabinet in bathroom. Very rewarding.Dave's GSH
arrived to supervise his work.

Tuesday was AT work. Wayne and Jonathan went along. Coming out of Bishop's
Hollow the trail comes up a long hill through a hay field them makes a 90
degree turn near the top. Hiker's report missing the turn and getting lost
so we were sent out to install new markers.

After a fair amount of leaping, lurching and fumbling, the much delayed
Miller outing was held Wed.
I came home with a headache, Dave went home mad but oil has been poured on
the troubled water. It is now someone else's turn.

Speaking of cherry pie.

On a really good note, Meredith procured her driver license last week. Not
to miss a beat, Maria now has her driver learner permit. One of the
activities I enjoy very much.

Car stuff:
Mark took his rig to Erwin to bring the Stude home from being painted. It
looks really good. Now, the hard work starts. Detail work on all the trim
etc, the kind of work I hate.

Saturday was dyno testing on the new and improved engine for Pat's
dragster.It did not quite measure up to expectation but under the hope spring
eternal heading the real test will be Friday night at the track when it will get
a dose of NO2.
Peak power of about 825 HP was at about 7,500 RPM . I missed that, here it
is over the peak on RPM curve.

A couple of notes.

1. Tentative plans call for a family hike on the AT this coming Wed.
Details to be announced.

2. I need to know who is coming to Summer Camp. It sounds like planning for
this year may be complicated.
