Peeked Out

7 photographs·329 words

How long has this hunkering down been going on?

This week we ventured out into the world. And ! we went to church and
Sunday School. I do not like to take pictures during service so I snuck
this one after SS.

No "must", no "required" did not have to register. Wow ! Just like the good
ole days. Sure felt good .

Truth be known, we went to visit LLLL because her son David (and his good
friend) were being inducted? as Eagle scouts. See below along with the
correct number of parents. Cliff and Christopher can relate.

When Laura graduated from TTU some years back, she purchased a new car. A
Dodge, Shelby Daytona.She and Bob kept it in excellent condition.
But, things happen, she got lightly T-boned damaging some of the Shelby
trim.
She found a twin for sale by owner North of Chicago. Purchased and we
retrieved behind our truck.
Afterward, the parts car went to Wayne, then Herb, then back to Wayne when
he needed a replacement rear window. Now, he sold Laura's Daytona, the
parts car is no longer needed and is loaded for trip to last roundup.

I used the Bobcat to load the car. Per my usual practice, I left the key in
. Later decided probably should not do that with it parked near the road.
When I walked down to retrieve the key I found the cat was on guard for me.

We moved into the rental house just across the hill here in 1965 come
Spring time some really pretty flowers popped up. We took bulbs with us to
house on Shadow town road then brought bulbs here.

I am sure all know about Sally getting her finger wounded. I got to inspect
the wound Thursday evening. Not to be outdone by a mere child, I will
include this weeks blood and gore picture.
