February 28, 2021 · Sunday

So Far, So Good

8 photographs·324 words

I have been very pleased with the home health people. This week, the
medical person assigned to check on me after the procedure signed off on
me. Free like a bird. I have been trying to pick up the pace a bit at the
track. I am afraid my knee may be a problem

Another person works with GSH and with me , we each have exercises to do.
One which overlaps is batting a balloon back and forth. God for GSH
flexibility and balance, good for my limbering up my neck.My ability to
rotate head over left shoulder had become a serious issue while driving.

Dave and Carol came by one day.. A Couple more of the Friday morning Burger
Bar group showed up.. A few more folks showed up for church this morning
but the "Elders" are delaying SS for a few more months. Tempted to take my
business elsewhere.

We had a lot of rain but also had some warm, dry days. We consolidated all
the remaining Winter wood supply. Intend to burn it all this month. It is
better to stay a year ahead on firewood. So, we also moved the two
woodpiles left out and stacked with next Winter's wood.

Willing workers. This wood was split last Summer.

Should be plenty.

The slow drain project continued. It now appears that when the termite
treater drilled holes to inject his potion he drilled right through the
washing machine drain line. We suspect the problem will recur by and by.

In spite of good intention, I had not started the '63 since putting it in
storage some time back. So, on one of the pretty days I fired up and
brought it out front.Started right up, sounded good. Drives good (if you
know how with no power anything).

Cat was glad to check it out.
