July 8, 2012 · Sunday

Much Ado

6 photographs·409 words

Bill Hambright died this week. Bill retired from ECC about the same time I
did. I did not know him at ECC but got to know him through breakfast with
the GOBs.

Bill was from Macon Ga. A Clemson graduate, never married, one sister and
at least one niece. He went through ROTC and served as a platoon leader in
Vietnam, a forward artillery observer I believe. He did not talk much about
Vietnam. As a matter of fact, he did not talk much about anything. When I
flew to Korea last year, I sat next to a fellow who was raised in the Hill
country,a Montnard(sp?) . His parents were christian and had served as
interpreters so they got to immigrate. I told Bill about meeting the fellow
prompting Bill to talk at some length about his relationship with the
tribes. A very positive relationship.
Bill was a heavy smoker. It turns out, he knew he had lung cancer but
choose not to fight it.
My friends are dying. I miss them.

I suspect people are tired of reading that I am fiddling with old cars so I
will keep that part brief. Both the '55 Chrysler and the Alfa have been
checked out and returned to storage. Each under their own power.
The '63 got some attention but needs a tune up.
The Ferrari has proven a somewhat popular attraction since I activated the
insurance and it can be taken on the road. We took it to church today.
Clearly, it is a road car not a work car.
Quite exciting to some (actually one or two at most), the sawmill engine
has been rescued and is now stored in the auto storage building. Stay tuned.

No AT work this week as a four day week is planned for the week coming up.
I have the 6AM, 2 PM shift each day.

We did have a fine July 4th.

Joe and GSH rode the community band float to play in the kpt parade. I will
send a picture when someone posts.

I did make it to the Blountville parade. It was better than average. I
shall include a band picture for the benefit of those who have moved on in
life.

The highlight was an outing one Reservoir Road. See if you can almost taste
it !

For your quiz, identify the bird. It is of the woodpecker family.

Then Identify the driver. Miata today, Ferrari tomorrow.
