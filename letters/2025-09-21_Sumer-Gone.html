September 21, 2025 · Sunday

Sum'er Gone

9 photographs·236 words

Last week, Anita told me Kenneth was making a presentation at TTU on
Monday. I mentioned that to Sanford , he asked "What time do we need to
leave"? I am really glad I got to go and pleased but not surprised at his
very professional presentation.

As all know , I am trying to teach Diane everything she needs to know to
keep this place running. Last week, it was the Axe, this week, the chain
saw.

I was delighted to be introduced to the newest member of the family. I even
got her name straight without looking at my cheat sheet.

A lot of the week was spent working on the A-C. The front tires are mounted
and wheels installed on the tractor. Bad news is, one of the rear tires has
a serious leak. Rats.

Diane has it all painted. My plan (Ha) is to replace all the painted parts
etc then try to get it running. Stay tuned.

I clearly remember a song about Autumn leaves. A few days head start.

I am really glad the pool is in operation and is drawing in some users.

A lot of dreer usually morning and evening. The one in full view has a hurt
right front leg but seems to be using it a bit more.
