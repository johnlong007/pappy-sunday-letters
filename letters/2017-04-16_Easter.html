April 16, 2017 · Sunday

Easter

15 photographs·324 words

Who knows the story behind this pictured item?

​Company was in town for Easter, Friday night we got to hear John picking
(and see grinning). Then Saturday J& S came by. I am delighted to report
she cleared another hurdle with ease.

​Not so sure about John, I had solicited the aid and comfort of one of my
ECC friends, Jim Holston, a retired, hands on, EE, to help with the
electrical system on the IH. He was very pleased with the interest shown by
each of our visiting EE's.

​
There was another visitor around but during AT work Saturday Allison and
Meredith took him off on a wild ramp chase. So, my inspection time was
limited.

​As part of the IH project and as a dutiful brother, I made a run to Erwin.
Happy to report the lost seat has been found.

​
Less happy to report We were not able to resolve the problem with the fan
motor speed control. We has a you tube video but could not get it worked
out. Stay tuned.

Thursday was AT work day. I shall include a before and after picture
showing my efforts. Not shown is a lot of walking to get in and out.

​Saturday , while Allison and other were ramp hunting, Laura and I walked
our assigned section to refresh the blazed and do some measurements at the
shelter.

​I will need additional willing workers soon when mowing season arrives.

Lest we forget. Looks a lot like Easter last year.

​The 49 got a fair amount of effort this week. I am ready to get it running
as soon as the signs are right.

​In response to those who questioned why no blood and gore pictures the
last week or so:

​Just saw a note from LLLL.
It made me think of my words of wisdom most recently delivered to Savannah
yesterday. "There is no guarantee of tomorrow".​
