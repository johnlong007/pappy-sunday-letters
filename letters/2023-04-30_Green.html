April 30, 2023 · Sunday

Green

13 photographs·464 words

Flying to England, one may go over Ireland, usually early in the morning.
The country will be glowing green. It has been that way here this week.
Paw Paws are in full bloom. It looks like a bumper crop.
If anyone wants to go into business we have a great many seedlings. Or,
Lowe's has for them $35 a pop I understand.

We have pretty much stayed home and let the world beat a path to our door.
The first path beater was niece Ella with a container of fresh strawberries
from Scott's farm. She has a Summer job there. She also delivered some of
our ramp harvest to Aunt Flo.

Some will remember my East German friend Alf. I keep in contact with him,
he said he is cavorting in Spain. I believe I could have done this kind of
rock climbing in my younger days.

I do not have a picture of our most recently married family members to hang
with the others.So, when this came in I decided it is just what they will
want on the wall for me to show all visitors.

Somewhere I have a picture of me crossing the finish line at the Indy half
marathon. Never got to do a full marathon. Drat.

On the home front, I have stuck with one or two cups of coffee in the AM.
Also, we have been able to do our 8 laps/day at the track. GSH seems to
enjoy the outing except when we get a stiff wind.

Katy procured 3 tomato plants for me. Afte much, much pondering I decided
where to plant. We were just getting started when young, energetic help
arrived..

Three major concerns:
Deer
Groundhogs
Blight

Do note the circle. I mowed with the string trimmer. Some critter nipped
the asparagus one time after that we put up a fence and left it to Anita
to nip.

A last week event. Albert's second downed pine tree being harvested.

Turns out A & Cs grandchild was here to see the log turn into a fireplace
mantle.

I have temporarily given up on the power brakes for the '55. In the best
tradition of making do and getting by, I have mounted a brake master
cylinder from a '63 . There is still some plumbing to do before executing a
test run. Stay tuned.

Being an equal opportunity bird feeder (free lunch for all !!!) we put a
hummingbird feeder out near the driveway. In order to keep an eye on it
from my workstation (Ha), it was necessary to raise the curtain a bit.
