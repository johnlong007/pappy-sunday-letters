May 2, 2021 · Sunday

May Day !

13 photographs·409 words

Today was interesting, We went to Perkins as usual. Connie, our waitress
was not there. Unknown waitress showed up,recited our normal order
including asking GSH what kind of Muffin. She had decided to go with a
piece of pie which soon arrived. Then at least one other waitress came by
to see why no muffin. A lot more personal attention than we got a church.
In the early service, everyone else was masked. But as we were going down
the hall to SS passed some folks coming in for 2nd service without masks !

I did not dance around the MayPole. I did even better. Friend Kim has been
living in fear and seclusion for the last year. (She watches CNN and reads
the NYT, we do not discuss politics). But for me, she ventured out for our
annual ramp run. It was a perfect day. Ramps would have been a bit bigger,
but harder to find, in a couple of weeks. Would like to make another run of
stars align.

This is a picture of some of the earliest ones I planted here on the home
place. Spreading slowly but well.. The ramps are like onions, they make a
single bulb then next multiple. I would like insight into what to do about
the bunching up. Look at the second picture.

Of course the purpose of growing and harvesting is for consumption. We
brought in an expert chef.

Made enough for a harvest crew and ate em all.

I spent a lot of time and energy working on tires/wheels as part of the car
retrieval project. Herb's car had sunk into the ground. Tires flat and
wheels rusty. Really difficult getting tire bead loose from the rim.

But, the wheels cleaned up nicely. I believe I am ready to try again on
getting car up on four sound tires.

Identify these please. On the downspout just outside front door.

And this one, I had not seen it before.

Tis the season !

Wayne has decided to bring the old PU back to life. As I remember, GSH and
Laura made the PU purchase several years back. It has had various engines
including the 392 Hemi now in the Studebaker.

Our own wounded person is adapted very well. Don't thik she will starve.
