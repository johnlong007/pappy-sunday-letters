May 13, 2012 · Sunday

Dipsy Doodle

9 photographs·449 words

My do-gooder effort was giving Vanessa transport to TRI. She is off to
visit friends in far away places. Alos going back to Anchorage to determine
if the snow has all melted. I envy her, it should be a really great time
visit Alaska.

I also helped Anita cut a beam to go overhead in the structure she is
erecting. It was not quite on the same scale as a chainsaw moose but not
too far removed either. Notice "the line" I was directed to follow. I will
go back soon to take pictures of the installed product.

Does anyone remember the "Dope of the Day" from our first trip to Alaska? I
thought of that this week a couple of times. Once when Herb was helping me
drag the giant log down the hill and we almost let it get away to flatten
the car storage building and again when I got the new mower catty wampus on
the steep hill above the pond and cooked my new $50 drive belt.

On a rainy day, I worked at replacing the '37 headlight lens ( I previously
broke) with a sealed beam headlight. The bulb is from a '55 Chrysler (6v)
and the frame and chrome rim are Fiat.

>From Hughes Gap to the top of Roan on the AT is about 5 (steep ) miles. We
have been working for years to put in switchbacks to limit trail grade to
about 10 degrees. The top of the Roan is over 6.000 feet, highest point on
the trail until Mount Washington. When we got there to work Thursday, it
was 41 degrees and foggy. When we left at 4PM it was 45 degrees but clear.
The trail we had put in before zig zagges through balsam pines with lots of
soft dirt and pine needles underneath. With all the rain and heavy traffic,
sections had turned into a quagmire (for those who remember the Vietnam
situation).Three of us spent the day breaking big rocks (one ton size) into
small pieces to solidify the trail base.

In SS when the teacher goes over the "gifts" exercise, I have always
listed, weed eating and chainsawing. Then, I had to remove chainsawing
after my accident. But, I believe I can add rock busting as my primary
"gift"

GSH enlisted help on staining the newly refurbished table. Now, since that
is almost done, she has started on the chairs. That will take a spell.

For nature studies this week, when I hear the crows squawking I know they
are after a hawk. There must have been a dozen after one hawk today.

For your bonus pick this week Identify the car and driver.
