September 8, 2024 · Sunday

Friends & Family

15 photographs·387 words

Dave and Carol are leaving here after 25 years or so. They are going back
to Ohio to be near their children (and grandchildren) . I first met them at
church, got Dave involved with trail work and the Friday hikers then later
we worked together on do-gooder work every Monday for years.
The Friday hikers have slimmed down (figuratively). Dinner at the Chop
House.

Meredith came by. I am pleased to report I charmed her incredibly friendly
child into taking a couple of tiny steps. She has octopus arms and must
taste everything. White walnuts still in the hull, not so tasty I judge.

I did not go with Sanford to the Burger Bar on Friday because. The
waitress, Sharon, sent breakfast to us. We had it for lunch one day.

A & C have taken up regular Friday night care and feeding.

Wayne delivered what I believe is the last needed gravel load for the
circle.

It has now been spread and is open for admiration.

The pictured green patch is the flowers from the packet sent to us in
memory of Patrice's donation.

Wayne had brought in a load of Hickory logs. All by myself ! I hooked the
tractor to the trailer (to make it stay put) and used the Bobcat to unload
the logs.

We decided to find out for ourselves how difficult splitting Hickory wood
is. We decided it is tougher than we are. We need better splitters.

A lot of our gravel vered off during the downpour rather than going all the
way to Ellis Road.
Pretty much got it back by various means.

For years, the blade has been off center and si-gogling. Now, it is level
and centered and Elizabeth has a new skill set.

Back on the spike tooth harrow project, The odd shape and length spikes
have been driven into their own odd shaped and different size holes.
Another new skill !

We finally got the door in the Studebaker such that it closes properly, The
door panel upholstery was too thick and extended too far.

A lot more bush trimming around the house this week.
