July 22, 2013 · Monday

Rains Came Down -------

10 photographs·427 words

There was good news this week.

Vanessa sent a baby and child care report from the frozen North. Short
report is that everything is normal i.e. the baby is wearing her to a nub.

GSH tried to call Maria to come by and pick up her Christmas present and
got an answer from Maria in Seattle.

Katy sent in her noteworthy news while hob nobbing with the rich and famous
in California.

Not to be outdone, Benj is cavorting across the continent in Italy

Beth posted a much delayed update from Norfolk. I must remember to tell her
my "cat" joke.

In the meantime, Wayne and family were having adventures by day and by
night while on the road.

Turns out a fair number of the family have taken up residence in Wisconsin.
We expect to hear from them most ant day now.

The big news here was the Wed. evening downpour. Four inches of rain in two
hours.

The gravels washed into walnut field and into Ellis Road. The garden washed
into Albert's yard.
So we spent Thursday repairing the drive and relocating the gravel.

Then it rained again !

I have the driveway repaired but still have to retrieve the gravel .

For AT work, the powers in charge have decided a creek bridge located in a
wilderness area needs to be replaced with something more rustic. I was
asked to look it over to help devise a plan to get the center section down
to creek level, intact< so it can be a temporary span while center section
is replaced.

Stay tuned.

Laura and Meredith had a skiing outing. GSH went along as an observer.

Linda and her boys heard about the outing and decided to come for a skiing
visit.

Not wanting to let willing workers go to waste, I had a fun day of stump
grubbing lined up for them. However, Bob's flooded basement called so they
did not get to ski or grub stumps.

I had grubbed a few of the stumps . So, I decided that cutting flush would
be adequate until more help appears.

The Alaskan travelers returned and came over Sunday PM to pay their
respects.

If anyone thinks Charlotte is still a little girl:

Charlotte and Andrew each like to fly under the radar.

Finally, Rejoice !
