August 3, 2014 · Sunday

Repeat

10 photographs·202 words

About like last week except visitors from the frozen North swooped through.

Remember when boys ,Mormons or Seventh Day Adventist would come by? I would
tell them you can go or you can stay but you can't stay and watch.

​
​The cemetery wood has all been harvested (except for the giant trunk).

​
All the part we brought here is stacked in the dry awaiting willing workers
who want to share in the spoils coming to help with splitting into firewood
size. No big hurry now.

The most exciting news was actual work on insulating the water reservoir.
We have been talking and thinking for a long time. Now, work is well along.
Two inches or Styrofoam and a layer of plaster with fiberglass embedded.
Much more work to be accomplished.

AT work this week involved renovating water bars and harvesting ramps which
are now going to seed.

Part of my last addition to my ten year plan was to get rid of some cars
and tractors.

​
