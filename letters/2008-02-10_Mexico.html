February 10, 2008 · Sunday

Mexico

8 photographs·143 words

Anita has gone on a trip to Mexico a couple of times. I went with her last
year and went back to the same place this year. The organization has a web
site. When I get the name, I will send it along.
I went down with six other men. Four of them worked on carpentry work
building all the furniture needed for a small country churches.
The rest of us worked on maintenance projects, buildings and grounds along
with automotive
We fixed our own breakfast and lunch but ate at a local restaurant in the
evening. The medical and dental teams arrived two days before we left.
A good trip but always good to get home.
We walked two blocks to church last Sunday. About 10 adults and 6-7 children.
