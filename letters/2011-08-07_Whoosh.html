I started the week with a couple of dangling participles

Flat tire on the Bobcat
Wood splitter back out of operation
Power steering in the Studebaker not functioning

The good news is I have pretty much got the whole place mowed and looking good..Not counting the SW hill and woods.

The Bobcat tire has not gone flat for a couple of days now. I had it off the rim three times before getting to the current status.

The wood splitter is fitted together ready for welding and another run.

The power steering remains inoperative but a water leak has been stopped. I think.

Friday night it was off to the races. The pit crew had a fine time.

Part of my five year extension to my 10 year plan is to get rid of at least one car and one tractor each year.
