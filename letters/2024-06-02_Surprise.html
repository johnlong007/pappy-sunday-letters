June 2, 2024 · Sunday

Surprise !

12 photographs·313 words

We had a video conference with the UVA Dr. The first thing he said was " It
has been a year since this started and you are still alive !". We also went
in to check with ankle Dr. fitted an ankle brace and said 3 more weeks on
crutches.

A lot of what I do is trying to teach Diane how to keep this place going.
We keep 5 gallon containers with Diesel fuel, another of Gasolene and more
with hydraulic. I had taught her to do a smell test. This week, I learned
that it is not adequate if the container has had water added.
We had to drain and purge the lawn mower fuel system.
Another lesson for this week had to do with adding air to a small, low
pressure lawn mower tire from a large, high pressure system that will cause
the tire to explode. It did.

Once more, most of our work time was on painting the disc parts.

Sanford stopped by.

Gwendolyn may like to know that the chestnut trees have the promise of a
bumper crop. At least the deer and turkeys will be well fed.

Cucumbers.

Diane is renovating the traffic circle. The Crab apple tree has been
removed and the stump grubbed.

Now, the Patricia remembrance flower seeds are planted

WW11 airplane show at TRI this week. We got to watch most of the week.

The ruptured water line project is winding down. Next step will be seeding
the now filled ditch. I hope Wayne comes home with a plan for the excess
dirt removal.

If Herb tried to teach Farmer Lisa everything she would need to know to
keep the place running, she missed a tractor electrical class.
