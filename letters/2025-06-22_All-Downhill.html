June 22, 2025 · Sunday

All Downhill.

11 photographs·358 words

I don't remember when I started to notice the changing seasons. A few years
back I started dreading Fall because it meant Winter was just around the
corner. Now, it means much more to me.

The last couple of weeks the NP has come in with lab results in hand and
asked if I wanted a transfusion. I said I want to go home.. This week, she
just gave me the come hither. I really can't tell much difference after a
transfusion.
Diane dragged me into orthopedic dr. Steroids and painkillers. That helped
a lot on the back.

Bailey again.

Big progress for the week. How long has the pool been out of service ? It
was never intended as a pool as such. Part of the house temperature
stabilization system. It worked. Now, We are trying to convert it into a
swimming pool. After a prolonged data collection period, Diane will
determine what kind and how much external solar collection is required.

The mower is ready for putting on display ! It has been a fun project.

Alan designed and fabricated a replacement brush guard. So far, it works
well.

Ah, to be young again. This person sure looks a lot like Linda. Acts like
her also.

Running the planner on this slab is what set my back off into pain. Still
there. Predicted to be a hot weather week. Diane wheeled the slab out,
wrapped in black trash bags and parked it to slow roast the powder post
beetles.

Roasting along.

I got back on the mower to test my acheing back. Jammed the blade and
burned the belt in no time at all.

Diane has been here a couple of years now and I have made her angry several
times. We got a heavy rain one night, she went out to set up her
syphon/drain lines and found both hoses shot full of holes. Almost seems
too much for accidents.

Dr. Sago said we should go for DIY freezing skin cancers.stay tuned
