December 27, 2009 · Sunday

Charlie Brown Christmas

3 photographs·161 words

The demise of Puppy Dog put the fear in me. So, wee took Atlas in for a
1,000 mile checkup. The vet pulled some teeth and cleaned the remainder. Said
he is fine shape.

I cleaned a set of wheel covers, installed wheels/tires on the car and set
it down off the stands. It is real close to a shakedown run up and down the
driveway. A long way from the road.

I made it to Erwin to see Herb and his family and to see Aunt G. She was
spending the day with Rusty so I missed her and most of Herb's flock had not
yet arrived so I missed them. Oh well, I reckon one gets credit for trying.

Also went by to see Hal and Sue. Hal is not doing well.

Christmas was a fine day. We made the local rounds. But, left camera at
home. We did go eat a belated Christmas dinner with Anita et al.
