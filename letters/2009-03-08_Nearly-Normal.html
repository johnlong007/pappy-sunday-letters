March 8, 2009 · Sunday

Nearly Normal

9 photographs·409 words

No church related do-gooder work and no AT work. Too much snow for the trail
and broke down helper for do-gooder.
But, I did visit Hal who is now resting between cycles and doing well
mentally. Otherwise it was family type things.

The deer and the turkeys have been here all week but since they still look
about the same I will not include any pictures.

Actually, Anita looks about the same also (since she only had a non-birthday
this year) but I will include her picture anyway. The real reason for the
picture is that some fence has actually been installed. Not just posts but
fence.

I had to drain the pool to clear out all the trash which got in during the
roof work. It seems a good time to insulate it so I have commissioned a local
engineering firm (D & D) to do the planning for the project. Other associates
may be contacted about actual installation in order to procure pool tickets
for the finished product. The hope and expectation is to turn it into an open
all year facility. Well, it is already open all year, maybe a usable all
year.

Linda wanted two tires for the exploder. I found a set mounted on large
Chrysler wheels. I broke them down to deliver the tires to her. Then, she said
thanks but no thanks. Then, I noted the wheels would bolt onto the in progress
mowing machine so I remounted the tires. Both of which now leak.

Can you tell what is different about the view of Linda's house? I will give
you a clue, look at the departing tail lights.

Linda also has willing workers. At least curious workers. The garage
entrance area is not prepped for pouring of concrete.

Dale used his all purpose back hoe to pull the trailer tie downs. I had Adam
and his visiting friends dig around the post so I could attach the chain. I
took the camera over and announced I was going to take a picture of the
workers and the watchers. Adam immediately asked his buddy "do you want me to dig
a while for you?" Subtle he is not.

Garden work was the second big event Saturday. The real big event was
procurement of a new puppy.

Last but not least, the trimming of the locks.

Rachel, it was 76 degrees here today.
