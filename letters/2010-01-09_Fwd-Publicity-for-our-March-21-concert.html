Fwd: Publicity for our March 21 concert

2 photographs·125 words

I have attached the front and back of the postcard we received from the Arts Council this week. Our concert is advertised on the front of the card. Thought you might like to see it.

I am going to offer our mailing list to the Arts Council for their publicity mailings. We need to drum up some support for this concert. Admission will be $5 - less than the $12 they are charging for most of their events. We thought $12 was too high and worked out an agreement for a lower admission charge. So - we will need more bodies. Talk it up to your families and friends.

Let me know if you don't want your name and address given to them.

Ellen Finney
