November 30, 2025 · Sunday

Many Thanks

12 photographs·287 words

No dinner and show tonight, Laura is on G'mother duty. So, early edition.

I think the leaf collecting season is over for this year. A White Oak tree,
the deer love the acorns.

The '63 Chrysler came with a single chamber brake master cylinder. I
changed to a duel chamber, now it is leaking and I would like to go back to
the original. Dug out my inventory of single chamber cylinders and decided
none are usable. I will order a new one and wait for help to arrive for
installation.

With cold weather coming and old, cold bones, we are going through the
firewood. Diane may be burning the furniture by Springtime.

Working indoors on the '37. The front bumper is now ready to install.
Cannot install until the engine is back in place. That will be a while.

Charlie came to check on us before going back to the frozen North.Of
course, he wanted to do something helpful. So, he slid under the '37 to
check brake lines.

As a fill in outside job, we dragged an old, horse drawn slip up to the
work site.

We got it mostly ready to repaint but have made no progress on new
handles. Stay tuned.

One of the smokehouse groundhogs came out to see if Spring had arrived.

Rachel et. al. managed a Thanksgiving event here on Saturday.It was great,
children, grandchildren and great grandchildren. Also a selection of
consorts. I took several pictures which are available upon request.

The kids liked playing with my Christmas present, farm toy set.

Another formal introduction.
