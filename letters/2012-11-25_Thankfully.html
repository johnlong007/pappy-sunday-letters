November 25, 2012 · Sunday

Thankfully

8 photographs·327 words

Larry Kidd is in ICU at HVCH with Wegener's disease. Today was end of
fifth week on all sorts of life support equipment. Debby insisted I go
in to see him. When I spoke to him he turned his head a bit and
managed a bit of a smile.

I have rigged up wiring on the '37 but have not attempted to check it
out. It should be clear to everyone that I have little confidence in
my electrical abilities. Stay tuned for the checkout.

GSH continued to wrestle with her project. It may indeed turn out to
be a silk purse. It would appear that what she needed was an
opportunity to work on it without benefit of my helpful suggestions.
Stay tuned.

AT work was the same ole same ole. However, it was a very nice day at
Indian Grave Gap and the places I chose to work were less difficult
than last week.

George Reed has no family around here. He has a sister who lives in NC
and a brother in Utah. The Utah brother has a daughter who went
through AF , ROTC finishing about 20 years ago and has been on active
duty since then. She came to visit George last Wed . and stayed until
today. George brought her by Saturday evening, we talked quite a while
then went out to dinner. She is stationed in Albuquerque for the next
couple of years. I intend to go visit her next Spring. She promised to
jump out of an airplane with me !

We had a private pre- Thanksgiving dinner here Wednesday evening, a
full fledged dinner Thursday with Laura and family and a
post-Thanksgiving with Anita and family Saturday. A couple of well
stuffed turkeys.

In between, I went with Allison to visit LLLL while there, Alan
roasted the fatted bratwurst over an open fire ! All in all, a really
fine Thanksgiving week for us here in River City.
