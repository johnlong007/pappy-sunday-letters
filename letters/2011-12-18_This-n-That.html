This 'n That

7 photographs·448 words

I am having less frustration using AOL for pictures . So, If anyone wants a high resolution of a particular picture, let me know .

Anyway, When I was going through pictures with Momma, she told me one was her, Herb and Sis sitting on the rumble seat cushion from Their car. She even told me where the picture was taken. Perhaps the subjects will remember?

Anyway, Herb informed me the car was an Essex and he believed it was brown. Probably a 1928. I foraged around and found one. Perhaps Sis will confirm?

A very average (think about that statistically) week here in The hills.

Monday, we cut down a tree at Blountville. Since it was close to the barn, we tied a cable to it, tied the other end to a stump and put Anita in the middle to keep tension on the cable wit a come along. Meanwhile, back at the base, I would run the chain saw. Needless to say, Anita was jumpy as she was positioned directly under the intended path of the tree. (Benj, how is that for inducing drama?)

For AT work day, we worked on water run-offs where the trail follows and old logging road bed near the TN/VA line.

The forest service people are trying to drop a bomb on us. They want to require that we wear all the safety equipment all the time also, that any pictures we take will belong to them. We shall see I suppose.

Around here, I mostly fiddle with the M'cycle wiring. Dwight came by today and worked on it a bit also. I believe there is a good chance it will live again (by and by).

While I had the chain saw handy, GSH decided she wanted to redo the couch we have had for many years. I have no idea why.

Speaking of GSH, you will be astounded. I purchased a barely used treadmill from Dave Dunham. I figured I could use it when cannot run on the road for one reason or another. Plus, the system has pretty well stopped us going to the track where GSH could roller blade. So, Using all my powers of persuasion as well as the opportunity to watch an "Inspector Morse" video.

Not much progress on the car. I decided to adjust the gear shift linkage . I did. Now it will not go into any gear except reverse and then only when the engine is not running.

But, Wayne came by and we decided to hone our tire/wheel breakdown skills in case worse comes to worse and I have to find a job.

Anyway, ready or not, Santa is coming to town.
