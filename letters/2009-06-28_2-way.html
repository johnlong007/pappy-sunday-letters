June 28, 2009 · Sunday

!/2 way

7 photographs·462 words

The past week was week 26. The year is 1/2 way gone. Wow. Last Sunday was
June 21 st. The summer solstice. Not everyone knows that in recent years
this date has also become "nude hiking" day on the AT. Probably that would be
of little interest except Charlie, Christopher and a couple of their
friends left to hike a week on the AT last Monday. I failed to tell them about
the big day until it was too late. Alas, maybe next year. When the solstice
falls on an AT work day, we do encounter a specimen from time to time. I
went with Anita to pick the hikers up in Hot Springs on Saturday. As best I
can tell they had a fine trip.
My week was not very exciting .
I went with Dave for do-gooder work Monday. It went fine , I talked with a
LOL while Dave worked.
My driver (GSH) took me to Erwin to check on Aunt G. She was in the rehab
room doing a bike thing.Her face was bright red. She said from some lotion
Todd had given her.

One piece of literature I brought home said no driving for two weeks,
another said for four. By Wed.(two weeks) my driver was so tired of hauling me
around that she agreed with the 2 week limit and I went back to driving.
I stopped by to see Hal and Sue but no one was home.
All week long I fiddled with tractors. The JD and the "H".
John came over one evening to help. We dragged the JD up the drive, it
started and he ran up the back of the tow rig before I could manage the hand
clutch. But, it kept running and he drove it on up.
Later, Herb came over and backed it up on the landing showing Kaylee how it
could be used to flatten buckets en route.

By threats, complements and occasional bribery, GSH helped several times .
Some times when small hands (wee paws) were needed and sometimes when over
five pounds force was needed. But, as always cheerful, willing and gracious.

D & D came over today and started work on the pool solar heat exchanger. I
have got to get the fill line hooked up tomorrow and start refilling the
pool.
I went in to skin Dr. Wed. and had a couple of live ones and a few minor
actors removed. Got to go back Tuesday to get the stitches out.
Today after we got home from church (in the heat of the day), I walked
three miles in 50 minutes. Plus, my breathing continues to improve. So far, so
good.
