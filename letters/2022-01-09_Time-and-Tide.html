January 9, 2022 · Sunday

Time and Tide

8 photographs·257 words

The week has zipped right by. Some of everything from the weather.

I got a note from Maria saying she was cavorting in the big Bend in Texas
on her way to Colorado. So I dug out some big bend pictures from days gone
by. Beth, One boy, four girls and no job.

I went up to the retaining basin to check for signs of sprouting in the
grass seed. It is coming right along. Look close and you will see the green
tint.

Look close here and you can see our escort service as we walk to the
mailbox. Cat trots along, dog runs everywhere at a high rate of speed.

Usually 7 deer and a very large flock of turkeys hanging around. These deer
were on the lawn outside the pool room until I went outside.

Bring up a child ." PBBD has been admiring
the '37PU. When Pat inspected it he found a lot of places which
need improvement. (just like me). I decided it was past time she learned
how to apply body filler.

When I work in the shop, I tend to make a mess. GSH is really good with the
broom.

Hunting for Big Band pictures, I came across a stack of pictures taken by
Mom and mostly not marked. I put my experts to work trying to identify as
much as possible. Not sure what the next step will be.
