April 27, 2025 · Sunday

A good Week.

12 photographs·291 words

This week was the 5 day Chemo week. That was not bad at all but NOT what
made it a good week.

I got to meet my newest GGC !

And be entertained with a not quite so new one.

In voices from the past, Dave Hrivnik stopped by and got me caught up on
life in his fast lane.
Later ie the week, Dave Edwards came by to discuss various technical issues.

Anita went to the Exchange Place event and brought us 4 tomato plants. Let
the battles begin !

I went with Sanford to visit Bob Harvey. He told me how to get to the
overlook on the Nolichucky across from Lost Cove via the Wishbone Trail. W
& L did the hike Saturday . Pictures are available from Wayne.

Squirrel warfare !

The red wagon is almost ready to go on display. The reel lawn mower is
also. I got a rig set up to sharpen the reel.

Wayne and Diane got some of the horse drawn implements mounted with a
promise to finish this week.

Kathy Case brought us some ramps. Store bought ! Jim and Jan Foster brought
ramps they harvested for me.

Diane cleaned the ramps and planted the nubs.

It will take 2 or 3 years to make a patch like this. Notice a few blades
are turning yellow. Those who intend to go ramp hunting had better get
moving.

Not only did I start the week with chemo but also got a blood infusion.

We saw a few hummingbirds this week. Hard to pick out but trust me.
