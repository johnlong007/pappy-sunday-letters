Fathers Day

0 photographs·198 words

Our first Alfa was a 1982 which GSH purchased from a dealer in Johnson
City. An ECC bigwig had traded it in. It had the Spica electro-mechanical
fuel injection was always a problem.
After many years of struggle Dwight installed a Pinto manifold and a Holly
carb. It started and ran great. I did a lot of body work and I believe Alan
painted it. But, by then we had a newer Alfa and the little blue Miata so
the 82 Alfa got parked.

For no good reason at all, we got it out this week. It started right up
(with 11 year old fuel) but the clutch linkage broke. Stay tuned.

Eric and Mari (GSH's niece and spouse) have been in California for the last
20 years. They finally bailed and Mari is now on the staff at Glenville
State College in Glenville, WV. We visited them today. 275 miles//4 hours.
They bought a giant, first class garage with a nice house, great view and
100 acres. Next week, Mari. Eric and their son Jonathan will be competing
(and Eric working as chief stewart) at SCCOA events at Bristol

I failed miserably on pictures. Maybe next week.
