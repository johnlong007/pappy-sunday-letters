July 17, 2016 · Sunday

Win Some

14 photographs·353 words

The quack of a cardiologist doubled my blood pressure medicine dosage and
assured me, twice, "no side effects". I decided to read the small print and
guess what most common side effect ! light headed, tight chest and dizzy.
Just what the dr. ordered.

I quit taking it about 10 days back and no more light headed, tight chest
and dizzy.
Plus with my new lap timer I am able to measure with accuracy and
precision. It is proving very difficult to get built back up but at least I
can push hard enough to have to pant for air. Comfortable lap speed is
3:25. I did 16 in 53:25 with a best of 2:25. I really want to get it down
to 10 minute mile. I always run in mid-day sun so when I try to get my 10
minute mile I will go in the evening and see how much that helps.

Worked on the AT two days this week cutting blowdowns from the big storm.
One day I worked at Wilbur Dam with Bob Peoples and Jim Chambers.

Another day, GSH went along and we worked with Joe and Robin to clear Pond
Mountain. Wilderness area so no power tools.We met Carl and his crew at the
top of the mountain.

It was washing machine all the way on the home front. Stay tuned.

I did get all the brakes installed on the FE. However the flex lines will
have to be replaced. Difficult to remove.

I went to Erwin to aid and abet Lisa get the tractor going. Battery was
down. She is a quick study. While there, she showed me What she plans to
use to use to deal with uninvited critters. Also showed me a collection of
Herb's ribbons and wings. Made me proud again.

Wayne worked on the thermal energy storage unit most all week. I have high
hopes and expectations. Stay tuned.

Went with Dwight to the drag race. Tried out the new 1,200HP engine.
Performed perfect but they went too fast and lost. Ask him to explain.

The stump jumper has been readjusted !
