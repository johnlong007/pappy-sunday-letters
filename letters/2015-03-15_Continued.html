I hit the wrong button. Forgive us out typos and misspelled.

For some, the most exciting thing to happen this week was taking an oil
bath.

I decided it was time to get the LBT out and run it a bit. The last time I
ran it, the oil filter leaked and sprayed all over. I decided I had
installed the wrong filter, bought a new filter and refilled it with nice,
new oil.
This time, it started right up, drove it down drive and almost back when it
sprayed oil all over again. I checked and the filter was loose with gasket
gone. So, I logically assumed they had sold me a defective filter, went to
Advance, bought a new one and installed along with the last of my new oil.
Since the filter is on engine side away from the driver, I stationed GSH at
the open side door to see if and where the leak was occurring. Started
right up, all well, raced engine and blew oil all over GSH. She was not
amused and less so when I wanted to take a picture before she could wipe
her face.

After further testing involving another filer, used oil and a less than
eager son-in-law, it has been determined that the oil pressure is too high
when engine is reved and is blowing out the oil filter seal.

LBT is back in shop, pan is off and oil pump will be next. Stay tuned.
