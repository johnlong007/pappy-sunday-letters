October 16, 2016 · Sunday

Hills and Valleys

6 photographs·224 words

Janette Coates died. She was a good person and a friend of many years.

John and Savannah are married ! Beth, Katy and Charlie are still married,
multiplying and dividing.I think Aaron is in limbo land.

Wayne continues to be handy to have around. I have been told old folks
should not be on the roof or standing on the top of a ladder.

Alan was in the area most of the week. He is working on a plan to vacate
the frozen North. I skipped AT work day to help him find a surveyor to run
line at hillside. No success so far, stay tuned.

He did get introduced to the big crawler, No progress on the steering
issues so far.

We did make progress on the '63. GSH finished seam around rear window and I
started replacing the molding strips.

Several new items were procured this week the only one pressed into service
was my new and improved mop bucket !

There were also apple pies and park outings.

One evening Dave and Carol had the old Friday Hikers over for a soup supper
at their house. It was very nice. But, Dave has more health issues than
Dwight and does not handle nearly as well. Moans and groans.

It was a joy to attend the wedding. Let me count the ways.
