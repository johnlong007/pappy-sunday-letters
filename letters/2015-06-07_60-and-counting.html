June 7, 2015 · Sunday

60 (and counting !)

7 photographs·275 words

Friday we went to JC and ate dinner at Logan's. It was quite nice.

Then Saturday evening we had an outing. It was also nice.

Before that I went with Herb for a joint visit to Dr. Sago. She had a
report printed out for him that pretty much traced the history of the
current issues. It was very through and very interesting. He has had a busy
week, Started chemo and radiation on Wed. along with all the other Dr.
visits. But, so far so good.

While there, she zapped a few for me, scraped a few and excavated one
semi-serious one. So, I will include a picture for all the sun lovers.

After we got back from our dr. visit we worked in the garden. It has been
a long time since we had hoed corn together.

As usual, I spent a fair amount of time on various auto issues. The vw has
a noise in the running gear. So, once more, I replaced the front axle/cv
joint. It did not cure the problem.

B& L brought Christopher's Miata back from exile. GSH is giving it a trial
run. I think we are going to keep it and pass ' The Miata" on to A & C. It
this time it looks like the odds are about 50/50 that she will decide to go
along with it when it departs.

No progress was made on the truck problem. That will be at the top of the
list for this week.

Someone needs to make a chart showing the world map with all the family
spotted on it. Including Gwendolyn down in her cave.
