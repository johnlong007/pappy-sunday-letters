July 5, 2015 · Sunday

The Forth

9 photographs·272 words

If I knew how, I would post this picture on my Facebook page.

Christopher sent a picture of SLC at night with fireworks. So, I decided to
reciprocate by sending one of Blountville at night with fireworks. Did not
get a picture from Lead fireworks. Guess all the gun slingers have left
town. I wonder what kind of 4th celebration was going on in France? We had
a fine one when we lived in England. Had the church family out to our house
for cookout.

The Blountville parade went off high and dry. The most spectators I have
seen at any previous.

After the parade some spectators raided the RRB patch. Recognize the
pickers?

Speaking of pickers, how about pickers and grinners?

The remnants of the Colson family stopped by after depositing Adam at West
Point. I had saved some gourmet food for just such an occasion.

As I have mentioned, the LBC is intended to start a new life in the frozen
North. It has been in the shop for a couple of weeks getting all checked
out to start the next 281K miles. Now, it has been moved out and the Alfa
moved in. The Alfa is great mechanically and I think is a really pretty
car. However, the electrical system, switches and relays, is not reliable.
I am going to try to install some by-pass switches. Not the "try". stay
tuned.

Truck has been difficult to start. With the new batteries it cranks fine
but does not start. However, upon drifting (now that I have learned how
after 18 years) it starts upon releasing clutch.Work is to be continued.
