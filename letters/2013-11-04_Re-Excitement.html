It may be a slipping belt causing the squeak in which case it needs to be
tighter.

But, it could be the alternator bearings are failing causing the noise and
extra drag on the belt.

Get a long screwdriver ot a metal rod and press to ear and to the
alternator case with engine running. If the bearings are unduly noisy you
will be able to hear .

Hang in there this too shall pass.l
