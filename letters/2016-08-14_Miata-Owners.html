August 14, 2016 · Sunday

Miata Owners !

11 photographs·442 words

If you can keep speed above 55 or so you can drive through a downpour with
only the top of your head getting wet.

I read thru JC obituaries first thing each day. I am looking for friends
and family names. Monday, I was shocked to see the name of Shelby Jones
Leedy listed. She is the one I have been going to visit every week ot so in
Erwin. I thought she was doing well. I was wrong. Shelby and I go back a
long way together. Always a friend in finest sense of the word. At the
funereal, several of our high school classmates were there. Beverly, the
girl who keeps us all tied together was there and brought us up to date on
others. It will be three years until 65th reunion time. A difficult year in
many ways.

On the good news side, it is hard to think of anything I enjoy more than
working with our children and/or grandchildren. Andrew was here most of the
week and I had a fine time. I suppose he might have a different
perspective. We made it all week before I nailed him once on Summer Camp
etiquette. Ask him. We did a number of fun things. Along the way he
mastered all he needs to know about cooking as he goes to TTU. How to make
beanie weenies and how to make dried beef gravy. His mom should insist on a
demonstration.

Most of the time we worked on the fire engine brakes. We now have
operational brakes on both front and one rear wheel. Stay tuned. GSH keep
lopping away at the lower limbs on the trees beside drive . Andrew did a
lot of brush tossing.

Sarah passed through on her way to an outdoor adventure.She passed through
on her way home looking for a bath, a bed and food.

Friday, the AT willing workers had our annual ASU visit, 116 students and
three instructors. The project was a trail relocation on the section of
trail for which GSH and I are responsible. To save work time, we trucked
them across the open field to the trail head.

The work went well, this kind of outing will restore ones faith in the
younger generation. We only got about 1/2 done so I expect Carl will leave
the project as is and pick up next year.

I continue to try (in my own way) to help Lisa cope with things dumped onto
her. This week, John brought Herb's unfinished car back I also am sorting
out some of the equipment items he and I had been sharing for years.
