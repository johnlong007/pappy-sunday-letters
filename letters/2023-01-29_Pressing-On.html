January 29, 2023 · Sunday

Pressing On

15 photographs·333 words

We have been going to the track for 8 laps of sunshine and health every
suitable day. Monday we were met by Anita et al.

It was delightful, According to the owner and operator, good GGS Josiah had
never seen a mud puddle. He proved himself a natural born boy by middling
the first puddle he came to,

Writing about children being children !

Our outside spigot froze in the recent cold spell. The water to it has been
turned off but PBBD turned it on to run the washing machine. Generated a
Noah grade flood in the shop. Floor cleaning has now been accomplished
along with shop cleaning.

In moving stuff, I dug out the original transmission for the '63. It ate
first gear, many years ago, I replaced it with one purchased from Houser's
junk yard. Looked on the club web site and contacted a knowledgeable club
member. He said rebuild it, a pearl.

We got back on dealing with the Oak and Poplar tree trunks a couple of
years back. The poplar trunk was across the Oak. Forks of the Oak were
above and below the poplar. Sort of a mess.
Sawed up one Oak trunk and dragged it out to the sawmill.

Then I removed the trunk stub on top of the Poplar

Then drafted the young and agile to cut the Poplar from its root ball.
Preferably without getting smashed.

Well done!

On a less exciting level, we cut two more firewood length cuts from the
slab pile.

With a prediction of rain on the way, PBBD set up a system and drained the
retaining catch basin. Before and after pictures.

Trouble dog is well named. But, well liked.

Perkins breakfast buddy (David Quillen) took a 3 day vacation to go
somewhere and capture pictures of eagles fishing.
