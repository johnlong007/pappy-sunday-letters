October 2, 2011 · Sunday

The Sequel

5 photographs·239 words

I know all sorts of people are doing all sorts of interesting things. But,
not here. Just muddling through.

The legs are attached but table is not nearly completed.

She is thinking about benches.

I worked a full day mixing and matching parts to get a steering rack for
the car. Finally got it ready and installed. At that time, I discovered the
steering valve body on the made up rack is about two inches shorter than the
one which Was removed. So, the steering shaft will not reach. I had to
remove the replacement rack. Back to zero.

Dwight came over Saturday. "We" decided to remove the shaft from the
original rack and put it into a replacement. That went right along until we
found that the internals were different. Ford had switched from a spur gear
to a hypoid. So, work stopped for supper and further consultation.

Finished off the late corn and planted turnips. No picture included but
supplied upon demand. When we came in tonight the deer were checking it out
. They must like turnips.

Did get the BIG rock split on the AT. A very large piece split off. Maybe
four by four by one foot thick. Fell over on the planned route and will be
an integral part of the trail soon.

How about a bit of news from hither and yon?
