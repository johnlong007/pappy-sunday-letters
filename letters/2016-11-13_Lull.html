November 13, 2016 · Sunday

Lull

11 photographs·551 words

Still waiting for some interested person to come help me update my
distribution list. In the meantime----.

Today, I got a note from Alan while we were in SS (I sneaked a peek)telling
me that Cliff was passing the 10 K mark in his run. Since I heard no more I
assume he is still trucking along.No mention of his other children but I
did watch the band performance at Auburn game as best I could. They scanned
along the trombones but could not identify a Rachel.
We made a run to Erwin with Wayne, the truck and the dolly per a request
from Lisa to tidy up the place, more later. When we got home, Anita was
here with a fresh made banana pudding. It evaporated while I extracted a
current report on all her children, grandchildren spouses and fishes. Then,
we went to visit PBBD and wrested a report from her concerning her son and
his betrothed. If you want more information extract it from Aaron. Back to
Kingsport for Sunday night with B&L along with Mereidith to hear about
Laura's children and Meredith's cat.Got home and had a note from my long
silent sister. Now, If I just knew for sure that LLLL is alive and well it
would wrap up a fine day.

As part of trying to help the females in the family, I think I will give a
pictorial outline of what a proper GSH does in a normal week in River City.

Herb cut down a number of trees which were annoying him. One of his friends
trying to be helpful took the logs, sawed then into boards and returned the
finished product. It has been tight stacked flat on the ground for several
months. At the direction of James and Lisa, We went to Erwin and retrieved
the wood. Leaned it up against the wall and pressure washed.

i gave part of it to a friend of a friend. He is a preacher and makes wood
toys and items to give away (as Bob does). I told him that any which was
not claimed by the family he could have.

The Fire engine is stalled until carburetor returns from repair shop
(Charlie). The crawler is parked in the dry until I hear from repair shop
about tackling the no left turn problem. The '63 is static waiting for the
parts I ordered to arrive. So, time to start another project.

With the aid of GSH we positioned the '49 Dodge on the landing. It is just
like the one my father purchased in '49 when I got rid on the '37 pickup
and the truck I first learned to drive. A manual, non-synchronized
transmission. It requires double clutching while shifting both up and down.
A skill very few people now possess. When we get the truck operational I
will teach anyone interested.Probably starting with Rachel.

I made it for AT work this week. We went to the Beauty Spot and worked on
trail renovation. Nothing exciting there so no pictures, However, there are
a lot of forest fires burning and the area was covered in smoke. Literal
Smoky Mountains. In one of these pictures you should be able to see the
Nolichucky in the gorge.

Anything worth reporting from Huntsville? Cookeville? California? Utah?
