November 14, 2010 · Sunday

Loss

10 photographs·583 words

My dog has been slowing down for the last couple of years. This last year
he hardly ever rode on the tractor with me and was spending more and more
time sleeping. Mostly in the sunshine outside the front door. Monday morning
we went to Erwin and both dogs went along. But, it was obvious he did not
have any energy.

When I left to go run he would watch me and recently only come over on the
ramp to wait for me to come home. He had a bad night Sunday night and did
not get around much Monday morning at all. When I left to run he looked at
me but when I got back he did not raise his head. I sat on his mat and held
him until he died.

What I saw in Erwin.

Monday afternoon I worked on the car a bit to try to occupy my mind.

Tuesday was AT work day. Hughes Gap is on the road between Buladean and
Roan Mountain. The old trail South goes right up the side of the mountain on
a steep grade for about one mile. We are putting in a series of zig zags up
the mountain which will reduce the grade but increase the length to about
three miles. Tuesday we worked most of the way up, so a long walk in (and a
long walk out). Before too long we will switch over, drive to the top of
Roan Mountain and walk down to the work sites.

I think I shall include a before and after picture of a section of trail we
dug Tuesday. Carl is painting the white blaze signaling that this section
is open for business.

Another shakedown run on the log splitter revealed two week links.

Several years back I cut a big cedar tree . By using the chain saw and
wedges I split it into large slabs. I decided the time has arrived to do
something with the wood. When I threatened to convert it into firewood GSH leapt
into action saying she wants to make something. So, Saturday we spent the
day working to make relatively smooth surfaces which are roughly parallel
surfaces.

The new dog is coming along. He is very different from Puppy dog (died 11
months ago) and even more different from my dog but is going be OK. He is
very social, he likes to go outside but does not want to be out without one
of us outside also. The only time we put a leash on him now while around
here is to go get the mail when we want to stop him from crossing the road. As
soon as we start back we turn him loose . He will run all over the place
but gets back to the house by the time we do. He does like to chase
Squirrels. He sits on a chair by the window, gets all excited when one comes to
the bird feeders and will chase them all the way over into the woods when
turned out. He also chases turkeys and deer. Until the deer stops and looks at
him at which time he heads back to the house.

I do appreciate the updates from around the world.

GSH has agreed to bake the fatted turkey for Thanksgiving. It will be
mid-afternoon on Thanksgiving day. Those who plan to gather here to share need
to RSP before Dwight goes to procure the guest of honor.
