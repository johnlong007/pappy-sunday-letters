February 8, 2009 · Sunday

Mixed

10 photographs·305 words

Hal continues to do very well. He completed the first 21 day cycle and is in
7 days of rest. He was pretty worn down by the last day.

We had a sort of warm day so I got out and cut the big poplar in front of
the house. It was shading the sweet gum which is my favorite tree.

Then the snows came down. It was colder than usual for snow. Must have been
something going on at high altitude. I like to watch the birds during the
snows.

Every day, a big adventure for all is the trip to the mailbox. Note the
different track. GSH on the right, Puppy a nice straight track but mu dog is
quite warped. He has had a deformed leg since puppy hood.

The Cletrac is pretty much ready for action. I brought the parts David
Bradley mower in and have started on it. Maybe pictures next week. My helper,
Gwendolyn, has promised to finish the painting.

Friday I went with the Friday Hikers. The scheduled was a hike starting at
Chestoa. However, the leader decided trail conditions were too bad so we went
to Bays Mtn Park instead. It was a nice day and a good social event. The trail
has good views of Kingsport, and all around Bays Mtn.

Saturday we finally got in a work day and finished the relocation project at
Damascus. We replaced a section of steep ,eroded trail with a zig-zag trail,
longer but less steep. The original project was divided into 10 segments.
Segment one was left to last, It is the first section encountered heading South
out of Damascus.

No word yet on when we will be going to Indy. to see the girls.
