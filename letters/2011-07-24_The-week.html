July 24, 2011 · Sunday

The week

1 photographs·297 words

Hot and muggy.

Running every day @ 11:30 has been going well. I like hot weather.

Dr. cut out a live one. Now I have to go back to dermatologist for further work. Rats.

In the Summer . a lot of our AT work is weed cutting. I worked with an interesting person this week (Mike Tuttle ). Teaches business at a college in Fla.One son has Tuttle construction company in Port Huron Michigan. The other went as an exchange student to Norway, ended up going back, married a local and lives there.

Allison polished the Alfa. GSH says she is going to drive it or sell it. We do need to reduce the fleet. I drove the Studebaker up and down the drive several times. It runs really well. The voc. school director came over one day. Asked if they can enter it in fair at Gray Station. I told him yes. I hope he does but am doubtful as it would require a bit of spit and polish to get ready.

The body ID tag is missing. Mark Coates said he will make me one if I can show him how it is supposed to look. So, I went to visit Tex. Took a photo of the tag on his "53. While there, he showed me how the PS is supposed to be hooked up. We had it switched. Now, If I can get new tubing made up, will try again.

Buddy Dave Dunham is back from visiting his Dr. in Ark. More pills. Anyway, I purchased a treadmill/clothes rack from him.As soon as I can get help moving it upstairs will try it out. Maybe even try using it.

Picture insertion is not going well. Maybe I can get some training by next week.
