January 13, 2008 · Sunday

Dorkle

7 photographs·160 words

What do you want, the good news or the bad news?
1. The Bobcat quit running
2. The truck would not start
3. All that work on the Alfa was , as Dr. Deming would say, a journey
through the swamp.

But, I got the clothes dryer going, we got the Bobcat going, believe the
real problem with the Alfa has been isolated and believe the problem with the
truck has been isolated (I have the starter out and ready to go to the shop
first thing tomorrow).

For AT work, we built a bridge/footlog across Jones Branch.
Pat has started a new project.
Charlie has decided to get rich by hauling junk to the crusher.
Unfortunately, the truck would not start so he is still poor.
Really special Chocolate covered cherries this year.
Visited in Erwin Sunday PM. Aunt G plans to go home this coming Thursday.
