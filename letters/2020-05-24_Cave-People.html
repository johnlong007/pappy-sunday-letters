May 24, 2020 · Sunday

Cave People

11 photographs·449 words

Actually, I crawled out of the cave, blinked my eyes at the bright light
and went about my business this week. Three different nurseries, bought a
bunch of plants but still did not get quite what I wanted.
I have now planted some hot peppers, Various species of tomatoes and two
cucumber plants. Laura gave me two plants of the kind I could not find.
Will plant those tomorrow. Maybe a few more peppers later.

The gap between us and Albert is now closed. I rounded up bits and pieces
of barbed wire and cobbled up a fence.It has been a challenge. So far the
fate of the Oak logs has not been decided.

Some time back we cut a bunch of bamboo at farmer Lisa's. We made a run
over to collect some of the reeds. I read up on loads protruding before
going. Left as long as I thought i could get by.

The purpose was to deter the birds from the cherry harvest. Last year we
procured reflective tape and intimidating balloons. Not real clear results.
So this year we decided to do a better job installing. Stay tuned.

Photographer and helper at work.

Picture of neighbor Albert. Other side neighbor, Larry, stopped me to say
the pine tree on our strip beside his house id dying and he would like me
to cut it down. Albert owns the lot in between(Ultimate Frisbee). I waved
him down to ask if I could drag the dying pine across his lawn after I cut
it. He said yes but wanted to know what I had in mind for the bamboo .
Picking right up on that, and gave him several and two bungee cords to tie
on his cart. Albert is a really good neighbor. Larry is so so at best.

For those who don't know what they are missing, scrambled eggs and ramps. I
bet we have some in the family who have never tasted,. Alas.

We have a quite small strawberry patch. The deer cleaned it out last year,
not a berry nor plant left. Replanted and this time I have it covered with
chicken wire.
Just enough for GSH.

Every weekday night Star Trek comes on at 8. After a hard day of working
and or watching, my willing workers all gather to relax and share
family time.

I read (and exchange notes with GGD in Portland).

Today, for only the third time, I visited my father's grave site. The
second time was when my mother was buried beside him. I miss both.
