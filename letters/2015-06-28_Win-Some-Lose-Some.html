June 28, 2015 · Sunday

Win Some-Lose Some

10 photographs·524 words

My worst move for the week was going to the wrong meeting spot for AT work.
Then, after realizing I was at the wrong spot I hastened to the correct
place. However, the other folks knowing me, decided to come find me. Passed
like ships in the night. I came home an they went on without me. This week,
I will have to beg forgiveness (again).

I did better on do-gooder work. For the first time in quite a while we went
with Dave and Carol to do lawn work for a Lil ole widow woman. It was fun.
Also went to Erwin one day to check on Herb and work in his garden. It has
been raining and I knew the weeds would bother him.

At home, I had noticed the top in one of the white pine trees up near
grape arbor was dying . Pine beetles. I cut it out and burned it. Then I
inspected over behind the neighbors and found two trees dead there. Cut
down but have not yet removed.

Progress has been made on the VW noise problems. Bob took the car to his
experts. They changed a front wheel bearing (The third try). Then I put the
best tires on the front and noise is mostly gone. May decide to take it and
lap the US after all.

The red raspberries are getting ripe. So, I decided to mow around the patch
way up on the hill. Then I decided to mow the entire hill, Made me wish I
had some of the previous mowers (Aaron and Beth I think) to help. It takes
about an hour to run out a tank of fuel and after an hour in record heat I
am ready for a break. It took several days but about have it done. Little
dog is a pretty good helper. Not as good as my old dog but still OK.

Lots of grounhog hotels up on the hill, Some with multi outlets and sun
decks.

Some sort of critter has been triming the cucumber vines so I set the live
trap. Caught a possum. Thinking it was probably not the culprit, I opened
the trap to let it go. Fat dog took off chasing it down the hill and up the
other side into the woods. Just fast enough to not catch it.

We had company Friday and Saturday. Teresa and a friend came by. She is
retiring after the coming week. I expect we will see more of her. She was
quite pleased to see the Miata alive and well, hear all the stories about
grandchildren driving it and glad to know it is going to live on.

Today, the church had a patriotic music program. It was good. The director
asked members to send pictures of family members who had (or were) served
in any branch. I sent several and all were used. Also had some retired and
active duty service members be honor guards, It was moving. I surely
appreciate all who served more than in years past and wish I had done a
better job of conveying that appreciation.
