December 1, 2019 · Sunday

Being Thankful

10 photographs·321 words

At the risk of being trivial to young'uns but important to me, only 21 days
until days start getting longer.

Staying more or less; healthy, wealthy and wise.

A family of children, GC and GGC with assorted spouses all of whom like one
another,

A strange week in many ways.

The '37 Pick up (not GSH, she is a '36 model) took up all my indoor shop
time. I have decided to go with the reproduction grill and doing "heat and
beat" to fit. I suppose Charlotte will learn how in her metallurgy studies.

With the wind and rain, all the leaves are down but not out. I blew off
hill into drive way then used the lawn mower to shred and blow onto circle.
We will see how that plays out.

The "63 is now ready to go to shop for a new top installation. Wayne got
the fuel gage working and I adjusted the brakes.

A fun outing was harvesting firewood at the Long farm with Wayne and GSH.
Note we drew a crowd of very interested spectators.

One can look at this and easily deduce that I went through chain saw
certification training several times (At 3 year intervals). Andrew, what is
missing?

Ever see one of the you tube videos where the musicians play to a herd of
cattle ? I suppose it would be a shock to find out it was not the music
that drew the cattle.

The rains came down and the detaining pond came up.

Gwendolyn, this is also your mandatory cat picture.

Visitors from the frozen North showed up. I got a chance to talk with
Andrea a bit and a walk through her family farm on Google earth. I was
impressed.

In lieu of a close up cat or dog picture.
