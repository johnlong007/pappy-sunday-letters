January 25, 2009 · Sunday

Same old, same old

7 photographs·312 words

My do-gooder buddy Dave had his knee worked on (again) and will be out of
commission a few weeks. A new AT maintenance coordinator took over this year, he
is much quicker to cancel than previous person. We have not been out in
several weeks and are not going this week.
But, I did work on the Cletrac and did make some progress.

Also helped Anita a bit on Blountville project. A fairly large tree located
close to her barn had died. She wanted it cut. Since she is getting serious
about building a fence and the tree would either come down on the barn or the
fence to be, I decided to get on with the project. It is noted that GSH caught
the tree in mid-fall.

We also cut a bunch of the walnuts here to thin them but no pictures there.
I think the feathers on the turkeys are changing. I do not remember the two
tone job.

We made another run to work on the drag race car. This time front end
alignment was
the objective. It involved washers, a dial micrometer and a lot of trial and
error, perhaps iterations would sound better. Also a lot of pondering.

About the only do-gooder work was helping Herb clean the roof on his storage
buildings.

All the timber work just about wore my GSH to a frazzle.

I did get by to see Hal, he has started a series of chemo by pill. 21 days
on, 7 days off. Probably to continue for a year. They had tried to take him off
steroids too rapidly and have had to restart but will try to phase out. All
in all, he seems to be doing as good as one could reasonably hope for.
