June 19, 2016 · Sunday

Farther Along

11 photographs·301 words

Good news !

We went by long term care in Erwin to see "old" friend Shelby and left her
the 8th grade class picture from my year at Unicoi and from Jimmie's year
along with basketball team picture. Left pictures for her to get everyone
identified. That should keep her busy for a while.

Then we went on up to check on niece Lisa. She is coping with life as a
farmer. Crows are getting her corn as it sprouts. Hay got rained on.

Out to visit the Reservoir Road residence of PBBD. Trimmed hedges. Sort of
a fun project as easy to tell what one has done ,well or otherwise.

I went with David Quillen to look at a road building project he had
undertaken . The most impressive part to me was the mouth of a cave he has
opened. Next in line was his motor home which he is all set to take off to
drive around Lake Superior. Life style of the rich and famous.

No work on the '63 this week to speak of and no progress on the Fire
Engine. I did crawl around under it a bit while my helper cleaned my ears
for me.

Most of the crew worked on installing steps in a steep section of trail
while I worked on renovating a mostly level section.

Blountville old tractor show on Saturday. Every year it shrinks. I keep
expecting it to disappear. I left at 12:30 to go to the funeral for
Charlotte Chew. A traumatic event. My parents are gone, one brother is
gone, peers are going.
However, looking at the pictures they were showing of Collins and Charlotte
over the years inspired me to come home and coerce GSH into donning her
(handmade by her) wedding dress for a picture.
