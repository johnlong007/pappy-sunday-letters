May 21, 2017 · Sunday

Abnormal

10 photographs·311 words

GSH dozed off while sitting in a rickety chair on the deck. Fell over and
landed face first. Her glasses cut her eyebrow leading to fine black eye.

Fortunately she had a Monday appt. with Dr. so they cleaned and bandaged
the wound.​ Now, a week later it is not near as impressive.
I was glad that she could take such a tumble and not break any bones.

No AT work this week and no do-gooder.

We did make a run to Erwin and took more ramps to Flo, went up to see Lisa
but missed her.

Another day, we went to wine and dine with Meredith.

​Sort of do-gooder, Dave and Carol came by to get a ramp donation. I also
contributed some firewood to their cause.

The garden is started. One row, just like last year. Also a couple of
things by the drive.

​ No work on the '49 or the '63.
A lot of work on the crawler. A lot of wasted motion. But, I did get it
running. I now know that the fuel filter will not pass fuel after water has
got around it. It may look new and clean but it will not pass fuel.

​A fine event at the park Sat. PM for the graduates.

In more exciting news, I lost my shade changing glasses. I think I knocked
them out of the door storage compartment of the Miata. Made two trips to Wal
Mart to see if someone had turned in as might have been lost in their
parking lot.

Then my left hearing aid quit working and I launched a search for the book
which came with the units . No luck there either. However, I decided to
switch and put it into right ear. Worked fine. Now I am hunting for ear
drops.

Clutter clean up continues, slowly. ​
