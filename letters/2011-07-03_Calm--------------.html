July 3, 2011 · Sunday

Calm -------------

6 photographs·264 words

For reasons I will not detail, I cannot get into AOL on the old dial up PC. So, the old dog is trying a new trick and for the first time will be using the new, giant screen LED PC. We shall see how it works.

For my Monday do-gooder I went to Blountville annex to mow the pasture. This trip led to a couple of things. Thing one is I broke my last shear pin and had to use a high strength bolt. Of course, it works fine but one worries that the next week link will be a lot more expensive. The other thing was working on the side of a hill. That caused the tractor to run out of fuel for thew second time since 1980. I had thought once was enough but does not seem that way.

For AT work I went with Kim to cut weeds and blowdowns on her section of trail. Seven miles of walking, mowing and cutting . Since it is a wilderness area no power tools are allowed.

After all the work involved in dragging the giant maple down from the hill and splitting the trunk I decided to convert it into chunks for the willing workers to split into firewood during summer camp.

Another worthwhile project was going to Seymour with Dwight to inspect a clean up job he had done on his Mom's house and property.

Speaking of summer camp, Wayne came over Saturday to put up the deck cover. I believe it is the best job yet.

Sunday, we all rested !
