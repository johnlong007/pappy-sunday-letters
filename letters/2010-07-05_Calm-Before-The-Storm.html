July 5, 2010 · Monday

Calm Before The Storm

9 photographs·357 words

As you may or may not remember, last Monday we were on the road coming back
from the Frozen North.
So ,in a sense, my week started with AT work on Tuesday.

The Red Barn shelter requires the longest travel time of any part of our
section. It is a popular site and can handle about 40 people in the barn.
There were two groups doing tent camping when we arrived. There were about 10
of us to work. The main project was weed mowing, brush lopping and water
bar clearing.

I worked with Dave.You can see the red barn in the first picture and
Grandfather mtn in the second.

Tuesday we did serious do-gooder work cleaning the roof and gutter for a
LOW. She is a delight to aid and abet. This Sunday the church is having a
birthday party for her, her 90th. Still active. still driving and still picky.
Wants her house and grounds just so. Reminds me of a close relative. No
pictures as I forgot the camera.

On the home front, the major activity was raspberry picking and mowing. I
mowed with String trimmer, push
mower, riding mower and the tractor with bush hog.
You will have to imagine the newly mown grass.

Somewhere along, I helped Bob deliver the stack of cherry lumber stored in
the Ag building to J'boro to be kiln dried. Something about the dreaded
powder post beetles.

On an exciting social event scene, Herb and Kaylee came to visit us and the
corn patch.

As sort of usual, we went to Seymour to try out the new and improved engine
in the drag car.

Pat was not at all happy with the track. He made two slow passes shutting
down about 1/2 through each time. Pronounced the track a loser and loaded
up vowing to return no more.
Other folks were equally impressed. Note the cell phones at the ready.

Sunday was July 4th. All interested and available family assembled at D &
D's for grilled ribs and corn with appropriated side dishes followed by a
spectacular display of pyrotechniques.

All in all, a most satisfying week.
