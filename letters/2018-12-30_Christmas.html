December 30, 2018 · Sunday

Christmas

11 photographs·230 words

As you may remember, last week I issued an appeal for night before
Christmas pictures. Many responded and I/we enjoyed and appreciated.
A late arrival which I do not believe was sent to all.

On the home front, not being versatile as the younger generation I did not
get a picture of the old folks together, But, here is GSH night before
Christmas watching "Taggart" on you tube. Check it out, she got hooked
while we lived in the UK.

This one came from Niece Lisa.

Another taste GSH brought back from our travels was an affinity for soft
boiled eggs.Recently I saw advertised a device for removing the top part of
the shell from a boiled egg.
The first attempt made a somewhat gooey mess.

After the new and improved help arrived (and watched a you tube video).
success was complete. Device was a lot of fun and is a big hit.

Another present was field tested with surprising results.

We got just a bit of '63 work in between eating and present opening.

Saturday was a modified version of our traditional family Christmas. I
thought it went very well. All our children, most GC with a taste full
selection of spouses and most of the GGC.
