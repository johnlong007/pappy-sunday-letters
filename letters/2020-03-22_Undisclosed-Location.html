March 22, 2020 · Sunday

Undisclosed Location

11 photographs·422 words

Ponder this, many of the people on this list do not know to what the title
refers.

Two big changes for me this week, the number of update received( ALL
appreciated, at least by me ) and no Sunday church service to be found.
Several report they connected to an online service but I resolved to cut of
my nose and spent my time constructively grumbling and pouting.
Next week I am going to try online.

At last ! the rootball is disconnected from the tree trunk.

Using a 16" chain saw on a 40" trunk is not an easy task. I had to keep
splitting off chunks to get saw body access.

Under the heading of everything takes longer and costs more than it should,
I got the Bobcat out to move the chunks away and found it to be low on
hydraulic fluid. Disappointing as it had been back to dealer for repairs
for leak repairs quite recently. To get it back in service Niece Lisa
procured and delivered a 5 gallon container of oil. Perhaps the leak source
can be isolated.

Getting away from the stump allowed getting back on the '37. My efforts
directed toward getting ready to get it running.

Cleaned out the bed.

Installed the heater, the younger generation would not know heaters were an
extra cost option until early 50s. rear bumper was an option along with two
windshield wipers. The wipers were vacuum operated which essentially meant
they did not work while going uphill in the vehicle.

Painted the cardboard door panels and checked starter operation determining
that my only 6v battery is useless.

Made another survey of the kingdom this afternoon. Max found a new front
door for one groundhog hotel. I thought he was going in to drag one out. It
is in the middle of lower RR patch.

The ramps are spreading nicely.

GSH had an escort on the walk down the drive. Max was out also but he runs
everywhere at a high rate of speed.

While we working in the shop, GSH came over to me with a piece of tile
wanting to know what it was.

When we lived in Renton in 1959 we bought a little house. GSH decided the
bathroom needed upgraded and installed tile around tub.
We still have a few pieces of that tile. You never know when one might need
it.
