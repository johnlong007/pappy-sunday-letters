May 1, 2011 · Sunday

Once More With Feeling

10 photographs·210 words

This time critters first.

While we are on the sensitive side of life, we have had plenty of rain so
the flowers are doing well:

You might be interested in a garden picture. The front garden is intended
to be harvested during Summer Camp. I planted a bunch of things this week.
One item was corn carefully selected to come in July 1st. Unfortunately, I
looked at the package after planting three rows and found it is 60 day to
harvest, not 70 day which I thought.



For AT work we went back to the Pond Mtn wilderness area.

Two days were spent doing tractor registration at Gray. Numbers were down
again this year. But, it was still fun. However, the second day was bright
sunshine. I sat behind a white table all day and obtained a painful sunburn.

We have decided to remove the large Oak at the turn up to the ramp. I cut a
number of the branches but am going to need careful planning and perhaps a
more agile helper to finish the job.

The last picture was taken today. After an eventful experience is home form
school.
We went to Iron Mtn. gap and dug ramps then on to Herb's house to visit the
goats.
