Re: AL Update- October 31

3 photographs·1329 words

I have long wanted to run a marathon but never got it done. Ran a 1/2 in
1990 in Indy along with 10,400 others.

Now my running is not much more than fast walking. I can sustain a 12-13
minute pace.

Delighted with all the checking in from hither and yon. I understand that
most keep in touch with parents and siblings via texting but the old folks
at home also like to keep in touch.

It probably goes back to our Scottish Highland heritage when the clan had
to stay united. That carried over in to Appalachian culture with close
family ties. I wonder how Catherine's parents worked out those issues. I
know it it does not seem nearly as strong in GSH's side of the family.

Anyway, I appreciate being kept in the loop.
