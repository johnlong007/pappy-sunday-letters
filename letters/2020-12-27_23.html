December 27, 2020 · Sunday

23 !

10 photographs·350 words

My last day at ECC was Dec. 27th. 1997. I had , made a 10 year plan , had
purchased the truck and the Miata. Never dreamed I would need a 23 year
plan.

I worked more on the 37 front fender. 83 years of dents. I could have done
much better and easier had I worked on the fender dents while I had it
removed from the body. Coulda, woulda shoulda. Glad I am not in a hurry. We
have plenty of firewood and the stove keeps shop the warmest room in the
house (when I have a fire going).

Some years back I issued a decree that if one comes in, two must go out
(cars are tractors). I need help. Wayne moved the latest into storage
beside the '63. Note the '57 has a seat and a seat back. made driving much
easier. Maybe even safer.

Al the slots are full. 63 has battery, tags and insurance. Need better
storage.

Twas the night before Christmas here also.

I bought my first Christmas present for GSH in 1954. By now, I know a lot
better what works and what does not. This year a box of candy . It has a
diagram showing each piece. 72 pieces. She has been know to hoard candy for
years. I needed help from Wayne and Diane to even get her to open her
presents. This box should last all year.

Proof of my observation that old people don't change, just get more
pronounced the way they are.

Christman snow, 11 degrees . Sheets froze as I was hanging on line. But
pretty scene.

The windshield gasket is now cemented in place. Next step will be to try
windshield to determine if gasket is actually going to do the job.

To the mailbox. Got a Christmas card from Lil sister. Our birthdays are
coming. I am torn, really want to go see her but do not want to infect or
cause concern.
