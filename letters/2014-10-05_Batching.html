October 5, 2014 · Sunday

Batching

5 photographs·235 words

I took a lot of pictures this week. I have sorted and narrowed but still
have several to attach. So, think I will attach 5 then another note with 5
more.

Nothing new to report from Herb this week. he did come over to deliver a
box of apples and toenjoy watching me try to keep the MM running. I did
feed him a BLTC sandwich. We will go back to Duke Friday so they can do
their own testing. I will need to work out a dog and cat plan.

Most will remember Larry Miller. Heard today that he died this week. He and
Sharon had sold out and had a house built back in their hometown.

GSH has lit into a fit of decluttering (inside, I have my own fit going
outside). She stuffed the VW full of stuff which we delivered to Goodwill.
Stuff included the chair set she had just finished restoring. Perhaps some
will remember tea parties using the chairs.

For a break from the decluttering pressure, we went for another moonlight
cruise on the good ship D&D.

GSH and Laura left Friday for a bike and barge outing in Germany. For a
historic first, GSH got her stuff packed two days early. She did not have a
bag to check. I have heard it reported that once more Laura's checked bag
got misplaced at CDG. A rather slow learner.
