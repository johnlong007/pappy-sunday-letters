October 12, 2014 · Sunday

Barely Normal

12 photographs·357 words

Herb and I went to Duke on Friday. Last time we went down and back via
interstates. This time more as the crow flies. straight across the
mountains down and back. Somewhat shorter and quite a bit more scenic most
of the way.
They did a bit of testing and a lot of talking. As it stands, he is
scheduled for surgery this coming Friday. A long slow process and was told
to expect to be in the hospital for a week or so. Stay tuned.

One of the people with whom we talked was Julie Blackburn (Little). She
said LLLL was friends with her big sister, Lislie I think , and they had
spent the night at our house. Anyone remember. I have her email if anyone
wants to check in.

Some will be glad to know that Lassie did indeed come home. Several things
got accomplished while she was gone. One was cleaning off the landing.
Another was cutting down some wild cherry trees at Blountville estate. Now,
I have to get them sawed up and moved over here into a dry place.

Another tractor left with a one way ticket.

Cat and dogs did very well with just me to tend tio their needs. One day I
saw the visiting black cat out front. Tigger cat was watching warily. He
followed all around but at a distance.

I went with Pat Loven to cut a blowdown.We drove on forest service roads
all the way to the AT and only had to walk a short distance to the downed
tree. But, still took over 1/2 day.

In case you were wondering. I did not have to eat cold Pork 'n beans all
week.

In case you were wondering , when a tree falls in the forest and you are
listening, it does make a noise.

John, Maria and Jake came over today. John and Jake checked out some new
and old toys.

Meanwhile, Maria was getting a cram course in domestics. Just to cover all
bases I suppose. This is the same girl who did a one person show for the
Cookeville philharmonic. Thursday night.
