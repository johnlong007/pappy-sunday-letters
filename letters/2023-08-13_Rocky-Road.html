August 13, 2023 · Sunday

Rocky Road

13 photographs·403 words

The blood work after a week of Chemo was so-so. The whole week Has been
somewhat difficult. Back in for another round of blood work tomorrow. we
shall see.

I have been reading up on Hummingbirds. Turns out the best states for
viewing lots and a lots of species are the hot, south west states. Just in
case we have any family out that way. Especially bird lovers.

Pretty soon, Steven is flying off and will land upside down but intact
we hope. I predict he will love it.

Three young bucks. I named them Cliff, Christopher and Charlie.

Some time back, I backed in behind the stacked firewood. Coming out, I
managed to not see the guy wire. It put a serious crease in the tuck
bumper. While waiting for the errant truck Master cylinder we decided to
embark on a busy work project. Wayne gotdrawn in to pulling the
bumper assembly from the frame

Disassembly was a significant undertaking.

Fortunately, I had replaced the bumper some years back and saved it. We got
it out, cleaned it and decided it would be easier to repair than the guy
wire damaged bumper. Stay tuned.

Truck minus bumper. The deer hardly let an apple hit the ground.

My gas powered string trimmer has become more than I can handle for long
sessions. So, an electric power unit has been procured and sent out for
field trials. I did not tell the operator that I usually find 2 or 3 yellow
jacket nests while running the string trimmer.

I spent hours planning the perfect site for 3 tomato plants and enlisted
semi pros to assist in procuring and planting, It has not been a rewarding
experience. Katy's pineapple pig, in the center, was dug up and flung out
twice, But is has struggled back to life. The other two look batter but no
tomatoes in sight. Talked with Don at church today he said the season is
winding down, Rats.

The Laura leftover plants look better but are not producing.

SF gave us a plant, It shed all its blooms but is back in business.

PBBD prepped the site along the front and selected the plants as shade
tolerant, They also shed blossoms but are now coming back.
