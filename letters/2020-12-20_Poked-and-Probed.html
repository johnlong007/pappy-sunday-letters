December 20, 2020 · Sunday

Poked and Probed

10 photographs·410 words

Back to Costco for a routine follow up on the new hearing aides. I am
pleased and impressed. First off, they are very prompt and efficient.
Later I went to the hospital for a CT scan. It also went well, prompt and
efficient. I believe as long as no Dr. is involved things move right along.
A few more hoops to jump through before anything actually happens . At
least we hope so.

To get the Christmas season off to a fine start, we got a package from Katy
!
Homemade jelly for GSH and home grown hot peppers for me. How personalized
can you get ? By good fortune we were having soup beans for supper. The
perfect test for hot peppers! I pronounce them Goldilocks. As you can see,
Max was quite interested in my reaction.

With more time confined to indoors I am doing a bit more work on the '37.
After being embarrassed when Pat painted the '63 and all my not very good
body work was exposed, I decided to try to improve some of the more obvious
defects in the '37. The body is very sound as to rust but in 84 years the
fenders got a lot of dents and dings.
It remains to be seen how well I can do.

I captured a willing worker to help with the windshield gasket.

Wayne delivered the seat frame for the '57. It is a power seat assembly the
likes of which I had never seen. One motor which drives 3 cables providing
a 'Six way" power seat. I was able to get the motor running but did not get
the drives working.

But to get it out of the shop and in a safe place, helpers arrived to
position it in the car. It is heavy, maybe 60-75 pounds.

The attaching nuts got on underneath the car. A job for an agile helper.
Wayne is going to be way in debt.

As all know, getting a haircut is a problem in the current situation.
Fortunately, my fall back, substitute barber made a house call for me. The
timing with cold spell is such that I am having to considered giving myself
a waiver to wear a head covering inside the house.

Here is a bird's eye view of the cat.

Merry Christmas to all.
