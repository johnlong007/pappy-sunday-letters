Very Average Week

Once upon a time, in a previous life, I was responsible for a group which
handled all customer complaints about ECC fibers. One of the men who
actually did the analysis and compiled the reports was found of stating
that all test results on the returned samples were "very average". That
always struck my funny bone.

A very average week.

Worked on the '63(Bob ordered correct paint, doing engine compartment)
Two days mowing around here(no pictures)
AT work one day(Wet and windy)
One day to Erwin(Fiddler on Roof)
Did my frequent driver bit by going to LLLL's to retrieve Laura(funny farm
pictures)
Bugging GSH to feed Fitbit(in the rain)

Where are the grandchildren today? Anyone know?
