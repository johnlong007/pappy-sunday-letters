June 9, 2024 · Sunday

The New Normal

13 photographs·222 words

Chemo week. Monday morning Bailey was my nurse. High school classmate of
Meredith. She has an 18 month old daughter named Matilda (Tilly).

We had a few things on the list for Bobcat wook. Consolidated brush pond.
Need the crawler in operation to smash the brush.

Linda and David showed up. Linda for various good deeds and David for one
big one, mow everything !

Our main task was a carry over, get the disc painted and reassembled.

All put together but a bit more work is required. Some parts are missing
and some incorrect.

To my surprise, the deer have found the apples.

Meredith came by with her little bundle from Heaven. She is not so little
now.

Diane has embarked on upgrading the turn around. Meredith is helping with
planning.
Linda is helping with executing the plan.

The wildflower seeds we were sent are beginning to sprout under the pin
flags. Stay tuned.

Joe is President and COO of the Blountville cemetery association. He
presided over the Decoration Day event Saturday and did an excellent job.

Alan and Catherine came back from adventure travel and did a Friday night
feast on Saturday night.
