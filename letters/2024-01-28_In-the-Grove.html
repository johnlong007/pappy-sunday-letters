January 28, 2024 · Sunday

In the Grove

11 photographs·210 words

After a week of cold weather had us burning the shop stove and fireplace;
it was time to replenish the wood supply.

Also time to make a lap around the Kingdom. Properly escorted..

Don Haas gave me a walking stick. It was a big help.

After the snow cleared off, we went up on the hill to prep the downed tree
for harvest,

I think there is one more slab of wood in this stump.

Willing worker had dental work done . Not yet finished as I understand.

Meals on wheels took pity on us and provided dinner and a show.

We are working to compile and digitize all the Summer Camp information.
John may want to use it for bedtime stories.

A bit of shop work. I think Wayne and PBBD have got the steering gearbox
mastered. I do grunt work.

After the Spring thaw we were able to walk laps in the driveway.

I continued to get 10K or more each day. One day we went to track and I
sort of trotted 4 laps. Aaron may get to escort me through a marathon yet.
