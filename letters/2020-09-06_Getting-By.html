September 6, 2020 · Sunday

Getting By

14 photographs·269 words

I heard yesterday that Kevin Poorman had died of the virus. Kevin was
married to GSH's niece Debby. About the same age as Alan. I heard today
that one of our friends from the trip to Nepal died this week.

For good news, we did get to go to church and SS today.

The log we did not move last week is still right where we left it. I will
try again this week. Cannot devise a one person plan .

For no real good reason, I decided to move some of the wood piles and
stack by drive. Plenty of good hard, hot work.

One pile makes a double row full length of the trailer. About a cord?
We moved and stacked one pile, one pile is still on the trailer and two are
left.

Saturday WWs showed up . GSH swept the deck and PBBD applied a second coat
of sealer.

Another worker continued the car buffing project. Getting close to
completion.

As a reward, Wayne took him for a ride.

Wayne also opened the shooting gallery . He is shooting the little pump 22
I procured and gave her while we were still in high school. Doubt that
would happen now.

Once more, we toured the Kingdom and checked the Paw Paw status. Almost
ripe but not a big crop.

Found a plant I could not identify.

Also found another ground hog hotel.

Dog is exceptionally well mannered.
