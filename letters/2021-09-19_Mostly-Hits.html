September 19, 2021 · Sunday

Mostly Hits

12 photographs·362 words

Most of my discretionary time this week was spent on redoing the retaining
basin.

But not all. I continue to appreciate the family moving back from the
frozen North.

Alan even took his Mom for a ride in the new toy. It is an impressive
machine.

Dave and Carol are retired school teachers who moved here from Ohio. They
now reside in an upscale neighborhood out past Meredith on Rock Springs
Road. They invited us out one night for supper. A really nice evening.
Friends are important.

Family is important also. Saturday Meredith brought the old folks some
bread. While she was here, I conscripted her for a bit of earthmoving with
the Bobcat. She was a really quick learner.
I have always thought if bank goes dry and I have to go back to work I want
to be a heavy equipment operator.I bet she might consider that also.

Came up the drive and found a fiddler on the roof ! Washing the skylights
no less. Getting ready for Winter. Sure did brighten it inside here.

I believe we have about the perfect pets for us. Play hard, relax soundly.

I scooped ou a lot of mud and soggy earth which I dumped on top of the
retaining dam as part of the plan to increase retaining capacity. Then I
put the box blade on the tractor to level the soggy dirt. Promptly sunk in
with the tractor undercarriage resting on the dirt. High centered.
Two hours trying to get it unstuck working by myself. Should have kept
Meredith here.

That all occured on Saturday, I expect my Grandmother would count that as
pushing my ox into the ditch.
Anyway, I snabble another helper and extracted the ox on Sunday.

A survey crew arrived and placed benchmarks for me to use in the retaining
basin improvement project.

Family is also important. UCHS homecoming. I should have gone to the parade.

I believe my potted pepper is going to make it. Tentative plan is to try to
overwinter.
