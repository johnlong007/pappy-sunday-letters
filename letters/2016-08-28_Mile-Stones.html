August 28, 2016 · Sunday

Mile Stones

12 photographs·225 words

Our PBBD had a birthday. She was born just few days after I graduated. As I
remember, Mom, Dad and Aunt G came to the graduation.

I am sure all have been worried all week about the missing trinkets. They
have been found. All accounted for except GSH's high school ring. Save that
search for another day.

For do-gooder this week, we went to visit Dave and Carol Dunham. Dave had
shoulder surgery (again).

The AT willing workers were back in action at Beauty Spot. This time we had
a bunch of young people from Wright State in Ohio. They come each year and
are usually the best group we work with. For a bonus, Wayne's step B-I-L
Mark came out again. I worked with him. Will try to include a before and
after of some of our work.

I have had a couple of 16' foot logs here for a spell. Kept thinking
someone would want for a project. Finally gave up and converted in Winter
firewood.

Would you believe that after training all those wood stacker's I had to
stack it by myself ? I kept wondering if it would pass Kenneth inspection.

No work on the FE or the '63 this week. Going to take a while at that rate.

Saturday the family went to Reservoir Road for a birthday event. Ha.
