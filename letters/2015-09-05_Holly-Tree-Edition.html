September 5, 2015 · Saturday

Holly Tree Edition.

9 photographs·109 words

I dug through my files and found a picture marked 1942.

The tree always seemed the same size to me. Never thought I would out last
it.

It had carvings in the bark on the side toward the house.I think on is 1928.

I took a picture before Jimmy had it cut down.

We peeled the bark from all the other chunks but left on that section in
case someone had a plan on preserving.

One trunk was completely decayed. The other is partially decayed and is
discolored.

It might be possible to polish a section and count the rings. My guess is
about 150.

I await further directions.
