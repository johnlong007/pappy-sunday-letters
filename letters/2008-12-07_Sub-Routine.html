December 7, 2008 · Sunday

Sub-Routine

4 photographs·582 words

Life has been a bit topsy turvy for a while what with wrecks, procedures and
people wandering in, around and through the house all day every day.
Monday we got back into the do-gooder business with an opportunity to
install a grab bar in a shower stall for a lady in our church returning from knee
replacement surgery. A simple thing to do except there was no supporting
structure behind the fiberglass shower stall. There was a closet, with shelves,
and stuff, and a sheet rock wall on the side toward the shower stall. Turned
out to be a four hour job with one trip back here to get a piece of paneling to
replace the mangled sheet rock. Anyway, the grab bar is installed and will
be firmly attached when the house falls down.

The FBC orchestra (which has been thoroughly infiltrated by our clan) will
be playing at both services next Sunday. GSH has been practicing more than
usual, a lot more than usual.

Due to the weather our AT maintenance leader moved the work day from Tuesday
to Wed. causing a conflict with my usual lunch with the GOBs.
It was cold and wet up near the NC line where we worked. Here, Ed Oliver is
clearing a water bar. I went with him to the top of the mountain clearing 29
bars along the way. The rest of the crew moved rocks into position to limit
vehicle access to a section of the trail Then, we all worked to clear
undergrowth along a section where the trail is located under a power line.

Thursday, we had a special lunch meeting of the GOBs going to Ridgewood to
barbeque. Herb came over to reclaim possession of a watch he had left to be
repaired by a contact one of the guys has.
Most all week I plugged along on the plows. I expect people to come from
miles around to admire them at the show in July
The house work is almost finished ! We have been promised a wrap up
tomorrow. I have been recording temperatures. I think we have picked up 3=4 degrees
even though we have lost 20% of the glass area.

I will give a deer and turkey report with out including pictures. (Since I
suppose they are the same deer and turkeys as before, of course, the house and
occupants are the same as before) Never less, the Turkeys continue to ascend
into the beech trees behind the house at sunset and come down at sunrise each
morning. I don't know where they are going in between. The deer seem to be
on the same schedule. I think the deer are eating the persimmons. (Have not
seen any possums in the simmon tree). There were seven deer wandering around in
the vicinity of the tree wham I went out to check on the turkeys last night.

The clutch slave cylinder on the Miata started leaking. I waited for it to
heal itself or for Charlie to come home and repair it. Neither happened so I
took it out, installed a kit and re-installed this afternoon. I just wanted to
prove to myself I could still function while being cold, wet and miserable.
If it leaks, I will blame it on having worked on Sunday.

P. S. Rachel, please note I did not mention the snow.
