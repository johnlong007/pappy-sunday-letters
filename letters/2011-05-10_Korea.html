Korea

We are here. A long but uneventful day yesterday. Left Nashville at daylight and arrived here at dark. All day plust 13 hour time difference.

Overcast and rainy today. Charlie and Kenneth are catching up on news. We are going on an orientation tour after while.

Charlie is still Charlie but slimmer.
