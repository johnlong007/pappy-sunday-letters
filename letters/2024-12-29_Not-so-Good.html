December 29, 2024 · Sunday

Not so Good.

9 photographs·129 words

Diane did a lot of M & R on the Wonder horse this week. I dug through old
pictures and found this one. The little pine dresser door was Anita's bed
and car carrier.

Most of our dry firewood is soft wood, which burns hot and fast. We had to
restock.

Wayne led a contingent of willing workers.

Jon kept the work area clear. We came back the next day and moved and
covered.

G had not missed a beat.

A & D gave a tutorial for benefit of one not yet trained WW.

For the first time since this all started. I came down sick.. A very
difficult few days.
