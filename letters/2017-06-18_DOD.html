June 18, 2017 · Sunday

DOD

13 photographs·515 words

My father was a good man. I still miss him even after all these years. Lil'
sister, Herb and Lil' brother each have different pictures of him and
different memories. I learned a lot in talking with many people while
working on my Pictorial family history. Perhaps I will write it all down
sometime.

Various people had birthdays this week. For my part, I turned 81&1/2.

No AT work this week. Tried to go with the Forest Service people on a tour
of Rich Mountain but could not get act together. They promised to give me
another shot.

In exciting local news, I turned the A/C system on ! The temperature
control makes little difference to me but I do like the humidity control.

Benj came over one day for some applied electrical engineering. Together we
got the '49 electrical system checked out and the engine running ! Rejoice,
first time it had run in many years.

Then we moved on to the IH. More rejoicing, we got it running and found
that the hydraulic system built up pressure so the steering works .

​
Does anyone remember when we put a fence around the horse pasture? I
believe Anita's Friend Rosemary was here helping. Anyway, I long ago took
it all down except the part across the front behind the trailer spot. Now,
that part has been removed. It looks better.

After all the to do about the cherry tree, Anita and GSH picked the cherry
crop. At least the part left by the birds.

Then we took the cover off.

​To rub salt in the wounds, one of the new trees is dead and the other may
by dying. They had been frostbitten when I purchased. I wonder if that has
caused the problem. We went back to Lowe's but no replacement until next
Spring.

But, The new strawberry patch looks really good.

​The Blountville old tractor event was Saturday, I got the blue ford out
and got it ready but decided to not take it. No available eager driver. A
lot of other people made same decision. The show was a flop.

​Except for the ice cream.

Today, PBBD came over and helped GSH on a woodworking project. This week, I
hope serious work will start on rebuilding the deck table. George Reed gave
her a couple of '16 foot 2 by 6's which should be adequate for the job.

Wayne came back from Germany. But, Bob, Laura, Benj and Anita all left
town. I think no one quite knows for sure where Maria is. I did get a not
from Portland saying Gwendolyn has landed. Should be perfect weather there.
Long days, short nights.​

We have an eye witness account that Aaron and Chelsea went through with the
wedding. It remains to be seen if Rachel will follow suit. Stay tuned.
​​

Just for a bonus, here is a picture of one of the richest persons in the US
along with his trophy wife. Not only does he have a trophy wife he also has
12 house cats.
