Muddling Along

13 photographs·273 words

The goal for the week was to get the DB mower finished and on display.
Close but no cigar

Wheels were in sad shape. But, after scale removal and filling as required.
The new tire and tube are mounted and inflated.

Alan demonstrated his welding skills by rebuilding the broken hitch.

The pitman rod was a challenge but now installed and functioning. Final
paint touch up. Now, nothing can go wrong.

The White Walnut tree around which Patricia built her deck is shading the
roof causing moss growth on the shingles. Family tree surgeons climbed into
action cutting back the offending limbs.

After much delay allowing much plotting, planning and capital equipment
procurement, the Last Walnut log has been brought down from the mountain.s

The pool temperature is creeping up . Users are flocking in ! I asked Diane
to get me a mermaid picture, this is her response.

For Dinner and a show night, I wore the hill climbing bike tour tour shirt
which Kenneth gave me. I have been watching the tour highlights each day
looking for him on the sidelines but no glimpse s o far.

Another dinner but without a show. Due to an unplanned family trauma, Wayne
was able to join us.

For this week of Summer vacation, I went to the cancer center every
morning. For various reason, different family members went along. One of
the duties is admiring pictures of various workers children and
grandchildren.

All creatures, great

and small.
