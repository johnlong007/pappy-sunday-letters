Fwd: Christmas hiatus and other news for Kingsport Community Band

0 photographs·237 words

From: Lick, Wendy Wilkinson
To: Kingsport Community Band members
Sent: Fri, Dec 16, 2011 5:49 am
Subject: Christmas hiatus and other news for Kingsport Community Band

Great job last night, guys!! We sounded good, and our audience was
appreciative. One gentlemen commented that our performance is helping to make
their Christmas season a happier one. Jeff Kappa's (trombone) dad was on hand,
just about bursting with pride as he pointed out his son to everyone who passed
by. You made your dad's day, Jeff.

Scot announced last night that the Walk for St. Jude's that we played for in
November, collected $45,000 in donations for St. Jude's! That was almost double
what they collected in Nashville. In fact, the only city in Tennessee that
collected more was Memphis, who not only have us outnumbered but also have St.
Judes right there in their midst. (And even they only beat the Tri-Cities by a
few thousand bucks.) Saying all that was to say this: you were a part of
something big that will help a lot of sick children and their families get the
care they need.

We will not have any more rehearsals the rest of this calendar year. We will
pick back up the second week of January, on Thursday, January 12. Invite your
instrument-playing friends to join us!

Have a happy and blessed Christmas!

Wendy Lick
Secretary
Kingsport Community Band
wlick@eastman.com
