September 28, 2014 · Sunday

autumnal equinox

7 photographs·600 words

No more trips to Duke this week but we do have a plan, sort of. It seemed
clear to me that the Dr's we met at Duke had no knowledge of all the
information which had been forwarded from Dr's here.
It now is becoming clear that even if they do they will still want to
re-run the various tests.
So, back to Duke next week to get some tests re-run. Tentative plan for
surgery the week after testing is complete
.
My idle hands were kept quite busy this week.

Two days working on the AT. Four piers have been constructed in the river.
The original plan was to have the 9 - 20 foot long timbers dragged in by a
team of mules (Wilderness area, no power equipment). That plan has been
dropped and now plan to haul in on a two wheel card with people power (Pat
said dumb ****). Change in plan called for a much smoother trail so a lot
more work on the trail
Pat and I widened and re-enforced a couple of creek crossing but mostly
busted rocks. Glad I can operate a sledge hammer. Pretty much two full
days.
Concrete pads were poured on top of the piers. Now, in keeping it all
"natural" the pads are being faced with rocks. Mostly split to order.

The LBT came with undercoating, I chipped it all off in order to treat the
underneath with a rust hardening agent. This week, I finished re coating
underneath with spray undercoating. It is not nearly as thick as the
original but best I can do. I am now ready to roll it out and try again to
make it run as soon as the MM is out of the way.
Speaking of the MM. After much trial and error, the hydraulic hoses are
reconnected and two attempts were made to get it running. The good news is
it runs good, sounds good and no sign of radiator water leaks. Bad news is
the carb is back in the hands of the repair man.

Friday, I mowed, and mowed, and mowed. No pictures but I am hoping that
will mostly close out the season.

Saturday, The Community Band had a concert at the Exchange Place. Look
close and see if you can spot ant familiar trumpet players. Look even
closer and see if you can spot a Lil Ole Grandmother with grandchild.

Sometime back I replaced the CV joints in the VW (Kenneth and John know
about that). Since then I have been concerned about the left side CV joint
making excess noise and upon inspection decided it had excessive free play.
So, I enlisted a couple of willing workers to swap it out with another new
one.

Disconnecting the suspension to replace the CV joint changed wheel
alignment so after driving to church this morning I enlisted one willing
worker (and GSH) to help with realignment.

Just in case some person in a faraway place does not know, Beth, Jake and
perhaps son Paul (with or without his grandmother) are relocating from
Norfolk to Greenville, SC.
Friday they took the dolly to Norfolk to retrieve their remaining vehicle
and move it to their new house (soon to be a home) in Greenville. They
brought the dolly back today
reporting all went well and the deed was done, well done.

Quite a few interesting pictures from remote (and not so remote) places on
Facebook this week. Keep posting. Also, a couple of even more interesting
updates from hither and yon. The ole folks at home do appreciate updates.
