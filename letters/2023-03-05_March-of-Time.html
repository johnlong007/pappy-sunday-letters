March 5, 2023 · Sunday

March of Time

12 photographs·363 words

A lot of time since living here. Of the four, I was the only one born here.
On the topic, I got a note from Dan. His Dad, my brother Jimmy fell. Broke
his nose, suffered a concussion, got put in ICU and ventilator installed.
Then Flo called a while ago and said she had heard they think he is going
to be oK.

Sure sign of new life. Looks like a good year. I expect fruit trees to get
nipped by a late freeze.

The weather has been good and we have been enjoying it. After over 40 years
of running (???) almost every day I have missed the last few weeks. But,
Thursday Anita covered for me and I shuffled around the block. It sure felt
good. I am really going to try to get back into the swing.

Feeling the urge to play in the dirt, we dug up a spot and planted a pack
of radishes. Turnips to follow as soon as I can get seeds. Catherine has
put in a request for part of the harvest to take to her Mom.

Not only has PBBD arranged for home health to come check on her Mom, now
she has lined up a person (on trial) to come spring me on a schedule to be
determined.

The main target for the week was to finish preparing the drowned giant Oak
for the sawmill crew. the first tem was calling in a sawyer to detach the
10 foot section not deemed to be timber grade.

The next step was determining how and executing plan to move the log to the
woodyard.

To be followed by sawing off fireplace length and splitting.

Our own family snowflake.

Alan and Catherine come over most evenings. Catherine does Martha things
while Alan attends to his Mom.

Another objective was tires and spoke wheels on the 55. Got that all done
and checked out the freshly flushed PS with the car jacked up, A road test
to follow soon. Stay tuned.
