March 8, 2020 · Sunday

Old Dog Tricks

13 photographs·416 words

With the aid and support of Joe, I can and have loaded CDs into the
computer and moved onto the little device which I can then play through the
car sound system.

Perhaps even more impressive, in a way, I fixed biscuits for breakfast one
morning.

Not as good as the ones Diane had made last week as I added too much water
. Made sort of thin but done all the way through and very tasty with
imported (from Maine) jam.

The downer for the week was death of Cathy's husband of 41 years. He will
be missed.

The brush clean up work has about been completed. I used the Bobcat to
gather up and transport the pile which slipped out of the noose last week.

The I rolled the truck down the drive and gathered a load of small limbs.

This time I put a rope through under the load so I could get load off the
truck easily. However, I tied it off to one of the logs which then got
dragged well out of place. Now, I will have to get Bobcat out and put both
log and brush back in place.

Work continued on the root ball. Has everyone seen the movie " Shane" ? You
should.

Work also continued on the '37. I now know that way back when I had the
fenders off body I should have used patience and dinging hammer to remove
the fender dents. Trying to fill (without patience) is proving difficult.

Over the last 20-30 years we have accumulated a large collection of VHS
tapes with the intent to watch "when we get old". It occurred to me that a.
we are old and B. we had no VHS player. Having recently learned about
Facebook market place, I was able to locate a player. Now all I need is
someone to teach me how to operate.

Work started on the guest bathroom renovation project. The room now has
three doors but no fixtures.

I have been pulling clumps of wild onions from all over. Plan to save for
Charlotte. Today we were out and about when I pulled several clumps and
threw in ground hogs fron door. It should improve their odor.

Spring is just around the corner.

Dog and cat escort us on all tours of the Kingdom.
