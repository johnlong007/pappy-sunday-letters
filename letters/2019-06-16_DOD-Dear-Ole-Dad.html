June 16, 2019 · Sunday

DOD (Dear Ole Dad)

14 photographs·508 words

While doing my daily 5 mile shuffle today I thought about what I "wish" I
had done different with my father. About all I came up with was I would
like to have asked him about his life before meeting my mother. I think the
only thing he ever told me was about riding a M'cycle in a cycle drone.

I mainly miss him when I see a weed or tree and want to know what it is.
Also, he could make toys and whistles from sticks.

Happy birthday Alan, Cliff and niece Martha. Remembrance for Diane.

Mostly aggravation on projects this week.

Made a run to Erwin to hunt for items listed as missing by person nephew
James has assessing the '63J. Radiator, Gas tank intake manifold pan, oil
pump and a few other items. Came up empty. Difficult for me to believe
those were not with the car when John first tore into it. My guess is 25-30
K to finish the job.

One of the riding mower front tracking wheels failed. I spent two days (
and $4) trying to find a substitute. After all that I found the substitute
I had procured was too big. Already had determined the two wheels Wayne
dragged out of inventory were too big. Finally put the original back
together with a piece of copper tubing in place of roller bearings. For
now, it is working. MDGB

.
A more successful endeavor was cleaning the interior of the truck. All the
pictured stuff was behind the seat.

Then GSH washed Charlie's mud out of the bed.

And Wayne washed the body (of the truck). (and delivered a load of junk).

The Jay Birds have been robbing the woodpeckers lunch box.

The woodpeckers had to run them off to get a bite.

I made a pole from smooth trunk which Wayne and GSH installed. So far it is
working. Woodpeckers eat while Jaybirds squawk.

I keep working on the 63 to get it ready to paint. Keep finding places
which are not up to my low standards. Worked on the rocker panel quite a
bit. I think it will pass now.

Decided it was time to repair the flood damage in the driveway. The big
news there is that the dog can now jump up onto the tractor and climb into
the seat with me.

Using all my charm (and rank) I persuaded Wayne and Steven to clear
vegetation on our section of the AT. There will be opportunity for others
in a month or so. In the meantime, the blazes need refurbished now. Some
have been defaced.

The worker bees have about closed up shop. I think we may decide to fill
pool before getting the tile work done. BUT , someone is going to have to
step up and learn to manage the salt system. Another item Dwight always
handled.
