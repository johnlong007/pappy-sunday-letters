June 17, 2018 · Sunday

This Father

11 photographs·303 words

A lot of events this past week. As you may know, I have been going to
Memphis for dental work. Last trip, I was fitted for two crowns. See above.
Monday, back for installation plus replacement of four old fillings.
​

​In the evening, in addition to various Memphis dining spots, we go for a
river walk. At the end of the river walk the trail swings around along the
levee beside another river.

​

After we got home, GSH determined she could not locate her dental bridge.
She thought down the sink drain. I removed trap and line with no success.
We have searched the house and cars. Anyone seen a lost bridge? Stay tuned.

​
I did go out on AT work day, I worked with Ted and Paul cutting back growth
beside the trail. We met a lot of hikers. One from Spain, one Switzerland,
One new Zealand and from all around the US.

​In trying to get something ready for Blountville Old Tractor event, I
dragged Wayne and the MD around. Not a lick. Parked it if front of the
house. Then I dragged GSH and the H up drive and parked in front of house.
Did not try to start it. Stay tuned.
The event was sad. 6 tractors and about that number of engines. Last year,
the 25 and we are giving up. At least several of the grandchildren got to
participate. Now, it is up to them.

​

​

​

​
Wayne finished bug damage repair and installed the front door. It is not
going to do much to keep burglars out until some more work is completed.

​

​Today, we had a Father's day event at BBBD's house. It was fun. I hope all
the family fathers had as good a day as I did.
