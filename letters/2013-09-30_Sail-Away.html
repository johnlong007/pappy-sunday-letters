Sail Away

11 photographs·331 words

The Alcan Highway was constructed in 1942. I had just turned six years old.
I do not remember which year but sometime while I was in elementary school
at Temple Hill during the '40's, I wrote a "research" paper about
construction of the highway.Sure wish I had kept it, I bet it was a fine
piece of work.
That started my love affair with going to Alaska.
Many years later, 1972 I believe, we all loaded in the "Little blue truck:
and drove to Alaska. 4,400 miles taking 7 1/2 days, Blountville to
Anchorage. We drove North from Fairbanks to Circle on the Yukon river to
watch the midnight sun but could not actually get inside the Attic circle.
We pretty much repeated that trip in the late '70s.
Then, the pipeline road was constructed allowing access to the Attic circle
by road. So, we went again. I think 1985 taking Momma, and Beth in addition
to the repeats. This time, we made it into the circle at mid night on June
21st.

GSH and I had talked many times about taking the inside passage cruise to
Alaska but had left that until we got old.

Having arrived, we took the cruise last week.

The ship was interesting, the passengers interesting and the crew
interesting. Even the scenery and trip were interesting.

Juneau, Scagway, Glacier Bay and Catc

hikin. Outside
the trip package plus getting there and back, we spent $2. Two newspapers.
The cruise was all inclusive for us.

I will include a tasteful selection of pictures. Full showing available

The trip was timed to best odds of seeing the Northern Lights. But, no
luck. Maybe at the end of the next 11 year solar cycle.
