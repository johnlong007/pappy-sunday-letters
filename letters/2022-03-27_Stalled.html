Stalled

14 photographs·233 words

I have not been able to get the frozen engine to break loose. I have not
been able to get the stuck door open. Time to park this turkey and work on
plan B.

Persons (not named) living in California have gardens .

This picture is of one of the first rows of ramps I planted several years
back. They are alive and spreading. Slowly.

One morning we had a skiff of snow.

Up until today Max had not displayed an upset constitution. Seems to be
getting better but still not eating.

For fun (it is Sunday), we peeled a couple of logs. Ready for the sawmill.
These are big 'uns.

During the high winds yesterday, PBBD heard a crash up on the hill behind
the house. She went to investigate. Looks like sawyers might be interested.

I need to do more earth moving, leveling and seeding on the drainage basin.

A couple of weeks back I took the car to Nissan and got an offer of 11K for
trade in. Then I got this flyer from the same outfit. @$ K I wish I could
go back and harass them.

What I can do is roust GSH out to go walk on the track.
