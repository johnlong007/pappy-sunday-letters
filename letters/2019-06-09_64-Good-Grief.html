June 9, 2019 · Sunday

64 ! Good Grief

12 photographs·396 words

We have had several family wedding recently. I bet not any of the involved
parties thought about what things will be like after 64 years. When I look
back I think our working together to get me through school then our working
together to get GSH through school have been most rewarding.

Now, standing on the sidelines and watching family life unfold is rewarding
but very different.

After many fits and starts I have finally been fitted for and ordered new
specs. But, told my left eye will require cataract repair before long.

I got the pathology report from the two sites Dr. Sago dug out. Bad news is
one was basal cell and one squamous. Good news is she got them all. Again.

The car went in for 40,000 mile service this week. took 15 months. Linda
certainly did us a favor pushing that purchase through. As a bonus various
family members are getting a lot of usage from the Miata.

The 63 is getting close, reckon anyone will want to drive it?

The truck came home with a load of rubble. Fortunately I had a couple of
fine helpers available to help unload.

The dirt pretty much filled the gullies in walnut field, Now, I need a bit
of seed to try to get sod established. Again.

Really exciting news is that Herb's old IH is gone. A fellow (and his wife)
from Va. came after it. I liked both of them. He teaches Calculus at a
community college. Farms, has animals and old tractors. His wife helps. He
was delighted with the tractor . I am delighted to see it go to someone who
appreciates it.

The easy with which it fire up inspired me to get back on the crawler
starting project. Turned on the electric booster pump, cranked on the
starter and black smoke came out. It is now getting fuel ! Did not really
try to start. Stay tuned.

Somewhat to my surprise, all the reflective tape and other items worked on
the black raspberry patch.

Thermal energy storage area work is winding down, Next will be removal of
the deck.

Lest we relax.

For her 64th. Look what I was able to do for GSH.
