Biking in Germany, Holland, Belgium & Nordic Walking Along the Mosel!

Frank's Forum

When the daffodils are coming up, you know that our biking season is not far away. In April, our tulip tours take off, the bikes will be waiting for you, and our guides will welcome you in Amsterdam. Ever since we started running bike tours, 46 years ago, who is counting, we sold out the tulip tours. We already have people signed up for the tulip tours in 2025, not too early, as these tours are very popular. I used to lead tulip tours each year, and each time I was overwhelmed by the gorgeous fields of colors. Sometimes you can see them from the airplane just before landing. Having said that, call us to book this tour in Holland, my birth country.

Another issue I wish to discuss with you are e-bikes. Loyal readers of the Sweep may remember that I was pretty down on e-bikes, but that has changed. I have used an e-bike on Cape Cod (my vacation) and lately in Florida where we biked with a group. Yes, they are heavy, which is the most common question we get. However, you don’t have to carry them on your back when you cross a river. It makes your pedaling so much easier and lighter. I am not going to get into the technical stuff now, but I suggest you visit a local bike store and ask them to explain the gears, then take a test ride. The first time I tried this, our bike store is on a steep hill, I faced the top and took off, I was flying. So, my friends, you heard it here, if you feel you need a little extra push bicycling, ask us for an e-bike.

Below, are two remaining tours for this year that have not quite filled, all others are waitlisted. Additionally, check out some of the 2025 tours we have opened. There will be more tours offered as we are waiting for more information from Europe. Lastly, we are considering a Cape Cod tour in early September. The owners of the first hotel have sold the Inn in Brewster to a large organization which I am not comfortable with, so we are now negotiating with other hotels. More information will come soon.

GERMANY: BIKE AND BARGE, THE MAIN RIVER
August 16 - 24, 2024
Land cost per person, based on sharing a cabin, $1700  REDUCED PRICE!
Single supplement, $1000

Many of you have traveled with us by barge in Holland, Belgium, and France, and have been clamoring for another destination where you can stow your gear and relax for the week. We have been listening to your requests and here’s your chance to check out our bike and barge tour along the Main River (pronounced like “mine”). Overnight on a great barge, savor the bratwurst and beer, and get set for some easy pedaling on dedicated bicycle paths along the river from Bamberg to Aschaffenburg. This is a destination that could be a setting for a fairy tale. Magnificent castles perched on hilltops, half-timbered houses lining the riverbanks, and magical scenery. The Franconian wines are fantastic, the scenery together with the best pastry shops, all make for a super vacation, and you live happily ever after! If you recognize a value, call us quickly. Big sale!

GERMANY: AN INTRODUCTION TO NORDIC WALKING ALONG THE MOSEL RIVER
October 4 - 12, 2024
Land cost per person, based on sharing a cabin, $1900  SUPER SALE !!!     
Single supplement, $1100

This course will introduce you to the pleasures of Nordic walking, step-by-step, with a nice instructor (Frank), who is certified in two countries and has the patience of a saint. If you enjoy leisurely walking you will even more enjoy this new sport with all its health benefits. There is no healthier exercise than Nordic walking: it relaxes your neck and shoulders, lowers your resting heart rate, and, most importantly, it exercises most muscles in the body. The poles used with Nordic walking will save your joints from wearing out, starting with your ankles, your knees, your hips, and your shoulders. Fun and great exercise, this trip will acquaint you with the Mosel River most visitors don’t see. Walk along this scenic river, on quiet trails, past vineyards and villages with half-timbered homes. Days are relaxed: we may barge a bit, have instructions in the proper Nordic walking technique and walk a few hours a day. You are out in the fresh air, looking at the ever-changing scenery as your Nordic walking style improves daily. Our home for seven nights is a marvelous barge, with comfortable cabins, all with private bathrooms. Sit on deck and wave at the locals as you enjoy a glass of regional wine, life is good. Any questions, call Frank. Only a few cabins left, Nordic Walking will make you healthier!

HOLLAND: BIKE AND BARGE, TULIP
April 3 - 11, 2025; April 17 - 25, 2025
Land cost per person, based on sharing a cabin, $2950
Single supplement, $1600

You will marvel at a rainbow of colors as far as the eye can see, bring a good camera. Add to the above the wonderful barge with meals you’ll look forward to each day, comfortable cabins, and a coffee, tea and hot chocolate machine. I cannot recall how many Tulip tours we have done over the years but I can tell you that some folks have done this trip more than once. In Gouda, you’ll explore a fascinating church with old stained glass windows, but you’ll also get to sample the famous cheeses. The store will be happy to vacuum-seal your purchase so you can share your bike stories and cheese with family at home. This tour will bring happiness into your life, we predict.

HOLLAND: BIKE AND BARGE, 70 PLUSSER
May 1 - 9, 2025      
Land cost per person, based on sharing a cabin, $2950
(minus your age if 70 or over)
Single supplement, $1600

Our goal is to provide an enjoyable and relaxing experience, so here’s the scoop: we’ve picked a really flat destination so the biking is easy, and we are offering accommodations on a barge (no energy required to pack and unpack each night). Start the day with a leisurely breakfast, bicycle at a relaxed pace for a maximum of three hours each day. We usually barge in the morning, then leave the barge and bicycle to our overnight destination. This is Frank’s favorite northern route through the province of North Holland, crossing over to the island of Texel, and returning along the shores of the IJsselmeer. We are offering you a SUPER price by allowing you to deduct your age starting at 70 (based on the date of departure) from the already low land cost! Is this for you? If you wish to take it easy, join a group of like-minded, retired friendly folks, you have come to the right place. Do join your peers.

BELGIUM: BIKE AND BARGE
September 10 - 18, 2025; September 17 - 25, 2025      
Land cost per person, based on sharing a cabin, $3100
Single supplement, $1600

Firstly, the Belgians speak Dutch and French, and they eat mussels; secondly, the architecture will take your breath away, that’s why people from all over the world visit cities like Bruges and Brussels. Lastly, the Belgians make chocolate that makes you wonder why you have been eating Hershey bars, be sure to buy lots before going home (there is ample opportunity at the airport in Brussels). This barge is one of our favorites with a wonderful deck from which to enjoy the surroundings, and delicious food (not mussels). You will be bicycling through a region blessed with flat terrain, exploring towns and villages going back to medieval times. Time indeed has stopped many centuries ago in this remarkable country where asking for a beer is an incomplete question: there are too many varieties, so ask for help. Folks who have biked here loved this picturesque country and so will you.
