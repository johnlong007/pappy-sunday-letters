July 20, 2008 · Sunday

How do you spell RELIEF

7 photographs·186 words

Summer camp has come and gone.

Sis did not come this year.Beth made a cameo appearance Monday. Aaron was
out of sight if not out of mind.Allison was off tooting her own horn (so to
speak). We would have had a very difficult time w/o Katy.I sure am glad we
waited for her.

The big process improvements were an outside change room for the girls to
avoid overload in the bath room and a shower set up for before and after
swimming. The pool stayed MUCH cleaner than ever before.

We also tried more special activity things than before. Mostly it went well.

Saturday, Katy had a birthday/coming home party. Sunday, we walked around
the kingdom and slipped up on a very large ground hog perched in one of the
apple trees.

It is going to be a bumper year for fruit and nuts. For the first time, I
have an English Walnut tree bearing. Also, the chinquapin bushes is are bearing.

This week, back to life in the fast lane.
