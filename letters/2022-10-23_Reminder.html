Reminder

12 photographs·302 words

The family has been scattered even more than usual this week. I am
appreciative that when near and/or far we get notes and pictures. The Aura
is great.

But, lest those in faraway places with strange sounding names forget where
their roots are connected.

Look closely, the persimmon tree is still loaded. We have several but this
is the closest to the house.

Another reminder.

This picture is a reminder to "don't force it, get a bigger hammer". The
real wheel hubs are keved on the tapered end of the axle.Hubs tend to be
very difficult to remove. In spite of the hammer we broke the puller and
failed to get the hub off the axle.

With a replacement puller, a long cheater, an Impact wrench and a hammer !
We got it !

The winner in only two rounds. The right side hub popped right off.

We had several chilly nights so I kept a fire going .

In order to keep the fire going, one must have firewood.

To have usable firewood, one must have a chainsaw. This stack has large
difficult chunks. I am in hope some trained wood splitter will show up and
do the willing worker routine.

Some years back, I salvaged three logs from a downed cherry tree. While I
had the saw in operation, I lopped the end from one of the logs to see how
deep the rot had penetrated. Looks like about 1/2 gone,

For a feel good story, we no longer ro with Sanford for breakfast at the
Burger Bar on Friday morning. I was just getting started on breakfast
Friday when Sanford showed up with McDs finest.
