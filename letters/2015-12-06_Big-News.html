Big News !

I always wanted a tri cycle toy tractor for Christmas as a kid. Never got
one (as a kid).

This week I made up for lost time ! A toy for the ages !

this week for another operation.

The best part of that trip was stopping to see James and his family. They
are fun to be around.

Stay tuned.

fast but smells good.

Sunday PM we went to the old tractor owners Christmas dinner. Violated all
the UT sensitivity rules.

Anyone spot Martha?
