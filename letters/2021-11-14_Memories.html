Little Sis has finished all the tests required before the panel of
judges decide if she lept the last hurdle or tripped on it. Should get a
go/no go answer before long. Stay tuned.

We went to Wed. noon lunch with the GOBs again this week. and we went to
Friday morning breakfast at the Burger Bar with Sanford and his Cloudland
classmates.I went to Perkins and church Sunday. Getting back in the grove
(rut).

Digging out long sleeve shirts for change in the weather, I came across
this one. Anyone remember ?

Or maybe these?

We made a swap with Albert of a strip of land beside his driveway for the
hill in front of our house. GSH planted a row of seedling along the new
property line. This maple is one of the seedlings so I fel sort of
responsible for the leaves. PBBD raked them out into the open where I
chopped with the mower.

No. 1 willing worker helped put in the truck. I think she is about over the
sticks and stones adventure.

Finally the shredded leaves were used to cover the freshly seeded
retaining basin dam.

I seeded with Ky. 31 fescue, would have been better to get out much earlier
but may sprout in the Springtime. May not.

The far corner is a quagmire. Not sure why or how that is going to work out
as it dries out. Stay tuned.
