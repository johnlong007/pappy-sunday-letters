This is Diane, using Pappy's computer.

The first half of my Annual Christmas Open House Event will be Saturday Dec
23, from 2:30 pm until 5 pm, at 196 Ellis Road. (The second half will be
sometime during the week after Christmas, not the 26th, 27, 28, or 29???,
and I'll send another note after the vote is in for the best day.) The
usual menu of chips, vegetable nibbles, etc., will be available, along with
the usual small-present buffet. Mostly it's to come & be sociable, thus
aiding in efficient visiting of many relatives at once for the out of town
crews. I would like to figure out how to fit in some eggnog, but I'll need
help with that.

Many of you on the list can't possibly come, and you will be missed!

Many of you could come to both events, and that would be excellent; please
do.

There is lots of hand sanitizer sitting around to keep hands very clean,
and we all also need to be more careful about any sneezes or coughs around
Pappy. If its a pretty day, lets be outside as much as possible. Life has
to be worthwhile, and seeing family is way up on the worthwhile list here.

Hope to see you,
Diane
