January 15, 2023 · Sunday

Age (and Mileage)

17 photographs·720 words

Most will remember that we used to go to Donna's to celebrate hers (13th.)
and my (15th.)birthdays! For the last few years Laura has been making a
cake with hard icing (just like my mother made) and I have taken a portion
to share with my sister. For the last few years going has become really
important to me. " Better go; one year it will be the last trip".

This picture was taken last year. Sure hope I can go with the class and
dignity she maintained until her last breath.

Just as a matter of interest, we went on several mission trips together.
One year on our way to India we met at the airport in Detroit where she had
her cake while awaiting our plane ride.

This year's cake. Thanks Bob and Laura ! Plenty left if anyone ones to
break off a piece.

Hal Ketron gave me the Oliver shirt.

I have noted several times having shortness of breath. Since the medical
system has already done all the easy things to my heart over the years Dr.
Ponder sent me in for a Lexiscan Stress test last Week.It was a new one to
me.Since the technicians did not get excited, I assume it was pretty
routine. I will have one more test in a couple of weeks then go in to hear
the rest of the story. Or, at least, the rest of this chapter.

My biggest free time project was digitizing 2676 pictures from negatives.
The pictures are in no order at all, and would benefit a great deal from
being worked with one at a time. If anyone wants to take on the project,
tell Diane, she has the backup copy.

We did one small task on the 37 this week. One flex brake line nut was
rounded off. With a lot of effort, we got it removed. A new replacement
inready to go into place.

About one and one half tasks on the 55. Diane procured PS flush fluid and
cleaned the pump. Now, it must be installed and the entire system flushed.
Stay tuned. Did I mention the leaky brake wheel cylinder turned out to be
an axle oil seal? New seal has been obtained. Waiting for installation.

Procured concentrated Simple Green and cleaned a few carburetors, These and
about a dozen more going back into the 30s are now stashed on the newly
cleaned top shelf in the shop.

PBBD worked industriously cleaning, sorting and organizing in the shop. We
have here a 50 year collection of fan belts. All the clearly defective ones
have been tossed and the remainder safely and neatly stored

The slab pile as constructed stick out each end from under our cover. One
warm day, we took the big chainsaw and trimmed each end of the pile. All
under the watchful eye of GSH.

Ale the sawed off pieces were loaded into the truck and are now stacked in
the handy access wood pille outside the shop.

Note to Paul, and anyone else with cause to sharpen and axe, Never go near
a grindstone. Never. Get a good , flat file and sharpen by hand in smooth
even strokes toward the body.

Here, you see two things, Why I sharpened the axe and PBBD learning a new
skill.

Kenneth asked about the dog barking so I have been paying note to the
occasional barking. One day, she got all excited so I turned her out. When
I checked later, she had defeathered, skinned and consumed a squirrel
except for skin, head and tail. I snabbled the remnants.

The cat is persnickety.

Our new neighbor is a deer slayer. It appears he cleans and leaves remnants
where they fall. Enlarge this picture and count the buzzards. I might worry
if they were circling over me.

A & C mostly come over week nights. One night they brought an appetizer
tray. Sanford came by Friday morning with to go breakfast from the Burger
Bar and also with a container of Tommy Toes.

Sunday birthday lunch ! open face hot dogs with the works !
