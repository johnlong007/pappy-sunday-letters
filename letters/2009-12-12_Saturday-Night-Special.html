December 12, 2009 · Saturday

Saturday Night Special

8 photographs·350 words

Anita is building a barn, or should I say another barn. At any rate, I get
called in from time to time to cut support posts for her.

The social whirl continued. I made a run to Erwin. Went with Herb to check
on Aunt G. She was in good form, while we were there, Flo came in. So, it
goes along in Erwin. GSH checked in on Hal and Sue, said Hal is wearing down.
Monday night, the Gray Station Old Tractor owners club had their Christmas
dinner.So much for this years parties.

We dove to Dechard to get ready for an early trip to St. Thomas in
Nashville on Wed. I went with Linda to pick Rebecca up at tumbling class. Took her
picture doing a double back flip with a 1 1/2 twist (or something like
that).

My heart surgery appears to have done what it was supposed to. After a full
battery of tests, the Dr. pronounced heart "perfect". But, during the last
2-3 years my blood pressure has drifted up to the 150-160 range so he is
putting my on blood pressure reducing pills. I go back to St. Thomas in one
year.
Friday was AT work .cutting blow downs after the hard wind (which tore up
my canopy and the supports).

Last picture, lolling in the sun.

Quite a bit of progress on the car but not real visible. Fuel tank is in
and after MUCH difficulty stopping leaks we have a brake system. The clutch
is going to require further work. No pictures of the car or the turkeys this
week (but can be provided upon request.)

Saturday was the date for the annual Christmas parade. As usual we were on
the front row.

It appears to me that someone is out of step.

I am heading out early in the morning to go meet Katy in Finland. We are
going to celebrate the Winter Solstice at Santa Claus village in Lapland.
Grandmother has suggested it would be simpler and cheaper to go in the closet,
close the door and take a picture.
