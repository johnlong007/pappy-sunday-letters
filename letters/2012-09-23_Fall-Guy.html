September 23, 2012 · Sunday

Fall Guy

10 photographs·548 words

I spent a lot of time mowing this week. I decided the mower was tearing the
soft ground on the steep hill pretty badly so resorted to the newly
repaired string trimmer once more. One day while I was up on the hill I
heard a series of loud, crashing sounds. Like big trees falling on the hill
behind the house.
It was a bright, sunny, still day. It appears the impact of the butterfly
flapping its wings in the southern hemisphere finally got here. Anyway, I
can attest that when a tree falls even if no one is there it makes a
racket, if it brings down a couple more, it makes a lot of racket. Also, if
the dogs and GSH are working on the hill just below, it sends them
scurrying.

I got a note from Herb saying he had got into Yellow Jackets and "got stung
all over" . I would like to note (crow) I mowed into two separate nests and
only got stung one time.

Monday was Dr. day. Both GSH and I went to our retired Dr.'s replacement.
As best I can tell he is still in Jr. high . At least he appears to be
about that age. It may take him a while to loosen up. He asked if I had
anything wrong. I told him when I brought a list in for Dr. Stirman he
would just look at it and tell me "you are getting old". So, I quit trying
but would make a list for him next time. Never cracked a smile.

Tuesday it rained so I spent the day working in the shop getting ready to
move back in for the upcoming cold, dark, short, dreary , wet, days. note
the towel on floor picture. Alas.

Since it had been projected to rain, Carl moved AT work day to Thursday .
For Thursday, I had another Dr. appt. Dr. Reeves, the plumbing Dr. Another
zero on the PSA . He said the chance of recurrence now is very slim. If it
does recur and PSA starts to go up gradually (perhaps 1-2 units/year) it
would probably be best to not take any action. So we talked about his
children for a few minutes. Same time next year.

Lots of hot peppers. GSH made stuffed peppers one night with one of
everything. Really very good but some are very hot.

GSH wanted to winterize her blue berries so I gather a load of pine needles
for her usage. Maybe a bumper crop next year.

One of the better moves has been the purchase of professional window
cleaning supplies.It took 1/2 day to do the entire house but was well worth
the effort. I did not do the skylights but will do that when I remove the
shutters in a couple more weeks.

We went to Stan's house for a SS outing Sunday PM. He has gone into the
Alpaca rearing business. Had two babies born yesterday. It appears the two
mothers cannot sort out the two babies one of which is not being nursed.
Vet is due out tomorrow to lend help. Stay tuned.

When we got home, the door stop was covered in sawdust. It appears we have
company wanting to move in with us.
