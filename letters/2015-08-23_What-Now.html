August 23, 2015 · Sunday

What Now ?

10 photographs·596 words

We are getting close to the moment of truth. I ordered Carolina Reaper
pepper seeds. Herb got them sprouted in the voc. school greenhouse. He has
some planted, I have one, Laura has one and Linda has two.
I took a picture of one pepper on Herb's plant. I have not heard a report
from Linda but expect it should be ready soon Stay posted.

Where I grew up we had a big, tall Holly tree. It was just in front of the
house and according to Mom was there long before her time. We had a reel
type pusher mower which we used to mow the lawn. Barefoot.The holly tree
had initials and maybe dates carved into it. The Auburn tree killer must
have passed through. Jimmy called and told me the tree was dead. He is
going to have it cut down. If I get a chance, I will attempt to count the
rings. Makes me sorta sad.

When I went in to Dr. Sago with Herb she took biopsy samples from his face.
The results came back positive. So, back in for more slicing and dicing.
Overall, he is doing fine. Going a bit bonkers from not being allowed to
drive. I suggested he drive a tractor to town but he has declined, So far.

I went in to see Cardiologist this week. He was to go over results of
stress test. He said I did great, should last another 10 years, come back
in one year to see him and we would do another stress test in 5 years.He
also spent 5 seconds looking at check in results and said he was going to
double my blood pressure dosage. I am going to talk with GP but have no
intention of doubling anything.

For AT work this week we went back to same relocation the ASU students did
and worked on bringing section up to our specs. One place they had looped
below a set rock outcropping. At my insistence, we beat it into submission.

We had a pretty good crop of grapes this year. While I was off doing AT
work, Laura and GSH converted the grapes into grape jelly makings.

Wayne delivered the LBT to Exhaust system place and brought it home with a
fine new system installed. It really sounds good. The Summer Campers who
were going to take it to Alaska had better start saving vacation !

Various members of the Long family came into town for the race weekend.
Friday night, I went to P & G'ing and got to talk with one prospect at some
length. But, no pictures. Saturday, Charlie came over with a person in tow.

I have almost all of the items on the Studebaker punch list handled but the
power steering pump fitting was leaking. It is ready to try again. Stay
tuned. The LBT is back in the shop. Engine compartment cover is mostly
installed.Hope to have it ready to prime in a week or so.

Last Spring, Laura gave me some of the imported purple potatoes. Today,
Anita and Beth came over and dug some. We are all awaiting the results of
their tase testing.

Wayne had a birthday, Diane plans to have one this week. GSH mane some
apple stuff. We took it and Beth out to D & D then came back by W & L. A
fine Sunday.

I understand we have family scatted from California to Switzerland , Texas
to Wisconsin.. If anyone hears from or spots one, let me know.
