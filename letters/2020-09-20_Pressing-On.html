September 20, 2020 · Sunday

Pressing On

10 photographs·314 words

OK, on your toes ! What is wrong with this picture?

Farmer Lisa keeps telling me she wants to spruce up the (for her) ole home
place. The house is on the edge of South Indian creek in a field my Dad
purchased from Lincoln Taylor in the late 40's. The dark far background
mountain is Temple Hill. Herb took possession of the place after
retiring from military and proceeded to accumulate stuff. A lot of stuff.
Some stored in barns and sheds, some outside.

This trip, we worked on sorting lawnmowers.

Last year I built a retaining basin at the upper end of the soccer field to
try to stem the runoff from heavy rainfall. It has helped but not
eliminated the problem. So, plan B.
I scraped the sod off the front edge of the soccer field and proceeded to
move the retaining pond dam .

The new retaining dam is about 2 feet high and all across the front edge.
The plan is to smooth and seed it. I may enlarge the upper retaining pond
as that is where I expect the retained water to soak underground.

We got a rain Thursday night which will bring the project to a standstill.
It is to be noted this plan includes a fair amount of hope which is seldom
a good omen.

The deer harvested our strawberry crop for the last two years. I have given
up and planted turnip seeds where we had strawberries, I wonder if ground
hogs like turnips.

Sunday is a day of rest. I cannot rest by doing nothing. But, on Sunday the
something has to be fun not work. Today, the fun was loading another stack
of wood on the trailer.
To be unloaded later.

Look close.
