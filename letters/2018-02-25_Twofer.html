February 25, 2018 · Sunday

Twofer

15 photographs·369 words

No notes last week as we left on Sunday to go visit Dr. Allison in
Nashville. I intended to compose after we got back but decided to wait and
see who noticed the missing notes. Guess what !

In spite of the resounding silence, here goes.

Last week was mostly work on the '63 missing parts Each door and each
quarter window have a place for a molded rubber seal. Two were missing. I
hunted, I hunted a lot. They would have been removed maybe 20 years ago
when Alan painted the car. I finally gave up and fabricated replacements.

While we were gone, Sanford took the seats to a friends shop and had the
seat covers GSH had sewn put onto the cushions I ordered from Gary Goers.
Some assembly work to completes before installation in the car. Stay tuned.

​​Back to Memphis. I have a several years tooth neglect backed up. M & R
are going to take a number of trips over next couple of years. This wee she
cleaned my teeth and did two fillings. Painless dentistry.

We also eat out a couple of times, visited Bass Pro shop, walked along the
Mississippi at sunset and watched the Peabody duck walk.

​​

One day we washed all the windows. Inside and out.

Also the skylights(outside only).

​​

Another day, George came up to try out some new (old) toys.

​He is a good shot. I am not.
A spot on my neck became irritated and quite sensitive. I sent a note and
Picture to Dr. Sago on Thursday evening. She replied with "come in tomorrow
at 1"
I did, she lopped it off and fried the spot. I appreciate her.

Is noted elsewhere, PBBD came by and fixed died beast gravy. Also a pan of
brownies.

​The AT maintainers dinner was Friday night. I wore my Tractor Supply blue
jeans. One of the young'uns made snide remarks about my turned up cuffs.

​So I came home a and copied a picture from 1953 UCHS annual and sent to
him.

​For sorta do-gooder work, We hauled two loads of firewood from Don's house
to Wayne.

​All in all, a good week here in River City. ​
