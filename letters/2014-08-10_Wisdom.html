August 10, 2014 · Sunday

Wisdom

11 photographs·313 words

Everyone knows " If you want to get there, take the Miata, if it does not
matter, take the VW".

This week we went with Anita and John to visit Kenneth in the frozen North.
While we were there, Maria flew in from New York and made the trip back to
Tennessee with us.

It was a great trip, we got to visit Kenneth, meet a bunch of his friends
(confirming that birds of a feather do indeed flock together) and tour
parts on the school as well as Madison.

As if by magic, a brot event occurred one evening .

​

​
Another day we were taken out on Lake Wobegon in a sailboat.

Walking around the campus we found an apple tree, a special apple tree.
Clearly John has great expectations.

One evening, we sat by the lake to watch the sunset.

When we visited at Texas A & M I saw a lot of young people in uniform. At
Madison, many of the girls wear uniforms. Not so much the boys.

​
One unexpected but delightful experience was meeting A number of young
students who knew Cliff and getting to hear a number of Cliff stories.

Because of the car problems and because I was concerned about Little Dog,
we came straight home missing our planed stop to see Christopher. It will
come.

All, well almost all, the animals were glad to see us. The cat pouted a bit.

I got up early Saturday morning, went to McD's to get GSH two country ham
biscuits and a parfait.

Sunday evening she returned the favor.

We have a row of potatoes. Yukon Gold, none have been harvested. The
Marigold flowers and other flowers left over from Katy's wedding are in the
same row. All free for the taking.

And, lest you think I forgot.
