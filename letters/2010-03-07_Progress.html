March 7, 2010 · Sunday

Progress

5 photographs·122 words

Being one handed, left at that, is no fun. I got very little done this week
and did not do anything to make the world a better place.

I did go back to dr.,twice, and have to go again tomorrow.

The report on my hand was quite positive, it is healing well. Should be
back to useful function in about a month.

Can you see the shattered fingertip bones ?

For the benefit of the inhabitants of the frozen north

I piddled some on making interior panels for the car. GSH also
contributed.

Sunday afternoon it was warm and sunny. So, we rolled the car out and chief
mechanic had a go at getting it running. It did not start.

Stay tuned.
