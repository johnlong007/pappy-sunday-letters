February 4, 2024 · Sunday

Good 'N Bad

11 photographs·311 words

It has been 3 months now and hurts worse than day one.

I think Monday was week 5 of this cycle. Results were the best yet and I
have felt better physically than for the last couple of years. I did go in
to see Dr. Sago again
She zapped the spot on my lip (again) and said she has it under control.
But one more time may be needed.

Meredith brought her little person and came to help pull my Ox (or
something) out of the PC ditch. I had a delightful time holding the turnip
termite. It all comes back

LLLL decided we needed more and better exercise, and fixed us right up. To
be continued, stay tuned.. .

Trouble really likes Bob. Check the dog grin !

We have a long to-do list. One item was to repair the Ag Bldg entry floor.
The floor had pretty much rotted away near the entrance.

Look at the finished product with no capital outlay.

Remember my mentioning a deer carcus up beside the upper pond ? Trouble had
in mind keeping it near by for future needs.

Wayne is managing M & R on the 37 steering gear box.

I am trying to get many loose ends tied . A big one is getting the indoor
solar storage unit back in operation. At my request, Bob agreed to manage
the project. He had experts in to study project.

The weather was lovely, we went way up the hill, dragging several chains
behind us and were able to drag a big log out. While we had the rig in
operation, I dragged a pine log out of the brush pond. We decided dry pine
burns clean and smells good.
