September 25, 2011 · Sunday

Fall Has Fell

8 photographs·637 words

A short week.

After getting home with the lawn mower I unloaded and checked it over pretty
good Gave it a grease job and mowed a bit.

So far it looks encouraging. I want to be able to scurry around the steep
parts and mow up under the trees. I intend to install a roll bar, seat belt
and some sort of limb protection shield before venturing onto the steep
parts. Also, it needs new rear tires.
Some instruments are not working but Dwight thinks he will be able to find
and correct those defects. So far, so good.

AT work was a challenge. It was back to the same work site atop Roan
Mountain. About 1/2 mile down the trail we are putting in one of about one
dozen zig zags. Where the trail switches direction, we put in a switchback.
The switchback we worked on has a large (real large ) granite rock
outcropping.

We worked on trying to split it at the seam for about four hours, gave up
and built a rock bridge beneath it.

Then, we decided that work is not up to our standards so this week we are
going back with renewed determination (and more tools) to split off the rock
at the seam. Stay tuned.

GSH worked on her table most of the week. Now, she is working on the legs.
Just think, after finishing the table she plans to build several benches. I
believe this project is going to finish her career just as the Studebaker
finishes mine.

Speaking of the Studebaker, the power steering fittings have held the
project up for several months. The rack JW intalled is a "Mustang " unit.
Turns out there are several varients depending on model year and Ford
supplier. We messed up a fitting on the one JW put in the car. Today, Wayne
helped and we pulled the unit out in just a few minutes. (Had I taken it out
to install the hose fitting in the beginning ALL this time and money could
have been saved!). Alas.
Dwight salvaged a unit form one of the race car projects but it has a
different steering shaft attachment fitting. So, I went back to visit Tex
(my friendly junk yard man) and got a rack with the correct shaft but looks
rough from having been out in the weather for years.

Now, it is time to mix and match and get this thing back together and on the
road.

The chestnut trees are loaded this year. Katy and Ross came by to harvest
some chestnuts as did Wayne and his kids. I decided rather than pick and
choos persons I would just pick and choose chestnuts.

For do-gooder, I worked at the church on a shrub planting, mulching project.

Saturday, the Forest Service put on a "picnic" for all the people who worked
in this area. Dave Dunham and Carol came. Dave is suffering from Lyme
disease. Also, his parents in Ohio are both in the short rows from lung
cancer which has spread widely.

When we started to go to the picnic driving the VW it was making a terrible
noise at every bump and every turn. I went about 1/4 mile, turned and came
home to swap for the Miata. I was quite sure something had not been properly
tightened after the people installed the new clutch assembly. Today Wayne
came over and after doing some other things we took it for a test drive. Not
a sound. Turns out we had gone to Blountville Saturday to hunt for Walnuts
and had a bucket with three walnuts in it in the back of the car. I had
removed those before the test drive.
