November 28, 2010 · Sunday

Eating & Wood Splitting

12 photographs·235 words

Our church leadership has decided we do not need a Sunday night service.
So, A new record for starting early.

First things first. The new dog is coming along fine. He is very different
from my dog. That is good. He likes to go outside and be outside but only
if he has one of us to be with him. He is an indoor dog and a people dog.
This week he had a fine time playing soccer with various persons and once by
himself with a punkin.

I did a serious repair job on the wood splitter. Split a whole bunch of
wood without difficulty then decided to try a really tough piece.

Stay tuned, next time I am really going to make it solid.

Dave was out of town and AT work was canceled due to a rainy Tuesday so
nothing to report there.

Thanksgiving ! Already covered but I will include the outside activities.
Hint ; the log splitter was busted.

Friday we had a leftover day. When the word got out L & D drove up to
participate.


A couple of pictures which each person can use to add a caption:

The social season has started and went into full swing for us. The
Blountville Old Tractor Owner's Club had our annual Christmas dinner today. Since
it was a lot like last year, I am not going to include a picture.
