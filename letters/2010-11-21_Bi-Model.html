November 21, 2010 · Sunday

Bi-Model

11 photographs·244 words

A fine week

Log Splitter:

A lot of time working, cutting, grinding and welding. But, not yet back in
service.This coming week for sure.

The Studebaker:
A bit of forward progress, I trapped another willing worker with nimble
fingers to help install fasteners in far away places.

I also went back to the place (junkyard) where I obtained the trunk latch
to check on some more parts I need. Some I located, intend to go back
tomorrow and negotiate.

Trail work was renovation of sluffed off trail between 19E and Bishop's
hollow.

Saturday I went with Herb to visit a friend from a previous life in the AF.
He lives across the wide Mississippi in Arkansas. We left Kaylee with
James and Lisa in Nashville, Visitd with Ed then back to Union City to visit
Sis.
I really enjoyed the visit with ED. Herb had flown 101's with Ed in the
UK, Later, while Herb was still in 105's, Ed transitioned into F-111's and
flew briefly in Viet Nam. The F-111 is the airplane I worked on in 63-65 at GD
in Ft. Worth. Really interesting to talk to a pro who had flown them
during the introduction into combat.

Back in Union City Martha and Robyne fed us supper while Sis was having
Lowes's install a new washed dryer unit.
Then, she fixed a bed for me and her companion.

Back to Nashville Sunday PM to see the family and pick up Kaylee.
