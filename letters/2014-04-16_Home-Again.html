April 16, 2014 · Wednesday

Home Again

9 photographs·594 words

Last week was spent making a great circle route visiting various and sundry
grandchildren with a couple other family members thrown in for good measure.

I took the matron mobile to Alabama for an Allison concert last Sunday.

Janet literally scooted in for the concert but left in such a cloud of mist
that I did not get a picture. Laura and Bob came in a rental vehicle in
which Bob left to go off to seek his fortune. Meredith came in the Obama
car, we swapped with her sending her back to Pensacola while we headed for
first night lodging with LLLL.

Spent the night with Colsons then went to Union City for second night with
my much beloved by all, little sister. I visited Dixie Gun Works but did
not spend any money.
Next morning we headed West using the GPS device. Soon found ourselves at
the end of a gravel road facing the Mississippi River. I now understand hod
people drive into rivers or lakes following GPS directions. Soon. a ferry
showed up to take us across the river ($14) dumping us on the bank of the
river in Mo . on the edge of nowhere.

Continued across Mo. on a diagonal to Columbia arriving just in time to
walk across town and eat supper in a local hot spot.

Spent the night in the BOQ (rather spartan) then headed out to find Dwayne
and Rhonda, friends from the bike ride in Germany. I have told several
people about them, that he farms 300A by himself, that he has a corvette
and a BIG tractor that I wanted to see. I was wrong, he farms 1,700 acres
by himself.

It is a picture book farm. They took us on a limited (time) tour and feed
us lunch. It was delightful visit. I want to take GSH and go back.

Drove across Mo and Okla spending night in Norman, then across Kansas and
Texas. Arrived in Rockport, Texas near College Station (Where all the inns
were full due to parents weekend) . Rockport was named after a nearby large
rock of which we could not locate nor locate a picture.

Texas A & M has about 50,000 students enrolled. A large portion are in the
corps (or corpse for Obama fans). It was of great interest to me to tour
the campus and look at the buildings and students.

Almost none are fat.
They almost all stand up very straight.
All we encountered were polite, pleasant and helpful
I only observed two smoking.

We sat in on a seminar presented by a local businessman (Mr. Brown). Look
close and you can perhaps spot Benj.

We also attended a UT/A & M softball game.
one poster caught my eye. I shall include picture for the benefit of Beth
and Jake.

The cadets wear uniforms. Benj explained quite a bit of the code and
symbols. About the only part I retained is that seniors wear boots.

Touring the G.W.H. Bush library was a delight. It covered his life and
career. I would like to visit as many other presidential museums as I can
manage.

Left Benj and proceeded to Pensacola to unswap cars and visit Meredith.
Another fine visit. The big event (outside the pleasure of her company) was
the Naval Flight Museum.

The plans called for staying over and watching the Blue Angels practice but
that was cancelled due to weather so we were homeward bound.

As you might guess, there are many more pictures along with a detailed
narration available upon request.
