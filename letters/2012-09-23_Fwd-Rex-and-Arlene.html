Hello fellow bikers!!

I'm happy to announce that Mom and Dad made it home Thursday evening. In
style, I might add. The flight insurance provided a male"rescue" nurse.
This gentleman, picked Mom and Dad up at the Hotel Ross in a lemo,escorted
them to the Frankfurt airport,boarded the plane and enjoyed business class
with Dad, (Poor mom was back in the cheap seats), navigated them through
the Detroit airport, rode with them to Kansas City, and then escorted them
home in another lemo! Dad really enjoyed all the upgrades provided by the
flight insurance!! (something to think about when booking your next
flight!!) Dad didn't have any health issues during travel and is
currently feeling fine other than being tired. He had an appointment with
his doctor Friday. The doctor wants Dad to have another scan to insure
that everything is as it should be. They hope to have that scheduled ASAP.

A great big hug and thank you for all the care and concern you showed us
during this "adventure". The support was appreciated! I hope to ride with
you again. On a less eventful tour of course!! Rhonda
