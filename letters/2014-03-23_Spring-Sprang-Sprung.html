March 23, 2014 · Sunday

Spring, Sprang, Sprung

12 photographs·492 words

A number of adventures this week !

For non-adventures I did not see Herb and I did not do trail work. As far
as I know, both are still where I left them.

I suppose the big news is GSH formed an attachment to a tick on her leg.
Not just any tick mind you but a deer tick. As is my want to do my first
response was to take a picture.(After she had peeled it off).

Having suffered along with Brother Dave and his Lyme disease for the last
few years we went off to Urgent care with no delay. They prescribed anti-
biotics and ointment.

The anti-biotics took five days and ointment is still being used. Current
wound picture:

Really glad we took immediate action.

It has been called to my attention that the cat has been getting all the
photo ops. So, this week how about a picture of the sock snabling dog?

As well as the cat.

LLLL has been whimpering about wanting to get out and play in the dirt.
Perhaps she came by it honestly as I decided to go help her. Turns out she
had another willing worker show up !

I understand the Northern contingent have not learned driving with manual
transmission vehicles. So, Janet will be the first in her family to have
this skill set. She took to it like a natural. A couple of hours in a big
parking lot with one of our fleet and she will be ready for the road.
She also enjoyed talking to the animals.

While we were there LLLL plowed her garden and planted some really
expensive but reportedly really healthy ,purple potatoes.

Even more exciting, the Colson family farm took possession of another
donkey while we were there. Two opposite sex donkeys should provide the
foundation for many stories to come.

Perhaps equally exciting Davy Colson, King of the Dechard frontier is now
beginning to earn his keep by putting meat in the pot for food on the table!

Back in River city , GSH has found a way to occupy her idle hands.

While work continued on the LBT. Almost all the patching is now complete.
As soon as I come into possession of a large piece of sheet metal to
replace the missing lower right side it will be time to move on to the next
step.

I keep an eye on Facebook as some post from time to time. Others, in far
away places with strange sounding names must have sailed too close to the
edge.

Anyway, all is better here than could reasonably be expected for the age
and mileage involved.
