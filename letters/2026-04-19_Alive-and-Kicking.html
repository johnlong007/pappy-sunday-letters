Alive and Kicking

12 photographs·136 words

I am not sure about the sparrows' return to Capristrona, but the
hummingbirds have returned to Blountville.

A delightful surprise. Long time hiking buddy Jim Foster brought us a sack
of Ramps.

He also said they are getting hard to find due to weeds. So, for those who
intend to go on a ramp harvest trip, better get moving.

Also delightful. One of the girls with whom I worked many years ago stopped
by. We shared many memories.

Spring has arrived at our nature reserve.

As near a free lunch as it gets.

Cub consultant.

Family handyman.

Serious wood splitting this week.

He is still alive!

I clean parts, Diane paints them.
