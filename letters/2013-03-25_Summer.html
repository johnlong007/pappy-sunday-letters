Summer

0 photographs·154 words

GSH and I are healthy enough and secure enough that we can do anything
(but not everything) we want to do. So, it becomes an issue to sort
out what we want to do.

For me, working with our children and/or grandchildren tops the list.
Traveling is good but working is better.

Summer Camp has been rewarding to me beyond all expectations but it it
seemed to have reached the point of diminishing returns. I would still
like to continue some means of keeping things tied together.

Consider this:

Any who want to come spend a few days or a week during the Summer
months, June ,July and August ( singly or in combination with other
eligible persons ) work out their proposal and let me know.

Or, For those (next in line) who have not gone on a GP trip ,John,
Rachel, Benj, Gwendolyn, Maria and have something in mind, let us
know.
