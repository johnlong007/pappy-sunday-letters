November 8, 2015 · Sunday

Plan Progress

8 photographs·428 words

I mentioned my 10-5-3 year plans some time back. In the last extension
there is an item "get rid of at least two vehicles or tractors each year".
Yesterday I made an inventory to see what remains.
Unless I missed something there are:

8 trucks
11 Tractors
14 cars

Meaning I need 16 more years. I am going to need help. A lot of help.

Some years back we put the hillside property into some sort of forestry
reserve program. A Forestry program prepared a 10 year plan. I think maybe
Dwight has a copy. Recently I had the same person come study this place and
prepare a 10 year Forestry plan for it. The plan has now been filed with
county. We have a copy if anyone is interested. Actually,he sent to me a
copy by email and could forward I suppose.

Not much happening on the home front. The turnips are ready .
GSH installed a cat door. Little dog can pass, big dog cannot, cat has not
been trained.
I took the Studebaker to Blountville on a trial run. No problem so far.
Herb continues to wait patiently. Actually, rather impatiently.
AT work was at the Red Barn shelter and was a lot more fun.

Monday night we had dinner at the Dunham's. I took a supply of The hot
peppers. Ate over 1/2 of one. Pegged my hot scale.

Finally got the oil changed and truck greased.

Bob took the vw in to determine cause of shimmy during braking. Warped
rotors. The Advance rotors I had taken back and exchanged before. Next I
am going to try to get them trued. See no point in exchanging (again).

Truck is giving started trouble. Eric said the starter solenoid should be
rebuilt. I believe that will have to be put on the to do list.

Today, we went to a concert. I really liked it. I recognized a lot of the
music as being Bugs Bunny background music. One item onthe first ten year
plan was continuing education. For several years we went over to ETSU one
day/week for old folks seminars. One had to do with music . It was great.
The teacher told about a lot of movies and how they came to have such good
music.

Anyway, it occurred to me that out of all the family musicians this was a
first.

I tried to check in on all the ball games yesterday to catch a peek of band
members are in stand performers but did no good. Anyone see anythink I
missed?
