May 5, 2013 · Sunday

Another GGC

12 photographs·428 words

I suppose we will all get the pertinent data by and by.

(The race was rain delayed so I am late going to press)

For the routine items, I spent an entire day trying to fit a window into
the driver door of the '37. GSH helped by lining the window guide with
soft,fuzzy material. After much effort and frustration I decided the window
glass was too wide and took it to the glass shop to get 3/16 inch trimmed
from the edge. Stay tuned.

Monday night was senior night for the DB baseball team. So, we went to the
ball game. I could include pictures of proud Mom and Dad escorting ball
player son out onto field. Or, I could include picture of ball playing GS
hitting a line drive. But, I think I shall include a picture of deer in the
headlight look of a trapped base runner.

Although I had told myself I would not go back to Dennis Cove to beat on
the giant rock, I ended up going back and beating on the big rock. But, we
took some better tools and tried more finesse and less brute force. I think
one more day will do the job.

Here is a section we have completed. We chip the rock down, install a
locust side log, fill behind with rocks, cover with dirt/grit and declare
victory.

Friday night was the grand unveiling of the new drag car. It looked good,
sounded good and ran good. Quite a number of GOBs stopped by to admire it.

Here is the first burn out and launch.

I think the car looks as though it should be starring a Steve McQueen movie.

Today DB had awards event for their seniors. I got to bask in reflected
glory from not one but two awarded seniors.

(Forgot to turn the flash off).

In the garden, the potatoes are peeking above ground, some tomatoes are
planted and the first four rows of corn are in the ground. If the crows,
coons, deer and weather cooperate we should have corn about July 4th
In other exciting news for the week, I got a lot of mowing done and
completed step two of the carry permit process. No pictures there but did
buy a new toy.
