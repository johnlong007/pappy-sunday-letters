February 26, 2023 · Sunday

Pale and Anemic

13 photographs·478 words

Laura escorted me in to see Dr. Ponder's PA on Monday. Learned nothing
much. She had a heart monitor device installed which I wore until Thursday.
It may just be lack of exercise, I am getting about the same steps each
week that I was getting each day. Pretty much every decent weather day we
go to the track for 8 laps

I got notes and some pictures from hither and yon. Some have left the West
coast to go skiing and some have stayed there to ski to work I understand.

Fortunately, the family is being replenished !

Early on, Nancy and Donna would tell stories about their big sister. She
hoarded things, especially candy, which, according to them, she would save
until not edible. I try to keep her from continuing that practice, eating
the last piece of Allison Christmas candy and it is only the end of Feb.

GSH built the pond as well as the decks. But she is no longer able to
maintain either. The pond filled up with limbs and leaves. It is now all
cleaned and restocked with at least one frog.

It has rained a lot this week. The drain line has been running full time
for the last 10 days. PBBD can tell you how much water has been drained. To
be prepared for possible heavy Spring rains, we dragged out another, larger
line and checked it out. We do not want water standing for days on our new
grass.

Albert's donated pine tree is now mostly knotty pine lumber. You can see
Albert on his tractor in the background. He had been up on the hill
ploughing his garden site. He stopped by to talk. He said seep potatoes are
four times higher than last year. He was impressed with the Bobcat operator.

With the proper supervision, we can mount a tire on a wheel in less than 10
minutes using no tool except a rubber mallet.

We now have a set of four mounted and ready to install. I think I shall try
on the 63 then on the 55 to see which I like best.

Those who have been in the shop but not recently will be amazed. The
project to organize the house and get rid of clutter is off to a fine
start. I am very pleased. About all I told PBBD is to not pay any attention
to me. When in doubt, throw it out.

Also, the generator and PS pump are both installed, connected and tested
with the car engine running. Flushing has not been completed and no driving
test. Stay tuned.

David is in Florida. With his camera.
Lifestyle of the rich and famous.
