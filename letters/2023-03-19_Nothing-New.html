March 19, 2023 · Sunday

Nothing New

13 photographs·396 words

I went in to see our GP Dr. Shafer this week. No answers to my short term
endurance issues. He did suggest trying vitamin B12. Neither GSH nor I took
any pills until a few years back. Now, I have a handful, morning and night.

Weather was variable this week. One afternoon was nice leading to the
sawmill operators springing into action.

That, in turn, led to spectators coming in from far away places. Like
Knoxville.

Of course, our longstanding rule of" you can stay or you can go but you
cannot stay and watch".was imposed. Fortunately having seen this crew in
action, I fed them the toughest pieces we had left to be split.

My reel to reel tape player went MIA many years ago but I kept a stack of
tapes. Some I would like to digitize but needed proper equipment. I found a
machine on Ebay in Knoxville ,which Steven checked out and procured. To no
one's surprise, it needs drive belts and wheels. The local experts have
repair in-process. Stay tuned.

This gnarled pile is the product of conscripted WW's. Another day, PBBD and
her helper got most of the rest of that section split.

PBBD likes to learn and practice new tricks. I had taken this saw to the
local pro and paid to get the chain sharpened. Upon usage, it cut well but
more curved tham my sharpening had ever caused. We shall see how this
sharpening session works in practice. Stay tuned.

It has been confirmed, her mother taught her well and she can indeed bake a
cherry pie. I wonder how many Summer Campers can still bake a cherry pie?

I think I mentioned the 55 brake booster in the shop for repair. Work is in
progress. Another stay tuned.

Our CNA is working well in my view. She is also deciding if we pass muster.
She says she likes going to the track. It started raining lightly during
this trip. Chris, note the hat. Looks better I must admit.

We have rich and famous family scattered all over the world. Some of them
send pictures to the little ole folks at home.

Rather than another deer picture, how bout this picture.
