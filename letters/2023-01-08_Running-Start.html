January 8, 2023 · Sunday

Running Start

13 photographs·420 words

Early in the week several of our grandchildren came by and gave me the
opportunity to learn more about life in modern times. I am grateful and
appreciative to and for each.
I find it especially interesting to quiz Kenneth about his search for the
Theory of Everything. I read everything I come across in the field. This
article just today.

https://nautil.us/the-remarkable-emptiness-of-existence-256323/

The weather this week was mostly warm and dry.
Good news and bad news on the 55. Wayne came by and bolted the generator
back in place. Also, the material is on hand to flush the PS system.
The bad news is that we have a leaking brake wheel cylinder. Rats.

Along with the warm day, I started the tractor for the first time in weeks.
It started right up and all the lights, and systems worked !

A group from church made a guided tour trip to Israel. Wed. evening they
gave their trip report. I went mainly because I had promised Karen Loven I
would. She is shown presenting in the picture below.

As a bonus, I got to church before the program started. One of the kitchen
ladies packs a to go dinner for me to bring home,

Note the knife.

Cleaning and organizing continued in the shop..

Some future generations may appreciate the effort. Some present might.

For our first 30 years or so I took pictures and had prints made. Then, I
switched to slides all of which Allison digitized and are now available
upon request.
All The prints are here in books and boxes. Some time back I procured a
device to copy and digitize the negatives. I thought I had finished but in
digging around in the back room I came across another box of negatives. It
will take me a while to finish but at some time all the pictures will be
available digitized as well as the original prints.

While Wayne was here, I got him involved in checking the status of the
Poplar and Oak tree trunks waiting in line for the sawmill. The poplar is
rotting fast. Oak is still quite sound.

PBBD has decided to persuade me to enhance my flexibility by leading me
through an exercise routine. The dog has proven an apt student.

GSH likes to go along to supervise our work program in progress. Lunch on
site.
