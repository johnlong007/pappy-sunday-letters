October 23, 2011 · Sunday

Surprise !

6 photographs·564 words

When I said I wanted reports from A to V, I was thinking of Alaska to
Vienna. It appears Aaron and Vanessa thought I meant them and decided to
upstage all the rest.

The humdrum news around here is that I closed the fan shutters at the end of
the hallway, Started closing the drapes at sundown and kindled a fire in the
shop stove on evening. We have not had a frost here and I have not yet
picked my pepper crop.

I said no obvious leaks on the Studebaker. Turns out I was overly
optimistic. Both coolant and PS leaks. I think I have cured the coolant leak
and it appears the PS leak is accessible and therefore repairable. We shall
see.

Now the good news !

I went on a do-gooder project with Dave Dunham. We went to clean the HVAC
system filters for a shut-in couple from church. The system has
electrostatic filters. Several of the ionizing elements were broken. So far,
I have not been able to find replacements but have not given up.

Closer to home, I pulled the fireplace insert out and cleared the ash the
chimney sweep had loosened. The circulating fan in the insert has always
been very noisy. Under the heading of hope springing eternal, Dwight has
ordered a replacement unit which we hope (Ha!) will be quite.

For AT work, we had the girls basketball time from Emory and Henry
University for a day. It was a cold, wet, miserable day but they were
cheerful , willing workers. Mostly.

Friday, we went with the Friday Hikers to the Pinnacle tower at Unicoi. It
is the old forest service lookout tower. It was a lovely day and a nice
walk. It is about five miles up but an easy wide trail with a gradual
incline. After coming back down, went on up to Herb's house to visit the
goats then up Limestone Cove road to Buladean, across Hughes Gap and down to
Roan Mtn. to get four more bushel of apples. (Per requests).

This picture was taken from top of the tower looking toward Temple Hill. The
road over River Hill is barely visible.

No report is complete w/o an update on the table project. Work has started
on the benches. I borrowed a radial arm saw from Dwight and was able to use
it to cut slots for the legs to fit into the benches. Of course, a bit of
finishing is required.

This is Debby Roberts Reynolds. She was doing registration at the AT
maintainers dinner Friday night. I hired Debby for TEC to come into Kodel
many years back. She was the first female engineer hired into a fiber
division. Out of all the ones I interviewed, I picked Debby because I did
not think she would be intimidated by the men. Boy, was I ever right about
that !
I went to her wedding. looked at pictures of her children. Her daughter is
now a Nurse Practitioner in Nashville and her son is working on a PhD in
Chem E at Univ of Delaware.
I like to talk with Debby because she always has really nice things to say
about me.

Now, I will await updates from all around the world including TTU ,
Wisconsin and Alabama .
