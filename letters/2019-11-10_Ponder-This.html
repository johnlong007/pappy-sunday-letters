November 10, 2019 · Sunday

Ponder This

13 photographs·428 words

I was a senior in high school in 1953. My uncle Clifton lived in Detroit.
He bought all the car magazines and sent along to me. In one of them, I
read about a person putting a Chrysler hemi in a Studebaker and running on
the salt flat. I think 160 MPH.
Right then, I decided that was for me. In 1966 I located and purchased the
car. In 67 a 1957 Chrysler with a 392 engine. Alex buried the car ,intact,
on his cousins farm in Piney Flats. then in about '90 the "Rock Crusher"
Chrysler transmission. Several years and much time and money later I have a
more or less replica of the salt flat car.

When David was here last Summer, he fell in love. After serious
consideration and consulting with his parents I turned it over to him for a
one year. At that time we will decide whether to renew lease or not.

He sent a note last night saying engine would race but car moved slowly and
a lot of smoke and bad odor. Today, he opened the clutch cover. Stay tuned.

Last weekend, LLLL and Rebecca were here.

We have had a fine pear crop and still have a number to be harvested. At
least we did last time I looked. Free to good home.

Our only garden crop was peppers. Picked the last ones before predicted
heavy frost.

Does anyone know what I was having for desert? Is it a dying practice?

Most of the week was spent preparing fire wood for this Winter and next.

All this wood is for next Winter. Since Jake was not available, I had to
split this load.

Then we loaded back in truck and stacked in barn behind the fire engine.

Gonna need a lot more than is here now.

Back to wood for this Winter, we split and stacked the slabs stacked out
front.

The cat stays outside most of the time but usually comes in about bedtime,
10;30. He did not come in last night and did not show up this morning. A
first time ever. After church, I launched a search expecting to find
nothing but maybe fur remnants.
He was penned in the '63. A big relief.

Oh, Dr. Ponder, the heart Dr., said my Aortic stenosis is getting louder
but keep on doing what I am doing and come back next year.
