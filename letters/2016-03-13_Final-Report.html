March 13, 2016 · Sunday

Final Report

0 photographs·52 words

My brother Herb died this evening.

He was cognizant and responsive up until last night before taking a sudden
turn for the worst.

The same age at death as our father.

A good brother
A good man

I will miss him, we have shared a lot in many ways over the years.
