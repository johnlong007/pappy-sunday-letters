Routine first:
made a run over to help Dave with the car. No pictures but mostly fun. Dave
is having a lot of health issues making him stay close to home.

We also snabbled Wayne and made a run to Erwin to put the cap on chimney
for little orphan lady.

At home, it was mostly Walnut shucking and leaf blowing.

​I had decided I had plenty of walnuts but remembered a tree at Blountville
annex with really large walnuts and decided to collect those. If other
folks are not interested in cracking walnuts I will have a big surplus.

​Carl slipped in another AT work day at Carvers Gap. The day after
Thanksgiving which turned out to be a lovely day. I have never seen so many
people and dogs on the trail.

​I was sitting with George preparing to eat lunch when I saw this group
coming. I jumped up to take picture then scurried back for fear the dogs
would get my lunch.

Many high points for the week, a lot of things for which I am thankful.
Family members who like each other and tolerate me/us.

We got a Christmas present! Hand delivered from Saint Louis.

One un-named GD-I-L came by and entered her family data in the family tree
program. Now, she too can be an official Mayflower Madam.

Heard from some scattered hither, thither and yon but not everyone. Wonder
what went on in Switzerland?
