May 6, 2012 · Sunday

Good and Bad

9 photographs·515 words

Two friends died this week. Anne Cosby one of the Friday hikers and Becky
Stallard wife of a friend at church.
I have known Anne for about ten years since she retired from ECC and took
up hiking.
Anne was a very elegant person always in good taste and always dressed to
the nines. 65 years old she fought a good fight with internal organ cancer.
Becky I did not know real well. Very quite and very sincere. 62 years old.
A brief struggle with GI obstructions.
It brings home there is no promise of tomorrow.

I took Sanford ramp digging on Hughes Gap Monday. Met a lot of
Northbounders and dug a lot of ramps. I sent out the blog site for
'Button". If you go back through it a couple of days you can read about her
time in Erwin. I am going to keep up with it until she passes Kincora and
Bob Peoples. I intend to include her picture.

It was hot Monday. After we got back, we went to a lot George owns to
remove a rock outcropping. Sanford kept telling me to take it easy "you are
going to overdo it". I finally figured out waht he was really saying so we
quit. To be continued.

As part of my project to stay in touch, we went to a baseball game. Here is
a picture of Benj stealing second.

A lot of towns and a lot of people are catching on that the hikers have
money and it pays to be nice to them. Kim told me she has heard it averages
about $3,000 to make the hike. This time of year "trail angels" appear and
leave food and drink at varuious places along the trail Wed., Jim Foster
and other folks set up a feeding station at Sam's Gap. I went to check it
out. It was impressive. They had about 50 hikers go through.

Thursday I went to TTU to see the staem engine. Three of Charlie's team
members were there to give a demonstration. I can attest it runs !

Then it was on to LLLL's for the night and Auburn on Friday. Has everyone
heard Parkinson's law? "Work expands to fill the time available".
So does a girl's possessions.

Back at the ranch. Garden planting gets started seriously the first day of
May. The upper garden is planted full with 90 day corn. Lower garden has
three rows of peanuts and a row of sweet potatoes.

GSH has continued multi-tasking. The dining room table is now ready for
finish thanks to able assistance of Bob.

Very little progress on the '37. Maybe it will soon come alive. Maybe some
of the returning students will decide to get it running. Who knows?

The love birds are multiplying and dividing. A family of robins in the bush
just outside my window. Bluebirds in the near end of the swing and some
other birds at the other end.

I think that is enough pictures. I bet other people in other places have
more interesting things they could note
