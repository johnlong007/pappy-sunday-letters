Ellis Road when we went to CC for labs Monday morning.

When we arrive, one of the nurses will draw a blood sample and take it to
the lab.
Shortly thereafter, the NP on duty will come out to go over the results and
explain the next steps.
This NP is Ashley, she's all business. I was by myself the first time she
was on duty. She told me, "Next time bring someone who can hear.""

It was time for platelets (remember the bleeding mouth). These two are used
to my sense of humor and like to kid me.
With the chemo pill, they are being very careful trying ti find the right
dose. Also keeping a close watch on signs and portents lest they get the
best of me. So, we went in for labs twice. A practice I think is scheduled
to continue. Stay tuned.

Diane dropped me off and went on to get sliced and diced. Sanford came by
and drove me home. No questions asked.Family, friends, faith.

Some work on the 37 nose cone this week. Trim pieces for around the grill
are missing. F & I is next.

The steering gearbox leak has been resolved. I believe it is time to
install the engine when the signs are right.

Sealed bags of black walnut kernels.

Fran came for a visit with two of her grandchildren. Kids that age are
still perfect.

We finally got to tour R &G house. I really like it. They expect to
complete their 10-year house improvement plan this year.

Most of our free time was spent outside, splitting beech . I look at this
picture and wonder why on earth my hand is in that position.

Another WW arrived along with jr. helper.
