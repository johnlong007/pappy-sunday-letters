April 28, 2019 · Sunday

Reality Strikes

10 photographs·449 words

As noted before, I have tried to increase my track distance to 5 miles/day.
But, not paying much attention to time. Thursday, I was shuffling along
with my mind off in a distant planet when a little ole lady walking passed
me.

I sort of wished I had previously announced that due to knee pain I was
going to stop running and just walk.

Rats.

Thanks to almost all for the Easter pictures. One to go which has been
promised.

My whole life has been "make do and get by". On balance it has served me ok,
maybe not well but ok. The instrument lights on the '63 are not working.
The dimmer and switch are part of the headlight switch. I have an extensive
collection of used headlight switches.

A new switch on line is about $10. Local, about $13. I spent at least 2
days trying to get one of the old switches to work. Installing and removing
is quite tedious but I did get better, Should have waited until Katy is
available. Never did get it to work.

Fuel gage is not working. Best guess is the float system in the tank has
failed. replacement is about $75. So, I took one out of the spare parts car.

Do not believe it will work. Along the same line, I pulled out the parts
car exhaust system to see if any parts could be used.

On a more successful note, Saturday one more of less willing worker went
with us to inspect initial section of our assigned part of the AT while I
went to inspect the so far secret ramp patch

Meredith might be interested. Looks like a good crop, still small and
probably need a couple more weeks to grow before hidden by may apples.

Back on the ranch, the ramps are looking quite good. I need more knowledge
about the life cycle of a ramp. Should I thin?

Anita took the cat in for routine maintenance. Fine except bare spot on top
of his head where the dog woolie him. Not quite sure how to deal with that
issue as they both seem to enjoy the tussle.

The pool room project moves along. I think it is going to look good one of
these days, months? Years?

We made a run to see Mr. Fidelity to check on state of finances. So far, so
good.

I was running string trimmer beside drive and noted the tiny flowers. Seems
I remember the Friday hikers getting all excited when spotting this kind.
What is it?.
