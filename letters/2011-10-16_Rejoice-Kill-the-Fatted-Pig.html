October 16, 2011 · Sunday

Rejoice ! Kill the Fatted Pig!

7 photographs·479 words

The Studebaker has power steering and no sign of leaks in the plumbing (but
still chasing a coolant leak).

Three steering assemblies were mixed and matched with many attempts to get
high pressure flex hoses to fit and seal before getting it all together. Of
course, it has not yet been out on the road to check actual steering.

On a more mundane level, I went to Mt. Mitchell with Jim Foster on Monday.
Mt. Mitchell is the highest peak in US on this side of the Miss. Although it
was a beautiful day and the leaved were perfect, it was cold, misty, and
very windy on top. We walked about 8 miles in a loop around the top of the
mountain. No wildlife was spotted but they had posted a sign saying the
bears are looking to get fat for the Winter and best to give them a wide
berth.

Sanford and I made a run to Roan Mtn to purchase apples for the Thanksgiving
apple butter run (at Linda's).The orchard people have many varieties of very
nice apples I intend to go back in a day or so and purchase3 a couple more
bushels. One for Herb and one for Laura. Anyone else?

The old folks from church (at least some of them) went on a barge ride at
Bays Mtn. park. No wild life was evident. On the barge or on the lake.

However, speaking of wild life. John's car has been dead for quite a spell.
To get things off dead center, it was decided to take it to the Mazda dealer
in Bristol to get it psychoanalyzed. I towed the car while Anita followed to
the dealer and back home. She was much more relaxed on the way back.

The dealer said PCM was the prime suspect and that for another $87 they
would be more certain. We dragged it home where the local mechanic crew plan
to spring into action at the crack of dawn Monday. Anyone with nothing
better to do might do well to drop by and watch them in action like a well
oiled machine I would expect.

In matters much closer to home, since Fall has fell it was arranged for a
chimney sweep to come by and do his thing.

Work continued on the table project. But, it appeared to be mostly planning
and pondering. The table itself looks much like last week so no picture.

To offer proof that Fall has arrived, here is a picture of the driveway
after a bit of wind. The pine needles and walnuts are falling.

I check Facebook everyday to see what people are willing to admit they are
doing. But, I still appreciate getting updates from hither and yon, A to V
perhaps. (Think about that).
