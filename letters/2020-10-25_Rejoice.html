October 25, 2020 · Sunday

Rejoice ?

15 photographs·397 words

As you should know, both the '37 and the '63 are nearing completion. So, to
make sure I do not run out of things to do we brought in another project.
A rare and vanishing breed. Anyone know what it is? Fortunately, the
previous owner, much like my brother, was a vocational shop instructor so
the body work is not only done but done well ! Unfortunately, like my
brother, he died leaving an unfinished project . The interior is at zero
completion.
In order to further complicate things, I went to see Mark Coates and asked
him to bring the '55 home from the Muscle Car Museum and prep it for
driving.
Now, it is going to be necessary to find a proper place to keep it in
climate controlled comfort.

All hands must be mustered for this project to be completed. GSH is
cleaning the ash trays for the rear seat compartment. Mostly taking
inventory to determine what else is needed.

I do not intend to get deeply involved with the new project until the '37
is further along. PBBD has been helping on that project while Ace
photographer records for posterity'

It appears there is excessive blow by. Try the easy things first, add mouse
milk. Stay tuned.

First learning to adjust fuel/air mixture at idle

Adjust timing by listening

Mark timing marks on crank pulley.

Thursday night is Free supper night at the Long's. I like to send Maria a
picture so she knows she is not forgotten. Anyone know what she has been "
up to " today? I do. Perhaps she will send a picture.

Friday night is free supper with PBBD and cat.

Sunday dinner is with PBBD and interested observer.

Main outdoor project was finishing the relocated retaining wall for the
retaining basin. A lot more earth moving to do but not until things get a
lot dryer.

The cat and the dog like to escort us for walks down drive, Wonder if it is
this pretty elsewhere. Geneva? Utah? Portland? Wisconsin? California?
Alabama? NC? Anywhere?

Not only are the leaves falling, last year's pine needles are falling. Our
driveway will be covered this coming week .

Good try.
