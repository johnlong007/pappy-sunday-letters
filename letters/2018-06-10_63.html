June 10, 2018 · Sunday

11 photographs·352 words

We went back to see Dr. Dew this week. Sure enough things are pretty
stable. Come back in 6 months.

A long time ago, before I left Detroit on the first day of June in 1955, I
made two purchases. One was a 1948 Plymouth (for $100), and the other was a
filmy nightie. She still has it.

​Niece Lisa is doing very well learning to a gentleperson farmer. However,
from time to time she needs a bit of aid and comfort. Wayne, GSH and I
went over one day. Wayne worked on trying to seal a leak in the roof over
the garage , cleaned and secured the gutters. Lisa, GSH and I mowed in and
around the BBs'

​

​The cat continued being a cat. Anita has been tending to his infected eye.
Noticed he had a limp and swollen foot. Back to the vet.

​​
​

​

After Mom died, she specified gifts back to the giver. We had given her the
swing set but I asked Herb to take it to put beside the pond where he could
sit and remember Mom.

​​
PBBD is doing a fine job of tending to her aged and somewhat decrepit
parents. One evening she came over to share a batch of potatoes and ramps.
I have enough ramps left for one more meal.

​Blountiville Old tractor event is this coming Saturday. I would like to
get the "H", "MD", maybe the Oliver and the Allis out to take. A start in
that direction was moving the IH crawler out of the way. It fired right up
(much to my surprise). Then quit (much to my dismay). Turns out we had
failed to open fuel shut off valve.

​Bob has been looking for storage room for his ever increasing inventory of
high dollar raw materials. He seems to be having more success at recruiting
workers than I am. Anyway, the bed of the big Black Truck is now loaded
with lumber.

​Eggs, sausage, cheese and ramps. Scrambled. Wonder if this menu is served
in California? Utah? Wisconsin? Anywhere else?
