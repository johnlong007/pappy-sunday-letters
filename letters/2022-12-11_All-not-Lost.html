December 11, 2022 · Sunday

All not Lost

11 photographs·291 words

My best guess is we have been going to the Bltv Christmas parade for over
50 years. The weather was good but spectators were down a bit this year. We
claimed our usual spot in front of the baptist church. When we had small
children, the people in the parade would toss candy toward us. That
practice stopped some years ago, now they just look and keep on going.
Yesterday the Bltv Christian Church float went past with many young people
singing and having fun. Just as it passed us, a young girl, maybe 10 or 11,
jumped off, ran up the hill to me and put candy in my hand. No prompting.

Central is no more. West Ridge has a rather large entourage.

It is to be noted that Meals on Wheels not only delivers but joins in.

The haircut lady makes house calls. I should have snuck in. Dr. Sago appt.
coming up and LLLL not here to peel my head.

Still making reverse progress on the 37. Everyone well enough versed to
know what is happening here? No progress at all on the 55 this week.

One thing is working out well for a brief burst of strenuous exercise. I
can put GSH in the car to supervise, and if available take PBBD along and
attack the woodpile. All of the splitting could have been done in one day
had I use the tractor. Sure glad I did not do that.

I decided against trying to overwinter the pointy pepper plant again this
year and harvested the peppers, Helper is sorting out trash under watchful
eyes.
