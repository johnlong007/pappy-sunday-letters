April 14, 2019 · Sunday

Opportunity

14 photographs·498 words

Some will remember Alf. He is our German friend. A most interesting
person. Alf has decided to write an autobiography and asked me to proofread
and make suggestion on content.
He is writing in English and aiming at an American audience. So, for any
who know him, send me your suggestions on content.
Alf is pushing 60, he grew up in East Berlin and served in the East German
army.

I continued working through Kenneth's thesis. Continue to be surprised at
how few typos and at how much of it I understand. I do hope his defense
will be live streamed.

Lisa sent note saying having trouble getting the lawn mower to start. Since
I already had a run that way planned, We went over and stopped by. I like
helping her because she is wiling to get her hands dirty and is a quick
learn. Show her one time and she has got it. Bulb primer must have dried
out. We used a straw to put a bit of fuel in carb inlet and it fired right
up.
We went on toward Flag Pond to try to locate a cousin, Tommy Runion. I
could not get his address in the &^*$%^#* GPS on the car. About that time
got an SOS from Wayne that Transmission in his Daytona had locked and he
was stranded. Headed home.

Tuesday, Kaylee and friend came for a visit. His parents immigrated from
Russia when he was a baby. They are each professors at ETSU. The family
speaks only Russian at home.
His name in Russian----Veniamin
Phonetic in English Ven ya min
Everyone got it?
Christina, try it.

I tried to start the IH crawler. It is Diesel with a high pressure fuel
injection system . The fuel injection pump has four output lines one going
to each cylinder.

I decided to disconnect the tubing at each injector nozzle to check fuel
output from the pump. Since the fitting are kept very tight, while pulling
on the wrench I reminded myself, this is a good way to get hurt when the
fitting breaks loose.

Back to the locked up transmission. Fortunately we have a spare car
although it does have a broken rear window. Saturday, Wayne arrived early
if not bright to pull the transmission. He decided he also wanted to pull
the engine for reasons which seemed to make a lot of sense to him. I
enlisted my chief photographer and only willing worker and went along to
"help".

Engine on ground, transaxle on fender.

Note willing worker. Has that Max look on his face.

Everyone can see that my lectures to GSH about being careful are having a
serious impact.

Meredith came for a visit and brought cookies.

I have heard that people sometimes drive many miles to see trees in bloom.
