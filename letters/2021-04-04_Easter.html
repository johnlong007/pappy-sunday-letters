I think this Easter was the strangest ever for us. Fortunately, PBBD
jumped in to rescue us.

Meredith has decided to create a fruit farm as best I can tell. Fruit trees
are second only to a milk cow in terms of effort involved to reap a
harvest. Check in after 5 years.

Organizing the shop has taken on a life of its own.

Everyone knows that old Chrysler products used right and left side wheel
bolts. Later, they switched to right and left studs with requisite nuts. I
have an extensive collection of both bolts and nuts. Now, thanks to my
willing worker, they are all sorted.

The car has 70K miles and I thought it was time to replace brake pads. I
was wrong. Pads were good for another m40K. But, thanks to Wayne and his
only remaining helper it now has new pads.

Early in the week it was cold. Snow flurries while we were at the track.

Lest we forget.
