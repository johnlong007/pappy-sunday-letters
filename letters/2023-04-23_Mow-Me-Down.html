April 23, 2023 · Sunday

Mow Me Down

17 photographs·405 words

No progress on the '37 this week. Lost ground on the '55. The MC Wayne
worked on still leaks. While he was helping adjust the brakes he announced
the rear axle seal is still leaking.
The good news is it starts and runs well, and, like the spare window, I
have spare axle assemblies. Stay tuned.

Mowing season is upon us. We have about 25 acres here I think. It is
gradually closing in on me. So far, thanks to the front mount mower, I have
kept all we can easily see pretty well mown. Check back this Fall.

The area out front where GM built the decks, pond and rock walkways is
cleaning up very well. Our private handyman delivered pea gravel to help
with the project.

I think I mentioned hoping a distant cousin might deliver some ramps. He
did. He also promised to go with me to a newly located site which will
require about a one mile each way hike. He said wait about two weeks for
continued growth.

Sure enough, the ones he brought are about 1/2 grown.

But they sure did make for a tasty breakfast repast ! We licked the platter
clean !

The saw millers got serious about the 48 inch Oak log. Note it is split in
half, doing "quarter" sawing.
I understand the other (end painted white) log is soon to be converted.

I suppose all are aware that we are equal opportunity employers. No
snowflakes allowed.

Albert's second pine tree. The first has been converted into furniture in
the raw.

Two big, long logs and a pile of brush.

I have rather large blood vessels which are close to my thin skin. I gouged
one.

Because of my worsening problem with lack of endurance and my
unwillingness to be away from GSH for very long periods, I have almost
stopped running around the block after over 40 years. However, we are
getting to the track about every day and getting two miles at a fair clip.
The wind was stiff Monday so Steph put on my windbreaker. I told her she
looked ridiculous.
Another day we were joined by the next generation. It was delightful.

I am thankful for everyday.
