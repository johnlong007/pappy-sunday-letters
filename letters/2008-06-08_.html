6 photographs·190 words

I just finished two fingering the longest "report" I have yet done. Then, I
tried to down load pictures and lost the whole thing. This will be much
shorter.

Wed. I worked with Dave to build a walkway from the back door to the drive
way for our terminally ill minister. He died the next day. However, it will
serve his widow well as she has a serious mobility problem. We are going back
soon to put up railing for her.
The funeral service was this evening. Sue Tabor and the girls were there.
I went to Erwin Thursday and took pictures of both Aunt G and the baby
goats. Also took pictures of Anita's barn and Dwight's outhouse (after last siding
outing). Unfortunately, I hit wrong selection while downloading pictures and
erased them all.
I did go out and take a few current fruit pictures for Rachel.

The Cherry tree
Blueberries
Chinquapin bush
Fresh bathed dog
Soccer field sans grass

All would have enjoyed the report I erased and the pictures which preceded
it.
