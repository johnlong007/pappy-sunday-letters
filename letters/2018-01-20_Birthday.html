January 20, 2018 · Saturday

Birthday

9 photographs·273 words

The year has gone by the quickest of my life.

Syble, a girl with whom I worked at ECC, called early Monday. We met at
Hardees in Bltv and talked for hours getting caught up on all the people we
knew. I took a picture but promised not to publish. Guess she did not have
time to get fully made-up.

Then Monday PM Anita came over with potato soup and a cake. You might
notice a person working on a corner. Anyone remember the cake with the
disappearing corners?

Then it snowed a bit and got cold​. We have been going to the track about
every day for about an hour. Usually good for 3-5 miles at my slow pace. We
went to Blountville but walked rather than shuffle.

Mostly we stayed indoors and cracked walnuts.

​The dishpan full of smashed walnuts turned out about a quart of kernels.

We have left a bucket of native butternut (White walnuts)

​Two buckets of hybrid butternuts:

​And several buckets of black walnuts. All free along with a nut cracker
(on loan) to interested party.

​After quitting the walnut project we got back on the '63. I have the
original boot but it is quite ratty. Have another I though was the same but
in much better shape. We worked for hours getting it cleaned and trying to
install before discovering it does not fit. Rats. Must be for Wayne's '68.

​Saturday evening, Anita had a happy birthday event for DOD. It was fun,
but no pictures.

Tomorrow, the plan is drive to Memphis for tooth M & R. Hence the early
report.
