Our busy and exciting social season continued this week. Friday night was
the "maintainers dinner". The maintainers are a subset of the hiking club
officially responsible for maintaining the AT from Spivey to the va. line
at Damascus. The maintainers are the ones who actually do the work.
Carl reports on various accomplishments and activities. The number of
different workers dropped again this year as well as total hours reported.
With some regrets I gave up our section after several years . Kim has
already reassigned it to a young go getter.
The high point of the event was while standing around a person I had not
seen since last year came up and said " Why are you getting taller while I
am getting shorter?"'
I have really enjoyed working with this group of people. One could not find
better folks anywhere.

Today we went to Erwin, I had some names of kinfolks I wanted to run by
Aunt Flo and also wanted to try lopping bamboo at Lisa's place.
She was not there but other family members were. I will be surprised if
family does not grow soon. 28 days for duck eggs?
Will also be surprised if ducklings survive turtles and muskrats.

Only lopped a dozen or so bamboo, good up to about 1". Above that need the
chain saw. Cannot cut with axe or stub will puncture tractor tires.
