October 2, 2016 · Sunday

Tractors Galore

8 photographs·93 words

No AT work and not a lot of do-gooder.

But did some gleaning of the church apple trees. Did some grubbing of
stumps in church lawn and did a bit of blood sampling for Dwight so not a
complete wash out.

But a lot of fun. Charlie came to town a day early for John's event and
spent Friday working on projects. Then, Friday pm we had brats in the park.
That went so well, we did it again on Saturday PM.

If anyone wants more explanation, let me know. Gotta go now.
