January 2, 2022 · Sunday

Good Start

10 photographs·441 words

My curiosity has been satisfied ! This week, I got sick. Started Wed.
Progressed on Thursday, worse on Friday and really sick on Saturday.Mainly
headache and tired with aching in every joint and muscle.
But, Sunday morning came back to about 80% and I can say glad I tried it
but rather not repeat. Not near as a bad as the real thing in "57 I am sure
I had something but unsure just what.

Saturday morning was a shot across the bow as far as TLC for GSH. I was not
sure I was going to be able to do mandatory tasks. But, I did and will now
begin to plan for next time.

We brought in some more muscle down here but still could not get the engine
to turn. Adeed more mouse milk in cylinders and decided to let it soak a
while.

As part of letting it soak and due to the projected ill weather we put the
wheels back on the car and wrapped it up with plastic sheets. Work to be
resumed at some later date.s

David was here. We checked the travel on the Studebaker test panel set up
and determined it gets a maximum of one inch limited by the slave cylinder.
Then we went to the car and checked for required travel to
completely engage to disengage the clutch disc. It requires 1 -1/2 inch.
Houston, we have a problem. To be continued.

David spends money to go to the gym and build muscles. I might even pay him
a pittance. If I had known he was coming we could have had more wood ready
to split. He got it all. I had prepared it for Gwendolyn but she left in
the dark of the night.

The weather was great all week. It was also great to have family visitors
drop in from hither and yon. I suppose California counts as yon.

We even had visitors from the frozen North. I gave the newest Buchanan a
family Buchanan sweatshirt. I will need a spy to tell me if she actually
appears in public wearing the shirt.

The Colson boys were deeply engaged in some sort of video identify this
place . Reminded me of the old Readers Digest "where are you picture"

As per custom for recent years, Laura fed most of the family Christmas
dinner . This picture is the childrens table.

Santas (and my) helper.

A & C have been coming over most evenings Dinner and a show. Much
appreciated..
