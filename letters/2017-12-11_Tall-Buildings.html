December 11, 2017 · Monday

Tall Buildings

10 photographs·286 words

This week I did not get in on AT work. Astounded to learn they went right
on without me.

I did make another run to help Dave with brakes on the '65. It now has
brakes . He promised to put wheels back on and take it for a spin.

Took GSH in to see Dr. Dew the neurologist. Essentially stable since last
visit. Prescribed watch diet and get plenty of physical and mental
exercise. Also, stay involved. (Looking to children and grandchildren on
that front).

The 1949 Dodge has been on the landing a long time. I have done my part and
turned it over to Wayne.Monday with GSH in control we moved it off the
front row.

Also on the home front we brought more fireplace wood up from the barn:

​Cleaned stove pipes for shop stove

(anyone heard the British song "My Father was a Dustman and I'm a Dustman
Too?", I remember it well, my father installed and maintained coal burning
furnaces. He hated cleaning so he took me to do the dirty work.)​

​ ​Then fired up the stove in the shop.

With the A/C system we can control inside temp. The system has been off for
last few months. Friday morning, inside temp was 64 so I turned system on.

​It works.
I have several bushels of walnuts waiting to be cracked.​

Saturday was a big day. I went to an auction and purchased a new toy.

​It was also Blountville Christmas parade day.

​Then Saturday night Joe came over and set up PC so we can get TTU live
streaming and see Maria singing and playing horn. (But not both at same
time)

Bonus picture of the week.
