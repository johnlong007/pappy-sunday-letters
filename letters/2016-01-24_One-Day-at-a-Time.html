January 24, 2016 · Sunday

One Day at a Time

8 photographs·321 words

Herb was not responding to the PT so Lisa had him delivered home on
Thursday. I went over to see him today and found it to be quite difficult.
On the other hand, did I mention that Dan and Jimmy stopped by here for a
brief visit? I do believe my scrawny little brother is going to outlast all
the rest of us. I got a birthday card and phone call from Lil' sister. she
is doing fine as best I can tell from 500 miles away.

A miserable week for running around. I promised LLLL I would come "run" in
her church sponsored 5K on Feb, 13th. I started going to the track to help
GSH with her Fitbit steps and to give me a chance to measure my progress. I
have to start slow and gradually increase. Each time I try to get any speed
at all I get light headed. We have been snowbound the last few days and
having to use the treadmill. The same thing happens. Even approaching 15
min./mile I get light headed .I can just see me trudging up that long grade
with the police car bringing up the rear right behind me!

It snowed. We started out to Erwin in the little car and decided roads were
too bad and came back. Got stuck at driveway entrant. Spent hours and many
tries getting on up the drive. Then got stuck at foot of the ramp.Let it
set Friday and Saturday but dug out enough to be able to back out and go to
church this morning.

No AT work this week.

Pretty good progress on the '63. GSH is working on the inside door panels.
I got the floor pan sanded and coated with rust reactor. So far a fun
togetherness project.

The Studebaker upholstery people called to ask a couple of questions and to
assure us they are hard at work. Ha!
