December 5, 2021 · Sunday

Locked

11 photographs·407 words

I spent a lot of time working on the "65 Imperial this week. The engine is
frozen . Because of the way it was shut down I do not know if the dirt used
to extinguish the engine compartment fire was ingested into the engine.
Trying to determine the situation, A boroscope was used to look inside the
cylinders via the spark plug holes.

The experts conferred and decided there was nor foreign material inside the
cylinders. Just to be sure, PBBD then blew out the cylinders with
compressed air.. The next step will be putting some sort of solvent inside
to try to free things. Stay tuned.

Quite a bit of engine compartment wiring has been burned. We dragged out
some old wiring harnesses from parts inventory and tried to find matching
replacement pieces.

When I parked the newly refurbished '63 the intent was to start ie
every couple of weeks. So, I finally got around to starting it fired right
up but the clutch disc was frozen to the flywheel. I was able to get it
free but glad I did not wait longer.

It is a lovely convertible. If I lived in a dry, sunny state I would be
begging for it.

I decided to try mounting tubeless tires on old , spoke wheels. This one
leaked around the bead as readily apparent.I broke it loose, cleaned the
rim and reinflated, we shall see.

Today , for the first time in ages, we went to Perkins, early service and
Sunday school. The ladies circle in which GSH worked; sent us home with a
Christmas present.

We went out to the hillside for supper and a show one evening. Alan and his
new toy were the show.

The IMperial is a '65. THe LBT is a '65 and parts van for it also '65. We
rummaged around looking for components which might work on the burned IMp.
Got a carburetor and various electrical bits.

LLLL and David came for a visit. I had taken delivery of a new toy and
asked a friend who is an expert to check it out. He brought it by and gave
LLLL a tutorial

David tried his hand at cat training.

Thanksgiving dinner in a far, far away place which has a warm dry climate.
