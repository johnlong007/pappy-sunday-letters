June 25, 2017 · Sunday

Above Average !

11 photographs·508 words

First things first, Sandy came in 6th ! Next time we need more people on
the team.

Meanwhile, I got a long term want marked off the list. Rich mountain is the
range across from Temple Hill. Bumpass Cove is at one end and Rocky Fork at
the other. The whole area burned over in 1952. Wayne Scott signed up the Vo-Ag
people to replant pine seedlings some time after that. As I remember we
worked in there for about a week. Then, In the late 60s we rode in
Higgins's creek and out Clark's creek with Clifton in a Jeep. I think those
were the last times on Rich Mountain. I wanted to go back and look around.
This week, I went with two forest service people who were working along the
ridge line.
We rode in about 12 miles in ATV along rough bumpy roads opening forest
service locked gates along the way.
Caught a couple of young men way back in the woods who had by-passed a
locked gate in a pickup truck, small AWD.
Reubin gave them a lecture then chased them out.When we came out Skeeter
dug the ditch out where they had By-passed the gate. It would have been
funny to see them come out the way they went in after he dug it out.

Thursday, we did AT work. Rain was predicted at 2 PM. It rained at 10. We
got wet and muddy but completed the days work.

​Wayne has returned and hit the ground running.

Many years ago, GSH and Laura purchased a Dodge PU truck. It has served
long and faithfully never leaving us stranded. Along the way, various
engines have been fitted including a Chrysler ​Hemi. Not sure what it has
now, maybe a 360.
Anyway, Wayne took the Chrysler 440 which, various Summer Campers removed
from Herb's old station wagon, gave it a complete overhaul, put it into the
motor home for a swing about the US. Now, It is destined for the Dodge PU.

First, the engine hoist had to be obtained which required pulling the 37 PU
out and pushing back in.
Stay tuned.
No work on any of the projects this week. ​

I toured all around this afternoon.
Red raspberries are almost ready All the other fruit and nut and grapes are
looking good. Even the Paw Paws.

​The new strawberry patch looks especially good. I seem to remember
pinching blossoms off new plants so the plant would send out more runners,
These are blooming and showing berries so I decided to go for the bird in
hand and left them alone.

​​Seems like there was something else to report? Oh yes, Alan made a cake!
Actually, two cakes.

For years I have been working (and continued at a reduced pace) on a
pictorial family history. Recent events have called to my attention that I
have no data or information on children's spouses or grand children's
spouses.
If anyone wants tied in with the big "Joseph Leonard Buchanan" picture I
need data.
