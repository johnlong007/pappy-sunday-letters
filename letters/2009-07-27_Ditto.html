July 27, 2009 · Monday

Ditto

7 photographs·340 words

Lots of excitement this week.

A car did get started on the way to China. But, it was not one of mine. It
came from Herb's collection. I did reduce the inventory by one engine. I
sold a '55 Chrysler 331 Hemi to a friend in the Gray tractor club. He is
going to put it into a Ford 8N tractor.

More noteworthy, Kenneth is making good progress on the Fiat. The engine
and drivetrain are in place and connected. He did the brakes last year. Now,
all the bits and pieces remain to be connected.Fuel lines, wiring etc.

The Oliver tractor which we moved up from the ag building has a busted
drive gear. I drained the gunk from the gearbox , got the gears shifting as
best I could (with able help from GSH), refilled transmission and towed it to
the storage shed. Maybe it will get into the repair lineup one of these
days.

The Buchanan/Byrd reunion was Friday and Saturday . It was fun and
interesting. Most of the folks are connected through my grandfathers first family.
I learned that his first wife was Mormon and at least some of their
children were Mormon. John and Burgin, the ones who went to Idaho via Utah then
on to California. Joe was a deacon in Baptist church but converted not long
before he died. Rumor was he wanted to cover his bets for the hearafter. He
is buried in Beans creek cemetery between two wives.

Speaking of busted tractors, I went with Laura and Allison to retrieve
Meredith. We went to Linda's on Saturday PM. Linda told me her tractor had
quit. I started it at which time it made very loud. rattling engine noise with
clouds of smoke coming from the crankcase. I shut it off. Sunday, Dale
towed it around to his new repair shed.

Sunday, we to sewanee , toured the campus, listened to two concerts and
retrieved Meredith.
