January 7, 2018 · Sunday

Getting Old

6 photographs·311 words

Is not for wimps.

Monday was a bitter sweet day. Children, spouses, GC with spouses, ggc
showed up Sunday night and Monday morning. A fine event and we feel
blessed. But no stockings.

Hard to let go of the way it used to be and relish the present for what we
do have.

GSH and I finished out last week hacking and coughing. PBBD got us in to
see the NP who had all sorts of tests run and prescribed anti biotics.
Walking pneumonia. I thought I was going upscale when I graduated from a
cold to the flu. Anyway, I reckon we will survive this also.

It has been cold all week. But, we still did very well on our outings at
Central. Did 5 miles one day when high was 11 degrees. Another day I was
not properly attired and the wind got best of me after one mile.

​The Dr. prescribed good diet, good exercise, physical and mental and stay
involved (children and GC listen up!) for GSH (or any other old person).
Anyway, Santa got GSH a laptop with lots of mentaling challenging games,
puzzles and the like loaded.
Fortunately, Jonathan came up to get my printer working and while here gave
his GM a tutorial on the device.​

​
I moved into the shop and fired up the shop stove. GSH polished on the '63
bumpers and I enlisted two helpers to get installed.on the car. Getting it
ready to get exhaust system installed.

​

I other exciting news, I shattered a tooth. Not much I won't do to help a
grandchild. Trying to work out logistics. Pictures upon request'

New years resolutions.

1.. Want to run (trot, shuffle) 26.2 mile route

2, Take one more lap around the lower 48.

May need help from select grandchildren with either or both of those.

I welcome feedback.
