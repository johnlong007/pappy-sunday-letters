April 5, 2020 · Sunday

So Far-So Good

11 photographs·432 words

Anyone recognize this scene?

How about this one? For reasons obscure to even me, My brother Herb
purchased some well use farm implements from our uncle Cliff. I helped him
round up and park in a nice neat row along with some of implements that he
and I had purchased.

Now, the site is being overgrown and Farmer's daughter Lisa wanted it
cleared out. The kind of project I enjoy so we spent one day working on the
project. I took a before picture. Will get an after when project is
completed.

PBBD most times comes over Tuesday evening to check on the ole folks at
home. This week she used the Portland Prodigal Child biscuit mix to prepare
a batch of biscuits. Chipped beef gravy. One of my most favorite dishes.

Last Fall Wayne had the Bobcat serviced and a hydraulic leak "repaired".
Since then it has only run and hour or two and I have added over 10 gallons
of oil. In hopes it was something obvious and simple such as a ruptured
hose, we cleaned it up and checked to the extent possible. Alas, must go
back to the shop. What will they say? Do? Charge?

I have worked for years and spent a bundle on the '63 (very much like the
Studebaker). Quite often I wonder, why?
Saturday Wayne took Thomas and they went for a spin. Sort of like when
David fell in love with the Studebaker. It was very gratifying for me.

Just a little bit of '37 work. I installed a piece of the vinyl trim half
way around the grill. Did a so-so job. But, I think with skill and patience
i.e. someone else doing it is going to work ok.

It was decided that putting up the canopy to get ready for convertible
season was more important than trying to start the '37. Also more certain.

This morning, we were invited to join in a SS class led by Wayne. It was
really good. It appears he was working from the top of Roan Mtn. I
recognize the scene. It was very much like being in a real class setting.
It went a long. long way toward making it feel like Sunday is supposed to
feel.

Red Buds are in full bloom. My truck is 24 years old. I like it much better
than the new ones.

We have a lot of flowers. Maybe Meredith could count the varieties for us.
