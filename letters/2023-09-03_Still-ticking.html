Still ticking

13 photographs·161 words

As you might expect SF got lured into a bit of mechanic helper work.

The truck bumper was the big project for the week

For the heavy lifting Wayne lept in.

Behold !

Another try at the 55 master cylinder. Stay tuned.

One apple tree has beens picked clean

Part of the harvest is beiing dried for Winter usage.

Part is being consumed as we go.

Paw Paws will soon be ripe for the harvest. In the absence of
competent help I mowed the area up to the trees.

We grubbed the big pine stump and smoothed out to seed for cover.

Pleased to report, one of the tomato plants has started to come on strong !

Paw Paws at the ready.

We got a delightful surprise when our family Dr. made a house call
