April 7, 2013 · Sunday

Ditto

10 photographs·456 words

A bit of everything this week. Well. maybe not everything but several
things.
Monday I went with brother Dave to clear blow downs from a hiking trail at
Bays Mtn. park. Just inside the park we encountered a flock of turkeys.
Three males were strutting around trying to impress the females (and each
other I suppose). These two reminded me of Obama and Kim Jung.

GSH has had a tough week. She engaged in an all day wrestling match with
Turbo Tax. After several hours talking with a person on the telephone, we
went to ECU (TT agent) and talked with a real person there. Then back home.
Finally discovered by right clicking and opening as a "new tab", TT would
open. Then she was able to finish our return. Terribly frustrating and
annoying to not be able to handle such issues. In her spare time, she
helped with the MM and her yard work.

One of the pistons would not come out of the MM cylinder. I spent several
hours using a lot of solvent (not enough) and a lot of hammering to drive
it out. But, it cracked the cylinder wall and I let the connecting rod
rattle around knocking a piece out of each side of the piston skirt. Rats.

Herb brought me some Persimmon and mulberry seedlings. As most know, for
the last 25 years I have been working to turn this place into a wild life
sanctuary. I hope whoever comes into it appreciates and maintains. I
suppose someone else will have to observe.

Friday I went with three friends to auto show/flea market in Charlotte. I
did not buy anything but got a catalogue which I hope will help me find
window seals and guides for the '37. The most interesting car I saw was a
barn find, original, one owner 28 Buick. Looked and runs good. $30.000.
Probably a good investment.

Anyone interested in how long it takes before the nail on a smashed finger
comes off?

Oh, we made a parts delivery run to Seymour. Pat and buddy Jack were doing
body prep for painting. They think three weeks and they will be on the
track. Stay tuned.

Closer to home, Wayne came over twice. The first time to help with the
noisy wheel bearing on the VW. (Decided more tools were needed). The second
to install the head gasket in the '37. As soon as I finish reattaching the
plumbing it will be ready for another starting test. Another stay tuned!

After the head gasket Wayne and GSH had a CCP practice session. Mostly
directed toward getting comfortable. Also trying to decide what is the
easier (revolver or semi-automatic) to use for the exam. It went well.
