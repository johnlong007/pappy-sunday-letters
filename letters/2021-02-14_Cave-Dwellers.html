February 14, 2021 · Sunday

Cave Dwellers

13 photographs·436 words

Friday morning Sanford came by and we went to the Burger Bar for the first
time in months. About 1/2 the usual group were there. Today we went to
Perkins for breakfast but none of the GOBs were there. Then to church. Don
Hass was there but not his wife Nancy.

Almost all our circle are old enough to have had one or both shots and are
gradually crawling out of seclusion. I think church will resume Wed. night
service and SS soon. Maybe this week.

The home health people came by and checked me over. Said they could help
with my stiff neck and painful hand but the rest was out of their control.
I am getting old. Then I went in to see GP Dr. who said all is well.

The washing machine drains into a dry well which drains across the drive
watering the paw paw trees. The water has been backing up into the sink.
Monday experts from the frozen North Launched an attack.

Saturday, the local expert had a go.

same result. Still slow.

The home health people left some exercises for each of us to do
between their visits. Anita has been coming by to take GSH though hers.
Both seem to be having fun.

The first time I have made pancakes without having to toss the first batch.

Notice the collector item syrup !

If I was young and footloose, know what I would like to do?

Has everyone seen enough deer shots? There are two groups of 6-7 each which
hang around here. They like the fresh tender grass in the garden spot I
seeded last fall. I had to stop for deer crossing on the way out. One
stopped to stare me down.

Alan and Wayne worked on turning the dark room into a space for a closet.
Bob and Laura are working to make it into a closet. Diane and Max are being
curious.

We have a lot of split firewood and a lot more waiting to be sawed and
split. Wayne needs a bit to finish out the Winter. There ain't no free
firewood.

Quite a few trees around here have reached maturity and are falling (like
me). This picture is an 18 ' beech above the pond.

My valentine treat ! All corners and edge pieces.

The retaining basin has maybe another 8-inches of capacity. It will be
interesting to see how long it takes to go dry.
