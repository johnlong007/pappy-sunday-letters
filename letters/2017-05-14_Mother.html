May 14, 2017 · Sunday

Mother

9 photographs·420 words

I miss mine.

I asked GSH if she wanted breakfast in bed this morning. I assume all 6
remember fixing and feeding? Also suppose the tradition continues.

On the subject of GSH, we went to the Neurologist Friday. We like him. He
has been right on time for each of our two visits. Listens, answers my
question in English .
Said almost stable since last visit, stroke damage almost healed, come back
in 6 months .

​Mental gymnastics. Look close.

Things we did not do, work on the '49 or the '63. I may have to farm the
seat cushion job out.

I did put new glow plugs in the crawler but did not get the fuel system
bled so I could try to start.

​ At what age do you think I will learn pulling hard on an open end wrench
in a confined space is likely to cause bleeding? If you want to see the
picture, let me know.

Speaking of bleeding, GSH fell asleep in the rickety lawn chair while
sitting on the deck. Turned over and split her eyebrow. Bled profusely.
However, out of fear for my life, I did not take a B & G picture.

​The dish has strawberries (from Anita) in it as well as ice cream.

What we did is harvest ramps. A lot of ramps. One day we went to Meredith's
secret patch. The ramps had died down and were starting to go to seed.

​

But we dug a sackful and decided to go to a higher elevation where we
expected the would be less mature.

Sure enough, another day went to Doll Flats and found the ramps "just
right" and untouched by human hands. Harvested several bags full.

​No point in digging ramps unless you plan to consume!

​I wanted to go to Erwin to take some to Mom's sister Flo but could not
spring free. So, I asked Lisa to stop by and get a bag full to deliver. She
did.
Next day, Flo called, When she started talking I thought for sure it was my
long silent sister.

Over an extended period of time, I have collected several gallons of very
stale gasoline in 5 gallon containers. My experts said "burn it". I have a
large, shallow stainless pan. Sat it in the incinerator and lit off about 3
gallons.

​
It is rumored and occasionally reported the various children and some
grandchildren are doing meaning full thing and passing important
milestones. Any truth to the rumors?
