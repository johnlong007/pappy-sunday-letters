June 3, 2018 · Sunday

Events

15 photographs·389 words

I think our family is really good at having spontaneous events.

​The thing which struck me about this one was the number of little ones
appearing out of the woodwork. Wish Beth could have made it. So far,
everything has been in the proper order. Anyone else remember what the
Priest said at John's and Savannah's wedding? What I heard was something
like "get married, have children, have grandchildren, then die".

I thanks we are running on borrowed time but I really like watching life
unfold right before my eyes. Allison, Janet. Kenneth and Benj. Next !

​Monday, decoration Day, we went to a concert. Beth and Rachel. Since I am
beginning to wonder if the fire engine will live again, I saw this and
thought we might develop a plan B.

​
The concert was fun. The picture below is Alan and his mother. I am told
some could see the top of Rachel's head. I suppose not being to hear a
particular person is a good sign.
​

​I was able to persuade Steven and Wayne to go mow and trim our section of
the AT. For those who have not seen it, you are missing out.

​

​

​
The cat got some sort of eye infection. Diane and GSH took him to the vet.
Came home with tube of something to treat the eye 2 times /day for 10 days.
Diane said it would require 2 or 3 people as he would not like it.
So, I asked Anita to help. Having been down this road before she had the
job done before I could help. She has also been coming by morning and
night. I feel most fortunate to have family who care. About us and the
animals.

​When we got home from church today, the cat was waiting. I had to go
around back and call him to get down.

​Laura provided more plants.

​
Thursday night two inches of rain moved driveway down to Ellis road. Friday
I put blade on tractor and repaired damage. Friday night it happened again.
It filled the brush pond and cut a ditch through walnut grove.

Aaron, Chelsea and noodniks came for a visit. Aaron made supper, Chelsea
entered family data in the family tree program. I only had to ask her one
time.
