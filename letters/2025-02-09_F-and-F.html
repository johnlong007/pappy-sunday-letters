F and F

13 photographs·502 words

Family and friends.

Bob Harvey is older than I am !

Sanford is younger. Both are good friends. Bob was one of the founders of
the Friday Hikers group we went with for years. He is a retired ME and one
of the most interesting people I know. Almost always after we got home from
a hike I would go on line to learn more about some topic we had discussed.
He is an assisted living facility where we stopped by to check in with him

For several years we went with Sanford for Friday breakfast with a group of
his family and friends. I have continued although I have dropped out of
Wed, GOB lunch.
Sanford has a daughter and a SIL (black shirt). Friday was the first time
his daughter joined the group but they all knew her. RL (blue shirt) is
sanfords BIL married to his LiL' sister.

Neighbor , Albert, came up to check on me. All the Summer Campers know
about Albsrt. A good guy and a good neighbor. He has moved up on the hill
with his son since his wife died. House sitting empty for many years. He
would like to sell it to someone who would take it as is and finish all
the renovation part way done.

Cards ! I get cards ! Family and friends send cards. I appreciate it.
Yesterday a very thoughtful note from way out West.

A & C are working on the crawler. This week they got the tracks free and
rolled out flat. I have a picture but this one is more impressive. Stay
tuned.

WWs showed up to help us restock the wood supply to get ready for the next
cold spell.

The septic tank problem solvers said get rid of those bushes or we will
soon be back. Family handyman lopped off and PBBD painted the stubs with a
brush killer.
The tires on the rogue were down to wear indicator bars which really
concerned my safety conscious co-pilot. I mentioned it to Wayne. Next day
the car showed up with new tires and a wash.

Rather than the track, today we walked down to the Exchange place. And
back, Laura had to tow me up the hills on the way back but I made it.

I put this picture in for those with daughters. Bring them up in the way
they should go.

The triple shovel is completed and moved into storage waiting for moving
up on the end of the building for display. It is the one with the varnished
handles and really turned out well. Cider press is now at the top of the
waiting list.

We also got as far as we can go on the water damaged home comfort wood
stove. Now awaiting inspiration.

The cat was out in the sunshine chasing its tail.
