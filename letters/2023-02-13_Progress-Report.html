February 13, 2023 · Monday

Progress Report

9 photographs·243 words

Work continues on tying up loose ends.

I put the cleaned power steering pump back on the generator, installed it
in car and promptly discovered the generator was frozen. So, the generator
is now in the shop to be rebuilt.

Along the same line, we patched the pinched tube, again, installed and
inflated, again, and it leaked down, again. To be continued. I do not give
up easily.

I did give up on attending the upcoming wedding and punted. Rats !!!

This was my week to catch the coughing hacking bug which has been passing
through the family. I suppose GSH will be next in line.

Sorting, organizing and labeling continued in the shop. We need to keep
moving, the '37 engine should be back soon meaning attention will shift
back to that project.

Cleaning up the woodyard also went along very well. We have a good start
toward firewood for next season. All this without putting the hydraulic
splitter on the tractor. Plan to save it until I get old. Like the box of
videos.

We are cutting it close this year. This stack is the last on this year's
firewood. Except for a bit of emergency wood still stored in the barn
behind the fire engine.

Early in the morning. Peace and quiet.

One grandchild lives in the land of milk and honey.
