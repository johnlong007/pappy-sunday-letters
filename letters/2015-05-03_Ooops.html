May 3, 2015 · Sunday

Ooops

8 photographs·168 words

Hit wrong key before I could spell check. Rats

Friday we went back to Doll Flats for AT work. The day met all the basic
requirements for cold, wet, and miserable. I had planned to go off and dig
ramps at lunch break but we only took five minutes as was too cold to stop
working.

Yesterday it was chilly when I got ready to run around. While getting
ready, I pictured Katy in her racing togs and thought she would appreciate
a real fashion statement.

For the first time in quite a while, we washed and cleaned the cars
yesterday. I think she looks very good for the age and mileage.

The truck requires two large (expensive) batteries. It has been showing
signs of battery failure recently. Today, it would not start, even with my
super booster, Rats.I have to get it going as need it Tuesday to haul a
load of locusts logs to AT work site.

Rumor has it that Janet is a graduate, Congratulations.
