November 17, 2013 · Sunday

More Cat Tales

9 photographs·532 words

I decided that going for the stress test was probably in order so had it
scheduled and went to JC for the test.
It is a standard test, the Bruce protocol. They do a bunch of preliminary
stuff then attach several sensors to the chest area and put you on a
treadmill. A target heart rate has been picked , as far as I Can tell based
only o age. Mine was 122/min.

The treadmill is operated in three minute steps with angle and speed being
increased each three minutes. When I reached the 122 the test conductor
asked if I wanted to continue. I said yes and went on until it reached max
allowable for me, 136 I think.

Anyway, they did not tell me anything, but, it seemed to go fine and should
hear from the Dr. by and by.

It was cold early in the week so I fiddled with the MM and the '37
Plymouth. But, did not take any pictures of either. Perhaps help will show
up at Thanksgiving to deal with both.

Carl moved Tuesday work day to Friday to avoid the bad weather. When he
moves the workday, it cuts into the number of willing workers. But, we went
for quality with 7 workers. We went to backbone rock where we have worked
off and on for the last couple of years improving an access trail to the
AT. The walk in is about one steep mile almost up to the AT. But, cutting
through a very rocky section.
The pin flags mark the lower edge of the finished trail.

John and I carry our hard hats in and carry them out. (Unless a forest
service person shows, it has happened once so far.)

The last time we were out, we were trying to pry big rocks. I cut a piece
of locust limb to use for a fulcrum. This time, Carl showed up with a real
,portable fulcrum. I suggested he patent it.

Positioning a step. I do not like steps in the trail. I prefer constant
incline unless it gets so steep as to be difficult footing when wet.

A finished section. Carl plans for 20/feet/day with his experienced
workers. But, not through a rock pile.

.I do believe they have a keeper!
I was quite surprised how readily she tolerated me. Aaron will have to
caution her about taking to strange men.

I was alos pleased to see all the signs indicating Vanessa is adapting to
Momma hood. Will have to wait and see how well Aaron is adapting.

Ah yes, the cat. Since I raised the bird feeders I have not seen the cat
make a catch. Today, It jumped for one, missed, then decided to go climb
the try to try to catch the fleeing (flying?) bird.
No success. However, we were relived that we did not have to call the fire
department to come get him out of the tree.

Everyone watch the concert?
