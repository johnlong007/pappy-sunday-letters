June 7, 2020 · Sunday

65 and Counting

13 photographs·468 words

Days go slow, years go fast.

I worked in Detroit for a year after HS, GSH went to Milligan and sparkled.

I worked May 30th, 1955, got off at 1:18 in the morning left detroit behind
in our '48 Plymouth (I had bought the week before for $100), getting into
Erwin late in the afternoon.

The wedding at Sunday noon right after church service at 1st Christian in
Erwin.

About all we had talked over was a general understanding, six children and
I would be a Mechanical Engineer to support the family.

This week, I went through 65 years of pictures and picked out about 50 or
so which I sent to our kids (on gmail) just to test their reflexes.

After all that dithering picture below is our actual celebration dinner.

In other news, for the first time ever I baked a pan of biscuits ! Our NW
grandchild sent me the fixings.

back to me being me, Brother Herb had more stuff than I do and left a lot
more out in the weather. Farmer Lisa asked to get his blue truck running.
We took two spare tires along, changed one flat, dragged it out and fired
right up.
Now, it needs a battery and Lisa reported the tire we installed went flat
before we got out of sight.

He had another truck which Lisa is loaning me (us). One brake was locked so
we loaded by jacking up and pulling onto dolly.

No flats on that truck. However, today I looked in the bed of the dodge and
found the spare we had taken had exploded all on its own.

Pretty often on Thursday evening we go to the Long's for B & G.

Coming back home a herd of deer were in the Walnut field. more than six.

All the bird deterrents seem to have worked. Wayne, GSH and Diane picked
enough for two pies which Diane produced.

Three people ate one entire pie at Sunday dinner. Speaking of Sunday, word
is we may be able to go back to church next Sunday. But, not a word about
SS so far.

Thomas has taken a contract to buff the '63. Under direct supervision of
Diane.

I made a good start on sorting out the tangled mess of downed trees. Being
very careful, at least by my standards as using a long chainsaw in
confined difficult spaces is a bit tricky.

One day, we went to Pat's farm to see the MD in action. But, like the
little pig they had started early and had the baling done when we did get
there. He did send me a video.
