June 27, 2021 · Sunday

Shrinking

12 photographs·382 words

Days are getting shorter ! I do not know when I became so aware of the
season changes and the length of the days. As soon as we pass the line I
start dreading Winter. I liked living on the equator. The only change was
wet or dry. Always warm

Linda sorted out my picture taking issues. My ace photographer has quit so
I have been only using the incredible device. Turns out I had not been
deleting pictures received in messages and had filled the capacity.

Several family members sent pictures along for me Monday so I have those in
last weeks folder.

And.

The picture below was taken to show the guardian of the Alfa It sure is a
pretty car.

Work on the clutch problem continued.. Diane brought in a foreign car
expert. After having the pivot arm welded twice, incorrect position each
time, we are now waiting for the arrival of a new master cylinder. The plan
is to install the new cylinder and pedal assemble. Then mark the arm
position so it has to be correct ! Right, nothing can go wrong.

I have cooked the starter motor on the mower two times due to no proper
ignition switch. Wayne tired of ordering new starters and repaired the key
start switch. He did have a couple of helpers.

We go to the early service at church Generally a bunch of old folks. Today,
they had baby dedication. Some have predicted a baby boom after the
pandemic.

Speaking of baby boom. The mother deer and fawn live up above the pear
tree. She is not bothered by the tractor of lawn mower. The dog and the
fawn look very similar. The mother deer chases the dog when he comes near.

Mother dear and baby.

Pepper patch is doing well. Need to water it. Anyone else raising peppers?
The groundhogs are pruning my tomatoes.

The retaining basin retains water a lot longer than I expected. I would
like it to dry up so I can build the dam higher. I mowed above and around
it Friday. Came really close to turning the mower over.
