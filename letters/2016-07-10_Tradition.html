July 10, 2016 · Sunday

Tradition !

9 photographs·372 words

The Blountville July 4th parade goes back as along as we have lived here.
Then, for several years Summer Camp was scheduled to include July 4th. We
still watch the parade and I miss Summer Camp. A lot.

I have been taking a few pictures using the incredible device and doing OK.
But, something went amiss on the parade pictures. Anyone know what I did to
cause This ?

Tuesday it was GSH's turn to go to the Dr. I tagged along. It was a lot
like my visit (different Dr.) He poked prodded, thumped, listened
questioned, X-Rayed and nodded wisely. Then pronounced, you are getting
old. Not quite in those words. We talked about various issues to which he
said "There is a pill that may help." It is on order but has not arrived.
Stay tuned.

To demonstrate her fine condition, She peeled the Elm log. Still not sure
what we are going to do with it.

Wayne is moving right along on the pool project. This week was sealing
around the various lines.

Friday was AT work. Back up to same site. It was misty and windy all day.
Just for variety a picture of lunch time is included. Once more, I worked
by myself pretty much. Renovated a section and ended by converting a big
(bath tub size) rock into a pile of smaller rocks. I like busting rocks and
am considered the expert for our crew. Guess I may have found my gift.

Saturday we made a run to LLLL"s to check on her and her family.

It was a fun visit. We came back via Cookeville to deposit Maria.

I had been advised it was time to mow my section of the AT. Also, a stork
went through Friday night so it seemed a good time to check the trail.
Wayne went with me to do the heavy lifting. It was a six hour round trip
with no rest for the weary. We passed 6 hikers coming from the area
impacted by the storm (two campers had died when a tree fell on their
tent). They all reported trail in terrible shape, One said she had counted
251 blowdowns in a 5 mile section. Stay tuned.
