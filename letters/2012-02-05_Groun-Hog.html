February 5, 2012 · Sunday

Groun Hog

9 photographs·274 words

Here it is Groun Hog day , about time to be finishing digging potatoes and pulling corn ! But, the job is done. We still have a fine turnip crop for those who care for turnips.

Not only did Anita harvest the corn and potatoes, she also enlisted my services in chainsaw art. Next I plan to attempt a chainsaw moose.

Tuesday we worked on the AT. The crew I was with went in at Spivey driving a forest service road to no business knob. There, we renovated about 140 feet of trail which had sloughed off. Here is a picture of the trail with leaves scraped off and another after we had finished. The practice of spreading a thin layer of leaves on reworked trail is referred to as fluffing the cat.

For those who live in far away places with strang sounding names, a couple of pictures to show the local weather situation.

Friday, we went on a hike with the Friday Hikers. First time in a spell.

I spent all my free time working on the '37 pickup. I sure do like having the work shop built into the kitchen.

A long way to go. Notice the splashes of blue. Herb remembers the family pu as being blue so I will be able to get this one original and like the one we had long ago (and not so far way).

We did go to Decatur Al. on Wed. for the funeral of Bob Rahm. Not a sad event as it was, he lived a long and full life and kept his marbles right to the end.

Time for football !
