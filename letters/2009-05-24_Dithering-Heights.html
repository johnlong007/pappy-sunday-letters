May 24, 2009 · Sunday

Dithering Heights

16 photographs·482 words

The special "Hard Core" work days were last Sunday and Monday. I am
forwarding Carl's report on the results just in case anyone is interested.
Sunday it was divided into ten teams of ten each with a leader from our
regular crew. Monday, we had to walk in three miles to the work site. Everyone
carried tools and when we goy to the site everyone started working. It was
really astounding for a person who worked for years trying to develop
self-managed work teams. No assignments, no crews, no leaders, the kids just
worked.

For Tuesday, the big event was picking up Vanessa at the airport. People
who are accustomed to making international trips of 10 days with a carry on
bag do not think in terms of a lot of luggage.So, GSH took the Maita to the
airport. Fortunately, it all worked out and I had fun kidding Vanessa. I
think she really enjoyed the Ms. Tennessee event.

Kingsport had a car event. It was at the Wal-Mart parking lot. Very few
Mopar's. Only one car with a Hemi it was a '51 Plymouth convertible with one
of the very new, small Hemi engines. Bah.

Wed. was the trip to Linda's to go with her to Vandy on Thursday.

The trip was uneventful. No news on LLLL. I met with the people at St.
Thomas and am to go back this coming Thursday for a consultation with slice and
dice dr.No pictures from Nashville.
Friday we went to see Hal and Sue. Hal is continuing to hold his spirit and
dignity . Turns out Sue has a breast cancer. I think not a real bad kind
as those things go. She is due to have surgery this coming Wed. One side
only.

Saturday was a BIG day. Two for the price of one. I went with Dwight to
visit the drag race car. We skipped breakfast with the GOBs in order to have
breakfast with the GOGs.

It was fun. I was impressed that they passed to going to a motivational
presentation in order to come eat with us. I am not quite sure why Dwight has
no food. It is not because he skipped breakfast.
Then it was off to the car!
Dwight worked on wiring all day.Pat worked on putting in thin metal fender
liners. After they got finished, we put all the yet to be attached pieces
on the car at the approximate final location and put the car on the scales.

After getting home, we ate a roast beast dinner with D,D and V before
stopping by to check on the Albatross then finished off by checking on the out
of sight if not out of mind Katy. Just in time for a welcome home event.

Finally a name that creature opportunity.
