June 2, 2013 · Sunday

Rock Science

4 photographs·235 words

Two major hurdles this week.

First, we finished chipping a path across the rock we have been working on
for several weeks. If anyone wants to check it go to Dennis Cove and walkin
about 1 mile in the direction opposite Laurel Falls. This picture is not
quite the finished product but close.

Wayne came over long enough to get the '37 out and run it up and down
drive. Linda and family were here Monday, Andrew helped get the '37 ready
for test drive.We did prove once more that SI engines will not run unless
the battery is connected. It is looking doubtful that it will be under
primer in time to take to Blountville tractor (and old car) event in two
weeks.

Rebecca stayed here in exchange for a goat from Herb.

We have had a delightful week. At least I have. She is not quite as willing
worker as the yappy dog but close. We worked on a lot of items garden,
mowing , brush covering BBs', cherry tree, Red RBs etc.

The biggest hurdle was draining and scrubbing the dismal swamp.

It is now drained, scrubbed and refilled. She wanted it back in operation
so we decided to delay repainting until a later date. Still have to add
salt and get the circulating pump going.
