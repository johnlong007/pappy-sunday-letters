March 2, 2025 · Sunday

Days of Yore

17 photographs·412 words

When Alan was born, we borrowed a portable bed from the hospital. When
Diane was born, we taped two cardboard boxes together to make a bed. For
Anita,we had just moved into our first purchased house and went upscale
with a dresser drawer bed. We had just joined the Baptist church in Renton,
the preacher came to visit bearing a single rose. He never batted an eye.
For Laura, I made a bed to fit between the seats in the 63, it served Linda
and Wayne also. The bed is still available if anyone is in need.

Any of the grandchildren remember this? Make you sad? Sure does make me
sad. Anyone building a house and in need?

The family continues to expand and I do not want to be left behind. Diane
made me a cheat sheet so I can check while watching the picture frame.

The Buchanan side of the family came from Mitchell county NC.For her own
good reasons, Rachel wanted to go visit the home place. The roads and
houses have changed. But, not the Beans Creek Cemetery.It was a fun outing
for me. Next we may try the maternal side.

Actually the storm did some damage. An opportunity.

The fair weather workers made progress getting to the source of problem on
the crawler.

The crawler has a wet clutch and a dry brake for each track. Turns out, the
seal was leaking and the dry brake got wet. I had to read up to find out
how they actually work. Turns out to be simple. They were designed by
engineers for their usage.

I think the scooter is ready to reassemble. However, , the rear bumper is
missing and ribbed rubber to use is missing from front and rear.

We have pretty much finished horse drawn implements and are started on hand
tools.
These tools were not operated by wimps.

In the Spring, ny thought turn to pruning the fruit trees. Last year , I
coaxed and assisted Diane, this year, I watched.

We dug out a 12" cast iron camping pot. Now ready for use.

Perkins buddy is in FL. so, Diane fed me Sunday Bfst.

We now know, she can not only make a cherry pie, she can also make an apple
pie.
