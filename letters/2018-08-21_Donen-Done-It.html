Done'n Done It

12 photographs·430 words

Something is missing , cannot quite pin it down but something is missing.

Dave Edwards is one of my last remaining contacts with the world of the R &
F.

He graduated from high school in '65 and pestered me until I sold him the
'65 Plymouth convertible. I delivered it to him in Rochester NY. He is a
picky person, like Bob, he wants things done right.
For unknown reasons, he keeps coming to me to help with car refurbishing
details. This time, he brought a rear quarter window over examination. I
believe the car projects is borderline life saver for him. Probably for his
wife Pat also.

Thursday, we went to Wisconsin to help Alan with his treasure relocation
projects. In this picture, there are two Fiat "1.9s". One to be relocated
to Reservoir Road and one to be parted out.
Here, GSH is carefully removing door panel retaining clips under the
watchful eye of her firstborn.

My role was remove other pearls. The first being a power window drive unit.

Friday evening the auto transport vehicle arrived and Saturday morning the
last Fiat to Tn. was loaded.

The final item to be removed from the donor vehicle was the engine. The
best part of that project was working with Mathis.
GSH and I rode home from church with Mathis and Janet Sunday. En route we
got a tour of his home. I also got to talk with Mathis at some length
before the games started. He has a very interesting background and has
found a job which sounds right down his alley.

Of course, there was time for strolls down memory lane along with bird
watching and nature photography.

While we were in Wisconsin, we went to a wedding. For pictures, one should
go to the performers.
I did capture a few points of interest (to me) prior to the rehearsal.

Hob nob, To associate <https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/associate#Verb> with
in a friendly <https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/friendly> manner
<https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/manner>, often with those of a higher
<https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/high#Adjective> class
<https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/class#Noun> or status
<https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/status>.

There was a reception dinner after the wedding.

I am really learning to enjoy being old and treated honored. I never saw it
coming.

We got to sit at the front table with the Parents and the priest.
His family name is Showers. I had fun with that.

Bride's Dad is Egyptian. His parents are alive, well and were present.
Coptic Christians. I knew a bit about the Coptic Christians but now I now a
lot more. If you have inclination, study up and learn how they are faring
in Egypt currently.
