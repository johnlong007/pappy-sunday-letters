May 4, 2025 · Sunday

Mayday !

14 photographs·291 words

I wanted to go dance around the Maypole but this is as close as I could
get. Many will notice the school playground is gone.

I feel awkward saying "my". but, i guess it is my family now. The family
continues to exceed my expectations !

Having family members come in and select artifacts they really want has
proven to be very satisfying to me. I think it all goes back to my wooden
egg.

When you marry you get the entire family. In this family, being safe and
comfortable with various personal protective devices is part of the culture.

One more good work evening and the horse drawn implements should all be on
display.

We put a new belt on the mower and also sharpened the blades

I beat up my arm working on the mower. I will not be surprised if I am low
on platelets tomorrow. Fortunately, Katy has dispatched some just in time.

Our vegetable garden is planted and the wagon is in service.

Alan came over to work on the crawler and got snabbled to repair siding
woodpecker damaged siding.

The pine tree which was leaning is no longer leaning.

We heard a racket one evening. Diane dashed out to find one feeder down and
a fat Raccoon wadling away.

The original problem with the crawler was failure to be able to make it
turn left. A & C have installed a new seal to cure that problem. Now, they
have moved on to preventive maintenance and cosmetic work. I am glad I can
still do operator work.
