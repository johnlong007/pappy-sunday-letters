June 9, 2013 · Sunday

Inactive

9 photographs·171 words

I did not graduate this week. I did not go to or fro Nepal. Did not go to
California. Did not leave on a trip to Alaska. I almost went to Erwin.

Brother Dave (in the mountains church friends are brother or sister), and I
got back on the do-gooder cleaning gutters for a little ole widow.

GSH wanted to go visit her sister Nancy and husband Lewis. Nancy had a face
lift a few years back. On an unrelated topic, she has pet chickens. Lewis
has quite a collection of guns.

I skipped AT work so I could go oa hike with Maria and Rebecca. We went to
the Pinnacle tower at Unicoi. A fine outing, it is a ten mile walk.

Then we took Rebecca home and visited the animal farm.

Some may be interested in knowing that GSH and I can actually load brush on
the trailer. Now can we unload?

Can she bake a cherry pie?

Chinquapins are in full bloom.

A quite week on Ellis Road.
