August 11, 2019 · Sunday

Wheel Spinning

9 photographs·332 words

We finished last week with the '63 having a shiny new paint job. Forest
Green, just like new. I left it along giving the paint time to cure until
Tuesday when PBBD gave over to check on the ole folks. She took the masking
off, GSH cleaned blue from the new tires and I sanded the trunk lid.
Why did I sand the trunk lid? Because I "knew" a new paint job must be
finish sanded. Paint went flat !
I sent a picture to Pat the painter. He replied very nicely "Urethane paint
should not be sanded, it will lose shine".

Sure enough, flat as a flitter.

We have paing left and Pat said he will be back for the Bristol race this
weekend. If he can fit it in he will come by and repaint as required.

At what age will I become wiser?

I mentioned Bobcat hydraulic oil leak. The dog helped me load Bobcat .
Wayne took to shop to get leak repaired along with routine 25 year checkup.

GSH polished the car bumpers and I installed back on the car. Still have to
get the lights back in operation before it is street legal. But, I do not
want to drive until painting is finished lest it get dirty again.

Friend Don gave us a box of tomatoes Wed. night at church. I ate two after
we got home. Since then we have been having tomatoes at breakfast, lunch
and supper. I emailed this picture to him Thursday morning. Guess what !
brought me another box of tomatoes to church today.

I think I can make a lot more progress on the holding basin after the
Bobcat comes home. I am aiming for 50' by 50' by 10 deep. Calculate that
will hold about one inch rainfall which will then slowly seep into the
ground. What can go wrong.
