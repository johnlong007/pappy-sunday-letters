Trip Report

6 photographs·301 words

Overseas Adventure Travel is a sub group within Grand Circle Travel. They
bill themselves as more active and catering to "small" groups. Never more
than 25. We have traveled with them before. Pretty middle class folks.
They have two 100 passenger ships which are used on the Adriatic. They are
trying to increase their off season business so offered a better price
trip.
We flew into Zagreb, the capital of Croatia, spent the first and last
nights at a hotel in the center of town.
There were two groups of 25 each with a tour leader. The boat moved from
town to town along the coast. We would debark meet a local guide, walk around
town or take a bus ride to a local place and walk around it.
Most meals were on the boat.
Most of the places we visited were Christian. We visited one Moslem town
which had been heavily damaged and one village which had been purged of all
Moslems.
Here is a picture of our tour route

In the Moslem town, our local guide was a young, Moslem girl.

The weather was about like it is here. In one town, we hiked to the top of
hill along the old city wall. Our ship is at the end of the pier,

Most of the time, there was some form of entertainment on board in the
evening. The rest of the time people milled around and talked.

There were a bunch of California ethnic Chinese on the trip. They were
difficult to get to know but very interesting.
A high point of the trip was a dinner with a local family

They were living like we did when I was growing up except they also make
wine and brandy. We brought a pint of each back with us.
