Everyone

I wanted to thank you all for your prayers. My mother (Donna
McInturff) passed away Friday, February 6 at 11:25 pm.

I am very thankful that she passed peacefully. My sister from
Florida arrived at the airport at 9:35 pm and was able
to get to the hospital and spend sometime with her before she
passed. She started going dowhill this afternoon and things
progressed quickly
during the evening.

I am also very thankful that we were not forced to decide to remove
her from the ventilator as her heart stopped at 11:25 pm.

My three sisters and I were all able to be by her side and let her
know it was ok if she went. She died about 15 minutes later.
