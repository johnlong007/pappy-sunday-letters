March 24, 2024 · Sunday

So So

13 photographs·412 words

Monday lab results were not really encouraging. Then, in the evening
Trouble tried to cross 126 and got hit by a car. Broke her neck so she went
quick and clean doing what she wanted to do. I found out I had really
learned to like her for her boundless energy. She is our third in a row to
meet a premature death.
I think if the family really thinks I need a dog the only one of interest
would be another Pom.

The mouth/tooth story continued this week. My dentist prior to Allison,
punted and sent us to an Oral surgeon in Bristol. I got in to see him on
Thursday. On time and a very professional warm up team. Dr.
really pleasant and comfortable. Looked at pictures, looked at tooth and
gum. Said nice things about the tooth and Allison's workmanship then said
"let's not get in a hurry, I think you need to get it cleaned and it will
be OK".

Just below is the original note from Allison when all this started.

You probably need it cleaned out thoroughly. More than aunt Diane
can do. I can do it but I'm far away

2/3rds of brother Jimmy's children stopped by for a visit. It seems the TB
was a false test result alert. I think the plan is to put him into a
"waiting for God" facility when he gets out of the VA.

Got another willing worker trainee.

The BB's are covered.

We moved the limbs from the downed pine tree to the brush pond. I
consolidated with the tractor and blade. Now, I need the crawler to smash
the whole works.

We got the truck back from the welding shop. Next, I must straighten the
bent bumper and reinstall it.

Our only pet. This cat is expensive. one morning he was hacking and
wheezing. Anita took him to the vet for treatment. Now, we are trying to
keep him inside when it is cold outside. Like Bartlebe, he would prefer not
to.

Catherine delivered the pine tree log and Alan turned it into boards. No
waste around here.

PT work continues.

LLLL came to visit and give me a much needed haircut. While here, we went
to visit neice/cousin Mari and Eric in WV. They have animals and many toys.
