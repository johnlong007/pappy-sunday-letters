October 21, 2018 · Sunday

Vote !

20 photographs·464 words

Vote early, vote often.
We were there when they opened on first day of early voting. Quite a few
people, A good sign since this is East Tn.

Wayne was out for Fall break this week so he was back and forth a bit.
First thing Monday he came after the truck as he wanted to get rid of some
scrap metal. Probably left overs from the village smithy I suppose. Anyway,
we rounded up the collected scrap (not counting cars, trucks and tractors)
before he could get away in the truck.

Weather forecast was for getting cold. I rounded up netting to cover the
strawberries but delayed one day. Guess what, the deer invaded that night.
Also pruined the tomatoes and are keeping all the persimmons picked up.
However, the barn door is now securely locked.

Everyone remember the '86 Dodge truck GSH and Laura purchased many years
ago? Wayne has been in possession for a spell. He brought it back on the
dolly. Said he plans to take a running go at it next Summer. When he rolled
it back off the dolly, snagged the bumper.

Fortunately, using the tractor front tire to push truck back into shed the
bumper when right back into place.

Due to a combination of circumstances, Thomas was left in my TLC for a
while. Picture is distorted. Not sure about boy.

A big part of my indoor time was spent on the '63 left front wheel brake.
I, being me< recruit help wherever I can.

Our SS teacher is Joe Taft. He arranged a barge outing at Bays Mtn. park
for his ole folks SS group.

Another day we made a run to Erwin to visit Farmer Lisa. Wayne wanted to
have another go at roof leak. We also aimed to patch the Muskrat Ramble
(you tubr it) hole in the driveway and retrieve the red dodge car.

This story is to be continued.

While we were busy with the car, GSH and Lisa were clearing weeds and vines
from the BB bushes.

The driveway has a muskrat hostel underneath. The emergency exit now has 5
gallons of gravel and 80# of concrete. Reckon that will work?

After many fits and starts, the left front wheel is all buttoned up I
expect the other side to go a bit quicker.

While working on the crawler fuel system,

We learn ed that a trove of Yellow Jackets have set up housekeeping in an
old mattress left on the Mack back. The cat was very interested.

Finally !
