October 6, 2019 · Sunday

Tweedle Dum

9 photographs·336 words

I am confident that the family members who proclaim they do not post
updates is because nothing ever happens in their lives will be inspired
after hearing about our work on the old folks home.

Monday Albert came by and said his mail box was on the same post and he
would like it replaced. Another trip to Lowe's.

I discovered a tooth was missing from Bobcat bucket. Got a replacement at
dealer for $3. Surprise there. Then broke out the welder and tacked all the
teeth. Found my instant shade change welding helmet has died. Another
surprise.

After adding another 5 gallons of hydraulic oil ($45) Bobcat is working
again. I figured I better get as far as I am going on retaining basin
before the predicted rains arrive as will not be able to get in again for a
good while (If I do will not be able to get out). So, now I am ready for
rain to come down and pond to come up. Satay tuned.

Pulled the valve cover on the '63. Found one valve stuck and a bent push
rod.

Have not been able to locate a new replacement push rod so I pulled one
from the swamp parts car.

After I worked with Niece Lisa to pull the fence posts, I threw the chain
up on the mower deck. later, while mowing, the chain fed down through blade
nut access hole onto top of the stump jumper and wound around the drive
bring the mower to a halt.
Getting to the chain required dropping the stump jumper. Taking the
retaining nut loose required a long cheater.

One reason is about as good as any for an event I suppose. PBBD had an
event this afternoon. 5/6ths of our children, a selection of GC and GGC
showed up and as best as I can tell, a good time was had by all.
