November 24, 2013 · Sunday

Bring Up a Child------------

13 photographs·567 words

Anyone interested in hearing more about the cat? I have got more feedback
on the antics of that cat than any other topic including antics of GSH.
I enjoy watching the cat. It stalks birds, squirrels, deer and turkeys, so
far I can only confirm catching birds and chipmunks.

He sat under the bird feeder for a good while, jumped twice and caught
feeder but came up empty pawed. So, decided to climb the try and see how it
looked from above.

After while, since no birds came within range, he came down. In case you
wonder if cats, or at least this cat, come down forward or backward, we now
know.

While not watching the cat, I cleaned on the MM and finished assembly.
Dwight has procured paint and Diane has mixed and matched to obtain what we
hope to be close to MM "Harvest Gold". Now, how is it to be applied ? Might
be a job for one or more high skill, low cost, willing workers.

I do intend to move the tractor and the '37 out very soon to make room for
the to be chosen Winter project.

It was cold on top of the mountain when we went out to do AT work.

We were roughing out a relocation to be completed with the conscripts
enticed in during Damascus trail days. A "bread crumb" trail as we do not
want them to get lost (or injured). Mostly grubbing Laurel and moving large
rocks. Not quite sure why we worry about a bunch of kids getting hurt and
have hazards removed by a bunch of old codgers.

This shows a section I prepped. Meanwhile some of the others were wrestling
with a big stump. We will go back later and clear some of the rocks shown.

The Summer floods had left a bit of damage by moving part of our gravel
into the neighbors field and leaving ditches in the driveway. So, rather
than try to retrieve the gravel we had more delivered.

Close supervision is required for heavy equipment operation.

During lunch one day, it occurred to me that we do not always dine in
accordance with the best of Miss Manners.

GSH never rests. She was searching her button collection for the current
Christmas Wish project.

We are still thinking about and talking about building a house on top of
the hill at the Hillside. On the way back from AT work I stopped to take a
picture of a house which I think fits in well with the surroundings.

Wed. night was Thanksgiving dinner night at church. Then Saturday evening
we went to Erwin to Shallow Ford Baptist Church , the church of my
childhood, where the youth group fixed dinner for the old folks and waited
on them hand and foot. It was fun.

It was over and clearing out when I took this picture. The girl with white
sweater is Freida Bowman. A cousin (two ways) same age, with whom I walked
to elementary school then went on to high school together. A really good
person.
