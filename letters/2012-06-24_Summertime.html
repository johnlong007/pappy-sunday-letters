June 24, 2012 · Sunday

Summertime !

8 photographs·397 words

My do-gooder work consisted of retrieval mission. I suppose it depends upon
how one looks at it as to whether that counts or not.

While everyone else was going to Alaska to be there for the midnight sun,
Vanessa came home. Just in time for her 29th birthday celebration.

In the meantime, back on the ranch in River City, Work on the race car
proceeds.

Closer to home, a couple of things took place. For one, Bob helped modify
new handles for my Oliver "40" plow just in time for the plowing season.
If you have not heard about the time I tried to plow the garden with this
plow hooked to the tractor with GSH as tractor driver remind me to tell
you. The short version is that she does not respond well to voice commands.

I know you are waiting for the follow up on the DOA VW. I took it back to
the local mechanic, the one who had installed the new clutch. He called to
report the four bolts which hold the axle housing to the transmission had
broken. A big relief. Although I am virtually certain that was a sign the
bolts were not properly torqued upon installation, I told him to go ahead
and replace the bolts and to install a new timing belt. So, car is back on
the road and we hope for a long period of trouble free driving.

Speaking of trouble free driving, The Studebaker/Chrysler now has an
exhaust system. It really sounds good. However, experience has shown it is
only good for about five miles before running out of fuel. Best opinion
seems to be the high volume electric fuel pump is heating the fuel too much
and causing vapor lock. Have not yet decided upon a solution. Maybe smaller
fuel pump, maybe a return line to keep fuel circulating, maybe just run
wide open to keep much higher fuel flow. Wayne did say it seems to have too
much power for its weight, Dwight says about 8#/HP.

Mostly this week I fiddled with the '63. I think all the trim has been
rounded up. I took a seat from the parts car to install while interior is
being refurbished.

The berry season is upon us. I hear a blackberry cobbler calling now so
believe I will close..
