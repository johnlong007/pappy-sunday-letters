July 25, 2010 · Sunday

Adventure, Romance, Intrique

9 photographs·310 words

Dave is Having a slow erratic recovery from Lyme disease. Some days OK but
more not feeling well at all.
But, Monday he felt OK so we did do-gooder work for a LOC from church. That
involves some work and a lot of sitting and listening. But, very rewarding
in the here and perhaps the hear after.
Tuesday I skipped the AT work. Dave came over and we cut cherry trees for
Anita. Also rewarding as she is appreciative.

Wayne came over to do sandblasting for his engine project and to haul off
treasures we sorted out during Summer Camp.

Of course, the big news event was procuring a dog. Bob had his information
network poised to ready to spring into action. A young male had been turned
in to Kpt animal shelter. GSH and Allison went down and inspected him.

Friday we took him to the vet to be tutored. The signs are that he will be
a fine addition to the family.
The ongoing frustration for the week was the little crawler. It has been
parked out front for three weeks. I have tried, Dwight has tried, Herb has
tried. So far, cannot get it to run. You can add your own caption here.

This was Fun Fest week in Kpt. We went down Thursday night for supper.
Guess what GSH had.

Ice cream.

Friday and Saturday the Buchanan family reunion was held in Jonesboro. I
did not get any new information , pictures or stories. But, it was fun. Most
of the participants are from Joe's first family. We did go to Bean's creek
to see the new grave marker installed for first wife (Mary Jane Hicks).

Sunday I went to Erwin to see Herb (and raid his garden). They had just got
back from a birthday event for Kaylee. She was quite proud of her haul.
