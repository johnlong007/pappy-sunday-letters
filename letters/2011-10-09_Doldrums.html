October 9, 2011 · Sunday

Doldrums

5 photographs·374 words

Monday bright and early we headed out for a 3 day-2 night hiking
/backpacking on the foothills trail . It starts in South Carolina and ends
in NC. The two of us, Jim Foster and Kim Peters.

It went very well. Started about noon Monday, out about noon Wed. Left at
daylight Monday, home at dark Wed. Jim and Kim used hammocks. We took a tent
but did not use it. Just rolled out.

We walked about 25 miles and only passed one couple who were going in the
other direction.

Of course, once we got home GSH went back to work on the table.

I, on the other hand, did a bit of work on the "car". It has leaked coolant
at the expensive cross over pipe in spite of many attempts to stop the
leaking. I took the pipe off and per Dwight's directions put in two gaskets.
No progress on the steering rack. But, if we ever get it running I will give
a report on the leak (if it is good news).

Friday and Saturday the Apple event was held in Erwin. I took several bushel
of walnuts over for Flo. Then, we went down to the center of Erwin for the
floating class reunion of UCHS. Several members of the class of '54 were
standing on the corner.
One was GSH's heartthrob. He gave her a very enthuastic hug and she said she
would have been thrilled had it shown that much interest once upon a time.
Anyway, he is the one with the blue shirt. Ponder that.

This afternoon, we had a SS outing at the teachers house. Also, the mother
of Dave Dunham died last night. They lived in Portsmouth Ohio but he came
back here today so we stopped by to visit with Dave and Carol.

Oh, I took the new and improved mower up on top of the hill where the pine
and walnuts trees are so big and thick I can no longer mow with the tractor.
I think it is going to be just what I need but do need some shields and
covers before I go too much further. So, stay tuned.
