The Eyes Have It

6 photographs·240 words

Has anyone noticed that our extended family keeps multiplying and deviding?

So much so that the family picture gallery has a new annex. Perhaps others
will be motivated to contribute .

My brother had a number of Chrysler automobiles , His departure left one
nice, low mileage car parked in the field. I hate to see things going to
waste and hate to see automobiles parked out in the weather. So, Wayne
helped me retrieve one of Herbs which we brought here quite some time back.
It took a bit of time and $$ to get it in safe operating condition, theen,
we learned that no one wants a nice, economical, safe easy to maintain old
American sedan. So, with the consent of Lisa, it was donated to kidney
foundation. Both Herb and Dwight would approve I believe.

​Once more could not get act together for AT work, Otherwise it was the
same ole same ole.

Moving fireplace firewood from the barn up close to the house. One should
cut firewood a year ahead, Which means I should be out harvesting next
Winters firewood now. Maybe I can get in on one of those do-gooder projects
where church people furnish wood to little ole people.

​We did clear off some downed trees on the pine covered hill out front and
brought it up to burn in shop stove.

​One day PBBD came over while I was out and about.
