Moiling Along

13 photographs·335 words

No AT work this week due to schedule conflicts.

But, we did have a social event going to eat supper with Dave and Carol
Dunham. Dave has more health issues than any two people I know.

On the home place, we moved the last of the fireplace wood from the barn to
here at the house. I believe Charlie split the wood we brought up. May have
to call him back to active duty.

It has been a while since I gave any advice on t​he role of a GSH. This
week the shoe is on the other foot.

​
Under the heading of outdoor living .

​
And not to be outdone

​ Work continued (at a slow pace) on the '63. The hood has been cleaned and
installed. It will have to come off but for now it is installed.

Linda came up Friday with Andrew. He helped me install the arm rests . Adam
and Sarah arrived Saturday.

​I have removed the trim from around the windshield getting ready for
windshield replacement. Mark Coates gave me name of person to do the job.
Stay tuned.

The tearing down and building greater project continues. It looks like
ripping stuff out is about finished. Also, block wall construction appears
well along.

​
I did help Dave Edwards a bit with the power windows on his car and have
compiled a list of items needed for the '63. Plan to check it twice then
send on its way this week.

The really big event was a family get together Saturday .

Children
Spouses
Grandchildren
more spouses
And Great Grand Children.

Oldest grandchild was there. I took the new 1911 out for his inspection. He
took it apart, put it together and gave live demonstrations of proper
usage. I was impressed . I would bet on him in any shooter confrontation
with any weapon and any range.

Oh, we also had an indoor event.
