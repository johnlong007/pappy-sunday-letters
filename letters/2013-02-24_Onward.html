February 24, 2013 · Sunday

Onward !

9 photographs·379 words

The big adventure this week was getting BTES to string a fiber optic
cable to the house so we not have (relatively) high speed Internet. I
understand a knowledgeable person can do all sorts of things, instant
mail, telephone, face to face a whole bunch. I have sort of hinted
around in hopes Joe will come by and teach us some new tricks.

No AT work this week as work day was on Thursday, the day BTES came to
install cable.

In other exciting news, it has warmed enough that we ventured out to
start clearing more downed pines and brush out of the woods. As I
remember, Linda and GSH planted the trees on that hill in the early
80s. I had gone in and sawed a bunch at the base to thin but had just
left the sawed off parts to decay. John et al dragged a lot out some
time back . Since I could not see and signs of their return to
complete the job I commissioned GSH.

The Maintainers spring event was this week. I got a 2,000 hour patch.
Actual hours are about 2,200. That is a years work hours !

Winter wood supple is going to work out perfect I believe. We moved
the last of this years wood up near the house for the expected brief
cold spells. We have plenty split and stacked for next winter which is
as far ahead as this grasshopper is planning !

Along the same line, GSH found herself getting drafted to help get
the MM out of the barn and up here to the house. Saturday, Wayne came
over. We dragged the '37 up and down but could not get it to run.
Dwight arrived just in time to say "I told you so". "The valves are
stuck open".

Now, both the MM and the "37 are work in progress.

Sunday we went to Knoxville to visit Sue Tabor. I forgot my camera but
Nancy was there and took a picture with my incredible device. However,
I do not quite know how to extract and dispatch said picture. Stay
tuned.

We got a couple of very nice, newsy, informative notes from various
family members this week. I am appreciative. On the other hand
.
