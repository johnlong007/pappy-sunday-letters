August 27, 2017 · Sunday

Passing Through

13 photographs·448 words

The eclipse was a once in a lifetime opportunity and I bungled it. Rats.
But, it was interesting, we went to PBBD's house and watching through my
welding lens and shared one set of official specs.
At the peak 96% I think, I could tell a lot of difference in amount on
incident radiant energy on my skin but little difference in ambient light.

​
Back to the routine, For the 18th year in a row, we had a group in from
Wright State for an AT work day.Year after year they prove to be an
excellent group of hard working, polite young folks. Carl was busy working
at the Fair so Jim Chambers had taken over planning and executing. He and a
few other had prepped a site about 2 miles in at Iron Mtn gap.

By days end, 350 feet of trail was refurbished. Jim gave a safety pep talk
before starting.

​​

I worked with John and a couple of kids on refurbishing steps in a
switchback.

John was in pain watching one try to drive a stake,

​Steps turned out well.

​Jim put on a feed after work day, Bob Peoples loves talking, especially to
young girls. He is retired AF and in the short rows from liver cancer but
tough as nails.

​​

Jim Chambers is retired Marine. He is divorced and semi-hermit. But, on
Internet he has connected with a high school "flame" from many years ago .
Now they are making sparks fly. I had fun kidding her.

​

The refurbished swing came home. The owner and the cat are happy to have it
back.

​One trailer, flat bed, is finished and legal. Saturday I decided to have a
shake down run and loaded the tractor.

Made it to hillside with no events. Unloaded and decided to make a check
out run.

​Tractor quit at top of the hill so I drifted back down and determined no
fuel to fuel pump. Jumped to the logical conclusion that fuel filters were
plugged and went by NAPA after church today to procure new ones. By
coincident, Wayne arrived just in time to install and noted the old ones
looked new. Fired up and drove about 100 yards and it quit again.This time
Wayne cleaned the gravity fuel feed line and remembered that was the same
thing which happened last time causing tractor to have like new filters. I
bet if the taln is not cleaned it will happen again !

t

​To end the week on a happy note, an August BD event was held tonight. ​.
PBBD brought a stack cake to bring back memories on my "granny garland". It
worked.
