Much Ado

15 photographs·329 words

Did I mention the Axe head Paul found? I have been working on it off and
on. It took three trips to get a handle and I still had to shorten about 6
inches. Bob procured some gun blue chemicals

​

GSH is recovering from her back injury. She still uses blower but left
handed.

​
Also good with her legs.

Of course nothing gets done without my willing worker.

​Less willing worker. Any of the Summer campers remember loading and
dumping brush.? Still plenty of opportunity.

​​I hope to get some Winter wood splitting done during apple butter
visitors. In preparation, I got the home made spliter out and checked out.
Wayne helped fabricate a semi-successful remote valve handle. Most of the
pieces are bigger than I want to toss around.

​One day we went for a ride in the mountains. Planned to go see the viaduct
on BR Parkway, Made a wrong turn and got lost. Maybe next week try again.

Monday was AT work day. We met up with a group on Univ. of NC students and
took them to Roan Mtn to spend the day. It was cool and foggy when we got
there but cleared off a bit after lunch. The AT goes over Jane Bald then
down through a swag. Carl took the kids over the top and part way down the
other side. They tend to group up and I prefer more space to work so I
pretty much worked alone all day.

Getting ready.

I cleared the grass and weeds growing over the trail shown in this picture,
Can almost see the other in the mist.​

Then I went on down into the swag and found a lake in the trail

​Dug a drainage ditch.

​By the time I worked my way back to the top it had cleared off. View
toward Grandfather mtn.

​​View on the drive down (for those who live elsewhere).

​Today, back at the home place
