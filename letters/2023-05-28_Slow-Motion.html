Slow Motion

15 photographs·416 words

As you may remember, the Oncology person prescribed a bone marrow biopsy .
Turns out to be a fairly big deal. Four hours allotted and put me out like
a light. I am not sure how they got the core drill through the bone but I
was assured it would be painful and local was not even considered.

Was and still is a pain in the backside. Another two weeks for results
to be explained.

Progress for the week ! Getting the pistons out of the block did
indeed call for a bigger hammer. The deed got done and the block has been
delivered to the machine shop.

All the parts were retaining and are being cleaned, one by one.e

Meals on wheels now provides dinner and a show.

Big celebration Saturday morning.
Steven had a birthday
Charlotte graduated (another engineer !)
Thomas is heading off to Lackland AFB.

PBBD fixed a multi course country breakfast featuring ramps and all the
fixings.

The home place beautification program continued. All home projects start
with (an expensive) trip to Lowe's. We now have a row od portable potted
plants.

One day the dogg commenced barking a different sounding bark. I told PBBD
it is turtle season. Sure enough, the dog had cornered a box turtle. She
took it way up on the hill and freed.. A few years back, ran over and
smashed two turtles at one time in the turning circle.. They are buried in
the pet graveyard. Event made me feel terrible for not seeing them and
stopping.
Anyway, when the dog commenced barking again Daine went to check and sure
enough another turtle. She took it back to unite with lover in the woods.
So, please keep an eye out when traversing the driveway.

Alan cut a chunk from one of the Oak logs some time back. It has now been
converted into firewood.Sort of rewarding work.

All kinds of cavorting around the world this week. I really like the Aura
frame Meredith provided. If anyone does not have access and would like,
tell Meredith.

I asked Wayne for a picture wearing his finery but got ignored.

Some overweight critted broke the cord causing sunflower feeder to fall and
be distorted. Alan repaired it better than new.

Pets at play, note cat in tree.
