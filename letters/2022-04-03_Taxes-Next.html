Max died.

He was the best fit of any dog we have ever had.Small, smart, high energy.
He was 1 when Rebecca found him and 4 this year. I miss him. The cat misses
him.

It is ok to start looking for another dog just like him.

I got a note from friend Don saying he had a hickory log which was free to
good home.Not knowing for sure how much hickory was going to be needed plus
curious about what kind of boards it would make, we went after the log.
It is now in line at the sawmill.

We got a pleasant surprise. Visitors from the left coast. It was really
good getting caught up on their adventures.

One day we went out to the hillside to observe the chipping/clearing
operation. I do like that rig. Much better than burning the brush.

Soon after we bought the property I hired my uncle Cliff to bring his big
A-C crawler over and cut a side road . It has been neglected for several
years but Alan has now cleared it to within a few feet of the ROW road his
neighbor maintains. I am not sure what Alan plans next for access.

The washing machine drain has been getting slower. With help from PBBD we
dug down to the line and cut a section out to gain access. Plans are
percolating as to what and how to proceed toward clearing the obstruction.
Stay tuned.

Various grandchildren are becoming homeowners. Along with owning a home
comes mowing a loan. I looked at the mowers at Lowes' 2-5K. Also know how
important fighting climate change and staying fit are to the young
homeowners I decided to offer to supply a reliable, easy start lawnmower. I
used one like this for 18 years on a quite large lawn. I have 2 in case of
need. First come, first served.

No band, no soccer, no football.

Recognize location?
