Fwd: Korea!!

0 photographs·654 words

Alright! I'll try to do a bit of an update now.

This trip has been absolutely fantastic so far! Even if thiswas the
only day I stayedit still would have been worth the money and time on
the trip.

Seyun and his brothe Sejun and their father met me at the airport when
i arrived. Seyun has by far the best english and does all of my
ranslating for me. His brother is an exreelynice guy as well. Which
is good because during the week Seyun is taking classesso i wil be
hanging outwith him.

Im reluctant to tell to many of my stories now because theni wont have
anything to talk about when i get back! and I dont think i can do them
justice.

The father is quite a character, hahaha. he has told me many times
that i am "handsomeboy!" He is looking forward to drinking beer and
soju (a VERYpopular liqour) withme and was a bit diapointed that I was
to tired last night. Its interesting, not only is drinking socially
very acceptable, but getting moderatly intoxivated seems to be as
well. From what I gather once boys turn 18 they drink with theyre
father often.

The grandmother is also absolutely hilarious! she talks and talks and
talks to me in korean and just laughs when i can't respond. She held
onto my arm and also called me handsomeboy for quite a while last
night.

Unfotrunatly theyre grandfather is sick. He still occasionaly dresses
in traditional korean clothes and doesnt speak abit of english.

Also his aunt, uncle, and three little cousins visited last night

Dinner was fantastic!!! it was absolutely delicous! everyone gets a
bowl of rice and then all the other food is put in dishes on the
middle of the table. you then grab some food from he comunal pot with
yourchop sticks, stuff it in yourmouth and chase it with a bite of
rice. They made i think 4 different traditional korean dishes that
each were very very good.

To sleep you find the hardest thing in the house and curl up on it.
I'm not kidding. the beds are kinda like a matt, about 2 inches off
the floor, about 3 feet long and about 2 inches longer than i am. I
call it a matt for lack ofa better word. it is extremely stiff and the
top surface that you sleep on is a hard plastic that trys to simulate
the look and feel of marble. (the grandparents bed is atually marble)
then you put a planket over that and a blanket over you and youre good
to go! It was similar to sleeping on a camping matt, which i think is
quite comfortable so i was quite happy with the arangement.

Shopping....
I went to the grocerey store with the family last night, it was
absoluetly packed. the aisles are about one thrid size, and everyone
just wonders around and bumps into each other. It's really realy
funny. it's not considered a bit rude,if someone bumps you then you
simply step aside. It reminds me strongly of the gas simulations we
would watch in chemistry where the molecules just bounce around off of
each other and dont change directions until they hit something else.

The time here is 13 hours ahead so its pretty easy to figure out.
(except for you ally your practicaly as far west as me ) I was unable
to sleep any on the way here so its been an easy adjustment to make. I
went to bed around nine last night and got up at 7.

thats all for now. ill try to send another another day but i really
just feel like this is taking away from all the stories i will get to
tel later!

-charlie
