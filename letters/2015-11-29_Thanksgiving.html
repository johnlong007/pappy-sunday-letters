November 29, 2015 · Sunday

Thanksgiving

10 photographs·202 words

A lot to be thankful for. Family, past,present and future.

Got a package from Lil' Sister making me confident she is out there
somewhere.

Went to Erwin to see Herb and had a fun visit with him and some of his
children and grandchildren.

Jimmy had won a free trip to Chicago to see his daughter Ellen. We got to
drop him off at TRI and pick him up on his return.

No AT work this week.

We did go see J & J upholstery people. Agreed on colors and materials. He
said three weeks for the job.

Had Thanksgiving dinner with one of our children. I do not normally take
pictures at another persons events but did take one of the reception
committee.

Did help Wayne harvest some firewood. Got so inspired I cut down a couple
of the dead pine trees beside drive.

No serious auto traumas this week.

After the deer ate the new strawberry plants I covered the bed. The plans
were recovering nicely when I weeded the bed about two weeks ago. Since
then, the cover and blown off. Plants have been eaten off down to the
ground. If the buds are gone, the plants will die.
