One Small Step

10 photographs·288 words

The bad news is that GSH took a spill stepping off the deck onto the
sidewalk. The good news is nothing broke. Gave us both a fright.

After many missteps, the Alfa clutch is now functioning properly. We also
have our own family version of an Italian mechanic.

No meaningful progress on the '53. Resident experts looked and pondered.
Measured and mediated. I think shift linkage adjustment will be next order
of business.. Alan crawled under and looked but I am pretty sure this
picture is not of him.

On the other hand !

The tractor is back together with new axle seals, final drive lubricant and
new brake shoes. The brakes have been iffy for many years. I hardly
noticed. I can scare wait to try it out .

Not everyone has a dentist who naked house calls . I have two !

It boggles me to face the reality of grown grandchildren. We went over to
local Long family for dinner and a show. While there, we celebrated a
birthday in absentia for Katy. It included singing happy birthday .

We got this picture after getting back home. Looks like she was with us in
spirit. BTW, her in laws were at church early service today. First time I
had seen the in well over a year.

I read in the WSJ that going through a scrapbook is a good way to get
parents to tell about their life events. I had recently dug out a
scrapbook kept by GSH during her high school days. Linda spent some time
poring over it with her Mom.
