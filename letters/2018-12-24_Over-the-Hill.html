December 24, 2018 · Monday

Over the Hill

6 photographs·268 words

One way of looking at the Winter Solstice. I am not sure at what age the
length of day became so Important to me. But, it is now.

We got the week started right by going to help Mary have a birthday. Tried
to tell he she should hold at three but to no avail.

On the home front not a lot was accomplished. Moved the spare parts car
into the parts car holding pattern. Wayne seems to be using up his break
retrieving parts. I suppose the moral is that if you have an old car you
best have a parts car, maybe two.

GSH got our Christmas tree decorated and it is now resting comfortable
awaiting Santa.

I keep working on "final: sanding of the '63 before primer is applied. I
keep finding places that do not pass my inspection. So, a lot of time
sanding and filling. I hope getting closer. No pictures, looks a lot like
last week.

Carl did have an AT work day. We worked on the section near Wilbur dam. A
bit chilly when we got started. One seldom sees a crew with such unbounded
enthusiasm.

The major task was to widen and install side logs on this section pictured:

Lunch time. After luch Carl informed us we were not supposed to be eating
there because the entire section is closed to open food because of over
friendly bears.

I think I could outrun some. maybe all of this group so no need to worry.
