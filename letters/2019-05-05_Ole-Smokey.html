May 5, 2019 · Sunday

Ole Smokey

13 photographs·494 words

While other family members were doing exciting things, here at the ole home
place we were doing the same ole same ole.
Sanford grew up in Roan Mountain and knows everyone there. A friend has a
pipe and muffler shop there. So, because I did not want to drive it with
cab filled with smoke and noise, we loaded it on the dolly and delivered to
the shop.

Clearly a good ole boy operation but appears to be quite good at what he
does.

Sanford drove it home, at least most of the way. I think it may be more
than valve guide seals, may have an oil ring problem. Sanford let it die at
a stop light in Bluff city. He said caused by dead battery. Rather than try
it I loaded on dolly to bring on home. Turned out it was a cockpit problem,
fired right up.

The best of the '63 parts cars seems to good to leave inoperable. So, into
the shop to be next in line. Should be a fun project. Notice I have a new
helper. Another willing worker.

Put up the paint booth. Now waiting for painters.

Cathy Case was a teacher at Bltv. She and Jerry were also regular Friday
Hikers. Each a bit broke down now and have moved into Baysmont. Cathy was
our go to person for flora and fauna information. She is not bubbling over
about Paw Paw trees and some kind of butterflies. Wants to start a grove at
Baysmont. Said seedlings are $35 each at Lowe's. I have provided her with
several. Also told me about milkweed and some other butter flies. Milkweed
seedling $7 each. I need to get out and start digging.

Life cycle of the ramps. They are starting to turn yellow and die down.
Next a seed pod stem will come up with a blossom on top. Will try to get a
picture in due time. Ramps will be later up higher on the mountains . I
have one more ramp run planned.

Took some ramps to Erwin, some for Dan (uncleaned) and some for Flo,
cleaned. I showed Dan how to nip off root and suggested how and where to
plant around his place, they should do well. Probably a couple hundred feet
higher than here.

Flo had some plants her son had delivered to her. I copped a hot banana
pepper. When she protested I told her I would take my ramps and leave. She
relented.

Here is the garden so far. Three tomato plants from Laura and one "hot"
pepper.

Had a routine check up with heart Dr. this week. All the measurements are
good but valve noise is louder. He said better get it checked lest a heart
attack sneak up. Scheduled a sono gram. stay tuned.
