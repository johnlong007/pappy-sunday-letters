Trailer-3

13 photographs·376 words

I would like to report glowing success on the trailer project. But, I
cannot. The welding shop did not comprehend my instruction. So, rather than
make the cut down axle fit into the spring mounting brackets from the
removed axle, they made the cut down axle the same length as the ones I had
removed.
The reworked assemblies did not fit.

Back to the shop for rework. Believing that it was elf evident that. my
instructions were not perfectly clear, I paid for the rework without being
asked. The reworked axle fit right in on the flat bed trailer and I believe
with lights, breakaway device and high capacity tires it should be good for
5 ton loads.

​However, when I started to install the other reworked axle in the other
trailer a close examination of their weld has caused doubts in my mind. I
suspect they got in a hurry while reworking and figuring it was on their
bill. Anyway, I am going to take that axle back once more and ask him to
assure me and himself it is good for a 6 ton load.

On the home front, we did a bit of apple juice making and a bit of apple
drying.

​

​​

I am looking forward to a stack cake just like my grandmother made.​

The long awaited ASU/AT day came and went.

​

​I hauled kids in and out across the farm. It started raining just as the
days work was finished so we loaded a bunch of drowned kids to go back
home. The work went very well, I believe due to a combination of reasons
this was the most productive group yet.

Almost on the home grounds. GSH has been working with B & L to repair and
refinish the porch swing.

​While that was going on, I admired the master suburban gardener at work.

​
We have a lot of ground hogs this year. I trapped one which lives under the
ag, building. We relocated it to a farm not too far away. It will be
interesting to see if it finds the way home.

​And finally, another hint on last weeks mystery photo. Name the two
persons in the picture and where was it taken?
