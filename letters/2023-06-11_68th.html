June 11, 2023 · Sunday

68th

15 photographs·527 words

Monday, June 5th. was our 68th anniversary. Talking with all the sources I
have available I get the impression we have a pretty good shot at a 69th.

Nephew James with all his family was in Erwin visiting . I missed seeing
the family last visit. So. I made an extra effort this time. Lisa's
daughter Sarah graduated a couple weeks back from MTSU but was not with
them. Their daughter Sophie just graduated HS and will be at ETSU this
Fall. I told her she can use us for a home base any time she wants or needs.

Ella provided strawberries again. This time I showed some restraint and
did not get a fever blister.

Wayne went along to Erwin at my request. He stomped around in the thicket
looking at some structural steel which might be of interest to Alan. He
also found the spring tooth harrow I could not locate last Summer. Another
James and Lisa daughter. Wayne in the thicket.

Found one tick on my trousers and one attached to my back after we got home.

Not quite sure how it happened but a bunch of the cypress strips stored on
the bed of the big truck in the barn had relocated. We put them back on
the truck bed along with a few choice expletives deleted.

Some time back PBBD helped me thin a walnut thicket. I found mowing around
the stobs to be a pain so with aid and comfort sawed off at ground level.

SF is fitting in well in my opinion. I expect she will be even more helpful
in the weeks to come.
Sanford brings breakfast from the Burger Bar on Friday. This time the
waitress sent B&G (no charge) to us. Connie at Perkins sends a Muffin every
Sunday and David drops it off .

Family came from miles around. MA just in from the left coast came by upon
her arrival. I really enjoy learning about her work and would love to get
to visit the farms she describes.

Another visitor from afar . This one from a previous life. Sandra worked at
ECC for a spell. She used to drop by at work where we shared views on life,
liberty and the pursuit of happiness. When she visits now she always makes
me feel good because she remembers those conversations.

Today more visitors from not so far. It was a fun visit. I spent a lot of
time during and after the visit thinking of suitable bundle from heaven
names.

Summer Campers. What is the first thing you notice in this picture?

More visitors. A fine, fun day.

All is well.

As usual, we went to the track. I predict Katy will shake up the YWCA in
Bristol.

FYI, I trapped a coon. Kept it in a trap for a few hours. Felt sorry for it
and turned it loose.
Today, the dog found another fawn on a hill out in front of the house.
