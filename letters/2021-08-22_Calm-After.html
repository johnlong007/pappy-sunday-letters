August 22, 2021 · Sunday

Calm After

11 photographs·366 words

Saturday morning breakfast in the hills of Tennessee. salt cured ham, eggs
and garden ripe tomatoes along with real biscuits.

The real delight to me was Janet sent produce from her own garden spot in
Wisconsin to her GP's in tn. I wonder if Christopher would have shared had
I hinted.

For many years I never passed a stalled motorist. One night on the way home
from ECC about midnight I stopped for a person who had let his car run out
of gas. It was a '54 Chrysler Imperial with wire wheels. In due time, I
ended up buying the car to get the wire wheels. PBBD overhauled the car
engine, brakes etc and the car is now parked in storage bldg. I have been
working for some time to retrieve one of Herb's car in order to find it a
new home. These wheels will fit.

It took a fair amount of cleaning to get ready to mount tires.

A highlight of the week was GSH's sister Nancy coming for a visit. Nancy,
like her sister, is one of a kind.

I kept hearing a racket while working in the shop. Turns out the
woodpeckers have moved to that end of the house. One would think the post
would be easier.

More flora and fauna. All week long the turkey families have been hanging
around. Several females and several frying size turklets.

At my request, Alan has taken and interest in the Studebaker. We were able
to gain access to the timing mar. Stil not got it set just right. To be
continued.

PBBD continues to develop more skill sets. The mower had a slight crumpled
corner from getting backed into a tree by and operator with a stiff. non
rotating, neck. Craftsmen would have removed the hood and dinged, pinged
and hammered the dent out perfectly then repainted the entire hood. We
opted for filler and touch up paint. To be continued.f

The cat took Alan on an obedience training walk down the driveway and back.
He is a quick learner.
