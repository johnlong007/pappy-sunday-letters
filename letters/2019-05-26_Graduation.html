May 26, 2019 · Sunday

Graduation

14 photographs·372 words

Once upon a time a long, long time ago in a far away place:

Monday we took the dog to the beauty parlor. No haircut this time as I want
his whiskers and mane to grow long again so he will look like Max.

B & L came over to start the serious ramp season. People who go to the ramp
festival just do not know what a real ramp meal is like,

Everyone knows how much I appreciate a willing worker.

Easy to see the difference after a motivational and inspirational pep talk.
I did not find anyone interested in a free pine log so decided to convert
it into firewood. Did get it split using hammer and wedges. I sure do miss
the wood splitters.

Is this some sort of throw back to an earlier age?

We went to Union City to visit my little sister. She is doing great. Much
more mobile than I expected and said knee is pain free. Her concern is
tripping over her little dog, I am sure a serious concern. I have no good
solution to that problem. Sort of like our marble steps.

Various family members have been graduating from various institutions this
month. Steven from TTU and Charlotte from DB. Wayne and Lisa invited
friends and family in for an event.

Speaking of graduations, Sarah has sprouted wings, taken flight and landed
on Reservoir road. Adam has not quite been able to solo but we expect it
will not be long before his big event.

The 63 runs good but I decided we needed to check timing and dwell. All
were right on the mark.

Back to the event, I knew Gwendolyn was in a far away place with a strange
sounding name so I sent this picture to her.

Looking pretty good so far.

Guess what my mother would say upon pinching one of this child's cheeks.

Of course, in the midst of all the onward and upward event, trauma can and
does occur.But, when it does, we deal with it, right Benj?
