June 30, 2013 · Sunday

Philippines

14 photographs·161 words

Going to the Philippines has been on my to do list for years. A couple with
whom Linda goes to church are natives and told Linda I could go along when
they traveled home on vacation.

I did.

This will be a real short report. Full story to any interested parties.

Belinda and Jeremy are medical Drs. Each of their families are medical Drs.
Mostly ethnic Chinese. and VERY wealthy.

Belinda lined up a car and driver for me (I paid for it). I stayed some
with Jermies family in Manila and some with her Mom in northern Luzon (site
of the Villa Verde Trail).

I could not manage hiking the entire trail but did each end and did some
other tourist things more than meeting my original objectives.

They left Saturday to go to a small island for a big family reunion. Since
I had fulfilled my mission and did not want to crash their party, I headed
for home.,
