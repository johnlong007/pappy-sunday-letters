September 25, 2016 · Sunday

Fallen

6 photographs·464 words

Last weekend we drove to Union City to visit my Lil' Sister and it went
very well. GSH did not get too fidgity nor complain too much. That put me
to thinking maybe we could do the circumnavigating the US for which I
bought the VW.
But, I take the animals seriously. We can get away a day or two by getting
Anita/Joe and or Wayne to come by but I don't like to go off much more than
that.
We need a resident pet keeper, someone(s) who can live here for 1-2-3 weeks
while we go on tour. Rewarded by love and money in some portion.

Ponder that.

For and away the high point of the week was getting the little Oliver
running. I did fudge a bit, I hooked it to the 12v booster and gave it a
shot of either. Started up, ran great (FYI John). I took it down to the
brush pond with the intention of smashing the brush. Drove out on the
boards, got cold feet and retraced my route to the house. Anyone else want
to try it?

Next best high point was the community band playing at the exchange place.
The band president (Joe) did not go due to duties as the Patriarch of the
Long family but GSH played well and looked good.

After we got back home we went to Blountville to check in on Civil war days
event. It was held in the park behind the old court house. John Steele,
Anita, JoAnn Steele, GSH and I recovered that area from an abandoned
wilderness a few years back. I came home and hunted in my pictures for some
before pictures but no success with my filing system To be continued.
Anyway, it is with a lot of satisfaction I can see the area now.

We did have a work day on the AT. It was back on top of Roan Mountain. A
few years back we worked for months improving the steep downhill section
from Cloudland toward Hughes Gap. We installed a lot of steps and
switchbacks. With time, hikers had taken shortcuts around the switchbacks,
bypassed the steps and packed the landing below the first step such that
first step was too high. We worked on all those issues. As usual, I mostly
busted rocks,

Most of the time not spent on tractors was spent mowing. I set the deck
quite low in hopes of stopping the invasive weed from going to seed and
pretty much went over the whole place. I think that will do it for this
year.

The big frustration for the week was trying to get the IH crawler running.
Somehow it is not getting fuel. To be continued.
