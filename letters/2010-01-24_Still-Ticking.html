January 24, 2010 · Sunday

Still Ticking

9 photographs·221 words

Progress has continued on the car project. I finished undercoating and
fabricated and installed fender liners.This week, GSH decided to undertake
upholstering the seats and to lay out the holes in the new instrument panel.
She spent a lot of time working on the sewing machine before we gave up and
took it to a repairman for adjusting.

Tuesday AT work was a tough day. I carried a big chain saw from the river
at Chestoa to Temple Hill, 4 miles all uphill. Cut 28 blowdowns.Downhill
back but that makes the toes hurt. Good views of Erwin and Unake Springs.
Views of Herb's house but blocked by trees and bushes.

I am sure all are waiting with baited breath to see the instrument panel.
How about work in progress pictures?

The Friday hike was in the Laurel Fork gorge to the Falls.

On the homefront, work continues on clearing the wild cherry trees in the
horse pasture.

A friend, David Quillen has purchased property on the river near Damascus.
Sunday PM I went with him to look the place over. He has a road in and has
started work toward building a cabin. The property is just across the river
from the Va. Creeper Trail. The trail crosses the bridge shown in this
picture.

All in all, a pretty fun week.
