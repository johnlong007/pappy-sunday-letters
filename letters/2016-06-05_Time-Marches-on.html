June 5, 2016 · Sunday

Time Marches on

11 photographs·349 words

Monday was Decoration Day. We went to Erwin to check the status of the
Sandy Bottom cemetery where my maternal GGF is buried and the Shallow Ford
one where my GGM is buried.
Did not get in to Sandy bottom as the hay had not been cut and I did not
want to walk through it. (Daddy would not approve) I will go back as soon
as the hay is put up.
Did visit the Tinker cemetery in Shallow Ford where his wife, my GGM is
buried. While there, I took pictures of all the markers which I intend to
move onto the persons page in my Family Tree program as I am able to make
the connections. My intent is to go to see Flo soon and get as much help
from her as I can.

All along through the week I worked on the '63 front end. painted all the
parts which can be viewed but were best painted before assembly. I hope to
get the car so it can be pained with all the parts already installed.

It is time to order carpet and the cushions for the seats (which GSH is
recovering). Have not even thought about the top yet and have not tried the
repaired top cylinders. Stay tuned.

AT work was back at Cross Mountain. I worked with Mark Dibble attaching the
top strand od Barbed wire. I think the fence project is finished.

We went to Erwin to pick up some strawberry plants Lisa had ordered for me
and to lay off some more rows for her garden.

Back home ripping out the old s'berry patch and planting new plants was hot
work. Got it done just before the rain hit. The "new" patch planted last
year should be bearing next year.

Since this was a special June 5th we went to Wendy's rather than BK. I had
a difficult time moving picture from incredible device to the pc. It may
not turn out.

It was fun seeing everyone. Do appreciate the updates and notes which we
get from time to time.
