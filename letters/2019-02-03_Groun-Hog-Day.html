February 3, 2019 · Sunday

Groun Hog Day

11 photographs·333 words

No self respecting local would ever say "Ground Hog". A dead give away that
"you are not from around here are you."

Josiah's other grandparents came back from Utah. They reported the child
has already opened its eyes which from my observation puts it way ahead of
Beth's at the same point.

I hesitate to say it was cold here this week. But it was. I suppose some
members of the frozen North contingent might scoff but I still think it was
cold. It kept us off the track one day. But only one day.

Notice the flag.

For the first few days GSH worried about Max going out without being
watched. She is well past that now.

We stayed indoors and worked on the sewing machine quite a bit. Most of the
green paint has been removed. At least from the easy places.

Some repair work underway. It is missing several screws and some metal
parts from frame. I have not really inspected the machine yet.

The barn work resulted in more brush being created to move to the brush
pond.

The downed tree on our access road between neighbors was bugging me so I
decided to retrieve it. That was a mistake, log was big and heavy, ground
wet and soft. I got the log back here but dug a furrow in doing the job.

But, while out on the tractor I offered Max a ride, He liked it as best I
can tell and after that came when I called him to offer another ride.

Beth took pity on my whimpering last week and sent Jake out to train a
replacement log splitter. He has good form but no self respecting ax man
wears gloves.

The deer seem to be bunching up. When we come in we often spot a dozen of
so out beside the house.
