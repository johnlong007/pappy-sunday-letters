Old Dog Tricks

14 photographs·400 words

I had occasion to be around several quite young people this week. I also
had occasion to try dealing with some computer issues this week. I believe
starting to learn about computers at age 4 would have worked better than
age 84.

Last couple of weeks several of my dispatched weekly notes have bounced,
too big. Turns out I have been switching more away from my camera and
taking more pictures with the incredible device. It has a "re size" mode
which LLLL had shown me. Sure enough I had been resizing the pictures.
However it also turns out that unless one takes care when downloading the
pictures it moves the full sized ones. Tonight, we will see if trick has
been mastered.

And contrast

Weather was sorta difficult all week leading to a lot of indoor work on
the '37. I had progressed up passenger side and stopped at the grill
waiting for more, and better, help.

Went back and started up the drivers side.

Found the door needs a lot more work so spent a couple days on it. Front
fender also needs a lot of work. The blue primer really accents my poor
workmanship.

Rain predicted for Monday and Tuesday this week so may make progress.

I piled up the remaining brush with a single chain around it.

However, the chain links were so large my noose did not tighten when tried
to lift with tractor boom. Chain slipped off. Back to drawing board.

Charlie came by on Thursday. Just in time to have a go at the Oak root ball.

Root ball is ahead 1 to nothing.

While here, he also more or less offered to work on Miata light switch.

To be continued.

Of course, the big event was Anita's birthday. A fine event . I do not
normally take pictures at other persons events but for this occasion made
an exception.

For one reason or another, we stayed pretty cooped up today. So, we
decided to go for an evening walk (No British mystery movie tonight) at the
park.

Lo and behold, met Katy and her son's grandparents. A delightful encounter
as I now have a picture to put into pictorial family file.
