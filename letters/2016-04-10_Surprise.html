April 10, 2016 · Sunday

Surprise !

11 photographs·459 words

We got a surprise visit from a little bundle from heaven(or somewhere)
Monday. Aaron with child.For details of what,where, when why and how he was
passing through I will refer you to his mother. Anyway, it was a delight to
see them and she is still in the "perfect" age slot.

Today, I made a run to Erwin to check on Lisa and life in the fast lane.
She is settling in it seems to me. She was out feeding the goats and
looking a lot like a farm hand. Perhaps she needs a milk cow?

Mostly the work week was as usual, AT one day at the same location. trail
repair work. Digging out roots and making small rocks out of big ones. This
week is going to be on Saturday with a class from ASU. I may leave that for
the still employed club members.

Wayne has ordered all the parts needed to repair the fire engine brakes.
Stay tuned. Dwight still has the drive train part. Can't leave the barn
without it.

Speaking of Dwight, He and son Pat have been building a big block chevy
engine for a couple of years. Friday was the day for the shake down run on
the test stand. It seemed to go very well. They made 5-6 test runs 0-7,500
RPM measuring power, torque and many other parameters at about 100 RPM
intervals. As best I could tell the engine was producing over 1,200 HP. I
did not get a clear date as to when it would be in a car and ready for the
track. Stay posted.

GSH and helper dragged a lot of brush out of the woods to the brush pond
(in the absence of John and his willing worker crew). Somewhere along late
in the day I checked GSH's Fitbit to see if she had enough steps to
announce quitting time.

It seems I loosened the clip on her pocket as Fitbit was gone when she
headed for bed. We hunted, I hunted from 11-midnight. She hunted all the
next day. A little green fitbit in a big green field.

So I asked Laura to order replacement figuring I did not want GSH to miss a
step and if it should be found I would adopt the new one.

Then I got on line and read about how to find a lost Fitbit.

The animals had a good week.

Maria had her Junior recital Saturday. It was live streamed and I hope all
tuned in. It was great. I was a bit disappointed that neither John(guitar)
or Charlie (Mandolin) showed up on stage to aid and abet.

I have not heard much recently from students and workers scattered hither
and yon. I am still interested.
