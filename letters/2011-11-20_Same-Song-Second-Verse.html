November 20, 2011 · Sunday

Same Song, Second Verse

7 photographs·478 words

Several exciting things happened this week. Unfortunately, none happened
here. It was interesting getting survey reports on who actually read my
dispatches from home. I was a bit surprised and pleased.

For the non-events
1. No further progress on the car steering rack.
2. A bit of filling on the bench seat / table project.
3. I went with Dave to get the do-gooder A/C project finished but ordered
parts have not arrived.

For more significant progress:
We Harvested and split more firewood. I intend to keep the house warm this
Winter. The older I get the less I like cold.

A couple of things to note about this picture. First, I am sure the Summer
Camp wood splitters will appreciate how wood gets split when they are not
around. Second, the gloves. Since I injured my hand I have been mostly
having to wear gloves. I purchased three pair of heavy duty work gloves.
The pair shown in this picture is the last of the three pair. The little
dog had snabbled the other and gnawed at least one finger off. I have been
getting increasingly carefull about where I put the gloved down.
Unfortunately, Friday I left this last pair on a bench in the shop. Now,
they are also history.

The AT work day this week was on Saturday with a group of ASU students
going to do re locations at Pond Mountain. But, I went with Dwight to
Seymour Saturday.

Before that report, take a look at the new trophy !

Now, on to the report, hang on securely. Dwight and No. 1 son, Pat are
building a race car. No news there. They ordered a fiberglass body shell
and a load of bent to fit tubing made of some exotic metal. The tubing is
to make up the frame and cage, all the structure, of the car. Although it
is bent to fit, the ends come together in all sorts of angles so every end
must be shaped to fit then welded into place. The two structural engineers
spent the entire day Saturday fitting one piece of tubing.

After leaving Seymour, we made a dash to meet Charlie et al just West of
Knoxville.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, the community band was bringing in the
holiday season by presenting a program at the Kingsport mall.

Sunday, we made a run to Erwin to check on, the goats, The dog, and Herb.
Here they are:

An elderly friend from church died this week. He had a fairly rapid demise
into dementia and a fairly short stay in assisted living. His wife asked us
to help retrieve his belonging and return the items to their house. They
have three children all of whom helped in the moving effort.
