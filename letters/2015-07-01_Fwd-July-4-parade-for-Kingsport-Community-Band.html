From: Lick, Wendy Wilkinson
Date: Fri, Jun 28, 2013 at 4:21 PM
Subject: July 4 parade for Kingsport Community Band
To: Kingsport Community Band members

I will send another note out next week with our float number and
approximate location in the parade lineup. Meanwhile, here is the rest of
what you need to know about the parade:

At 9:00 AM July 4, we will meet at the Kingsport Town Center (formerly
known as the Fort Henry Mall), in the outside parking lot near Belk. This
is the parking lot alongside Memorial Boulevard, close to the intersection
with Fort Henry Drive, across from Walgreen's. We will carpool over to the
vicinity of the Renaissance Center by loading into a few of the larger
vehicles. The drivers will drop off their passengers as near the float as
practical and entrust their instruments and chairs to them (if desired)
then go seek parking. There should be some parking spaces within a few
blocks at that time of day. If you encounter a friendly officer, he may
let you park in a restricted area if you say you are with the band,
especially if you also can say you have passengers with limited mobility or
other health issues. If you encounter a less friendly officer, you may
have to park and walk back, so be prepared.

We probably have the same float as last year, which is a flat bed with no
benches. Please bring your own chair or stool - lightweight in case you
have to carry it a ways to get to the float. Some agile band members
prefer to sit cross-legged on the flat bed. You will not be permitted to
dangle your feet over the edge of the float or stand up, so your choices
are to sit cross-legged or bring a chair. Band members with large, heavy
instruments also might want to bring something to rest your instrument on
during the parade.

WHAT TO BRING:
* your instrument (leave your instrument case in the car)
* your music (the Washington Post March)
* flip-folder with lyre if appropriate for your instrument - there
will be no stands but sometimes flute players tape their music onto another
person's back.
* a hat to protect you from the sun
* sunglasses
* sunscreen
* plenty of water (!!!)
* smallish folding chair
* instrument rest if needed

The parade starts at 10 AM. We'll be sitting around waiting in the sun for
some time, then there's the parade itself, so you really do need to take
care of yourself. Heatstroke could ruin your whole holiday. Again, bring
plenty of water to drink, and wear a hat with a brim!

Dress comfortably. Shorts are fine. Red, white, and blue (or any
combination thereof) would be ideal.

The parade ends at Memorial Park near Dobyns-Bennett. Our float will
continue on and drop us off at our waiting cars at the mall parking lot. A
few of us will stay to take our intrepid carpool drivers back to their cars.

We will have a guest drummer, who will play during the march and will fill
in with a cadence during the times we're not playing. We also will have
guest tuba and clarinet players.

Traditionally we take up some money to tip the driver of the float. We all
appreciate a smooth ride! If you want to pitch in, bring a little cash.
Totally optional.

Wendy Lick
Secretary
Kingsport Community Band
wlick@eastman.com
