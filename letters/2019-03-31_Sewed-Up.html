March 31, 2019 · Sunday

Sewed Up

11 photographs·471 words

Lil Sister created a stir this week. Fortunately it was all a false alarm
and she is back on the road to recovery. Just checked with Martha who
reported that returning home id in the near future.

No work on the '63 this week but I did stop by and activate the liability
insurance. Tag and insurance, now time to get an exhaust system installed.
Maybe this week.

Those who know GSH well can have a caption contest.

Last Fall we had a locust tree fall. I sawed it into firewood size and
Monday we loaded on tractor and took down to drive to stack up for next
Winter.
The two helpers follow GSH wherever she goes while outside. When looking
for a dog I wanted small and high energy. May have overshot a bit.

With more to come.

Work continued on clean up in the Ag building. Borrowed a gas powered leaf
(dust) blower from Wayne.

Probably two days work left . Herb had about 5 times the space and at least
5 times the "stuff". We were over to see Lisa at which time I offered to
rent GSH to help her clean her storage areas.

I have a fair amount of old, horse drawn items. How many of these items can
you identify? By name an function? I brought some more down from the
smokehouse. The plan is hang on outside of ag bldg. Stay tuned.

n

For the first time in a spell, I went out for AT work. We all rode up the
forest service road to Doll Flats in Carl's truck. He sent 1/2 the crew up
the trail toward Hump Mountain. Tim, Kevin and I worked our way out to 19E
where vehicles were parked. We cleared water bars, cut blowdowns, trimmed
Laurel and renovated a couple of sections.

Nephew James and his entourage came in to visit his and her family Friday.
We went over Saturday. The kids sure do grow up fast.
I did suggest to Lisa she could bring all the kids in for a week this
Summer and get a lot of things done.

No real sign of ramps anywhere I looked along the AT at high elevation.
Mine are doing well. It seems clear they are spreading as I only planted
buds in a row, In another 2 or 3 years we may be able to harvest some.

Most exciting of all, expert seamstresses lept into action to check out the
sewing machine for sewing function. Moderate success, it sews but needs
more adjustment I am told.
I was encouraged enough to ask Bob to start thinking about what kind of
finishing should be accomplished on the cabinet.
