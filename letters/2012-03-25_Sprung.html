March 25, 2012 · Sunday

Sprung!

7 photographs·310 words

Redbuds are in full bloom, Cheeries in full bloom, peaches likewise. Even
thought it is a bit early 1/2 row of potatoes is now planted in upper
garden. We shall see.

The bluebirds of happiness have set up housekeeping. I think there is a
family in the other house also but have not checked it closely.

Do-gooder work was not real rewarding. A family from church is moving to
Nashville. They have their house here on the market and wanted some touch
up work done. We made two trips out (Ridgefields) but it appears to me to
need a lot more than touch up.

At home, work continued from time to time on the '37

Work also continued on the table and benches. These projects may do in the
two of us.

AT work was near the Beauty Spot this week. We were renovating old trail.
The trail tends to sluff off on the uphill side causing hikers to walk
along the lower edge. About one dozen willing workers renovated 350 feet of
trail.

Oh, the club annual dinner meeting was held this week.

One morning Anita asked that I cut down her aged apple tree. That task done
we moved on to loading timber in her truck, er van. I believe this is all
part of the nupital event preparation.

The SPA owners have decided to expand their facilities. I suppose to take
in more and perhaps larger patrons. Various helping hands were called in
for the project.

Work on the project was called because of rain before completion. However,
the heavy duty part has been completed.
Now I remember why I punted on gmail with pictures. I am giving up!
