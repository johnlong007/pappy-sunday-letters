September 27, 2015 · Sunday

At Last

11 photographs·370 words

As some know , and some may even care, The Studebaker/Chrysler has been on
my list for a very long time.. In 1953 or '54 I read in Hot Rod magazine
about the combination which at run on the salt flat.
I purchased the car in '66 and the engine(with a '57 Chrysler new yorker
wrapped around it) the next year.

I cautiously think it is ready to take out on the road at last. Today I
took it off the jack stands and drove up and down the drive going through
all the gears. Now, to get tags and insurance and go for a few longer trial
runs.

Herb has had a middling rough week. We went in for his follow up MRI from
the work on his right cheek/eye. The MRI revealed that he does have a mass
under the skin flap.Follow up plan has not been determined. In addition, he
had a recurrence of the seizure causing all sorts of local excitement. Not
sure what follow up that will entail.

No pictures of either of car or brother.

It is time to start thinking about getting the ole home place in shape for
Winter. Toward that end, I mowed the soccer field and the surrounding area.
I sure do like my mower. Also, I am getting better at operating it.

GSH and I spent a lot of time and effort splitting wood this week. Probably
have enough now but it is mostly green and should be held over until next
year. I will keep trying to find a standing dead tree to harvest.

For AT work, we went to Carvers gap to renovate trail and to install a
"Kiosk". A section of trail we had built some years back was getting
overgrown from both sides. We whipped it back into shape. This time, I
worked hard all day. On the way out, I took a picture of the "kiosk".

After work, The Forest Service people fed us. I took a sack of peppers
which lit up several peoples lives.

Saturday morning the community band did a concert at Exchange place.

Sunday evening, after church we gleaned apples from the church property
apple trees. Waste not, want not.
