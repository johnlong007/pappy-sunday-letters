February 10, 2013 · Sunday

All Is Well

10 photographs·458 words

A while back GSH and I met with a professional person to look at our
financial situation. These folks have some sort of format they go
through I suppose to be in compliance with Dodd-Frank or something.
Anyway, she asked "what do you really enjoy doing most of all".
Probably looking for travel or the like.
I said "working with one of our children or grandchildren". GSH said
she liked "having unexpected free time when she could do whatever she
wanted"

This week, it worked out for each of us !

Friday, Herb and I loaded a goat (Momma goat I think) into a cage in
the car and i delivered it to the lonesome goat in Dechard. The kids
put it and no longer alone goat into Dale's perfect. palatial goat
pen. When I left Saturday evening the two seemed to be getting along
very well. I did suggest that it might be well to borrow a moderate
size billy goat for a brief spell.

As I pulled into the parking space at Colson residence Friday evening,
Meredith pulled in beside me.

Saturday morning, The entire Colson family plus me and Meredith ran in
the 5K race put on by their church youth group. Linda posted a picture
on Facebook after the race.

Saturday afternoon, Sarah and I burned brush, logs and stumps, Dale
had CCP training and then Meredith and Linda used the tractor to move
stumps. Clearly an practically perfect day.

In the meantime (back at the ranch), Various talented grandchildren
were performing in various musical groups in Knoxville. GSH had passed
on the unique opportunity to run the 5K race (prepaid by Linda I might
add) so she could attend the various concerts. But! logistics became
too complicated for her and behold ! She had her unplanned free day.

Actually, most of my week was spent working on the '37 pickup
interior. One will note the urge to make do and get by remains totally
ingrained into me, to the core and perhaps beyond.

AT work continued on the relocation at Chestoa but since it was the
same crew and the same place I did not include ant pictures. The
section is convenient for anyone in the area to come check out. It
starts about 100 feet from a parking area. Actual work covers almost
all the things we do, so it might be interesting to walk it. We will
be there agin Tuesday this week, drop in to observe.

Later in the week (to avoid saying on Sunday) CCP training continued
here for GSH and Wayne. I believe all are going to handle it ok. It
will be my turn to practice the first pretty day this week. (Aaron can
critique their form).
