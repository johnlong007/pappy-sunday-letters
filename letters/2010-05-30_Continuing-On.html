May 30, 2010 · Sunday

Continuing On

11 photographs·323 words

A breakthrough. Larry Miller has been miffed at me for a couple of years
for unknown reasons. After a bit of effort on my part and a lot on the part
of GSH it appears he has decided to forgive me. So. Monday he came over
bringing two new Stihl chain saws for me to test run. I did. No pictures.

Tuesday we went back to Hughes Gap . When we were there the week before a
long stretch of steep trail was very muddy and difficult. This week, we put
locust log side rails and steps to rebuild those sections. I cut a 16"
locust, made it fall exactly where I intended and did not get injured.

The garden(s) are coming right along. In addition to the one which is
planted out front, I plowed the other two sites. If I can get motivated, I will
plant one to weed during camp and save the other to plant.

While waiting for the Studebaker, I have been piddling with the dead
Honda. This week, Dwight switched out the ignition system . Clearly creating a
silk purse.

The Miata came home! But, it had a loud noise. A bit of detective work led
to the speedometer. A local mechanic offered to extract, lubricate and
replace. We expect that to be a temporary fix.

Look but don't touch.
Saturday night it was off to Knoxville for further testing of the drag car.


It was NOT a rewarding evening. I think five runs, none without problems.
Finally got a 5.3 sec run at about 130 MPH (1/8 mile). I think a new engine
and rebuilt transmission along with new instrumentation will be the next
order of business.
For many years, my little sister and some of her friends have visited
Gatlinburg to attend Gaither concerts. Today, we went down to have lunch with
them. A fun visit.

When we got home, we found we had intruders.
