Studebaker !

Two steps back.

Dwight is at a stalemate on the steering rack. Turns out mixing and
matching parts does not always go as planned much less hoped.

I had procured a couple of pieces of grille script from Tex. One for the
right grille bar "Studebaker" and one for the left "Commander". I did not
have proper fasteners to go on the pins to attach the script so I attempted
to fabricate from a convenient sows ear. It did not work and the
"Commander" is missing. I searched the drive and the turn arounds at the
end of the drive to no avail. A replacement part is available for $50. I am
pondering.

I also noticed while standing behind the car that one side appears lower
than the other. Sure enough, it is lower , the left rear spring is bowed
upward rather than correct downward bow.
