November 15, 2015 · Sunday

Broke Down

12 photographs·370 words

The truck has been getting difficult to get running. Two new batteries and
it cranked like they were both dead. So, I watched a you tube video then
took the starter out and cleaned the solenoid contacts. Did not make a bit
of difference. Next, Bob ordered a new starter. That did the trick. Taking
the starter out and putting it back in certainly goes faster on the forth
or fifth time around.

The VW has been shimmying badly during braking. I replaced the rotors
twice. Finally took it to Bob's repair shop. They said rotors are warped.
So, dug the originals out of the scrap box, got them turned and shimmy went
away. But, the car is still making a noise from that wheal. Looks like
wheel bearing must be replaced--again.

Just when the truck went back on the road, the Miata started running very
rough and gave a check engine light.The code reader listed "number three
cylinder misfire" Internet help said 1999 and 2000 models have problems
with plugs, plug wires and coil. Christopher had changed plug wires and
supplied a set of plugs which I installed today. No change. Back on the
case tomorrow.

The Studebaker has been delivered to an upholstery shop. Owner is preparing
a cost estimate. He is also going to get the re chroming people to come
look and prepare an estimate.He said he will have estimates tomorrow.
Anyone care to guess?

Wayne came by so we could start the '53. No luck. It has an electrical
problem, maybe a fuel pump. I want to get Dwight over here to advise me on
a couple of issues as soon as he is available. I did get Wayne to do 1/2
the chimney sweep job while he was here.

I did not go to do AT work this week. The TEHCC dinner was Saturday, ASU
students were planning to come work on the trail Saturday but Carl moved
their day to Sunday. So, I went to dinner but not work day .

Did go to Erwin to help Herb a bit with his wood working project. Anyone
need some thick and thin wormy wood? White pine and some of that really
fast growing stuff.
