November 29, 2020 · Sunday

Thanksgiving

10 photographs·381 words

Family and friends.

As promised I went to the cardiologist this week. He was one hour late for
my appointment . Spent less than 5 minutes with me. Said it is time to get
on with the valve replacement. I asked if they do it at the drive by
window. He said if it goes well there will only be one night stay.

Scheduled a "consultation" with the plumber for one day next week.

BP was 170 after making me wait an hour.Dr. increased my BP pills without
a clue as to what drove it up.

The virus has taken on a life of its own. Fewer folks at Perkins this
morning.

Fewer at church.

Meredith invited us over for Thanksgiving dinner. A fine event. I do not
usually take pictures at other people's events but made an exception. We
got pictures from several other events and really appreciated those.

The Jack children were all in town along with various escorts. Benj and
Christina stopped for a bit. I believe we may have found a person who
appreciates the '37.

One day we went out to help PBBD do some woodpecker damage control. Being
well aware of a time long ago when I sent her up a tree, I was in full
safety mode while holding the ladder.

Turns out , my best is not very good so I got replaced and assigned as a
photographer.

I have mentioned Farmer Lisa's tales of woe regarding her pine trees too
near the Little ole widow's house. Saturday I was able to recruit a willing
worker to go help me lock the barn door.

The trees are down and the crew has been dispersed.

Wayne drove the '37 last week and reported it had no brakes even though it
had brake pedal. I pulled two wheels and the problem became obvious. The
wheel cylinders pistons were frozen in place.
Chrysler products were always advanced in terms of Engineering. In order to
compensate for weight transfer and wheel rotation, the brake shoes and
brake wheel cylinders are asymmetric
In practice that means wheel cylinders cost $70 each while the other brands
are about $15.
