September 25, 2022 · Sunday

Fall !

14 photographs·397 words

On my face.

Dropped my expensive glasses and stepped on them, smashed.
One hearing aid has quit working.
The Car has developed a running gear noise.
And, my boomerang won't come back.

Last week , I left you with the Alfa on jacks, no wheels. It now has 5 !
new tires , four on the floor and one in the trunk. Got it running ,sounded
really good and drove it back into the storage bldg.
PBBD is getting really good at breaking down and/or mounting tires on
wheels.

The 55 is also running well now. Wayne drove it around the place. Next
comes trying to get the power windows working. Is there an electrical
engineer in the family?

Note exhaust. I took the bumper off to get rechromed; prepared for about
$600. Came back estimated at 1K. More than I could swallow. Will now put it
back on as is.

The Rogue acquired a dent some time back. Wayne has procured a vacuum
powered dent puller and was able to improve quite a bit. Then he started
looking and found a dent in the front nose cone. Always something. He came
up to check the noise in the car drivetrain. Thinks likely a CV joint.
Maybe worse.

A & C finished the metal carport except for edge trim. Alan is really adept
at getting GSH to take her evening pills. I am grateful.

I moved the truck in just to check fit and to make it easy to access the
edge trim for installation.

I gave the front field a close trimming. After the clipping dried we
gathered to use it in the retaining basin.

Making hay while the sun shines.

Added more seed and fertilizer under the clippings.

There is a hearing aid commercial in which the new owner proclaims he can
hear the grass growing. I was looking forward to that until my hearing aid
quit ! Drat !

Now ready to pull the engine.

"Climb every mountain, scale ever peak"

This is our current mountain climbing ! Picture made today. Compare and
contrast.

Katy said enter this picture in the add a caption contest.

Tigger stayed outside the night before I took this one.
