June 29, 2010 · Tuesday

Day (or two) Late

14 photographs·247 words

What a week. Getting the lower garden ready for Summer Camp harvest
involves intense effort. We had new potatoes and peas one night along with green
beans. Corn is almost ready. I hope Missy keeps the coons away.

Tuesday was AT day as usual. Carl sent a whole bunch of willing workers to
install a couple of new markers. The site was in from Damascus 3 1/2 miles
.Being obvious we had too many people I cleared water bars and installed
drain ditches.No pictures of that.

For my delayed do-gooder work, we took Kim to A site about 150 miles North
in VA to begin a solo 6 day hike.

For Dave's delayed do-gooder, he came over and helped me drag out the
downed Maple tree.

Laura has a friend with a peach tree. If someone would take care of it,
Peaches would be big and delicious. As it is, they are small and delicious.

Saturday "we" went to visit the frozen North. Upon our arrival, Rachel came
running out (followed by Janet and Cliff), shrieking and with open arms.
The first time I have ever received such a reception. Then she ran right by
me to greet Kenneth.

The trip was great. Picture captions upon request (directed to the parties
pictured).

I have received NO comments on the draft Summer Camp Schedule. I take that
to meant it is perfect.

We have red raspberries ripe for picking. They are being offered for
picking on the halves.
