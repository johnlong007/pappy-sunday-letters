February 9, 2014 · Sunday

Days of our Lives

7 photographs·601 words

As I remember, that was the name of a soap opera Momma listened to when I
was an only child. Another was "Just plain Bill". That does not fit for a
descriptor of my week.

Good news ? I heard directly and or indirectly from family members as far
North as Madison, Wisconsin, as far East as Norfolk Virginia, as far South
as Pensacola, Florida and as far West as College Station, Texas; and in
various other places .

This Spring or for sure this year (good Lord willing and creeks don't
rise), I intend to make a circuit and visit all these people/places. Maybe
more.

I got severly repremanded last week for not including a cat house report. I
suppose everyone knows that if the dogs are outside and someone comes up
the drive the dogs come running to greet and hitch a ride. We do not go off
and leave the dogs outside. But, the cat has learned to come to the end of
the ramp and wait for GSH to get out of the car when we come in after dark.
Today, he was there waiting when we came home from church.

Carolyn Stonecipher is Ron's widow. Monday she had a very serious single
car accident. Her description sounded like she dropped right wheel off
pavement and overcorrected. Rolled the car and hit a tree with her door. We
have visited her and stopped in again today on the way home from church.
She expects to be released to a rehab facility tomorrow.

The parts van was terribly dirty and contained a load of junk. So, I
emptied the junk and washed inside and out.

The body is in very good condition. I decided to procure the vehicle for a
few select parts, window latches and gas tank (Anyone besides Wayne
remember what happened to the LBT fuel tank?) It is so sound I do not
believe I will send to the crusher. Charlie, Cliff and Christopher (who
else was in on that plan) may decide to take both vans on their Alaska
safari.

Working on the LBT brakes has been a comedy of errors.Front wheel
cylinders, rear wheel cylinders, Master cylinders flexible lines and fluid
tubing. Each component has been at least two trips to parts store with
five for rear whell cylinders and still had to settle for kits.

The LBT has self adjusting, drum brakes. All the components will be new
except the shoes which look good for another 10K miles.

All the wood stacked out fron here has been consumed. Saturday we went up
on the hill and foraged a bit.

A keen observer may note the saw looks famaliar. It is the same one which
got run over in the dark of the night not too long back. Wayne did a R & R
job. Runs fine, saws fine.

Most know the dog likes to play soccer. He will play by hiself but prefers
and opponent. Anyway, he frequently will send the ball over the hill and
down through the field. GSH has been building and ever increasing hight
fence to contain the soccer ball.

Once more, no AT work this past week. One of our workers ,Bob Peoples has
colon cancer and will have surgery this coming Thursday, several who have
gone out with the AT work group have met him.

Herb is right in the middle of his radiation treatment on his tumor.
