My Evening Sun

10 photographs·277 words

Might as well make the most visit to CC.

We talked to UVA Dr. He has stopped Chemo infusions. A bone marrow sampling
is scheduled in mid. Ja. After results are studied a plan will be
developed. Stay tuned.

The call for a strong, willing worker Did the trick, the scoop is now on
display.

The high point of the week, after they finished they got me by each arm and
moved me up the hill to the driveway. Made me think of the way we would
sometimes do Patricia. Pick her up and tell her to move legs as though
walking. Wayne will remember.

To ensure we never run out of things to do , we looked around and brought
in the next project.

A Sears buzz saw, model 700-140. It is not complete.

An Online picture of a complete unit.

I am afraid it may be too much metal working for us. The architect and
engineers are pondering .

The "37 pt50 has new tires mounted on old wheels.

We have a bunch of old tractors. If the tires are allowed to go flat the
tire will be ruined. Diana ordered a device to inflate the tires. It is
great. Foolproof !

Meredith likes to liven my days.

I told her why I never call a small child by their given name but wait
until they become a little person, in the meantime i find something
descriptive. (Alan was " turnip" termite).

I think this one is octopus arms.

Never a dull moment !
