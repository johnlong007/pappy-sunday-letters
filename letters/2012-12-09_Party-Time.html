December 9, 2012 · Sunday

Party Time

13 photographs·666 words

I believe Larry Kidd is going to survive and maybe be OK. When I went
in to see him it was obvious he recognized me. He smiled and turned
his head toward me. When I asked if I could take his picture he nodded
yes. When I asked who is going to drive his tractor in the Blountville
Christmas parade, he mouthed "I don't know".

A PUD is a "pointless up and down" in AT vernacular. The AT commission
has a target of no more than 10% grade where possible so as time
permits we do re locations to circle around the side of hills and skip
PUDs. It takes a long time to go through the process to get approval
to do a relocation and a surprising amount of taxpayer money. Anyway,
this week we worked on roughing out three re locations near Tiger
Creek. Some may remember that Tiger Creek is a favorite spot for ramp
harvesting. Still a bit early for that. Anyway, I worked with three
other people, Two used loppers to cut everything they could cut, I
operated the chainsaw and one helped me move the logs I sawed out of
the way. I ran out four tanks of fuel before both the saw and I ran
out of fuel. Actual trail digging will occur at some future date. We
did notice one interested observer.

The community band played at Baysmount. I went along because I wanted
to hear them play and because they had been invited to tour Bruce and
Mary Cuningham's house after the concert. Mary is a retired community
band performer and Bruce is a retired AT maintainer. Bruce worker at
Kodak. He got an MBA from Harvard back in the 50s. Kodak sent him to
Kingsport to be comptroller at TEC. Both Bruce and Mary have been very
active in all sorts of things for many years.
Bruce has a desk which he says came with his family to Boston before
the Pilgrams made their appearance.

After the concert and the tour, we stopped at Shoney's to take
advantage of their free hot fudge cake offer!

Friday, I went with the Friday Hikers to Steele Creek Park near
Bristol. It was a fine day and a fine hike. If anyone is interested,
let me know and I will forward the hike leaders report. I did see one
unusual animal.

Two of the Friday hikers are Jerry and Cathy Case. Cathy came from the
hills of NC just across the mountains from where I grew up. We share a
lot of words, values and customs. Anyway, in her house, I spotted a
butter dish just like the one I have which my mother claimed from the
trash after my GGM broke up housekeeping about 1940. However, Cathy's
has a bail, mine is missing. I need everyone to be alert for a bail
to match the one pictured.

As previously noted, Saturday was the Blountville Christmas parade. I
am proud to announce it is still a Christmas parade and they still
toss candy to the spectatators. Less proud to announce that in spite
of looking pathetic not a parader threw any candy to me ! The parade
was great, all sorts of parade things, Politicians, fire engines, boy
scouts, church groups floats, old cars, old tractors, old people,
horses with riders. I was surprised and delighted to see one of the
Long boys driving a shiny red restored Farmall tractor pulling the
Ruritan float, I saw the Sr. mister Long today and told him how
pleased I was they had finally decided to polish up one of their old
Farmalls.

I still have not checked out my electrical work on the '37. I am
waiting for someone to come by to aid and abet. In the meantime, I
keep finding things to do .

I also found things to do outside getting ready for winter.Notice how
good the painted retaining wall looks !

Anyone interested in a fixer upper?
