September 29, 2019 · Sunday

Mixed Bag

12 photographs·363 words

The old folks at home to like to be remembered. Meredith had a birthday
last week and a birthday cake I am told. We missed both.
However; she came by and brought us a piece of cake. Keep that in mind.

Herb's Ford tractor continues to be a bother. It does start and run good
but I cannot get the carburetor to working properly. So, rather than go
with a kit, a new carburetor is on order. We shall see.

Likewise the '63 with new battery and rebuilt ($85) starter starts and runs
fine. But, smokes out driver side exhaust.

The next step is going to be replacing the valve stem seals. Anyone need
more information?

Thursday for the first time in quite a while, we went with the AT work
crew. TVA had cut a bunch of locust trees beside the Watauga Dam access
road. Carl had permission to collect the logs for use on the trail. I
offered our truck for transport. GSH went along as chief photographer.

Lunch time. Benj , Jonathan and Christopher, recognize any of the guys.

Wood was stored at the work center for future usage as side logs and steps.

Saturday we went to Indian Grave Gap for a short AT walk. Wayne was there
ahead of us with German exchange student teacher friends.

We went down drive about dusk. Saw 7 deer under the chestnut trees. Bob
Harvey was over during the day to harvest chestnuts, He must have missed
some.

Coming back the deer were in neighbors lawn. I stopped to observe. Decided
to back out of the road. Tried using the rear viewing camera and bopped
neighbors mailbox post. Just a thump but turns out the post was rotten. I
have read that all the safety devices are a distraction to older drivers. I
believe I can attest to that theory and have provided a bit of data.

Sunday evening all present and voting had an event to welcome Christopher
as he passes like a ship in the night.
