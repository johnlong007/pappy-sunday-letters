July 3, 2016 · Sunday

About Half

10 photographs·275 words

There are a number of things I did not get done this week.

No AT work.
No trip to Erwin
No work on the '63
No Dr. visits

On the other hand.

I did a lot of mowing. Push mower, riding mower, string trimmer, hand
scythe and bush hog. Since the rain is holding off the mowing should last a
few days before it starts over.

Fed and watered the new patch of strawberry plants. Patch is looking good

Also fed and watered GSH. Also looking good.

A large limb broke on one of the nut trees. Had to finish the job with the
tractor.

Finally decided the Winter Rye cover crop was about ripe and mowed it on
both garden patches.

For one dog, the big event of the week was a new herding ball. It is 15
inch. Probably should have stopped with 12 inch as he is having difficulty
rolling this one backward with his paws.

Friday we went to the Blountville annex to mow horse pasture. Clearly it
has been too long since it was mowed. I kept hitting rocks and stumps to
the point I had to quit. Going to have to do some repair work before
continuing.

The fire engine project hit another snag. Joe got the other side of the
gear box bearing housing pressed apart. But, I still cannot figure how the
thing is supposed to fit together. I need a competent Mech. Engr.

Saturday, I missed the family second amendment celebration to go to the 100
birthday celebration of a friend of a friend. A good event, glad I went
details upon request.Hate missing local event.
