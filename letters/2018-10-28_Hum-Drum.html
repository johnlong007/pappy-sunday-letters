October 28, 2018 · Sunday

Hum Drum

12 photographs·328 words

After much difficulty, the frozen drive shaft came loose, mower sharpened
and tried out. Works OK so next time I see neighbor I will pay him.

Skylights in pool room were very dirty so I scrubbed them. I keep thinking
about one of the talkative GOB's who announced that no one over 70 should
be on a roof.

Speaking of things a person over 70 should not be doing.

My father installed coal fired furnaces. He also made sure they were
cleaned on a regular basis. Guess who got to crawl around inside doing the
cleaning ! It all comes back to me.

For AT work we were at Roan Mountain moving the same gravels we have moved
many times before. The rains come up and the gravel go down. Off the trail
at our run offs and disperse into the laurel thickets. We then crawl around
with rakes and shovels to put as many as we can salvage back on the trail.
This time, Carl decided to try a new plan. We dug pits at the run offs.
Plan (hope) is the gravel will settle out and we can just shovel back onto
the trail. Some may want to go check it out after the next down pour.

Quitting time.

Newly cleaned fireplace. First fire of the season.

GSH misplaced her teeth bridge and was without for several weeks. It turned
up so we went to dentist Wed. to get it refitted. He adjusted it a bit
pushed into place as much as he dared and said try using it to see if it
will work the teeth back into place. It is causing her a lot of pain.

The parts car has been procured. A little bit late as it where.

Discussion underway as to swapping entire lid or just the glass.
