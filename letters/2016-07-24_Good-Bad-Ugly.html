July 24, 2016 · Sunday

Good. Bad. Ugly

10 photographs·212 words

What do you want to see first? How about ugly?

I do not have to make an appointment to see Dr. Sago. Just send her a note
and go in the next day. She grubbed four places, froze and fried 8-10 more
and threw in a few kind words. We talked a lot about Herb,

No fire engine work this week (Charlie came and went w/o being able to stop
by. Maybe next time. Maybe Christopher will fly in from California}.

Not only can GSH pick BB's, she can make a pie.

Wed. we went to Dechard to pick up David for Mini-Summer Camp.I took a fair
number of pictures and may put together, at least for his Mom. On the way
down we dropped Anita off at Beth's new abode. It is a lovely place and not
far off the beaten path (unless one misses the turn). It was a 15 hour, 650
mile day the way we did it but well worth the time and effort.

Just in case some have forgotten what goes on, a Summer Camp reminder.

David got up close and personal supervision.

It was hot today and loafing was enjoyed by all.

I got a report from Alf in Germany but not a word from Rachel.
