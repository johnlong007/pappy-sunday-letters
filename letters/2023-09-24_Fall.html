Fall !

10 photographs·248 words

for years every time i have any kind of medical check the first thing they
ask is if I have had any falls, Tomorrow I may tell them I'm working on it.

A bit of progress on the 55 this week, Wayne found a reason it would not
keep running Fuel filters at carbs had dissolved the rubber gaskets.

He also replaced the engine air cleaner. I do believe Nissan radios in a
lot of their test results.

No picture but Alan is working to take two fuel level transmitters and make
one which works, Stay tuned.

But, his real project this week was getting the crawler in operation

People came from far away to check on rogress. I offered Max a tractor of
his own but he declined.

This is a picture of the correct jack for a 55 chrysler. I have a serious
assortment of jack but cannot find a correct one. Spent time and effort
trying to no avail. Stay tuned.

Wwe finlay got the brush unloaded from the truck, Truck is getting very
little usage an cold wetaher comin on, so we put it on the battery charger
and brough both batteries to full charge.

Trees are loaded and chestnuts are falling. Deer, turkeys
and Gwendolyn love em. Deer and turkeys are trying to get fat.

How girls are taught to ride sidesaddle.
