September 13, 2009 · Sunday

Dipsy Doodle

9 photographs·303 words

Sunday night, two weeks back, I developed a sore throat. I am still trying
to shake off the remnants off the longest running cold I have experienced.

I did go on the AT work day. We are in the early stages of planning to
replace the shelter at Curly Maple Gap. It is located about 1/2 way between
Chestoa and Indian Grave Gap. We went in by going up Martin's Creek and
walking up old logging roads.It is going to be difficult transporting the
building materials to the the building site.

I suppose I should mention the Labor Day outing. A well fed time was had by
all.

The Studebaker is now in the shop.

My hope is to have it finished by June 1. Hope took a blow when I started
spreading the parts out and determined many critical parts are missing. I
stopped to see the guy who disassembled the car about six years back. He said
give him a list and he will hunt. I plan to go back tomorrow. But, because
my confidence is almost zero, I am starting to hunt for a parts car.


No week would be complete without a trip to the drag track. Pat is still in
the debugging mode. He made three runs Friday night. The good news is that
the car stability issues seem to have been eliminated. The bad news is
Dwight is having fits with data collection and retention.

Saturday, we went to a forest management class in Limestone cove. It was
good and probably helpful to us.(Plus we got a free lunch.)

Sunday we went to a concert in the park near Johnson City. If you look
close, you should be able to spot Joe on the back row (in the sunlight).It was
good and we got another free lunch.
