September 21, 2014 · Sunday

First Things

11 photographs·419 words

The trip to Duke with Herb went well. We left here early Wed. Got there on
time, the people were pleasant and helpful. But, there was no indication
from any of the persons we contacted that they had even glanced at all the
information which Dr. Sago had transmitted.
Nevertheless, The examining Dr. looked, pondered, discussed,took samples,
ordered blood samples and said he would call Herb soon to discuss results
and options.

Stay tuned.

Got the MM carburetor back from Dwight and started the tractor. No sign of
radiator leaks so far. Tractor runs and sounds really good but would only
run at full choke. Did not fiddle with carb adjustments. Waiting for
directions from Dwight. While it was running, I could not resist operating
the hydraulic control valve. A mistake. A hose ruptured and I got
sprayed.Next step will be remove the two old hoses and have new ones
fabricated. Only a wishful thinker would have even hoped a 60 year old
hydraulic hose would hold pressure.

A little progress on the LBT. The engine compartment is a metal box located
between the driver and passenger seats. In the beginning, the interior of
the compartment panels were insulated. After some looking we found some
material to try and installed on one panel. Looks good but not sure it will
hold up under heat load. Also got about 1/2 way through spraying on
undercoating.

Has everyone been invaded by stink bugs? We have, we have the little shop
vac outside the door and have to clean all around before entry. Anyone know
of an effective deterrent to keep them away?

AT work continued on the bridge replacement near Dennis Cove and my day
continued to be "bust rocks".

Does anyone remember the scooter they rode in when small? GSH gave it to us
when Alan was born. It is now being renovated to be like new. I went to
Charlotte car even,flea market Saturday and spotted one. One other
interesting thing was the seats at the racetrack. Event was at Charlotte
Motor Speedway. All the seats have been replaced with colored seats at
random placements. The word is that was done so during races empty seats
cannot be spotted when camera pans fans or race.

I only saw one car which I found interesting. A 1952 DeSoto with 3-speed
transmission (only $17K).

Charlie was in town Friday. He came by with a young lady who expressed and
interest in "shooting".

I wonder if anyone else had so much excitement?
