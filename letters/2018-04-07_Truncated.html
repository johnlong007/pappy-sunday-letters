April 7, 2018 · Saturday

Truncated

18 photographs·369 words

A day early but still a dollar short. We are going back to see Dr. Allison
tomorrow for Monday and Tuesday appointments. We think she will at least
start building the bridge to nowhere.

Anita did some bartering with Mountain Man Mike and came in to possession
of some of the Red Oak wood Mike has sawed last week. So, I helped move it
to "Spring Park" to be stacked to cure.

​We made two trips.

​GSH and I collected the rest of the slabs, got them mostly sawed and
stacked.

​

A fiend had an Oak tree cut down at his house on Snob Knob. He offered the
limbs to me. GSH and I got one load which we donated to Wayne and another
which is still inn the truck.

​Jim has back problems so he was not able to help with the loading.

Guess what ! AT work day was Tuesday. We went to Grassy Ridge and I dragged
locust poles up the hill to the road all morning and peeled poles all
afternoon.​ The toughest AT work day I have had for a while.

​

When I got home, somewhat to my surprise, we had a cat!

The cat seemed relaxed but disappeared ​causing great concern on the part
of the new owner. No sign of the cat Tuesday night or all day Wednesday.
But the Calvary (Joe and Anita) came over while we were gone and found the
cat resting in former cat hideaway in the darkroom. All is well in catville.

First venture outside.

The cat has taken the dogs bed. She moved back under the washing machine.
A sunny spot.

​
​Enough of that !

The ramps here are coming along. I may go on a ramp hunting trip late this
week or early next week.

​
​Not a lot of '63 work this week. I did start remounting things to check
fit.

The TDBG workers found the sunny side pool wall to be full of small flying
insects, It appears the walls beside entry way are also infested. ​

It is not too late to send your Easter pictures to the ole folks at home.
We appreciate all who did send.
​
