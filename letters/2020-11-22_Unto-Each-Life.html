November 22, 2020 · Sunday

Unto Each Life

9 photographs·206 words

Some trees must fall.

Farmer Niece Lisa has some trees very close to a very difficult neighbor. A
couple of small dead ones fell which I could not tell had touched anything.
I have promised to come help clear the others later this week.

Her pond has some sort of invasive plant . I have sprayed and slowed it
before. Sprayed it again. See no prospect of eliminating. May be able to
control.

Laura picked her not ripe tomatoes just before the first frost. I put some
on the mantle to ripen. PBBD found and helped consume the last of the
harvest.

While it is not obvious, this is a picture of Rachel backing the '37
Plymouth PU into the shop.

Later, Wayne took it for a spin in the drive way with a most appreciative
audience.

I tried to get a picture of the visiting crew admiring the latest addition
to our family picture annex. If all goes well, addition will be number 13
and mark 1/2 way. Figure that out Math major.

Knowing what pride precedes, I prefer to limit myself to pleased.

All is well.
