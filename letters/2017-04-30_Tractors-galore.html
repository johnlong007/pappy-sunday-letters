April 30, 2017 · Sunday

Tractors galore !

7 photographs·318 words

Once more The Blountville Old Tractor owners club handled registration for
the show at Gray. My friend Sanford and I did the whole thing. About 30
hours worth. From Wed. noon until Saturday noon when we closed up shop.

This year the "feature" tractor was small garden tractors. They came in by
the trailer load. Hundreds.

I did see one tractor which should be of interest to anyone involved in the
Long family farm.

​When one has an old car or tractor, a spare is essential.

I though this Ford attachment might be on interest to Jake. I have a cut
off saw all it would need is the tractor set-up. I bet the kids would enjoy
helping saw wood. Might need a couple more belt guards.

​

​We did go ramp digging one day. Decided to go to Dennis Cove as those are
the least walking of any I know about. Only dug a few as they are not yet
ready. Intend to go back later this week. Probably to cross mountain.

​We worked just a bit on the '49. Trying to mold a piece of cardboard for
ceiling of the cab. Made a couple of trial runs each somewhat better.

​Then I went dumpster diving in the cardboard dumpster at Blountville and
extracted a large flat piece. I not have it strapped on the outside of the
cab hoping it will conform somewhat and can make it fit inside. Stay tuned.

​
No dr, appts,

Bathroom banging and hammering continue.

Two friends from ECC, one from church and one from tractor club died.

No word about multiplying or dividing.

Wayne did try his hand at MIG welding. Looks good. A furnace stand for the
family blacksmith. I hope Anita will teach her how to shoe horses.

​I meant to go around and take pictures of the flowers in bloom. Perhaps
Katy or Christopher will post some.
