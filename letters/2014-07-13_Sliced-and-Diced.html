July 13, 2014 · Sunday

Sliced and Diced

8 photographs·343 words

Bright and early Monday I went in to Day surgery for a right side hernia
repair. Had the left side done years ago. So far, no pictures.. The only
restriction was no heavy lifting so I have been taking it easy all week.
Fortunately, that worked out well as Wayne has been here a couple of times
and Friday Linda left me a load of willing workers.

Herb also came by to check on e but he is about as broke down and stove up
as I am.

Garden stuff is beginning to come in. Although I cut way back this year I
do have a few things. This one is in honor of my little sister,

​

We did go a softball game which was well worth the trouble.

​
Our tomatoes are not yet ripe. However, Linda brought a quantity when she
brought the kids. I am seriously considering making a run to her house to
keep hers from going to waste.

​
Went up to the shed one day and found a person rooting around.

Andrew is my Junior woodsman. We have no winter firewood so I picked a dead
tree to cut. Turns out it was not dead , it was a Mulberry complete with
non-ripe berries at very top, Plus, it is really soft, fast growing wood so
it is very poor firewood. But, he did a good job felling and bucking.

​To keep from having everyone training on everything, I put Adam in the
BobCat and David on the mower. Maria, an experienced BobCat driver, stopped
by to observe, She offered no advice.

In the meantime, Wayne came up with some items to test on pool insulation.

​
Saturday night was pot luck dinner night at Church with Joe and Anita. Kids
are right, not the same without Mr. Long but still a good event.

I got called off report to go rescue a damsel in distress. I will leave it
to the damsel to report.

Anyway, back to mostly normal this week I hope.

​
