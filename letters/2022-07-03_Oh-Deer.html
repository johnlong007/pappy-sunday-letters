Oh Deer

11 photographs·230 words

Our wildlife sanctuary is in full swing. Morning and evening the deer check
this apple tree.

There were three under this tree in the circle.

At least one squirrel learned to access bird feeder by climbing down the
sting. Alan suggested first line of defense. So far, so good.

The cat has a plan for helping with excess bird population.

We have not yet tried to start the '55. But, I did get the gas tank
drained. It contained several gallons when parked 21 years ago, I drained
out less than a gallon of highly condensed thick, red residue. It burned
for about 30 minutes.

I mostly work on things close to the house. Gsh likes to help more than
watch.

Sue Ketron came by and brought a supply of cookies and other items. We got
all caught up on mutual friends and not so friends.

I have been trying to deepen the upper part of the retaining basin. I have
hit rocks. I decided to put the sub soiler of the tractor and see if I
could pull the rocks out.

This was a home made unit I bought from neighbor. LLLL has my real unit.
May have to issue a recall.

Lots of birds. Many species.
