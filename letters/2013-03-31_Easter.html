March 31, 2013 · Sunday

Easter

10 photographs·419 words

The times they are a changing. No egg dying, no picture taking (here).

I am supposed to go with brother Dave to cut down trees in Bays Mountain
Park. But, he keeps delaying for one reason or another. Along that line.
Carl had an AT work day scheduled to go back to Dennis Cove (the rock!) but
he aborted due to too much snow on the ground to be safe.

At home, work continued on scraping, sanding and chipping on the MM. But,
it looks just like last week except now all four wheels are painted bright
red. No work on the '37.

Allison and Meredith were home on break. Allison requested B& G. They
arrived along with Laura and Benj. Benjamin has adopted the osmosis method
of learning developed previously by his Aunt Anita

At LLLLs' request, I went to her house Wednesday for a work day Thursday.
She had lined out:
finish the bridge
cut a path from bridge through the woods
get garden plowed.
We got all those tasks accomplished along with several other things the
kids did.

Sarah was telling me about college and other application which, while
completing, the only skill she is able to list is brush cutting and
burning. After watching her on the bridge building project I am sure she
has another skill but not quite sure how to describe it.

On the way home, the VW picked up a noise which I believe to be a failing
front wheel bearing. M & R is now scheduled for Wednesday this week.

Dwight has decided to install a separate driveway to the apartment. The
county highway people will install the ditch culvert contingent upon his
procurement. So, we picked it up and dropped it on site.

As I mentioned last week, one of the drive way pine trees fell. I trimmed
the limbs and detached the trunk from broken stump. It is an interesting
failure. It appears the trunk had been damaged several years back causing
internal decay. Perhaps by someone pushing a lawn mower?

GSH continued playing in her sand pile as well as various sewing projects.

Meredith asked about ramps. I checked on the ones here and conclude a
couple more weeks should do it in the mountains.

Several grandchildren have asked me about Summer visitation. But, nothing
specific so far. I do plan to be out of the country June 21 through July 7
but GSH will be here. Other dates and other events are being discussed and
considered.
