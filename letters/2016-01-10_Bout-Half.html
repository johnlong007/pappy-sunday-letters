January 10, 2016 · Sunday

Bout Half

7 photographs·404 words

Want the good news first?

Herb has been moved to rehab center. s progress is being made he can stay.
If not trying he gets one week. He did not seem to be trying at first but
when I was over this afternoon it Was going better.
He is tired, week and I am sure discouraged.

The fire engine is all the way into the barn, first time it has been in a
shelter for many, many years. The front end is blocked up and I am ready to
try pulling the front drums to work o the brakes as soon as the signs are
right.

This week we went to Laurel Falls again to work on the rock steps (again).
It was cold ! My hand aches when out side temp is below 45 or so. There was
a bead locust beside the trail about 8 inches in diameter. Management
decided it needed to be removed. All we have to saw with at this location
is small hand saws. Since the tree was leaning away from where we wanted it
to fall it was rigged with a block and tackle. Then Pat and I took turns
sawing . cut a notch and sawed most of way through. Hard work with a small
hand saw.

The gang pulling on the rope at which time the tree broke off at ground
level. It was rottem at the base. They thought it was pretty funny that we
had worked for naught.

Saturday, with the aid and comfort of GSH we moved the LBT out of the shop
and into the storage building. It started right up and ran good. But just
as I got it partly inside my make shift fuel tank disconnected, spilled my
fuel so it ran out of gas. I pushed the rest of the way with the Bobcat.

I thought the tractor fuel system problem was resolved but it was not. Quit
again

Had to drag it back up here so I can try to resolve the air lock problem.

Ms. Fuller who lived in the little house between us and Ann Gross died. 100
yeras old. The house belongs to her three sons. It it in fairly poor
condition I believe. I talked with one of the boys. He said they plan to
fix it up and either sell or rent. I am considering to make an "as is"
offer. Opinions?
