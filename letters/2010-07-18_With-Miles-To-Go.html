July 18, 2010 · Sunday

With Miles To Go

7 photographs·205 words

Summer Camp residue:

No pictures from Monday's do-gooder work at church. However, I suppose
taking kids back home to Wisconsin could be considered do-gooder

We went to a production of The Music Man. Of course, the real reason we
went was to hear the music. Actually, just part of the music. Have you ever
wondered what people look like when playing in "the pit orchestra"? Well, now
you can know !

Also, you can know what the members look like when not playing.

We came back a somewhat different route. At Lexington, we stayed on I-24
about 20 miles, picked up the Mountain Trail Parkway and followed it to
within 20 miles of US 23. Then followed 23 into Kingsport. It was a lovely
drive, about the same distance as the Cumberland Gap routes but much prettier.
However, once more, no pictures.

Saturday was race cars and engines day. But, this time GSH went and we
visited her niece Fran who lives near Cosby.

We got home just at sunset.

Today, I walked around and looked at all the things I need to get done and
was overwhelmed. I think the time has arrived when I have more than I can
take care of .
