July 21, 2024 · Sunday

Stable

12 photographs·378 words

For many yeast, a person near and dear to me told me I laughed at things
other people did not think funny. Struck me as a compliment. I think
it goes along with being the youngest child in a multi child family.

Work continued at less than a frantic pace on the harrow. Thanks to Farmer
Lisa, Wayne and Bob we now have pieces ready to install, cut to fit. The
next step is drilling square holes. Each hole is to be a really tight fit
with various size hand forged spikes.

A Katy birthday visit.
Over the years when proud parents show me a new baby I usually tell them I
don't see a baby I see a 15 year old. Today, I did not see a grown young
lady, I saw a knee high child.

It is to be noted that none of the low hanging fruit one hears about are to
be picked here.

Bob's big Walnut log is in position. I understand it awaits his directions
for sawing.

Once upon a time, I had a ripe tomato on July 4th. We almost have one now.

PBBD says red meat will help my red blood cells. A & C are doing their best.

Nephew James stopped by. He is living in Birmingham with some of their kids
and wife Lisa is in Unicoi county with the rest.I got all caught up on kids
adventures. I told him about my father leaving us during WW2 to work in
Vancover. The only thing I remember about his time away was when we snuck
off on a Sunday PM and went swimming in the creek.Mom knew before we got
home, lined us up and applied the limb.

I procured a clutch slave cylinder with increased travel. While Davis was
visiting, he installed it in the Studebaker. I spent two hours in this
position getting the under dash linkage attached. Now my back muscles are
sore.

PBBD is continuing her outdoor beautification efforts. Wayne is aiding.

If it takes four and 20 blackbirds to bake into a pie, How many goldfinches
would be required?
