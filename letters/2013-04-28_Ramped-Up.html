April 28, 2013 · Sunday

Ramped Up

9 photographs·473 words

The Gray Station tractor event was this week. The Blountville club handles
registration at the gate. For the last couple of years that has boiled down
to me pretty much.I worked Thursday afternoon and all day Friday. Sanford
helped a good bit of the time. I found out he know almost everyone and
loves to talk . So, That made it a lot more fun than usual. Once more,
participation has declined. We use the Blountville club trailer to hold our
supplies during registration. It has been parked outside at the Kidd's all
along and had got dirty. Monday, we helped Debby wash and clean the
trailer.I looked over Larry's treasures. He has left her an an awful lot of
"stuff" with which to deal.

We did work on the AT one day. I went with three other men to install "
turnpikes) through a couple of boggy places. That requires draining the
site, putting down fabric to let water through but retain the fill, putting
down gravel (produced on site from rocks by means of a sledge hammer) then
covering over with grit dredges from the stream nearby.

One day, GSH, Kim and I harvested ramps. We went back to greasy creak to
the
acres of ramps site. The vegetation is growing up rapidly. It is going to
be difficult to identify the ramps in another week or so. Lots of "through"
hikers are going through here this time of year. We meet a dozen or so each
work day. Several went by as we were harvesting ramps , one stopped to
investigate. I sent out a note about Kim's later encounter with him.

One of our high school classmates died Thursday. He had undergone back
surgery and died. It is a bit disconcerting. He was a EE graduate from
TTUand was a good person. We went to the funeral Saturday then went to
Dechard.

For her Christmas wish, Rebecca asked her Grandmother to make her some sort
of strange outfit. It sounds like a bunny suit with a hood to me. Anyway,
we went down there to pin down the design specs and to order the materials
of construction. I went along because I knew Meredith would be there.She is
my most enthusiastic Ramp consumer.

While waiting for the MM parts to come back from Dwight's motor shop, I
have been sanding on the '37. Just for fun, I decided to give it another
test run. It fired right up and ran with much less smoke than before. (one
week vs. 40 years). However, it still ran rough and clearly needs a tender
touch tune-up.
