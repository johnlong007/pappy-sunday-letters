Almost Half

13 photographs·292 words

Ponder that for a while.
Not too long back the tractor ran out of fuel while still showing 1/4 tank
on gage. This time it ran out while showing 1/2 tank. Priming and starting
a diesel fate getting air in the system is a real pain.

Laura said she needed more bamboo for bean stakes. So, off we went to the
local supplier. I now know the reeds are not at all stiff while green and
growing. I do not know if they will become rigid upon drying, Stay tuned.

We have devised a clever plan to trap birds. Put netting over the
Blueberries. Hope for 4 and 20.

An even more devious plan to entice birds to the cherry tree. Strung up a
bunch of multi colored reflective tape and a couple of beach balls with
large eyes. Stay tuned.

Current phase (paneling) of the thermal energy storage room is near

Planned to make a serious ramp run but for lack of a nail (or something
like that) the plan fell through. I did go out this afternoon and find a
seed pod . I wonder if ramps grow in Fresno?

Kenneth graduated from Wis. ! Due to wedding conflict we did not get to
attend event. But, it was live streamed. I tried to take a screen shot as
he walked by but missed.

Martha ! sweet, understated, always flying under the radar Marha is now
married.
Cliff arrived.

I am sure autographed wedding pictures will soon be available. It was a
lovely service, I am glad we got to attend.

Mother's day out.
