June 24, 2018 · Sunday

Grump !

10 photographs·365 words

The days are getting shorter. At least daylight part. I am not sure when
that became such an issue with me but it certainly is now.

Maybe it has to do with days and weeks zipping by. Never used to notice
that either.

Monday the termite person came and treated under the foundation and around
the house. Supposed to take care of the bugs for 25 years. That should do
it for me even if I match Granny Buchanan.

​Tuesday was AT work day, moved from Thursday to avoid predicted rain. It
rained. Drenched but kept working. Took this photo at Lunch at the Nick
Graindstaff marker. The one Benjamin did not find. Mowed about 5 miles but
had to quit before reaching the shelter.

​GSH took her helper and went up to pick BBs. A very poor crop. Maybe a
quart. Not sure what went wrong. Weather or birds.

​Wayne was here part of the week working on various projects. We tried
again on the H and determined it is not getting a spark. To be continued.

​The front bumper on 885 got deformed many years ago, Made some progress on
putting it back into a more presentable shape.

​Over the years, I have acquired a fair collection of blacksmith tools.
Long since gave up on building a shop but held on in case Anita decided to
build one. In the meantime, it appears to me that Charlotte is serious
enough to be worthwhile to loan her the equipment. I intend to go when she
has open house at her shop.

​Outside part of the project is getting close. When the Wisconsin people
get here we need to have a serious talk about pool plumbing.

​
GSH developed a film behind the replacement lens after her cataract
surgery. We had one corrected Friday with other eye scheduled next Friday.
Friday evening while we were at Blountville track eating Rotary hot dogs, I
was trying to get her to tell me if vision in the corrected eye was
improved.

​
This time of year I spend a lot of time mowing. Also a lot of time working
on mowing equipment.

​Look close.
