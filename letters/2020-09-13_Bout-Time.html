September 13, 2020 · Sunday

Bout Time

12 photographs·377 words

It has been several months since a wind storm brought down several trees I
have been dealing with trees, sawed down or blown down since then. This
week I think we got all caught up on tree clean up.
After a lot of pondering, I came up with a plan for removal and transport
of the big log left on our 30' strip.

PBBD and neighbor Larry.

Log loaded, chief photographer helping with clean up.

Moved over to the ever growing to be sawed pile. It is to be noted that
none of the very large Oak logs are included.

If no one can line up a local with a portable sawmill I am going to be
tempted to purchase one . Maybe Alan would like to harvest his own house
timber.

The crawler has been left outside too long. Decided to move it into the
barn. Finally got it going but I believe there is a problem with getting
adequate power to the glo plugs.

Always something. At least it is inside now and on the to do list.

The county recently put a deer crossing sign on Ellis Road at the curve
where people like to throw their trash. I am considering putting a Turkey
crossing sign on our driveway.

Winter wood supply. We still have two big piles to move and stack.

Paw Paws are ripe. On our Sunday PM tour of the kingdom we picked up
several. Here, PBBD noticed several in the tree down front. The survivor
tree.

BLTC sandwich for lunch in the making.

Another day, another lunch. Chicken salad, I make it pretty well.

In other events, we went to Costco where I was tested and ordered new (and
we hope improved) hearing aides. Ready in two weeks. Told the tester I want
to be able to hear my wife and I do not want to have to fool with adjusting.

These are supposed to be automatic.

We also made a run to Erwin.Promised Farmer Niece Lisa I would help her
with reclaiming some of the place being encroched by boundary vegetation
and my brother's items.
