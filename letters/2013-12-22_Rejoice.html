December 22, 2013 · Sunday

Rejoice !

14 photographs·500 words

The days are getting longer !

Winter arrived with a bang. 74 degrees, a new outdoor record. Made a fine
day for the Blountville Christmas parade. Fortunately, it has not yest been
banned. As usual, the Long family brought up the rear of the parade.

Several folks suggested the '37 should have been in the parade. Maybe next
year, for this year it has moved into storage awaiting warm weather and a
paint job.

Anyone recognize this sight?

One owner, low mileage

Hard to believe it has been all over the continental US and as far as we
could make it toward Hawaii with at least 8 and sometimes more people. The
box my father made to carry our "stuff" on top is down in the barn in case
anyone wants to recreate the whole thing.

Did everyone drive it? I have forgotten, seems we took it out of service
about 1980 or so. The obvious parts which will need to be replaced are the
mirrors and the window latches. So, all should keep an eye out.

GSH says she can hardly wait to get it back on the road. I think this will
be the most fun project yet.. I plan to get it running (and stopping)
before spending much time on the body.

A cold, wet day on the AT. We walked in about two miles through about 2-3
inches of snow and slush. I picked the wrong shoes and had wet feet before
getting out of sight of the car. The project was to go to Low Gap, walk in
to "the Meadow" and cut all the woody growth since the last time three
years ago. I used snippers and cut hundreds while in a stooped over
position. About a two acre site.

The community band had a mall concert Saturday. It was very good. I bask in
reflected glory since both GSH and S-I-L Joe play in the band.

While it was warm Saturday I decided time to finish digging the potatoes
and carrots. Left the beets alone and still have a fine big patch of
turnips.

GSH and helpers came to greet me on my way back up the drive.

GSH has spent a lot of time sewing this week. I can scarce wait for the
presentation.

LLLL sent me a note asking for pictures of her doing something from high
school days. Look at these I sent and add your own caption! Apparently some
students are working on some sort of project.

I did some more painting on the MM but miles to go there.
Nothing new to report from the cat house, we all continue to get adjusted.
