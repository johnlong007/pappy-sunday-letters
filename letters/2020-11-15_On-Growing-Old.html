On Growing Old

13 photographs·389 words

http://holyjoe.org/poetry/holmes1.htm

For some time now, prostate, heart, skin cancers get the same answer, can't
really fix anything but can make do until something else gets you.

A visit to the cardiologist this week. Aortic valve noise is worse. Gotta
go back and get a sonogram to measure how much worse and get feel for rate
of change .

The good news is they are getting good at replacing that valve without
slicing and dicing. Not sure how they decide the time is right.
Maybe like our moving bedroom downstairs, Always tell people "one day
before one of us falls down steps". Maybe one day before I drop dead?

Preparation for the downstairs bedroom took a big step forward this week.

Spent a lot of time fiddling with the ignition switch on the '57 this week.
The switch which came with the car would not turn to the start position. I
found a switch in my spare parts inventory which will fit, has keys and
should work. However, there are four wires and four terminals. Quick math
major, how many combinations is that?

This picture is the switch which was in car and does not work. Replacement
has four terminals.

However, many years ago I learned how to jumper the ignition so starting
the car is not an issue. Here Wayne is wheeling into the canopy cover. How
would you like to have this thing bearing down on you on a winding mountain
road?.

Anyone want to decode the build code?

About the only outdoor activity was fighting falling leaves.

Sweetgum are the prettiest.

When we bought this property the hill to the left coming up the driveway
was a jungle. I came home from work one day and found GSH and LLLL out with
hand tools starting to clear it. I think I know where Sarah got it.

We have a post out in front where I feed the woodpeckers. It seems that is
not enough as they also like munching on the house siding.
With the help of PBBD the latest holes have been patched.

It appears the repaired garden spot is healing nicely. The part under the
walnut tree less nicely.
