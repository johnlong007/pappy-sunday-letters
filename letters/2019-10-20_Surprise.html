October 20, 2019 · Sunday

Surprise !

9 photographs·395 words

Niece Mari lives in California. She was in town for her high school reunion
and stopped by. It was a brief but delightful visit. I got caught up on her
work and family. It is comforting to confirm there are people in California
who still believe in God and country. ( Chelsea bailed.)
Mari was quite young when Nancy and Alex split , I am not sure how much she
remembers of her dad. But, she is a lot like him in all sorts of ways.

Tile work got well along in the thermal energy storage room. No idea yet
how much it will bump the final temperature. I have hope but PBBD tells me
that is not a plan.

Our SS teacher retired from staff at Bays mtn park. He gets to take a group
on a no charge barge ride each year. Each brought their lunch which we ate
in a lakeside pavilion before embarking. No Spring chickens in this crowd.

Most of the week was spent in car storage building sorting parts. I bought
a '52 Studebaker race car about 1970. It had a Chrysler Hemi engine which
had been allowed to freeze breaking the water jacket.
B-I-L Alex welded it for me. But first, I had to strip it down. The block
is now (50 yeqrs later) residing in with Herbs items. I made a run to Erwin
to hunt for the parts to put engine back together. Found none.

Farmer Lisa and best friend helped with the search.

A major objective for the week was get the parts '63 running so it can be
put back into storage. In spite of the best efforts of available willing
workers, it did not start! Rats ! to be continued, stay tuned.

The '52 Studebaker race car had a manual transmission with overdrive
adapted to the Chrysler Hemi. My intention is to bring the unit back to
life during the Winter indoor season.

Thee picture here is a block like the frozen one with the adapter unit in
place.

We got ome rain. Not much but some. I checked the retaining basin. I regret
leveling dam and filling the "pond". I may decide to get the dam rebuilt.
Hope does spring eternal. At least sometimes.
