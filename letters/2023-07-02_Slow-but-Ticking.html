July 2, 2023 · Sunday

Slow but Ticking

15 photographs·370 words

Sue Ketron sent us a care package. Actually, several packages. Something
for all tastes.

As I may have noted, I made a mess when I installed the trunk lid seal on
the '55 many years ago. PBBD is correcting my poor workmanship. Family came
in from all around the US and Canady to admire her work. While here, Wayne
was sizing up the task and tools needed to replace the leaking inner rear
axle seal. Stay tuned.

In other forward progress, A & C worked up one of the three Oak logs which
Wayne dragged out a while back.

Today, Meredith came by on a social call , then, we all went to Kpt to
check in on S & M to see their new abodee.The previous family had six
children which they raised in that house. I can see how it kept 8 people
busy. Rocks and trees.

The tomato plants Laura donated are doing OK. We may have a tomato by Labor
day.

RR berries are almost ready. The patch by the soccer field looks good. I
have not climbed the hill to check the big patch.

Apple trees are also looking good. We propped up a couple of limbs. The
deer are taking care of the famous low hanging fruit.

I had to go to the cancer center on Monday. Fortunately SF and PBBD sprang
into action. Saturday, family came from far and near to fill the stands and
cheer us on.

Thursday, the center worked me over, Blood, platelets and draino.
They put me at far end with different nurses than before. This one is
holding the platelets bag. I could not read it to see if it is one Wayne
donated. She tried to be all business. They kept me past her quitting time.
She took me up to the other end and hugged me before leaving.

Last week's nurse( Bailey) stayed over to finish with me. Note that Linda
has her trained. That is a raisin Lil Debby.

Parking lot when I was released.
