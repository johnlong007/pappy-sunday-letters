Pressing On

10 photographs·332 words

Can you identify these persons and the PU truck?

How about the location ?

My father disposed of his '37 Plymouth when he bought the '49 Dodge one
ton. About 1975 or so I found one advertised in Fairboult Minn. We all
loaded in the little blue van and brought it home. The picture below is
taken from on line to motivate me.

I decided to back up and take a running go trying to get it in much better
shape before calling pat in to paint it blue.

It got cold early in the week. We had fire in the fireplace and in the shop
stove for several days. That spurred us on to split more of the last years
wood to get more ready to burn this Winter.

For unknown reasons some of my welds broke and I had to do some major
repair on my home made splitter. So far, it has held up.

I had not heard anything from my Lil Sister so decided to go check. LLLL
went along which worked out well. Her PC was on the blink. Linda got it
going. As I understand a neighbor had been working on the PC. He must have
added new security programs which worked too well such that Sis could not
get anything in or out. Linda removed a bunch of stuff and had her in
operation when we left.

In Dechard we found David and Dale working on the Studebaker. The clutch
has been cooked. Roasted.
To be continued.

Back at home, Wayne came by to impress his mother with his technical skills
in tire changing. I am glad all his schooling has not been for naught.

Along the same line, PBBD came over and helped harvest the last of the pear
crop.

So far not many takers on my pear offer.

Anyone identify this scene?
