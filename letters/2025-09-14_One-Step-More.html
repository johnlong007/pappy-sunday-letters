September 14, 2025 · Sunday

One Step More

18 photographs·515 words

Monday morning, for something over 100 Mondays in a row I have been at the
cancer center before 7:30 AM. The girl in the picture is Bailey, she tended
to me for my first Infusion and several since then. She went to DB with
Allison, has a 2 year old daughter and her husband is a NP.
.

Infusion are becoming more common as the system Is shutting down. Trying to
make the most of it, Diane goes to Bojangles rather than sitting and
waiting.

We stack next year's firewood between
trees to cure. Wayne had sawed into firewood length slabs which we moved
up here to split and get ready for this coming season. Sawed but not
stacked firewood was scattered all around. We gathered it and stacked it in
the empty bay to cure.

This load is the now dry slabs sawed and ready to split for the fireplace.

The big news (other than Kate) was turning Diane loose with the Axe. All
that wood splitting paid off as she is unerring with her axe aim.

Left end is shop stove wood, right into the fireplace and cen
ter can go either way. The big Hickory chunk is being reserved for
Gwendolyn.

A most interesting visit. I had met the child before but now over 9 days
old and eyes are open.This time, he was looking right at me until Sarah
spoke, causing him to turn his head and focus eyes on her.. I wonder if he
will snap to like that in 15 or so years?

Sure enough, we have a new name written ! I have not yet been introduced ,
rumor says all is well.

Work continues on the crawler.. It may become yard art.

Work also continues on the Allis Chalmers. Diane keeps painting and I keep
taking parts off to clean and paint.

The paint room.

Two of the people with whom I did trail work for years stopped by. Paul and
Ted, the kind who helped make our country great .

We have had a lot of small trees fall along the edge of the woods
behind the house. One day, we dragged a bunch of it out.

Seven deer were hanging around yesterday. We have an Oak tree at each end
of the house. They come by morning and night to harvest the acorns.We have
one buck with an impressive rack, 2 or 3 does and at least three fawns, one
of which is hopping along on three legs.

Even the deer respect God and country.

The AC mowing machine cutter bar toe shoe is missing. Wayne has fabricated
one using the piece of auto spring I used for ramp digging for years.

Farmer Lisa stopped by to bring me up to date on events around shallow
Ford. Herb said things will change within 10 years. He was right.

.
