April 4, 2010 · Sunday

Mixed

11 photographs·420 words

A car is gone!

I traded the parts '53 Plymouth for pieces of the Studebaker lost by JW.
One item on my 5 year plan is to get rid of at least one car and one tractor
each year (net).
I procured the parts car as parts for the '53 Plymouth which I had visions
of driving when originally procured. Now, it is obvious I am not going to
drive and no one in the family wants it. So, I intend to put it up for sale
shortly and as such do not need a parts car.

I made a trip to Erwin to visit Herb. James' wife Lisa was there with their
kids. It was really fun getting to spend a bit of time with them. I took
pictures and got name rank and serial number data from Lisa to put them in
the file. A sample.

Tuesday was AT work day. We spent a long day marking a route for a
relocation about one mile long. The relocation to go around a PUD (pointless up
and down). After we finished the leader, Carl, said "I don't like any of
that". I think I have completed my career in trail relocation layout.

Nothing to report on car progress or do-ggoder. Plenty of deer and turkey
visitors. Anyone forget what the turkeys look like?

We went to Linda's to help plant potatoes. It was too wet so no planting
but did other tasks.
She and Andrew mowed their lawn. The first mowing.

Then we all came back here for traditional egg dyeing event. This year, we
had visitors from Charlie's foreign relations studies.

Although it was too wet here also, I figured the signs were right and
conscripted willing workers to plant my potatoes.

For Meredith, a ramp report. These are the ones I planted in the woods
above the pond. They seem to be growing but not reproducing.

Sunday, Laura had a birthday, so, we had a birthday celebration.

Summer Camp is scheduled for July 5-9. The frozen North contingent has a
conflict.

Janet (and maybe me) have been asked to be in the pit orchestra for the
summer musical. Unfortunately, the summer camp week overlaps with tech week,
which we really couldn't miss, and the performances are the following
week. Is it possible to move the week of summer camp to either June 28-July 2
(or earlier) or July 19-23 (or later)? It's okay if it can't work, just
wondering if it might be possible. Thanks!
