December 4, 2016 · Sunday

Social Butterflies

8 photographs·438 words

Today is December 4th. I turned the heat pump on for the first time.

Our social season started (and finished) this week. Sunday school class
dinner, Old tractor owners dinner, DB orchestra event and a trip to Barter.
I am worn to a frazzle.

Not much in the way of photogenic happenings.

I worked on the '49 truck bed quite a bit. Debating with myself on just how
far to go with repairs.

​I also obtained parts to repair the car of Herb's but did not get repair
finished. Brake lines require a special flaring tool which has not yet
shown up. Stay tuned. I cut out the rusted through section of line.

​
We continue to think about and talk about renovation of the downstairs bath
room and the pool room. Wayne and Alan came by to discuss the issues. I
listened and nodded wisely.

​AT work was sort of fum in a perverse fashion. We walked in the Laurel
Falls gorge. Some of us stopped at the first bridge to do repair work,
others went on the the shelter for repair work. I stayed at the bridge. The
bridge is constructed of 4 by 4 s, some of which had rotted. To remove and
replace the rotted parts required laying on the walkway and stretching out
into space . Hard to believe that I am one of the more flexible ones of the
crew.

​We did not get finished and plan to go back this week.

The deer and Turkeys are gathering together for the Winter. Several deer
are hanging around and a large flock of turkeys and taken to roosting in
the beech trees behind the house. They like to come in the yard and eat
acorns.

​I got a note from Kaylee one day saying she would like to come by. Not
suspecting any ulterior motives, I told her to come right on. She said her
Mom no longer drives and has turned car over to her. It reeked of fuel
vapors. I crawled underneath and found fuel dripping and the
underneath rear section
(where the exhaust system is located) saturated.
Of course all repair jobs start with Google it. Turns out that car had a
recall 10 years ago,

Told her to take off to the dealer (in JC) where it was purchased, park
right at the door so they could smell it and check on the recall. She sent
me a note saying she is to take it in Tuesday for a no charge fix. I am
pleased, also surprised they let her drive off in it.

Otherwise, it goes along.
