February 24, 2019 · Sunday

Smile !

10 photographs·399 words

Away back in ISCC days, i became acquainted with the Smiles organization.
Later, at CHCC I went on a mission trip to visit their major activity site
in Romania, GSH and Lil sister went the next year.
Because of the active support of CHCC the organization set up an office at
our church and left a person ( Dawn Wilson) here for several months. While
she was here, we loaned her the LBC.
Each year all the agencies/individuals we support send a person through to
report (and beg). This week, the Smiles folks were here and, for the first
time in 13 years, Dawn was with the CEO.
Today, she showed me a picture she had made while here.

The foundation has grown an amazing amount over the years. I would love to
go back but barring that, I would be delighted if a family member went.
Maria? Glance at their web site.
https://www.thesmilesfoundation.org/about-us/personnel/

Meanwhile, back on the ranch, Monday was in Memphis with my own personal
dentist. Spent the day with mouth wide open and varying numbers an size of
hands and tools in my mouth. Monday evening we made a repeat run to a local
chicken house.

Like the rest of the know world, the rains had been coming down and the
floods coming up.

Back on the reservation, the rains had more direct impact. It can be seen
that the brush pond is full and overflowing. Repairing the gullies down
through the walnut patch is going to be a problem.

Speaking of problems. This could just as well be teardrops.

One day I decided to go through my shirt collection. Tee, sweat,running
etc. From hither, thither and yon. If anyone(s) is interested I will be
glad to share. Otherwise, after a decent interval, they are going to be
donated to some worthy cause.

We continued to work on the refinishing of the old sewing machine. It is
now ready for finish sanding. I decided to put it all back together so we
can check the machine to determine if it will actually sew. Stay tuned.

The dog is a fine helper, He helped me put screws into frame by chewing on
my fingers.

Spring is almost upon us. We have little yellow flowers. How about
elsewhere?
