August 1, 2010 · Sunday

Rejoice !

12 photographs·196 words

The crawler lives again! Dwight put the magic touch on it.
Now, I am going to consider rigging some sort of blade assembly.

Janie Tabor was home this week.She is a nurse and lives in Denver. Might be
well to keep in touch just in case. She brought Sue up to spend the week
on the farm. Sue asked me about pressure washing her ramp. I said I can do
better than that, I will loan Janie the washed which will give her a project
for the week.

Adam was here for a week of fun and frolic. Monday we cut and removed a
downed tree for a friend. Tuesday was AT work. He got a patch for a days work.

Wed. was back to earth. We dragged the beech tree down from the hillside.
It is now out front waiting for the portable sawmill.

Thursday was to the lake to ski. I made a brief appearance. Long enough to
watch Linda ski.

Although the camping trip started Friday night, we went to watch
grandchildren perform in the DB band. You can pick them out .

Several trips to Rock Creek Park to visit the campers.
