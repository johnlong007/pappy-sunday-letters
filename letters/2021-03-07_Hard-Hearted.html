March 7, 2021 · Sunday

Hard Hearted

11 photographs·385 words

My heart doctor is Dr. Ponder. I like him. He works on 60s cars. Has about
15 I believe, probably in the 30- 40K range.

For years he has been listening to heart noise and telling me I will need a
valve job. I have been telling him I went to stall until they get good at
inserting with cuttin open. We congratulated each other. Dr. Ponder said I
should be good for 5 more years without heart related issues.

Below is a picture of the artificial Aortic valve. It has a
four flap diaphragm. The opening is about the size of my little finger.

The way it looks installed. (Not my heart in picture)

Thursday I had a whole battery of tests checking everything about the valve
operation. Report was it is operating correctly, sounds just right come
back in one year for check up.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, the rains came down and the retaining basin
filled to overflowing. Very little damage to the dam . When and if the pond
dries up this Summer I intend to make dam higher, maybe a foot or so.

Over the years we have had roof leaking issues. Having a new roof installed
has not been a permanent solution. Wayne contacted the company which has re
roofed auto shed and the barn last Summer. After inspection they
recommended rearing the damaged sections. Wayne had spotted damage while
string electric lines in the attic. The roofer said the damage space
pictured was caused by defective workmanship with previous job. A roofing
nail had been put in line with a shingle seam. He said that is the most
common cause of roof leaks. It takes a long time to show up.

I dragged out the cheery tree netting to get a piece to cover washing
machine drain line repair reseeding. All my resident helpers at work.

One pretty evening a WW showed up. We picked up brush in the walnut woods.

WW building muscles without going to gym.

In the distant past, I let all this kind of tasks accumulate until an
entire crew of WW's showed up for Summer Camp. I sure miss 'em.
