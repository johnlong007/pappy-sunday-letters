December 7, 2014 · Sunday

Dec. 7th

9 photographs·453 words

My uncle Jim (Mom's brother) graduated from UCHS and joined the USAAF.
After training he was sent to Hawaii and from there on to Clark field on
Luzon. He was there when the japs hit a few hours after they hit Hawaii. I
was almost six when the attack occurred. I remember all the family
consternation because they knew Jim was there. If you have not read the
book " They Fought Alone" you should.

He told me about being under e truck shooting his 45 at the airplanes and
being able to see the pilots looking down and laughing.

For the last few weeks I have been spending a lot of time scanning pictures
into the files and sorting files to put things into the pictorial family
history. GSH had frour uncles (brothers) in the Navy at the same time in
the war. Can you imagine the strain put on their family?

Monday I went to Johnson City to visit a friend with whom I had worked at
ECC. He was home on hospice care. We had a good visit. Today I went to his
funeral service.

Monday evening I went to the funeral of Eva Bowman. She was the wife of
Hugh Bowman, mother of one of my lifelong best friends (Junior Bowman) and
a farmer for whom I had worked a lot while growing up.

Last week I said I had moved the '63 into shop so I could determine the
amount of body work it will require. To do that, I needed to remove the
paint and all the body filler hidden under the paint. That is pretty much
completed and the car moved back into the shed so I can get back on the LBT.
The rust is not as bad as I expected. Now, I will try to find someone to do
the repair job correctly. That is, cut it out and weld new panels in place.
With quite a bit of effort, good luck and maybe a few willing workers I
should have three vehicles ready to paint next summer.

AT work was cold wet and miserable. three of us worked on renovating a
sloughed off section of trail while the rest of the crew put steps in a
switchback. Then I worked with Pat Loven to cut a locust log across the
trail using dull mattocks !

Once more, I have not heard from Lil' sister. But, still intending to go
help her move to new home early next year. Did get to Erwin to check on
Herb. His face is healing as he continues the HBO2 treatment. He is
collecting as much information as possible about the underlying growth
before deciding his next course.
