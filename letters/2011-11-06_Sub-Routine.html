November 6, 2011 · Sunday

Sub-Routine

8 photographs·302 words

Rejoicing in Mudville ! Aaron arrived from the land of extended darkness.
Perhaps good, perhaps bad depending on how one views it, he has a truck
load of belongings to be unloaded into the Reservoir Road Annex.
Unloading will start about noon tomorrow Monday. Anyone who cares to
provide aid and assistance is invited. Otherwise, we will do the best we
can with what we have got.

Some of us went to Cookeville for the homecoming events on Saturday. Some
of us went to watch John perform in the homecoming events. Some did both.

GSH has had a busy and productive week which did not include any work on
the table.

In addition to sawing wood for the coming Winter, clearing brush from
around the house, we finished mowing around all the pine trees and clipping
the honeysuckle.

For AT work, we went to a different place this week. Where the road crosses
the mountain between Elizabethton and Shady Valley The forest service
purchased a farm a few years back. The AT now runs along the crest of the
mountain and through the center of what was a mountain top farm. We went in
2-3 years back and made the trail smooth and wide, "wheel chair accesable".
Since the farm is still being used to run cattle, we put in a zig-zag
entrance (Kissing Gate).
The powers that be, decided we need to improve a test section of the trail
to try to encourage more usage by wheel chair bound folks. So, we raked a
50 foot section, put in a concrete speed bump (water diversion guard ) and
packed gravel for about 25 feet.Stay tuned , as test results come flowing
in, I will post.
