January 19, 2025 · Sunday

Surprise !

13 photographs·304 words

I passed another birthday!

Alan, Diane, Anita, Laura, Linda and Wayne were all here. Unlike some
birthdays past, we did not go visit Donna in the cold, cold North or my
sister . I miss each.

Bob and Laura sponsored a warm up event on the actual day.

Work continued on mostly shop projects.. Another cultivator project was
completed and moved down to the antique storage area to be put on display
when the signs are right.

Progress was made on the lay off plow. Our most difficult project so far.

This picture may be of interest to any blacksmith in our midst. It is a
handmade part which holds the plow point on the plow beam.. Diane looked on
line to find it is a high dollar artifact.

Family pictures are getting behind on family growth.If need be, I can point
fingers and name names.

The Wonder Horse is in its final stage. Like a shiny new car it is getting
a clear coat.

I did the wire wheel rust removal on the triple shovel cultivator. However,
it did not pass inspection and more detailed work has been ordered. Also,
another set of handles must be procured.. Stay tuned.

Working in the shop means going through a lot of firewood.

We were working away when Chris the construction owner showed up. Put him
to work moving some heavy chunks..

Something reminded me of the last lines of this poem. I looked it up
online and reread it. Do any or all the Summer Camp graduates remember
Thursday evenings?
Adventure dogs
Popcorn
My reading of the poem.
Those who have children should be reading it to them.
