November 15, 2009 · Sunday

Who Knows (or cares)?

5 photographs·269 words

I went by to check on Hal Wed. but he was not home. I took that as a good
sign.

The turkeys and the deer continue to keep the Winter Rye under control.

Running has not gone well this week. A bit disappointing.

Mike had decided that the leak problem was due to the chimney. Acting
accordingly, he had a cover made.
Since I had put a homemade cover on, he had wrapped the whole thing in
plastic and it was still leaking, I suggested he use the water hose. Turns out
the roofers had not installed the shingles under the flashing below the
chimney causing the leak. We are of differing minds over who should pay for
the chimney cover.

Most of my discretionary time was spend fiddling with the Studebaker. Late
in the week I stuck the fenders and hood on so we could fit the radiator.
We hope to have it running before Christmas.

One diversion was a trip to Blountville annex to notch logs for the barn
project. Both Anita and the horses are always interested and involved.

Friday was AT work day. I cut two trees both of which required wedging to
make full in the desired direction.

Friday night was the Fall dinner for the maintainers. Several maintainers
were given fleece vests for having exceeded 1.000 hours of AT work.

Sunday we went to Dwight's old home place to collect items to be preserved.
Sort of a sad undertaking. I have read nomads are the happiest people in
the world as they only own what they can carry.

Anyway, on to another week.
