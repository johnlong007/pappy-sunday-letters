January 26, 2014 · Sunday

Truncated

7 photographs·497 words

Health ,wealth and happiness: After three weeks of waiting for my scalp to
adjust to the new location, the stitches were removed. Reported to be
healing nicely with no further follow-up required.
Got a call about the Ultra sound saying all was/is normal with no problem
indicated.

So, maybe good for a few more miles. Slow miles. For the first time in over
30 years of regular running/jogging/shuffling, I am having a knee problem.
A couple of times it was really painful so I stopped my daily run. Now, I
am trying to be careful and not twist it in hopes it will recover before
the Feb. 8 event.

No AT work this week, too cold.

I spent most of my week indoors removing all the doors from the LBT then
using the rotary wire brush inside and out . Also finished removing all the
brake system components and am now ready to start in a forward direction.

Guess what Wayne dragged in !

The body and running gear are in better (much better) shape than the LBT.
But, restoring a different vehicle is not in the plan. As soon as I get the
LBT cleaned, wire brushed, brakes and running, I will start taking parts
and pieces from this one .I think this project is going to be the most fun
project I have undertaken.

Another Polar Vortex is scheduled for the coming week. Since we have used
up all the wood stationed here at the house, I had to harvest and relocate
wood today. My grandmother was wont to say "it is ok to get your ox out of
the ditch on Sunday unless you pushed it in on Saturday". I am not quite
sure how running out of wood fits in there.

I have been using our fireplace wood in the shop stove. But, that uses a
lot so I decided to harvest a dead locust from the hill out front. You
would be amazed at how getting smacked through the face with a limb
followed later by getting head bone cut and scraped helps one decide that a
hard hat is really not so foolish after all.

Ross may think all a GSH does is cavort all over the world. Perhaps it is
time for a Katy refresher on proper role of a GSH.

But, to complete the picture, I fixed lunch today. After visiting my
upscale sister, I figured we should bring a bit of couth into our repast.

While the chickens have not yet started coming home to roost, the turkeys
have:

Various and sundry people ask me about travel plans for 2014. I tell them
that for the first time I can remember, I do not have any travel on the
calendar. None.
I am open to suggestions.
Oh, cat is fine.
