October 6, 2024 · Sunday

Northeren Adventure

10 photographs·205 words

I love molasses ! A song I bet is posted on you tube.

Spike tooth harrow is painted and ready to be stored I believe. Need some
good, strong WWs

Walnut crop has been harvested, hulled, washed and stored to cure. One
fifth of the harvest last year.

Chestnuts have been harvested. A bumper crop and only one customer.

Kenneth, home from the bright lights of the big city came by to visit. I
always have some questions for him and he always answers ! Mostly in terms
I can understand.

Maria also came in from visiting far away places with strange sounding
names. She also deals with my questions with patience. I am appreciative of
each of them.

Then A & C took us along to visit their grandson . This is a picture of
their country estate. Anita would love it.

Next day the long awaited visit to Andrea's home place. Her dad took us on
a full tour. It was great !

Then back to the real world to visit J &M's new home place.

Buchanan's all the way down !
