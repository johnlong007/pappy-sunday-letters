February 20, 2022 · Sunday

Presing On

12 photographs·383 words

We made it to the early (old folks) service this morning. Attendance about
1/2 from two years back.

Only 2 or 3 masked riders.

Recognized all the songs and knew the words. Substitute preacher who spoke
quite low so I never heard a word.

SS had about 1/3 of pre virus. Teacher was well prepared and did a good
job. Lesson about Job. It was the standard, literal message. I do not take
Job literally at all. Believe that interpretation misses the point.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch:

Pill pusher at work. .

See all those pictures posted above the windows. !3 married GC posted. Past
the last picture is a save this date notice. Plenty of open space left.

Rogue is rolling along. I had sorta planned to trade it in at 80K.
However my agent says it's a lousy time to trade. I may take it in at 85
and tell them to do everything needed to get another 85.

Some time back I was asked about procuring some hickory wood. I put out the
call and this week I got a note from Don Hass to come get a load.
If you take a close look at the beams we put down to get the wood off the
ground, It might appear to be a tripping hazard.

Anyway, the only sure usage I know of for hickory has to do with " all to
the tune of a hickory stick". So, here it is: do it yourself hickory
handles, chips, furniture, obedience training materials?

Not much sawmill activity this week. But, I did go drag down a middle size
Oak log because I want to see how well the Oak has been preserved. Stay
tuned for the Sawyer report.

Also on the curiosity list, I went up to check on the run off
retaining basin. The water is not absorbed into the ground as rapidly as I
hoped.But, I have a plan!

We had some warm days so I dragged GSH to the track where she walked two
laps. Under duress.

Back home, she fell on the sidewalk and skinned her forehead.

Just around the corner.
