July 18, 2021 · Sunday

Always Something

7 photographs·269 words

Finally got the clutch assy. installed and the lever carefully marked to
be welded Again, 3rd time. Put the whole works back together, new master
cylinder, new slave cylinder, freshly welded pivot arm. Depressed the
clutch pedal one time and the weld broke. I suppose not enough metal left
on the end of the pivot shaft to get a good weld.
Time to punt. Order a new clutch pivot shaft and start over.

It must be a genetic thing. Wayne has been trying for some time to get the
PU to run properly. Most recently it would not start at all. Close
examination revealed out of fuel. After pouring in 5 gallons of my
secret supply
of high Octane no E fuel he discovered the talk seam had cracked.
Repairs are planned.

New seals arrived for the tractor, so it has new brake shoes and new axle
seals on one side. The shoes are installed on the other side but it turns
out two seals are required for each side. Once more, waiting for parts.

Gave up on anything good occurring with the '63 and put it back in storage.

Did just a bit of checking on the Studebaker. David had reported running
rich and rough. I did find the number 8 plug wire was not connected. Seemed
to smooth right out. There is a serious problem with Clutch release.

My harvest ! Deer and Groundhogs are eating everything. Birds are
cleaning out the berry crops. I keep telling myself I wanted an animal
sanctuary.
