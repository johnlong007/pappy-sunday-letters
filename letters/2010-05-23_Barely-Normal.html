May 23, 2010 · Sunday

Barely Normal

8 photographs·221 words

The work on AT Monday with the conscripts was at Hughes Gap. It was wet,
slick and muddy. We built 2,100 feet of trail. Generally the switchbacks
are built with large rocks.

Since Katy abandoned me and the garden, I have had to move back into
hoeing. It al came back to me quickly. I am trying to get front garden ready to
harvest things for Summer Camp.

Critter report:

I went to Erwin to check on car progress. The kids sanding on the car
really wanted to hear it run. It really was fun to see them huddle up to look
and listen.

Progress on the drag car was less rewarding. Dwight continues to have
difficulty getting signals transmitted to his computer. Hope springs eternal,
well, almost eternal.

To no ones surprise, Allison graduated from DB covered in glory.

After the graduation event, a number of interested parties celebrated by
having an eating outing on the Jack deck (not burning). For a follow up, a
reduced number had an outing at the Brewer residence Sunday. To spare the
feelings of those who could not/would not/did not attend, no pictures are
included.

Other items of more or less interest:

The Cherry tree is covered. The BB bushes are mostly covered and the sore
hand is gradually being put back into service.
