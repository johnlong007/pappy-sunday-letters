Blountville Christmas event

0 photographs·102 words

Pappy has asked me to send this note to find out who is interested in a
Blountville Christmas event. The proposed time is Dec. 31st (PM) and then
Jan 1st (AM). The event would be low-key. Carry in a crockpot dinner;
hang stockings; fill stockings before you leave or early the next morning,
carry in breakfast items the next morning. Open stockings; eat breakfast;
socialize. Stocking stuffers only; no presents; low-key.

If interested, please let me know. I'll fix a stocking stuffer list after
I hear from people. send answers to me at dwbedb@gmail.com. also please
forward to anyone I left off.
