June 28, 2020 · Sunday

Just Reward

11 photographs·364 words

I do not feel "old". I was the youngest in my family and being old still
feels sort of unreal. I remember growing up and having grandparents who
were old and wise. At least they were old.
Sometimes I wonder how our grandchildren will remember me/us.

But, for the present, we had a really nice outing for lunch one day.
Father's day outing as it were. It has sort of slipped up on me looking
around and thinking how fortunate we are. As best I can tell, our children
like us and each other.

Some will look at this picture and see David and think "he made it".
Some may see the car and wonder.
I see the whole scene and think "Linda made it!"
I sure am glad.

There was a lot more sawing left last week than I realized. But, now I
believe it is all ready for splitting. I intend to leave right where it is
in hopes some willing workers will appear by and by.

I got tired of switching batteries around and put a big 960 CCA @ 0F in the
crawler. Wayne and Thomas went over to top off fuel and get it running.
Battery was dead !. One more problem with which to deal.

Everyone Can identify?

I got the walks and ramp pressure washed and trimmed overhanging bushes.
Decided I could not wait for new belt for mower to arrive. Went to tractor
supply and paid $47 for a not quite right belt which will not last long.
Got home put it on and used for a few minutes. Found I had a note from
Wayne saying the new belt was just delivered. Rats.

I have no pretense of a garden this year. But I do have tomatoes, peppers
and cucumbers in and around the circle. Red raspberries are coming in. So
are the birds. Grapes are looking good so far. BBs about done.
Marigolds are touted to be helpful for tomatoes. I have plenty. We shall
see.

Max went to the beauty parlor.
