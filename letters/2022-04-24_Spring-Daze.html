April 24, 2022 · Sunday

Spring Daze

11 photographs·375 words

I had a follow up appt. with Dr. Sago. The ones she was following up on
looked ok. While there she zapped several more. None seriou we hope.

The sawmillers swung into action Monday The mill can handle a 30" log. Note
this one had to be trimmed just a bit.

I had never heard of "Quarter sawed" but now I know, sorta. The first pass
took a 7" slab off the top.

Followed by several 5/4 th. boards

Then the 7" cut was turned on edge and sawed into 5/4th boards.

Got it? Ask Alan to explain

I accordance with my ability to make easy tasks difficult, I replaced the
two rear wheels on the '55. Brought the wheels down to housr and determined
that each held pressure . I had not been able to hold the chuck in
proper alignment is the confined, dark space .
Anyway, the wheel covers were badly tarnished so decided to polish. One
done in picture.l

PBBD did quite a bit of work on the '37. Washed and worked on finding and
filling dents and dings. She is doing the detailed work I hate.

I dreaded going to Erwin without Max. He always had such a fine time. But,
we went in order to check in on the Erwin part of the family. Farmer Lisa
gave me an update on various members, seems to be going Ok for now.

I did get a note from Dan today saying brother Jimmy is in the VA and
likely heading for assisted living with the home place to be put on the
market.

The pond is being overgrown with some invasive plant and it also appears
the bamboo has a problem.

I am getting pretty good at Saturday morning breakfast. Reservations
required.

My retaining basin worked but needs some design improvements. The first
order of business being to drain the retained water.

Snce the Bobcat has been abducted, I am going to try earthmoving in the
retaining basin with the tractor and scope. I made a couple of passes and
decided it needed sideboards to increase capacity. Stay tuned.
