May 3, 2009 · Sunday

Spring

12 photographs·408 words

Pretty much settled back into the groove (rut) around the old home place.
All sorts of creatures, did not even bother taking pictures. The deer and
turkeys are out most morning and evenings. Squirrels all over the place,
birds and ground hogs, lots of those.An entire extended family live under the
smoke house. Perhaps we will dig them out during Summer Camp.
I visited Hal. I think he is getting worn down but is still himself. He had
an MRI which indicated some growth but no change in treatments was
induced.

I went to see Maria in another track meet. She astounds me. A most
determined person. The most impressive performance was running the third leg of
the one mile relay. She got the baton probably 100 feet behind and passed it
off about 25 feet ahead. Then she waited for the forth leg runner to run
her lap.

In case anyone is interested, I dragged the David Bradley mower out of the
garage, spent most of two days adjusting it (instead of reading the book)
and am ready to declare it finished. As soon as I can get the Cletrac
"finished" I will hook it to the mower, take it down front and get a picture of
GSH operating the mower while I tow it with the Cletrac. Maybe.

I should give credit, my willing worker helps with the tractor and the
mower.

Dwight took the Cletrac starter apart to rebuild it. But, when I tried to
start after reinstalling, I found the battery had no charge. I have had
another battery charging for days and will try it again tomorrow. I really do
not want to buy another 6V battery.

I went to Erwin to visit Herb and Aunt G. We also made the social rounds at
the county maintenance garage and the vocational school garage.

Can anyone identify the little insects?

At work was a good day. The regulars plus a few captured through hikers.We
dug a section of relocation through a rock pile up near Wilbur Dam. A
before and after picture.

A lot of time and effort was spent trying to clean the deposits from the
pool walls and floor in preparation for bonding on insulating panels. Acetone
works the best but I am not enthralled with breathing the fumes for
extended periods. Stay tuned.
