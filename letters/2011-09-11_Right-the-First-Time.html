September 11, 2011 · Sunday

Right the First Time !

3 photographs·176 words

It seems awfully mundane to be reporting on my week when others are out
living in exciting places and doing exciting things but here goes.

It rained Monday, hard, with a wind coming in on the West end of the house.
The rain ran in just as it has for years !
But, I now believe I understand the problem. It appears that when rain hits
in just the right direction it gets in behind the siding and then can run
down and get behind the flashing. Next is to take the siding off and put
some sort of rain proofing behind it I believe. On a happier note, I ran in
the rain and the dogs are learning to come greet me. I picked tomatoes as I
came by the garden.

GSH worked on her table most all week (Once more she got some help from Bob
the woodworker). I am going to send this while I eat supper then, if all
goes well, complete the rest of the week.
