Anyway, back in River city Life droned on.

I had a treadmill stress test Monday. If anyone is interested it is a
standard protocol in which the speed and angle are increased every three
minutes until one reaches some predetermined pulse rate based on age. My
target was 120 . It took 10:48 to get there could have gone longer but they
said stop so I did. I will go in Tuesday to have the Dr. explain what it
all means.

No pictures there.

Wed. I went with Herb to see Skin Dr (Sago). She worked him over pretty
good and zapped three for me just in passing. One of mine may require
follow up. She took samples for biopsy on his in order to keep track of any
activity I suppose. But, so far so good.

No pictures there either.

But, we dropped Ella off to stay with Flo and upon returning to pick her up
I did get a picture.
\
Most of the home work this week was on the Studebaker. I pretty much got
the odds and ends under control except the fittings are leaking on the
power steering.

Friday for AT work we had 120 students from ASU for a day. We had been
working on site preparation for quite some time. The day went better than I
could have asked. I continue to be impressed at Carl's planning, organizing
and executing skills.

Saturday we pressed apples to make apple juice. Perhaps apple cider.
