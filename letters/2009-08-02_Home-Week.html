August 2, 2009 · Sunday

Home Week

9 photographs·280 words

Still no do-gooder work and no AT work. Did go visit Hal and Sue, twice,
once with GSH and once with Anita and Linda. Hal has a small tumor which they
plan to attack with some sort of focused beam surgery.

A big event was Meredith has returned from music camp so we started on
mastery of the car with a manual transmission! She had no trouble at all so we
quickly moved on to smashing cans and followed that with one trip on the
open road. Next we are going to switch vehicles and start defensive driving.

Lisa had jury duty so the kids stayed here. They helped me stack the wood
pile left spread out after summer camp.

Tomato season is here. They are getting ripe slowly this year. Also, a
family of three turkeys have included the tomato patch on their morning and
evening route. Note the extra legs on momma turkey.

Friday night I went with Dwight to see the dragster he and Pat built in
action. The event got canceled due to rain. We all went to Pat's house with
the R & F for serious race talk. Got to see Pat dealing with the apple of
his eye. Also got to see thew trailer at its new home.



Aaron is home on leave. So, that was adequate reason for a party.

Sunday afternoon was a SS party at the teacher's house on the river at
Phip's bend. A very nice event with some very nice people. We are very
fortunate to have our family and our friends.
