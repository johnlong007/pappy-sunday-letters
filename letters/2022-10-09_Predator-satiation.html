Steven stopped by and I was able to talk with him for quite some time.
However, it is a lot like me trying to talk with Kenneth. I get in over my
head very quickly.

I thought surely there is some area In which I know more than our
overeducated grandchilldren. See subject.

Clue : the walnut crop is way down this years.

Every morning and every evening. The animals come through picking up
acorns from the Oak tree. Not quite sure what the groundhog is after.

Mereditrh stopped by. Much easier to get Jack family news out of her than
from her parents.

I am pretty annoyed with Mr. Nissan so we went looking for a car one day.
The only similar one I found with a manual transmission. Just thinking
about it.

It looks as though the pecans may actually mature this year. The
squirrels will like that.

Chestnut trees are loaded. Animals like to get fat on the chestnuts.

A lot of time and effort is spent collecting and cleaning black walnuts.
Pressure washing in the truck bed works well.

Sanford came over one day to help me get the '63 clutch freed. Dragged it a
few feet in first gear while on the asphalt and it came loose. I sure do
like it. may drive it some this week. very little work on the '55. Next
stepp is to check operations on the power brake booster.

Not sure what to make of the retaining basin grass .

The only vehicle left on my wish list.
