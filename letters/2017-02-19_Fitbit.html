February 19, 2017 · Sunday

Fitbit

11 photographs·239 words

Anyone else figure it it is addictive?

Saturday GSH, Diane, Anita, Linda and I went out to clear a blowdown
reported on the section adjoining ours on the AT.
Diane dumped Three of us at Low Gap and took GSH on around to Cross Mtn Gap
to come in the other direction. We found and cleared the reported blowdown.

Some time later, I discovered my Fitbit was missing. After some
deliberation, Anita and Linda went back to hunt and I went on to meet the
others. Before I got to them, I found another much larger blowdown which
will require a chainsaw.

​
The searchers did not find the missing Fitbit so, I enlisted GSH and Wayne
to form a search party Sunday PM.

​Rejoice !
The regular AT work day was Tuesday. We went to the ranger work center and
sawed, drilled and assembled the parts to build two bridges. They should go
together like lego toys.

We went in to see Dr. for GSH on Monday and got scheduled for PT. In PT
they said her legs are doing well but needs work on hand and arm. Anita is
doing home PT.

​I have mostly been working on the floor and door of the '49. Making
progress I think.

​Beth , Jake and Suz came by. Child is outpacing almost any weed you can
imagine.

​Got an exciting update from Rachel.
Anything else brewing out there anywhere?
