November 6, 2016 · Sunday

Recollections

10 photographs·406 words

It must have been in '82 or '83 that I first met Dwight. Diane was working
at Oak Ridge and we went down there for a camping trip. Pretty sure it was
at Melton Hill Lake. Not sure if any besides GSH and I were involved. Maybe
they will remember. Anyway, we were set up for the evening and a person
came wheeling up on a M'cycle with Diane perched on behind.
Took a while to recover from that.
On Wednesday afternoon, I was in the living room talking with Dwight's son
Pat. Dwight was still in bed (as it turned out with about and hour to go).
Dwight called out "he is not here to work on that crawler". Those were the
last words I heard from him.
A lot of events in between those but never dull. I will miss him.

Last week I spent 3/4 of the book value on the vw so I would not have to
work on it. This week, I spent 1/2 day helping Dave Dunham work on his
truck Boggles me too. Power window quit operating. We replaced the works
for about $75 and got it fixed. Did get a free breakfast out of the deal.

Another day, We helped Don Hass cut down and saw up some trees he believed
were too close to his house. We kept part of the wood but not exactly
select firewood.

GSH sometimes fusses about forgetting things. So, I decided to give her a
test by requesting B&G one morning.

Another day, we took the dogs to the vet in Erwin for shots. While there,
we went by the Sandy Bottom cemetery. That is the one we cleaned off a few
years back using rented jailbirds. My GGF is buried there along with other
more distant relatives.

A couple of times we stopped by to check progress on the cabin restoration
project. I was most impressed watching mike make replacement timbers for
the walls. He handles that extra long saw like a toy.

In between running hither and yon, I hit a couple of licks on the '63 while
GSH continued leaf blowing. I believe she has found her calling.

Several family members posted updates this week. I am appreciative. Lonely
here in Nowhereville.

I whimpered a bit about needing help updating distribution list but must
not have sounded sincere as no help arrived. So, Deal with it John et al
