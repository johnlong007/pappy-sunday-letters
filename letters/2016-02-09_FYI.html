skift n2 [SND skift n.1 2, skiff n.1 2 “A slight or flying shower of rain
or snow”, skiftin “A light fall or sprinkling of snow”]
1 also often skiff, rarely skiffing, skiffling: A light fall of snow (or,
rarely, rain); a thin layer of snow or frost on the ground, or of ice on
water. widespread exc NEast, Sth, SW See Map
