December 25, 2011 · Sunday

Rejoice

7 photographs·462 words

Christmas is here and so are we !

The days are getting longer .

Katy is home.

After a long lull, Dave and I got back to do-gooder work Monday. Among other odds and ends, we installed caps on the outside faucets for a church couple. Not exactly high tech work but it was appreciated. We tried to replace their electrostatic filter elements with regular filters but could not find filters to fit. The project is in limbo again.

Most of discretionary time on the home front was spent at Blountville annex aka "Spring Park", cutting privet hedge. We had it all cut down a couple of years back but like the man going to St. Ives, every sprout sends up seven sprouts. The place is going to require a severe herbicide dose or a herd of goats.

Anita found the butt of a fairly large Cherry tree log and decided it looked a lot like a bowl. In the spirit of aiding and abetting, GSH, John and I roughed it out. I will check in on this saga from time to time. I did tell john that when his mom decided to do something it would not be wise to bet against it getting done.

As most are aware, this week was the run-up to Christmas. I went grocery shopping !

While I was busy putting staples in the cupboard, GSH was off at the Dr. getting her foot maltreated. She has suffered from infected toe nails for years (jungle rot?). The Dr. grubbed one toenail and scheduled her to return in two weeks for an encore. In the meantime, back at the ranch, I have been waiting on her hand and toe so to speak.

I did get away long enough to make a run to Erwin to check on the goats, Herb and Flo. Herb was in a grouch, seems unknown persons have been stealing his scrap metal, including several hundred pounds of copper. I gave him several helpful suggestions on means of possible prevention methods but he mostly appeared interested in bringing their careers to sudden end.

On a happier note, It was a lovely drive over to Erwin with a lovely red sky. Going toward River Hill, I stopped to take a picture of Temple Hill (the pointy hill in the center, background). The cemetery Aaron and others helped me work on clearing is visible in the picture also. I could send a higher resolution picture upon request.

For the festive season, Dwight procured some fresh sausage leading to a spontaneous B & G event.

The Christmas present unwrapping continues here. I think I shall include one picture. As near as I can tell, it must be a present Santa supplied upon request of the Kroger dog.
