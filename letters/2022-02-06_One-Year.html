February 6, 2022 · Sunday

One Year

11 photographs·388 words

Friday was one year after having the artificial Aortic valve installed. I
went in Friday for a battery of tests checking valve function. The results
were posted on my echart. Right down the middle as best I can tell.
About 1- 1/2 year ago Dr. Ponder told me to get it done or I woul;d
probably not last a year . The year has gone by very fast. I am still alive
and feeling fine.I think I am glad and hope others are also.

Nephew Dan came over Saturday and brought my brother Jimmy (his Dad) along.
It was really fun and interesting getting caught up on that branch of the
family. More and more I appreciate family and friends. The Church has not
been adding a lot of value for the last couple of years. But, Sue Ketron
brought food and visited one day. Pat Loven called and offered to bring
food for supper but I asked for a rain check as Joe and Anita were feeding
us. Sanford came by one day with McDs for lunch. All is well.

In the 70s I took classes at ECC and did film processing, developing
printing etc. My film has kept better than a lot of the prints I did from
the film. I purchased a little scanner which will digitize the pictures on
the film. PBBD has been working the bugs out. She has not quite
finshed with color correction. It appears the film discolors with age.
Might have something to do with my film developing skills.

In her spare time, she and GSH worked on the '37. She has a lot more
patience than I and is doing a better job.

Meredith gave us a device which will receive and post emailed pictures. She
came and got it into operation today. If you have the interest and want to
send pictures, check with Meredith for details. We do really appreciate
being kept involved.

The sawmill is ready for sawing. It is running in the first picture and the
second is a close up of the moving saw blade.

Not wanting to delay the grand opening, we moved the practice log into
position today. Stay tuned.
