GGC. I am sure the parents and GPs have pictures by the gross. But, I like

At the other end of the life scale, we went to Howard Colson's funeral. It
was perhaps the most meaningful tributes to the life of a good man I have
ever witnessed. I do believe he understood and practiced that Christ came
that we might have life and have it more abundantly.

I missed the AT work day and did not hit a lick on the MM. I did scrape and
sand on the LBT. It is going to require a lot of body work.

Friday I spent all day getting the recurring skin cancer on my head
excised. Maybe this time. Otherwise probably amputation.

Laura left home Friday. However, Banj went right on with his birthday
Saturday. GSH invited him over for B & G. While here, he gave her some
OJTwith the little music device. In looking at the picture, check out
the
GC lineup to make sure yours are not out of date.

I suppose no report is complete w/o a cat report.

On the subject of random events, Wayne came by to harvest firewood.

The big Mulberry tree down in front of the house died. My last experience
with a mulberry was quite painful so I left this one to him.

I look forward to pictorial reports from various grandchildren out doing
exciting things in exotic places.
