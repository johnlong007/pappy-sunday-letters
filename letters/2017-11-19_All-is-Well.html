All is Well

13 photographs·310 words

The deep earth temperature here in ET is about 60 degrees. The design of
this house is such that no matter what the inside temperature has never
gone below 50. However, is Summer campers know, the water storage tank (aka
pool) never gets much above 60.

Plans are being formulated to try to bump the room and the water by at
least 10 degrees with no external sources and to plan ahead for outside
solar collectors to get on up near 80.

A number of items are on the list but making the inside walls into high
absorbing and retention is a key element. I will take another picture on
Dec. 21 st.

This week had the usual trip to help Dave with his car project and the
unusual occurrence of two AT days.

13 of the core group spent another day moving gravel uphill at Carvers Gap.
It was cold wet and windy.

​Carl has scheduled one more trip this coming Friday in case anyone is
interested.
I mentioned Bob had planed a truck load of wood. He donated the sawdust and
I constructed our own compost bin.

​Work continued shucking the hull off black walnuts. Don has a nut cracker
he says works so I am going to try to procure one also.

​Saturday, GSH and I walked our section of the AT to clear a blowdown
blocking access to the water supply at the shelter.

​It was a white pine and easy to cut. Another tree under it was not quite
as easy.

​But we got it ! Cleared the trail and ate lunch before heading out.

​​On the way out we cleared a couple more blowdowns which were more
difficult.

Leaving my helper frazzled by the time we gout out of the woods but with a
mile to go before we rest.
