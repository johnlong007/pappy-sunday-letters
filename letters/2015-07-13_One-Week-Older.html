July 13, 2015 · Monday

One Week Older

7 photographs·216 words

We got back home late last night. This morning when I moved weeks pictures
into PC and tried to edit I ran into a problem Cannot edit until I get some
technical help. So, unedited photos.

I did go to Erwin to check on Herb. He has now finished both Radiation and
chemotherapy. Dr. expects 6 weeks are so to regain strength. In the
meantime low and slow.

We did make a run to Jonesboro co-op to restock his fuel supply.

Tuesday was AT work day.I went with a crew to renovate trail which had slid
downhill. It was three mile walk in and the same distance out.

Back at the ranch, I fiddled with the truck some (still will not start) and
with the Alfa. It needs a rear wheel stud.

Wayne is under contract to line the heat sink with fiberglass. He procured
some bigger, heavier materials to install another test patch.

I did a bunch of mowing and various people harvested various berries.

Saturday morning I went with Sanford to a "cruise in" in Elizabethton. Then
we stopped by Blountville just in time to see a shoot out in the main
street.

Sunday ,we went to Dechart for lunch with LLLL then off to The University
of the South to hear Maria play.
