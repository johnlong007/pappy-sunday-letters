November 22, 2015 · Sunday

Code of the Hills

8 photographs·302 words

Learned something new this week, When the check engine light changes from
steady to blinking it had best not be ignored. At least on a Miata. Had a
code which translated at "random misfire". Drove it home from church using
a 1/4 tank of fuel. The cat converter was very hot.
Wires, plugs and new coil seem to have corrected that problem. Now, it has
an O2 sensor problem. A sign of a cooked cat I believe.A fairly expensive
sequence of events so far. Stay tuned, not through yet.

The vw also has a check engine light which I clear from time to time. It is
always "no. 2 glo plug". Intend to swap it out by and by.

The really big event for the week was going with Herb to DMV to get driving
license . First time he had taken driver exam since 1946. But, he passed.

We went by J & J so GSH could talk with them about upholstery job. It is
going to be expensive. Oh, the freeze plug I cemented in blew out .Really
unhappy with machine shop incompetence. Going to cause me a lot of grief.
Will have to pull engine to replace all of the plugs

Got so wrapped up in car problems did not go for AT work. Should have gone.

But I did go to Erwin to help Herb with his leaf spreading. That was fun. I
want to go back when he fires up the giant roto tiller to chew them up.
Note some of the equipment. A small dump truck, A crawler (of which I am 1/
2 owner), the giant tiller.

Went in to GP for routine check up. At least that went well. Good for
another six months.

Kindled fire in fireplace. First of season. I like it.
