January 8, 2017 · Sunday

Chilly

7 photographs·293 words

The good news is That I can run as fast as I can run without any sign of
light headed, dizzy or tight chest. The bad news is, I cannot run fast at
all.

Katy and Maria came by and gave me encouraging words. Sort of like "for a
far girl you don't sweat much". Anyway, I told LLLL to sign us up for the
5K. I will provide cover for anyone who wants an excuse to go slow.

It was not cold early in the week when I made a run to Erwin. Looks like
Lisa is doing fine but maybe a bit more than her fair share of M & R issues.

Speaking of M & R issues, James sent me a note saying he has located a
house to rent near his new workplace. I think renting for a spell before
buying is a wise decision.

It started getting colder in mid week so Carl moved Thursday AT work to
Wed. I worked with one other guy on top of the ridge just before the Laurel
Gorge shelter. We were making little rocks (obstructing the trail) into
small ones (paving the trail). I shall attach an almost before along with
an after picture. It was cold and windy on the ridge.

I did finish the cross bed beams on the '49. Now I am going to take the bed
off to get it out of the way and start repair work on the cab.

Also got a bit done on the '63. Another 5 or 6 years and it will be ready
to roll out.

Next, we got snow.

All in all, a pretty good week for life in the ole folks home.

Anything going on elsewhere in the world?
