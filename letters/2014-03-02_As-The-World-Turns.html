As The World Turns

8 photographs·524 words

Same ole same ole:

LBT report:

The bottom of the vehicle is mostly surface rusted. A number of places
where salt and water accumulated are rusted through I am attempting to
clean all the surface rust and coat all the rusty surfaced with "rust
reformer". It reacts to make a really hard, black surface. About 1/2 half
of the surface has been prepped and some has been coated. In trying to get
to a very difficult place inside the front fender well I twisted into a
pretzel and came out with a kink in my left side. It has been very painful
for a week now and causes difficulty in working around underneath. But, I
think it is getting better.

The small places which are rusted through are getting a pop riveted patch
on the inside and sealed with (expensive) metallic epoxy on the outer
surface. Then, the entire patch will be finished off with regular body
filler.

After much grief, the brake system has been filled, brakes adjusted and
pumped with no obvious leaks. Cannot really build high pressure until the
lines are bled. Since that is a two person job it is waiting until the
signs are right.

Fortunately for my restricted mobility, Charlie came by Friday. He removed
the drive shaft, cleaned and lubricated then re-installed. Still have to
get a split boot for it.

The original plan was to get this thing in and out during EST. This week
had better go well.

Do Gooder report:

The trail workers went out Thursday. In spite of my vows to not go when the
temp was below 40, I went. It was 18 degrees when we got to the work site
but I was dressed properly and day warmed soon. We went back to
Wataugalake and finished the by-pass trail section. Biggest
job was removal of the large, downed tree.

Today, GSH and I went to visit Bob Peoples. He had colon cancer operation
and is back at his hostel recovering. The hostel is only a few miles
fromthis work site so we stopped by on the way back so
GSH could inspect the work.

Sanford went with me to make a run to Erwin to check on Herb and to install
a starter in the Mack Fire truck. However, my back was such that the
starter project was delayed. Herb looked like has face had been attacking
by the cat which attacked me. He is making progress through his treatment.
About a week to go I believe.

Cat report:

This cat is not a feline mental giant. Laura more or less insisted I get it
a toy to while away any idol hours not spent eyeing birds etc. In spite of
my best efforts, it has shown no aptitude or interest for batting a ball
around in a do-loop.It took a lot of trying to get this picture.

GSH did come to the rescue and refill the pepper dispenser.
