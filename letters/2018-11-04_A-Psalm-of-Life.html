November 4, 2018 · Sunday

A Psalm of Life

14 photographs·808 words

Read it.

Two funerals this week. Both old folks, both having outlived most of their
friends. As most know Ron Stonecipher was a good friend for over 40 years.
Through hime, I got to know his extended family starting with his
grandfather and going through his grandchildren. His family lived in Oneida
where I visited with him many times.
His brother whom I got to know very well, became CEO of Boeing. The funeral
this time was the father in law of his brother's wife.102 years old. The
person in black suit is Mike Stonecipher, Ron's nephew.

Bruce Cuningham was the comptroller at ECC for many years. I worked with
Bruce a bit at ECC and a lot on the AT. Bruce died a few months back. His
wife, Mary was a bundle of energy being not only involved in everything but
in a leadership position. She died at 92. The last time I worked with
Bruce, we worked on the Curly Maple shelter and walked out down a difficult
trail along Martin's Creek. (it comes out of the mountain very near where
Aunt Flo lives (Beside Martin Creek School). He was about 85 then and so
tottering he fell four times and could not get up without assistance.
The last time I worked on the AT with Mary she was also tottering, must
have been well past 80, maybe 85 also. She had her own Female size digging
tool which was worn to a nub. She could not bend over and dig but scooted
along on her backside but dig she did.
Remarkable people whom I am proud and humbled to have known with whom I
worked.
The community band played at Mary's service. Their first piece was very
good and the second at the end was exceptional! Really glad we went wish it
had been recorded.

Equally important, we went to see David play soccer. I know almost nothing
about soccer. First game I ever watched I think. Our family was well
represented and I am glad we went, well worth the trouble.

Some will remember Sue Ketron. Hal died. Sue always made Christmas goodies
that Hal, her brother Henry (who has also died) and I really liked. She
sent me a note and said "come and get em". We did. Not sure what is more
feel good, eating the goodies or being remembered. It reminds me of how
important friends are.

For those who think I make GSH work to hard, here is the pampered chief.

We came in about dusk tonight to find the front field contained a heard of
deer. I took several pictures which turned out poorly as tey wander across
the drive. Then I turned light of high beam and got one good picture. Truly
a deer in the headlights. Is shooting in your own driveway legal? They were
still there when we drove out again an hour later.What would Ross do?

The mornings are getting cooler and I am going outside later. This week, I
finished installing all the brake hardware on the '63. Next will be adding
fluid and trying to purge the system and adjust the shoes. Stay tuned.
Another exciting event was finally getting the floor mopping done. The
pictured mop cart is one of the best purchases I ever made. All the male
spouses can take heed. Maybe hint for a Christmas present.

Did I mention progress on the mower last week? I think so. It is sanded,
painted with rust reactor and ready to paint. Just not quite sure if I want
to paint it and if so what color. Still have not seen Albert to pay him.
Reckon he has missed his tools yet?

Parts car progress was limited to removing the gas tank and unloading the
car from the dolly. I was quite pleased with myself for getting the tank
off without spilling a drop. Stale gas sting awful and lasts a long ,long
time. Then I dragged the tank on an angle beside the drive, the seam was
rusted and out came a lot of gas, odor and all. The tank must have 8-10
gallons left inside.

Fall is here, Hard to believe anyone would want to live anywhere else.

We wandered ( We, GSH, the cat and I), around the place. My mother often
said my father could not walk across a field without picking up any rock he
passed. GSH is that way on sticks and limbs.

Last years pine needles are falling

Speaking of not living anywhere else, the property next door to our old
place on Shadowtown Road is on the market. 20 acres. I think it is a
bargain.
