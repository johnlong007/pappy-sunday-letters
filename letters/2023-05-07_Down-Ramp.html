Down Ramp

11 photographs·542 words

Pancytopenia.

I Visited our family GP who offered his opinion on the cause of my severe
loss of endurance. Now he is turning over to the blood person who will be
in contact to make an appointment. Niece Lisa has had a lot of
experience with the medical profession. She says they
respond inversely with patents age. Who was it said to give granny an
aspirin and send her home? Stay tuned.

For us, the good news is family and friends are great. All different but
all great.

Most will remember Bob and Anna Sherrill. Bob came by this week to bring me
up to date and possibly to pick up some helpful hints on dealing with
spouses memory issues.

We had a ramp fest in honor of LLLL being here. A ramp fest here is a "you
snooze you lose event".

Note the platter has been licked clean. Right after bfst, Meredith and LLLL
headed to the hills to the secret ramp garden. .

I have not been on a ramp harvest trip for two years. This past year I have
become increasingly concerned that I could actually make it out and back.
Fortunately Wayne was willing to share the risk. We came back with a full
pack.

Meredith told her Mom how tasty grilled ramps are. Tonight, Laura left
(after dinner and a show0 with a big bundle of ready to grill ramps. Stay
tuned.

A new nurse showed up for a routine visit Wed. I got so excited when I saw
her vehicle I grabbed a device to take a poorly aimed and executed
picture.A very interesting person. If I was in the market for a new truck I
would look at one like hers.

The machine shop which has the '37 engine notified Wayne that the cylinder
head is not safe to reuse. Wayne came over and took the head off one of our
spare engines. I cleaned it up a bit.
As usual, Sanford came by on his way home from the Burger Bar with
breakfast for us. I was asking him about the name of the machine shop when
he announced he would take the head to them "right now".
He did.

Converting Albert's down Pine tree into a Rachel mantle continued. Once
more, Wayne chipped in to do some of the heavy lifting.

Bring up your children in the way they should go.

PBBD has arranged a cleaning crew to come in from time to time. We got
back from the track one day before the crew was finished and the floor was
dry. Per instructions, I stopped at Pals for 3 Pal Jr's. We then had a
picnic in the shop. I think those two are laying out the plan to go visit
Aaron.

Hard to believe, Aaron had a birthday. 41 ! Our oldest grandchild. I saw
our youngest grandchild, Thomas, last week . He had his head peeled and
said he is enlisted in the Air Force. I am delighted. God and Country are
still important to some.

It seems that Catherine is training our dog to pre-wash the dishes.
