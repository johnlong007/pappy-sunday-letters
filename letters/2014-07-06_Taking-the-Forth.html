July 6, 2014 · Sunday

Taking the Forth

12 photographs·268 words

A good family week.

>From time to time someone will tell me something like they know I am proud
of our children. I do not much care for that word but there are many things
about our family which do indeed please me.

Way up on the list is the way we jump in to help each other.

​Another is family get togethers.

I am not sure why, but the Blountville parade was mush larger than I have
seen before.

A lot of people, a lot of flags., A lot of cars and tractors plus a few
animals.

​
​After the parade, we went to Erwin to check on my brother (and his BB's).

Both were fine.

Earlier in the week, we had another work day on the AT. The crew we had in
from trail days had dug a section improperly. The workers had dug down to
solid ground but had dragged dirt across and covered the organic material
on 1/2 the base.
We are having to go along, dig that part out and make the trail wider and
deeper all down on solid ground.. Should be able to see it all in this
picture.

Wayne asked me about the raspberry crop and I promised to check. One
evening I looked out and discovered why no berries are getting ripe.

​
Bob has found a person who is interested in making the '63 back almost like
new.

Stay tuned.

Softball season is upon us.

​
​

GSH has started a new project to renovate three silk purses.​

​

​Anyone else have a celebration?
