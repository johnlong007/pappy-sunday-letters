Doral Buchanan - ERWIN
ERWIN — Doral Buchanan, age 75, 330 Smith Street, Erwin, died Wednesday,
June 19, 2008, at the Johnson City Medical Center.

A Beans Creek, North Carolina Native, he was the son of the late Hubert and
Birdie Stockton Buchanan. He served during the Korean conflict in the United
States Army. He then worked as a plumber at Eastman.

He was preceded in death by his wife, Dorothy; daughter, Brenda Sue
Buchanan; and sisters, Betty Campbell and Cora Snyder.

He is survived by: Sons: Michael Wayne Buchanan, Greeneville, Doral Buchanan
Jr., Erwin, Jeffrey Buchanan, Johnson City, William Randall Buchanan,
Bristol, William Doral Buchanan, Erwin; Daughters: Linda Sue Richardson, Erwin,
Doris Kay Lewis, Erwin, Wilma Tate, Erwin, Melissa Ann Woodfin, Elizabethton,
Charlotte Buchanan, Erwin, Diane Buchanan, Erwin, Melinda Ann Buchanan, Bluff
City, Rita Arechiga, Houston, TX; Brothers: Larry E. Buchanan, Erwin, Frank
Buchanan, Erwin, Haynes Buchanan, Erwin; Sisters: Jessie Buchanan, New York,
Shirley Harris, North Carolina, Jean Harmon, Erwin.

Graveside services will be held on Monday, June 23, 2008, at one o’clock at
Beans Creek Cemetery in Beans Creek, North Carolina, with the Reverend Donald
Seekins officiating. Visitation will be held from ten o’clock until twelve o’
clock, Monday, June 23, 2008, at Valley Funeral Home. Please meet at the
cemetery at 12:50 for the service. Pallbearers will be members of the family.

Other visitation with the family will be at the home of Diane Buchanan, 111
Bumpus Cove Road.

Condolences and memories may be shared with the family and viewed by
visiting our Web site at www.valleyfuneralhome.

These arrangements are made especially for the family and friends of Doral
Buchanan through Valley Funeral Home, 1085 N. Main Ave., Erwin, TN.
