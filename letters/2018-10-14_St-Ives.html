October 14, 2018 · Sunday

St. Ives

13 photographs·347 words

Sunday PM GSH and I headed to Dechard to pick up LLLL on our way to Union
City to see my aged and somewhat decripit Lil' Sister.

We had a fine , brief (that helps make it fine) visit. Not nearly as broke
down and stove up as I feared. I did my best to persuade her to press on
with knee replacement. However, she expressed fear she would not get her
moneys worth of usage. I know the feeling.

Back through Dechard. Dale and Linda have an animal kingdom.

AT work was on Monday so I missed out. Back to life on the farm. Decided to
give the Bobcat slow leak tire another go, Here trying to polish the inside
of the wheel shoulder. Mud and grit had caused it to rust and pit. I slowed
the leak but did not eliminate. Stay tuned.

Then finished pulling brake wheel cylinders from the '63. It appears the
rear wheel were doing no braking.

I purchased some farm equipment from neighbor. His brother had died and
neighbor collected the tools. The only part I wanted was a rotary mower.
However, it has been left outside for years with the drive shaft dangling
such that the sliding sleeves stayed full of water. So far, my efforts to
free the frozen parts have failed. Stay tuned.

The rest of the story.

When the grasshopper come by this Winter complaining about no firewood. I
am going to tell them too bad.

George came by to try out a new toy. Wonder if Google's algorithm can pick
that up?

Many years ago, I went with Laura and GSH to procure a spare car a bit
North of Chicago. Here it is. It may live again.

On the way home from a roof inspection trip forLisa in Erwin, I spotted
some street walkers which GSH asked to join.

Did anyone notice what cat was watching last week?
