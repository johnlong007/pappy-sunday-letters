June 12, 2011 · Sunday

Lone Ranger

9 photographs·299 words

I doubt anyone is very interested in what I did this week. Actually it is a
lot like what I did last week except with no adult supervision.

Some person or persons may be surprised to learn I put new tires on the
Miata. Actually it was forced on me by parking a sharp relatively immovable
object too close to the driveway.

In my best reenactment of "Shane" (minus the repressed love interest) I
finally finished splitting the log! Now, the question is either ,So what ? or
Now what? Actually , I think it would make a couple of really impressive
dug out canoes. Perhaps a bit like Noah's ark since the pond is long gone.

A lot of the middle of the week involved mowing. Meredith will be glad to
know I have mowed all around the raspberries (looks like a fine crop) and
did not stir up any bees. I did not take any pictures but both dogs went
along to help me. Actually, they pretty much helped me all week. Part of the
week was continuing garden prepping.

A major project was lawn prepping for D & D. After having used Laura's
tiller for one day I decided to bring in the big guns. It went rather well.
Now, it is up to the seeders and the weather.

Tuesday's AT work day was on Friday this week. I went with two other
willing workers from Moreland Gap to the Bitter End. We cleared a major
obstruction near the water supply at the shelter and 39 blow downs on the five mile
hike out. Before and after pictures to follow.

Some be surprised to know the lonesome travelers decided to come home !

At home, things quickly returned to normal.

Our belated 56 anniversary celebration !
