De coding

0 photographs·101 words

It is common for one of my fellow AT workers to say something along the
line of : "you guys go ahead and cut that tree, I will stay here and fluff
the cat".

As I best I can tell literally "fluffing the cat" is considered to be near
the bottom of the list for meaningful endeavors.

In looking at my last week endeavors it was not easy to find any which
lowered the entropy of the universe. Hence, "fluffing the cat".

By the way, I have asked Meredith to take, and post, a picture of her
father fluffing their cat.
