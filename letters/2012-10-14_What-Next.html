October 14, 2012 · Sunday

What Next

13 photographs·285 words

Last week I included a picture of a new section of AT trail I had roughed
out about 75' long. This week I intend to include a picture of the
replacement trail Carl and Ed decided would be better located. Note that
"my" trail is covered with leaves and brush this week.
The good news is that the replacement trail encountered two quite large
rocks, each about 2' thick and 3' wide. In both cases we were able to split
the rocks into three sections making nice, flat steps. I do indeed have a
hard hat. But, was not doing any work in the picture shown.

Since Ron, Gene and Hal died I have slowed my Wed. visiting. But, this week
I went to visit Sue. She is doing well. We got all caught up on news,
rumors and gossip.

Almost as exciting was getting the upper garden seeded. Still have to do
the lower as soon as the peanuts and sweet potatoes are harvested. We did
get one row of peanuts harvested.
John, Benj and Thomas did the work. A quite good crop, not sure yet what to
do with them. Also one potential problem in that Thomas ate one and
announced it "does not taste like a peanut".

Saturday was apple butter day.

Lit the fire at 7:30 and brought the kettle off the fire at 7:30. Made 11
gallons. All the jars (quarts) sealed. Not sure how GSH plans to
distribute. No one liked my system last year so I am hunkering down.

During the slow part of the day, a hot pepper tasting was offered. I
believe John has the potential to be a serious contender.

The cartoon is for Katy.
