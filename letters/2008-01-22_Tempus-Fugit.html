January 22, 2008 · Tuesday

Tempus Fugit

8 photographs·202 words

Due to predicted bad weather, we did not do AT work Tuesday. So, I went with
a friend to see the evacuation of a farm which had been in the family since
1791. Interesting but sad, it had been suffering from neglect for a lot of
years.
The Alfa is finally back in operation ! When I can get stuff moved out of
the way I will get it out for a trial run. I do believe that was the longest
journey I have made through the "swamp". The problem was the clutch, not the
transmission. Charlie did most of the work but I did ring in a helper with "wee
paws" to adjust the clutch travel.
I had a two cake birthday.
The Friday hike was to a waterfall down near Greenville. Actually, on the
far side of the mountains from Higgins' creek.
A Hawk crashed into the window and died. I think a "sharp-shinned" which
feed on small birds.
Work continues at Blountville annex.
Herb gave us some real tomatoes. Also, I stopped at the Mennonite store and
purchased some Lebanon Bologna for Pat. Thus, the sandwich picture.
