November 11, 2007 · Sunday

Another Week, No Dollar

7 photographs·236 words

The Monday do-gooder work did not go well. We tried and failed, to install a
basketball post/backboard assembly. Too heavy and too high. Then, I found the
car tire had a screw in it. A souvenir from the Blountville parking area I
expect. However, I did get new brake pads on the Miata.
Tuesday went better, worked on the AT. It was cold and windy, never got
above freezing. My nose dribbled so much I made it sore wiping it. We did get a
lot of work done.
Wed. we had a special edition of do-gooder work which went much better.
Thursday I went to visit Gene Rutherford first thing in the morning. He is
doing well but not feeling very well. In the afternoon, I helped do-gooder
buddy Dave Dunham but rear brake pads on his truck. That project went well.
For supper, we had the last BLTC sandwich of the season.
Friday the Friday hikers visited the Gray fossil site. Then I went to Erwin.
After getting home I amputated the nose on the spare Fiat so I could get the
car out of the way and make room for the Alfa. Sunday PM I retrieved the
Alfa from Wayne's house. It will not engage in any gear. I fear an internal
problem in the transmission. Wish I had Charlie here to swap the transmission out
for me.
