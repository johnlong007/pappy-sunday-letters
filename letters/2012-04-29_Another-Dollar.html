April 29, 2012 · Sunday

Another Dollar

9 photographs·457 words

It was cold for the first part of part of this week. So, I stayed inside
and worked (fiddle and fumbled) on the '37. Aaron says he wants me to work
with him on one or more automotive projects by and by. I wonder if he would
still think that if he had to watch my working. Anyway, the fuel tank is
back in place with a line more or less up to the fuel pump.

Then decided to check the fuel pump and the carburetor before going
further. I am in hopes some competent person will offer to rebuild both. I
have a kit for the fuel pump.

I did pick up the rest of the truck glass. So, now all we need is
installation. The door window glass will be quite a challenge I expect.
Windshield and back window should be simple. Although, the windshield is
hinged and the top to allow it to swing out a few inches.

Would anyone like to see the result of tripping while closing the sliding
door on the shop and falling backward into the scrap metal pile?

Some time back, Herb gave me some parachute cord. It is a very heavy duty
woven material, nylon I suppose. I cut off a section about 1' long which
GSH made into a pocket and inserted a squeaky. It has held out for almost a
full week and is still alive.

Dave is incapacitated and AT work was on Saturday so I could not go.
Because ; I did the registration desk for the Gray Old Tractor Owners
annual event. I will attach a picture from my work station for Thursday,
Friday and Saturday.

We had a lot of tractors and even more old engines than usual. Anyone
remember the old feed grinder which I had stored in the barn? I let Don
Haas have it with the provision he would put it to good use. He did.

For several years, Daddy had a corn mill set up in our barn. It was used to
make meal for cornbread. Some years back, I bought a mill of the same kind,
a Meadows. A person had one at the show in operation. He was selling little
bags of corn meal. I had Daddy sharpen and adjust the stones in my mill but
it has never been put back into use.

I did purchase a new tire for the lawn mower from a vendor. About 1/3 what
id would have cost on Amazon.

I am going to attach one more picture to see if anyone can deduce the
connection.

Not as much in the way of adventure, mystery and romance as many of the
family members but after all, we live in Blountville.
