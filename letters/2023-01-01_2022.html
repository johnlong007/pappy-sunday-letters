January 1, 2023 · Sunday

2022

11 photographs·290 words

Car about 7K miles
truck about 600
tractor 25 hours
Bobcat 30 hours

Net worth, down 25 %, thanks Joe

Went in to see our GP. mostly worked on my damaged foot. I think it is
getting better,

The home health case worker gave me a reality check. I think what I heard
is I should get a person to come relieve me a few hours each day. Look to
Family first then church and friends.

We did a little work on the 55 this week and quite a bit on shop clean up
with much more to come. This picture is the inner tube inventory. Now
tested, deflated, wrapped and packed. Pearls of great price perhaps.

Dog is doing well. The deer herd was grazing out front, dog watched a while
before deciding to try to harvest one. She did send them leaping and
bounding.

Never let a ww go to waste

It appears the deer herd recovered from being scattered.

A somewhat delayed family Christmas get together was held here with family
from far and wide,

I did not keep count or check the roll.

At least one member here from a foreign country.

I tried really hard to get Charlie to put tow bar on the truck, hook to the
fire engine and take it back home with him.

We made track outings a few time.

Dr, is getting practice for making house calls.

The 63 belongs to Laura. It is tagged and insured. I made my best shot at
getting it out and sending it home with Benj. No need for a tow bar.
