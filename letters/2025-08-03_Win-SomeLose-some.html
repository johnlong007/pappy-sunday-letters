August 3, 2025 · Sunday

Win Some/Lose some

15 photographs·440 words

All those years of studying heat transfer finally paid off ! When I decided
to convert the water temperature stabilization unit into a swimming pool, I
read about pool temperature and found 80 or over to be the target. On that
basis, I made my calculation on what insulation would be required.

Diane has done an excellent job getting the plumbing , filtration and
chemical control system in operation. Parents are bringing themselves and
their children from miles around.

I think I have passed 100 Mondays in a row at the cancer center. Jonathan
is the nurse and Diane brought me a ham biscuit from Bojangles.

I am sure everyone has been waiting for the news ! The second David Bradley
(Sears) mowing machine is on display !

A new name is written up in glory ,and it's Buchanan.

Many years ago I went to the evening school at the vocational school taking
every welding class and developing adequate skills. I then procured various
pieces of welding equipment including an AC/DC stick welder which Dwight
adapted for TIG welding. Pat is on the Food Country truck race team. Friday
morning on his way to the shop, he stopped by to show Diane how to rig the
unit for TIG welding.

We had a couple of downpours. The roof leaked . Diane called the people who
have been doing our roof M & R. A couple of workers stopped by, Said they
are pretty sure they found and fixed the problem. Said no charge and left.

After the first downpour, I scraped the drive. That made it much worse when
the second downpour hit . However, I still had the scraper on the tractor
and am now ready for the next rain.

We split wood. Wayne split wood, Andrew split wood. There is still a lot of
wood to be split.

Wayne has been fretting about moving the last Walnut log to the sawmill.
Mother nature has stepped in, looks like he can float it to the mill.

The call of the pool. With free lunch, at least no charge.

I have been whimpering about needing the next project. Farmer Lisa has
leapt into action and found the one horse turn plow my father procured when
he purchased our horse Prince, in 1947.
I have not been able to implement a procurement plan.

Gwendolyn, Steven, others, miss being here?

Diane is working to teach Blackbeard to eat out of her hand.
