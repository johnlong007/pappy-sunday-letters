January 31, 2016 · Sunday

Wobegon

5 photographs·198 words

A quiet week.

Made the trip to Erwin a few times. Lisa is trying to ease back on various
medicines so Herb will be more alert and more mobile. Stay tuned.

We were mostly snowed in so we worked on the '63. I scraped and wire
brushed underneath while GSH worked on interior panels. It would have been
smarter of me to high pressure wash underneath before bringing into the
shop. 50 years of dust, some rust and peeling undercoating falling on, over
,around and through are not pleasant.

We did go to Elizabethton to check on Studebaker interior progress. Zero.
All talk, no action.

No AT work, too much snow.

Wayne came by to help check out the tractor fuel problem. Once more, stay
tuned.

The VW is in the shop. It appears that the brakes and drive train are going
to be replaced (again).

I /we have been walking on the treadmill because of the mud and snow. I
have been able to increase speed and distance. Today we went to the track
and I did 13 laps in 43 minutes. Awful but better than it was a couple of
weeks back.

Anything going on elsewhere?
