June 23, 2019 · Sunday

Summer

10 photographs·416 words

We have been inside the Arctic circle three times on June 21. A couple of
us on Dec 21st. A couple inside Antarctica circle on Dec 21st. I am very
glad we never put things off. Still have a couple more items on to do list.
Stay tuned.

For all the Sun lovers, one of the places Dr. Sago zapped last trip came
back at increased vigor. This trip she grubbed it. Zapped a few more while
I was there.

The good news is I finally got new specs. Bad news is I still cannot see to
suit me. Is everyone (anyone ?) familiar with "The Deacon's Masterpiece" ?
I am beginning to think that may be me when every part quits on the same
day.

GSH's sister Nancy came by for a visit. Nancy has had a soap opera life and
passed it on to some of her children. But, they had a fine visit as best I
could tell.

Changing generations, Chelsea was in visiting her M-I-L and some of us
gathered to admire her and/or the children. Perhaps both.
It was a fun visit but left me boggled. While we were there, she was
talking, eyeball to eyeball, with Aaron.

Almost as exciting in a very different way, Sarah began her adult life last
Monday. The life in which one has more money but less free time to spend it.

Back at the home place, we spent time and effort, but no money, cleaning
up the pool room. The workers left a lot of dirt, dust and cement residue.
The filter system is going to require new plumbing and new management.

I believe we should fill with water and get salt system in operation before
starting tile project.

Wayne has been working to take care of some serious maintenance issues with
the place. One is the drainage system all around but in particular in back.
The drain lines were installed many years back but have become somewhat
non-functional. After locating the lines, he now has them draining OK. Not
quite sure what to do about leaves plugging the gutters.

My intent is to scope out the soccer field and rebuild the pond as a catch
basin to reduce flood damage down walnut field. Stay tuned.

Work on the 63 continued. Masking for primer is almost complete. Should be
ready by the long weekend.
