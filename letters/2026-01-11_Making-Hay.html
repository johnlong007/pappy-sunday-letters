January 11, 2026 · Sunday

Making Hay

12 photographs·197 words

Work continued on next winter's firewood.

Work also continued on teaching my resident willing worker how to keep the
home fires burning. She is now able to sharpen a chain saw to cut on the
straight and narrow.

So far, she has not been willing to go with the gas powered saw. Her saw is
coal powered via TVA.

A lot of wood ready for splitters to split. Ash is tough, beech tougher.

A & C are starting new careers in forestry money making skills.

More artifacts for the display. Dave Edwards donated the swing scythe. The
long handle hand scythe is the one Jimmy used, the other I used. Herb used
the bigger hay scythe. We mowed the cow pasture each week.

The beech tree removal left a lot of large limbs. Wayne and Diane harvested
and put into truck bed for the convenience of any visiting WWs.

Curious onlookers.

Meredith and her urchins are a delight.

So far, I have resisted telling her that they will grow up and be teenagers.
