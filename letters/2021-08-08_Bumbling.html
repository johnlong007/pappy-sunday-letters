August 8, 2021 · Sunday

Bumbling

13 photographs·406 words

Alfa front wheel brake calipers. These were removed from one of the two
wrecked Alfas we parted out.

Willing worker/junior mechanic working under close supervision to
recondition salvaged brake caliper.

Still somewhat willing worker under master mechanic supervision installing
caliper on '81 Alfa.

Moving right along proving once more "bring up a child ".

Doing my best to provide opportunity for hand flexibility for rehabbing,
mechanic helper was called into action to rebuild the brush guard which was
knocked asunder.

My always willing helper waiting patiently for return to duty. I am glad to
report that for now, all the mowing is caught up. String trimmer, push
mower. rider and bush hog, all called into action.

Wayne has spent a good bit of time and energy working on the old pu truck.
He stopped by to give it one more try and while here he decided the air
hose on reel needed replacement.

A major milestone in child rearing is when you find observe your child
seeing something which needs done and doing what is needed without being
directed. A red letter day !

I believe he has made it.

One of my least favorite jobs is mowing the steep hill behind the house
with the string trimmer. It makes my knee hurt. Faced up to the task this
week and got it mowed. About 1/2 with the rider and the rest with string
trimmer. The hill is not a problem for the rider but the trees are. Mower
has to be kept up or down, sideways is a problem. I have tried that.

We usually have a light lunch. often on the deck. Ham salad and farmer Lisa
donated tomatoes. I did go over and get her lawnmower running so a case of
win win.

One night was dining out and nature viewing.

I let the pepper patch grow up in grass hoping the wildlife would not find
the plants. It did not work.I am pretty sure it is the deer but might be
groundhogs. I tried to get PBBD to bring her groundhog dog over to have a
go but she was not receptive to the idea. The cat was on the trail sniffing
all around.

A big event was sampling practice wedding cake(s). All variations were
excellent.
