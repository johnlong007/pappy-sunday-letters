September 14, 2008 · Sunday

Ho Hum

7 photographs·232 words

I had a place on my back which has been bothering me. So, I went in to get
it checked. Not a skin cancer. He told me I am getting old and can expect aches
and pains.
No Do-Gooder work or AT work this week. I did get all caught up on mowing
the place. If anyone wants to collect fruit and/or nuts, the signs are right.
Walnuts, black, white and English.
Paw Paws and Persimmons.
Chestnuts and Chinquapins.
Carrots and beets.
Apples.
All free to good home.

We went on the Friday hike. Plenty of huckleberries but no external
container.

We talked to a contractor. He is going to replace the shingles, repair roof
as required, replace all skylights, vertical fixed glass and repair or replace
the siding as required.
Should get started in about two weeks.

Our local carpenter decided to modify a cabinet to fit into the corner in
the utility room. The room has a projection from cabinet she installed in the
kitchen. For her, a house is not a home it is an opportunity for overlapping,
unending construction projects.

Last week I mentioned we have deer. So, this week I will give the turkeys
equal billing. They like to eat the seeds from the tall fesque.
