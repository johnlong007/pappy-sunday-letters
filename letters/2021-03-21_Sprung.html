March 21, 2021 · Sunday

Sprung

9 photographs·306 words

Remember the picture of Bill Hughes on his Whizzer? Soon after the picture
was taken Bill wrecked, totalled his Whizzer and broke his back. Spent
months flat on his back in a cast.

Made a run to see Farmer Lisa this week. She wanted a spot prepped for a
flower bed and another for a garden. Should make the deer and the Musk
Rats happy.

Herb had a very nice car he had purchased from the widow of Dr. Monroe. As
best as I can tell,the car had a carburetor fire. I plan to let
bringing this car back to life be a Summer project.

The crawler has four Glo Plugs which are needed for cold starting. Part of
the checking procedure is making sure each plug is pulling the correct
number of Amps. So, PBBD ordered a clamp on ammeter. Here, under close
supervision she is making a practice run.

After all the working, we did get the crawler running.

We gathered another load of brush and took to brush pond. My willing
workers went along for the ride.

After a long wait while all the involved parties became comfortable with
virus control procedures, we went to LLLL's then on to see Sis. Friday
evening we checked her class room. I was very impressed at apparent order
and cleanliness of the school facilities. Appeared as tidy as a hospital.

Down and back to Union City on Saturday. Martha brought in lunch for us.
Seems that she is very wary of the virus.

My sister is amazing. At 91 she still stands straight and steps smartly.
She had been involved in an auto accident causing her quite a bit of pain
causing her car to be considered totalled.
