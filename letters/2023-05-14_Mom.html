May 14, 2023 · Sunday

Mom

15 photographs·341 words

I decided to go ahead and prep the ramps we dug last Saturday.I expect this
is the crop for this year. I suggest hitting Laura up if you want a final
ramp fest.

PBBD went out to check the tomato patch. A critter had dug up one of the
plants. The Big yellow one selected just for GSH.

She replanted it and we put a metal fence around the patch under the
belief it was a groundhog which had gone under our mesh fence. Katy was
here today and allowed the plant looks like it will survive .

I mowed the retaining basin we have been working on for years. The plan is
to catch the runoff then meter it out at a controlled rate to avoid erosion
through the walnut field. We also intend to not let water stand over the
grass for any extended time., hench the siphon system.

The machine shop reported the replacement head looked good but the original
block was not sound. Wayne told them we would try again. He and PBBD fished
the engine out of the car shed and began disassembly.

So far, progress has been slow and difficult. Three bolts twisted off in
the block. Stay tuned.

Dirty work if you can get it.

Farmer Niece Lisa's daughter graduated from the tech school with an
associate's degree. Going onward and upward to ETSU next. Boy friend tagged
along to the event. He is still enrolled in something business. I told him
"Take welding!". he listened and laughed.

Ella is working at the scott strawberry farm. Through her, we got
strawberries and ice cream

FYI

We had visitors this PM. Anita brought her Mom a potted plant. Made me
remember my mother giving her a potted plant. We had a fine visit. I amused
the baby and our pond frog amused the Long children.
