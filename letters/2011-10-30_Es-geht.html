October 30, 2011 · Sunday

Es geht

7 photographs·604 words

If you are waiting for my report on events in ET to brighten up your day,
you are in a heap of trouble!

Which do you want first Good news ? Bad news?

On a good note, Janet and Allison, the Bama duo were here for Fall break
and even without me having to bribe them requested B & G. While they were
here they were invited to admire the table and benches.

A progress report on the table and benches. No further work on the table
this week but it is going to get further attention. The legs were installed
on the benches and finitial sanding on the bench seats completed.

For comparison, Dwight came over and we started the Studebaker. The power
steering rack leaked-a lot. It is going to require removal and rebuilding
if a rebuild kit can be located. Rats.

AT work was at Carvers Gap again this week. The trail has been moved from
straight up the hill over into a route through the woods leading up the
hill. The relocation was to protect endangered species in the open field
and to give the eroding trail a chance to heal. To discourage people from
leaving the new trail and crossing the field, we erected a six strand
barbed wire fence. As you might guess. people still cross the fence and
cross the field. Even worse from my view, they do not know how to cross a
fence without damaging the fence. So, I spent the day working with a couple
of helpers in restoring the fence. It was a perfect day in a lovely
location and we did a good job. All the time knowing that very soon it will
all be for naught.

Larry and Sharon Miller stopped by. Larry had a couple of new handguns he
wanted to try. One a 500 cal Magnum and one a 460 cal Magnum.

While Dwight was here, he pondered the electrical problems on the "new"
mower. Headlight, fuel gage and fuel shut off solenoid to not work. Hard to
believe, but he did not resolve any of those problems. However, the only
one which has a practical impact was the inoperable glo plug pre heating.
Turns out that was an operator problem which we will not discuss further.

Way back when, GSH planted the sprouting tip of a donated (from Herb) sweet
potato in her flower bed. With frost predicted, she harvested her sweet
potato crop. A bumper crop at that !

To keep my record intact for the week, I took the mower up on the hill to
finish mowing under the pine trees (all the old time Summer Camp kids will
remember the pine trees, right?). Anyway, the mower does great and I got
about 1/2 done when the steering cylinder tie rod end fell apart. It
appears the previous owner had left out a cotter pin when assembling the
steering,the nut backed off letting the end come loose . Of course,the
movement damaged the rod end such that it will have to be replaced. In the
meantime, I cobbled it together and hope it will hold to allow me to finish
the mowing.

We have dogwood trees and Persimmon trees behind the house, one evening,
there four Pilated Woodpeckers in the dogwood trees eating berries. I tried
to take pictures but none came out well, So, use your imagination.

Overall, it seems GSH had a more productive and socially significant week
than I did.

I did get reports form hither but not yon.
