February 26, 2012 · Sunday

My world

9 photographs·359 words

Hard to believe that we have Grandchildren scattered all over the world.

Where did this week go ?

On the third visit, we got the Ford 8N tractor going. Anyone want a good deal on a tractor let me know.

I got the floor of the '37 patched (with the metal epoxy Bob ordered) painted and ready to start putting this vehicle back together !

A good outing on the AT. We continued digging a relocation at Hughes Gap. It was cold and windy with some snow flurries.We got out about 3PM just before the rain hit. The person in blue is Mary Cuningham. She is well past 80.

Working with GSH the LBT is now stored in the Ag building. It was a major task. I towed it to the door with the tractor. Then use the wench (and the winch) to drag the rear end around and into the building. Then use the wench to drag rear end around again so it is parked inside pointed just like it was outside the door. While working to move the LBT I took my gloves off and dropped on floor. The next thing I saw was the little dog dashing across the field with one of my gloves. Far and away the maddest I have been at that dog. GSH retrieved the glove before it was seriously damaged.

A big adventure was chainsaw re certification on Saturday. A brief class session then all day outside in the cold wind at Davis Springs. Each person has to demonstrate all the skills. Felling, trimming and bucking. We had a very good instructor. I learned a lot especially about using wedges to control falling direction and to avoid pinching the saw.

Wayne came over with the 16 inch saw. We learned that one cannot cut all the way through a 36 inch log with a 16 inch saw. We also learned that it is tough work running a crosscut saw through green oak.

Have I mentioned that we signed up to go on this years bike ride with the (Miller) group we have ridden with several times before. Late August in Bavaria.
