January 27, 2019 · Sunday

13 photographs·563 words

Our 6th. child was born when we were 30. The times they are a changing.

I think one of the smartest things I ever did was Summer Camp. Among other
things, I taught a bunch of kids how to split wood (not like in the movies
where they frail at it, makes me wince). So, why am I out here in the cold,
by myself. splitting wood?

A highlight for the week (no pictures) was Meredith coming by (with Conor)
to borrow the little black car (Miata) in order to teach Conor how to
really drive. I am not sure if I mentioned to him that it only took Rachel
30 minutes or so to master the art .

The ban on AT work has been lifted. Carl has asked that each person with an
assigned section inspect and maintain as soon as possible. North bounders
will be coming soon. Therefore I would like to solicit one, 2 or more
willing workers to go for a refreshing walk in the woods this coming
Saturday.

Max has livened this place up this week. He is fitting in quite rapidly. GSH
was worried about him getting cold. So, I found a ski face mask, cut open
sown top and made him a suit. (My specialty, make do and get by.)

It makes him a lot easier to spot when He goes ripping over the hill to
check for stray cats.

Remember when we first started planning to get a new roof put on the bark
and annex?

This week the roofers came and did the work. Total roofing of car storage
building and barn was about 10K. Sure hope it turns out to be a worthwhile
expenditure.

Notice the walnut tree cut off even with shed above the Bobcat. With some
trepidation, and against my better judgment I climbed the ladder with
chainsaw and notched and sawed into the trunk. GSH was too busy wringing
her hands to take a picture for me.

I had chains hooked to trunk above cut and pulled with tractor to make it
go where I wanted. She did get a picture just as it started. Who says ole
folks can't have fun.

Not much work on the '63 this week. But, I believe it is about ready for a
coat of primer. We worked on final prepping.

GSH and I have never worked very well on projects. We are too different,
she wants to do it "right", I want to do it. But, I decided it was time to
try again and got Anita to help me move an old treadle Singer sewing
machine out into the shop. For the first time in my life I find myself
thinking it is too cold to go outside and work.

So far I am trying to stick with sanding rather than paint strippers.

as all know we, have family scattered all over. I do my best to keep up
with all of them. Some make it easy:

We got in our track time every day this week. Coldest day was about 20.
Worst was one day with 20 MPH wind and 25 degree temp. I am afraid to stop
as may not get going again.
