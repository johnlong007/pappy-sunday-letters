a Night light is on order.

critters are taking the place !

I came up with a fool proof plan. Got rid of the string and replaced it
with a fine wire. Put a lid over the feeder. Rodents will slide down the
wire over the edge of the lid and hit the ground running ! Nothing can go
wrong !

Something went wrong. A sonic devic is now on order. Stay tuned.

We sat outside for a spell after track time today. Many species of birds.

Smal rodents heard the cat is gone.

Signs are that it will be an abundant Paw Paw season.

A lot of construction work is underway at the Blountville site. We have
noted many more bike riders on the walking track. My guess this mountain is
to soften the wreck should the rider take the curve too fast.

All the horse drawn implements are on display. We put up some of the single
and double trees for attaching implements to the horse trace chains.

I think breaking down and mounting tires on wheels may be a lost skill.
Here, at the old home place, we still do the job. The mower is in need of
all new tires.

The mower has a small front support , balance wheel. The tire was rotten
and the wheel rusted. Note all the PPE.

Note what happens without proper PPE.

Kenneth, our rich and famous grandchild (one among many), gave me a shirt.
Alan told me the color coding significance.
Our visits to RR keep getting more upscale!

Back to reality, the crawler is going back together one newly painted item
at a time.

Wayne came by to show off his design and fabrication skills.
