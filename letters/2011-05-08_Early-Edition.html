May 8, 2011 · Sunday

Early Edition

7 photographs·230 words

I am going to go by and pick up Kenneth at 5 PM today (3:30 now). We are
going to a friend's house near Nashville tonight and fly out early in the
morning. We will return late on the 19th.


A pretty normal week. For do-gooder work GSH and I helped Dave and Carol
rip out a walkway and replace with lawn to be.

Trying to keep up with mowing in addition to the regular things.

It was cold on the top of Roan Mountain on our work day. 31 when we arrived
and all socked in. 33 when we left and still socked in. We came down to
the park area and had a free meal after our work day compliments of a new
long distance hikers organization.

.

A lot of end of school year activities. I went to watch John play soccer
and Maria run track (no picture).

In return for GSH helping me with the garden, I helped her with her newest
silk purse project.


r

Allison is home from school. She has finished digitizing ALL my slides and
is looking for new and additional slides to conquer. In the meantime, to
celebrate, B & G were enjoyed by various and sundry folks in from the
highways and byways.

Now, just as I am leaving

Back in two weeks. In the meantime, GSH is in charge.
