December 23, 2007 · Sunday

Night Before the Night Before

6 photographs·221 words

Our do-gooder work this week was at the church on Monday. It was also food
hand out day. It all came back to me. I dropped out because I feared we are
aiding and abetting in creating a dependency culture.
We worked at Blountville two or three times clearing along the property line
so it will be ready when Anita gets started on fence building. One side is
pretty much cleared now.
Anita continued to work on the barn/house. The floor is pretty much in place
now.
Wayne and Jonathan took a load of scrap metal to JC then Charlie and I
helped him harvest a load of firewood from a wooded building site. The most
interesting parts were when Wayne got the truck stuck in the mud followed by my
cutting a big, wild cherry tree which did not fall in the selected direction.
When I went to Erwin to check on Herb and Aunt G (both doing well) I
harvested one of Herb's carefully trimmed white pine Christmas trees.
The plan was to decorate it this afternoon. The reality is the afternoon was
spent fiddling with some baulky (cheap) lights which refused to cast light
on subject tree (or anything else).
Note there is ONE present under the tree. I put it there.
