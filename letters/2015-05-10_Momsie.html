May 10, 2015 · Sunday

Momsie

8 photographs·434 words

I miss mine. Took her for granted and now she is gone.

A this'n that week. Two days on the AT, Dr. trip, Erwin trip, gardening and
auto repair.

I am going to write a book. Something like " Easy jobs made difficult" or"
simple tasks made complicated (and expensive)".

The truck has been showing week battery signs. It has two big 12v batteries
one of which is only active for starting the engine which has a 24v
starter. I checked the two batteries and found one had internal short and
would not take a charge, the other seemed so-so. I went to WM at exit 7 and
bought one battery in spite of book saying replace both. would not start.
Bought second battery and installed, Would not start. Old battery had
pulled new battery down. Two days on battery charger and problem seems
resolved.

Had LBT out running. I am still operating on little fuel tank as do not
want too much fuel in shop. When I was ready to shut down I just turned the
fuel off and let the carb run empty. However, I did not turn the key off so
several days later my one fairly good 12v battery was down to zero.

On the other side, Herb checked in with his medical team and got a plan !
25 radiation treatments over 5 week and one/week chemo treatment also for 5
weeks. Starting date not yet pinned down and all to be done in JC.

I made my trip in to see heart dr. It was a good visit. A little Indian Dr.
I had never heard of before. He asked me why I went to Nashville to get
heart work done. I told him . His boss is the one who POed me causing me to
go to Nashville.

Worked on the AT Tuesday and Thursday. Still at Doll Flats.A good place to
work as plenty of ramps handy for digging during lunch break.

While I was gone, GSH finished insulating the inside of the LBT engine
panels. Now, if we can get the exhaust system installed it will be ready to
prime. Or almost ready.
May 10 is last frost day for here so time to plant garden. Put out six rows
of corn but then looked at time to maturity and found it is 88 days. So, I
must go get some more seed with shorter time.

GSH got a mother's day present. Well, actually her dog got a present. A
ball. We shall see.

Everyone serve or get served breakfast in bed? Anyone? confession time.
