September 18, 2016 · Sunday

Family All Over

8 photographs·424 words

Aaron came riding into town bringing with him his daughter and a young lady
who reminds me of Sally Fields.

Beth and Jake came into town with a daughter who reminds me very much of
Katy. Get ready world. No pictures there.

I have not been hearing much from my Lil' Sister. So, Saturday we went to
check on her. A good trip, glad we went. Found her to be just like last
time . I had to give her a run down on the health, wealth and happiness of
ALL the extended family members . Wish I had recorded it. If you have been
sending updates with a lot of positive accomplishments you can rest easy.
Otherwise I just had to use my imagination. She wants to come up for THE
wedding but may need help with transportation.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, I have been promising Lisa I would help her
get a gate across the entrance to Herb's place . He had some things stolen
and she would like to head off a rerun.Last week she and I agreed on what
and where. Tuesday I went to Southern States and procured the materials. It
should be a most formidable structure. I think my brother would be highly
amused.

Stink bugs have returned. I read that they breath through their surface and
soapy water will kill'em. So, I have a spray rig outside the door where
they gather to sneak in. I also read they are invasive and have no natural
enemies. But, one got in and got caught in the web of what looked like a
quite natural spider to me. Made my day. Anyone know the spider species? I
need more.

A little more work on the fire engine. Wheels are all mounted and lowered
to ground. Drive train all installed, Tow bar attached, waiting for the
signs to be right to drag it out and get it running.

Dragged little green crawler out but did not get it running. I heard help
was in town but had other fish to fry.

Tried to start big crawler and failed. I think it has an air leak in the
fuel system and FI system will have to be bled. A bit like George
Washington I suppose.

I did not do trail work Thursday, GSH thought it would be more beneficial
if I stayed here to be on standby duty to go sample Dwight's blood sugar.

Late and getting sleepy.

Did Rachel and Martha march at halftime? Anyone spot either on TV?
