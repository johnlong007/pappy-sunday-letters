July 6, 2008 · Sunday

Prelude in D Minor

11 photographs·169 words

The tile floor in the utility room is almost completed. Washer, dryer,
cabinets and wash basin are back in place.

The AT work this week involved building a ramp down the bank beside a road
crossing. After the ramp, we put in rock steps forming a switchback. This week,
the work will continue with another ramp down to where the trail levels off.

We did several do-gooder projects. This one involved replacing a rotted
storm door frame. Dave does good work.

Wayne was batching this week. He brought the kids over for B & G plus cherry
pie. Sue Tabor joined us. Another day we worked on the soffit for Dwight
outhouse project. One more outing should do it.

Blountville Fourth of July parade. Central band starring John. Thomas scur
rying for candy. Maria and her Mom being Cow Girls.

Some went camping at Rock Creek. Sunday PM, a July $ outing at D & D's.
