June 13, 2021 · Sunday

Fed and Watered

11 photographs·242 words

Gwendolyn baked more biscuits than we could eat at one sitting. But, the
second sitting did the job.

Our family is really good at no prior planning parties. I got a taste of
what has been going on in Wisconsin all these years.

Meanwhile, back on Ellis Road, I can whip out a Southern style breakfast.

We visited the Southern branch of the Wisconsin wing of family on Sunday
PM. We also had a fine feast there. But, I did not take a picture. Trust
me, it was tasty.
I did take garden pictures. Various family members (but not all who have
gardens) have posted pictures this week. How about getting with the program
?

That is a tomato plant beside Rachel. I wonder if any have ripe tomatoes
yet?

Always something.

As I well remember, my only new year resolution was to stand up straighter.
I have been doing pretty well when walking but all pictures I see of me
just standing have a bowed back. I included this picture because it looks
as though I may be making progress. May be like teaching the cow to eat
sawdust.

A crew of WWs erected the canopy . They read the directions first. Wish I
had thought of that years ago.

Lonsome WW helped erect the newly repaired pop up.
