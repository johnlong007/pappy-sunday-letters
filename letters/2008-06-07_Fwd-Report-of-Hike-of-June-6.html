Fwd: Report of Hike of June 6

6 photographs·228 words

The hike of June 6 was a key-swap hike on the Appalachian Trail between Chestoa and Spivey Gap. The northbounders were Kathy and Jerry Case, Anne Cosby, Carol Dunham, Judith Foster, Jan Mather and Anna Sherrill; the southbounders were Bob Harvey, Chuck Mather and Brian Paley. The two groups of us met at No Business Knob Shelter and ate lunch on a big log behind the building, which at the time was occupied by four inert Britons, who may have been recovering from jet lag.

The distance we covered was 11.2 miles, as per AT guidebook, but 11.0 miles according to an old weathered sign at Spivey Gap.

Although it was a hot day, we were rarely in direct sunlight and it was a pleasant hike -- until we were reassembled after the hike in Chestoa, where the heat suddenly got to be bothersome. BUT, we all were revived by ice-cold, hand-squeezed lemonade, generously furnished by the Case family.

The trail goes by many overlooks, such as this one, of the Nolichucky Gorge. The bridge near the center of the picture is at Unaka Springs, the site of a health resort of over a hundred years ago.

I got Chuck to pose for me at this overlook.

We came across a northbound through hiker standing on the trail, looking with admiration at this five-foot black beauty.

Bob Harvey
