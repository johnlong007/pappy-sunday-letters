September 5, 2021 · Sunday

Boys Toys

10 photographs·291 words

Alan continued mowing the hillside with his new ,air conditioned, tractor.
Knowing they were coming to town, sooner or later, I had not done the
mowing for the last two years.

We went out every day or two in order to check progress and be entertained.

Others came from miles around to watch. The willow patch in the wet spot
had really become a thicket. It was also a bedding down place for at least
one deer.
I always used low grade 1/2 inch bolts for shear pins. Advised Alan to buy
a sackful.

As I remember, the first week after the start of school Summer break, Wayne
dragged the pickup out and announced he wanted to get it raedy to use when
school resumed in the Fall.
Today ! He got it running and sounding good. More shakedown efforts to
complete, stay tuned.

The cloudburst retaining basin has worked well to retain water. Too well. I
expected it to catch the runoff, retain it a few days and all soak in
within a week or so. Not even close. Also, The pond does not have adequate
capacity. So, I decided to drain it then make the upper part deeper and the
dam higher. However, I had not built in any provisions for draining (since
it was not expected to hold water for any time.
Draining is under way by brute force. To be continued.

Aaron used the opportunity to take his kids on a nature walk.

They found a Paw Paw !. Probably the only one for this year's crop.

Coming in a close second in the entertainment contest.
