March 30, 2008 · Sunday

Most Interesting Week

11 photographs·509 words

Our regular crew went onto the AT to do trail repair and cut a reported
large downed tree. We found several inches of snow so did no trail repair but
being the saw operator, I did cut a walk through section from the downed tree.
We do not remove it all in order to obstruct ATVs (not allowed on the trail).
Family willing workers assembled to erect the wind delayed side walls on the
outhouse project.

A big event for the week was the long awaited trip to Buckeye Falls in
Unicoi county. It was about a four mile uphill walk up logging roads starting at
Rocky Fork. Then about one mile more or less downhill through the woods. The
downhill was through Buckeye trees all the way. By and by, a stream developed,
the beginning of Clark's Creek. We continued as the stream got a bit larger.
Then, we came to a Laurel thicket which was about maybe 50 feet thick and
growing along the lip of a cliff. The stream drops 450 feet over the edge and
down the face of the cliff. I crawled through the thicket to the edge, held
onto branch with one hand and stuck my camera out over the edge to take a
picture. The Laurel is on a steep bank and (I assume has shallow roots on the rock
cliff so it did pick my pulse rate right up.

Alf, our friend from East Germany arrived to spend a couple of days. I
wanted him to get a taste of local culture so we feed him soup beans, cornbread,
potatoes and coleslaw one night with some of our local friends here. The next
night, we had family in and fed him biscuits and gravy.
He went with me on the Friday hike. After we got home, he took the Mack for
a spin (could not get it into fifth gear) , then Dwight gave him an
opportunity to fire various handguns. I have observed that all our visitors from "no
gun" countries want to shoot guns. He is good, very good, said he had never
before shot a handgun although he was in the east German army.
He has a very interesting background and for me a totally unique view of the
world. He said he would come again and I hope he does.

I am back to running every other day . So far, the knee is doing ok and I
hope getting well. Maria asked to go with me to the track so she could get in
some hurdle practice. I thought I got a really good picture. She said the
hurdle was set too low causing her to clear too much. I still thought it is a good
picture.
The last significant event was erecting the outhouse rear wall with Dwight
and his three stooges.
I checked again today and it is still standing. I bet we get the front wall
up this week.
