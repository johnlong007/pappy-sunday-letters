February 13, 2022 · Sunday

Washed Up

10 photographs·263 words

Another trip to Dr. another bunch of tests, another clean bill of health

One of the things which has changed in my life is having to learn to run a
washing machine. The one we have has served long and well but it annoyed me
one time to many. Both Joe and Wayne are glad to see it retired. The new
one is one button but the button must be held pressed for a few seconds
(Diane told me, I have not actually tried).

I am still holding out on loading the dishwasher. I should be able to
handle as it is also one button.

Note the nice, smooth board ramps.

The sawmill continues to be a big draw. This week it brought viewers in
from as far as Chicago !

Not wanting sawing to be limited to my availability I went ahead and
trained a replacement driver for the Bobcat

Also, a substitute tractor driver for the tractor.

Much to my surprise, Alan allowed another person to saw a slab from a log.
We have a grandstand and plan to set up a ticket booth.

EJ does not appear impressed.

We are now working from our last rack of Winter firewood.

PBBD continued plugging along on the '37 pickup while being admired by
my'36 model

A small, high energy dog.

Meanwhile, the living picture frame ( a Christmas present from ALL the Jack
children.) provided constant company. All contributions noted and
appreciated.
