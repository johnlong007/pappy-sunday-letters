March 13, 2009 · Friday

Early Edition

6 photographs·278 words

The plan is to leave at 2 AM to drive to Charlotte for our flight to Mexico
to begin the bike ride. Return is scheduled for next Saturday when, if all
goes well, we should be home about dark.

It was warm early in the week so I played outdoors. But, Dave is still out
of commission so no do-gooder projects. Carl must have had other items to deal
with so no AT this week.

I did visit one of the Friday hikers who had recent back surgery and Hal
Ketron. Another of the Friday hikers is into the short rows due to cancer. We
may try to have one more get together.

I finally located the end of the pool drain line after turning a big portion
of the yard upside down. The pursuit caused me to have to cut another walnut
tree. After finding the line, I pushed a smaller hose up inside it well pass
the point where the existing line is broken and ran the open end further out
in the field where, I hope, it will remain uncovered.

Not being able to stand the warm weather, along with GSH, we planted a row
of potatoes. They should be ready by Summer Camp time. However, if we get
prolonged cold, wet weather, they will rot w/o sprouting.

March 11 marked 5 years since my surgery. I must schedule an appointment to
determine PSA level. If it is still zero, he will pronounce that I dodged that
particular bullet.

March 12 marked another birthday for GSH. She is once more the same age as
I.
