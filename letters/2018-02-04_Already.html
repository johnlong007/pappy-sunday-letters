February 4, 2018 · Sunday

Already !

14 photographs·372 words

As most know, We bought our one and only new car in 1963 while we were
living in New Orleans and I was employed by Boeing working on the Saturn C5.

​Good progress is being made toward bringing it back to life. Carpets are
in and interior except front seats. Sanford checked the outfit which did
Studebaker interior for me. he was told they would complete the job which
has stymied PBBD and GSH. So, I am going to try to get into their shop soon.

However, the point of this story is, we bought a new car ! Our second, one
every 31 years !

​Acturally, LLLL did all the work but we have a new car. We gave it a shake
down run by driving down to see Lil' Sister Friday and back home Saturday.
Meet Linda and David in Murfreeboro​ to go along. It was a fun trip, went
fine and got to see both Sister and her new dog.

​I also made a visit to Dixie gun works. I have been looking for a new toy
for some time but decided for the $$ involved I should go to a reputable
business. Not trusting any open system, I shall not include a picture. Stop
by and try it out.

We did go out for AT work for the first time in 6 weeks. Met up at the
parking lot in Chestoa.

Carl split into two groups. The group I went with worked on the trail from
Rafting site, about 1 1/2 miles on up the river back to the parking lot. A
large tree had fallen leaving a gap in the trail. We filled with rocks and
covered over with dirt.

A pretty good view looking down on Unaka springs from the trail.

​Then we worked on the trail on back to the parking lot and built steps on
the ramp at beginning of trail.

​Covered the finished product with leaves to keep from being muddy.

​The rest of crew showed up and decided to do a bit pf work on the ditch.
Notice worker:watcher ratio

We had a couple of light snows during the week but not enough to test the
vehicle.
