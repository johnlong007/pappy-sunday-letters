February 20, 2011 · Sunday

Progress at Last !

9 photographs·357 words

At least in regard to some things of importance.

When I made a list of things I did not do during the week, I failed to
include I did not jump out of an airplane. I have hopes that some member of the
family will lead me on that adventure.

AT work was fun in a perverse fashion. We are doing trail Improvement on a
section above Hampton. At one spot, a large rock outcropping had the trail
constricted to about 6 inches wide. Since we did not have all the proper
rock busting tools with us on Tu., we decided to build up the pathway to make
it wider and complete the rock removal Wed.

Wed. I worked with Bob Peoples and we got serious about rock removal.

GSH has been sewing.

But, lest you think her diversion has stopped the car project you would be
close but not quite. The side panels are pretty much cut to fit but when I
went to procure fasteners, the store was closed. Back on that job tomorrow
with a picture to follow.

I went with Dwight to Seymour. He worked on the race car electronics (not
electrical mind you) while I helped Pat fabricate a massive hitch to
install on the motor home for the purpose of towing the race car trailer. The
most impressive part of the whole project for me was having the tools (also
skill and knowledge) to fabricate the hitch right there in the shop starting
mostly from heavy flat steel plate.

Dwight (and Diane) came over Sunday (in addition to Laura, Anita, Wayne and
Thomas). They all just looked good, Dwight helped me watch the race and
also checked out all the lights on The Studebaker !

The interior work has barely started but I intend to get it street legal
very soon. Stay tuned.
For a bit of a bummer. We learned that texting was not included in the
package price of the electronic device. So, a short lived career as a texter
for me.
But, my email skills are improving using the device so be patient and don't
forget to write.
