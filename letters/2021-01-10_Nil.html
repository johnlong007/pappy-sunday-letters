A historic slow start to a new year !

Laura went with me for my mandatory meeting with the second heart Dr. It
went well. I have one more procedure scheduled then TAVR in early Feb.

In order to help with the excess parts (junk) removal program I have been
working on getting stuff loaded into the truck.

One of the big ridder mowers has a large B & S engine which is not frozen.
For no real good reason I decided to pull the engine rather than send it to
crusher with the mower.

Engine ID plate.

It snowed one night, we missed one day to the track but got the next. I
usually do 8 laps while GSH does 2. I have had to stop pretending to run
and now admit I am walking. Same speed.

Exciting news on the windshield project. I broke it.

The WSJ had an article promoting ole patriarchs should prepare an " ethical
will " for their descendants with expectation
that they would ponder and get together at regular intervals to discuss
how their lives were being impacted.

I believe I have the first bit of wisdom to pass on in mine, don't try to
force windshield glass into position.

While we were visiting Philippe in his home town of Lamage he took us out
to dinner. The waiter came around with a cheese cart which Philippe talked
us through.
We did not have a cheese cart but did have a selection of imported (from
Wisconsin) cheeses.
