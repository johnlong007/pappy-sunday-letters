August 3, 2008 · Sunday

Ho Hum

7 photographs·181 words

Dave was out of town Monday so no do gooder. Pat Peoples died with funeral
on Tuesday so no AT work.
I spent most of my spare time working on the Trail 90. I am putting in an
engine which is too large and not made for it. But sort of a fun project.
Also finished clean up after summer camp. They left the hulk of a car on the
landing. I finished taking off the parts I thought might be worthwhile and
relocated the remainder (as well as the spare parts).
Also (with the help of Maria) finished splitting and stacking the winter
wood supply

The critters about wiped out our sweet corn. In a classic demonstration of
locking the barn door, I put out my live trap and caught a critter.

Saturday the rains came down and the floods came up. I sure am glad we are
getting close on a roof replacement endeavor, otherwise, I would be greatly
distressed.
