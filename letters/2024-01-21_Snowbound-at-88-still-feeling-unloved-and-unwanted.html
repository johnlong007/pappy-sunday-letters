January 21, 2024 · Sunday

Snowbound at 88 (still feeling unloved and unwanted,)

16 photographs·254 words

Everything appeared normal when I went in for lab work on Monday. Platelets
were still marginal so I got a good lecture rather than an infusion. Then,
the snow came down with no regard for my carefully planned birthday
celebration.

I have got my 1oK steps everyday for the last couple of weeks.

As a result the celebration was quite subdued.

Fortunately, Dave Edwards had delivered a beast feast.

And Laura had prepared her annual hard icing cake which they delivered ,
For reasons I do not recall, no pictures.

That was the only traffic we got for a spell.

One family member had a fine time playing in the snow.

Then, the temperature dropped.

Fortunately, Wayne, driving the Miata, was undeterred and showed up to help
his big sister work on the 37.

I helped clean parts. Does anyone in the younger generation know what is
pictured here?

My father would be pleased with this. She is using a dinging hammer.

The cat likes to watch birds and plan lunch. I found the headless body of a
Dove on the doorstep.

Sunday afternoon, we walked a lap around the place. Cat and dog went with
us.Here we were up by the big Oak saw log. Cat escort.

The frozen pond.
Dog and cat out there somewhere.

Still out of commission
