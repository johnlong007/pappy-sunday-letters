March 24, 2019 · Sunday

Sap is Rising !

14 photographs·589 words

Sure enough, Spring arrived

While the rain was upon us, we started cleaning the strictly agriculture
building. Summer campers may remember doing that some years back. Since
then a lot of items have been added by sort of standing at the door and
pitching inside.
Anyway, we found a couple of interesting items which may be looking for a
good home.

I believe each of the pictures below were taken in 1957 which sort of
narrows the field as to the little person was. Made on Christmas day in
1957 at our little house in Knoxville. As I remember the horse cost $27 a
big investment when GSH was bringing in less than $200/month.

This one was made during the Summer of '57 while we were trunk camping in
Cades Cove. Notice the '48 Plymouth trunk.

We are making good progress, as you can see, we are lining up keeper items
along the wall. That stove is just like the one my mother used from 1935
until we got electricity in 1948 or thereabouts. It was top of the line. I
found one in Montgomery Al. and purchased. Had visions of setting up in
shop and training GSH.
Anyone want to give it a try ? There is also a wringer washer with tubs.
Mom had one and GSH and I had one we used from '55 until '60, then bought a
well used automatic. Should have stuck with the wringer.

Quite a bit of lumber, unless some better need comes along I intend to use
to replace tier poles in the tobacco barn. Some will remember those rickety
poles.

Another trip to Memphis ! This time via Union City to visit my sister at
the home for old and decrepit folks . She is amazing. Just a few days
after knee replacement and gets up and about on her own power. Is using a
walker for security but clear it was not really necessary. I expect she
will be at home with her dog very soon.

On to Memphis to dental school. The waiting room fills up a few minutes
before 9 and again a few minutes before 1. Then empties in a matter of
minutes as the budding dentist come out to lay claim to their patients. I
always stand in the back and watch. I find it really interesting to see how
each dentist is different in greeting patients.

We got out in time for a long walk along the mighty Mississippi. Clearly
still up, clearly not as high as it has been and probably will be higher
yet this week .

Another sign of Spring. Time to get the mower ready for another season. One
of my better investments. I could not keep this place beat into submission
without it.

Work on the thermal energy storage facility continued. Ceiling paneling
appears completed.

More signs of Spring. I think tis clear the ramps are spreading but also
clear that only spreading slowly. So far, I think I have harvested one ramp
her and fed it to Savannah.

Guess who got a haircut.
Can everyone identify each of these creatures?

Oh, I almost forgot, Allison used me for her state board demonstration of
skill and knowledge, Passed the exam and should be able to hang out a
shingle this Summer.
I get a gold crown. I hope with stars.
