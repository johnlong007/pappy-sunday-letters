December 9, 2007 · Sunday

Social Season

7 photographs·181 words

The work on AT for last few weeks has been largely confined to moving large
rocks around.

Friday hike we went from Indian Grave Gap to the Nolichucky, along the river
then back on the AT to starting point. This is across from Poplar NC looking
down at a coal train in the gorge.

Saturday, We replaced the crawler cylinder which Dwight had repaired. It
cured the system problems! Check out was to smash the Alfa hulk to make it
easier to haul off to the crusher. It did not smash easily.

Saturday was also Blountville Christmas parade. John was in Central Band.
Easily spotted. Wayne's kids raked in candy as it was a rainy day and lots of
people stayed away.

Saturday night was the Gray Station Old Tractor owners pot luck dinner
followed Sunday PM by the Blountville event.

Turns out our power was off all day Sunday but we did not figure out it was
just us until 8 PM. The transformer failed (after 27 years) no obvious reason.
