August 18, 2019 · Sunday

Sanded Out

12 photographs·388 words

I noted one of my high school buddies died. Here is the group I ran with my
junior year in high school. All three were a year ahead of me.
No one ever had a better group of HS friends.
Also note, all married "til death do us part" which has happened to two of
the four.

After Pat painted the '63, a lot of my poor workmanship in prepping the
primer showed up. We had paint left and Pat promised to repaint but first
correcting the defects and sanding the entire panel to be repainted would
be required. NOTE, it would have been a lot easier to do it right to start
with !

Anyway, a lot of time and effort in prepping the car (again) this week.

Pat noticed the trunk lid was warped. I was able to bend it back into
correct position.

Then, I got inside the trunk, loosened the attachment bolts while PBBD
held the lid in position (by sitting on it) and i tightened the bolts. I
made her practice unlocking the lid several times before getting inside
trunk.

Wet sanding, both me and the car.

Cleaned the tires and installed original wheel covers. Actually, only two
are original. One we lost on first trip to Alaska. It rolled into weeds
beside the road. Stopped and put kids to hunting but mosquitoes were eating
them alive so we went on.
The second came off while crossing a bumpy bridge in Cleveland during heavy
traffic. Did not even look in the mirror for it.

I did run the string trimmer in the turn around.

And scrapped the driveway.

We reclaimed the Bobcat after getting M & R completed. Wayne was driving on
the way home, a traffic light changed and we slowed but kept going. A brake
line had ruptured. Came on home carefully without incident. If you have
never practiced driving in traffic without using the brakes you should.

After unloading Bobcat I could hardly wait to go play in the dirt. In about
30 minutes made A lot of progress.

As promised, Pat came back and repainted the just sanded panels. Grand
unveiling will be Tuesday evening.
