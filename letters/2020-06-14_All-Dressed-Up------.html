June 14, 2020 · Sunday

All Dressed Up -----

12 photographs·424 words

For a good many years we have been going to Perkins at 8 on Sunday then on
to early service at church at 9.

Perkins has been open for the last 4 weeks. Church has started to open but
early service (the ole folks service) requires masks. I have never been
good at following orders and probably too late to learn. So, will continue
to wait until the mask rule is forgotten or ignored or both.

Speaking of all dressed up, the Chestnut trees appear headed for a banner
year. I expect that is good news for a couple of people and for the local
wildlife.
Local wildlife, as we left for non-church this morning we had to stop in
the driveway and wait for Momma turkey and a covey of turkeys to cross,
then recross.
Then this afternoon about 5 the deer herd ventured into the front yard.
Scouting out my garden I suppose.

I found a strange rock this week. Very dark core and built up in layers. I
tried to send Collins a picture but his email bounced. ??
Anyone know anything about rocks?

In much more important news, Adam graduated.

And we were honored and pleased he came to spend time with us on his way to
his first assignment. He knows the name but it will probably be renamed by
the time he gets there.

I did no car work ! But, Thomas is plugging along. I believe Wayne is
planning to drive the '63 in July 4th. parade.

It was hot this week. 91 one day. I did a lot of hard work out in the hot,
hot sun and I am not an Englishman. I do like hot weather.
The goal for the week was to clear the tangled mess where the big Oak tree
fell. The problem at the beginning of week was the very big, very high
leaner hanging overhead.
I did get it down without injury.

The tangled mess looked like this picture.
Tractor, Bobcat and the big chain saw plus a lot of sweat. Only a
moderate amount of blood this week.

Now, all that is left is a very long Poplar and a very large 35 foot Oak.
Fate has not yet been determined. Stay tuned.

As usual, PBBD came over on Sunday to join us at the track for a spell and
fix lunch for us.
