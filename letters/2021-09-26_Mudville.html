September 26, 2021 · Sunday

Mudville

8 photographs·181 words

After the worst has happened,
with nothing left to fear,
the sun continues shining with undiminished cheer,
and winds continue blowing and skies continue fair,
as hearts continue bearing the thing they could not bear.

Saturday we had visitors from far and near ! It was great. Some came before
this picture, some after. All were appreciated.

For a few, it was a first visit .

Others fell right into old habits.

When not tending to the hurt,lame and afflicted (all the same person), I
continued the new and improved retaining basin project. We got some rain.
Now, we have our own bog.

I think Harry Truman is credited with "if you want a friend, get a dog".

When I saw the above picture, I thought I have seen that person before.

Alan seems serious about procuring a sawmill. While he was on duty, I
induced Catherine to go on tour to look at some lumber in the raw.
As usual, we had an escort.
