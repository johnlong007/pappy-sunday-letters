October 23, 2016 · Sunday

Almost Normal

13 photographs·330 words

Remember all the ways I could mess up making a cup of coffee in my little
one cup coffee maker? I continue to find new ways to get it wrong doing the
blood sugar testing on Dwight. Fortunately, all the things I have done so
far have caused him amusement rather than distress. My latest was putting
the little strip in wrong end to.

The really big event for the week was installing the gate for Lisa. If you
have not seen "Blazing Saddles" you should. Pat Loven helped along with GSH
. Speaking of GSH, perhaps I should start a new series on what GSH's do for
the benefit of Savannah? I wonder if John lets her see my notes?

Next pictures are for her:

Work day on the AT was perfect. We worked at Carvers Gap.I was sent with 3
othgers to walk in (3 miles) to near the shelter and drain a bog in the
trail We cleared the alligators and drained the swamp. We dug down about a
foot along side the trail then paved the walkway with rocks.

There were a lot of hikers out. When we came out, we found a young man,
wife and about 10 child in distress. They had started out on their first
three night backpacking trip having lest car in Hampton and getting
dropped off. Then the lady had not been able to make it to the top of first
climb (round Bald). She was a rather small person. When a plan was
developed to get them back to vehicle I picked up her pack to put in in the
truck. I believe it weighed close to 50 pounds.

We went out to see D & D on Saturday. While there, I took a couple of
pictures.

Made a bit more progress on the '63, Fiddled with the IH a bit, had seals
and bearings installed in truck rear axle. I wonder if all the wedding
persons got somewhere?
