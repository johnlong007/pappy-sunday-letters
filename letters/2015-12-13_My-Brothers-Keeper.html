December 13, 2015 · Sunday

My Brother's Keeper

12 photographs·264 words

This week we went to Vanderbilt for the operation. Sliced and diced on Wed.
Lisa delivered him back home on Saturday "grouchy and confused". i.e normal.

A sighting of our Lil' sister was reported but I cannot confirm.

I gave him the choice of me staying here to work on the fire engine or come
help him by pacing around. Stayed here and worked on the fire engine. I got
it up closer to the barn, loosened both front wheels on axle but did not
get removed due to tight brake drums. Pulled the drive shaft which goes
into pump gearbox and determined that guide bushing is worn out. This week
the search for a new part will get underway

Her gave us a pack of really tasty (if you like it) salt cured ham. Anyone
interested in breakfast can come plead their case.

We also continued to forage for firewood. Old folks do like a hot spot.The
heat pump keeps the house warm but no hot place.

AT work was trail renovating near Temple Hill Gap. I am including a before
and after of the section where I was most of the time. It was lovely day to
be out and I was ready to get out, way out.

In a fine barn door locking exercise, we weeded the strawberry patches,
covered with pine needles and carefully placed the netting over each patch.
I hope whatever has been chomping the plants will get entangled.

For various reasons did not get to do any football watching. Any famous
people make it onto any broadcast?
