April 17, 2011 · Sunday

Sequel

15 photographs·422 words

I got a lot of feedback re. cat fluffing. Some folks seem to regard cat
fluffing as a higher calling. Therefore, perhaps we should have a contest to
submit the best picture of a family cat fluffer fluffing a family cat, or
any cat for that matter.
I heard the expression again this week when one of the AT workers had not
worked the previous week. He had stayed home to FTC.

You will be glad to know that I got a twofer this week for do-gooder work.
Recruited a new and improved helper and worked at the church. As ground
cover during the giant tear down and build greater campaign some bare ground
had been sown with Rye. Last Sunday I overheard some pillars of the church
complaining that the Rye was unsightly and should be mown. So, bright and
early Sunday GSH and I mowed the Rye.

Just like the modern equivalent of the little ole German ladies, GSH
gathered the fresh mown hay and loaded it in her ox cart.

The pasture at the Blountville estate co-op has a lot of wild cherry trees.
Since wild cherry can be fatal to livestock (and I suppose humans should
they decide to ingest) a program is under way to harvest the trees.
Several more were felled this week, The hard part of the job remains.Cl;ear
the brush and drag out the logs.

A good week for family atheletics. Benj. had a number of ball games. I made
it to one of them.

John had a soccer game . I was late getting there and missed seeing him
score 2 of Central's five goals. Also, Maria had a track meet. To keep my
string going, I got there after she had run her events doing very well. To make
amends, I took her to BK for supper.

I worked on the AT one day. Walked about 11 miles and did a fair amount of
digging.

Most, but not all, the digging was trail work.

The big event was the long awaited arrival of the sawmill. According to
Anita, about 2,000 board feet was sawed out. It was also loaded on the trailer
and moved to Blountville Lumber Company lot for storage and air drying.

Before wrapping up I should give a brief report on the critters.

For a car report, all the bright work is now installed and the first piece
of carpet is in place.

Can anyone think of a good story to go with this picture?

A pretty fun week.
