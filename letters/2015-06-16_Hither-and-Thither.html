June 16, 2015 · Tuesday

Hither and Thither

12 photographs·439 words

Various and sundry things this week.

The strawberry plants arrived and are planted in the newly composted bed.

The LBT has been shuttled into the barn to await being taken to town to get
exhaust system installed. It started easily and ran very well. Hope is
springing out all over. Bruce Montogomery the CHCC minister to the ole
folks came by to minister to us. We took him out for the grand tour.

We went to Erwin one day to check on Herb, the tree he had cut down and
pick up the strawberry plants. He is handling the medical treatments well.
Over 1/2 way through now.

Thursday was AT work day. Back to backbone rock , a "blue" trail 2.4 miles
long, over 2,00 feet elevation change leading to the AT. We have been
cutting zig zags to reduce the grade to around 10 % and are almost all the
way to the AT. Thursday was a hot day which started at 9 with a two mile
climb with pack and tools. Here is a picture of my section for the day and
my condition after coming down to the parking lot.

After much vacillating and wishy washing, we rented a car and headed for
Peru Saturday. A fine trip and a fine welcome. He fed us homemade chicken
soup complete with herbs and spices from his garden.

Early Sunday we made a run on to the the frozen North via one of my least
favorite cities of all ! But, it was an easy run in our Camry (I liked it
!).

Sunday afternoon we got to watch Martha cover her family in reflected
glory. Alabama, here she comes !

Then, we toured the bike shop (Charlie, eat your heart out), went for a
walk and attended a concert.

Early Monday we wished Alan and early happy birthday and headed South. The
rain came down and the floods came up , also, the North wind did blow. But,
in our nice secure Camry we made it. Martha (a different one) was there
with Sis when we arrived. We were fed a nice (healthy) supper and I
wished Martha an early happy birthday also.

My sister seems to be settling in nicely to her new house. Not sure it is
home yet but it seems very nice to me. We shared declining health stories,
the trials of life including children and pets before turning in for the
night.

Made it home today with no problems, almost 2,000 miles at 35mpg in quite
comfort. Renting was a good decision and going on the trip was an even
better one.
