December 4, 2022 · Sunday

Prediclection

10 photographs·312 words

Not near as much coming and going as last week.

But, someone left a present. Sniffles, head cold, cold, Flu, Covid whatever
one prefers, it is passing through.

All will remember the family golden rule "you will feel better if you get
up and get moving".

Although I am not doing well on my "running" due to various constraints, I
have been getting in short bursts of workouts. What PBBD is here, she leads
the way.

Some adjusting on the 55 brakes but still do not feel right. Next step is
several laps in the driveway followed by more adjusting and perhaps
swapping out the brake booster.

A fair amount of work in the warm. dry shop on the 37 engine. The Freeze
plugs were in very good condition for their age.

Deer all over. Our new neighbor came by and announced he had heard reports
of a buck sighting. He pronounced he would have it in his frying pan. I
think he may have , trouble came home with some fresh innards.

Sleeping it off !

Laura told me a cat will find the most comfortable place in the house to
sleep.

The middle finger on my right hand got the most damage from my Texas
chainsaw event some years back. It is warped and sensitive. I caught it
between the fireplace frame and a chunk of wood. I did and does hurt.

PBBD and I wrestled with valve removal for several hours to no avail. Once
more, Wayne showed up with not only skill and knowledge but also the
correct tool. The entire family gather to watch and be admiring. Sorta.

Sunday night at the movie.

I read there are now 8 billion of us on the tiny blue dot.
