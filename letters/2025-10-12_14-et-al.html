October 12, 2025 · Sunday

14 et al

10 photographs·313 words

This past week was Chemo week. I have now completed 14 cycles (and am still
alive !).

My next cycle is scheduled for the last week in December.

Picture is Amy, she has three children and a good sense of humor.

The weather was nice all week so I lit in to mowing the whole place. I am
all caught up for now.

Mowing makes it easy to spot the walnuts and easier to collect. We picked
up, hulled, washed, and dried bushels.

Diane now has 95 one gallon bags of cleaned, dried but not yet cured
walnuts. We are hoping for help in hanging bags on the shop trusses for
curing.

I do appreciate visitors. Young and old. Family and friends.

Don and Sanford. Not young visitors. Paul and Ted came by later in the
week,then Dave Edwards on Sunday.

No work done on the A-C. Hope is springing. This week for sure.

Beth managed an apple butter run.

I and others took a lot of pictures. Mine are available upon request. I
mostly sat, watched and relished seing our family working together and
enjoying it. I can attest, it is a memory building experience.
I could visualize my GGM making apple butter in that kettle in 1925. I know
the location where it happened. She was a middle aged widow, living alone,
at the time. Aunt G told me she made apple butter runs by herself. I bet
the girls helped with peeling !

Alan has fabricated and installed a pool ladder. I have tried the pool once
and was not sure I could get out.
I think I will suggest to him that his grandchildren might like and use
some sort of spring loaded diving platform.
