This picture was taken today, two days after her birthdate.

Last Sunday morning at breakfast I told David about our trials and
tribulations with the gutter drain line. Monday, he showed up. Got a long 2
by 2 out of ag building, sawed a couple of fish hooks in it, drove it up
the drain line and told me to drag out root wad. I did. It was quite
impressive. Sort of like an anaconda

Then helpers installed what we hope is a proper drain line started.

Grass seed mat.

The string trimmer and operator are doing a number all around.

I fired up the gas fueled trimmer and got a lot done also. I have to stay
on the level. Having balance issues.

This should help anyone trying to find our driveway entrance.

I mowed the hill above grape arbor with the rider.Found I had quite an
appreciative audience.

In other news, lab results Monday were not encouraging. However, I have
felt better and able to do more this week.

For a bummer, after 6 years, I lost one of my hearing aids.
