June 4, 2017 · Sunday

Signs and Portends

12 photographs·332 words

A quiet week in our little corner of the world.

Due to circumstances beyond my control I did not do a lot of worthwhile
things including AT work. But, according to Carl's maintenance report, they
went right on without me. Rats.

I did take one whack at do-gooder. I suppose it counts even if within the
family? Anyway, we went to Erwin to check on Lisa, the tenant farmer.
Actually, we went to look for some unaccounted parts for Herb's '63 which
we are talking about and thinking about getting re-assembled. We did not
find the parts. To be continued.

However, I did mow around the BB bushes.

​We went back later in the week to help cover the bushes and to pick up
some S'berry plants Lisa had ordered for us. No pictures of the covering
but I will get one next time I am over that way. Looks like she will have a
good crop.

But, I did get pictures of the new and improved S'berry patch.

As PBBD likes to say "hope springs eternal".

In between those trips, we had lunch with David and Edna at their cabin
overlooking the junction of two forks of the Holston river. I went on tour
after we had lunch. We did have a tourist watching over us at lunch.

look close in this one

PBBD procures sunflower seed to I refilled the two feeders. Last night,
both got torn down, Bottome ripped off one and the other smashed. What do
we believe the culprit was?

​Hard to tell but the wire cylinder is mashed flat.

Linda is here for a brief visit. She toured the place last night. Announced
some cherries are ripe, some BB's and some black r'berries.​

Looks like a good year for the red r'berries.

We did have visitors from afar.

Elsewhere, Wayne has left the country. Gwendolyn has left home​. Janet has
disappeared into the wilderness and Maria is hobbled.

What did I miss?
