August 10, 2025 · Sunday

Unto All Things

17 photographs·350 words

I got an early release Monday with the usual don't get sick don't bleed
warning. So, instead of a ham biscuit we got McD's sausage biscuit and
added a slice of Nancy Hass tomato. Friends are great !

Monday PM we went to Erwin to claim the turn plow Farmer Lisa dug out. We
pulled off to get a picture of the remnants of the Sandy Bottom Cemetery..
My GGF was one of the last buried there due to recurrent flooding. High
paid mgmt folks went ahead and built a hospital.

I believe my father bought the land across the road in front of our house
in 1946 or 1947. At about the same time, he bought a horse (Prince) and
some implements.

We came home and took the plow apart for cleaning and painting.

Some time back, one of the driveway pines fell. Alan sawed it into saw logs
and firewood. Diane loaded the firewood so we could move it to the
splitting site.

A lot of the walnut is split and stacked in this picture. Probably will not
dry enough to burn this Winter.

I sort of quit trying to manage my skin cancers 3 or 4 years back. However,
I got one on my neck which was tender and annoying. Diane sent a picture to
Dr. Sago who told her to bring me in. She dug it out as can be seen. She
also dug out 2 more and froze 3 more. Be warned.

We were working along splitting the Walnut when help arrived. I can attest,
wood splitting provides excellent stress relief.

The big stack is most of the Walnut. Leaned up against it is the pine. We
have not decided where to stack the pine.

All this stacked on the left is the remaining Walnut.

Friday evening on Reservoir Road.

We sent out an APB for swimmers. One mermaid showed up.
