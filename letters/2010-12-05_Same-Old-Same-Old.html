December 5, 2010 · Sunday

Same Old, Same Old

7 photographs·196 words

Almost but not quite.

After years of progressive difficulty understanding what people are saying,
I went in for a full fledged hearing exam I found out my hearing is fine
the problem is being caused by being around old folks who mumble and young
ones who slur their words.

I went back to my favorite junk yard and procured a couple more bits and
pieces for the car project. An emblem for the trunk lid and "Studebaker"
script for the grill.

Work continued on the log splitter. I now have the parts all ready to weld
in place (again) as soon as the weather abates.

We did work on the trail one day. It was cold but 9 of us dug a total of
about 400 feet of trail.

Friday night, we went with Dave and Carol to Natural Tunnel Stata park for
a night light program. It was really good. I had not been there before. We
rode the chair lift ($3.00) down into the bowl. There, they have a patio
like affair with portable fireplaces set up and a program by local semi-pro
entertainers.
