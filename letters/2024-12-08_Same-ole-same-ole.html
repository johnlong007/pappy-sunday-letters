December 8, 2024 · Sunday

Same ole, same ole

15 photographs·246 words

>From the Family Tree work. That's Robert the Second, King of Scotland.

More and more, I like the cold less and less. A lot of time indoors burning
through our Winter wood supply.

A lot of time going through files and folders from the last 60 years or so.
Things with name, rank and SN were shredded.

We finished taking the second cultivator apart. Much more rust such that
most fasteners twisted off.

The fun part, cleaning the rusty parts,

Everyone (anyone) know what she is rebuilding?

To avoid the possibility of idle hands, cleaning of the cider press
components has been started.

Most of our fire wood is soft, fast burning. Wayne helped with restocking
out front.

One of the windows had a small puncture. No idea what happened. Mickey and
Sarah made a house call to replace the window.

Nephew Dan came by being concerned that I am on my last legs. It was a fine
visit. I learned his son Parks is now married and his wife is expecting a
baby girl in due time. Makes me feel old.

Anita sure can pick cats !

As usual I had breakfast with David Quillen at Perkins today.. He was on
his way to Bumpass Cove to help get the oxen out of the ditches.
