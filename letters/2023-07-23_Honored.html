July 23, 2023 · Sunday

Honored

9 photographs·319 words

I really like the picture frame Meredith gave us last year. It lets me keep
up with family members without getting all the unpleasantness which came
with Facebook. I continued to be both pleased and honored by all the family
members who have chosen this time to make blood donations. Thank you.

This week, our youngest grandchild finished basic training at Lackland
AFB. About 70 years after my brother .

Thomas has a long way to go to be, and look, this cool. I hope he keeps
this image in front of himself.

I did quite a bit of mowing this week. PBBD did a lot of work with the 55.
Here, she is putting thin strips of paper in to check the tightness of the
trunk seal to the lid. The fit was not tight enough and the lid/seal leaked
with a water test.

We are pondering trunk lid adjustment. In the meantime. The passenger
door inner panel was in pretty poor condition. A spare panel was pulled
from inventory and work is underway to make one good panel from the pair.

Peach trees are difficult in this area, GSH has one which has had good and
bad years, The tree is much reduced in size but has 5 good looking peaches.
Trying to protect from insects and critters is high on our list.

In more promising news, PBBD procured a "keyboard" in order to practice her
piano lesson here rather than trot back and forth to her house.

For the first time in a few weeks, I went to church (early service only)
today. When I got home I found Meredith was here . She has more or less
volunteered to help upgrade the area in the turnaround.

The resurrection lilies are popping up. I like them.
