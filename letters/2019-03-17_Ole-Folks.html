March 17, 2019 · Sunday

Ole Folks

11 photographs·319 words

GSH had a birthday this week. Now, she is as old as I am.

In other exciting news, my aged and somewhat decrepit sister had a knee
replaced. She is at Union city nursing and rehab if anyone cares to contact
her. Doubt she is wired for email.

In the finest tradition of locking the barn door, we went to Erwin and
helped Lisa lock the barn door. Herb had a lot of items (refrained from
saying stuff) worth stealing. However, my impression is her local thieves
are not very bright and have been taking items designed for manual labor.
Hard to believe they plan to use and worth nothing much at the flea market.

We worked on the '63 quite a bit. It now has a tag and brake lights! No
insurance yet but maybe next week.

Right front signal light was a problem. Fortunately, I have several spares
to work from

Correct, original jack was an even more difficult problem. I have not yet
located the original. Perhaps Wayne will find it by and by. I did dig out a
correct, but very rusty one and refurbished it to acceptable level.

Rolled the car out and fired it up. Starts good, sounds good but still
smokes. I am going to put off valve stem seals in the hopes that it will
quit or maybe when get exhaust pipes going out rear I will not see smoke.

Animal Farm.

Chris and company started installing the pool room siding. I also noted
that the pool back wall temp is now 10 degrees above ground temp.

GSH and her cat helper at work.

Reckon the cat is descended from the sacred cats in Egypt? He seems to
think so, Maybe like one of the Babylonian lions?

Spring !
