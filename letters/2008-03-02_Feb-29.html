March 2, 2008 · Sunday

Feb. 29

12 photographs·118 words

A busy week.
Soccer field work continues when it is dry enough.
We moved the little crawler up to the house.
Sis came in Wed. We went to see Erwin Thursday. Stopped to see Aunt G then
went to Sam's Gap.
Sister with new style hat, came from Wal- Mart I believe.
Although Beth promised me first go at interrogation of Jake, I deferred to
Sis out of respect for both age and ability.
Birthday waffles for Anita.
Willing workers pushing Crawler into shop, moving rafters to barn and
clearing brush at Blountville.
We moved a load of rocks for Sue Tabor. Well populated with spiders.
