Sanford went through basic at Ft. Gordon in 1963. Monday he took us down to
watch our grandchild receive a promotion to Captain rank.

I was honored to be allowed to do the pinning.

Dr.. Sago keeps up with my vitals. She decided the signs were right to
remove the live one on my lip.

Our tomato plants are doing well so far.

Last week, Alan cut down a pine tree, this week he started to convert it
into timbers to stack the Walnut lumber. Stay tuned.

Niece Lisa delivered cake and strawberries.
Dan sent a note saying his Dad's body has been cremated.

Our oldest grandchild with his family arrives for the weekend.

They took the two youngest on to RR house and left two willing workers here.

Among other things, they started on the job to paint the disc.To be
continued.

Our family MD showed up with her family.

Benj wanted the bird book he remembered from Summer Camp. He also
remembered my admonition about putting gifts in the receiver's hand. I was
very happy with both.

For anyone interested in knowing more about the origin of the turnip
termite story. I started calling Alan "turnip termite" because of the way
he went after the available food supply. It soon became just "turnip" and
continued through the others.

*LIL ABNER: THE 1940 DAILIES Volume 6 (of 27)by AL CAPP (Softcover)*

The year begins with Capp's parody of* John Steinbeck's* *The Grapes of
Wrath* (also made into a movie this year). Dogpatch is wiped out by turnip
termites, so our favorite hillbillies are lured to Boston to pick oranges
in winter! Li'l Abner also learns how to woo "Dogpatch style" from Adam
Lazonga, whom Schreiner speculates is based on controversial philosopher
and free thinker *Bertrand Russell *or possibly playwright *George Bernard
Shaw*. In another episode* Mammy* and *Pappy Yokum* get a 100 year-old
letter and rush off to the California gold rush. *Earthquake McGoon* is
introduced in the daily strip this year, as is the popular phrase, "As any
fool kin plainly see!" Plus the annual *Sadie Hawkins* Day Race. The cover
is drawn by *Peter Poplaski*.
