August 24, 2014 · Sunday

Good News !

10 photographs·705 words

Monday, for the first time in ages, we got back on the do-gooder act. As
you may (or may not) remember, I work with Dave Dunham. Dave has more (real
and imagined) health problem that a dog has fleas. Anyway, one of our best
customers has been Jean and Charlie Goodman. Charlie died but Jean trundles
on. She called and asked if we would come trim her shrubs again. We did, GSH
and Carol joined in as a major part of this outing was hand holding and ear
bending.

No AT work for me this week. Carl had a couple of projects going on but I
did not see any place I could add unique value so I stayed home. At home,
the major project was continued repair work on the rotary mower (Bush Hog).
No pictures, but after many fits and starts I think it is operational
again. Need to make a few more test runs and will then be ready to go mow
the hillside.

Tuesday was 1,000 mile checkup on my skin cancers. The place on my head is
so far, so good. She zapped about one dozen or so and said come back in six
months. We talked about Herb and his skin cancer. It is unusual and
reassuring to find a Dr. who is willing to show such personal concern for a
patient (him, not me).

The LBT got hit a couple of licks.

​You should note the auxiliary fuel tank. Will probably have to build some
sort of cover over it if decide to leave it when Charlie, Cliff and Kenneth
take their trip to Alaska.

I cranked on it a bit and engine turned ok but after some problem getting a
spark it hit on 2-3 cylinders but only for a few seconds. So, off came the
carb which was delivered to the pro shop (i.e. Dwight) for rebuilding. It
has been returned and retesting should soon resume.

A test run was also made on The MM.

​
​iT also cranked ok but never hit a lick. No spark at plugs. The hood has
since been removed and further testing will soon resume. Stay tuned. The
extra person is Dave Edwards. Dave has sold his business in NC and is
heading out on a US tour in his giant Provost motor home. He has a really
nice, looks like new '04 dually which he would love to place with a good
home.

GSH has been working on refinishing some collector items for last few
weeks. The cat has been helping her.

​
In the meantime, the hardtop has been left on the car as it gets left out
in the rain. So, we are now in the process of erecting the canopy in the
usual location.

We had birthday B & G event for Wayne. But, no pictures.

Also had an event for PBBD.

Beth and Jake attended. They are in the process of moving to the Greenville
SC area and purchasing a house with a creek and 10A of land. I am in the
process of trying to fix Jake up with an appropriate tractor.

I know lots of GC when off to college (or college work) this week. Allison
started class and teaching at ETSU. (By the way, Lisa started teaching at
Milligan.) Th ones most directly impacting me this week (literally and
figuratively as it turned out) were the Colsons.

Same thumb as the one I mashed last week.

For various and sundry reasons I went to visit the shrinking Colson family
Thursday evening and stayed over on Friday. As you may know, each family
works out their own modus operanti. The Colsons buy things with small gas
engines and when they quit, as small gas engines do, they buy another
keeping the old one just in case. Linda always has a list of projects for
me in order. First on the list war rebuilding the washed out fence.
Fortunately willing workers were waiting to spring into action.

​However the fence did get repaired.

​
​Then the small engine repair work began.

​
​and continued.

​
Much opportunity remains. It seems Andrew has the interest to make an
excellent small engine repair man. Stay tuned.
