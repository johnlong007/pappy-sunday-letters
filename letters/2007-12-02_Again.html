December 2, 2007 · Sunday

Again

8 photographs·198 words

For do-gooder Monday we helped with the church decorations.
Tuesday was trail work. Moved rocks, big rocks, all day. We installed 9
steps in a switchback and put stepping stones across a creek.
Stopped to see Aunt G on the way home. Rusty and "Carter" were there. Aunt G
finally found something to talk about besides "The Will".
We worked clearing brush and moving logs at Blountville a couple of times.
The barn is actually going up.
A big Hawk is keeping an eye out from that tree above the garden.
The big event for the week was helping Herb getting his (Cliff's) IH tractor
so it will start without using ether.

So I will visit from march 26 to 29 or 30. Just for a couple of days on may
way back to Germany. I will fly in from Washington Dulles direct to TRI or
to Charlotte.

Alf is East German. He was a soldier in the East German army in the 80's. He
is an itinerant electrician, a world traveler/adventurer. He was our escort
on the Hungary bike ride last year. I invited him to come visit.
