September 1, 2019 · Sunday

Family

10 photographs·271 words

God, family, country. Still important here.

Do you remember last week I included a picture of little girl Greer and
posed the question of family resemblance ? I studied at length and think I
see Maria.

I read this week that a missing link in the human family tree has been
identified with a face constructed. So, the question is, who in the family
most resembles?

I spent a fair amount of time mowing this week but it looks just like last
time so no picture.

No work on the '63 either. However , we did quite a bit on the spare '63.
The only parts I swapped out were the hood and the radiator. I decided to
go ahead and spray primer on the hood.

When I did , a bubble came up which GSH could not resist touching. It
appears there was a drop of oil on the hood. Now, I will get to refinish
the whole hood. Rats !

The car had rust all around the rear window. I took the trim out and turned
over to GSH to polish while I started repair on the rust places.

Stay tuned, miles to go.

Last week, a picture came in from the left coast.

Various family members arrived from hither and yon to visit the new
residence of our RAF family members.

One all the way from NY with a new toy to demonstrate.
