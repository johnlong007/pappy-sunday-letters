February 14, 2010 · Sunday

Review

7 photographs·242 words

Sue had Hal brought home from the hospital Friday. We stopped by on the way
home from church today. He is on hospice care. He is mostly sleeping.

Dave is still having trouble getting his pills adjusted and is not able to
do much work.

The weather continued cold and snowy so Carl cancelled the AT work day. He
has alrady canceled this week work day also.

Now the good news;

John got his car repaired and back on the road. We shall see if he learned
to keep an eye on the CV joint boots.

Wayne also discovered the virtues of procrastination haveing to poach wood
in the snow.

Speaking of snow, who knows what is pictured here?

I have pictures of birds and deer but unless there is a specific request I
believe I shall skip and go on to more imporatant subject matter.
I always like to include a picture of my willing worker. In this weeks
picture. it can be seen that event when he is resting he stands guard.

Once way back when in a previous life, I counted up how many more band
concerts I would get to attend. At that time, grandchildren never crossed my
mind. Those were for old people.
Now, we have a double header. Look close.

Of course, the really big news that all are waiting for is the car. It has
a coat of primer ! Now, serious sanding can start.
