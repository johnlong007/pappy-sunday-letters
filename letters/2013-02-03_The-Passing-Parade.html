February 3, 2013 · Sunday

The Passing Parade

14 photographs·260 words

Dave Dunham lost his only brother to lung cancer this week. In the
last three years he has lost a sister, his Mom and now his brother.
His Dad has been at deaths door for years with lung cancer. On the
good side, he thinks his Lyme disease is under control .

I gave up on moving the brush from the big Oak we cut last Summer and
burned the pile. It left a fine mound of ashes so we decided to try to
bake sweet potatoes. Leaving them overnight is too long (unless you
like roasted/charbroiled sweet potatoes).

AT work was back at Chestoa digging trail. It was a perfect, tee
shirt, day. A big crew, 12 I think, we made a lot of progress.

Then it turned cold and I moved indoors. I spent two days sorting
nuts, bolts etc. I kept asking myself "why am I doing this?"

After I finished that job back on the '37 PU. Sanded the inside of the
hood, primed it and had the first serious fitting on the cab.

In case anyone thinks we went to Union City to visit my sister just to
be thoughtful look at the pie.

After the snow came down, the birds came out.

Growing up, in the Shallow Ford Community, children attended Temple
Hill school. The actual Temple Hill is the background mountain in this
picture taken from Herb's front yard.

He has four of the fattest goats since the Billy Goat Gruff. The two
white ones (mother and daughter) are awaiting transportation to
Dechard.
