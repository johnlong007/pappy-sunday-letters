April 3, 2011 · Sunday

Staying Tuned

9 photographs·411 words

Does it count as do-gooder work when it is within the family? How about if
one is having fun, does that count?
Dave has about dropped out on me so this week I recruited Dwight to help
with a project for a little ole widow. A remarkable person she is 91 and
still drives and gads about. She is also very finicky about her house and
property. I have made a number of house calls with Dave. This time, she had a
drippy outside faucet. One of those deep wall, freeze proof things.We (that
is Dwight) tried to repair it then gave up and replaced it. It was fun
project but sort of slow and I forgot to take camera so you need a mental
picture of her leaning out the back door keeping an eye on Dwight.

For AT work, the group went to Carver's gap on top of Roan Mountain.
Jonathan went along. My geology expert tells me that the big rock about 1/2 way
up the hill is one of the oldest in the US at about 1.6 B years. If you are
into young earth, it is still 1.6 B years old but was created that way
6,000 years ago.Or maybe 6 days.

I worked replacing the non-skid track on the bog bridges. Jonathan worked
on draining swampy places in the trail but I was not with him to take a
picture. However, I did get good reports on his work ethic.

I suppose it will be ok to insert a smoky mtn picture. It was made in the
general direction of the park.

In the more mundane, GSH has about finished the carpet padding in the car.
Next the actual carpet!

The mandatory trip to Maryville was to run the engine on the test stand
with a new carburator. It has a single four inch venturi with a single four
inch throttle valve.After much pondering, tweaking and calling the factory
the engine produced and additional 30 HP, up to 830 and everyone was
delighted.

CHCC is adding a giant family life center which will soon open for
business. Saturday, we helped move book and furniture into the new and improved
library.
