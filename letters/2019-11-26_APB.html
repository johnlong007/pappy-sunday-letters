Life is short and full of trials. Great and small.

How about maddening?

In 1970 the family loaded in the van and drove to San Francisco, caught a
TWA fly to Hawaii and visited with brother Herb and his family for a few
days. (Would you believe we drove via Seattle on the way home?)

I put the pictures from that trip in an album, A blue fold out album. Just
like the first Alaska trip except blue. I cannot find the album. I need
serious help.
