Thrills, Adventure (No Romance)

11 photographs·166 words

Pretty normal all in all, I went to hand Dr, who sent me to hand therapist.
Spent a day working on the AT and harvested a sack full of ramps on the
way home. Watched a baseball game and a soccer meet. Took the Studebaker to
Erwin to get it painted. Went with Dwight to Seymour to check out the data
collection systems. Went to a country ham dinner with Nancy , Lewis and
grandson Dillon. Started tractor mowing here and finished off the week by
harvesting more ramps with Meredith and cutting a few trees for Anita (Very
carefully).
