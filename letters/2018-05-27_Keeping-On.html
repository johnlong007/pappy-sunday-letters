May 27, 2018 · Sunday

keeping On

13 photographs·427 words

Many a slip twix cup and lip. I have heard.

Almost all know how much I enjoy working on small engines. Lisa brought her
non-functioning mower for serious application on the tender touch. It is
now operable and perched in the back of the truck waiting for pickup or
delivery.

​
I wonder how many know the history of this right front fender from the '63?
I brought it down from the storage building to use in locating placement of
the side emblems on the car. Spots are now marked but not drilled. Fender
is back in storage to await its fate.

​I suppose under the do-gooder heading, Dave Edwards brought window runner
from his car project over my somewhat less then Divine guidance. Best I
could tell his was don't force it and don't get in a hurry.

​This week was back to Memphis to see Dr. Allison. My first appointment was
at 1PM on Wednesday. We were afraid to risk trying to get there on time
going Wed. morning so we went down Tuesday but went via Union City to visit
Lil' Sister.
A fun visit with her and her new dog. I did not take any pictures as she
looks about the same as last time but did copy a page from people of Obion
County book.

​
On to Memphis. Allison has moved into a different gated community. We went
for a river walk one evening.

​Allison did not get through the gate in time.

​So we went on without her. It is a fine, long river walk trail. Clean and
well maintained.

​%^&$(*#$@*!!
Got home to find ground hog(s) had eaten strawberry plants just as season
was in full swing. May just give up.

​
Followed by the rains came down and the floods came up. Cutting a pretty
good gully through the field.

​Family members were coming into town from far and near. Katy had put in an
order for ramps to feed some of those from afar as an authentic hillbilly
food. So, Sarah cleaned a quantity. Then we took the nubs up on the
hillside among the May apples and planted.
Later, since Katy did not show, we helped out by having fried potatoes and
ramps for supper. Duty called.

​
Joe and Anita had an event at their house Saturday evening to amuse and
perhaps edify the Wisconsin visitors.
Far away family visitors were missed but talked about. Fill in your name
far away family member.

​Do the Wisconsin visitors look amused, edifyed or mystified?
