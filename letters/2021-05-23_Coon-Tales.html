May 23, 2021 · Sunday

Coon Tales

8 photographs·359 words

Laura gave me 4 tomato plants. I planted in a row beside the driveway
across from go around. The next day one plant had been dug up and cast
aside. I replanted and put cages around all the plants. Next day, another
plant had been dug up and cast aside. I dug out my live animal trap and the
next day had a fine, fat coon.
Not wanted to exterminate him (or her, or it, whatever) I took the cage
over past the junkyard and turned him loose.
The next night, I heard a commotion, looked out and the coon was under the
bird feeder having a snack on spilled sunflower seeds. That evening Andrew
helped and we set the trap again baited with marshmallows. Sure enough,
this morning the coon was back in the trap.
To get back this time he will need to swim across the lake and walk 9
miles. Stay tuned.

Most every Friday, we go to the Burger Hut in Elizabethton for breakfast
with a bunch of Sanford's family and classmates.. One will not find a nicer
bunch of folks but no spring chickens.

Tis the season for graduating, getting married or both. Andrew graduated
last week and will be heading to Utah this coming week to join the ranks of
taxpayers. He and Adam, already well graduated , came through to visit the
ole folks home. I was able to get some work out of each.

Steven also graduated and is on his way to higher levels of academic
pursuit. I think he is in the picture below. He tends to fly under the
radar.

Another graduate !

Back in River City, the part to replace the replacement part arrived and
has been installed. Just in time as the place gets ahead of me quite
rapidly. I resisted mowing today. Although it would be fun some might think
it work.

I had hoarded some ramps. Saturday morning was eggs, bacon , toast and
ramps. Two bad no one has taught me how to make biscuits.
