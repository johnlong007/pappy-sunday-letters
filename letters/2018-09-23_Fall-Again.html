September 23, 2018 · Sunday

Fall (Again)

14 photographs·424 words

I mentioned going to Dr. last week. This week they called for me to come
back in for additional sampling. Pale and anemic. Checking to see if losing
blood, not making enough or something else. Stay tuned.

All the heavy rains some time back cut a trench down through the walnut
field. We chopped up the pine branches from tree Wayne topped and used
branches to line the trench. Will see how well that works.

Dave and Pat Edwards came by again. Dave is working on the rear window
mechanism of the '63 Plymouth. He mainly needs a lot of reassurance.

Thursday I went on AT work day. The state had put a culvert under a gravel
road which is located about 75 feet up the hill above the trail. The runoff
from the culvert comes right down and across the trail. So, we put in our
own culvert.

Our SS class is a bunch of old folks. Friday we went to lunch at Farmer's
Daughter in Chucky. Waited over an hour to get in. It is family style and
very good. Worth the wait? Maybe.

Sunday we went to a birthday (90) event for Bob Harvey, one of the
co-founders (along with Collins Chew) of the Friday Hikers. Another bunch
of old folks but also fun.

Laura made a birthday present for Bob, he was quite pleased.

Saturday night we went to a drive in movie. First time in a long, long time.
Rachel, we were at the Holiday drive in theater in Erwin in June 1956. GSH
said she had to visit rest room. Came back and said "We better go to
hospital". Fortunately, it was almost next door.Sure, enough, she was
correct.

The movie was "Rachel and The Stranger". A couple of years back I bought a
video of the movie so we could see the finish. You may have it.

Saturday Diane worked on prepping apples to dry, Wayne worked on gutters
and interior designer came by to consult on pool room tile work. We feel
most fortunate.

The mouse milk trial failed. Car is back in shop waiting for valve guide
seals. Only the cat is glad.

It has been pointed out to me that I missed a couple of cat owners last
week. For those, can your cat do this trick?

Anyone planning to be by here this week?
