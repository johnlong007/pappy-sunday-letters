Irresistible Force

10 photographs·223 words

Some years back I fabricated a wood splitter to mount on the tractor. My
brother Herb donated the hydraulic cylinder. Foraged the rest.
In several years of operation I learned many things.
Most important is something will give when the cylinder is actuated, if the
piston and wood to be split are in a straight line, all is well.

PBBD was helping and we were moving right along when untrained helper
showed up

Put a piece to be split in on and angle and broke my wiels with no effort.

In the finest tradition of making lemonade and counting it as fun( God will
understand). sinc Wayne has been wanting to develop welding skills we put
him on the job today. His work looks OK but needs a new pin to attach the
cylinder at the front. Then test time. Stay tuned.

I took welding classes at the voc school. Final exam was weling a 4" pipe
held in a horizontal position (requiring a 360 weld) the pipe then put in a
test rige and bent 360 degree.
