July 9, 2017 · Sunday

4th Try

16 photographs·202 words

Bob sponsored a picnic at Rec. area Monday PM to celebrate fourth and to
let family meet Benj's A & M friends.

​It was a fine event.
The Texas trio came by to check out the firing range. I was impressed by
their skill and knowledge in handling firearms.

​

Then Tuesday PM, we had the Blountville parade. Another fine event. It
seemed to me that a larger crowd was in attendance. Flags, band, fire
engines all the good things some people still like.

​Tuesday evening Joe sponsored an event.

Entertainment was provided by passing babies around and by a P & G ensemble.

​Charlie came by for another go at the fire engine. We have decided to try
for next years July 4th parade.

​Lawn mowing went better. Beth took two of hers for a spin on the mower
while I entertained the third.

​AT work was building a bridge over the Doe River where the trail crosses
19E.

​To be continued this week.

I received a strange picture from Christopher. It looks like he may have
taken up competitive log rolling. ​Anyone know anything ? I may have to
check.

​

I wonder if Rachel is still married? And Aaron?
