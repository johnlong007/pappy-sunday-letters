I Walk the Line

8 photographs·614 words

Actually, several of us walked the line.

​When we purchased the first tract of property on Reservoir road we did not
have it surveyed. A survey of the front part was made when D & D built
their house(s) and the line on East side was surveyed when the property on
that side was sold some years back. Now, Alan is in the process of getting
the West side line surveyed. The back is well marked as it joins the
park.After the survey is completed serious thought will be given as to
having the timber harvested. Some is due, some overdue.

While we were back over the hill in the back I gave Rachel a lot of
suggestions to think about concerning double wide trailers or perhaps even
a log cabin with an outhouse. Never heard of anyone having to clean that
kind of bathroom. They do have to be relocated from time to time. When I
went on a mission trip to Romania the first work assignment I got was
building an outhouse. It all came back to me.

Aaron and Chelsea were in with their ( growing in more ways than one)
family. No pictures there but I did get to talk with Chelsea a bit and put
in a request for data on her pedigree to make part of the family pictorial
record.

I mentioned some time back cracking a tooth and getting Dr. Allison to look
it over. Well, I should have gone to Memphis to get her to make crown. I
could have paid her first years tuition. Anyway, I have been crowned again.

As part of the hillside project, I got the big trailer out to work on
making it legal. It needs brakes on all wheels. Now, only has on one axle.
Also will get the lights working and install mud flaps. I am going to get
it so I can haul the crawler or get rid of it. Stay tuned.

While working on the trailer and the towing vehicle, it has been determined
that a tire cord has separated.

​Since only one is defective I plan to replace the one with a used tire and
wait to buy a full set.

The garden is growing nicely with peppers and tomatoes. These are in the
turn around.

The peach tree had a pretty good crop but the peaches have been
evaporating, We have been blaming the resident gron' hogs and I know they
are guilty but tonight I apprehended two dear under the tree. That would
explain disappearing without even hitting the ground. Anyway, a better crop
than most years.

​For AT work it was back to the same place. Andrew might recognize this
picture. Section ten is finished and ends 5 feet from the trail. Ten new
sections are marked waiting for the ASU Students.We will be going back to
the side this week to do some side logs and rock work.

​I went in to see Dr, Sago this week. She has sort of become the family
Dr. She asked about various ones and talked about how she and her husband
had worked out education, job and family issues. Also worked on the top of
my head right near the one which caused so much trouble.

Picture upon request.

I am trying to get organized to go visit my Lil' sister. Linda did talk
with her and confirmed she is alive and well. About all one can ask I
suppose.

I have been looking at the route of the upcoming solar event.. I want to go
to the center. Is Cookeville the best option for our extended family?

We need a planner and a plan.
