July 17, 2022 · Sunday

Events

12 photographs·425 words

Some will remember Perkins waitress Connie. Since GSH has quit going in
with me on Sunday morning, Connie has been bringing me a Muffin to bring
home. Also, David has been dropping it off for her on his way back to
Blountville while I go on to church.

One of the surprises of my innocent youth was going into our house in Erwin
when Hazel and Sherrill were there visiting. Spock was in the house ! My
father allowed it! " A daughter is a daughter for all her life"

Today, Don met me at the door at church and said he had some tomatoes for
me. He knows GSH likes yellow tomatoes.

LLLL made a special trip home to bring her Mom a fresh, home grown peach !

After SS , one of the Hyder girls (sister of Gene) came to me and said she
had been instructed to determine if we were interested in receiving meals
on wheels. Gave me the whole spiel. I exuded appreciation and gratitude and
told her things might change but for now family and friends are taking care
of us.

As you should remember, the tractor has three lights which go on with
ignition and off with engine start IF all is OK.Now all are functioning
properly.

A new alternator . a new oil pressure switch, and a new hydraulic vacuum
switch.

No progress on '55 this week. One of the next steps will be getting bumpers
rechromed . many $$$$. Dragged spares out of storage to decide which to
have done.

PBBD did quite a bit of work on the '37. We think the brakes are back ready
for testing. We had a lot of leaks in the system

The steering wheel has about 4 inches of free play. Should be about 1/2
inch. There is no adjustment travel left on the gearbox. Time for plan B.

The thermal energy storage tank has been out of service a long time. Plan
is to get it back in operation this Summer. The first step required is
sealing around the top between the wall insulation and the tile floor. Work
started but on hold waiting for sealant resupply. Intent is to fill, cover,
collect data and get PBBD to determine what we will need in the way of
solar collectors to keep it warm all Winter.
