What Now

13 photographs·210 words

A skiff of snow Sunday night. Followed a salt truck in on the way to CC.

Diane delivered a ham biscuit while I waited for blood to arrive.

It takes two people to enter blood into PC before entering it into me.

FIFO, Linda. I am humbled and grateful to those who have donated and are
donating.

Bob wanted to see David's wood work shop.

While there, he toured the motor home. It is now in florida for the next
several weeks,

We finished the small pieces of beech. We saved the big pieces for stout
young WW's.

Then we restocked the fireplace wood supply.

The '37 wheels have clips to hold hub caps. Some of the clips were broken.
Diane broght the wheel in to replace the clips.

One day she got the shop stove going and warmed the shop enough to work
there. Here, she is applying the final coat of paint to the repaired
headlight pedestals.

Then serious snow arrived. The caped crusader shoveled a path to the wood
rack.

Some time ago, we installed pin flags to border the driveway.
