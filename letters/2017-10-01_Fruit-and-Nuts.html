October 1, 2017 · Sunday

Fruit and Nuts

14 photographs·386 words

Some may find it difficult to believe but not everyone recognized all the
harvest I posted before. So, for the benefit of those who thought nuts came
in bags and fruit in cans:

Katy can identify.

​​
Today a person showed up who had never tasted a persimmon. The taste
exceeded her expectation !

​This has been a bumper year for everything except BB's. We are buried in
black walnuts.

​
On the subject of walnuts, how about these, also a big crop, in the front
yard and down near the hazel nut bushes.

I assume everyone knows what these are

​The trees are loaded. The burrs fall on the ground where the deer stomp to
open and eat the nuts. The turkeys scurry around to eat the table scraps.

​
At long last, we waxed the car. It continues to be fine convertible weather.

​As an item of interest, GSH dug out a candle holder, a candle stick, and
spent a lot of time polishing.

​She does not remember who passed it on to her. Does anyone? If anyone
wants to put in a bid, tell her.

As noted elsewhere, a pie person stopped by one day. Not simple Simon.

​Anita showed up with an axe head Paul had found. I promised to clean it up
so she can give back to him on his 21st. birthday.

​AT work day was great. We went to Bitter End and walked in about a mile.
As usual, I busted rocks most all day. Hard to find a better work site for
a perfect temperature day.

​It is also hard to believe what a fun group to work with. Several of the
grandchildren know what I mean. On the sad side, Saturday we went to a
memorial service for one of the team.​

To top off a fine day, Supper was waiting when I got home (lunch was 6
peanut butter crackers and a moon pie).

Are all aware Beth has scheduled an apple butter day for the 21st? Various
entertainment will be provided. Fire engine rides (if Charlie gets it
running) Wood splitting competition a new champion may be crowned sine
Gwendolyn is in a far away​ place with strange people.

Other things to numerous to recount occurred but enough for now. I hear
duty calling.

​
