Al Capp and the Dogpatch economy

The land in Dogpatch would only grow turnips, and every year the Turnip
Termites would eat all the turnip blossoms. Hilariously starving people.

In the 40's and 50's newspaper comic strips were a part of life. I think
everyone who grew up during that period knew all the characters in Lil'
Abner.

One year while we were in high school some club sponsored a costume event.
Patricia came as Mommy Yokum, complete with corncob pipe and took the prize.

Some years later, when Alan displayed a voracious appetite for mother milk,
we began to refer to him as "turnip termite" which soon got shortened to
turnip for us and the immediate family. The practice followed for all the
others as their turns came.

Now, if I refer to your pride and joy as a" turnip" you will know why.

It has been a good turnip year, More here and more to come

My view of the world for most of the week.

So far ,so good. I think the next round is in early April. Until then, Lab
blood tests every Monday. Usually, it takes two weeks to bottom out then
start recovery until the next round.

Life on the farm goes on. Fruit trees produce bigger and better if pruned
if sprayed with dormant oil spray.

The sap is rising in all nature including young men.

My PC consultant. One more trip should do it.

My truck is still out of commission.. But, the cavalry arrived.

The log we dragged out, sawed up then split is now stacked waiting for next
Winter.

Remember the giant Oak we have worked on for the last couple of years? it
is being processed one board at a time.

Rachel had a birthday, both literally and figuratively. Her grandmother and
mother came to the party. Her GM has led an incredible life.I hope to add
her story to the family history project.

Sarah keeps saying she wants to have Paw Paw trees. Her mother decided to
help out.
