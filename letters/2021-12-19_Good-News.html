Good News.

At last ! Lil Sister had her artificial Aortic valve installed . It went
well and she is back home getting TLC from daughter Martha.

On the home front, a group from Blountville Baptist came by and sang
Christmas carols to/for us. A first!

My foot is healing, still painful but not nearly as much.

In this picture you can see the cradle Bob built for EJ.

In this picture you can see the cradle I built for Laura. And then Linda.
And then Wayne.

Tghe project for the week was to free the engine in the '65. Squirted the
cylinders with mouse milk, poured diesel fuel into intake mabnifold and did
a lot of straining. No success. to be continued, stay tuned.

Thehome health nurse comes by two times week. I asked this one if she had
ever seen the nurse clothes of yor. She said she had to were one for her
graduation picture. First and last time.

We got a card from Ev Adams, our church friend in the UK.

God news and bad news about the Blountville Christmas parade. The weather
was not good so spectators were sparse. But, the candy throwers had candy
to spare and threw in our direction. Normally we get ignored not being cute
or adorable.

The last load of wood had a few oversize pieces. PBBD attacked wit a
vengence. My plan is to restock with sawed to length sections in case
additional willing workers show up around the holidays.
