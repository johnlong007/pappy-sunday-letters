February 12, 2017 · Sunday

Running Around

9 photographs·379 words

Rejoice !

The automobile fleet has been reduced by one. At least my fleet. Some time
back I told Linda that upon her oath signed in blood that she would find a
competent mechanic/garage and have all routine and non-routine work
performed on the Ferrari as recommended and/or needed, I would sign it over
to her.

She assured me that she has found such a person and put him on the payroll.
The first bad news was that broken signal light stem cannot be repaired and
must be replaced.

Monday I went in for a routine check up by cardiologist. All is well. He
said the reason I did not have any "spells" after the stents was because I
was completely off the various pills the quack had prescribed. The reason I
get much reduced spells when I push to hard now is because I am back on but
MUCH reduced dosage. He did strongly caution against extreme exertion.

Most of the effort around here was on the '49. I continued patching rusted
out places while GSH did painting on places which will be out of sight in
the fully assembled state.

​Summer Camp people will remember the incenerator with fondness. Easy to
see I am clean out of willing workers to help with routine maintenance.

​Ironic, is that the word? A few years back GSH and I had no pills and few
Dr. visits. Fortunately, we did find a competant, willing worker to keep
our pills sorted and our appointmens scheduled. I am thrilled to have help.
GSH is less excited about the whole scene.

​On the other hand, the cat could not care less.

​
Saturday was the 5K run sponsored by Linda's church. Linda trotted along
with me the entire route. The first mile is mostly downhill meaning the
last mile is uphill. But it was perfect for my situation. I can only
increase speed very slowly. So, believe it or not, the return was easier
and maybe faster than the beginning part.

​We had rounded the final corner and were slowing to a stop. I believe I
actually ran the last 100 yards or so. Anyway, it went better than I
expected. I had told the Dr. dead is ok, dead last is not.
