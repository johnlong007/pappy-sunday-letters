August 30, 2009 · Sunday

OK

12 photographs·308 words

I suppose everyone knows I am a hunt and peck typist. Also, I seem to
always end up starting much later on my report than I intend. The net result is
not much text and heavy on the pictures. Anyway, a picture is worth more
than ten thousand words. I suppose that was before it became so easy to
doctor pictures.

Another week, another birthday event. This one was actually on the day.

The recent windstorm blew a tree down on the corner of the barn roof. We
replaced the damaged part with slightly used tin . If I can obtain enough, I
would like to cover the entire roof.

I was out checking for tomatoes one evening when these three turkeys came
strolling down the driveway, turned in at the tomato patch and proceeded to
eat all the small tomatoes.

I put the live trap beside the Mr. Stripey tomato plant and captured
another culprit. We took him/her on a field trip.

The state of VA. purchased a 5,000 A. trac of land which is now the
"Channels State Forest. We were asked to come help lay out hiking trails for
them. It was a fun day but at the end we decided it would be best to start over.
Stay tuned.

I spotted this barn on the way over and stopped for a picture on the way
home. I figured Anita would like it.

A friend gave us a basket of Elder berries.

Saturday we had a special AT work day with 66 students (mostly girls) from
ASU. Here, Bob Peoples is keeping them entertained during lunch break.

Sunday, Charlene had a birthday party for her mom and her brother Greg. We
went to that then, after church, we went to a church friends house for Ice
Cream. A record for being social in one week.
