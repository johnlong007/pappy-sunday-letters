February 7, 2016 · Sunday

Normal ?

10 photographs·280 words

I hope Herb has bottomed out and beginning to recover. They have cut back
on some of the medicines which seems to have helped his responses. With
help and effort he is able to sit in chair or wheelchair.

I worked on the FE a couple of brief spells in early PM when sun is shining
on the right front wheel. Got the brake drum and wheel cylinder removed. I
also crawled around underneath it to try to figure the brake system.
Appears to have a vacuum booster system ,

Mostly I helped GSH with '63 interior and, while not doing that I continued
cleaning underneath.

We did AT work on Thursday. The shelter at Watauga lake has proven to be
quite popular with the local bears. So much so that the section has been
closed to day hikers for the last year. We worked at the shelter, some
procuring and installing a stone step and other pondering the bear proof
pack pole.

The people doing Studebaker upholstery called Saturday "bring your trailer
and $ and come get it at 4 PM". We went over at 4 without trailer or $. It
was not near ready. They vowed and declared it will be ready tomorrow. Stay
tuned.

When we got home the deer were at play.

Did go to the barn and bring up a small load of wood. Enough for maybe a
couple of weeks.

Wayne came by to give a hand on odds 'n ends. I believe (hope) the tractor
fuel problem is resolved. Also installed a LED ligh fixture in kitchen. I
would like to phase out the old light fixtures in dining room and the shop.o
