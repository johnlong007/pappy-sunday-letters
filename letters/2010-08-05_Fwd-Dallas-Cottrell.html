Band Members,

Francess Cottrell asked me to send you a message informing you that her father, Dallas, passed away at home Tuesday evening about 8:15 p.m. without any pain. Both Frances and her sister Gail were with him. For you new people, Dallas has been a member of our trombone section for many years and a mainstay of the band. He played until not too many months ago - this year missing the July 4 parade for the first time I can remember.

They would be honored if members of the Community Band would be honorary pall bearers. Visitation will be on Saturday from 9:30 am to 11:00 am with the services immediately following at 11 - all of this will be at Oak Hill Funeral Home.

I have inserted below the obituary from today's Kingsport Times-News.

Ellen Finney

C. Dallas Cottrell
KINGSPORT - C. Dallas Cottrell, 91, passed away peacefully at his home in Kingsport on Tuesday evening (Aug. 3, 2010). Dall was born on Oct. 15, 1918, in Straw, Mont., and was a son of Ralph E. and Bess Lancaster Cottrell.

A few weeks later, his mother died of the 1918 Spanish flu and his father relocated the family to Newkirk, Okla., where Dall grew up. Dall played trombone in his school orchestra and developed a lifelong love of music. He graduated from Oklahoma A&M (now Oklahoma State University) with a degree in chemical engineering and came to Kingsport to work for Tennessee Eastman Company in June 1942. He worked in the acid division, lastly serving as superin- tendent of the plasticizer department, and retired in 1979. He was a charter member of the Local Chapter of the American Institute of Chemical Engineers.

In Kingsport, Dall met the love of his life, Nina Watson from Havana, Fla., and they were married June 23, 1946. Dall designed and together they built their home in Preston Woods. He enjoyed woodworking all his life, making many beautiful pieces of furniture for himself and his family. He also had a lifelong appreciation of steam engines and spent many happy hours visiting steam engine shows and museums. Dall was very involved in the restoration of the Exchange Place where he helped rebuild the Cook's Cabin and volunteered as the blacksmith on occasions. Until recently, Dall and Nina were active in their church community, Mountain View United Methodist Church, where Dall held all key leadership positions over the years.

After his retirement from Eastman, Dall picked up his trombone again and played in the Has Beens Dance Band and the Kingsport Community Band, thoroughly enjoying all their performances, especially playing in the Fourth of July parade. Always finding plenty of reasons to laugh, Dall thought the greatest asset anyone could have is a well developed sense of humor. Dall fully enjoyed life, loved his family and friends, enjoyed his work, especially enjoyed his 31 years of retirement, and delighted in making music with his friends. Dallas Cottrell will be missed and fondly remembered by all who knew him.

He was preceded in death by his parents, Ralph E. and Bess L. Cottrell; his stepmother, Lillian Cottrell; and his only sibling, Eugene E. Cottrell.

He is survived by Nina W. Cottrell, his wife of 64 years, of the home; daughters, Gail (John) Campbell and Frances Cottrell, both of Kingsport;

grandson, Jay (Sarita) Campbell, Charlottesville, Va.; granddaughters, Emily (Tommy) Boles, Greensboro, N.C., Alexandra Cottrell, Charlotte, N.C., and Abigail Cottrell, Kingsport; nephew, Fred (Janie) Cottrell, Conway Springs, Kan.

Visitation will be held in the J. Wayne Hamilton Memorial Chapel of Oak Hill Funeral Home on Saturday from 9:30 to 11 a.m. Funeral services will follow with the Rev. Robert Countiss officiating and eulogy by Jim Skillen.

A graveside service will follow the funeral at Oak Hill Memorial Park in the Garden of Everlasting Life.

Pallbearers will be Bob Abernathy, Jim Finney, Scot Fleming, Dan Gonzales, Wayne Kilgore, and Gordon Porter. Honorary pallbearers will be the members of the Has Beens, the Kingsport Community Band, and the Friday "Board of Directors" lunch group.

In lieu of flowers, the family requests donations to the Kingsport Community Band, in c/o of Ellen Finney, 5404 Heritage Lane, Kingsport, Tenn. 37664.

The staff of Oak Hill is honored to serve the family of C. Dallas Cottrell.
