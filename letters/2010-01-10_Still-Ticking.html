January 10, 2010 · Sunday

Still Ticking

7 photographs·271 words

It has been cold and snow covered in the hills of East Tennessee. Perhaps
not by the standards of the frozen North but enough to make one pine for a
touch of global warming.

Being dauntless as well as not too bright, Dave and I got back into the
swing of do-gooder work Monday. But, being not quite mentally in the game I
failed to take the camera. Of course, we did and outside project which took
several hours.

I also got by to check on Hal. he is still himself but not very encouraged.
His substitute Dr. told him to consider hospice rather than further
treatment. Regular Dr. will be back on duty this week. A conference is planned to
decide on future course of action.

Working on the car consumed most of my discretionary time. But, Dwight was
spending time helping Charlie with the Spitfire so he was not available
very much. We still got a trial dashboard pattern installed. The fender inner
liner splash shields did not come back from JW. So. replacements are being
fabricated in the field. GSH cut out a pattern.


My dog misses puppy but is also enjoying being an only dog. It has made me
appreciate him more .

Guarding my clothes, guarding a bone and escorting GSH to the mail box (the
high point of the day).

Not everyone has a GSH willing to help with all sorts of projects, however,
the job does have some rewards.

I only missed one day running due to weather. After I got back today I
checked BP.

115/66 Think I will stop testing right there.
