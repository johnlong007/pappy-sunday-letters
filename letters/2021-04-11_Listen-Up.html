April 11, 2021 · Sunday

Listen Up !

9 photographs·448 words

I your dentist tells you to avoid stick candy, pay attention ! When Allison
was working on one of my gold crowns her German professor checked in. He
said something like " this one must be gold, if he loses everything he can
knock it out and sell it".
I forgot about the sticky candy and pulled the crown off. I remembered his
advice and asked Allison if I should sell it.
PBBD lept into action and glued it back in place. The next step is to be
determined.

All the tires on the car I plan to bring back to life are flat. I made two
trips to Erwin this week fiddling with that car and working on other items.
I brought one of the wheels with a ruptured tire back over here. Bloke it
down. picked the best tire I could find in inventory and mounted it on the
wheel. It leaked. Leaked on inside and outside bead. Broke down and cleaned
3 times. Finally decided there must be another problem, put it in the pan
of soapy water.
See the bubbles? nail hole.
Took it back to Erwin and found I had not mounted it on the same wheel at
all and wheel does not fit the car.

Attention to detail, my middle name.

I have got the brakes fried on one side and will eventually get all the
tires inflated and mounted- I hope.

I thought GSH looked quite spiffy for church today and decided to take her
picture after we got home. In looking for just the right spot I noticed a
patch of flowers. Anyone know what they are? Max?

SS reopened today, the same people who were there last time we opened were
there today. The really old folks. Three of them ar real close to 90 on one
side or the other, All have been well shot. But, All except us were still
wearing masks. How will this ever end?

The shop clean up project continues. I have a 40 year accumulation of nuts
and bolts. Sorting and repackaging are continuing at a moderate pace.;

Toda, we went for a walk on the one mile track at school where Niece Lisa
works. Along the way we passed a group taking what seemed obvious to me to
be the pre-wedding pictures. Knowing that we have a wedding in the works
and might be considering such pictures I thought they might be interested
in seeing what is in vogue.

Tried to get a ramp run organized this week but failed. Stay tuned.
