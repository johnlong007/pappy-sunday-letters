Rejoice

13 photographs·251 words

.
for most of 2020 21, 22 .and early in 2023 I was running 20 laps/day at the
track. Today, for the first time in a long time, I walked a lap.

I am most fortunate. More than almost everyone.

Meredith pointed to me and asked Julia "who is that". She grinned, pointed
a little finger and announced "Pappy !".

The nurses (and other staff) at the CC are really good to me. In all this
time I've never had a bad experience.

Diane says she likes to learn new things. I like to help her. This week, it
was how to sharpen an axe. With a file ! Never a grinder !

Lesson 2 was how to grub a stump.

So far, in spite of many leads, looks, requests, hints and whimpering ,no
new outside implement project has been located.

So, we have been moved inside to the 1937. We drag parts outside when signs
are right. A lot of time and effort into refurbishing the front bumper.

Also outside with the wheels and hub caps.

Turns out the dustman also does roof leaf blowing.

I would never have been able to stay ahead of nature without this machine.
Not only does it mow it also shreds leaves.

and pushes brush.

All the lower limbs have been trimmed and pushed into the brush pond.
