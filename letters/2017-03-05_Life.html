For many years the family would not have a single trip to see a Dr.
Sometimes one trip to take an un-named child to the emergency room.
Now, it is news when we go a week without having to keep a dr. appt. So,
news is, no dr. appt this week but back on the program next week.

We did continue full tilt on home therapy for GSH.

Manual dexterity is painting a chain with rust reformer.

​And with spreading lime on front lawn to cut back on moss.

busy sorting As sone as we get wrappers coins will be rolled. I read on
line that picking up coins from flat surface was good for fingers. ( I
suppose picking and grinning is also.)

Another thing I read was that was good for mental agility was solitaire

​Last and most important, she has her own , makes home visits, physical
therapist.

​For my part, no AT work, a little on the '63 and about finished drivers
side body work and moved around to passenger side.

​The bathroom workers have been making a lot of noise and hauling off loads
of materials. I have been helping as much as I can by staying away.

fine on the way down and while we were there. On the way back she crawled
under the seat and ate one strap off my carry on bag and ate a hole in the
bag.

It was fun. Also saved David from having to go to exercise class.
