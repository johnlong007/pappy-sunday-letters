October 24, 2010 · Sunday

Finally !

12 photographs·233 words

The mowing here has been completed. I still have to go do the hillside. No
pictures except upon request.

We went on a backpacking trip M-T-W.

_Click here: Great Smoky Mountains National Park - Old Settlers Hike -
GSMNP_ (http://www.gsmnp.com/pages/oldsettlers.html)

We went along with two friends Kim and Jim.

The weather was great (until Tuesday night). I kindled a fire Monday night
but we got in later on Tuesday and it rained during the night.The little
alcohol stove GSH made worked very well. I recommend to all the backpackers.

The OST passes what was a heavily populated part of the property taken for
the park. A lot of foundations, chimneys and stone walls. The walls were
built to keep the pigs out of their gardens.They let their pigs run loose to
feed in the woods.

Jim and Kim are both good sports and fun to hike with.

Jim is a lay preacher. It would appear he was looking for divine guidance
and perhaps trying to part the forest.
It rained !

After we got out we had a celebration:

Saturday, Wayne came over and finished welding a brace on the log splitter.
It is ready to put into service as soon as I mow the hillside and remove
the mower from the tractor.

Sunday afternoon we walked around the kingdom and surprised a deer in the
punkin patch. The new dog gave chase.
