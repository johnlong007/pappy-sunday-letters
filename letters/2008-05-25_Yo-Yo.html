May 25, 2008 · Sunday

Yo Yo

8 photographs·167 words

The utility room floor is almost covered with tile now. Dwight supplied tile
cutter and operator training.

Buckeye Falls is the highest waterfall in the Eastern United States. It is a
series of 3 cascades. Each about 300' high. This week, four of us went in via
Clark's creek. A difficult but fun hike. The base is very steep and very
slick.

In an attempt to do a good (at least better) job on this years tomato crop,
we are planting them in a different spot. Also planted some peppers. Still
some room left TBD.

Part of my 5 year plan is to reduce the fleet of cars and tractors. I
purchased this one almost 30 years back. Finally decided it was time to find it a
good home. A friend in the Gray tractor club wants it to restore and drive.

The end of this years ramp season. Anyone want any?
