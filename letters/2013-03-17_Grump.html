March 17, 2013 · Sunday

Grump

11 photographs·460 words

I went to Erwin to visit Herb, James, Kaylee and the goat. A goat
picture is attached.

I stopped at McD's on the way home and ran into Rusty with wife and
now 7 year old son Carter. The last time (and several times before) I
visited Aunt G in the hospital before she died she told me she wanted
me to have her class ring and had told Rusty and Todd. So, I asked
Rusty about the ring. He said she wanted Carter to have it.

Ed had a booklet covering the 32Inf div record in WW2. A lot of the
people he served with had put names and addresses in the front of the
booklet. I made a copy then talked through the booklet with Ed and
made margin notes. It is about 20 pages held with a paper clip. I
cannot find and and am very distressed. Anyone able to help me?

I went to see the heart Dr. for my one year/1,000 mile checkup. He
said BP is too high (160/90) and prescribed a new pill. Rats.

Enough whimpering.

Well almost enough, GSH worked on the income tax. It certainly helps
ease the pain knowing all our tax money is well spent. I just wish
they would raise the rates so they could have more to spend equally
wisely.

Vanessa is back in town. I understand she intends to stay here and
both multiply and divide. It was fun seeing her and getting caught up
on things. I talked with GSH on the way home about the proper
role/activity for a lady in waiting. My suggestion was to teach her to
knit so she can make cute baby things while waiting for the blessed
event.

Charlie ,eat your heart out. Have you seen Dwight's steam engine. Not
only does it run it has a steam whistle!

Saturday, I was drafted to go on an AT outing requiring and
experienced sawyer. We hiked into the Iron Mtn shelter clearing a
number of blow downs and then cleaned up the trash and the fire pit at
the shelter. Mostly the hikers do not leave much trash but the fire
pit would have made a worthy dig in Israel.

Maria had a birthday event. Lots of friends old and young. Normally, I
leave the BD pictures to the parents but could not resist taking a
picture of the real Maria being Maria.

All my discretionary time was spend cleaning on the MM while waiting
for the cylinders to come back from being cleaned and polished. I
found a can of MM spray paint I had purchased years ago. So, even
though it will not be good for the final paint job I had to give it a
test run.
