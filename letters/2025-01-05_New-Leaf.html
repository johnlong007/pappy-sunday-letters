New Leaf

12 photographs·295 words

I must have invented a new mouse trap.

The Family Doctor makes housecall. Benj shed the cap before I could pounce.

Family Dentists make house call.

Young, good looking and on the way to becoming rich and famous.With her
mother

It should be noted that according to the Aura pictures, the number of
Buchanan grandsons doubled. Stay tuned for vital statistics.

Grandson Kenneth is doing a road show with next stop in Chicago. All those
budding Einsteins would be astounded to see him in his native habitat doing
manual labor..

Beth stopped by for my routine Pep Talk. Rumor has it she was at the
leveling off and holding age when this picture was made.. Remember Jack
Benny?

For some time we have been refurbishing horse drawn implement artifacts.
The plan is to hang on the ag bldg with the spike tooth harrow. To clear
space in the shop we moved all the completed parts down to the building.
Wayne did the heavy lifting.

I went to Kansas and brought two David Bradley mowing machines back some
years ago. One was rigged for tractor pulling, one for horse. The tongue
for the horse-drawn mower is the last piece to be restored.

Another cultivator is close. Need advice on getting proper fit on the
handles.

The Wonder Horse is going to be like new soon. A lot of TLC.

After some delay and some dithering, The final bathroom renovation is
underway. Wayne ripped out the site this week. Installation crew should
arrive before long. Stay tuned.

Last weekend, when Diane decided I was really sick, she moved Patricia's
recliner in for me.
