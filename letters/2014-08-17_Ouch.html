August 17, 2014 · Sunday

Ouch

12 photographs·482 words

Friday we had 100 students from ASU come to work on the Backbone Rock
access trail leading to the AT. Carl split into ten groups with two Of his
regulars with each group.

We had 10 sections of 120 ' each marked off. Carl stayed with the first
five groups and sent me on up the mountain with the other five groups.
This year it was mostly female.

​The ones where I was were really good workers. Stayed spread out and kept
working with not much talking.

​
After while, I decided to quit wandering around and started to build trail
around a rocky outcropping.

​

See that big rock ? The one in the middle. I dug it out above the trail,
dug a spot for it and rolled it down to guide into the hole. Decided to
ease it into place and brake travel with my leg.

We did get a lot done but there is till much to do. As we go further up the
mountain more of the day is consumed with travel . We were in about one
mile where I was working.

On the home front. A few milestones. After a few fits and starts the rotary
mower (bush hog) is back in operation. I thought the new blades were out
of balance but the balance problem was due to the new blades being thicker
than the old and not able to swing freely.

See the cheater Herb is using on the 3/4 " power handle? I had my thumb (on
my bad hand) wrapped around the top of the blade which we were trying to
loosen. He squeezed my thumb. It hurt. Just not my week.

On a brighter note ! No pictures but Wayne came over Saturday and we got
the '63 running. Fired right up, smoothed right out and really sounded
good. But, the fuel pump was leaking, squirted gas all over the engine
compartment and (since the car has no exhaust system) led to an immediate
termination of operations. Really did not want to burn down the car and the
entire storage building.

Also, while help was in hand, The LBT was moved out and the MM. Finished
with primer on the LBT and am now ready to get both it and the MM running.
Maybe this week.

​

What should I move into shop for next project?

GSH has been working refinishing furniture all week. Any going away to
college kids who want to check for possible usage had best stop by PDQ.

​​ As part of our clutter eduction program, I am willing to offer my bike
(and perhaps GSH's) for loan to someone who promises TLC.

​

Our corn finally started getting ripe, But, the turkeys decided to help
themselves.

​
Not quite as exciting as going to Japan ,but more than going to the beach.
