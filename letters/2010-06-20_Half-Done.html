June 20, 2010 · Sunday

Half Done

11 photographs·325 words

Proving that charity starts at home, or something like that, for do-gooder
work this week we helped Dave's youngest son, Geoff, put new brakes on his
car.

A big Oak tree out past the old apple trees is dying. We were planning to
cut it down so walked out to look it over. As I approached, I interrupted a
Red Tailed Hawk having breakfast of fresh squirrel.

The hawk flew out squawking. It was soon joined by another hawk. Both
swooped and squawked. It appears they have a family in a nest a bit further up
the hill

A big event for the week was the first lake outing of the year.

I rented a willing (?) worker to help me cut up and drag out the big Beech
and Hickory tree which blew down on the hill above the apple trees.

Then I borrowed a couple of willing workers to help start tractors to take
to the Blountville old tractor owners show.

We decided the cletrack was beyond our job classification and were going
to have to call Dwight in for support.

The show went well. We set up Thursday evening (including borrowed chairs
from Prbys church). Friday I worked registration all day. A slow day,
entrees were down and attendance was low. A person from WCYB was there to do a
news clip. It ran Friday night. The weather was good Saturday and entrees and
attendance were good.
Saturday evening we took all the tents down and all the tractors and
engines disappeared in a giant cloud of dust.

Today was a fun day.
D & D came over while we working outside. Dwight more or less volunteered
to take over washing the slab outside the pool room (getting ready for
Summer Camp).

Then various Jack and Long family members showed up for a social call.

All that followed by telephone calls from out of town members of the clan.

All is well.
