Never put the top down without unzipping the rear window.

Make sure the window is clean before unzipping and lowering.

Wash with plenty of water and bare hand.

Before unzipping window, release latches, raise a few inches ,close latches
and let top rest on latches while unzipping window.

When zipping window closed, position latches the same way. The zipper will
operate easier and last longer if not under load zipping or unzipping.

Put a towel under and another over the window after unzipping and before
lowering top.
