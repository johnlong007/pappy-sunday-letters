June 8, 2014 · Sunday

59 !

16 photographs·649 words

Old Dog learns new trick !

​
Allison went shopping with GSH getting her to procure some new clothes.
Doing pretty well for the age and mileage I believe:

​ Cat does seem impressed.

On the other hand so to speak, she confirmed that wasps build nests inside
concrete blocks in the early Spring.

​
My big adventure for the week was learning that a roofing nail repair job
on a tractor tire cost $141.03.

​
I have had a number of struggles with inanimate objects this week. The PC
died, crashed and burned.

​Joe came over, chanted and burned incense (or maybe I was incensed),
anyway, he got it to come alive. After that by using my making do and
getting by skills (world class I might add). I was able to figure out that
heat is causing it to kick off and shutting it down when not in use,
keeping the sun off the machine and keeping and external fan running it
will keep going. Not sure those ensure a long and meaning full life.

In years past we have set up a canopy in the drive for the Miata in
Summertime when top is down and backed it under overhang in the Winter when
hardtop is installed. The overhang slab has cracks where the steel beams
were installed and water seeps through causing drips. I have been told the
canopy is unsightly so parking under overhang is to continue this Summer
leading to "seal the cracks!".

​We have commissioned Wayne to work on a couple of major projects this
Summer. To get him off to a good start, GSH fed the whole lot of them B&G.
As a group they are among the best at putting B&G away.

​While they were handy, I enlisted Gwendolyn for a bit of common labor on
the LBT. She does well but declared she is more interested in a car
project. Namely the Alfa. Stay tuned.

​
Laura made a run to Sam's with Dwight and while there restocked GSH's wine
cellar. Allison made the delivery. Not sure what she was smirking about,
possibly considering her little ole grandmother the wino.

​Oh car, heal thyself !
Friday I headed out to go visit Linda and say Hi to the folks with whom I
stayed while visiting in the Philippines (visiting their son and D-I-L). I
stopped in Colonial Heights and when I restarted the VW it was making an
unusual noise. I decided to drive it over to Wayne's house where we
determined it was from the clutch/transmission area. I sent Linda a note
and after thinking (but not saying) many bad words I brought it home and
parked it. Thinking all along that if I need to get somewhere, take the
Miata.
Anyway, today Wayne came over and persuaded me to take it for a test run.
No noise.
Not sure what I will do now, stay tuned.

Anyway, Dwight spurned my offer to take him to Seymour Saturday and I
scrubbed the trip to Linda's so I went to a car show in Glen Ayre with
Sanford and his brothers in law. We rode in like new 1964 Buick Riviera.

I thought this car was the best one there.

​
Calamity Lisa came over Sunday PM for a bit of skill honing before taking
the carry permit course.

​I do not think she will have any trouble passing the skill test. I suppose
it remains to be seen how she does on written exam,

​We have enough ramps left for one more meal. Got any bidders. One night we
tried a different recipe. Turned out rather well but not as good as fried
potatoes.

​ GSH went to church meeting Thursday morning. The whole time she was gone
little dog waited and watched.

​And per popular request !

​I eagerly await word from those scattered hither and yon.
