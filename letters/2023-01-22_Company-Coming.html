January 22, 2023 · Sunday

Company Coming !

11 photographs·421 words

I first met Syble when I interviewed with ECC in August, 1965. I worked in
the same areas with her for many years and she was my Dept. and division
sec. in due time.

Since I retired (many years ago) she always remembers my BD . For years we
met for lunch to get caught up on mutual acquaintances. We missed the last
few years due to the virus, but I did get my once a year birthday Email.

Monday, right out of the blue,she showed up with a card and a cake.

Guess what,or should I say who, also showed up ! Not out of the blue but
out of the wild west. Bearing Christmas gifts no less.

On a less happy note, one night the cat did not come in for Catherine to
spoil and was not waiting to come in when I went to the door in the early
morning. Later Diane found him wheezing and showing no interest in cat
treats so we knew he was ill. She took him to the vet and found a
recurrence of his Asthma from the same time last year.
Today, after medicine and TLC he is pretty much back to normal.

The sawyers fired up the sawmill! Remembering my experience with putting
GSH on the tractor and expecting her to follow my signs and signals I was
quite impressed to see Alan's Bobcat operator in action. Obviously a better
trainer than I.

Never one to let a willing worker go to waste.

Watching synchronized wood splitting ,Summer Camp training all came back.
Perhaps I should try to import campers one at a time? Any volunteers out
there? GGC ?

Shop cleaning, sorting, thinning etc. continued. Know what we have here.?
It is not a bathtub full of alternators. An insider story.

Grump !

5 new tires arrived. I was afraid to have a tire place mount lest they
damage my rare, powder coated spoke wheels. I damaged one.

Just to let everyone see how domesticated I am.

My brother Jimmy and Dorothy, his first wife, ( also second and third as I
remember), had three children before finally giving up the struggle. After
that, Dot married a somewhat older man . He died not too long back and Dot
moved into a WFG facility. While waiting, she and a man I have not met
have tied the knot.
