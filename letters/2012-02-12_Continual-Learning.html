February 12, 2012 · Sunday

Continual Learning

5 photographs·558 words

What did I learn this week ?

1. Silicon brake fluid is slippery when spilled on a concrete floor.

2. Dr. Earle P. Barron a licensed practitioner of NAEP is a former minister at First prbys in Erwin and was recruited for the position by my uncle Howard Garland.

3. I can still run a 5K at 10 minute/mile clip (maybe a bit better).

4. The Kahn Academy is great !

As those who keep up with my endeavors know, I am trying to get a working knowledge of Quantum Mechanics. Meredith sent me a suggestion that I look into the Kahn Academy as a possible resource. I had heard and read about it but had never thought it applied to me. I was wrong ! I opened the site and have been watching 1 or more videos about every night. I can just see the headlines "Old dog learns new trick".

A few weeks back, Linda inquired as to whether I was going to run in her church sponsored 5K again this year. Since it was a good while off, I said OK. GSH and I went down Friday via Cookeville to check on the student prince(s) and picked up Kenneth to go along with us. His role was to tutor me in Quantum Mechanics.

It was very cold and very windy for the race event Saturday morning. So cold we did not get a before during or outside after picture. (but, I did not wind burn my exposed body parts). This picture is an inside after (and during a lecture). Kenneth is going to be a fine teacher, very patient.
Dale and Sarah ran together, Linda, Rebecca and I ran together. It was all a fun event and one to be remembered I would guess. Like a drive to Indy.

For do-gooder work, Don and I tried again on Hals' Ford 8N. We did not get it running. No spark. We have a tune-up kit in hand and plan to try again (third time etc) this week. Stay tuned. The other do-gooder was taking Vanessa to an allergy person. Turns out he was a minister in Erwin in a previous life and his wife a teacher in the Unicoi school system. small world.

AT work was a lot like the previous weeks. More trail renovation by the same ole crew. (pictures upon request, Ha!)

This picture is the shelter at no business. I took a picture of the art work and inscriptions on each of the walls if anyone would really like to see, let me know.

As before, most of the home work was on the '37. I started out to do the brakes first thing. Finally got the master and wheel cylinders apart and cleaned. Finally found enough parts to put the wheel cylinders back together. Decided I would use the old seals in the master cylinder. After spilling two( $10 each) containers of silicon brake fluid I decided that was not working. Guess what ! Today I once more dug through my inventory of NOS parts and found I do indeed have a MC overhaul kit. So, I am ready to try again . I can feel the hope spring up.

George came by and helped wrestle the bed off the frame. Ready to wire brush and paint the frame.

Lest you forget.
