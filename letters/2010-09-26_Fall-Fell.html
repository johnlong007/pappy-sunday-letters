September 26, 2010 · Sunday

Fall Fell

13 photographs·315 words

Health, wealth and happiness.

Running continues to improve. I am almost back to feeling as well as I did
before this all started a couple of years back. Not as fast but not light
headed. Drinking a lot of water.

Soccer dog:

Note the cleanly clipped field under the walnut trees. I spent most of the
week mowing with all the available equipment. We have an invasive grass. It
is an annual species and is now going to seed. So, it is best dealt with
by cutting it close right before it goes to seed. I am trying to do that.

AT work. Final prep for replacing the Curly Maple Gap shelter. The old one
is to be torn down and a new two story one erected on the same site. Curly
Maple Gap shelter is about 1/2 way between Indian Grave Gap and the
Nolichuckey River. One access route is up Martin's creek and keep going.

The spectator in action: Maria the multi-talented missed soccer practice
last week in order to be a Nut Cracker. The result was getting to be a bench
warmer for most of the game this week. However, as all know by now, John
covered himself in glory with the entire family basking in reflected glory.

Herb has been sick this week (contrast that with being ill which might be
consider normal. I went over to visit but missed him. So, I surveyed the
kingdom.

Wayne came over Saturday and installed a new radiator in the Miata . My
hand is almost recovered but I do not do well on things requiring finger
dexterity.(such as installing a radiator). As either a bribe or a reward, GSH
prepared B & G for supper. Apparently the word got out as persons came in
from the highways and the by-ways.

Just to show my sensitive side:

A good week here. How bout there?
