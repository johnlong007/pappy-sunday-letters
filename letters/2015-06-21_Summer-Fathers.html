June 21, 2015 · Sunday

Summer Fathers

10 photographs·314 words

A short but busy week.

Herb has a couple of weeks to go. He is going strong but stays tired and
sleepy. The rains have arrived and his (and my) garden are growing up in
weeds. Black raspberries are gone, BBs are coming in and red raspberries
are looking good. I sprayed the grapes but afraid I waited too late and
brown rot is hitting.
Thursday was AT work day. We went to Low Gap where I worked with Tim and
Bill to renovate the water source.

Friday was free lunch day! At least for some.

Also the day to check out fit and finish on the LBC (with a blue top).

Saturday was Blountville Old Tractor day. We get about 10-15% smaller each
year. How long before we reach zero? The show went along pretty good until
about 3 PM when the rains came down and the floods came up.

The club pays a tractor pull club to put on a pull at 5PM after the show.
We pay them $850 and get to keep the gate at $5/person. We collected $265 I
believe.
This cannot go on forever.

A bonus picture for Charlie !

In outdoor critter news, we have a mama deer with a tiny fawn. Did not get
a picture. Also a mama turkey with turklets. The first day I counted 7,
yesterday 6, today 5.

Something has been topping my tomato plants and trimming the cucumber
plants. I put netting over the cucumbers and baited the live trap.

The renovated strawberry patch is looking good.

We have a number of family members who seem to have taken to exotic foods
from exotic places. Lest the forget their roots, how about fried Early
Harvest apples with sausage and GSH biscuits?

Hard to believe but I have bee a practicing father for 59 years. Reckon I
will ever get it right?
