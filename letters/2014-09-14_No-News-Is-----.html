September 14, 2014 · Sunday

No News Is ----?

9 photographs·496 words

How about health .wealth and happiness?

If that does not do it, How about the status of my big toe? Four weeks now.
It does not hurt so much but still sore and does not bend. Daddy used to
tell us about "Big Toe Soup". A scary story made more so by Uncle Earnest's
leg being buried out in the garden under the apple tree.

No news on Herb this week except we are going to Duke for an appointment on
Wed. Probably go down Tuesday.

Everyone knows I finally got the hillside mowing done. Looks good. It was
especially pleasing in that the mower worked well. I had been struggling
with what to do since it threw a blade while I was mowing there last year.
Unsure whether to repair or replace. Next test will be mowing the
Blountville annex.

For AT work we spent two days at Carl's house cutting pieces to be used to
build the bridge. This thing is going to be like a bunch of Lego blocks.
All the pieces pre-cut to be carried in and assembled. This week the
temporary bridge is to be installed. I hate to miss it as should be like a
Chinese fire drill.

Pat and Karen Loven have bought some property which has mature Walnut trees
he wants to sell. I told him I would loan him a Timber Stick so he could
get an estimate of value. That got me looking and I found an article in the
recent hand me down Tennessee conservationist from PBBD. I had made and
learned how to use a timber stick at the forestry camp. When we were
clearing stuff out after Mom died I found mine and have it safe, warm and
dry. The one I loaned Pat I had purchased before finding mine.

It is a treasure close, but below, my wooden egg.

I finally found a person interested in digging some potatoes. Can now
confirm there are some there. I do not deliver.

We found a slide in camper listed on Craig's List. I thought about going to
look and perhaps purchasing but got to thinking no one would use it and I
would have one more item with which to deal. Any dissenting voice. I can
supply the ad.

Tried aging on the MM. Same problem so took carb back to carb repair man.
Stay tuned. No work at all on the LBT

The cat ignores me but stays under feet of GSH

No chestnuts this year but I decided to check on the Chinquapins and found
bushes were loaded. I came down to get camera to take a picture for Benj.
Burs were all open and nuts falling out on the ground. When I got back I
found I need not worry , they were not going to waste.
Those are the two old ones. one of which is crippled. There is another big
flock that help make sure nothing is being missed.
