Health and Happiness

13 photographs·582 words

For my part, the change in cholesterol pills appears to be working as far
as reducing the aches and pains. Various tests have been done to determine
if the heart murmur has been worsened . Since no one has called to tell me
hold everything and prop your feet up, I am assuming that all is well.

Along the same line, Herb found out 1/2 a stent is not better than none. He
is still in the medical center in JC. Lisa reports that they may have got
the stent working and bypass surgery may not be required. Stay tuned.

I did not do any work on the lbt this week. I did do two days on the AT and
spent a lot of time fiddling with the crawler.

Here is a picture of a picture made when Ted delivered the crawler:

​As you see, it had a framework installed. I spent the week putting the
framework back in place ;

​The blade and bracket came with a tri cycle AC. I have in mind to adapt it
to the crawler. However, many question and problems remain to be tackled. I
am not at all sure I really want to do that. Stay tuned.

One day I went with Pat Loven to cut a blowdown on his section of the AT.
It just happened to pass a patch of ramps. I harvested a few but think I
went off and left my digger, I intend to make a run back as soon as
opportunity allows.

Another good deed was going to visit Sue Tabor. She is doing fine. I have a
friend who is interested in Hal's car so I went by to discuss with her. She
said it would be OK to show him the car as soon as s-i-l Mark is available.
Another stay tuned.

Rather than show another picture of a patch of ramps how about a flower?

got me all psyched up about how I want ours to be upgraded.
