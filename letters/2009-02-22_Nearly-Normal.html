February 22, 2009 · Sunday

Nearly Normal

8 photographs·388 words

Dave is still broke down with his knee so we have not been doing do-gooder
work for a spell. In the meantime I continued to mostly stay indoor fiddling
with the David Bradley mowing machine and the Cletrac.
I did get by to see Hal. He is about 10 days into his second 21 day series
and is moving somewhat slower but still moving. After having been to visit him
one day, Sue called the next and invited us for dinner. Jill and Henry (her
brother) also. It was quite nice, we talked about the good ole days at ISCC.
Then, Momsie invited Sue Tabor over for supper last night so we talked about
all the rest of the ISCC people.
Tuesday we did AT work at Laurel Falls. It was cold, in the shade and below
freezing all day. It was all rock work. We are going back this Wed for more
rock work. I am going to try to cut steps into a solid rock wall about 5 feet
high. Stay tuned.

The turkeys have been back the last few days. However, the flock is now
about 40 rather than 50. Maybe the others are setting. Maybe roasted.

This was a veritable frenzied of social activities week. The AT maintainers
dinner was Friday night. Low key, low cost (3 $/person) but still fun. We do
need more young folks, as one can see, this is not a bunch of Spring chickens.
Maybe one must be retired first.

Having mastered carpentry and electrical, GSH decided to take up plumbing.
She reconnected the water line to the refrigerator ice maker. I think it is
still leaking inside the ice maker. Stay tuned.

Saturday, we dragged the Cletrac out under the canopy for initial testing. I
poured in a gallon of anti freeze without sign of leaking, But, when I
started to add gas, it began to run out under the tank. I pulled the tank off and
found the problem. Stay tuned in case I also find a solution.

Continuing the social week, we went to Laura's house for a better late than
never birthday event for Christopher .
Last, and perhaps least, for the benefit of the inhabitants of the frozen
North, a picture from Sunday afternoon.
