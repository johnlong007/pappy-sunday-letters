The big event was moving gravel at Carver's Gap on the AT.
Last week we moved 10 tons to the top on Round Bald and spread 25 tons on the trail to the top. Saturday, ASU brought their freshman class from their Waturga College in to spread the 10 tons for us.

It was delight to see and work with the kids. About 90 or so young persons, maybe a few more girls than boys.
They looked like Katy six years ago.
One girl with a nose ring
One boy with long hair
No tatooes
No one fat
