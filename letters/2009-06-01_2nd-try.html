June 1, 2009 · Monday

2nd try

0 photographs·238 words

We woke up Monday morning to a turkey rapping at the window.

Tuesday's AT work was shaping up from the big relocations done by the Hard
Core group. It will require 2-3 more outings to finish. Tomorrow the crew
will be going to Carver's Gap (Roan Mountain) for the same kind of work.

I got the garden ready to plant and turned it over to Katy. She will miss
summer camp but while the kids are weeding they can thank her.

While out I got a good shot of young Red Tailed Hawk with the light just
right. Later it got too near the crow's nest causing the crows to go into
attack mode.

Sue came through her surgery in fine form. Both she and Hal are relieved.
Hal was also feeling better as he is between chemo series.

My trip to St. Thomas left me all psyched up that might get by on Stents
but after the Dr's conferred that was ruled out. Multiple bypass surgery on
Wed. the 10th.

We covered the BB's and the cherry tree. Looks like a so-so crop. But. the
RR's look good. I have to finish mowing around them. So far, no bees nests.
I have also mowed the soccer field. I am trying to get things in shape
before they lay me up (or down).
