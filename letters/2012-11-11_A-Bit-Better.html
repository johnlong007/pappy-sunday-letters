November 11, 2012 · Sunday

A Bit Better

11 photographs·624 words

Larry has completed three weeks in a coma. We have visited Debby in
the CC waiting rtoom several times. Last night she said she is going
to ask for another brain wave test and a second opinion on his
status.The first opinion was no sign of brain activity. They had no
discussions concerning such an occurance. No will, no paperwork, no
expressed wishes.

For the (maybe) good news. I took the giant TV/Monitor to the family
repair shop. The owner opened it found some shorted capacitors, placed
and order for replacement parts and is waiting with soldering iron at
the ready. Stay tuned.

Thanks to a large, yellow tomato supplied by Laura, the BLTC sandwich
season got extended into November this year. I made sandwiches for
lunch 2-3 times week for the last 3 months or so.

To get the social season started, we had a couple of friends over for
supper. Soup beans and cornbread. I like friends who are willing to
come on a moments notice and eat my kind of food (including hot
peppers) with obvious enjoyment.

Carl had an AT work day Tuesday. The weather forecast was ovewrcast
with a high of 52. We went to Indian Grave Gap (on up the mountain
past Rock Creek Park.)

When we got to Erwin it was startng to rain. When we were going up the
mountain it turned to sleet. At the top, it was 31 degrees and
snowing. We were not really properly prepared. However, I had three
pair of gloves and two packs of (Christmas present) chemical hand
warmer. It was a cold, wet, miserable day to be working on the
ridgeline of the mountain. But, my hand did not get cold!

Wednesday night at church, a truck driver owner- operator asked me to
come cut down a tree on the hill behind his house. I enlisted Dave
Dunham to come supervise and went to his house Thursday PM. When we
got there, he had already pulled the tree over with his middle size
crawler tractor so I spent my time admiring his truck, both inside and
out. He can haul loads up to 200,000 pounds.

Herb has been experiencing car trouble so I decided I should go visit
him (and the goats, and Kaylee). The goats are fine, all four of them.
Linda, I believe they could be made available to a good home.
Kaylee is a senior this year and is looking toward college. I have
been giving her my best sales pitch for going into the Air Force. She
would be good and it would be good for her. Stay tuned.

For my at home, indoor time it was the '37. I decided to quit worrying
about hooking up the voltage regulator and got back on some body work.
The most visible part being installing a front fender. The dash
instruments were in a round glass covered recess. Fuel gage, amp
meter, temp and oil pressure. Some were missing and some not
functional. So, under the banner of "making do and getting by" (
Charlie) I adapted the instrument cluster form a '55 Chrysler to fit.
Now, I still need the visiting electrical person to stop by and
supervise while I hook it all up correctly.

In case you are wondering what GSH has been doing, she has decided to
creat a sow's ear.
Upon her instructions, I attacked our nice family heirloom couch with
my chain saw cutting it into three pieces. She has now undertaken to
put it back together minus the center 1/3. We shall see.

On an equally high skilled note, today we went to the Youth Symphony
concert featuring three of our children's children. Perhaps you can
spot them in the picture.
