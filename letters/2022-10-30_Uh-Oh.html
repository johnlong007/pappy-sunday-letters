October 30, 2022 · Sunday

Uh Oh

11 photographs·351 words

For years we have been measuring the progress of children, grandchildren
and now GGC by such measures as first words, first teeth, first rolling off
bed etc. Now, thanks to A & C I believe we have a new gold standard.
Earliest age for"uh oh" .

Outside forces have determined that we need a dog A moderate size and high
energy dog. Dog is doing well so far, I went for a walk and both pets
followed along.

Time to put in your Thanksgiving order. I read that the price is double
from last year.

We made progress on brakes for the '55 this week. Located new wheel
cylinder cups and left shoes at the shop to be relined.

I have been putting off routine maintenance on the mower. This week we got
all the filters changed, hydraulic oil changed and a grease job. Now ready
to park for the Winter. It has an electrical problem. I miss Dwight.

Niece Ella has an almost new car. She got a low tire pressure warning and
came by for me to check. We brought all five tires up to the specified
number but the light did not go off. I suggested stopping at auto parts
store to get it reset. Person (I suppose a man) gave her the brush off..I
doubt I would ever darken their door. I hate men being condescending to
female drivers.

We get home health people in and out all week. This one talks. Among other
things she said people with memory issues will remember skills they
mastered early (she said before 18). I trotted in, got GSH's trumpet and
got this picture.

The home health people have a lot of staff turnover.This lady is Darlene, I
like her and hope she stays on. Patient and has a good sense of humor.

It is always a treat when visitors from afar drop in.

Foe those who have moved ito higher planes, a reminder of times gone by.
