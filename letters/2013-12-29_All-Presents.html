All Presents

10 photographs·226 words

A fine week.

Almost all the family members passed through.

39 stockings by the chimney with care.Katy and Ross, Beth, Jake and Paul
were nearby but got better offers for the night after Christmas.

I think Christmas presents are interesting. Everyone knows they ofter
reflect what the giver would like to receive. Sometimes we try to guess
what the other would like (or should like).

This year GSH gave me work gloves and I gave her a work coat.

For those who missed the event:

GSH has been working on a Christmas wish project for quite a while.

I remember as a kid, on the afternoon of Christmas days the young men in
the community would assemble at the church parking lot and shoot guns. We
sort of resurrected that practice. The big hit this year was Alan's cap and
ball revolver.
