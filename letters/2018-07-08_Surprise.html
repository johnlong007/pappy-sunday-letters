Sometimes I get frustarted with me. I seem to keep starting new projects
and get side tracked before finsishing any old ones. Well, This week , I
more or less finsished a project (of sorts).
My work tractor , the 885 had a wheel and tire failure 2 or 3 years back.
I, being me< delt with the isuue by talking a wheel/tire from the MM parked
under the tractor annex behind the barn. There it has been parked with one
fron wheel since then. Leaping into action, Wayne ordered a replacement
whell a year or so back. Then, last week things got serious when a tire on
the lawn mower failed. Since I spent 1-2 days/week on the mower it had to
be repaired. When Wayne went to Miller's to get a lawn mower wheel he
procured a replacement front wheel for the 885 .
So, a lot of work in the hot, hot sun breaking down and replacing tires and
wheel (including a slow leak front tire on the MD) but one item is checked
as completed.
