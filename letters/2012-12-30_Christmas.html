December 30, 2012 · Sunday

Christmas

5 photographs·139 words

The family Christmas event was Friday evening and all day Saturday.
All were here except Aaron (and PJ), Vanessa and Esmeralda. Maybe next
year. Santa left me some L.L Bean last forever ,waterproof boots. I
can hardly wait to try them out, come on snow !

Friday evening I got a call saying Larry Kidd had just died. Another
reminder, there is no guarantee of tomorrow. I have not yet talked
with his wife, Debby.

No AT work this week but I did get by to visit Sue Ketron. She is
doing great, just the way Hal would want her to do.

Very little work on the '37. I did get the small fuel tank hooked up
but have not put fuel into it yet.

While I had my attention diverted, someone put a foreign car into the shop.
