Perth

0 photographs·170 words

I hear you are going. You will love it. We visited there with some
missionary friends. Went to church, stayed for a pot luck dinner.

Fremantle is nearby. All the WW2 servicemen sent to the
pacific theatre came in through Fremantle and through Perth. The little ole
ladies at the dinner told me about the yanks while their eyes lit up. One
recommended a book ( Fleeting Attractions) which i procured after getting
home

Steven left yesterday to go to Brisbane taking my book with him.

One place I wanted to visit but failed to get done is the Jack Hills near
Perth. The oldest surface Crystals on earth have been found there. Over 4
B years old. If you go, bring me one.

PBS issued a four part documentary ( Australia's first 4 Billion Years). if
you get a chance, watch at least some of it, Also read about the history of
Fremantle in WW2.

I hope you have a great trip.

Will expect a pictorial trip report.

Pappy
