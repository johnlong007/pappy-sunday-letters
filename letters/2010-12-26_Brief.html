December 26, 2010 · Sunday

Brief

2 photographs·149 words

Apparently Santa got grounded somewhere between here and the North Pole as
he has not yet arrived here. But, hope springs eternal.

We had a 7 hour time shift from where we were to where we are. I have read
the body adjusts at about a rate of one hour/day.
Either way, we have both been a bit groggy the last couple of days. I got
up before 5 Saturday morning and before 6 today.
Unfortunately, church was canceled due to real and imagined snowfall.

Yesterday, we made the rounds of local family members to check if Santa
had arrived at their locales. Sure enough, he had. Not quite sure what to
make of that. I did not take any pictures as I am sure the respective families
will keep everyone informed.

Today, we played in the snow.

Do the summer campers remember when these pine trees were small?
