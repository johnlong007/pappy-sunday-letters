We ( Pappy and Grandmother) now have more GGC than GC, and counting !

Mom's youngest sister died a couple of weeks back. She was the family pack
rat with a house packed with family artifacts. Her boys. Gary and Mike are
going through sorting things in preparation for an estate sale I think.
Anyway, I asked Mike to send me pictures of artifacts and scanned copies of
family paper items his Mom had preserved.
He has been sending me a lot of scanned items which I will get into the
family history project, available to all. My GGM (giver of the wooden egg)
was a daughter of William Parks. He was a man of means with a big house
located over in the field near the creek. Mike has been scanning items
related to him ,going back to the civil war time.
