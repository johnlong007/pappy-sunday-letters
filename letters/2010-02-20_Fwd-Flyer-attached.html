Fwd: Flyer attached

0 photographs·93 words

Attached is a flyer that I hope can be forwarded to your friends and family members announcing our concert on March 21. We need to recruit audience members for this event. We have lowered our ticket prices below those being charged for other concerts in order to make sure we have a good crowd.

Do talk this concert up among your friends and family members. We have been told that the policy on children is up to us - with the suggestion that if they need a seat, they should pay.

Ellen Finney
