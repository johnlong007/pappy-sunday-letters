May 29, 2016 · Sunday

Not Very Average

9 photographs·284 words

All the children. all the spouses, all the grandchildren and all the GGC
passing through. It may be a spell before that happens again. Made GSH and
me very pleased.(Would not like to admit being proud).

Started the week by doing shuttle service for Wayne and family. They were
off to Hike from Chestoa to Devil's Fork. Keeping the family tradition
alive by doing it the hard way. They started from a river crossing and
ended on top of a mountain.

After seeing them off, I went by to help Lisa put up a caution fence in her
hay field to keep The person who mows from disturbing the deer habitat. To
insure the unmolested deer have an ample food supply, we also laid off four
rows in the garden for her to plant corn.

I stopped on the way home and purchased 4 tomato, 4 pepper and 4 cucumber
plans. Planted 1/2 and gave the others to Laura. Garden is now planted and
ready for harvest.

Quite a bit of progress on the engine compartment of the '63 but no new
pictures.

AT work day was back on the fence project. One more session should wrap it
up.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, GSH embarked on a painting of the deck
project.

As some may know by now, Charlie and betrothed one scheduled their wedding
for today (not sure why next Sunday, June 5th. would not have fit). I
dropped by the Long Family Farm to check on site preparation on Saturday.

After site inspection, we went to Kingsport to catch a glimpse of other
visitors.

Today, the wedding went off perfectly. I believe I will leave pictorial
reporting (mostly) to other parties.
