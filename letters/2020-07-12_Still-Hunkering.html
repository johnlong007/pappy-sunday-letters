July 12, 2020 · Sunday

Still Hunkering

13 photographs·401 words

Nothing much has changed for us.

The manager met us at the door at Perkins this morning and said masks are
required. Still no church or SS.
Got a notice of Wed. night service with no rules posted. We went. Maybe 10
people.

Our social life.

Did I mention getting rid of scrap metal last week? Wayne got rid of the
load. 3 c/#. Made enough to pay for the gas.

Made a run to Erwin to finish job on the truck Herb gave to Ella, New
battery and one lug bolt. Fired it up and parked it front and center.

I think spraying the invasive pond plant got it but Algae is serious. Lisa
had cousin Tom bring back hoe in and dig out some mud.

I think Herb purchased this car from Dr. Monroe's widow. (He owned a '55
Chrysler 300 , gave it to his son, a classmate). I think if farmer Lisa
permits, my next project will be bringing the car back to life. It had a
carb. fire which Herb extinguished with sand! Boggles the very mind. Stay
tuned

We had visitors right before lunch one day.

One would think serious aspiring music persons would be a bit more
dignified.

Since David has not shown up to mow, I mowed above the soccer field. Looks
good.

But, the upper pond has shrunk and left a wide, mud shoulder. I got too
close.

Last week We got the deck pressure washed and procured sealant. This week,
the sealant was applied. Deck designer/builder seems well pleased.

All my high skilled wood splitter disappeared like the morning mist.
However, the wood remains to be split. Dug out the home made tractor
mounted wood splitter and put into service.
A lot of the pieces I do not want to lift onto the splitter. Gonna have to
get creative. A lot easier to hurt ones back than to get over the injury.

A good red Raspberry crop this year. We walked around the kingdom this
afternoon and found the ones by drive have not been picked out front.

Wild Kingdom

Hooking up the hydraulic lines on the log splitter is difficult. I do think
I may have found a clue for cause of my anemia.
