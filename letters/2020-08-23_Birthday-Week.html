August 23, 2020 · Sunday

Birthday Week

12 photographs·430 words

We had an open house/birthday celebration Saturday. However, I did not take
pictures. A follow up event was here Sunday and I did take a picture.
Unfortunately (for him) the birthday boy was not present to be honored but
we struggled on without him.

String trimmers make excellent yellow jacket nest finders, I know where
three nests are now. Normally I am aware enough to avoid getting stung. For
the latest nest I thought I was past nest but was not. they swarmed all
over my right hand. The one I hold out front on the trimmer .

About the only worthwhile work we accomplished this week was clearing the
limbs I had dragged over (from the still standing dead pine!) and
depositing on the brush pond.

To clear the way for the limbs required relocating the (we hope) saw logs.

Moved the logs across the drive. The really big ones are still located
where I dragged over from neighbors place. Did not get the dead pine cut
down. Getting more cautious in my dotage.

One day when we came in and up the drive, a large flock of turkeys, great
and small, sauntered across drive.

I believe I asked last year who knows the local name for this pictured vine.

The retaining pond worked but it is not close enough to the front of the
soccer field to catch a lot of the runoff. So, we are getting gullies
washed in the walnut field. I believe I will move the "dam" forward to the
front edge of the field but leave the deeper hole for water to sink in.
Since school has more or less restarted it looks like no willing workers
are going to be available.

Except these two. They went with me to check the pond and the Pawpaw crop.

Max is always willing. When he sees me moving toward any riding equipment
he will be there waiting when I arrive.

Tuesday my head was all stopped up. Tuesday night I lost all hearing. Sort
of a weird sensation. PBBD made me an appointment with family Dr. He looked
, said canals are plugged, sent nurse to rooto root. Looked like a mouse
nest came out of each ear.
Always something.

Today we went to real, live people church. Ignored most of the rules.
Ignored evil eyes. Next Sunday, SS is to reopen for business. I miss that a
lot more than regular service.
