May 11, 2008 · Sunday

And Goes

9 photographs·288 words

The big events for the week were AT work at Roan Mountain and a trip to
Linda's.
I could show progress on spraying brush killer at Blountville or even a
picture of the bran with a shingled tin roof but will leave that for a later
date. Also, the baby birds have graduated and flown the coop. In local news, I
caught a groundhog in the live trap. I delivered it over near the junk yard
where it should find years of happiness.
Three pictures from the work day at Roan Mountain. The first is our crew
putting a new stile in at the entrance to the trail in an attempt to stop four
wheelers.
Another is Grandfather Mountain, you can see the chin and nose, he is in
reposed position.
There was a variety of wildflowers. I decided to attach a picture of one
since the baby birds have departed.
The trip to Vanderbilt was uneventful. Sis and Martha joined us for lunch.
Linda had a once over lightly exam at which nothing untoward was detected. In
six months , she will return for more bone and blood testing.
David had a birthday and lost his first tooth (literally as it turned out).
Linda took a day off Friday telling her sub to let the kids use the day for
Annual exchange and signing. I got to watch the kids leave for school with
their escorts.
Linda picked her strawberries, Workers finished with the bathroom tile
project and I mowed most all day.
Saturday, I helped Adam put up and electric fence to (we hope) keep the
goats out of the garden.
