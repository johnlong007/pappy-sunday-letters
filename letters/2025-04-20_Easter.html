April 20, 2025 · Sunday

Easter

10 photographs·204 words

I compile an Easter folder each year. Still missing many family pictures
for this year.

One never gets ahead on mowing but I did get pretty well caught up.

The roof shingle replacement crew showed up. Their work schedule was
daylight until dark with no fiddling around.

We have about finished hand tool refurbishing. This week we got most put on
display. The red wagon and the lawnmower are still in process.

Family came in from far and wide to get checked out on our work. I would
hope and perhaps expect all the children and grandchildren, maybe even GGC.
to know what the tools are and how to use them.The green item is the tongue
for a horse drawn David Bradley mowing machine. My father bought one new in
1947. I have two, one horse drawn, one tractor.

Wall to wall.

The horse drawn implements are a different story. Stay tuned.

Patricia's table has found a new home.

My red blood cells are the issue with transporting oxygen through the
system. The experts say, red meat. A & C are doing their part.
