If you keep having weeks as productive as this one I'm not going to
recognize my home in July!!!!!! It would be quite embarrassing to get lost
in Blountville. I guess it's clear why my mom is never home when I call. Not
that I expect her to be home when it's nice weather. She will see me soon
enough.

I'm going to Sweden July 3rd to go sailing in the archipelagos; rumor has it
her dad wants to take us down to Lithuania. Should be an amazing way to end
my Europe studies. The ticket I'm flying home with has me landing in
Knoxville at 6:55pm on Saturday July 12th; just in time for summer camp -
complete with jet lag. :) I am really looking forward to summer camp. I have
decided there are 2 things I really miss: Fresh foods from a garden/orchard
and walking outside barefoot. Of course my family and friends too, but I
knew I would miss people. I didn't know I would miss the country life.
Summer camp should be great.
