October 19, 2014 · Sunday

Healthy, Wealthy and Wise

9 photographs·431 words

I went to see Dr. Reeves this week. Ten years since he removed prostate.
Almost no chance of cancer recurrence now and if it did would not expect to
take any action.
I did have a good visit with him, we talked about Dwight, Herb, Laura
,various children, grandchildren and Laura's eyes. He said I do not need
to come back for another follow up unless I just want to talk.

Speaking of Herb, everything is on hold at Duke. I hope and expect he will
be back talking with local DR.s this week and will await further directions
from him. Duke has been a disappointment to me. Sort of a ready, fire, aim
sort of experience.

Back here in River City GSH made several apple pies and I did my part by
eating along with various and sundry helpers from time to time.

When not eating pies, and piddling with the LBT we harvested firewood from
Blountville annex. The first bay in the barn is well stocked. Next will be
an opportunity for a lot of wood splitting. My plan is to keep the newly
installed HVAC system set on 65 but to keep a fire in fireplace as
required. Also, the A/C system does not include the shop area so I will
need a lot of wood for shop stove. Plenty of opportunity for budding Abe
Lincolns.

Wednesday night, the rains came down, the floods came up and the temporary
bridge we had put across the river washed out. One end stayed secured and
other end swung down the creek without a paddle.

Thursday, we worked, all day in the rain. Dragged the old bridge out,
Installed hand rail on the new bridge, built access steps and ramps at each
end and opened the bridge for hikers !

Since Herb did have to go to Duke Friday to get sliced and diced, we went
across the Beauty Spot in Unicoi County instead. The area is as pretty as
anywhere in the smokies and a lot closer and less crowded.

Sunday afternoon some of us went for a walk in the woods. We walked up Bear
Creek, climbed up side of hill and inspected a potential building site.

Anyone know where Bear Creek is located?

I bet other folks did more interesting things.

Being admitted to hospital.
Deployed to for away places with strange sounding names.
Crossing a picket line.
Marching in band at a football game.
Watching football games great and small.
Warm places and chilly places.

It goes along (I could write that in German but forgot spelling)
