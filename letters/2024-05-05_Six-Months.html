May 5, 2024 · Sunday

Six Months

15 photographs·324 words

Not a good week. Body and mind are wearing me down.

After much thought, we decided to plant our 3 tomatoes in the lawn. After a
bit of digging, we gave up on that plan.

Dug up the rotten sawdust /dirt compose and filled three containers.

Sanford came by one day so I enlisted him to carry to the spots from last
year's failed attempt. Each plant has a new cage around the pot.

Following directions, we dragged some out of control bushes from the
circle. I understand plans are in progress for upgrading.

One of the better moments of the week was being able to help Alan a bit
with his MIG welding. I hope to get him interested in stick welding. Much
better for farm equipment.

Intake and exhaust manifolds are now installed on the '37.

Sarah and Mickey had us over for supper and lawn work admiring.

Many years ago, I bought an old drag disc at a farm auction. Trying to
find something fun, meaningful, worthwhile and doable for a crippe, I
dragged it out and down to the front lawn.

Willing workers in action !

Some are more helpful than others.

Linda came to town to give me a haircut and persuade me to embark on a
routine exercise program. Which will last the longest?

Meredith brought her child to be admired.

The standard for any project is no one gets hurt and nothing gets broken.
The axle shaft is 1 inch with a big, square nut threaded on the end. In
trying to hold the shaft while turning the nut, we avoided getting a longer
cheater and went for a bigger hammer.

The nut is still frozen and one of the blade spacers is broken. Rats.

Bath time.
