October 29, 2017 · Sunday

Post Partum

12 photographs·369 words

A bit of a let down after the big homecoming event Saturday. But, we muddle
along.

Two big events for the week.
Tuesday we went with the Edwards to ride along the Blue Ridge Parkway. A
perfect trip. They have a LEXUS ! Temperature was perfect and colors were
full Fall foliage.

​The other big event was a trip to Wal Mart. I have been compiling a list
for about two weeks. Did not look at the list until ready to check out and
had every item (plus a few extra) How about that for exciting.

My old helping hands buddy Dave Dunham and his GSH Carol came over one day.
They have an outdoor pit fireplace and wanted wood, dry, split wood.

Loaded

​Bob and Laura have gone cavorting to the left coast. In the meantime, back
at the ranch, Meredith and I have been left in charge of the yet to be
picked and pickled peppers.

One of the peppers is a rare and we hope not vanishing breed. While GSH and
I were visiting an park in Melbourne I spotted a colorful plant which I
recognized as a pepper plant. I plucked a few and in violation of a
multitude of real and imagined laws brought the home with me in my little
book tucked in my pocket. I gave one seed pod to Mom and asked her to raise
me some plants. She did. Herb, Laura, Linda and the greenhouse have kept
them going. But, no one raised and last year. It was only with difficulty
and help from Lisa that a seed​ pod was found and a bit late in the season
Laura coaxed three plants to life.

Hers is coming along but not yet ripe. Pods need to be full red.

I have a plant also, it is behind but I have moved it into a pot and into
the pool room.

We have a confused peach tree.

Cat came indoor last night for the first time since Spring time.

Getting ready for Winter, I chopped back the bush which shades my corner of
the world.

​​Not as colorful as most years.
Disposal of stale gasoline.

​Bonus pick of the week !
