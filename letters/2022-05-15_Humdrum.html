May 15, 2022 · Sunday

Humdrum

10 photographs·338 words

This week I continued to scan negatives of past pictures from the film
age. Then, to relieve the tedium, I decided to copy all my old back up CD's
onto a memory stick. Still miles to go before I rest on each of those
projects.

Today we started the '63. Fired right up. Sounds great.

Also played in the dirt a lot working on the retaining basin, mowed a bit
and supervised the sawyers. Ha !

Anita often comes over in mid morning so I can go shuffle around the block.
Did you know Anita has grandchildren? GSH never admitted having
grandchildren, only that her children had children.

PBBD usually comes over Tu. Sat. and Sunday. I am becoming dependent. Now,
A & C have been coming over in the evening. Turns out Catherine is a WW.
Laura gave me tomato plants and Catherine planted. Then, Laura sent more
plants and PBBD planted those.

Playing in the dirt went on all week. Kept draining the last drops and
finding seepage had replaced.

Toe improve the drainage plumbing we rolled out more hose.

I sure do like our new washing machine. It does require pushing two button
but after preparing written instructions and personal demonstrations, I
believe I have it. Fortunately I still have a trainer to help hand clothes
to dry.

Saturday morning breakfast has become somewhat routine. Real Ham and B & G
from Joe and Anita.

Anyone remember the giant Oak log I dragged back from Albert's field last
year?

I think this is more Oak, They also sawed the Hickory I retrieved from Don
Hass.

Several years back I went with D & D to clean out his parents house. His
Mom had a birdbath Dwight had given her many years ago. Our birds like it.,

Part of the time we go to the track. I shuffle a few laps then walk one
with GSH
