My uncle Ed fought with the 32nd. Inf. Div . Their last big battle was the
invasion of Luzon .
Over the years, he told me quite a bit about that part of his experience.
If you care to learn what he experienced, a good place to start would be
the site posted here.

http://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USA/USA-P-Triumph/USA-P-Triumph-26.html

The Villa Verde Trail runs from Santa Maria to Santa Fe. I thought I would
go to Santa Maria, hire a guide equipped with food, water and provisions
and spend two days hiking the trail.
I had a car and driver so on Tuesday last week, we headed for Santa Maria
to scout it out.
As we approached the mountains my immediate reaction was "this looks just
like going from JC to Erwin!"

We got to a sign which indicated the end of the road with a bamboo bridge
across the creek. On the far side of the creek the 32 div marker was
spotted. It is falling over from neglect.

I walked up the trail a ways. It is just like an abandoned logging road
around here. Then we went back a couple of miles to the villege to ask
about guides. There are none.
So, bought some water, came back to the trail and walked up it until I
drank all my water. 95 degrees and 95%.

The trail is ok, zig zags and very stepp side drop offs.But, good vies out
over the valley.

I decided to try the other end on the next day. Santa Fe.
We went there bright and early. Found that the trail is being paved to a
small villege on top of the pass (the place they were fighting to reach).
We drove out to it, about ten very slow miles.
I was standing at the pass holding a copy of the map I sent out when a
family in a van stopped. The lady spoke good english. After we talked a
while, she invited us in for lunch. Needless to say, I was apprehensive but
could not say no so we went.

The lady said there is a jap shrine on top of the hill, so, when we left,
we walked up to it.
