May 18, 2008 · Sunday

Also Ran

9 photographs·448 words

A picture is worth more than 10,000 words. So, I saved a lot of words this
week. Since Linda had me send out an airplane tour report earlier, this one is
for the rest of the week.
Actually, this groun hog is from last week but I failed to include a picture
so here it is. I have not gone by the junk yard to check on house hunting
progress.
Do-gooder was work at the church. I had been asked to bring Bobcat to move 9
yards of mulch. But, I said AWD's with front loaders would do better. Two
people offered, each within 15 minutes of the church. The entire job only took
about 5 hours portal to portal.
I sprayed weed killer and ran the string trimmer at Blountville estate.
Decided that maybe not everyone has seen the tastefully roofed structure so here
it is. Use, well used, tin.
One almost always encounters reptiles while running string trimmer. We just
eyed each other. When I mow with the tractor, I sometimes squash 'em.
Putting down tile in the utility room is underway. In the meantime, there
will be no clothes washing. That may be a problem as I often go through two
sets/day. Worse than a kid.

Sunday I went with Meredith to harvest the fatted ramps. It was cold, rainy,
windy and generally miserable on the Iron Mountain where we went. But, we
extracted a sack full. I am expecting to get invited over for potatoes and
ramps in the near future. The season is winding down. Note the yellowing of the
leaves. My intent is to go back Thursday and do the final dig of the year.
Anyone who wants to participate should let me know. If we can get a suitable
vehicle we will go a different place with even better digging.

Damascus has learned to take advantage of their location near the AT. Each
year they sponsor a big event called "Trail Days," each year, our Maintenance
group recruits hikers to work two days, Sunday and Monday in return for supper
each night and a "Hard Core" patch. Now, we have to limit the number to 100
people. It is a big reunion for former hikers who come back year after year.
The exposed belly belongs to "Dragon Slayer" (all have trail names). She
through hiked years ago. She is an elementary school teacher in Hendersonville,
NC.
Sunday night we feed them at The Laurels, Monday night at Bob Peoples
hostel. Kincora, if you want to look it up. The most popular hostel on the entire
trail.
