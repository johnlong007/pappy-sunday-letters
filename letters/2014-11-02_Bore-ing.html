November 2, 2014 · Sunday

Bore ing !

8 photographs·595 words

Was LLLL taking a dig at my subject matter titles? I think I have been
affronted.

Family news: Jimmy brought his ex-sister in law (Linda) and her husband
over to look at my family tree materials and my tasteful collection of cars
and trucks. I found out she has a large collection of JC press obituaries
she has clipped over the years as various and sundry relatives have
departed. I intend to go by and make copies. I have started work scanning
prints to enter into the family record. Sometime someone(s) had better look
at all this material I have assembled. Jimmy says he has turned over a new
leaf (again) . We shall see.

I went to JC to have lunch with Herb one day after he got out of his HPB O2
session one day. He does not report any improvement but his face looked
less sunburned to me. He got a report from last weeks MRI which ,as I
understand< did not show any change since the previous one in March.

Got a report from Lil' Sister. She is packing 65 years of household
belongings into boxes and looking for a home, just like a boll weavil. Said
she may try to rotate through all the nieces and nephews.Stay tuned.

I said last week that I am now the local AT go to guy for rock busting. We
got a report of a large boulder sliding down and blocking the access trail
at backbone rock (Benj will remember the site). So, Thursday I went with
Pat to remove the rock. We had to pulverise it as sliding it down the hill
would have destroyed the outhouse located at edge of parking lot .Several
other showed up to get in on the fun. After beating the rock into
submission we dug on a relocation until quitting time. A very rocky section
as the name implies.

One day we retrieved the last of the downed cherry trees from Bltv estate.
With the aid of my willing workers, we sawed it up and brought it home. I
decided to split it and stack here in front of the house as fireplace wood.
You will note the manual splitting tools are in the back of the truck. I
split one piece then got the tractor and installed the splitter. Went much
better after that. I sure miss my manual splitters !

It got cold this week ! I turned the "heat" on in our new A/C system. It
will take us a while to get the right combination but so far we still like
our warm spot in front of the fireplace.

Linda and Wayne got me started running around 1980. By about '83 I was
running every day and have kept at it since then , slower and slower but
still moving. I have run in temp below zero and above 100. One day this
week it was about 35. My toes got cold ! Toes never get cold while one is
running ! I wonder what has changed? Also, my foot is giving me trouble
with the broken toe. I always figured the knees would go. Should have know
it would be something I would do to me. I am going to start looking at
something I will do without having to pound the pavement. I think it is
coming. I read this week that the keys to a long life are eat healthy,
exercise and don't smoke.

GSH has been making apple pies. There is one in the oven now awaiting my
arrival.
