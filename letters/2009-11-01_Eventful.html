Another trip to see Dr. This time a follow up on my last skin cancer
removal and blood check on Cholesterol. said he had got it all on the skin
cancer. Cholesterol is not as low as it was last time but wants me to keep
taking the simvastatin. I am undecided. Stay tuned.

We went hiking/backpacking M-T-W. Anita dropped us off at trailhead . My
pack weighed 32 pounds with food and drinking water when we started. It
weighed 42 pounds w/o food or drinking water when we finished. It rained on us.

After we got home, we cleaned up the leaves. I found this insect hanging
from the gutter. Can anyone identify it?

Some progress on the Studebaker but no picture . Maybe more visible
progress this week will rate a picture.

But, progress on Pat's drag car. We went tp Rogersville Friday night.
Dwight had procured a new differential with a higher gear ratio. The car had
been ending 1/8 mile at about 7,000 RPM. Friday night it ended at almost
8,000. The time dropped to 5.25 seconds. I think by raising the transmission
shift point he will get real close to the 5 second target. Stay tuned.

Caption this picture.

Sunday PM I went with Herb to visit the graveyard at Bean's Creek where my
paternal grandparents are buried. My cousin Jackie and others are
interested in having a marker put up for my paternal grandfather's first wife is
buried. It is not going to be simple. He is buried on the front row in the
cemetery. On one side of his grave there are a series of three small stones
flush with the ground surface. On the other side there are no markers for 15
feet or so. I am sure there have been people buried on both sides. Stay
tuned.

I went on to Erwin to see Aunt G. She is in a room by herself now and
suffering from ADD. It was a good visit, she was in good form and talked for
quite a long time. A lot about her one and only GGC ,Carter, a budding genius.
