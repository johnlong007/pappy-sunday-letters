April 5, 2009 · Sunday

Misc.

12 photographs·421 words

Monday I went to see our family Dr. I have been having increased frequency
occupancies of getting light headed while doing my running. He has been after
me for years to take Cholesterol pills. Gave me a prescription for generic
pills which GSH procured for me. I have not yet decided if I will actually take
them. I am also scheduled to have a bunch of testing done this coming Friday
to determine if there is some blood flow restriction. Stay tuned.
Tuesday we did actual trail building on a relocation over near the Beauty
Spot. It is in the same area where Benj helped work some time back. We will be
working in the area for quite a while although we did open one section
Tuesday. It was cold up there.

On Wed. Anita and I went to visit Dave and Pat Edwards (and Beth). Dave has
started working on the '65 Plymouth I let him have. He had requested my help
and I really wanted to see him get his hands dirty.
We pretty much did the fuel system and the brake system. He wears thin
rubber gloves! So, no dirty hands for him!

This place is doing pretty well turning into a wildlife sanctuary. We have
birds, turkey, deer and plenty of grounhogs , skunks and possums. Also, for the
last few years I have been planting ramp roots in the woods. The ramps are
popping up but they do not appear to be multiplying as I would hope (like the
deer and turkeys).

Saturday was a trip to Seymour to "help" with the drag race car. Dwight
hauled a load of misc. parts to do the rest of the job. Pat thinks it will be
ready to run this Fall. It took all day to install the shift assembly, obviously
shift assemblies require a lot of pondering.

They also require welding which Pat can do without bothering to don a helmet.

No week would be complete without a GSH report.

While I was gone, she went by to check on Hal and Sue. Hal had got his MRI
report which he told her about. It did not sound too encouraging.

Things are pretty much in bloom. It figures, as a cold spell is expected to
hit early this coming week.

I would be interested in hearing reports from all the various branches of
the family tree. I know there have been some events.
