March 15, 2015 · Sunday

Together Again

5 photographs·356 words

GSH had a birthday. It was on Wednesday and we had a very hectic day with a
lot of self-induced trauma and drama. The net result being that while I had
at least 1/2 mile worth of road paved with good intentions, she did not get
to go out for dinner at all. But I did feed her.

We went to Erwin to check on Herb. Most people , at least most men, have
facial hair. He has one side with facial hair and one side with legal hair.
He is not fully decided how to handle the situation. I believe a full beard
would be quite interesting possible bringing people in from miles around.

Other than that, he is doing fine, better than I expected. Getting plenty
of exercise and planning his annual battles with the critters over
possession of the garden fruits. He had obtained a copy of the medical
reports from the trip to Vanderbilt. I read the entire report and am now
more impressed with the surgeon than I was before. I believe the chances
are quite good that he really did "get it" this time.

We had an out of town visitor this week. As part of his physical and mental
preparation for West Point, Adam has entered some sort of competition in
which he will need to demonstarte proficiency with a "9MM" I promised to
loan him one if he would come get it. I also lined up a real, honest to
goodness, pro to check him out in all aspects. She did and gave him an
excellent passing grade.

With Spring in sight, I plowed a spot for the garden. Now, all I need is
some willing workers. Katy?

I also plowed a row in the upper garden for a potato row. I am told that
March 20th is the proper sign of the moon for potato planting. I think one
will have to pay attention to rain also but I intend to try. Stay tuned.

In other exciting news, the oven was delivered (by Lowes people) and
installed (by Wayne).The first batch of brownies acame out practically
perfect.
