Hey, Y'all:

I just want to reach out to remind A.T. volunteers and Trail club hiking
enthusiasts to be vigilant in your prevention and regular checks for ticks.
I know there's some thought that "it's too early" for them, but the warm
winter we had likely means we're just as likely to encounter them now as at
the height of summer.

Here's more information on preventative measures
<http://appalachiantrail.org/home/explore-the-trail/hiking-basics/health>.
