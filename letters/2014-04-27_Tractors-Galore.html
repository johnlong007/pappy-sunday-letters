April 27, 2014 · Sunday

Tractors Galore

13 photographs·725 words

The Blountville old tractor club members have always handled registration
for the Gray tractor show. The Gray show was this week and I handled all
the registration with the able help of Sanford.

​
We worked Wed. PM and all day Th. Fri. and Saturday. Mostly it is a lot of
fun talking with people about their choice of tractors. I did get fussed at
three times for what people perceived to be failures of the host personnel
(me in one case). Funny how people want to keep on fussing even after
receiving an apology. Friday afternoon a lady came by with three little
kids . I offered to hold the little one (7 months) while she filled out the
registration. Child took right up with me so I asked the Mom to take a
picture.

​
Don took the little crawler over. It got more than its fair share of
attention. One guy asked about buying. I told him $2,000 and he did not ask
again.

​
Today we picked up the club trailer and took it back to Debby Kidd's farm
for storage.

​
When I went to the heart Dr. in October, he said the pills I was taken were
being connected to memory loss in old folks and changed me to something
different. Starting about Christmas or so I began to get more than normal
aches and pains. By late March it was bad enough that for the first time
since 1982 I had to quit my daily running.
Laura said it is your pills doing it so I stopped taking before our trip
and scheduled a dr. appt after we got back. By the time I got in to see the
Dr. on Monday a lot, maybe most of the pains had gone way.
But, he said your heart mummer is worse and scheduled tests to check for
heart damage. I was not thrilled. Stay tuned.

For good news, Herb came by. His face is healing very well. They have
removed most of his wrinkles. The little ole widows are going to be lined
up at his door.

The hornet nest fell down. I peeled a side back to look inside. As best I
could tell, it had about five floors. I wonder if the first bee has a plan
or she designs it as it goes along. Seems clear it was all one unit rather
than a farm house with a bunch of additions.

The cat has been given free run of the house since the weather has warmed
and we can leave the shop door open at night. He gets along fairly well
with the Kroger dog but less well with fatty.
After they go to bed he comes to check me out.

​
The black cat was out sunning himself this morning when I let Tom out.

​
The sink GSH uses lost some finish. A plumber was called in to replace the
sink. Best plumber picture I could get.
I
.
The new airport access road is wide and smooth. Speed limit is 45. Most go
faster, some much faster. It is heavily patrolled with many getting pulled
over. When I was coming home Thursday evening, as usual I was following a
rabbit. His brake lights went on and a highway patrolman coming toward us
made a u-turn behind me with all his lights on. However, he zipped by me
and pulled the rabbit over, I went by before he got out of the car lest he
try to flag me down. Have not got above 47 on that road since then.

On the home front I got all the mowing done on Monday and Tuesday. Looks
like it will take about 2-3 days to do everything. I may decide to let it
grow up in weeds.

​
The fellow driving the big blue plow tractor is Jimmy Deakins. He also owns
one of these 8N tractors with a Cadillac engine. He wanted to do something
different so I let him have a 331 Hemi a couple of years back. It will show
up in an 8N in due time.
