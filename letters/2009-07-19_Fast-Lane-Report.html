July 19, 2009 · Sunday

Fast Lane Report

9 photographs·264 words

The big events for the weeknone.

The events:

Kenneth is back working on the Fiat most every night. It goes right along.

The turkeys and turklets appear to be well and happy. More than one family
I believe.

I helped Wayne pull the engine from a car more or less donated by Herb. As
a reward, GSH prepared B & G.

Running each day continues. It is getting better but slowly. Saturday, we
walked down into the Laurel falls gorge. I had to stop and rest several
times on the way out.

Herb came over to check on his decrepit younger brother. Fortunately, he
brought some fresh corn. I do believe the best ever.

An old Oliver tractor has made its way to the front row. Fiddled with it
off and on all week. Cannot get the shift lever installed such that gears can
be properly shifted. Tomorrow, I am going to get it right.

Thursday night, a storm came through. One of the big pine trees beside our
barn blew down and hit the corner of the barn. It really belongs to the LOL
next door but I guess I will just repair the barn rather than bug her.

No do-gooder work as Dave had company. The visit to see Hal was about like
the week before. Also went to see Francis Rutherford. She is doing well but
getting skinny.

Katy, this garden picture is after the willing workers weeded it. There
will be food to harvest after your return I believe.
