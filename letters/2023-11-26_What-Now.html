What Now ?

14 photographs·311 words

As most know, I like to have a goal and a plan for getting there. For quite
some time my goal has been to stay able to help take care of Patrice to the
very end.
Now I am without a goal or a plan.

L to right. My GGM Mary Jane Parks Howell, Granny Garland, My mother, Hazel
; GGM gave me the wooden egg before I was 4 years old.

The plan for the week was Thanksgiving Thursday, Apple Butter Friday and
Family event for Patrrice on Saturday. The vent was in spite of my
instructions.

I am ever so thankful the kids went on in spite of me.

Family members came in from near and far. A bit overwhelming.

A bit of work continued on the 55. We finally found TDC and marks on the
vibration damper. Now, we still have to get a timing light hooked up and
set the timing. Stay tuned.

Thanksgiving dinner was a double header for us. Long family then the Jack
family.

A Jr. Willing Worker. Learning from the master.

Remember Todd from fishing trips? Todd and wife Kim came by. The first
time.

Beth decided to get a good early start and take advantage of all her free
help.

The WW's showed up after it got a bit warmer.

PBBD lured a couple off to help clean GM's pond.

Alan thought for sure the saw mill would entice helpers.

The apple butter crew cut into the fire wood supply. Fortunately we have
ways and means to replenish'

A load of boards to deliver to Reservoir Road tomorow and a load of sawed
slabs on top for here.r

Replenishing firewood.

Busy week, winding down.
