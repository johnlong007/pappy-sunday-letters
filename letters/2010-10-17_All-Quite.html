October 17, 2010 · Sunday

All Quite

10 photographs·378 words

Sort of a recycle of last week.

Dave is not doing very well. We went by his house but they were not home.
Later in the week I got a note saying he is having a tough time dealing with
all the medicine.

We went back to tidy up a few details at the Curley Maple Gap shelter. The
forest service people had cut a big white Oak tree. We sawed it into
firewood length . Used about 10 pieces for seats around the fire ring and split
the rest for firewood.


On the home front, I tacked a beam on the log splitter to keep it from
flexing. It will require a full weld seam before trial runs.

Also on the sort of home front, the pasture annex was opened for business.

On the racing scene, I went with Dwight to Seymour Friday night. They
loaded the car to go to the track and I came home. The test was to determine
if the change in rear end ratio got the car to the finish line quicker. The
report is "no". They ran Friday night and Saturday night doing about like
before until a transmission input shaft failure sent them home . However,
not to worry, I have on good authority that hope springs eternal. Stay tuned.

Fall is here and the leaves are in full color. I finally finished all the
string trimmer mowing and started mowing the pine and walnut and oak hill
with the tractor and rotary mower.
Beth, do you remember planting the oaks on the hill? Several are 8-10
inches in dia . now, the place is turning into a wildlife sanctuary as intended.

We are going on a backpacking trip M-T-W. GSH has worked diligently getting
gear together. First, she repaired a tent, then fashioned a sleeping sack
for two and built an alcohol cook stove from bits and pieces.


Right now, she is starting to pack our stuff into the back packs. I would
love to include a picture of the completed project but assume it will not be
available until shortly after bedtime.

I am considering taking all the folks off my distribution who do not send
some sort of acknowledgment from time to time. Would that be petulant?
