July 18, 2018 · Wednesday

Young David

14 photographs·344 words

​I got to talk with Charlie a bit last night. I gave me a jolt to realize
we have GGC who would have qualified for Summer Camp. I wish I had sent out
a notice. Maybe Beth and Aaron would have come.

Resized the pictures giving me trouble Sunday night and here they are. As
per my usual practice, I did not work with David on AT work. He went on off
up the trail with Tim. Tim sent me a before and after picture of the
section on which they worked.
Looks like heavy duty rock work.

Each new worker gets a patch presented by Carl.

​

​I towed the Studebaker and it started right up. Ran good in spite of 2
year old (no E) fuel. The last time I drove it, it overheated. We added
coolant and ran it a good while. Today, David drove it several laps in the
driveway. He likes it.
Will be a while before I am willing to venture far from home. Cooling and
freeze plug doubts.

​
Our grocery bill increased. However, David made supper two night. Same menu
each time.

​A lot of the same old things all would remember. Clean up shop, weed, sort
stuff, wash cars.

​He even got to try out the 1911. Did very well with it. Second to Aaron.

​Wayne is prepping for a WI run Friday. Anyone want to ride along. Up
Friday, back Sunday is the plan. Bringing home car and M'cycles.

We did a bit of work on the '63. rear window is now installed. Ready to
install sets and get exhaust system installed.
​
Tires on out of use cars lose air. Sometimes slow, sometimes not so slow
but years go by. Letting weight rest on a flat tire will destroy the tire
in short order. Today, we jacked up several cars and put blocks under neath
to keep weight off. Hot, dark, buggy and dirty work. Just my style.

​Lap runner.

​

Instruction for care and feeding of cat.
​

Any news from anywhere?
