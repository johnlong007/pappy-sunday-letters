March 30, 2025 · Sunday

Rocky Road

18 photographs·277 words

I got out of the cancer center with no infusions Monday. Only a firm
admonition to not get sick or bleed. So, I got an infection, a nose bleed
and a serious backache for good measure. Not sure if the backache is a
passing fancy or a portend of things to come. Stay tuned.

On a better note, Sanford and Don stopped by Wednesday after brunch with
the GOB's.

We spend a lot of time and effort trying to level the cherry slab. It is
not flat and we each have back pain.

Wayne decided the house needs a new roof. Perhaps forth time's the charm.

Not even close to the due date and the tax lady is working away (under
close supervision).

A high point for the week.

Much higher on the hog than we ever got here.

Almost mowing season.

somehow the mower housing cracked.

Fortunately, we have an ace welder who does house calls.

A sure sign of Spring. Look close and you can spot an eternal triangle. It
was fun to watch unfold.

Diane has been working to make family artifacts go into the hands of those
who really want them. I continued to be glad to be part of the process. The
rule is you must 1. want the item and 2. be present to collect.

The heavy equipment mechanics continue to plug along.

The implement M & R crew made serious progress this week.
