March 20, 2022 · Sunday

Spring

18 photographs·244 words

And an Old Man's thoughts turn to:
The only thing I can raise left alone by the critters.

This is for the world travelers. Been there?

Has everyone watched the program "Treasures in your Attic"?

We toured the Agricultural bldg one day.

I have mentioned Dave and Carol often. We had lunch with them one day. Good
to check in with friends from time to time.

I decided to let the engine turning task rest a while and turned attention
to trying to get rear door open.

By taking the inside door panel off, I was able to get a picture of the
door lock/latch assembly. Rusted solid. I have stopped work without
resorting to brute force. Time to regroup.

I think the sawmill crew worked about 1/2 day. Turning out some really nice
lumber.

Next in line. The Really BIG Oak(s) and another poplar.

Waiting in hopes I will try to raise a garden.

Sunday afternoon bursting with excitement !

Just as I arrived at church for early service a fire engine and emergency
vehicle turned in to the lot.
Turned out one of the worship leaders had collapsed.. They carted him out ,
I have not heard anything more so expect it is not too serious.
