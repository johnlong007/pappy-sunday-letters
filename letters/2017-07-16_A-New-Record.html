July 16, 2017 · Sunday

A New Record !

14 photographs·486 words

For conclusion broad jumping from a standing start.A spent years working in
the quality management arena and once upon a time was considered to be an
expert.One thing stands out, to avoid the journey through the swamp take
the diagnostic journey first.

Monday morning I started the week with the intent to switch the IH off the
little portable fuel tank and over onto the built in fuel tank. Nothing to
it, flush the tank of old fuel, flush the lines with new fuel, unhook
little tank, hook up fuel tank and off in running in no time. Ha !

While it was running on the little tank it would not hit a lick on the fuel
tank. Fired right up on ether.

Obviously the fuel pump (electric) is not pumping.

Hot out there.
Time to replace the fuel pump out of inventory.

​A lot of difficulty getting fittings to fit.

​​This was all spread out over several days. Finaaly the calvery arrived.

​Turns out it was not the fuel pump at all. The carburetor has a solenoid
operated needle valve to stop engine dieseling during shut down. I had
checked and cleaned it but somehow it seems to have been blocking the fuel
flow. Three days in the hot - hot sun for nought. But, It seems to be ok
now. All systems seem to work properly. Next I will let it down, button it
up, run up and down drive, take pictures and begin to look for a new home.
Stay tuned.

The main difference between being retired and being employed seems to be
the pay rate.

Anita, GSH and I took off on day before breakfast to go mow weeds and clip
briar's on our section of the AT. We moved right along and made the round
trip in 6 hours getting out before the high for the day (91) arrived.

​Do gooder runs in the family, GSH, Wayne and I went to see niece Lisa in
Erwin. She tells sad stories about being able to take a shower in the
fireplace during hard rains. Measurements were taken and a cover is to be
fabricated since fireplace is not in use except as a shower and other
choices are available.

Thursday was back to AT work.
We went back to the bridge site to finish the bridge. I was told to turn
the rock left from last week into gravel. Mission was accomplished.I did
not take a finished product picture.

​The old bridge was supported by logs which were used to build a turnpike
at end of the new bridge.

​I suppose two runs out of three at bats is not too bad.​

​
Dave and Pat Edwards came by. We caught up on their family. They on ours.
Dave asked about " that girl who is going to be a CEO or president, maybe
both." Who could he have been talking about?
