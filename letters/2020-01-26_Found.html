January 26, 2020 · Sunday

Found !

10 photographs·374 words

As you may remember, a search has been underway for 2-3 weeks looking for
the '63 side emblems.
Today, I found them, right where I had placed for safekeeping and where I
had already looked . I had started searching online for replacements and
found none.

Also, after a long period of avoidance, for fear I would damage the paint,
I started buffing the '63 paint.
Turns out to be pretty easy to not do damage buffing. The paint had a
number of dust specks due to outside painting. I found it is possible to
remove specks and it is not wise to get into the surrounding area. I
created a lot of work for myself.

After buffing come polishing. This picture is posed as GSH was not at all
comfortable with the polisher.

Good progress was made, but, miles to go before we rest.

GSH is a demon when it comes to dragging brush to the brush pond. She will
not run out of material.

I finished trimming the trunks and dragged a lot of brush down hill and
cross drive. Much more to go. I am toying with buying a PTO driven brush
chopper. Wonder if I could rent an operator? No way would GSH get near such
a machine.

Wayne came over to give me aid and comfort on the "63 front shocks. While
here, we went up to determine what type Oak fell in the storm. Best guess
is Red Oak. I looked on line at prices and estimate worth about $100 as is.
Probably a lot more sawed and split into firewood.

The front shocks. I worked several hours trying to install without success.
Got the old ones out . New one must go up into a cavity. To do so, it must
be partially compressed. Too weak. Could not do in such an awkward
position. One is supposed to compensate for being old by being crafty.
Flunked that test.

Took Wayne a few minutes.

Some people went to NC to inspect a newly arrived bundle from heaven. We
stayed right here and got visited.
Also got a haircut.
