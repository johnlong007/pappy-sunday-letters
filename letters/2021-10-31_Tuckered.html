October 31, 2021 · Sunday

Tuckered

10 photographs·277 words

Leaves were falling. Rain was coming. decided I did not want to blow the
leaves off so I swept 'em. Kept thinking I heard a voice saying "anyone
past 65 is too old to be on a roof".

Some members of the family do not get to see Fall colors. Some drive to the
mountains. We just go in and out our drive.

PBBD came over this morning and I went to Perkins . Waitress Connie sent
her a muffin. Then, at church people are crawling out of their bomb
shelters and the bettween break bar ahs reopened. I filched two do-nuts.
The bad news is our SS class has dropped from about 40 to 10 today. I do
not expect it to recover much at all.

GSH has healed her cracked and broken bones best I can tell. She can walk
but is unsure of herself.

Alan received a picture from Rachel.

More work done on the retaining basin before the rain arrived. I think the
gradient is close ,maybe close enough. I do not know how much rainfall we
got to bring this much into the pond.

The remaining problem is the soggy wet portion. My plan is to wait until it
dries enough for me to level then seed the entire area. Probably 2-3 weeks.

More good progress. Wayne and Diane installed the windshield in the '37.
Some electrical work to do then ask Pat to do a pre-paint inspection before
I actually ask him to do a paint job.

Waiting on the antelope,
