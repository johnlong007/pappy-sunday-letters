Maximum heart rate is the upper threshold that your cardiovascular system
can sustain during physical exertion. This rate decreases with age. The
maximum heart rate for males (HRmax) can be estimated using the Karvonen
method as 220 minus your age. So for 87 year old men, the maximum rate is
approximately 133 beats per minute. A modified HRmax formula specific to
women has been developed as 206 minus 88 percent of your age2
<https://www.healthly.io/en/heart/exercise-pulse-rate-age/87-years/#cite>.
This method results in a 129 beats per minute maximum pulse for a 87 year
old female. See also: Resting heart rates for 87 year olds
<https://www.healthly.io/en/heart/pulse-rate-age/87-years/>
