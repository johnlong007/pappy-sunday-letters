April 6, 2008 · Sunday

More Normal

8 photographs·158 words

I worked on the Oliver crawler quite a bit this week. It had been left in
the open and had water in the transmission and differential. Both had to be
opened and cleaned.

On my trip to Erwin, both Tootsie and I were treated to an examination of
the new, bear proof, pregnant goat pen.

Eagle scout event, a first for me and as far as I know, a first for the
family .

AT work involved carrying a saw for 11 miles and cutting 29 fairly large to
real big downed trees. Normally, we only cut out a walk through to discourage
four wheelers.

The front (and last wall) of the outhouse goes up! Note the scenic array of
backsides.

Wayne is building a bike hauler to tow behind his vehicle. He also is taking
beginning welding. My dog is observing along with his welding instructor.
