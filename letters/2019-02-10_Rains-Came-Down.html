February 10, 2019 · Sunday

Rains Came Down

13 photographs·407 words

And the floods came up. Wed. night we got 2 1/2 inches of rain.

Once upon a time we had a pond which made a lovely catch basin. But, having
tried for years to get it to hold water for extended periods I finally gave
up and filled it in to make a soccer field.
It did not get much usage as a soccer field as campers preferred Albert's
lawn for Frisbee.

Now, good Lord willing I am going to rebuild the pond/catch basin this
Summer. May need use of some equipment operators.

Across Albert's lawn we own a strip (30') between Albert and Larry. The big
white pine is on the strip. You can see a gap on left side where the very
large limb broke under the heavy snow. Last week I dragged the trunk out
and ploughed up a furrow.
Tuesday I dragged all the limbs across Albert's lawn to our property. A lot
of work !

Then the next day, we dragged the limbs to the brush pond, cut into smaller
pieces and threw on pile. It was 76 degrees that day. A new record.

Lisa sent a note and said her storage buildings had been broken intto and
some things taken. We went over to look over scene of the crime and stopped
en route to visit Aunt Flo. She is 91 and spry. Planning for 100.
Then on up to the house. Pretty clear the local thieves are not mental
giants. I believe Barney could locate. Proving might be another matter.
BTY, Diane did the good neighbor thing and aided in apprehending thieves
who broke into her neighbors house. Nailed em.

Most of our indoor time was on the sewing machine. We made a lot of
progress removing the green and white coats of paint.

Max is doing great. A high energy outdoor dog. He is learning the property
lines.

A granddaughter of GSH's father's sister contacted Laura through 23and me.
That got me started hunting through my 22 years of pictorial family history
folders. Sunday PM i spread out on table with intent to begin organizing.
Later, I stopped with my new found headache and a resolve to work on the
project more often for shorter periods. I do want to get it done.
