October 9, 2016 · Sunday

Win Some, ---------,

10 photographs·385 words

Today we went to Erwin, Lisa had told me she could not get Herb's truck
started. I enjoy helping Lisa with mechanical things, she is interested and
wants to learn or is a very good pretender.
Did not take too long to determine carburetor float was stuck closed. A few
taps with pliers and she fired truck up. Then we moved to the small dump
truck. Ether, booster battery and it fired up. Then proceeded to flood gas
all over carb. It would appear float is stuck open. Left that for another
day.
My only picture was of some sort of spider edifice which I thought someone
could identify.

Who can identify these items, along with the story behind?

Saturday, we took Wayne and related entourage to Mt. Mitchell for an
outdoor adventure. Dumped them in cold, wet and miserable conditions but
they all looked delighted to be there. Sort of.

You have seen our little green frog swimin in the water, just to be
different, you might want to admire our guardian of the shop door.
He/she/it ? just ignores traffic in and out even while perched on the door
sill.

I did better on Dwight blood letting this week. Did not let the timer time
out. Did not drop the needle, got blood on first try, now if I can repeat
each time.

Last Sunday one of my new Wal-Mart tires went flat. I could not see any
problem so I decided to take it back . That cost me 1/2 day and $11.
it had a nail. I suspect a brat in the park nail. Beware.

I put a seat cover on the crawler seat ! I also started it, failed to turn
fuel on and ran it empty. Cardinal sin for a diesel engine.But. I am
getting much better at bleeding air out of the system. No work on '63 or
fire engine this week.

Thursday we did trail work.Pat Loven went with me to remove a reported
blowdown. It was 40" in diameter and too much for our saw, So, we cut steps
and built ramps.

Kathy Moffit asked about her former students this morning at church.

I bet some of our grandchildren went to ball games Saturday.

Do we think Savannah is really going to go through with wedding?
