April 1, 2012 · Sunday

Try Again

9 photographs·471 words

If I run up against a blank wall I will just do attachments.

I have really felt lousy this week. All my life this feeling would have
been a garden variety cold. Now, my consultants tell me it is
allergies.Fortunately, Herb gave me a quart of his home produced, Unicoi
county honey. I am told the best way to avoid allergies is to eat honey
produced by bees feeding on the same pollen causing the allergies.
Apparently it builds up the immune system. Anyway, it sure is good honey
and I am feeling much better.

The week has mostly been the same ole same ole.

As the opportunity presented itself, I worked on the '37.

Once more, the AT work was at Hughes Gap. The trail is cutting right
through a large field of ramps. I collected a sack full just picking up the
ones dug to make the trail. The ramps are early this year and will be grown
in 2-3 weeks I believe.

We are getting a lot of through hikers now on their way from Ga. (takes
about 6 weeks to get here). Bob brought several along Tuesday. At the end
of the work day, they each get a patch.

While I was busy having fun, GSH was busy working on our tax returns. Makes
one feel good to know it all goes for good causes.

The preview screen has filled and I do not know how to add more. So, I am
going to attempt attachments.

As most were able to deduce, another driver education class has started. I
have two objectives for the trainees, one to drive for 70 years and never
put a scratch on vehicle, the other is to be able to drive anything .

Several are familiar with the magical dishwasher. Making it operate
apparently involved a fair amount of voodoo. It appears that the voodoo
potency wore off and the dishwasher quit responding in spite of chants and
incantations. Fortunately, the good fairy delivered and only slightly use
twin and an apprentice plumber showed up for installation. It can be
observed that the plumber still has room to develop.

Regarding the birds and bees. It appears the happy BB family has multiplied
and divided. The happy couple are wearing their wings and beaks to nubs
feeding the family. However, so far they have not stuck their heads outside
the front opening. I will stay alert.

Closer to home. I found a bird nest. Not in a bird house, in our house.
Inside the light frame in the first bedroom. Not at all clear to me how
Momma was able to traffic back and forth. Probably through a hole in the
fan screen.
