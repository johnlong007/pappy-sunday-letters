May 5, 2018 · Saturday

Mowing

18 photographs·549 words

Dave Edwards has a much smaller house now than before the last move. When
we first visited to help with his car project he had a big plush chair in
his basement/garage. He asked if I wanted as he wanted to get rid of it. I
assured him I did indeed. So, he delivered it one day this week. While
here, I rounded up several more car parts for him so he is doing OK on the
deal.

​Last week, we sawed a downed tree on Long farm. This week, we loaded the
sawed wood and brought it here. I have mostly just been stacking as is.
Perhaps some log splitters will show up sometime this Summer.

​
Mowing season is here. I like to mow, the place looks good for a few days
after I finish mowing . This week I mowed with String trimmer, push mower,
rider and the tractor/bush hog. For the moment, mowing is caught up. Before
starting the rider, I gave it a good check out and installed new blades. I
would not be able to keep up without the rider.

​
Dave and Carol wanted ramps. But, did not want to walk very far. So, we
went to Dennis Cove. Plenty of ramps and 1/4 mile walk.

​

I have mentioned my Melbourne pepper before. It seems to have over Wintered
very well. May 10 is usual safe date for late frost here. Jumped the gun a
bit and transplanted the pepper back to circle this week. Stay tuned.
​

Not quite sure how I did this but had not shown any blood and gore for a
while.

​​
​ Niece Lisa sent me a note saying lawn mower would start but not run. I
had been laying off to go take ramps to Flo for a while so decided to do
two for one. Flo passed 90 last Summer. She has a little pick up truck
which she uses to run around Erwin. She had been out and had a bed full of
potted plants. Flowers, tomatoes and peppers. I should have taken a
picture. While going through the plants she explained to me what she
planned to plant different next year.

It seems Lisa has used "E" fuel in mower and Herb's truck. I suspect each
needs a new fuel pump.

We went back to the long farm and sawed another downed tree into
firewood.Got me to wondering if there is any prospect of turning wood
harvesting over to a replacement. Not looking real good just yet.

​
Pool room work goes right along.

​I took pictures of several of these each with different colors.

​

​This is a picture of the floor under the door sill to entrance airlock. I
think it is also the route the termites used get into the wall. The pool
room carpenters are replacing all the damaged wood. A plan is being
developed to ind and destroy nest which will be under the slab floor.

​

​

​
We have a friend at church who goes all over the US moving big loads. Real
big loads.

​​
Tomorrow, it is back to the dentist. So, early edition. One of the days I
am going to skip entirely to see if anyone knows or cares.

​​
