September 16, 2018 · Sunday

Hurdles

11 photographs·528 words

No AT work this week, But, much to my surprise, they went right on without
me.

Martha sent me a note. Little sister has gone into some sort of assisted
living for a bit or R&R for her stress fractured leg below knee.
Distressing. Maybe only a couple of weeks. Maybe.

GSH and I go to the same family Dr. and schedule back to back appointments.
We had our 6 months or 6 thousand mile checkups this week. We are rocking
along with all vitals right down the middle thanks to genetics, clean
living, clean mind. clean teeth and , in my case, a tasteful selection of
pills each morning and night. Back in 6 months.

I procured short sections of flexible Exhaust pipe to install on the '63 so
could run for extended periods of time. It runs good and sounds good on the
93 non E. But, smoking up the neighborhood. Could be stuck oil rings but
probably valve guide seals. I am not much of a fan of additives (mouse
milk) however under the hope springs eternal heading I am going to try
something. Low risk.

Hurricane predictions and projections all week. Water diversion behind this
house has always been a problem. Still is. I decided to take the brute
force method and see if that would help. Jury is out as we have not got any
of the projected down pours so far.

We did take in some hurricane refugees Saturday. In an attempt to give them
a day to remember, we had apple squeezing to obtain apple juice.
At least two willing workers.

They got to sample the product without waiting for it to age. Let them use
my TTU "hot" cup.

Perhaps we will get a follow up report on the fate of the semi-finished
product when it is fully finished.

The little plants are the 2 week old turnips. It became obvious I had done
a poor job seed distributing. PBBD procured a bit more seeds so I did the
missed part. It will be interesting to see how things turn out as first
were planted by the "signs" making these out of phase I suppose.

I planted two paw paw trees down in front of the barn. GSH lopped one off
with the nice new loppers LLLL gave her for Christmas a couple of years
back. I was able to hold her away from the other tree which is beginning to
bear. If Jake wants Paw paws. There are still plenty but my guess is this
week will do it.

The pine beatles like to attack trees up toward the top where the tree is
about 6 inches or so. I noticed the top of one of the white pines turning
brown. Wayne more or less offered to cut it off below the infection. Now, I
have got to get rid of the part he cut out.

OK cat people, Gwendolyn, Meredith, Katy, Sarah,LLLL, Christopher can your
cat do this? I know motor is out of the running.
