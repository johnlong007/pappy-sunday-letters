November 25, 2007 · Sunday

Gullible's Travels

3 photographs·156 words

To those who question my statement about do-gooder activities that one can
have reward in the here or the hereafter (but not both) , I refer them to
Matthew 6 : 1-4.
So, once more there are no do-gooder pictures.
I did go visit both Herb and Aunt G. Neither have changed much since last
time so I did not take any pictures.
The big event was renting a 15 passenger van and going to Wisconsin to visit
our representatives to the land of the frozen North We had two empty seats
as both Joe and my (unnamed) sister wimped out. In spite of, or perhaps because
of that, we had a great trip.
A friend at church has a new baby. Pat made a blanket for the baby. I can
include a picture for her since it will be in spite of rather than because of.
