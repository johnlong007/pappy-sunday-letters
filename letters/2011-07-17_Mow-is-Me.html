Mow is Me

3 photographs·255 words

It may be possible to teach and old dog new tricks but I am sure it is slow and painful.

Aaron set this PC up for gmail and I intended to send out my update that way but decided to hold up until next week. I do have a gmail address and a cell phone number (It receives and prefers text messages).

kbuchanan1939@gmail.com
phone is 423-765-5546

For the first time in ages I did not go out on any do-gooder projects this week. No reward in the here or the here after. I hope and expect to do better in the week to come.

On the home front, we continued to put things away after little bundles from heaven departed.

Not all their worldly possessions departed with them.

I finished painting the body of the SC , stuck the nose cone, hood and fuel tank on and enlisted GSH to take it to the barn.
First, she had to get psyched up.

Then we put the John Deere away.

For the benefit of those who could not attend:

While Aaron and Vanessa were here, he took the Studebaker for a quick lap.

One of my objectives has been to teach Vanessa the fundamentals of being a country girl.

Just in case anyone is wondering, the corn is now ready. Too bad about Summer Campers.

Does everyone know about Jonah and the gourd vine. I think the same thing got mine.

Next week, I will do better. In the meantime. Is there any news from anywhere?

I cannot get it to go with pictures.
