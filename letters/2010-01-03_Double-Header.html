January 3, 2010 · Sunday

Double Header

9 photographs·276 words

How often does one have "Christmas" and New Years in the same week?
Obviously once in a blue moon.

I am going to include a bunch of pictures so Aaron can get a feel for what
he missed. We missed him. Aaron, can you identify everyone?

The car project is progressing , slowly. We think we are ready for a trial
run up and down the driveway but are waiting for the weather to warm a bit.
I did procure 5 gallons of premium grade, no ethanol, fuel. Stay tuned.

I went by to check on Hal. It appears he is having a recurrence of tumor
modules. He was feeling better due to massive steroid doses. They will be
going back this week to determine next course of action.

One of the Friday hikers had a New Years eve party. It was very nice, a
soup supper. They live in a very upscale house beside the golf course. The men
mostly watched the football game until about 10 then everyone said time to
go home and we all left.

Friday I went on the hike. It was up Chimney top. The double peak mountain
down I-81 about mile 35 or so. Not a long hike but really steep and pretty
slippery due to all the rain. One peak has an abandoned beacon tower and
the other a short, abandoned fire tower. I will include a picture made on the
beacon peak looking toward the other peak. The other picture is looking
down toward the Rogersville side.

Final pictures are GSH relaxing with a Katy Christmas present after the
dust had settled and wildlife on the hillside.
