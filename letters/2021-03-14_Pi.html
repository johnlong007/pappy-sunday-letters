March 14, 2021 · Sunday

Pi

10 photographs·546 words

AT PBBD's direction, our primary Dr. got us signed up for home health care
starting aftwer my heart procedure. I have been very pleased with the
people and the services. However it appears to go one month at a time and
our month is over.
They said all sorts of nice things to me before saying goodby

The main things they worked on for me was my hand and my painful neck. I
think a lot of improvement in each and I intend to keep on with the
exercises they left for me. The Dr. saying herert should be good for 5
years or more got me to thinking. If I can reduce ongoing pain for next
five years it will be well worth the trouble.
It also got me thinking a bout a five year plan. If I actually have some
time left, what should my goals be? I am open to suggestions.

When I was in the 6th and 7th. grade at Temple Hill I took a lot of
pictures. I helped Mom put the pictures in a scrapbook which she gave to
me. I have recently digitized the pictures and am using such contacts as I
can muster to help identify the persons of interest.

I believe this Is Bill Hughes. At least I recognize the motor bike. He had
a Whizzer. Johs sword brought it all back. I would have given my eyeteeth
for a chance to ride his Whizzer but under no circumstances would I have
actually done so. My father would not have approved. I still want to ride a
Whizzer. I bought one once on Ebay but the seller backed out.

But I am thankful for some other things. My friend Dave Dunham is about 20
years younger than I am. He is broke down and stove up. He has an outdoor
fire pit and asked for some easy to split wood.I sawed and delivered a load
to him. Caro fed us supper. About the only friends we have who have stayed
open all the way. Sanford and the Friday Burger Bar folks are now open for
business, at least most of them.

GSH had a birthday and is now as old as I am. As we often got to the Longs
for dinner and a show on Thursday evening,Anita had an early birthday cake
for her mom.

Then as we often go to PBBD's on Friday night, she had another cake plus
Laura came with a cake. So, Katy, a three cake birthday.

In the Springtime, an old man's thoughts turn to pruning the apple trees.
The pole saw worked great on all the high, big limbs. The low small ones
required the loppers.

Another WW showed up to use pole saw and remove some limbs shading the roof
causing moss to grow.

In a sparling display of carelessness, I cooked the ammeter on the crawler.
Fortunately Wayne came through with a replacement and PBBD installed it.

Later we tried to get the crawler running. It appears all were asking for
divine intervention but no help arrived and it did not start. Stay tuned.
