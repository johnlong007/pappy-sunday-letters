October 20, 2024 · Sunday

All Good.

14 photographs·264 words

I may hang out a marital counseling shingle ! David and Kylie are almost
past the point of no return. So, they came by to get the benefit of my 68
years, four months and 10 days wisdom.

Mr Stripey has done well.

The fence row across the upper edge of Farmer Lisa's place caused most of
the big stuff caught in the flood waters to drop in her front lawn. She has
it mostly piled up waiting for disposal.

The flood water spread across the entire bottom but was moving slowly so
that trash dropped out but no erosion occurred. We took the truck over and
collected a lot of the debree.

In other flood relief. Ron and Fran are here from Cosby to work with their
church on some site near here.

Saturday, we had lunch with Anita at the ruritan booth in downtown
Blountville. Heritage days I think.

The deer prep the chestnuts and the turkeys do clean up.

At last the drain line has been repaired. Still have to pour concrete. Stay
tuned.

A & C have the crawler beat into submission.

All new gutter with all new leafguard

Alan sponsored a McRib feast Saturday.

Family came in from miles around.

One of my all time favorite Summer Camp memories was being in here, with
knee high Meredith and hearing her say "I want to go outside where my
friend Rachel is."
