May 3, 2020 · Sunday

Muddling Along

10 photographs·284 words

I try to read all I find about the virus then sort through and decide what
I believe and what I intend to do or not do.. I plan to go about my
business, but be a bit careful.

First things first.

Hard to believe we have a 7 year old great grand child.

The ramps I have planted here are becoming mature with blades dying down.
Next, they will send up a flowering stem which makes a seed pod.

LLLL showed up here Monday, Tuesday, we went to the secret ramp patch just
off the AT and harvested enough for a decent feed.

No car or tractor work.

Did make a run to Erwin to retrieve the cherry logs. I think Wayne and Bob
are working together to determine best usage.

The serious activity was continued attack on the big Oak.

I had to remove the root ball to gain access to the tree trunk. But, had to
remove it piece by piece.

Then get the trunk into manageable size

Rather pleased with this operation. I put jack under spot where I wanted
trunk sawed. Put an Oak 2 by 6 on the jack to support trunk on either side
of Jack (and to avoid sawing into the jack}.
It worked, pieces did not move at all after Wayne cut through.

Much time and effort later, the two sections of the trunk are on our
property.
Now I should be able to get in and out to remove all the other wood and
clean up the brush as I promised Albert. Stay tuned.
