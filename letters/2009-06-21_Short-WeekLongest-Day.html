June 21, 2009 · Sunday

Short Week/Longest Day

8 photographs·299 words

Sort of three parts to the week for me.
I, with some willing workers from time to time, worked on the Blountville
Old Tractor show.
Since my number one tractor painter abandoned me, I had to finish painting
the Cletrac. But, Herb, Dave, Charlie and John all showed up to help get it
out of the shop and loaded on the trailer which was attached to the Ford
8N.
Charlie then took it to the show ground to be followed by the little AC we
did last winter. No picture of it this year. We worked a lot and finally
got the Farmall H running and ready but all the drivers left so it did not
get to go.

The show went well. I worked on registration at the main gate. However,
Saturday PM, about 3 PM just before scheduled children's event's, the storm
came. All the vendors and exhibitors shut down and headed for home. The same
thing happened last year except we had a tractor pull scheduled for the
evening. It got canceled but we still had to pay $1,200. Hal and Leslie
stopped by the gate Saturday morning. Hal was feeling awful but still moving. The
old engines are always a big draw as they create a lot of smoke and noise.

after the rain quit, Joe and Charlie got the tractors back home for me.

Meanwhile, back at the ranch, Dwight, Diane and various helpers primed then
painted the pool with not only pretty but thermally insulating, glass bead
paint which we believe will make it easier to keep the water warm

Health report is so far, so good.

The person who took NO pills for years !
