Round Three

0 photographs·224 words

Last week when we went to St. Thomas some of the medical reports had been
sent. Some had not. The Cardiologis (Dr. Boyd) was looking at the reports
prepared by the Kingsport Dr (Dr. Turner). rather than at the actual test
data when he said I should see the surgeon for bypass surgery. But, he said we
needed to bring two copies of the heart catherization test cd. One for him
and one for the surgeon (Dr. Shuman).
Linda called the heart center and had the cd sent by overnight mail to Dr.
Boyd. I went to HVCC hospital and picked up another copy to take with us
today.
When we got there today, Dr.Shuman had the cd in hand and had looked at
it. But, Dr. Boyd had not. Dr. Shuman said he wants to review it with Dr.
Boyd and will call me in the morning with their joint recommendation. He
also said he had not viewed it as severly as Dr. Turner and did not believe I
am in immenent danger. He said Dr. Boyd handles difficult stents and has
handled other situations like mine.He also wanted to look at the data from
the stress test but said he would call and get it sent overnight to him.
