January 1, 2017 · Sunday

Reflections

8 photographs·200 words

​As usual, Santa came to our houses late this year but did arrive.

​

GSH got a well deserved electric chair, shake and bake.

​While not feasting here or elsewhere, I worked on the truck bed.

​
I started the year with a couple of resolutions,
Get my eye/glasses work done, visit Kenneth, make grandchild trip. Going
into 2017 I will carry over glasses resolution, add visit at least one far
away grandchild and make one out of country grandchild trip.
But, perhaps most difficult, run, an 8 minute mile.

One of the Deming QC rules is "if it is important, measure it". I obtained
a stop watch/lap counter this year, started going to the track and
discovered how much I had slowed. In trying to build up speed, I got into
the light headed issue.I believe the stents have corrected that problem but
now I am back to SLOW. First I have to get a 2 minute lap. I intend to try
some time this week. Stay tuned.

We lost a lot of family, friends and classmates this year. It hurts. But,
going to John/Savannah wedding, seeing Beth and children, Katherine and
child to be made it all OK.
