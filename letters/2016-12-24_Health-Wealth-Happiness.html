Health, Wealth & Happiness

0 photographs·252 words

In 2009 I had surgery involving 5 blood vessels in and about my heart.
After it was finished I asked the Dr. if that would get me to the end of
the road. He said I might have to have a stent installed "in a few years".

This year, as I have been trying to pick up the pace on my running it has
been getting slower. The rate of change has increased rather sharply in the
last few months to the point I went to see cardiologist who scheduled me
for a battery of tests.
The result of that was scheduling me for a heart catheterizing. I had that
procedure on Friday. One stays awake and involved for that procedure. In
due time, the Dr. said he had encountered rather serious blockage but could
correct with two stents. I told him to go ahead.
He had me kept in the hospital last night. When he came in this morning, he
gave me an in-depth explanation of what had occurred and what he did.
Laura was there, listened carefully, nodded wisely and appeared to
understand it all. So, if you want details, ask her.

I have more and different pills. Plavix he stressed. take faithfully for
the next year to avoid clots forming in the stents.

A bunch of dos and don'ts for the next few days until the access hole heals.

I can ease back in to running next week. At that time, I will know if the
problem is resolved.
