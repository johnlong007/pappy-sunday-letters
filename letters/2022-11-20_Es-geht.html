November 20, 2022 · Sunday

Es geht

11 photographs·275 words

About all I remember from my class in conversational German. But, it sums
up our week.

PBBD is spending a lot of her retirement time tending to the halt. lame and
afflicted. While here she is mastering new skills and acquiring new
knowledge. It was slow painful slogging this week on the '55 brake job. She
had the left front parts on and off many times and we could never get it
right. The Chrysler engineers left more degrees of freedom than we could
master.
Fortunately Wayne showed up with a new look and spotted the immediate
problem right off. So, stay tuned

She continues to work on the '37 when it is judged too cold for outside.
This week she removed a side panel in the engine compartment. We also set
up the MIG welder with intentions to do the body work correctly.

I was not sure this wound deserved a band aide but all know how children
are about getting wounded.

Deer leaping and bounding all over the place. In order to keep them
healthy, we put out a salt block.

One day, I attacked the woodpile and split wood. I am appalled at how
quickly I run down. I have to stop for a breather every few swings.

This one has a decent set of antlers. Rudolph?

Speaking of new skills, if not knowledge. Look what I can do.

All God's children got a place in the choir.

One ear up and one ear down. Dog is coming along.
