April 26, 2020 · Sunday

Progress !

13 photographs·453 words

After several weeks of Preparation the Oak log has been moved. Now, I know
the tools are at hand. I used the block and tackle rig anchored to a large
Poplar to pull forward 10-12 feet'.Then , because I wanted to get it
further up the hill, I anchored to a second Poplar and pulled it sideways.
The piece of metal is to keep it from digging in too badly. The system has
a 7:1 ratio but was all the tractor could pull due to traction. To be
continued. Sarah, note the rollers.

In preparation for moving, I had to jack the log up. Albert came by to
watch and asked if I was not concerned about working under the log out
alone. I told him if I got smushed he could tell people he died doing what
he wanted to do. Next step is going to require jacking it up again.

The block and tackle rig.

View from the end Wayne cut last week where the trunk forked. Bob's 20
inch saw was used. Log is 32 feet long.

Know what this is? Rejoice.

The other indoor objective for the week was brake lights on the 37. The
light switch is a pressure switch on the masster clinder. Unlike cars since
about 1960, brake ligs on old ones meant you had brakes. Now, it means you
mashed the pedal.

Beth and Jake came by. Of course, I conscripted Jake to do some work, under
supervision, I could not handle with my fumble fingers. We toured the
kingdom with them. Dog and cat went along. Examed ramp patches.

Note the F &I trim on the grill. Not as good job as I want, think we may
try again.

The work area at the log was a muddy mess. Max became an equally muddy mess
while helping me. He enjoyed his bath.

All have heard of "a man and his dog". How bout a little ole lady and her
cat?

Always on the alert for free food, we went to PBBD'd foe supper one night.
While there, Sarah showed us her pending flower garden. She also showed us
a fish.

I offered to plow and acre or so for a real garden but she declined.

For those who remember Alf, at one time I asked him what "Edelweiss" as in
Sound of Music means.
He said "a little white flower"

So I suppose these qualify.

If you have seen Babylon pictures you have seen the lion statues guarding
the entrances. On on either side. Here are our guardians.
