May 28, 2017 · Sunday

A Mist

14 photographs·293 words

A Sunday School teacher we had said life is sort of like a mist. Now we
see it and then it is gone.

Kaylee and Parks stopped by for a few minutes one evening. Another evening,
John and Savannah stopped by for a few minutes.

Steven and Jonathan came over to help put a cover over the cherry tree and
Anita came by to help close the spots we missed.

​

​It was up to GSH and me to get the BB's covered.

​​Did I mention Little Dog helping?

The black raspberries are getting close. As I remember, Christopher goes
for those.

​All the project work I got done was making a cardboard pattern for floor
mat in the '49. Now, I must find a suitable mat.

​Lisa also came by with her typical teen.

​Friday was AT work day. We worked at Mountaineer Falls.

​A lot of North bound hikers went through. Probably 30 or so. Traffic is up
sharply this year causing concern about how much traffic the trail can
support. Bob (who runs a hostel) talked at length about the difference in
hikers this year.

As usual, Diane came over a couple of times. Pushed pills, brought a
project for GSH and went over the items I need to procure to get the seat
project back on track.

Ramp fests continue. Laura fed us potatoes and ramps one evening. I believe
I could get used to being waited on hand and foot.

​
Wayne left the country. Gwendolyn is getting set to jump off the edge of
the nest. Hard to tell where Kenneth is this week. We are scattered from
sea to shining sea and beyond.​

Anyone know who this is?

Or where this was taken?
