October 27, 2013 · Sunday

From the Cat House

13 photographs·502 words

No cat pictures. But a lot of time and effort. I did coax it up on the
swing with me one time and managed to pet it w/o damage to either of us.
About the time I think we are making progress one of us will let one or
both dogs escape (faster than a speeding bullet) leading to an instant dogs
chasing cat event.

I picked, GSH canned and various people claimed pears this week. Still
plenty left. Free to good home.

Also harvested some garden produce. A Lot of very hot peppers.

AT work was fun. We working on cutting a relocation around the side of a
hill top. After we were finished I sat down to wait at the start of the
relocation while the others went to far end to check on progress and to
painting blazes on their way back.
I saw movement out of corner of eye. Looked around and a big, black bear
was standing on the old trail right above me outlined against the sky. I
fumbled around to get my camera out of my pack. When I looked back the bear
was standing up sniffing. Then it starting running down the hill . I got a
picture just as it crossed the new trail Note black spot.

I was sitting there by my pack and hard hat when the bear went by.

I took a view picture on the way in and from same place on the way out.
(Just for folks who live elsewhere).

The parts are back from the shop for the MM. Sure enough, I carried the
head from a 400 cid chrysler engine down from the shed. We removed the
valve spring retainer caps, used clips from Dwight's bottomless inventory
of "may need this someday" parts and got the valves installed.

On the other side of the engine, the head is all cleaned up and fitted in
place. Not yet torqued.

Meredith was in for a flying visit. She put in a request for B & G. While
here, her Dad had her try out his new toy and also my new 9MM with a laser
sight. She is good. I also tried the 9MM. I like the laser sight. I could
hit a coke can while shooting from the hip !

I started a fire in fireplace one night and kept it going for a couple of
days. But, it is supposed to warm up this coming week. I have a bunch of
places flagged on my weather site.

Places where various children and grandchildren (also a couple of GGC) are
domiciled. Some are warmer (Texas), some are colder (Anchorage) all are
missed and notes appreciated.
