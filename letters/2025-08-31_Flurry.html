Flurry

18 photographs·361 words

Any farm kid knows what it means when the crops are "laid by" We are
working to get a lot of outside projects laid by.

First, I got to meet our newest. A keeper best I can tell.

A new name is written and its Daniel !

Delayed, but almost as exciting, we got a confirmed sighting of the elusive
Martha, Stella and Granny

After the water temp set a new record, and after Alan installed a ladder,
could delay no longer.

Our drive exit is at the low point for all the runoff on our property. It
takes the end of the drive gravel into and across the road. I think we will
have to design and install some sort of deflector along the last 75 feet or
so beside the drive, Stay tuned.

The A-C tractor took a lot of time, $ and effort this week. I want to get
it laid by so Diana can get back on the PT50

The Wheels are ready. Hub is ready, the spindle is ready.

Tires are waiting. Anyone want to learn a new skill?? Refresh an old skill?

Don stopped by and checked the Mag to get a spark. No success.

However, Wayne stepped in and finished the job. Tractor now has nise spark.
Hard to believe that the tractor was parked with fuel in the tanks and in
the lines. It is now a stinky gooey mess. So, cleaning must be completed
before starting the engine will be scheduled.

While here Wayne did some other tasks which I can no longer do. I really
appreciate it.

He also moved the last Walnut log to the sawmill site.

A handy helper stopped by.

Lest anyone thanks I cannot do anything for myself.

Another of the frequent visitors. It appears to have been in a fight.
Notice the skinned patches. They seem to be healing.

A steady stream of South Bounders.

Hard mast. Know what species?
