Looking Back

11 photographs·529 words

GSH and I have been most fortunate. Among other good fortune we have
traveled and lived in different places. We have always attended church
wherever we were, even if we could not understand a word being said. It is
a joy to go into a strange church family in a strange country and be
accepted on face value.

Probably the warmest acceptance we ever had was a the URC in Workington.
Many have met Joan and Andy who invited us to dinner the first Sunday we
attended.

Many have also Met Ev , one of the nicest people I have ever known. Every
time we have gone back for a visit I have told her that if she invited us
to tea, we would accept. She always has. So have I.

While we were in the UK, Anita gave birth to Kenneth. Ev, knit him a "cot"
blanket. I believe all the GC since then have received one also.

We got our annual note from Ev this week. It occurred to me that some might
want to thank her. Maybe even send her a note, maybe a picture.

While Kenneth is the focus, he slipped into town (without giving me fair
warning) and gave a presentation at TTU on Friday. However, the word got
out and we went to Oak Ridge Saturday where I got a personal presentation.
I was both honored and mildly surprised, not at his presenting but at my
mostly understanding. One of my wisest moves has been visiting him at CERN
when he could show us the inner working of the LHC. Kenneth sent me a PDF
of the presentation. If anyone else is interested he might also for them.
Before I went off to CERN, GSH gave me a great courses video on Quantum
Mechanics. It is available for loan. I have watched it 3 times .

As a bonus on the OR outing, Emily was there and Rachel showed up. Emily is
getting more relaxed every time I get to see her and Rachel hit the ground
relaxed. Also appearing along with Jake and Beth were 4, count em, four
noodniks.

Anita left for Sandy Utah today.

We go to church with the "other" grandparents. Her Grandmother lit up with
delight when I called her "granny" today. Sometimes, I enjoy being old and
able to get away with about anything due to age and mileage.

On the home front, it was cold all week. But, we still got in our track
time. In case you are interested in current track fashions:

Last week, the plan was Alan was going to put a coat of primer on the '63.
That did not work out and I am glad. I continued with the "final" sanding
and found the rear wheel cutout and quarter panel were not up to m usual
low standards.

I did a lot of sanding and filling work this week. Will look at it again in
the light of day tomorrow.
