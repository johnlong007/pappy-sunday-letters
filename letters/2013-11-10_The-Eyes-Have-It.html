November 10, 2013 · Sunday

The Eyes Have It

13 photographs·521 words

GSH had cataract surgery, Left eye three weeks ago and right eye this week.
My role has been administer eye drops as prescribed (and directed). She has
yet to exclaim about how much better she can see. Perhaps it is like my
hearing aid, a mixed blessing.

The eye work prevented my AT work on Tuesday but expect to get my reward in
the here after.

It has been pretty good weather so one day I pushed the '37 outside to sand
on it. Materials are in hand and I am ready to declare it ready to paint.
Just waiting for a skilled painter. Dwight has promised to volunteer his
son Pat for the job during Thanksgiving break.

Also moving along, the MM is now assembled. Kim came by one day to raid the
garden and while here helped me set the hood on over the newly fabricated
exhaust pipe.

It may be noted the pipe is a bit catty wampus (or is it si-googling?) and
will require more adjusting.

The garden has done well this year. Various people have shared to varying
amounts. This week GSH assisted in cutting the corn stalks and was eager to
learn how to shock corn stalks.

As usual, the dog also assisted.

The ground has now been seeded with Rye and is ready for Winter.

The cat is getting adjusted to life in the country. One day I watched it
lurking beneath the bird feeder. The dog also watched.

On another jump, the cat caught a bird in his mouth and hung onto the
feeder with
his paws. I have since moved the feeder about a foot higher to make it more
of a challenge.

Friday I went to LLLL's to help with cutting brush and to give GSH a brief
vacation.

Does everyone remember my many, many lectures on the subject of never,
ever, no how, leave a tool or implement flat on the ground? Well, see
exhibit "A" as to what can happen when said principle is violated.

I had a fine visit (except as previously noted). Some may also remember
mini-lecture to the tune of "tools (or cars) can be replaced, people
cannot" I found the different perception of appropriate clothing
interesting.

Andrew was hosting a hot dog outing Friday night, Rebecca and David were
co-host(est)ing on Saturday night. We cut brush and downed wood such that
both had plenty for their bonfires.

I wonder if A & M still has their bonfire?

The apples are about gone. Turned into apple pies. Today's pie if not he
best ever was definately in the top five.
