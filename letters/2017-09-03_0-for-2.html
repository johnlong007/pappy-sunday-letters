If you have a plan, nothing can go wrong ! Correct?

I always like to start the week with a plan for what I want to accomplish
during the week. I gave up on save the world quite some time back. Now, it
is a bit more limited.

This week my intent was ;

1. Get the second trailer legal, lights and brakes

and

2. Mow hillside and retrieve rig.

More on those later. On the positive side my running is going pretty well.
GSH has been getting her 10K steps most every day and I have been "running"
a 5K about every other day. I have to start real slow and gradually build
up . From 4 minute laps to 3 minute laps. Always limited by light headed
rather than breathing.

GSH walks while I run and drags brush while I am working on the trailer.

Sometimes my propensity to make do and get by annoys even me. The trailer
wiring was a mess. But, it worked for brakes, signal and running lights. I
have added brakes on all wheels which require wiring. I should have ripped
it all out and started from scratch. Instead i jumped, spliced patched and
cobbled. Now, some of the lights work and some do not. To be continued.

The other big project was mowing hillside. It is 93 % done.

For various reasons, I decided to mow up and down the steep parts. I
determined that without weight on the front of the tractor the hill beside
the little storage building is too steep . Fortunately the mower limit's
vertical travel of the tractor. However, rolling backward down a steep hill
is no fun.

​

​​We did have an AT work day. I walked out to check the shelter and found
one small blowdown and a small leaner. I think we are going back this week
so will get them soon..

Charlie and Kathryn (with child) have moved to TN. To welcome them, we had
a Long party tonight.

Next weeks plan is being formulated. Nothing can go wrong !​
