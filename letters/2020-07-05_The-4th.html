July 5, 2020 · Sunday

The 4th

13 photographs·391 words

All the way through 8th grade,school started with pledge of allegiance.
While still at Temple Hill I was allowed to help take flag down and put it
up. Those tasks required learning flag protocol. It was an honor and a
serious responsibility. The flag was treated with respect second only to
the Bible.

The things going on now cause me serious concern. What have the parents,
teachers and politicians done to our young people?

We have a lot of young ones in the family. I hope all will be responsible
citizens.

At least two have taken the oath to preserve and protect.

Aaron

Adam

My week was marred by miss steps and blunders. Made a trip to Erwin to get
the blue truck Herb left for Ella operational. failed. Will have to buy a
new battery.

PBBD helped me measure the downed Black Oak timber. I think about 7,000
board feet. Still hope to find a use.

We did not try to calculate the poplar, cherry or pine.

We had our regular 6 month visit to the Dr. Older but no wiser. Watch out
for the virus. Aortic valve getting noisier, restricting flow more. I can
tell. Time to seriously think about valve job.

Joe and Barry decided to go ahead with Blountville July 4th. parade. I was
very pleased with them. Most cancelled.

After the parade numerous family assembled for food and fireworks

Sunday afternoon, the party moved to Meredith's for more food. No mention
of fireworks.

Which bird is going in the wrong direction?

We enjoy watching the birds. Here and at PBBD's.

Wayne is getting closer on the Ford truck. Took it up and down drive a few
times. Needs carb work. I wonder if Dwight trained Pat/

Wayne promises he is going to get rid of accumulated metal. I loaded all
the loose pieces in the truck which awaits him. Next I intend to load those
two cars he dragged in to get one rear window for the now departed Daytona.
But decided to coat the trailer with waterproofing first. Tried to enlist
LLLL for the task but she insisted it be pressure washed first.

And that's the way it is.
