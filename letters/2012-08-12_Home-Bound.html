August 12, 2012 · Sunday

Home Bound

8 photographs·262 words

I think it appropriate that I should include pictures for the benefit of
Katy, illustrating what being a GSH entails.

Of course, such behaviors can and should result in being hand fed a BLTC
sandwich.

And Sunday afternoon spent enjoying ones own ,hand made,deck and furniture.

With the aid and assistance of two willing workers, a bunch more wood has
been split and the big log converted into big chunks.

It seems I have become the rock splitter/worker for our trail crew. Tuesday
we worked at Backbone rock. We are enhancing the blue trail which runs from
the highway to the AT. A short ways up the trail there a section was quite
steep and root covered. The trail runs between two converging rock faces.

We dug out the roots and installed custom split and shaped steps.

In case anyone wants to give it a go, a lady went along with us to go
collect mushrooms while we were working. She explained her harvest to me
including the name and taste of each variant.

When we stopped Tuesday, Bob said we should return and finish the steps
this coming week. In addition, about 100 ASU students are scheduled to be
here Friday to work on the re locations on up the hill on the blue trail.
Not sure if I will go along or not.

All in all, a quite week in Mudville. Summer is about over. I still have a
long list of unfinished projects.

We have corn, beans and tomatoes available. So far, the crows have been our
best customers.
