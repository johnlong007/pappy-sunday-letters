December 29, 2019 · Sunday

Christmas 2019

12 photographs·461 words

Exceeded expectations.

Monday we went to sign off on the top which is to be installed on the '63.
The material is just what I remembered so I told him to press on. He said
it will only take another week or so. I hope it is so well done I can
forget the bad taste at start.

Niece Lisa puts out an SOS from time to time. We went over to check on some
items. I took my good battery charger to put on back hoe battery. All in
preparation to making an operator of her.

We also worked on the water flow problem to the pond. It was stopped. We
located the entrance from the branch.

Nephew James came in to visit Later in week and cleared more of the
entrance. We went over today and found a good flow rate and pond level
going up.

Official start of Christmas was digging out decorations. We have a
selection of nativity scenes collected from around the world.

In keeping it simple and traditional, we put up a cedar tree harvested by
Anita (or maybe Joe).

GSH has taken to walking with hands together behind her back. In the hope
of getting her to stop the practice, and to help keep hands warm, Santa
left har a muff. Fur side inside, skin side outside (or vice versa).

Recently a grandchild now living in California was visiting sporting a
really nice sun shade hat. He promised to get me one. He must have spoken
to Santa to get off the hook.

Note, a rare event, a posed picture.

We had family and friends coming from far and wide, coming and going at
seemingly random times, PBBD had events on two days then Laura had an all
day event Saturday afternoon.

I usually do not take pictures at events held elsewhere.
Why could I not resist taking this picture?

Before my teen years, on Christmas day (unless Sunday) in the PM the boys
would migrate to the church parking lot and have a shooting match. I got to
watch. JR Bowman had a 410 over/under. I now own one.
While Adam was here this week, I got him to clean it along with others.

We were listening to Statlers sing "Maple Street Memories". GSH said she
wanted to go walk down Maple Street where she lived while in elementary
school.

We went.

Here is the house.

Lady owner came out. She is a half back from upstate NY who has lived in
the house for 19 years. A nice lady who invited GSH to come in and look
around.
