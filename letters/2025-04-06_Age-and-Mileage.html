April 6, 2025 · Sunday

Age and Mileage

12 photographs·360 words

This picture is "Laura's Ark". She came home from the hospital in New
Orleans in it and continued until displaced by Linda in 1965. Our new 1963
Chrysler 300 had a fold down section between the front bucket seats. I took
it out and made the arc to fit.

This picture was taken beside Lake Pontchartrain. Ark would have been in
the car. After we got the little Blue truck in 1965, the Ark fit perfect on
the engine compartment between the seats. Linda and then Wayne rode
hundreds of miles that way.

I really like the flag. While we are outside working or bird watching it is
visible.

The heavy equipment dismantlers stayed busy. Can it live again?

Diane has a site on her device which identifies birds by picking up their
chirping. I can hardly wait for the upgrade when it translates into
English. What would this one say about watching it taking a bath?

We went back to visit Bob Harvey again. A dlightful person .

When my father bought the mountain property, he bought three Mowing
scythes. The big one on the left was Herbs. Small one on the ground was
mine. I had Jimmies but cannot locate . Rats !

The other two I procured for use here. Now, I need capable, willing workers.

The next step in hand tool implement renovation is storing and displaying.
The plan is to hang on the side of the ag building.

We have given up on getting a decent stand of grass out front and shifting
to Periwinkle, Our fence to stop tromping and stomping has been a failure.
Diane has procured a fence for which I have high hopes. This week, the
plan is to transplant a lot more sprigs.

Some hand tool renovation continued, The major project was the red wagon.
Diane is engaged in dent removal.

A single row of ramp root stock a couple of years back. In a few years,
whoever lives here can have a ramp festival !
