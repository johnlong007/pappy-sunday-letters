*Dear Mrs and Mr Buchanan,

*Thank you for travelling with us!

My name is Romina Kuharic and I will be your Trip Leader during your 15 days
winter adventure,

I come from Croatia and therefore I’m very happy that you've decided to
explore my country .

I have lived and traveled abroad and I know how important it is to have
somebody abroad who will welcome you and share his culture and customs. That
“somebody” for you will be Me and the local guides. I’ll try to do my best
and what I ask from you is to bring curiosity and love for traveling. It’s
the best you can do and the result will be your overall satisfaction!

*My personal tips:*

-This part of Europe that you visit is safe and you can feel comfortable
about this trip

- *Make sure you pack your medicine and some of your clothes in your hand
luggage so that you have something to wear in case of ‘lost luggage’*.

-winter months in Croatia are very cold and rainy. The temperature at the
moment goes from 30 to 48F (2 to 11C). Have good walking shoes, walking
canes and warm rain jackets! Make sure your shoes aren’t slippery, as we’ll
walk in the nature and on uneven terrain..

-We’re out of the tourist season. Tourist season goes approx from April to
October. At the moment most hotels, restaurants and souvenir shops are
closed and towns and island villages are very desert. On the other hand,
it’s a perfect occasion for a unique experience and a possibility to get
into touch with real Croatia.

-12/11 we depart Zagreb and drive to Neum (Bosnia and Hercegovina). The bus
drive will last all day (with stops). We’ll embark approx at 6-7pm.

-our group of 26pax will share the small ship with another 24pax OAT group

*The most important*: don’t forget to bring your smile and lots of good
will! Travel with your eyes and heart wide open!

**

I will do my best to share with you this interesting but also controversial
story of my country and surrounding regions, their history, people and their
lives. It is an amazing and yet undiscovered part of Europe that has a lot
to offer especially with its natural but also artistic beauties.

*Maybe you already have some ideas of places and things you would like to
see…or some special interests. If so, let me know upon your arrival, and I
will be glad to help you with it.*
