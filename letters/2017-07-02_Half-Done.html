July 2, 2017 · Sunday

Half Done

12 photographs·437 words

Not quite a very average week.

Mostly stayed around the home place and fiddled with the IH tractor when
not mowing.

I cracked a tooth last Friday night. Stopped by to give Allison some hands
on practical experience..She examined it, nodded wisely and tried to find a
dentist. Turns out dentist do not work on Friday. So I went in to my
regular dentist Monday and had a temp. crown installed. Drat.

Another day we went to check on progress on the Pat and Karen cabin.

​LLLL came home to check on the ole folks at home. I tried to get some help
on planning one more trip but no success. My father instilled so deeply
into me that the animals come first that I have a really difficult time
going off and leaving them
Thinking about a drive around US. Maybe a fly drive to Nova Scotia open to
suggestions and offers on house sitting.

We did get the IH tractor running well. The wiring is a mess. So, with the
aid if not comfort (Rachel, time to start thinking about duties of a GSH)​
we skipped all the wiring and installed a jumper switch for the ignition
and another for the starter.

Aside for hearing the tractor start and run the most exciting part was
opening the tool box. My reflexes are dulled, I got stung 3 times on my
hand before I made my escape.

Wayne and GSH worked on rebuilding the deck table while I was gone to do AT
work (at Carver's Gap) Thursday.​

​
​
Carvers Gap has the most heavily trafficked section of trail on our part.
There were a lot of folks out Thursday. Keeping the trail rebuilt where
people take shortcuts is a constant battle.

Notice where we have tried to block the ditches beat out on either side of
the trail We can manage runoff on the trail but not in the side ditches
formed by hikers.
When we came out a group of MC riders from Ga were at the bottom. A bunch
of old codgers (like us). Riding Cushman scooters.

​(For the benefit of anyone far away from the mountains of home.​

The red raspberries are ripe. Linda picked a lot around here, Wayne picked
some we have picked some there are still plenty. Today, we went out to
hillside and checked there. Not so many on the place we looked.

​But we did get good company for supper.

Tonight, at the Long's, we got to see new (and improved?) GGC number
5.Actually, we got to see number 2,3 and 4 also.
All is well.​
