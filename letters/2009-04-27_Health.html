April 27, 2009 · Monday

Health

0 photographs·159 words

Went to see GP this morning to get results from testing to determine cause
of light headed (and sometimes concurrent chest tightening) while running.

Echocardiogram and Transthoracic Echo
"normal"

VAS Carotid Doppler, Bilateral Carotid Doppler:
"no evidence of a significant carotid stenosis"

He talked about the stress test, I got no written report.

What I heard:

I may have some coronary blockage, he will refer to a cardiologist for an
appointment. That person will talk to me and review the same information
before deciding if I should have a heart catherization.

Possible results of said examination could be
1. No problem
2. Stents
3. By-pass
4. pills

Until then, I am taking a pill/day (simvastatin 40mg) and a baby aspirin.

Also, when I am running if I start to get light headed, slow down until it
goes away. That worked well today.
