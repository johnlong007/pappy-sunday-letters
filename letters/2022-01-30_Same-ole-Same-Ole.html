right in the middle with instructions to come back in 6 months.

Alan and others worked on arming the sawmill this week.

He set up his transit and worked to level the trailer under the mill then
worked to level the mill on the trailer. All with great precision. I think
all that is left is all the required fluids.

In addition, A & C have been having dinner and a show with us

PBBD worked on the sawmill, the '37, shopping and bringing up another load
of firewood. When keeping a fire in the shop stove in addition to the
fireplace, we go through a lot of wood.

Friday morning, our cat was on the table, breathing fast and heavy. I put
some treats in front of him which he ignored. Called Anita. She took him to
the vet where they ran many tests, kept him overnight and pronounced severe
allergy reaction. He has recently started traveling around the kitchen and
dining room by jumping from cabine to counter. Along the way, nibbling and
licking anything left in a cup or dish. I suspect Onions.. Back home noon
Saturday and seams ok.

Most of the family know how I came to have a thing about chocolate covered
cherries. Clearly, Katy does, she sent me a birthday present.

We went to SS today.This picture is our classroom at starting time. Two
years ago it would have been 40 people.Some dead, some departed, some
hunkered down.

I mentioned PBBD shopping for us. She brought in fresh strawberries.
