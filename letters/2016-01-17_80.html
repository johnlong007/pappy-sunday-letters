January 17, 2016 · Sunday

80 !

10 photographs·366 words

Many years ago I told my sister she could be old when she reached 80. Now,
she is 86 TODAY !!! and I have changed my mind.

I had sort of intended to go visit and offer my condolences but the VW has
quit and I miss the heated seats. Especially for long drives (or short
drives in cold weather). This year I had a three cake birthday event. For a
first, the event was managed by a couple of very talented and thoughtful
granddaughters.

Herb has remained in the Rehab center in JC all week. It is not going well
at all.

On Thursday, we went back to Laurel Falls for the third and final week. It
was colder but we started work an hour late (10) and I dressed better so it
was a fine day to be out.

Since the cold arrived about a week ago it is obvious I need and indoor
project. So, time to bring the '63 back into the shop. To my middling
surprise and delight, it started right up. First will come cut and replace
the rusty metal body parts. At the same time, GSH will be working on the
upholstery.

Speaking of upholstery, we stopped by the shop to check on the Studebaker.
No progress. I intend to check in at least once/week and take GSH along to
help explain things to him. Stay tuned.

GSH got a FitBit for Christmas it is going great, She has met goal every
day but did not get counted on Thursday when I was gone and she did not
wear it. I was pretty sure I was needed for something around here. I will
include a picture of her running up her steps after we got home from church
today.

We have a big flock of turkeys hanging around this year. I got a picture
then the little dog saw them and put them to flight.

Alan asked about the house next door I had mentioned. It is on a 2 acre
tract with the big Yellow pine trees around the periphery. I do not intend
to get serious about it without a definite plan as to who will occupy.
