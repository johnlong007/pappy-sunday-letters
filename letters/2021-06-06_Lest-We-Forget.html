Lest We Forget

10 photographs·467 words

I went to Erwin Monday to take Aunt Flo to visit Bud's gravesite. However
Mike had already taken her. We talked about those she knew who died in
combat. She mentioned Cecil Cousins and Roy Tinker. Each being a big
brother to my friends at Temple Hill. Neither body was returned. Both died
in European combat.

Today is the anniversary of "D" day. A bit strange that I can remember
Pearl Harbor but not D day. I wonder if Sis remembers?

Other anniversaries. Our official portrait :

Gwendolyn earned all sorts of points in the here if not the hereafter by
using up some of her home time to come visit us. Not only visiting but
making biscuits, sausage, scrambled eggs with ramps and real ham

Family members came in from miles around to be fed, watered and
entertained. I had picked a folder of pictures covering 66 years or so.
Wayne began narating right before time for the party to end. Anyone
recognize the table? We moved into an unfurnished house in Huntsville.
First item we purchased was that pictured table. Remind me to tell you
about the Mormon visiting team and having to sit on the floor.

Herb left his tractor to Farmer Lisa. I have been sort of advising on care
and feeding. One of the first words of advice were do not run it out of
fuel. She ran it empty. Fortunately it was in the shade. A real pain to
bleed and get it going. But, we got it.

Last year I spent time and money getting the big trailer legal. I am ready
to give it a test run but do not trust the tires. So, I have been getting a
selection of spares ready. Trailer tires have very stiff sidewalls and do
not seat easily. I used all the tricks I knew before trying a new one. I
did get it seated.
Part of getting a tire seated involves putting a jacking strap around the
center and cinching it to expand the sidewalls. When releasing a strap
under tension after the tire is inflated the buckle pops open with force.
One ripped a chunk out of my right hand some years back.
This time, being very careful, I only got nicked.

I sprayed upholstery glue on the bead to get it to seal. Messy but it
worked.

No Summer Camp willing workers, no big gardens. But, a patch of peppers.
All hot. Only four tomato plants and two cucumbers. Not nearly as
impressive as some others. Ask Christopher for a picture. John ? Allison?

Fortunately, I do have one WW from time to time.

Update on the retaining basin.
