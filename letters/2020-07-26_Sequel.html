Gather ye rosebuds while ye may,
Old Time is still a-flying;
And this same flower that smiles today
Tomorrow will be dying.

I worked with Bob Walters when I first went to ECC. He was a draftsman who
worked in maintenance dept. Wanted to be an engineer. I did some things to
help him along the way and he graduated from UT as an ME in due time. He
continued to work in maintenance after graduation and became one of the
regular Indy group.
He liked to quote the verse above.
One Friday night he was stopped at a traffic light with wife in car on
Center street. A speeding, drinking driver hit their car in the rear
killing them both.

As an engineer I like to see data and be able to sort out things for
myself. I judge almost all the covid numbers being published are without
meaning.

Try these data for useful meaning.

Serious rejoicing !
