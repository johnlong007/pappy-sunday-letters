January 13, 2019 · Sunday

Age

5 photographs·345 words

January is a big month for birthdays in our family.

Wednesday, Laura, GSH and I drove to Memphis via Union City in order to ply
my aged sister with cake and good wishes.

Then we pressed on to Memphis and being well warmed to the task by then had
a repeat performance for Allison.

While in Memphis, Allison did part two on my teeth procedure. Not quite
sure what the name for the procedure is. However, it was on her mandatory
list and I had to show significant improvement in healthy gums since
initial work.
I did, at least the gums did. My years of benign neglect finally paid off.

Allison took many before pictures and x-rays and many after pictures and x
rays. I think she has to give a presentation. I am not sure it will be open
to the public but you can ask.

On the home front, a bit more progress on the "63 interior.

When I was doing my year in review, I failed to check the Fitbit. I have
now corrected that oversight. for the year, I logged 2,636.34 miles.
Had I known and planned ahead, I believe that would have been just right to
get me to Gwendolyn's doorstep in Portland. With the miles I failed to
record I could have gone by way SLC and Fresno

Herb's oldest child is Cathy Pate. She is married to Jimmy Pate. On
December 28th he had an automobile accident. We went to visit him in the JC
hospital today. Well broken up with surgery to follow as soon as he is in
shape to stand it. Some time back, the WSJ had an article about "self
driving" cars. I was struck by the last paragraph.

I am going to paste it here as food for thought.

After all, it’s not keeping to a lane that’s hard. It’s predicting what all
those capricious and distracted human drivers around you might do next.
That’s what keeps the computer programmers up at night.
